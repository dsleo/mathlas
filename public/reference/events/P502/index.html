1978 · Logic & foundations

Wu's method of characteristic sets for mechanised geometry theorem proving

Ritt's characteristic sets turn elementary geometry statements into algebraic elimination so that machines can prove them.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1950

    Ritt's characteristic sets in differential algebra

    precursor

    Ritt, Differential Algebra, AMS Colloquium Publications (1950)

    Ritt's characteristic sets, developed in the late 1940s, are the algebraic idea that Wu's method makes effective.

    Evidence (2)
    • Characteristic set introduced by Ritt in the late 1940s Wikipedia: Wu's method of characteristic set
    • Ritt's monograph Differential Algebra, year 1950 Open Library
  2. 1978

    Mechanised decision method for elementary geometry

    first statement

    Wu, Wen-tsun, On the decision problem and the mechanization of theorem-proving in elementary geometry, Scientia Sinica 21 (1978) 159-172

    Wu states the characteristic-set decision method for elementary geometry in its English form.

    Evidence (3)
    • English exposition, author and year Crossref
    • Wu's method introduced in the late 1970s Wikipedia: Wu's method of characteristic set
    • zbMATH: Wu, 'On the decision problem and the mechanization of theorem-proving in elementary geometry', Sci. Sin. 21, 159-172 (1978) zbMATH Open 3586510
  3. 1978

    Automatic proofs of elementary geometry theorems

    first application

    Wu, Wen-tsun, On the decision problem and the mechanization of theorem-proving in elementary geometry, Scientia Sinica 21 (1978) 159-172

    The same paper applies the method to geometric theorem proving, the first purposeful use on the target problem.

    Evidence (3)
    • Method applied to elementary geometry theorem proving Wikipedia: Wu's method of characteristic set
    • Paper title names mechanization of theorem-proving in elementary geometry Crossref
    • zbMATH: Wu, 'On the decision problem and the mechanization of theorem-proving in elementary geometry', Sci. Sin. 21, 159-172 (1978) zbMATH Open 3586510
  4. 1986

    Basic principles of mechanical theorem proving in geometries

    key development

    Wu, Basic principles of mechanical theorem proving in elementary geometries, Journal of Automated Reasoning 2 (1986)

    The 1986 article sets out the principles of the method and their use across geometric theorems, making it matter for automated reasoning.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1986 Journal of Automated Reasoning article, author and year Crossref
    • Wu's method as a pioneer of mechanical theorem proving Wikipedia: Wu Wenjun
  5. 1994

    Machine Proofs in Geometry (monograph)

    consolidation

    Chou, Gao, Zhang, Machine Proofs in Geometry, World Scientific (1994)

    The 1994 monograph gives the first systematic book-length treatment of the characteristic-set method for geometry.

    Dating or attribution note

    Chou-Gao-Zhang are the systematic developers of the geometry programme; whether this is the first book-length treatment is an inference, not checked against a bibliography.

    Evidence (1)
    • Monograph title, authors, year (Series on Applied Mathematics) Crossref
