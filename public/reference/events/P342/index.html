2004 · Combinatorics

Graph limits and graphons

Dense graph sequences were given analytic limit objects, unifying extremal combinatorics and statistical physics.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2004

    Dense graph limit object described

    first statement

    L. Lovász and B. Szegedy, Limits of dense graph sequences, arXiv:math/0408173 (2004)

    The preprint represents limits of convergent dense-graph sequences by symmetric measurable functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv records the 2004 public preprint and describes the limit object. Original bibliographic record
  2. 2008

    Convergent sequences of dense graphs I

    key development

    Borgs, Chayes, Lovász, Sós, Vesztergombi, Convergent sequences of dense graphs I: subgraph frequencies, metric properties and testing, 2008, pp. 1801-1851

    Establishes the metric and testing theory of dense graph convergence that makes graphons central.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for this title, year 2008, pages 1801-1851 zbMATH Open
  3. 2012

    Graph-limit monograph published

    consolidation

    L. Lovász, Large Networks and Graph Limits, AMS Colloquium Publications 60 (2012)

    The monograph systematically presents graph limits and their applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • AMS bibliographic record identifies the 2012 monograph. Original bibliographic record
