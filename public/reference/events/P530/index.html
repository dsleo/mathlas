2020 · Differential geometry

Synthetic Lorentzian curvature from optimal transport (non-smooth singularity theorems)

Triangle comparison with time separation and optimal-transport characterisations of Ricci curvature were combined to define curvature bounds without smoothness and to reprove the Hawking and Penrose singularity theorems there.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2018

    Transport entropy characterizes smooth strong energy condition

    precursor

    R. J. McCann, Displacement convexity of Boltzmann's entropy characterizes the strong energy condition from general relativity, arXiv:1808.01536 (2018)

    A smooth-spacetime transport characterization precedes the synthetic extension.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted August 2018 arXiv
  2. 2020

    Synthetic timelike Ricci bounds formulated

    first statement

    F. Cavalletti and A. Mondino, Optimal transport in Lorentzian synthetic spaces, synthetic timelike Ricci curvature lower bounds and applications, arXiv:2004.08934 (2020)

    The preprint defines timelike Ricci lower bounds using transport in Lorentzian synthetic spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted April 2020 arXiv
  3. 2020

    Synthetic curvature applied to Lorentzian comparison

    first application

    F. Cavalletti and A. Mondino, Optimal transport in Lorentzian synthetic spaces, synthetic timelike Ricci curvature lower bounds and applications, arXiv:2004.08934 (2020)

    The paper uses the new transport bounds for geometric comparison applications in Lorentzian length spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • The paper's title and abstract identify applications arXiv
  4. 2024

    Timelike Ricci bounds for low regularity spacetimes

    key development

    Braun, Calisti, et al., Timelike Ricci bounds for low regularity spacetimes by optimal transport, Comm. Contemp. Math., 2024

    Extends the optimal-transport timelike Ricci bound framework to low-regularity spacetimes.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for this title, year 2024, article 2350049 zbMATH Open
  5. 2025

    Synthetic null hypersurfaces studied

    key development

    F. Cavalletti, A. Mondino and E. Manini, On the geometry of synthetic null hypersurfaces, arXiv:2506.04934 (2025)

    The later paper develops geometry of null hypersurfaces in the synthetic setting.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted June 2025 arXiv
