1984 · Group & representation theory

Conformal field theory

Belavin, Polyakov and Zamolodchikov classify critical 2D models via Virasoro.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1970

    Virasoro algebra in dual-resonance models

    precursor

    M. A. Virasoro, Subsidiary conditions and ghosts in dual-resonance models, Phys. Rev. D 1 (1970)

    Introduces the algebra of constraints that became the Virasoro algebra of 2D conformal symmetry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1970. Crossref
    • Wikipedia notes the Virasoro algebra as the quantum symmetry algebra of 2D conformal theories. English Wikipedia, Conformal field theory
  2. 1973

    Conformal operator product expansion

    precursor

    S. Ferrara, A. F. Grillo, R. Gatto, Tensor representations of conformal algebra and conformally covariant operator product expansion, Ann. Phys. 76 (1973)

    Develops conformally covariant operator product expansions in four dimensions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1973. Crossref
  3. 1984

    BPZ: infinite conformal symmetry in 2D QFT

    first statement

    Belavin, Polyakov, Zamolodchikov, Infinite conformal symmetry in two-dimensional quantum field theory, Nuclear Physics B 241 (1984) 333-380

    Introduces the infinite-dimensional local conformal algebra (Virasoro) for two-dimensional critical models.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, year and pages verified (Crossref) Crossref
    • Wikipedia attributes the 2D development to the BPZ article (dated 1983 there, the preprint year) Wikipedia: Conformal field theory
  4. 1984

    Critical exponents from conformal invariance and unitarity

    first application

    Friedan, Qiu, Shenker, Conformal invariance, unitarity, and critical exponents in two dimensions, Phys. Rev. Lett. 52 (1984) 1575-1578

    Uses conformal invariance and unitarity to constrain critical exponents of two-dimensional models.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, year and pages verified (Crossref); content taken from the title only Crossref
  5. 1984

    Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equations

    key development

    V. G. Knizhnik and A. B. Zamolodchikov, Current algebra and Wess-Zumino model in two dimensions, Nucl. Phys. B 247 (1984)

    Gives differential equations for correlation functions in WZW models.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1984. Crossref
  6. 1986

    Cardy's operator content from modular invariance

    key development

    J. L. Cardy, Operator content of two-dimensional conformally invariant theories, Nucl. Phys. B 270 (1986)

    Constrains the spectrum of a 2D CFT by modular invariance.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1986. Crossref
  7. 1988

    Ginsparg's Applied Conformal Field Theory lectures

    consolidation

    Paul Ginsparg, Applied Conformal Field Theory, Les Houches 1988 lectures

    Lecture notes that became the standard first reference, earlier than the 1997 textbook.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record hep-th/9108028: Applied Conformal Field Theory, Les Houches 1988 lectures. arXiv
  8. 1997

    Monograph on conformal field theory

    consolidation

    Di Francesco, Mathieu, Senechal, Conformal Field Theory, Springer (Graduate Texts in Contemporary Physics), 1997

    Standard graduate monograph treating CFT systematically.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book title, publisher and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
