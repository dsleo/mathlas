2013 · Graph theory

Ramanujan graphs / interlacing

Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava proved existence of bipartite Ramanujan graphs of all degrees via interlacing polynomials, also solving Kadison-Singer.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1988

    Classical Ramanujan graph constructions

    precursor

    A. Lubotzky, R. Phillips and P. Sarnak, Ramanujan graphs, Combinatorica 8 (1988)

    Explicit constructions cover special degrees before the all-degree interlacing result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher record dates the classical paper to 1988 Springer / Crossref
  2. 2013

    Interlacing families for all-degree Ramanujan graphs

    first statement

    A. Marcus, D. A. Spielman and N. Srivastava, Interlacing Families I: Bipartite Ramanujan Graphs of All Degrees, arXiv:1304.4132 (2013)

    The public preprint introduces interlacing-polynomial existence arguments and states all-degree bipartite Ramanujan graph families.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted April 2013 arXiv
  3. 2013

    Interlacing applied to graph 2-lifts

    first application

    A. Marcus, D. A. Spielman and N. Srivastava, Interlacing Families I: Bipartite Ramanujan Graphs of All Degrees, arXiv:1304.4132 (2013)

    The method proves existence of good 2-lifts and infinite bipartite Ramanujan families in every degree above two.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract states the 2-lift target arXiv
  4. 2015

    Ramanujan graphs of all sizes

    key development

    Marcus, A. W., Spielman, D. A., Srivastava, N., Interlacing families IV: Bipartite Ramanujan graphs of all sizes, FOCS 2015

    Extends the interlacing method to bipartite Ramanujan graphs of every size, not only every degree.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, FOCS 2015, DOI 10.1109/focs.2015.87 Crossref
