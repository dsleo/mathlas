1960 · Optimization & control

Control theory becomes a mathematical discipline (Kalman state space; ICM 1966 section)

State-space methods, optimal control and filtering turn engineering control into a mathematical subject, and the 1966 Moscow congress lists a section on control theory.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1868

    Maxwell, On governors

    precursor

    J. C. Maxwell, On governors, Proc. R. Soc. London 16 (1868)

    First mathematical analysis of feedback stability by linearisation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Maxwell, On governors, Proceedings of the Royal Society, 1868. Crossref, DOI 10.1098/rspl.1867.0055
    • MacTutor biography of Maxwell. MacTutor, biography of Maxwell
  2. 1945

    Bode's feedback amplifier design

    precursor

    H. W. Bode, Network Analysis and Feedback Amplifier Design, Van Nostrand 1945

    Frequency-domain feedback theory preceding state-space methods.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive copy, 1945. Internet Archive dli.ernet.15701
    • Wikipedia places Bode among the founders of frequency-domain control. English Wikipedia, Control theory
  3. 1960

    Kalman's general theory of control systems

    first statement

    Kalman, R. E., On the general theory of control systems, IFAC Proceedings (Moscow 1960)

    State-space framework for control systems stated as a general theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • IFAC Proceedings Volumes record for Kalman's paper, year 1960 Crossref
    • MacTutor Kalman biography lists the 1960 state-space work as foundational MacTutor, Rudolf Kalman
  4. 1961

    Kalman-Bucy filter

    key development

    Kalman, R. E. and Bucy, R. S., New results in linear filtering and prediction theory, J. Basic Eng. 83 (1961)

    Derives the Riccati-type variance equation that defines the optimal filter, giving the Kalman-Bucy filter.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal of Basic Engineering record for Kalman-Bucy, year 1961 Crossref
    • MacTutor Kalman biography quotes the Kalman-Bucy filter as the outcome of this paper MacTutor, Rudolf Kalman
  5. 1962

    Pontryagin et al., optimal processes

    key development

    L. S. Pontryagin et al., The Mathematical Theory of Optimal Processes, Interscience 1962

    Maximum principle for optimal control.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record of the English translation (Pergamon edition listed as 1964). zbMATH record 0117.31702
  6. 1963

    Kalman: linear dynamical systems

    key development

    R. E. Kalman, Mathematical description of linear dynamical systems, SIAM J. Control 1 (1963)

    Controllability, observability and canonical forms for linear systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Kalman, Mathematical Description of Linear Dynamical Systems, SIAM J. Control, 1963. Crossref, DOI 10.1137/0301010
  7. 1969

    Kalman, Falb and Arbib's Topics in Mathematical System Theory

    consolidation

    Kalman, R. E., Falb, P. L., Arbib, M. A., Topics in Mathematical System Theory, McGraw-Hill (1969)

    A textbook-style systematic treatment of mathematical system theory from the state-space viewpoint.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record for Topics in mathematical system theory, first published 1969 Open Library
