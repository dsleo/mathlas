1967 · Geometry

Fractal geometry: self-similarity, dimension and computer experiment (Mandelbrot)

Mandelbrot combined self-similarity and fractional dimension to model coastlines and noise, defined fractals via Hausdorff dimension, and used computers to map Julia-set connectivity.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1918

    Hausdorff dimension

    precursor

    Felix Hausdorff, Dimension und aeusseres Mass, Mathematische Annalen, 1918

    Hausdorff's fractional dimension supplies the dimension concept that fractal geometry later uses.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper 'Dimension und äußeres Maß', Mathematische Annalen, 1918 Crossref
  2. 1967

    Coastline length and statistical self-similarity

    first statement

    Benoit Mandelbrot, How long is the coast of Britain? Statistical self-similarity and fractional dimension, Science, 1967

    Mandelbrot argues that the length of a coastline depends on scale and gives it a fractional dimension.

    Evidence (1)
    • Science paper with this title, 1967 Crossref
  3. 1975

    'Fractal' named in Les objets fractals

    naming

    Benoit Mandelbrot, Les objets fractals: forme, hasard et dimension, Flammarion, 1975

    The 1975 book introduces the word 'fractal' and collects the self-similar and dimensional ideas.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library: first publication year 1975 for 'Les objets fractals' Open Library
    • MacTutor: the work that gave the theory its name is Les objets fractals (1975) MacTutor
  4. 1980

    Computer mapping of Julia-set connectedness

    first application

    Benoit Mandelbrot, Fractal aspects of the iteration of z -> lambda z(1-z) for complex lambda and z, Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences, 1980

    Mandelbrot applies computer experiments to the iteration family and maps the connectedness locus, the first purposeful use of the fractal idea on a dynamical problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annals of the NY Academy of Sciences paper, 1980 Crossref
    • MacTutor: Mandelbrot's discovery of the set in 1979-80 with computer graphics MacTutor
  5. 1982

    The fractal geometry of nature

    consolidation

    Benoit Mandelbrot, The Fractal Geometry of Nature, Birkhauser / W. H. Freeman, 1982

    A systematic treatment bringing self-similarity, dimension and computer images into a single account.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: 'The fractal geometry of nature', first publication 1982 Open Library
