1892 · Dynamics

Lyapunov stability

Lyapunov gave general criteria for stability of equilibria using auxiliary functions.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1846

    Dirichlet's criterion for stability of equilibrium

    precursor

    Dirichlet, Ueber die Stabilitaet des Gleichgewichts, J. reine angew. Math. 32 (1846) 85-88

    Dirichlet's energy criterion for the stability of an equilibrium of a conservative system, the model that Lyapunov's auxiliary functions generalise.

    Dating or attribution note

    The content was not read; the link to Lyapunov rests on MacTutor's account that Lyapunov's fluid-ellipsoid work used the sign of the potential-energy variations and on the bibliography item on the extremal-energy criterion 'from Lagrange to Lyapunov'.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Ueber die Stabilitaet des Gleichgewichts, Crelle 32 (1846) 85-88. Crossref 10.1515/crll.1846.32.85
    • Lyapunov showed stability when the second and higher variations of the potential energy are positive; the bibliography lists a history of the extremal-energy criterion from Lagrange to Lyapunov. MacTutor, Lyapunov biography
  2. 1885

    Lyapunov's master's work on rotating liquid figures

    precursor

    Lyapunov, On the stability of ellipsoidal figures of equilibrium of a rotating liquid (master's thesis, 1884-85)

    His first stability study, on ellipsoidal equilibrium figures, set the problem that led to the general theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor: he defended the thesis on ellipsoidal forms of equilibrium of a rotating liquid in 1885. MacTutor Biographies/Lyapunov
    • zbMATH lists the French translation of this study, 1904. zbMATH Open
  3. 1892

    Lyapunov: The general problem of the stability of motion

    first statement

    Lyapunov, doctoral thesis, Kharkov, 1892

    Lyapunov gave a general nonlinear approach to stability of motion with two methods, the second using auxiliary functions.

    Evidence (4)
    • Wikipedia: he defended the thesis at Kharkov in 1892, a global approach compared with linearization. Wikipedia: Lyapunov stability
    • MacTutor: doctorate awarded on defending the thesis on 12 October 1892. MacTutor Biographies/Lyapunov
    • English translation of the thesis: The general problem of the stability of motion, Int. J. Control 55 (1992); the centenary of the Kharkov thesis. Crossref 10.1080/00207179208934253
    • Read: translator's footnote 'imprime en 1892 par la Societe mathematique de Kharkow'; preface dated Kharkow, 5 avril 1892; first and second methods. Liapounoff, Probleme general de la stabilite du mouvement, Ann. Fac. Sci. Toulouse (2) 9 (1907) 203-474 (Numdam AFST_1907_2_9__203_0)
  4. 1907

    French translation of the stability memoir

    key development

    Liapounoff, Probleme general de la stabilite du mouvement, Ann. Fac. Sci. Toulouse (2) 9, 1907

    The translation made the thesis available to a Western readership, although it drew little attention for years.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref: Annales de la Faculte des sciences de Toulouse 9, pp. 203-474, 1907. Crossref
    • zbMATH record of the French translation, 1907. zbMATH Open
    • Wikipedia: the work was translated to French and received little attention for years. Wikipedia: Lyapunov stability
  5. 1930

    Perron: the stability question for differential equations

    key development

    O. Perron, Die Stabilitätsfrage bei Differentialgleichungen, Mathematische Zeitschrift 32 (1930) 703-728

    Perron shows that the expectation that a system with negative real parts of the characteristic roots of its linear part stays stable under small nonlinear terms fails once the coefficients of the linear part vary with time (the 'Perron effect'), and gives a corrected theorem.

    Dating or attribution note

    Read the first two pages in the GDZ scan (Math. Z. 32, pp. 703-704): the introduction states that earlier investigations (his own Math. Z. 29, 1928, 129-160) show that for dx/dt = linear + small terms the integrals tending to zero are as many as without the added terms, that the methods use characteristic roots and so do not carry over to systems with variable coefficients f(t), that 'after long attempts to prove' the natural conjecture he found it 'not correct at all', shows this by an example in section 2, and then gives a correct theorem under an added boundedness condition. The paper does not name Lyapunov on these pages and the rest (sections 2-) was not read; the label 'Perron effect' and the link to Lyapunov's first method are the standard reading, not checked here.

    Evidence (2)
    • Read: introduction (pp. 703-704) on the failed conjecture for systems with variable coefficients. Perron, Die Stabilitätsfrage bei Differentialgleichungen, Math. Z. 32 (1930) 703-728 (GDZ PPN266833020_0032, LOG_0049)
    • Crossref record: Math. Z. 32 (1930) 703-728. Crossref 10.1007/BF01194662
  6. 1936

    Chetaev recognises the scale of Lyapunov's work

    recognition

    Chetaev, On stable trajectories of dynamics, Kazan Univ. Sci. Notes 4(1), 1936; The Stability of Motion (Russian 1946)

    Chetaev was the first to grasp the importance of Lyapunov's theory and developed it in the 1930s.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia: Chetaev, at the Kazan Aviation Institute in the 1930s, first realised the magnitude of Lyapunov's discovery. Wikipedia: Lyapunov stability
    • Cited reference: Chetaev, On stable trajectories of dynamics, Kazan Univ. Sci. Notes, vol. 4 no. 1, 1936. Wikipedia, Lyapunov stability (references)
  7. 1946

    Chetaev, The Stability of Motion (Russian monograph)

    consolidation

    N. G. Chetaev, Ustoichivost dvizheniya, OGIZ, Moscow-Leningrad, 1946; English translation Oxford 1961

    A Russian monograph presenting and extending Lyapunov's theory, earlier than the Western monograph of 1961.

    Evidence (2)
    • The Stability of Motion, originally published in Russian in 1946 by OGIZ, Moscow-Leningrad; translated by M. Nadler, Oxford, 1961. Wikipedia, Lyapunov stability (references)
    • zbMATH lists a 1955 second edition of Chetaev's Stability of Motion. zbMATH (see 1961 milestone note)
  8. 1960

    Kalman and Bertram import the second method into control theory

    key development

    Kalman and Bertram, Control system analysis and design via the second method of Lyapunov, J. Basic Eng. 82, 1960

    The second method became a standard tool for nonlinear control and guidance systems.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: J. Basic Engineering 82, 1960 (part I). Crossref
    • Wikipedia: interest rose sharply in the Cold War as the second method fit aerospace guidance systems. Wikipedia: Lyapunov stability
  9. 1961

    LaSalle and Lefschetz: Stability by Liapunov's Direct Method

    consolidation

    LaSalle and Lefschetz, Stability by Liapunov's direct method with applications, Academic Press, 1961

    A monograph presenting the direct method for a Western audience.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record, 1961. zbMATH Open
