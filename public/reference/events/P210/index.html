1945 · Algebraic topology

Axiomatic homology

Homology theories were characterised by axioms such as excision, allowing comparison and later generalised theories.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1944

    Eilenberg's singular homology theory

    precursor

    Samuel Eilenberg, Singular homology theory, Ann. of Math. 45, 1944

    Develops singular homology, the model example the axioms were written to characterise.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Annals of Mathematics 45 (1944), p. 407. Crossref 10.2307/1969185
    • Singular homology is the quintessential example satisfying the axioms. Wikipedia, Eilenberg-Steenrod axioms
  2. 1945

    Eilenberg-Steenrod axiomatic approach

    first statement

    Eilenberg, S., Steenrod, N. E., Axiomatic approach to homology theory, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. 31 (1945) 117-120, doi 10.1073/pnas.31.4.117

    States the homology axioms (homotopy, excision, dimension, exactness) so that results hold for any theory satisfying them.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'Axiomatic Approach to Homology Theory', PNAS, 1945 Crossref API
    • Wikipedia: the axiomatic approach was developed in 1945 and allows proofs such as Mayer-Vietoris Wikipedia: Eilenberg-Steenrod axioms
  3. 1952

    Eilenberg and Steenrod's Foundations of Algebraic Topology

    consolidation

    Eilenberg, S., Steenrod, N. E., Foundations of Algebraic Topology, Princeton University Press (1952), zbMATH 0047.41402

    The book that systematises the axiomatic homology theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Foundations of algebraic topology', Princeton 1952 zbMATH API (Zbl 0047.41402)
    • MacTutor: Steenrod's book with Eilenberg, published in 1952 MacTutor Biography: Norman Steenrod
  4. 1962

    Milnor's axiomatic homology theory

    key development

    Milnor, J. W., On axiomatic homology theory, Pacific J. Math. 12 (1962) 337-341, zbMATH 0114.39604

    Develops the axiomatic framework for homology theories further.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'On axiomatic homology theory', Pacific J. Math. 12 (1962) zbMATH API (Zbl 0114.39604)
  5. 1962

    G. W. Whitehead's generalized homology theories

    key development

    George W. Whitehead, Generalized homology theories, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 102, 1962

    Drops the dimension axiom to define extraordinary theories, the route to K-theory and cobordism.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record, Trans. AMS 102, 227-283 (1962). zbMATH 3202148
    • Omitting the dimension axiom gives extraordinary theories, important in the 1950s and after. Wikipedia, Eilenberg-Steenrod axioms
