1913 · Differential geometry

Abstract Riemann surface (Weyl's manifold definition)

Weyl gave a rigorous definition of Riemann surface as a topological manifold with charts.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1851

    Riemann's dissertation on complex functions via surfaces

    precursor

    Riemann, Grundlagen für eine allgemeine Theorie der Functionen einer veränderlichen complexen Grösse (1851)

    Riemann defines functions through surfaces spread over the plane, with geometric rather than formal definitions; this is the origin of the concept Weyl made abstract.

    Evidence (2)
    • Riemann's Grundlagen, collected-works printing 1867, confirms title Open Library search
    • Weyl's book united geometric function theory developed by Riemann MacTutor: Weyl biography
  2. 1882

    Klein on Riemann's theory

    precursor

    F. Klein, Über Riemanns Theorie der algebraischen Functionen und ihrer Integrale, Leipzig 1882

    Klein presents Riemann surfaces as geometric objects tied to algebraic functions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record of the paper in Klein's Gesammelte Mathematische Abhandlungen (1923 reprint). Crossref, DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-51959-8_19
    • MacTutor biography of Klein. MacTutor, biography of Klein
  3. 1910

    Koebe on uniformisation of analytic curves

    precursor

    P. Koebe, Über die Uniformisierung beliebiger analytischer Kurven, Erster Teil, J. reine angew. Math. 138 (1910)

    Koebe proves the uniformisation theorem for general Riemann surfaces, a main result the abstract notion made possible to state.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Koebe, Über die Uniformisierung beliebiger analytischer Kurven, Erster Teil, Crelle 138, 1910. Crossref, DOI 10.1515/crll.1910.138.192
  4. 1913

    Weyl's book defines the Riemann surface as an abstract manifold

    first statement

    Weyl, Die Idee der Riemannschen Fläche (Teubner, Leipzig, 1913)

    Weyl gives a rigorous definition of a Riemann surface as a topological manifold with an analytic structure given by charts, independent of embedding in the plane.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1913 book record Open Library search
    • Book united analysis, geometry and topology, making Riemann's idea rigorous MacTutor: Weyl biography
  5. 1913

    Same 1913 book as first systematic treatment

    consolidation

    Weyl, Die Idee der Riemannschen Fläche (1913)

    The same book is also the first systematic treatment of abstract Riemann surfaces, so this milestone shares its work with the first_statement.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book as the first systematic treatment; Sario's 1956 assessment cited in the bio MacTutor: Weyl biography
  6. 1925

    Radó's theorem on triangulability

    key development

    T. Radó, Über den Begriff der Riemannschen Fläche, Acta Szeged 2 (1925)

    Radó shows that Riemann surfaces with countable topology are triangulable, justifying the abstract definition.

    Evidence (1)
    • Jahrbuch record: Radó, Über den Begriff der Riemannschen Fläche, Acta Szeged 2, 1925. zbMATH record 51.0273.01
  7. 1960

    Ahlfors and Sario's Riemann Surfaces

    consolidation

    L. Ahlfors, L. Sario, Riemann Surfaces, Princeton 1960

    Standard modern treatise built on the abstract definition.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Ahlfors, Sario, Riemann surfaces, Princeton Math. Series 26, 1960. zbMATH record 0196.33801
