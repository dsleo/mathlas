1956 · Topology

Exotic spheres: algebraic topology reaches differential structures

Characteristic classes and homotopy groups are used to show manifolds homeomorphic to the 7-sphere can be non-diffeomorphic, then to classify homotopy spheres.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1956

    Milnor's exotic 7-spheres

    first statement

    Milnor, J., On manifolds homeomorphic to the 7-sphere, Annals of Mathematics 64 (1956)

    Milnor shows that some smooth manifolds homeomorphic to the 7-sphere are not diffeomorphic to it, using characteristic classes.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annals of Mathematics record for Milnor's 7-sphere paper, year 1956 Crossref
    • MacTutor notes the 1956 paper's Steele Prize citation for Annals 64 (1956) 399-405 MacTutor, John Milnor
  2. 1956

    Characteristic classes applied to smooth structures on spheres

    first application

    Milnor, J., On manifolds homeomorphic to the 7-sphere, Annals of Mathematics 64 (1956)

    The first use of algebraic topology (characteristic classes, homotopy) on the question of smooth structures on spheres.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annals of Mathematics record, year 1956 Crossref
    • Wikipedia Exotic sphere article describes Milnor's 7-sphere example from the mid-1950s Wikipedia, Exotic sphere
  3. 1959

    Milnor's Differentiable structures on spheres

    key development

    Milnor, J., Differentiable structures on spheres, American Journal of Mathematics 81 (1959)

    Extends the classification of differentiable structures to a wider range of spheres.

    Evidence (1)
    • American Journal of Mathematics record for Milnor's paper, year 1959 Crossref
  4. 1963

    Kervaire-Milnor group of homotopy spheres

    key development

    Kervaire, M. and Milnor, J., Groups of homotopy spheres I, Annals of Mathematics 77 (1963)

    Defines the group of homotopy spheres and reduces its computation to homotopy theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals of Mathematics record for Kervaire-Milnor, year 1963 Crossref
  5. 1968

    Hirzebruch's Bourbaki survey 'Singularities and exotic spheres'

    consolidation

    F. Hirzebruch, Singularities and exotic spheres, Seminaire Bourbaki 1966/67, No. 314, 1968

    A Bourbaki expository talk surveying exotic spheres and their link to singularities, one year after the Kervaire-Milnor classification.

    Dating or attribution note

    Survey role from the Bourbaki expository type; the talk's content was not read. Milnor's 2015 'Introduction to exotic spheres' (Zbl 1320.57003) is later and is not used. Milnor's 1965 h-cobordism lectures (Zbl 0161.20302) are not a survey of exotic spheres.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'Singularities and exotic spheres', Hirzebruch, Semin. Bourbaki 1966/67, No. 314 (1968), Zbl 0213.47701 zbMATH Open API
  6. 1970

    Cerf's pseudoisotopy theorem

    key development

    Cerf, J., La stratification naturelle des espaces de fonctions différentiables réelles et le théorème de la pseudo-isotopie, Publ. Math. IHES 39 (1970)

    Proves the pseudoisotopy theorem, which in dimensions at least 6 makes the group of exotic spheres equal to the group of components of a diffeomorphism group.

    Evidence (2)
    • Publications Mathématiques IHES record for Cerf's paper, year 1970 Crossref
    • Wikipedia Exotic sphere article states the 1970 pseudoisotopy result Wikipedia, Exotic sphere
