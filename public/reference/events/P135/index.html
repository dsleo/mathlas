1788 · Mathematical physics

Lagrange's analytical mechanics

Mechanics was reduced to analysis based on virtual velocities, with no diagrams.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1736

    Euler's Mechanica applies analysis to mechanics

    precursor

    Euler's 1736 treatise marked the systematic use of analysis in mechanics before Lagrange removed the diagrams.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor says Euler's Mechanica of 1736 was a major advance with systematic successful use of analysis MacTutor, Euler
  2. 1782

    Lagrange tells Laplace the treatise is almost done

    precursor

    A private letter reports a treatise on analytical mechanics resting only on the principle of virtual velocities.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor quotes Lagrange's letter to Laplace of 15 September 1782 MacTutor, Lagrange
  3. 1788

    Mécanique analytique published in Paris

    first statement

    Lagrange, Mécanique analytique, Paris (Veuve Desaint), 1788

    The treatise derives all of mechanics from virtual velocities with analytic methods and, in its preface, no figures.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor says the Mécanique analytique was published in 1788 and quotes the preface that no figures appear MacTutor, Lagrange
    • Wikipedia gives first publication 1788 English Wikipedia, Joseph-Louis Lagrange
  4. 1788

    Mécanique analytique as systematic treatise

    consolidation

    Lagrange, Mécanique analytique, 1788

    The same work is also the first comprehensive treatment of mechanics as a branch of analysis.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor says the work summarised the mechanics done since Newton and turned mechanics into a branch of analysis MacTutor, Lagrange
  5. 1811–1815

    Revised second edition of the Mecanique analytique

    key development

    J.-L. Lagrange, Mecanique analytique, nouvelle edition, 2 vols., Paris, Courcier, 1811-1815

    Lagrange revises and enlarges the treatise; the second volume, nearly finished when he died in 1813, appeared in 1815.

    Evidence (2)
    • Mecanique analytique, vol. 1, Paris, Ve Courcier, 1811 (scan) archive.org
    • Second edition 1811-1815; volume 2 published posthumously in 1815 Wikipedia, Mecanique analytique
  6. 1834

    Hamilton's general method in dynamics

    key development

    W. R. Hamilton, On a general method in dynamics, Phil. Trans. 1834

    Hamilton recast dynamics with a single characteristic function, the next major step beyond the Lagrangian formulation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Hamilton, On a general method in dynamics, Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society 1834, pp. 247-308 Crossref 10.1098/rstl.1834.0017
