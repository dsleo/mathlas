1887 · Algebra

Differential Galois theory

Picard and Vessiot carried the Galois correspondence to linear differential equations, with Lie groups replacing finite groups.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1887

    Picard on algebraic groups of transformations

    first statement

    Picard, Sur les equations differentielles lineaires et les groupes algebriques de transformations, Ann. Toulouse 1, A1-A15, 1887

    Picard associated algebraic transformation groups to linear differential equations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record: 1887. Crossref
  2. 1892

    Vessiot's thesis

    key development

    Vessiot, Sur l'integration des equations differentielles lineaires, Ann. ENS 9, 197-280, 1892

    Vessiot developed the Picard-Vessiot theory using Lie groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: 1892. Crossref
    • Says the thesis is what is now called the Picard-Vessiot theory. MacTutor biography of Vessiot
  3. 1904

    Vessiot on Galois generalisations

    key development

    Vessiot, Sur la theorie de Galois et ses diverses generalisations, Ann. ENS 21, 9-85, 1904

    Vessiot extended Galois theory beyond linear equations.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record: 1904. Crossref
  4. 1948

    Kolchin's algebraic matric groups

    key development

    Kolchin, Annals of Math. 49, 1948

    Kolchin recast the theory using algebraic groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: 1948. Crossref
    • Says he became a pioneer of linear algebraic groups while applying Ritt's theory to Picard-Vessiot. MacTutor biography of Kolchin
  5. 1953

    Galois theory of differential fields

    key development

    Kolchin, Am. J. Math. 75, 753, 1953

    Extension to strongly normal extensions.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record: 1953. Crossref
  6. 1973

    Kolchin's book

    consolidation

    Kolchin, Differential Algebra and Algebraic Groups, Academic Press, 1973

    Systematic monograph.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lists the book in its sources. Wikipedia: Differential Galois theory
    • Mentions 1973 in connection with the book. MacTutor biography of Kolchin
