1595 · Algebra

Viete: trigonometry applied to algebra (van Roomen's problem)

A degree-45 equation posed by van Roomen was solved by recognising a trigonometric identity.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1591

    Viete's In artem analyticam isagoge

    precursor

    François Viète, In artem analyticam isagoge, Tours 1591

    Viete introduces letters for unknowns and parameters, the symbolic algebra that his answer to van Roomen relies on.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor places the Isagoge among Viete's main algebra publications of the early 1590s. MacTutor, biography of Viete
  2. 1593

    Van Roomen poses a degree-45 equation

    precursor

    Adriaan van Roomen, Ideae mathematicae pars prima, 1593

    Public challenge to mathematicians.

    Dating or attribution note

    Whether the problem is printed in this very volume was not checked.

    Evidence (2)
    • Roomen proposed the problem in 1593. MacTutor, Viete biography
    • 1593 book scan. Internet Archive bub_gb_qinevzxnHFoC
  3. 1595

    Viete publishes his answer

    first statement

    Viete, Responsum ad problema (answer to van Roomen), 1595

    Recognises the equation as a trigonometric identity and solves it.

    Evidence (1)
    • Viete solved it by seeing an underlying trigonometric relation; answer published 1595. MacTutor, Viete biography
  4. 1595

    Trigonometry used on an algebraic equation

    first application

    Viete's answer to van Roomen, 1595

    First purposeful use of angle-multiplication identities to solve an algebraic equation.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same work as previous; 'first' not verified.

    Evidence (2)
    • As above. MacTutor, Viete biography
    • MacTutor notes Viete knew formulas for sin nx. MacTutor, Trigonometric functions
  5. 1615

    Ad angulares sectiones published

    key development

    François Viète, Ad angulares sectiones theoremata, ed. Alexander Anderson, Paris 1615

    The posthumous treatise on angle sections sets out the multiple-angle relations behind the degree-45 solution.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor lists Viete's posthumous work on angular sections edited by Anderson. MacTutor, biography of Viete
  6. 1646

    Collected Opera mathematica of Viete

    consolidation

    Francois Viete, Francisci Vietae Opera mathematica, 1646

    Collected edition of Viete's mathematical works, which brings together the trigonometric-algebra material of the event.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record of the 1646 Opera mathematica of Viete. Open Library work record
