1847 · Combinatorics

Kirkman's schoolgirl problem and combinatorial designs

Kirkman's triple-arrangement puzzle gave an early case of combinatorial designs.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1844

    Woolhouse's questions in the Lady's and Gentleman's Diary

    precursor

    Woolhouse, question on combinations with no repeated sub-combination, The Lady's and Gentleman's Diary, 1844 (and a triad version in 1846)

    Woolhouse's questions asked how many triads can be formed from n symbols with no pair repeated, which is the triple-system problem Kirkman then solved.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia's timeline gives Woolhouse's 1844 general question and 1846 triad question English Wikipedia, Kirkman's schoolgirl problem
    • MacTutor says Kirkman's first paper answered a problem from the Diary of 1845 MacTutor biography of Kirkman
  2. 1847

    Kirkman's paper on triple systems

    first statement

    Kirkman, On a problem in combinations, Cambridge and Dublin Mathematical Journal 2, 191-204, 1847

    Kirkman fully treated the construction of systems of triples in which each pair occurs once, for orders congruent to 1 or 3 mod 6, six years before Steiner posed the question.

    Evidence (3)
    • Wikipedia dates the paper to 1847 (vol. 2, pp. 191-204) and describes it as comprehensive English Wikipedia, Kirkman's schoolgirl problem
    • Internet Archive holds the Cambridge and Dublin Mathematical Journal vol. 2 dated 1847 archive.org sim_cambridge-and-dublin-mathematical-journal ... 1847_2
    • MacTutor says Kirkman solved the Steiner-triple problem six years before Steiner (the paper was presented in 1846) MacTutor biography of Kirkman
  3. 1850

    The fifteen schoolgirls problem and its first solutions

    key development

    Kirkman, Query VI, The Lady's and Gentleman's Diary, 1850; Kirkman, On the triads made with fifteen things, Philosophical Magazine 37, 169-171, 1850

    Kirkman's puzzle asked for a day-by-day arrangement of fifteen girls in rows of three, adding the requirement that the triples split into parallel classes; the puzzle became far better known than the earlier paper.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia dates the problem to 1850 and the Phil. Mag. solution to 1850 English Wikipedia, Kirkman's schoolgirl problem
    • Crossref record of a Kirkman note of 1850 titled 'On the triads made with fifteen things', Phil. Mag. 37, 169-171 Crossref 10.1080/14786445008646578
  4. 1853

    Steiner restates triple systems

    key development

    Steiner, Combinatorische Aufgaben, Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 45, 181-182, 1853

    Steiner independently posed the triple-system problem, unaware of Kirkman's paper; his version, not Kirkman's, later gave the systems their name.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record for Steiner's note, Crelle 45, 181-182 Crossref 10.1515/crll.1853.45.181
    • Wikipedia states Steiner published without knowing Kirkman's 1847 paper English Wikipedia, Kirkman's schoolgirl problem
  5. 1936

    Yates introduces incomplete blocks in experimental design

    first application

    Yates, Incomplete randomized blocks, Annals of Eugenics 7, 121-140, 1936

    Yates used designs in which every pair of treatments meets equally often to reduce block size in field experiments, the first statistical use of the combinatorial structure.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record of Yates 1936, Ann. Eugenics 7, 121-140 Crossref 10.1111/j.1469-1809.1936.tb02134.x
  6. 1937

    Witt names the systems after Steiner

    naming

    Witt, Über Steinersche Systeme, Abhandlungen aus dem Mathematischen Seminar der Universität Hamburg 12, 265-275 (Crossref year 1937)

    The earliest title I found using the name 'Steiner systems'; the volume is dated 1938 in some citations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record of Witt's paper (issued 1937, Abh. Math. Sem. Hamburg 12, 265-275) Crossref 10.1007/bf02948948
  7. 1939

    Bose's systematic constructions of balanced incomplete block designs

    consolidation

    Bose, On the construction of balanced incomplete block designs, Annals of Eugenics 9, 353-399, 1939

    A long paper constructing balanced incomplete block designs with finite-geometry and difference methods, linking the statistics to the Kirkman-Steiner tradition.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record of Bose 1939, Ann. Eugenics 9, 353-399 Crossref 10.1111/j.1469-1809.1939.tb02219.x
  8. 1971

    Complete solution of Kirkman's problem

    key development

    Ray-Chaudhuri and Wilson, Solution of Kirkman's schoolgirl problem, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 19, 187-203, 1971

    Existence of a Kirkman triple system for every order congruent to 3 mod 6 was proved in print, completing a problem open since 1850.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record of the 1971 paper Crossref 10.1090/pspum/019/9959
    • Wikipedia says Lu Jiaxi proved the general case first in 1965, but his submission was rejected in error English Wikipedia, Kirkman's schoolgirl problem
