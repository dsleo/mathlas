1907 · Logic & foundations

Intuitionism

Brouwer rejected the unrestricted law of excluded middle.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1907

    Brouwer's dissertation on foundations

    precursor

    L. E. J. Brouwer, Over de grondslagen der wiskunde, dissertation, Amsterdam 1907

    Brouwer's thesis lays out his critique of formalist and logicist foundations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Jahrbuch record: Brouwer, Over de Grondslagen der Wiskunde, dissertation Amsterdam, 1907. zbMATH record 38.0081.04
  2. 1907

    Brouwer's dissertation on the foundations of mathematics

    first statement

    L. E. J. Brouwer, Over de grondslagen der wiskunde, Amsterdam: Maas & van Suchtelen, 1907 (doctoral thesis)

    Brouwer's thesis sets out his critique of logicism and formalism and his view of mathematics as mental construction, which MacTutor marks as the start of the intuitionist school.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists the 1907 first publication by Maas & van Suchtelen. Open Library work OL1280913W
    • The 1907 thesis attacked the logical foundations of mathematics and marks the beginning of the Intuitionist School. MacTutor, Brouwer
  3. 1912

    Brouwer's Intuitionisme en formalisme

    naming

    L. E. J. Brouwer, Intuitionisme en formalisme, Amsterdam: Clausen, 1912

    Inaugural lecture whose title names intuitionism as a position in foundations.

    Dating or attribution note

    First use of the word in a title; whether Brouwer coined it earlier in text was not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Jahrbuch record: Brouwer, Intuitionisme en Formalisme, Amsterdam: J. Clausen, 1912. zbMATH record 43.0111.01
  4. 1913

    Brouwer's 'Intuitionism and formalism'

    naming

    L. E. J. Brouwer, Intuitionism and formalism, 1913

    The earliest zbMATH-indexed title in which intuitionism is the subject, named as a school.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Brouwer, 'Intuitionism and formalism', year 1913. zbMATH Open, API ti:"intuitionism" search
  5. 1918

    Brouwer's set theory without excluded middle

    key development

    Brouwer, L. E. J., Begrundung der Mengenlehre unabhangig vom logischen Satz vom ausgeschlossenen Dritten (1918)

    Brouwer's 1918 paper builds set theory without the law of excluded middle.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper with this title and year 1918 zbMATH Open
  6. 1930

    Heyting's formal intuitionistic logic

    key development

    Heyting, A., Die formalen Regeln der intuitionistischen Logik (1930)

    Heyting's 1930 papers give the first formal calculus of intuitionistic logic.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper with this title and year 1930 zbMATH Open
  7. 1933

    Gödel on intuitionistic arithmetic

    key development

    K. Gödel, Zur intuitionistischen Arithmetik und Zahlentheorie, Ergebnisse eines math. Kolloquiums 4 (1933)

    Gödel translates classical arithmetic into intuitionistic arithmetic, relating the two systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Gödel, Zur intuitionistischen Arithmetik und Zahlentheorie, 1933. zbMATH record 0007.19304
  8. 1934

    Heyting's Intuitionismus survey

    consolidation

    A. Heyting, Mathematische Grundlagenforschung. Intuitionismus. Beweistheorie, Springer, 1934

    A survey volume on intuitionism and proof theory, the first systematic treatment among the zbMATH records checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Heyting, 'Mathematische Grundlagenforschung. Intuitionismus. Beweistheorie', year 1934. zbMATH Open, API ti:Intuitionismus search
