1884 · Logic & foundations

Logicism: reducing arithmetic to logic

Frege tried to base arithmetic on logic alone; Russell's paradox shook this and Russell and Whitehead's Principia used a theory of types to avoid it.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1879

    Frege's Begriffsschrift

    precursor

    Frege, Begriffsschrift, Halle, 1879

    Frege's formal language for pure thought, the logical system on which his logicism rests.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: Begriffsschrift, 1879. Internet Archive
    • Dates the Begriffsschrift to 1879. MacTutor biography of Frege
  2. 1884

    Frege's Foundations of Arithmetic

    first statement

    Frege, Die Grundlagen der Arithmetik, Breslau, 1884

    Frege argued that arithmetic is reducible to logic and analysed the concept of number accordingly.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: 1884. Internet Archive
    • Cites Frege 1884, 1893, 1903 as defining mathematical notions through logical ones. Wikipedia: Logicism
  3. 1893

    Frege's Grundgesetze volume I

    first application

    Frege, Grundgesetze der Arithmetik I, Jena, 1893

    Frege carried out the reduction in his formal system, deriving arithmetic from logical laws.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: 1893. Internet Archive
    • Cites the 1893 work. Wikipedia: Logicism
  4. 1902

    Russell's letter to Frege about the paradox

    precursor

    Russell to Frege, 16 June 1902 (private letter)

    Russell pointed out that Frege's axioms yield a contradiction.

    Evidence (1)
    • States Frege received Russell's letter on 16 June 1902 pointing out the contradiction in his axioms. MacTutor biography of Frege
  5. 1903

    Russell's Principles of Mathematics

    key development

    Russell, The Principles of Mathematics, 1903

    Russell published the logicist thesis and the paradox bearing his name.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: 1903. Internet Archive
    • Says the paradox arose while working on the Principles (1903). MacTutor biography of Russell
  6. 1908

    Theory of types

    key development

    Russell, Mathematical Logic as Based on the Theory of Types, Am. J. Math. 30, 222-262, 1908

    Russell presented the ramified theory of types as a way to avoid the paradoxes.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Am. J. Math. 30 (1908) 222. Crossref
    • Says the mature theory of types appears in the 1908 article and Principia. MacTutor biography of Russell
  7. 1910–1913

    Principia Mathematica

    consolidation

    Whitehead and Russell, Principia Mathematica, 3 vols, 1910-1913

    A monumental systematic derivation of mathematics from logic using types.

    Evidence (3)
    • Archive.org record: vol. 1, 1910. Internet Archive
    • Archive.org record: vol. 2, 1912. Internet Archive
    • Lists Principia as 1910, 1912, 1913. MacTutor biography of Russell
