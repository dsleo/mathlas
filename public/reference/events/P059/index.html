1917 · Number theory

Hardy-Littlewood circle method

Contour integration of generating functions and exponential sums are used to count representations of integers (partitions, sums of primes), the method behind later Fourier-analytic density arguments.

Also known as: Hardy-Ramanujan-Littlewood circle method

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1917

    Hardy and Ramanujan announce the partition asymptotic formula

    first statement

    G. H. Hardy and S. Ramanujan, Une formule asymptotique pour le nombre des partitions de n, Comptes Rendus Acad. Sci. Paris 164 (1917), 35-38

    Hardy and Ramanujan announce their asymptotic formula for partitions, the first public step of the complex-analytic method later named the circle method.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH lists the note in Comptes Rendus 164 (1917), pp. 35-38 and links Gallica. zbMATH Open document 2610181
    • Wikipedia attributes the initial idea of the circle method to Hardy with Ramanujan in 1916-1917 on the partition function. Wikipedia, Hardy-Ramanujan-Littlewood circle method
  2. 1918

    Asymptotics of the partition function by the circle method

    first application

    Hardy & Ramanujan, 'Asymptotic formulae in combinatory analysis', Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 17 (1918)

    Hardy and Ramanujan use contour integration over arcs of the unit circle to find the asymptotic of the partition function, the first purposeful use of the method.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1918 Proc. LMS record Crossref record
    • Initial idea attributed to Hardy and Ramanujan's work Wikipedia: Hardy-Littlewood circle method
  3. 1920

    Hardy and Littlewood's Waring-problem papers

    precursor

    Hardy & Littlewood, 'Some problems of Partitio numerorum I' (Göttinger Nachrichten, 1920)

    Hardy and Littlewood adapt the method to Waring's problem and name the circle-method approach in the series of papers.

    Evidence (1)
    • Developed in a series of papers on Waring's problem Wikipedia: Hardy-Littlewood circle method
  4. 1923

    Goldbach-type representations by primes

    key development

    Hardy & Littlewood, 'Some problems of Partitio numerorum III', Acta Mathematica 44 (1923)

    The method is applied to the representation of numbers as sums of primes, the key development that made the method central.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1923 Acta Math. record Crossref record
  5. 1981

    Vaughan's monograph on the Hardy-Littlewood method

    consolidation

    Vaughan, The Hardy-Littlewood Method (Cambridge Tracts in Mathematics 80, 1981)

    Vaughan gives the first book-length systematic treatment of the method, later reissued in a second edition (1997).

    Evidence (2)
    • 1981 book record, The Hardy-Littlewood method Open Library search
    • Second edition 1997 in the Cambridge Tracts series Wikipedia: Hardy-Littlewood circle method
