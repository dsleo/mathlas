Yang-Baxter and solvable lattice models

Yang-Baxter equation.

Also known as: Yang-Baxter equation

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1964

    McGuire's exactly soluble delta-interaction N-body problem

    precursor

    McGuire, J. B., Study of exactly soluble one-dimensional N-body problems, J. Math. Phys. 5 (1964) 622-636

    Solves a one-dimensional N-body problem by factorised scattering, the setting in which the Yang-Baxter relation first appears.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in J. Math. Phys. (1964), DOI 10.1063/1.1704156, pages 622-636 Crossref; zbMATH 0131.43804
    • Wikipedia cites Jimbo that the equation appears in McGuire's 1964 work and Yang's 1967 work Wikipedia, Yang-Baxter equation (History)
  2. 1967

    Yang's exact results for repulsive delta-function bosons

    first statement

    Yang, C. N., Some exact results for the many-body problem in one dimension with repulsive delta-function interaction, Phys. Rev. Lett. 19 (1967)

    States the factorisation of the many-body scattering matrix, the consistency condition later named after Yang and Baxter.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in Phys. Rev. Lett. 19 (1967), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.19.1312, published 4 December 1967 Crossref
    • Wikipedia says the Yang-Baxter equation appears in Yang's 1967 work Wikipedia, Yang-Baxter equation (History)
  3. 1972

    Baxter's solution of the eight-vertex model

    first application

    Baxter, R. J., Partition function of the eight-vertex lattice model, Annals of Physics 70 (1972) 193-228

    Uses the star-triangle consistency relation to solve the eight-vertex lattice model exactly, a major solvable lattice model in this line.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in Annals of Physics 70 (1972), DOI 10.1016/0003-4916(72)90335-1, pages 193-228 Crossref; zbMATH 0236.60070
    • Wikipedia says the search for solvable lattice models culminated in Baxter's 1972 solution of the eight-vertex model Wikipedia, Yang-Baxter equation (History)
  4. 1979

    Quantum inverse problem method

    key development

    Faddeev, L. D.; Sklyanin, E. K.; Takhtajan, L. A., The quantum inverse problem method I, Theoret. and Math. Phys. (1979)

    Builds the general operator framework that makes the Yang-Baxter relation the defining condition of quantum integrability.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper titled 'Quantum inverse problem method. I' in Theoret. and Math. Phys., Crossref year 1979, DOI 10.1007/bf01018718 Crossref; zbMATH 0449.35096
  5. 1981

    Yang-Baxter equation named in a title

    naming

    Kulish, P. P.; Reshetikhin, N. Yu., Yang-Baxter equation and representation theory. I, Letters in Mathematical Physics 5 (1981) 393-403

    The earliest zbMATH title found that uses the name 'Yang-Baxter equation'.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH title 'Yang-Baxter equation and representation theory. I' (1981); a title search for 'Yang-Baxter' over 1950-1980 returned no record zbMATH Open 3790922
  6. 1982

    Baxter's monograph Exactly Solved Models in Statistical Mechanics

    consolidation

    Baxter, R. J., Exactly Solved Models in Statistical Mechanics, Academic Press (1982)

    The first book-length systematic treatment of solvable lattice models and the Yang-Baxter relation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Monograph indexed by zbMATH with 1982 year (0538.60093) zbMATH
