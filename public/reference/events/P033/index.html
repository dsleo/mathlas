1983 · Topology

Gauge theory transforms four-manifold topology

Solutions of the Yang-Mills and later Seiberg-Witten equations give invariants distinguishing smooth structures on four-manifolds, results unreachable by classical topology.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1978

    Anti-self-dual connections on four-manifolds

    precursor

    Atiyah, Hitchin, Singer, Self-duality in four-dimensional Riemannian geometry, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A (1978)

    Classifies self-dual Yang-Mills connections on four-manifolds, the analytic tool later used for topology.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appeared in Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A in 1978 Crossref / publisher record
    • Donaldson's instanton tools came from particle-physics Yang-Mills equations MacTutor Donaldson biography
  2. 1983

    Donaldson announces gauge-theoretic invariants

    first statement

    Donaldson, Self-dual connections and the topology of smooth 4-manifolds, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. (1983)

    Announces that moduli spaces of anti-self-dual connections give invariants of smooth 4-manifolds.

    Evidence (2)
    • Bull. AMS 1983 announcement of the topology of smooth 4-manifolds Crossref / publisher record
    • Donaldson theory started by Donaldson (1983), giving the restriction on definite forms Wikipedia: Donaldson theory
  3. 1983

    Donaldson's theorem on definite forms

    first application

    Donaldson, An application of gauge theory to four-dimensional topology, J. Differential Geom. (1983)

    Full paper using Yang-Mills moduli to restrict the intersection forms of simply connected smooth 4-manifolds.

    Evidence (2)
    • J. Differential Geom. 1983 full paper on gauge theory and four-dimensional topology Crossref / publisher record
    • Donaldson's theorem restricts quadratic forms of simply connected 4-manifolds Wikipedia: Donaldson theory
  4. 1990

    Donaldson-Kronheimer monograph

    consolidation

    Donaldson, Kronheimer, The Geometry of Four-Manifolds, Oxford University Press (1990)

    Book-length systematic treatment of gauge theory on four-manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Oxford monograph chapters from 1990 (DOI prefix oso/9780198535539) Crossref / publisher record
  5. 1994

    Seiberg-Witten equations

    key development

    Seiberg, Witten, Monopoles, duality and chiral symmetry breaking in N=2 supersymmetric QCD, Nucl. Phys. B (1994)

    Introduces the monopole equations (Seiberg-Witten equations) from supersymmetric gauge theory, a more tractable replacement for the Yang-Mills moduli problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Nucl. Phys. B 1994 paper introducing the Seiberg-Witten monopole theory Crossref / publisher record
    • Seiberg-Witten invariants are easier to work with than Donaldson invariants Wikipedia: Seiberg-Witten invariants
  6. 1994

    Seiberg-Witten invariants of four-manifolds

    key development

    Witten, Monopoles and four-manifolds, Math. Res. Lett. (1994)

    Defines the Seiberg-Witten invariants for smooth 4-manifolds and shows they reproduce Donaldson-type results more easily.

    Evidence (2)
    • Math. Res. Lett. 1994 paper on monopoles and four-manifolds Crossref / publisher record
    • Seiberg-Witten invariants defined for smooth oriented 4-manifolds Wikipedia: Seiberg-Witten invariants
  7. 1994

    Thom conjecture via Seiberg-Witten

    key development

    Kronheimer, Mrowka, The genus of embedded surfaces in the projective plane, Math. Res. Lett. (1994)

    Applies Seiberg-Witten theory to the genus of embedded surfaces in the projective plane (the Thom conjecture).

    Evidence (1)
    • Math. Res. Lett. 1994 paper on genus of embedded surfaces in CP2 Crossref / publisher record
