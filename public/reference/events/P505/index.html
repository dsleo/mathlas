1982 · Number theory

Lenstra-Lenstra-Lovasz lattice basis reduction

Geometry-of-numbers ideas give a polynomial-time algorithm that factors polynomials and attacks integer programming and knapsack cryptosystems.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1982

    LLL reduction appears in print

    first statement

    A. K. Lenstra, H. W. Lenstra and L. Lovász, Factoring polynomials with rational coefficients (1982)

    The published paper introduces lattice basis reduction.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lenstra, Lenstra, Lovasz, Factoring polynomials with rational coefficients, Math. Ann. 261 (1982) Crossref record
  2. 1982

    LLL factors rational polynomials

    first application

    A. K. Lenstra, H. W. Lenstra and L. Lovász, Factoring polynomials with rational coefficients (1982)

    The new reduction method is used on polynomial factorization.

    Evidence (1)
    • Polynomial factoring over Q performed in the founding paper Crossref record
  3. 1983

    Fixed-dimension integer programming

    key development

    H. W. Lenstra, Integer programming with a fixed number of variables (1983)

    Lattice methods enter a further algorithmic target.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lenstra, Integer programming with a fixed number of variables, Math. Oper. Res. 8 (1983) Crossref record
  4. 1986

    Lattice basis reduction in a later title

    naming

    C.-P. Schnorr, A more efficient algorithm for lattice basis reduction (1986)

    The method's name appears in a later paper title.

    Evidence (1)
