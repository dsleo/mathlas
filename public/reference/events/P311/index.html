1999 · Category & homological algebra

Khovanov homology

The Jones polynomial was lifted to a bigraded homology theory, starting a categorification programme.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1999

    Jones-polynomial categorification posted

    first statement

    M. Khovanov, A categorification of the Jones polynomial, arXiv:math/9908171 (1999)

    The public preprint constructs a bigraded link cohomology whose Euler characteristic is the Jones polynomial.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the public manuscript to August 1999 and states the construction. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2004

    Rasmussen applies Khovanov homology to the slice genus (s-invariant)

    first application

    Rasmussen, J., Khovanov homology and the slice genus, arXiv:math/0402131, 2004; Invent. Math. 182 (2010) 419-447

    Rasmussen used Khovanov homology to define a knot invariant that bounds the slice genus, the first use of the theory on a classical problem.

    Evidence (3)
    • Wikipedia states the first application of Khovanov homology was Rasmussen s-invariant, giving a slice genus bound and the Milnor conjecture Wikipedia, Khovanov homology (raw wikitext)
    • Preprint math/0402131 titled Khovanov homology and the slice genus, submitted 9 Feb 2004 arXiv abstract page
    • Published Invent. Math. 182 (2010) 419-447, by Jacob Rasmussen zbMATH Open record (API search)
