Geometric group theory

Finitely generated groups are studied as metric spaces up to quasi-isometry, so large-scale geometry classifies algebraic properties.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1856

    Icosian calculus (Hamilton)

    precursor

    Hamilton studies the icosahedral symmetry group through the edge graph of the dodecahedron, an early geometric-combinatorial group.

    Evidence (1)
    • A precursor is found in the 1856 icosian calculus of Hamilton Wikipedia: Geometric group theory
  2. 1911

    Dehn's word and conjugacy problems

    precursor

    Dehn, M., Über unendliche diskontinuierliche Gruppen, Math. Ann. 71, 1911

    Dehn formulates the word, conjugacy and isomorphism problems for finitely presented groups, the programme GGT later answers.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Math. Ann., 1911 (DOI 10.1007/bf01456932) Crossref record
    • Dehn's formulation of the word and conjugacy problems in 1911 MacTutor: Word problems for groups
  3. 1981

    Groups of polynomial growth and expanding maps

    first statement

    Gromov, M., Groups of polynomial growth and expanding maps, Publ. Math. IHÉS 53, 1981

    Gromov's IHÉS paper treats finitely generated groups as metric spaces and links growth to nilpotence, the start of the large-scale programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in Publ. Math. IHÉS, 1981 (DOI 10.1007/bf02698687) Crossref record
  4. 1987

    Hyperbolic groups

    key development

    Gromov, M., Hyperbolic groups, in Essays in Group Theory, MSRI Publ. 8, 1987

    Gromov introduces hyperbolic groups, which became the central class of GGT.

    Evidence (2)
    • Essays in group theory, MSRI Publ. 8 (1987), pp. 75-263 (zbMATH 0634.20015) zbMATH record
    • Gromov's 1987 monograph introduces hyperbolic groups Wikipedia: Geometric group theory
  5. 1993

    Volume titled Geometric Group Theory (Sussex 1991)

    naming

    Geometric Group Theory, London Math. Soc. Lecture Note Series 181-182, 1993

    The 1993 volumes from the Sussex 1991 conference carry the field's name in their title.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chapter in Geometric Group Theory (LMS LNS), 1993 (DOI 10.1017/cbo9780511661860.019) Crossref record
  6. 1993

    Asymptotic invariants of infinite groups

    consolidation

    Gromov, M., Asymptotic invariants of infinite groups, in Geometric Group Theory vol. 2, LMS LNS 182, 1993

    Gromov's survey treats large-scale invariants systematically and is the main reference volume for the subject.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book review of Geometric group theory vol. 2 in Bull. AMS 1996 (DOI 10.1090/s0273-0979-96-00669-6) Crossref record
