2006 · Number theory

Measure rigidity and arithmetic quantum unique ergodicity

Entropy and measure-rigidity methods for diagonal group actions show that Hecke eigenfunctions on arithmetic surfaces do not concentrate, a quantum chaos statement.

  2. 2006

    Measure rigidity proves arithmetic QUE

    key development

    E. Lindenstrauss, Invariant measures and arithmetic quantum unique ergodicity, Ann. Math. 163 (2006)

    A positive-entropy measure classification yields arithmetic quantum unique ergodicity for compact arithmetic surfaces.
