1994 · Topology

Heegaard Floer and Seiberg-Witten theory

Seiberg-Witten monopole equations gave simpler four-manifold invariants than Donaldson's, and Ozsvath-Szabo later built a Floer theory of Heegaard diagrams.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1994

    Monopole invariants of four-manifolds

    first statement

    Monopoles and Four-Manifolds (1994)

    Witten proposes the Seiberg–Witten equations as four-manifold invariants.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated record and abstract support this 1994 milestone. Original publication or repository record
  2. 1995

    Morgan's monograph on Seiberg-Witten equations

    consolidation

    Morgan, J. W., The Seiberg-Witten Equations and Applications to the Topology of Smooth Four-Manifolds (Princeton)

    A book-length exposition of Seiberg-Witten theory and its topological applications to four-manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref book record, DOI 10.1515/9781400865161, issued 1995 Crossref
  3. 2001

    Heegaard Floer three-manifold invariants

    first statement

    Holomorphic disks and topological invariants for closed three-manifolds (2001)

    The public preprint introduces three-manifold invariants using holomorphic disks in Heegaard diagrams.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated record and abstract support this 2001 milestone. Original publication or repository record
  4. 2001

    Applications of Heegaard Floer invariants

    first application

    Holomorphic disks and three-manifold invariants: properties and applications (2001)

    The follow-up paper applies the new invariants to specific three-manifold questions.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated record and abstract support this 2001 milestone. Original publication or repository record
  5. 2004

    Ozsváth-Szabó: properties and applications of HF invariants

    key development

    Ozsváth, P., Szabó, Z., Holomorphic disks and three-manifold invariants: properties and applications, Annals of Mathematics 159 (2004)

    Proves structural properties of Heegaard Floer invariants and derives applications to three-manifold topology.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Annals 159 (2004) Crossref
  6. 2007

    Kronheimer-Mrowka on SW theory for 3-manifolds

    consolidation

    Kronheimer, P. B., Mrowka, T. S., Monopoles and Three-Manifolds, Cambridge (2007)

    A monograph developing monopole Floer theory for three-manifolds, the Seiberg-Witten side of the Heegaard Floer comparison.
