1896 · Number theory

Geometry of numbers (Minkowski)

Convex bodies and lattices in R^n are used to prove existence of integer points, with consequences for quadratic forms and algebraic number fields.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1887

    Minkowski's unpublished lecture on spatial intuition and minima of quadratic forms

    precursor

    Minkowski, Raeumliche Anschauung und Minima positiv definiter quadratischer Formen (lecture; published 1991)

    A lecture, unpublished until 1991, already contained an early instance of the method later called the geometry of numbers.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor, quoting Dieudonne: it contains the first example of the method later developed as the geometry of numbers. MacTutor Biographies/Minkowski
  2. 1896

    Minkowski: Geometrie der Zahlen (first instalment)

    first statement

    Minkowski, Geometrie der Zahlen, Teubner, Leipzig, 1896 (completed 1910)

    Minkowski presented lattice points in convex bodies, with the first and second theorems, as a geometric method for number theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia: in 1896 he presented his geometry of numbers. Wikipedia: Hermann Minkowski
    • Wikipedia attributes the founding of the line of research to Minkowski's work of that name. Wikipedia: Geometry of numbers
  3. 1896

    The title 'Geometrie der Zahlen'

    naming

    Minkowski, Geometrie der Zahlen, 1896

    The field's name is the title of Minkowski's book.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia says the term arose from Minkowski's embedding of numbers in higher-dimensional space and his book of that name. Wikipedia: Geometry of numbers
  4. 1907

    Minkowski: Diophantische Approximationen

    key development

    Minkowski, Diophantische Approximationen, Teubner, 1907

    An elementary account linking the geometry of numbers to Diophantine approximation and algebraic numbers.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive record dated 1907. Internet Archive
    • MacTutor: the 1907 book gave an elementary account of this work and its applications to Diophantine approximation and algebraic numbers. MacTutor Biographies/Minkowski
  5. 1910

    Geometrie der Zahlen, completed edition

    consolidation

    Minkowski, Geometrie der Zahlen, Teubner, 1910

    The completed book edition collecting the instalments, issued after Minkowski's death in 1909.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive record of the 1910 edition. Internet Archive
    • Wikipedia cites the 1910 Teubner edition. Wikipedia: Hermann Minkowski
  6. 1959

    Cassels: An Introduction to the Geometry of Numbers

    consolidation

    Cassels, An Introduction to the Geometry of Numbers, Springer, 1959

    A modern systematic monograph on the subject after the 1930-1960 growth of research.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Cassels, 1959. zbMATH Open
    • Wikipedia cites Cassels and describes 1930-1960 research by Mordell, Davenport and Siegel. Wikipedia: Geometry of numbers
