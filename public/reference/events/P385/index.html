1897 · Number theory

Class field theory

Abelian extensions of number fields were matched to ideal-class-type groups, from Kronecker, Weber and Hilbert through Takagi's existence theorem and Artin's reciprocity law.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1897

    Weber on number groups in algebraic fields

    first statement

    Weber, Ueber Zahlengruppen in algebraischen Koerpern, Math. Ann. 48, 433-473, 1897

    Weber introduced class fields as extensions attached to groups of ideal classes.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content not read; first Weber paper in the series (further parts: Math. Ann. 49, 50).

    Evidence (3)
    • Record: Math. Ann. 48 (1897) 433-473. Crossref
    • Says Weber coined the term before Hilbert's papers. Wikipedia: Class field theory
    • zbMATH 28.0083.04: Weber, 'Ueber Zahlengruppen in algebraischen Koerpern', Math. Ann. 48 (1897) 433-473. zbMATH Open, document 28.0083.04
  2. 1897

    The term class field

    naming

    Weber, Math. Ann. 48 (1897)

    Weber coined the term class field in this period.

    Dating or attribution note

    Exact paper for the coinage not verified; assumed from the Weber series.

    Evidence (1)
    • Says it was Weber who coined the term before Hilbert's fundamental papers. Wikipedia: Class field theory
  3. 1897

    Hilbert's Zahlbericht

    consolidation

    Hilbert, Die Theorie der algebraischen Zahlkoerper, Jahresbericht DMV 4, 1897

    A report that synthesised algebraic number theory and contains the ideas of class field theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Says the Zahlbericht (1897) is a synthesis of Kummer, Kronecker and Dedekind and contains the ideas of class field theory. MacTutor biography of Hilbert
    • Crossref record: modern article on the Zahlbericht. Crossref
  4. 1898

    Hilbert's relative abelian fields

    key development

    Hilbert, Ueber die Theorie der relativ-Abel'schen Zahlkoerper, Goett. Nachr. 1898 (Acta Math. 26, 1902)

    Hilbert formulated the conjectures on class fields that Takagi and Artin later proved.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Acta Math. 26 (1902) 99-131, same title. Crossref
    • Says Hilbert's conjectures were proved by Takagi and Artin. Wikipedia: Class field theory
  5. 1920

    Takagi's existence theorem

    key development

    Takagi, Ueber eine Theorie des relativ Abel'schen Zahlkoerpers, J. Coll. Sci. Tokyo 41, 1920

    Takagi proved the classification of abelian extensions by ideal class groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • Says his most important paper of 1920 introduced Takagi class field theory. MacTutor biography of Takagi
    • Crossref: reprint in collected works. Crossref
  6. 1926–1930

    Hasse's report on class field theory

    consolidation

    Hasse, Bericht ueber neuere Untersuchungen und Probleme aus der Theorie der algebraischen Zahlkoerper, 1926-1930

    The survey that made the theory accessible.

    Evidence (2)
    • Says Hasse included Takagi's theory in his treatise on class field theory. MacTutor biography of Takagi
    • Crossref: chapter 'Klassenkoerpertheorie' in the 1965 reprint. Crossref
  7. 1927

    Artin's general reciprocity law

    key development

    Artin, Beweis des allgemeinen Reziprozitaetsgesetzes, Abh. Math. Sem. Hamburg 5, 353-363, 1927

    Artin proved the general reciprocity law, subsuming earlier reciprocity laws.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: 1927. Crossref
    • Says he gave a general reciprocity law in 1927. MacTutor biography of Artin
  8. 1929

    Principal ideal theorem

    first application

    Furtwaengler, Beweis des Hauptidealsatzes fuer die Klassenkoerper algebraischer Zahlkoerper, Abh. Math. Sem. Hamburg 7, 14-36, 1929

    Furtwangler used Artin's reciprocity to prove the principal ideal theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Abh. Hamburg 7 (1929) 14-36. Crossref
    • Calls Furtwangler's principal ideal proof the most striking application. MacTutor biography of Artin
