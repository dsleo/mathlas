1970 · Number theory

Local Langlands for GL_n

Harris-Taylor and Henniart proved the local Langlands correspondence for GL_n through the cohomology of Shimura varieties.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Langlands' letter to Weil

    precursor

    Langlands, letter to A. Weil, January 1967 (unpublished)

    A typed, unpublished letter sets out the non-abelian reciprocity ideas later called the Langlands programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: Langlands wrote a 17-page letter to Weil in January 1967 that circulated widely MacTutor, Robert Langlands
  2. 1970

    Langlands’ 1969 Washington lecture published

    first statement

    Langlands, Problems in the theory of automorphic forms, Lecture Notes in Math. 170 (1970)

    The Washington lecture, delivered in 1969 and printed in 1970, is the public source for the local GL_n conjecture as described by Harris and Taylor.

    Evidence (2)
    • IAS dates Langlands’ Washington lecture publication to 1970 and Langlands dates the lecture to 1969 Institute for Advanced Study
    • Harris and Taylor attribute the local GL_n conjecture to Langlands’ 1969 Washington lecture Harris and Taylor, Princeton University Press
  3. 1978

    Tunnell, 'On the local Langlands conjecture for GL(2)'

    naming

    J. B. Tunnell, 'On the local Langlands conjecture for GL(2)', Inventiones Mathematicae, pages 179-200, zbMATH record 3598559, 1978

    Tunnell's 1978 paper is the earliest title found in the searched window that uses the phrase 'local Langlands' for the GL(2) case, a precursor to the GL(n) problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record gives title 'On the local Langlands conjecture for GL(2)', author J. B. Tunnell, year 1978, journal article, pages 179-200, ISSN 0020-9910 (Inventiones) zbMATH Open API, document 3598559
  4. 2000

    Henniart publishes an independent proof for GL_n

    first application

    G. Henniart, Une preuve simple des conjectures de Langlands pour GL(n) sur un corps p-adique, Invent. Math. 139 (2000), 439–455

    Constructs the local correspondence and proves the Langlands conjectures for GL_n over a p-adic field.

    Evidence (1)
    • The article explicitly claims a proof of the GL_n conjectures and appeared in Inventiones in 2000 Inventiones mathematicae / Springer
  5. 2001

    Harris-Taylor prove local Langlands for GL_n via Shimura varieties

    key development

    Harris, Taylor, The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties, Annals of Math. Studies 151, Princeton UP (2001)

    Harris and Taylor use the cohomology of Shimura varieties to prove the local Langlands correspondence for GL_n, the first purposeful use of the global geometric method on this local problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Harris and Taylor’s monograph publishes their proof in 2001 Princeton University Press
  6. 2002

    Henniart characterises the GL_n correspondence

    key development

    Henniart, Une caractérisation de la correspondance de Langlands locale pour GL(n), Bull. SMF (2002)

    Henniart gives a characterisation of the local correspondence for GL_n by its behaviour under a small set of operations, giving an independent confirmation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Bulletin de la Societe Mathematique de France, 2002 Crossref
