Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2018

    Neural tangent kernel defined

    first statement

    A. Jacot, F. Gabriel and C. Hongler, Neural Tangent Kernel: Convergence and Generalization in Neural Networks, arXiv:1806.07572 (2018)

    The preprint defines the NTK for neural-network training.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted June 2018 arXiv
  2. 2018

    NTK used to analyze training and generalization

    first application

    A. Jacot, F. Gabriel and C. Hongler, Neural Tangent Kernel: Convergence and Generalization in Neural Networks, arXiv:1806.07572 (2018)

    The authors describe infinite-width gradient descent through kernel dynamics and analyze convergence.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract explicitly describes training dynamics arXiv
  3. 2019

    NTK spectra analyzed

    key development

    G. Yang and H. Salman, A Fine-Grained Spectral Perspective on Neural Networks, arXiv:1907.10599 (2019)

    Spectral analysis of the NTK studies learning bias and generalization across architectures.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted July 2019 arXiv
  4. 2022

    Neural tangent kernel applied to PINN training failure

    key development

    Wang, S.; Yu, X.; Perdikaris, P., When and why PINNs fail to train: a neural tangent kernel perspective, J. Comput. Phys. 449 (2022) 110768, DOI 10.1016/j.jcp.2021.110768

    Applies NTK spectra to explain training failure of physics-informed networks, a later use of the kernel for training analysis.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: J. Comput. Phys. 449 (2022) 110768 zBMATH Open record (search API)
