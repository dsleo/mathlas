1934 · Logic & foundations

Curry-Howard correspondence (proofs as programs, propositions as types)

Proofs were matched with programs.

Also known as: Curry-Howard isomorphism

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1934

    Curry's types as axiom schemes

    first statement

    Curry, 'Functionality in combinatory logic', PNAS 20 (1934) 584-590

    Curry observed that the types of combinators can be read as axiom schemes of intuitionistic implicational logic.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record: PNAS 1934 Crossref
    • Curry 1934 is the first observation in the Curry-Howard history Wikipedia: Curry-Howard correspondence (history)
  2. 1958

    Curry and Feys, Combinatory Logic Vol. I

    consolidation

    Curry and Feys, Combinatory Logic, Vol. I (North-Holland, 1958)

    The systematic monograph on combinatory logic, where the match between simple types of K and S and Hilbert-style proof systems is recorded.

    Evidence (2)
    • Book reviewed as published 1958 by North-Holland Crossref: Goodstein review, Math. Gazette
    • 1958 observation of Hilbert-style systems and typed combinators in the Curry-Feys book Wikipedia: Curry-Howard correspondence (history)
  3. 1969

    Howard's unpublished manuscript

    precursor

    Howard, 'The formulae-as-types notion of construction' (manuscript 1969; published 1980 in To H.B. Curry)

    Howard observed that natural deduction can be read directly as a typed lambda calculus; the note circulated but was not printed until 1980.

    Evidence (1)
    • Howard's observation dated 1969; the 1980 publication is cited as the source Wikipedia: Curry-Howard correspondence (history)
  4. 1975

    Martin-Lof's intuitionistic type theory

    key development

    P. Martin-Löf, An intuitionistic theory of types: predicative part, in Logic Colloquium '73 (1975)

    Martin-Lof's dependent type theory gave the propositions-as-types reading a working foundation and is the modern form of the correspondence.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: An intuitionistic theory of types: predicative part, Logic Colloquium '73, 1975 zbMATH record 3521950
    • Current formulation of the isomorphism credited to Martin-Lof (1975 citation) Wikipedia: Curry-Howard correspondence (history)
  5. 1980

    Howard's formulae-as-types paper in print

    first statement

    Howard, 'The formulae-as-types notion of construction', in Hindley and Seldin (eds), To H.B. Curry: Essays on Combinatory Logic (Academic Press, 1980)

    The explicit natural-deduction version of the isomorphism appears in print, honouring Curry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record for 'To H.B. Curry' (Hindley, Curry), 1980 Open Library search
    • Howard's paper is the 1980 citation for the explicit isomorphism Wikipedia: Curry-Howard correspondence (history)
  6. 2015

    'Propositions as types' title

    naming

    Wadler, 'Propositions as types', Communications of the ACM 58(12) (2015)

    A widely read survey that uses 'propositions as types' as its title, the phrase now used for the correspondence.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper record: Communications of the ACM 2015 Crossref
