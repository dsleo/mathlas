1906 · Complex analysis

Several complex variables and Hartogs

Hartogs discovered phenomena, such as automatic extension of holomorphic functions, with no one-variable analogue.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1895

    Cousin problems

    precursor

    Pierre Cousin, Sur les fonctions de n variables complexes, Acta Math. 19 (1895)

    Poses the Cousin problems on constructing functions with prescribed singularities of several variables.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1895. Crossref
  2. 1906

    Hartogs' extension theorem

    first statement

    Hartogs, F., Einige Folgerungen aus der Cauchyschen Integralformel bei Funktionen mehrerer Veranderlichen (1906)

    Hartogs shows that holomorphic functions in several variables extend automatically beyond their natural domain, with no one-variable analogue.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper with this title, year 1906 zbMATH Open
    • Hartogs' extension theorem cited to the 1906 paper Wikipedia: Function of several complex variables, History
  3. 1916

    Osgood's 'On functions of several complex variables'

    naming

    W. F. Osgood, On functions of several complex variables, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. (1916), zbMATH 46.0527.01

    Osgood's 1916 paper has the phrase 'several complex variables' in its title; the earliest title use found.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH 46.0527.01: Osgood, 'On functions of several complex variables' (1916). zbMATH Open, document 46.0527.01
  4. 1932

    Cartan-Thullen on domains of holomorphy

    key development

    H. Cartan and P. Thullen, Zur Theorie der Singularitaeten der Funktionen mehrerer komplexen Veraenderlichen, Math. Ann. 106 (1932)

    Characterises domains of holomorphy, extending Hartogs' phenomenon to a theory of natural domains.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1932. Crossref
  5. 1934

    Behnke-Thullen monograph on several complex variables

    consolidation

    Behnke, H. and Thullen, P., Theorie der Funktionen mehrerer komplexer Veranderlichen (1934)

    The 1934 Ergebnisse volume gives an early systematic treatment of functions of several complex variables.

    Dating or attribution note

    Only chapter 1 was confirmed; the volume's status as the first systematic treatment is not verified.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chapter 'Uber den Begriff des analytischen Funktionselementes' in the 1934 volume Crossref
  6. 1936

    Oka's series begins

    key development

    K. Oka, Sur les fonctions analytiques de plusieurs variables I, J. Sci. Hiroshima Univ. 6 (1936)

    Oka solves the Cousin and Levi problems over the following years, with part IX in 1953.

    Evidence (2)
    • Part I recorded in Crossref, 1936. Crossref
    • Part IX recorded in Crossref, 1953. Crossref
  7. 1966

    Hoermander's textbook

    consolidation

    Lars Hoermander, An Introduction to Complex Analysis in Several Variables, 1966

    A textbook organised around the d-bar method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book first published 1966. Open Library
