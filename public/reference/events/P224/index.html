1952 · Dynamics

Dynamic programming: Bellman's principle of optimality

Principle of optimality recast multi-stage decisions as recursion.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1947

    Wald's Sequential Analysis

    precursor

    A. Wald, Sequential Analysis, Wiley 1947

    Sequential decision problems solved by backward recursion, a close ancestor of dynamic programming.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Wald, Sequential analysis, Wiley, 1947. zbMATH record 0029.15805
    • MacTutor biography of Wald. MacTutor, biography of Wald
  2. 1951

    Arrow, Harris and Marschak, Optimal Inventory Policy

    precursor

    K. J. Arrow, T. E. Harris, J. Marschak, Optimal inventory policy, Econometrica 19(3) (1951) 250-272

    A dynamic stochastic inventory model solved stage by stage, an early multi-stage decision problem of the kind Bellman's method treats.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Econometrica 19(3), p. 250, July 1951. Crossref
  3. 1952

    On the theory of dynamic programming (PNAS)

    first statement

    Bellman, On the theory of dynamic programming, PNAS 38 (1952) 716-719

    Bellman's first public statement of the method, recasting multi-stage decisions as recursion.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • First publication on dynamic programming appeared in 1952 MacTutor biography of Bellman
  4. 1952

    Term 'dynamic programming' first used in a title

    naming

    Bellman, On the theory of dynamic programming, PNAS 38 (1952) 716-719 (as above)

    The first use of the term in a title, per the Wikipedia account of the origin of the name.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same paper plays first_statement and naming (rule 2). Russell and Norvig put Bellman's first paper using the term in 1952; a RAND memorandum of 1952 also carries the term in its title and may be earlier (not readable here).

    Evidence (2)
    • Bellman's first paper using the term appeared in 1952 Wikipedia: Dynamic programming (citing Russell and Norvig)
    • Same publication as first_statement Crossref
  5. 1953

    Bellman applies dynamic programming to bottleneck problems

    first application

    R. Bellman, Bottleneck problems and dynamic programming, PNAS 39(9) (1953) 947-951

    An early printed application of the method to a target problem, bottleneck allocation in production.

    Dating or attribution note

    Added in review as the earliest applied use found in a journal; the title identifies a problem class, the paper content was not read, and 1952-53 RAND memoranda (RM-1069 on production bottlenecks) may precede it. The RAND 1953 introduction (R-245) was not verified.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: PNAS 39(9), pp. 947-951, September 1953. Crossref
  6. 1953

    Shapley's stochastic games

    key development

    L. S. Shapley, Stochastic games, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 39 (1953)

    Uses a contraction and recursive value equation of the same form as the Bellman equation.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Shapley, Stochastic games, PNAS 39, 1095-1100, 1953. zbMATH record 0051.35805
    • Crossref record: Shapley, Stochastic Games, PNAS 39(10), pp. 1095-1100, 1953. Crossref
  7. 1954

    The theory of dynamic programming (Bulletin survey)

    key development

    Bellman, The theory of dynamic programming, Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society 60 (1954) 503-515

    A survey that organises the theory and its applications, with a bibliography up to 1954.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • Survey includes a bibliography up to 1954 Wikipedia: Dynamic programming
  8. 1957

    Dynamic Programming (monograph)

    consolidation

    Bellman, Dynamic Programming, Princeton University Press, 1957

    Book-length treatment of dynamic programming.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library work, Bellman, Dynamic programming, 1957. Open Library
    • Science book review of Bellman, Dynamic Programming (Princeton University Press), 25 April 1958. Crossref DOI 10.1126/science.127.3304.976.a
  9. 1960

    Howard's Dynamic Programming and Markov Processes

    key development

    R. A. Howard, Dynamic Programming and Markov Processes, MIT Press / Wiley 1960

    Introduces policy iteration for Markov decision processes.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Howard, Dynamic programming and Markov processes, 1960. zbMATH record 0091.16001
    • Open Library lists Howard, Dynamic programming and Markov processes, 1960. Open Library
  10. 1962

    Bellman and Dreyfus, Applied Dynamic Programming

    consolidation

    R. Bellman, S. Dreyfus, Applied Dynamic Programming, Princeton 1962

    Applications-oriented treatment following the 1957 monograph.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Bellman, Dreyfus, Applied dynamic programming, Princeton, 1962. zbMATH record 0106.34901
    • Open Library lists Bellman and Dreyfus, Applied dynamic programming, 1962. Open Library
