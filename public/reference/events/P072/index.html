1985 · Algebra

Quantum groups from statistical mechanics

Algebraic structures behind the Yang-Baxter equation of solvable lattice models are formalised as deformations of enveloping algebras, linking to knot invariants and representation theory.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1983

    Kulish-Reshetikhin transfer matrices

    precursor

    Kulish, Reshetikhin, Diagonalisation of GL(N) invariant transfer matrices and quantum N-wave system (Lee model), J. Phys. A 16 (1983) L591-L596

    Yang-Baxter type R-matrix structure in an integrable model, before the Hopf-algebra formulation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
  2. 1985

    q-analogue of U(g) solving the Yang-Baxter equation

    first statement

    Jimbo, A q-difference analogue of U(g) and the Yang-Baxter equation, Lett. Math. Phys. 10 (1985) 63-69

    Constructs the q-deformed enveloping algebra associated with a Yang-Baxter solution.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
  3. 1985

    Hopf algebras and the quantum Yang-Baxter equation

    key development

    Drinfeld, Hopf algebras and the quantum Yang-Baxter equation, Soviet Math. Dokl. 32 (1985) 256-258

    Sets up the Hopf-algebra framework for quantum groups and the quantum Yang-Baxter equation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
  4. 1986

    Drinfeld's ICM talk 'Quantum groups'

    naming

    Drinfeld, Quantum groups, Proc. Int. Congr. Math. Berkeley 1986, vol. 1, 798-820 (publ. 1987)

    The title introduces the term quantum group for the Hopf-algebraic objects.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title, pages and congress verified (zbMATH; print year 1987) zbMATH
  5. 1988

    Quantization of Lie groups and Lie algebras

    first application

    Faddeev, Reshetikhin, Takhtajan, Quantization of Lie groups and Lie algebras, in Algebraic Analysis (1988)

    Applies the quantum group construction to quantize Lie groups and Lie algebras, a concrete target problem.

    Dating or attribution note

    Judgement that this is the first purposeful application; the priority against other applications was not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title, venue and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
  6. 1994

    A Guide to Quantum Groups

    consolidation

    Chari, Pressley, A Guide to Quantum Groups, Cambridge University Press, 1994

    Systematic textbook on quantum groups and their representations.

    Evidence (1)
