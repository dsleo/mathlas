≈ 2006 · Algebraic geometry

Mirzakhani moduli volumes

Mirzakhani computed Weil-Petersson volumes, recovered Witten's conjecture, and established Teichmuller-dynamics rigidity (with Eskin).

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2006

    Weil–Petersson volume recursion published online

    key development

    M. Mirzakhani, Simple geodesics and Weil–Petersson volumes of moduli spaces of bordered Riemann surfaces, Invent. Math. 167 (2007)

    The published work relates simple geodesics and Weil–Petersson volumes of moduli spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher metadata dates the online publication to 12 October 2006; the bound volume is dated 2007. Original bibliographic record
  2. 2007

    Mirzakhani's simple geodesics and WP volumes

    key development

    Mirzakhani, M., Simple geodesics and Weil-Petersson volumes of moduli spaces of bordered Riemann surfaces, Inventiones mathematicae (2007)

    Shows the Weil-Petersson volumes are computed by counting simple closed geodesics, which yields the recursion.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Inventiones (online 2006, print volume 2007) Crossref
    • zbMATH record, 2007 zbMATH Open API
  3. 2008

    Growth of simple closed geodesics

    key development

    Mirzakhani, M., Growth of the number of simple closed geodesics on hyperbolic surfaces, Annals of Mathematics 168 (2008)

    Proves the counting asymptotic for simple closed geodesics using the same volume machinery.
