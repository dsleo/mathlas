1749 · Differential equations

D'Alembert and Euler: the wave equation and partial differential equations

The vibrating string led to the wave equation and series solutions.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1713

    Taylor's De motu nervi tensi

    precursor

    Brook Taylor, De motu nervi tensi, Philosophical Transactions 28 (1713)

    Taylor treats the fundamental mode of a vibrating string by a dynamical argument, before the partial differential equation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: De motu nervi tensi, Philosophical Transactions, 1713. Crossref, DOI 10.1098/rstl.1713.0004
    • MacTutor biography of Brook Taylor, who worked on the vibrating string. MacTutor, biography of Taylor
  2. 1749

    D'Alembert: curve formed by a vibrating string

    first statement

    d'Alembert, Recherches sur la courbe que forme une corde tendue mise en vibration, Histoire de l'Académie de Berlin 3 (volume dated 1747)

    D'Alembert derives the one-dimensional wave equation and its solution as a sum of two travelling waves.

    Evidence (2)
    • His most famous achievements include the wave equation and D'Alembert's formula; the papers cited are the 1747 Berlin memoirs Wikipedia, Jean le Rond d'Alembert
    • In 1746 d'Alembert published a solution to the vibrating string problem MacTutor, The function concept
  3. 1750

    Euler on vibrating strings

    key development

    Euler, Sur la vibration des cordes, Mémoires de l'Académie de Berlin 4, 1750

    Euler solves the string problem with initial shapes more general than d'Alembert allowed, opening the controversy on admissible functions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Euler published a paper in 1749 objecting to d'Alembert's restriction MacTutor, The function concept
    • Euler Archive: Sur la vibration des cordes, Mém. Berlin vol. 4, dated 1750 Euler Archive record E140 (bepress metadata)
  4. 1753

    Daniel Bernoulli's superposition of modes

    key development

    Daniel Bernoulli, Réflexions et éclaircissemens sur les nouvelles vibrations des cordes, Mém. Acad. Berlin 1755 (read 1753)

    Bernoulli proposes that general string motion is a sum of simple modes, which opened the debate with Euler and d'Alembert about admissible solutions.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record of a 2021 translation of Bernoulli's Réflexions et éclaircissemens sur les nouvelles vibrations des cordes, confirming the title. zbMATH record 1490.01021
    • MacTutor biography of Daniel Bernoulli. MacTutor, biography of Bernoulli Daniel
  5. 1766

    Euler's later clarification of vibrating strings

    key development

    Euler, Éclaircissemens sur le mouvement des cordes vibrantes, Mélanges de Turin, 1766

    A later Euler paper on the same problem, in the Turin Mélanges, by which the debate continued.

    Evidence (2)
    • Euler Archive: Éclaircissemens sur le mouvement des cordes vibrantes, dated 1766 Euler Archive record E317 (bepress metadata)
    • Lagrange's major study on vibrating strings appeared in the Turin Mélanges, engaging Euler and d'Alembert MacTutor, Joseph-Louis Lagrange
