1931 · Algebraic geometry

Tarski's quantifier elimination for real algebra (decision method)

Quantifier elimination showed the first-order theory of the reals is decidable.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1930

    Tarski proves the real quantifier elimination result (announced)

    precursor

    Wikipedia says Tarski proved the result in 1930, when he also showed the theory of real closed fields is complete.

    Evidence (1)
    • Tarski proved the theorem in 1930 in connection with real closed fields Wikipedia: Tarski–Seidenberg theorem
  2. 1931

    Tarski on definable sets of real numbers

    first statement

    A. Tarski, Sur les ensembles définissables de nombres réels, Fund. Math. 17 (1931) 210-239

    Earliest printed statement of the quantifier-elimination result for the reals.

    Evidence (2)
    • DOI record: Fund. Math. 17 (1931), 210-239 Crossref
    • Tarski's 1931 work on definable sets of reals MacTutor: Alfred Tarski
  3. 1948

    Decision method for elementary algebra and geometry (RAND report)

    first application

    A. Tarski, A Decision Method for Elementary Algebra and Geometry, RAND Corporation (1948)

    Applied the quantifier-elimination method to decide elementary algebra and geometry, which is the target problem.

    Dating or attribution note

    Taken from the MacTutor bibliography. The RAND report itself was not checked in a bibliographic record; the 1951 book is the verified published form.

    Evidence (1)
    • Works list includes "A decision method for elementary algebra and geometry" (1948) MacTutor: Alfred Tarski
  4. 1951

    Tarski monograph: A Decision Method for Elementary Algebra and Geometry

    consolidation

    A. Tarski, A Decision Method for Elementary Algebra and Geometry, University of California Press (1951)

    Published monograph on the decision method for elementary algebra and geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chapter record in book "A Decision Method for Elementary Algebra" (1951) Crossref
  5. 1954

    Seidenberg gives a new decision method for elementary algebra

    key development

    A. Seidenberg, A New Decision Method for Elementary Algebra, Ann. of Math. (2) 60 (1954)

    Gave an alternative decision procedure for elementary algebra, improving on Tarski's method.

    Dating or attribution note

    Significance (improvement on Tarski's method) is from the title and general knowledge; not checked against a history page.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publication record: Annals of Math. 60 (1954) Crossref
