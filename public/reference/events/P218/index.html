1969 · Dynamics

Catastrophe theory

Singularity classification was proposed as a model of discontinuous change in morphogenesis.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1955

    Whitney's singularities of mappings

    precursor

    Whitney, On singularities of mappings of Euclidean spaces. I, Annals of Math. 62 (1955)

    Classification of singularities of smooth maps (folds and cusps) that catastrophe theory later reinterprets.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, The Annals of Mathematics, 1955 Crossref
  2. 1956

    Thom's singularities of differentiable maps

    precursor

    Thom, Les singularités des applications différentiables, Ann. Inst. Fourier 6 (1956)

    Thom's early classification of singularities, the source of his later morphogenesis models.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: journal article, Annales de l'Institut Fourier, 1956 Crossref
    • MacTutor: Thom's earlier work made him well known before catastrophe theory MacTutor, René Thom
  3. 1969

    Thom, Topological models in biology

    first statement

    Thom, Topological models in biology, Topology 8 (1969)

    The public statement of catastrophe-type classification as a model of discontinuous change in morphogenesis.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Topology, 1969, Topological models in biology Crossref
  4. 1969

    Thom applies singularities to biological morphogenesis

    first application

    Thom, Topological models in biology, Topology 8 (1969)

    The first purposeful application of the singularity classification to biological form.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same paper plays both roles. Other early applications (physics, chemistry) were not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same bibliographic record as the first_statement milestone Crossref
  5. 1972

    Thom, Stabilité structurelle et morphogenèse

    consolidation

    Thom, Stabilité structurelle et morphogenèse, Benjamin (1972)

    The first systematic book treatment of structural stability, catastrophes and morphogenesis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record: René Thom, Stabilité structurelle et morphogénèse, Benjamin, first published 1972 Open Library
    • MacTutor: Thom presented catastrophe theory in this 1972 work MacTutor, René Thom
  6. 1977

    Zeeman, Catastrophe theory (selected papers)

    naming

    Zeeman, Catastrophe Theory: Selected Papers 1972-1977, Addison-Wesley (1977)

    The title and the popular account that made 'catastrophe theory' the field's name.

    Dating or attribution note

    The earliest title use of 'catastrophe theory' was not checked against Zeeman's 1972-1976 papers; 1977 is the earliest book title found. Derived institutional 1972 comes from Thom's consolidation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record: E. C. Zeeman, Catastrophe theory, Addison-Wesley, first published 1977 Open Library
    • MacTutor: Zeeman brought catastrophe theory to the general public MacTutor, Christopher Zeeman
