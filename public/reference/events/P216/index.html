1955 · Differential equations

Hörmander linear partial differential operators

Existence and regularity for general PDE are proved by Fourier and distribution methods.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1954

    Ehrenpreis on division by polynomials of derivation

    precursor

    Ehrenpreis, Solution of some problems of division I: division by a polynomial of derivation, American Journal of Mathematics, 1954

    Ehrenpreis works on division problems for constant-coefficient operators with Fourier and distribution methods, an ancestor of the general variable-coefficient theory rather than its public start.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article record: Ehrenpreis, Part I, Amer. J. Math., 1954 Crossref
    • Hörmander's thesis was inspired by Malgrange's thesis, announced in 1954 MacTutor, Lars Hörmander biography
  2. 1955

    Hörmander's general theory of partial differential operators

    first statement

    Hörmander, On the theory of general partial differential operators, Acta Mathematica, 1955

    Hörmander's Acta paper gives a general Fourier-analytic theory of existence and regularity for linear PDE with variable coefficients.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article record, Acta Math. 1955; zbMATH 0067.32201 for the same paper Crossref; zbMATH
    • Thesis published in Acta Mathematica in 1955 MacTutor, Lars Hörmander biography
  3. 1956

    Malgrange on existence and approximation of PDE solutions

    first application

    Malgrange, Existence et approximation des solutions des équations aux dérivées partielles et des équations de convolution, Annales de l'Institut Fourier, 1956

    Malgrange applies distribution and Fourier methods to existence and approximation of solutions of linear PDE; the earliest published record found for this use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article record, Ann. Inst. Fourier 1956 Crossref
  4. 1963

    Linear Partial Differential Operators monograph

    consolidation

    Hörmander, Linear Partial Differential Operators, Springer (Grundlehren), 1963

    The first systematic monograph built on Schwartz distributions and Fourier analysis for linear PDE.

    Evidence (2)
    • OpenLibrary record lists first publication 1963 (Island Press); a Springer edition is listed as 1964 OpenLibrary search
    • MacTutor says the material was lectured in 1961-62 and published as the 1963 book MacTutor, Lars Hörmander biography
