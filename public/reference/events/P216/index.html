1955 · Differential equations

Hörmander linear partial differential operators

Existence and regularity for general PDE are proved by Fourier and distribution methods.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1953

    Malgrange announces elementary solutions of constant-coefficient operators

    precursor

    B. Malgrange, Equations aux derivees partielles a coefficients constants. I. Solution elementaire, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 237 (1953) 1620-1622

    A short note announces that every nonzero differential operator with constant coefficients has an elementary solution, the result now called the Malgrange-Ehrenpreis theorem.

    Evidence (4)
    • Schwartz's Seminaire Bourbaki talk no. 87 (1954), 'Solution elementaire d'une equation aux derivees partielles a coefficients constants', pp. 345-350 (Numdam record, metadata read). L. Schwartz, Seminaire Bourbaki vol. 2, expose 87, Numdam
    • A later AMS paper cites Malgrange's note as C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 237 (1953), 1620-1622, for the existence of elementary solutions (found by search, paper not opened). Search result citing the note
    • Read: Malgrange's bibliography lists [19] the C. R. note 237 (1953) 1620-1622, [20] the second note 238 (1954) 196-198, and the introduction says the essential results were summarised in four C. R. notes. Malgrange, Existence et approximation des solutions..., Ann. Inst. Fourier 6 (1956) 271-355 (Numdam AIF_1956__6__271_0)
    • Read: the exposé (December 1953) presents the elementary solution of a constant-coefficient operator after Malgrange. L. Schwartz, Séminaire Bourbaki exposé 87 (Dec. 1953), Numdam SB_1951-1954__2__345_0
  2. 1954

    Ehrenpreis on division by polynomials of derivation

    precursor

    Ehrenpreis, Solution of some problems of division I: division by a polynomial of derivation, American Journal of Mathematics, 1954

    Ehrenpreis works on division problems for constant-coefficient operators with Fourier and distribution methods, an ancestor of the general variable-coefficient theory rather than its public start.

    Evidence (3)
    • Article record: Ehrenpreis, Part I, Amer. J. Math., 1954 Crossref
    • Hörmander's thesis was inspired by Malgrange's thesis, announced in 1954 MacTutor, Lars Hörmander biography
    • Read: Malgrange states that most of chapter I was found independently by Ehrenpreis, and cites Ehrenpreis, Amer. J. Math. 76 (1954) 883-903. Malgrange, Ann. Inst. Fourier 6 (1956) 271-355 (Numdam)
  3. 1955

    Hörmander's general theory of partial differential operators

    first statement

    Hörmander, On the theory of general partial differential operators, Acta Mathematica, 1955

    Hörmander's Acta paper gives a general Fourier-analytic theory of existence and regularity for linear PDE with variable coefficients.

    Evidence (3)
    • Article record, Acta Math. 1955; zbMATH 0067.32201 for the same paper Crossref; zbMATH
    • Thesis published in Acta Mathematica in 1955 MacTutor, Lars Hörmander biography
    • Read: Malgrange cites Hörmander, On the theory of general partial differential operators, Acta Math. 94 (1955), as new results on neighbouring subjects, in particular elliptic equations with constant coefficients. Malgrange, Ann. Inst. Fourier 6 (1956) 271-355 (Numdam)
  4. 1956

    Malgrange's full treatment of existence and approximation of PDE solutions

    key development

    Malgrange, Existence et approximation des solutions des équations aux dérivées partielles et des équations de convolution, Annales de l'Institut Fourier, 1956

    Malgrange gives the full proof that every constant-coefficient operator has a fundamental solution, solutions on convex sets, and approximation by exponential-polynomial solutions, extending mean-periodic function theory.

    Evidence (3)
    • Article record, Ann. Inst. Fourier 1956 Crossref
    • Read: Numdam record and abstract of the paper (Annales de l'Institut Fourier 6, 1956, pp. 271-355). Numdam AIF_1956__6__271_0
    • Read: introduction and chapter I (theorem 1: elementary solution with the L2-local property; solvability and approximation on convex open sets). Malgrange, Ann. Inst. Fourier 6 (1956) 271-355 (Numdam PDF)
  5. 1963

    Linear Partial Differential Operators monograph

    consolidation

    Hörmander, Linear Partial Differential Operators, Springer (Grundlehren), 1963

    The first systematic monograph built on Schwartz distributions and Fourier analysis for linear PDE.

    Evidence (3)
    • OpenLibrary record lists first publication 1963 (Island Press); a Springer edition is listed as 1964 OpenLibrary search
    • MacTutor says the material was lectured in 1961-62 and published as the 1963 book MacTutor, Lars Hörmander biography
    • Crossref: 'Distribution theory', chapter 1 of Linear Partial Differential Operators, 1963 (later printings 1964 and 1969). Crossref 10.1007/978-3-642-46175-0_1
