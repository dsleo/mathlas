1851 · Complex analysis

Dirichlet's principle

Riemann used a variational minimum principle from potential theory to guarantee existence of functions on surfaces; Weierstrass's criticism led to Hilbert's repaired form.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1851

    Riemann uses the principle in his thesis

    first statement

    Riemann, Grundlagen für eine allgemeine Theorie der Functionen einer veränderlichen complexen Grösse, doctoral thesis, Göttingen, 1851

    Riemann relied on a minimum principle for Dirichlet's integral to guarantee that functions with prescribed boundary behaviour exist, and he is credited with naming it after Dirichlet.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia: the name 'Dirichlet's principle' is due to Riemann, who applied it to complex analytic functions and assumed the minimum is attained English Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
    • MacTutor says the principle was used in Riemann's doctoral thesis and again in 1857 MacTutor biography of Riemann
  2. 1857

    Riemann reuses the principle for Abelian functions

    key development

    Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, Crelle 54, 115-155, 1857

    The principle again underpins the existence results for functions on Riemann surfaces, exposing it to Weierstrass' later criticism.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record of the paper Crossref 10.1515/crll.1857.54.115
    • MacTutor says the principle was used again for the results of this 1857 paper MacTutor biography of Riemann
  3. 1870

    Weierstrass criticises the principle

    key development

    Weierstrass, Über das sogenannte Dirichlet'sche Princip, Monatsberichte der Berliner Akademie, 1870

    Weierstrass gave a functional whose greatest lower bound is not attained, showing Riemann's assumption that the minimum exists was not justified.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia: Weierstrass published the first criticism in 1870 with a functional whose infimum is not attained English Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
    • Crossref lists reprints of the note (Mathematische Werke, vol. 2, pp. 49-54) Crossref 10.1017/cbo9781139567817.004
  4. 1870

    Weber titles a note on Riemann's proof of the Dirichlet principle

    naming

    Weber, H., A note on Riemann's proof of the Dirichlet principle, 1870

    The earliest zbMATH title found that names the Dirichlet principle.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: title with "Dirichlet principle", Weber, 1870 zbMATH Open doc 2721338
  5. 1900–1904

    Hilbert justifies the principle

    key development

    Hilbert, Über das Dirichlet'sche Princip, 1900; full proof in Mathematische Annalen 59, 161-186, 1904

    Hilbert's direct method in the calculus of variations restored the principle under suitable assumptions, answering Weierstrass' objection.

    Evidence (3)
    • Wikipedia: Hilbert justified Riemann's use in 1900 by the direct method English Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
    • Crossref record of Hilbert's full treatment in Math. Ann. 59 (1904) Crossref 10.1007/bf01444753
    • zbMATH 31.0418.01: Hilbert, 'On the Dirichlet principle', Jahresbericht DMV 8 (1900), pp. 184-188. zbMATH Open, document 31.0418.01
  6. 1929

    Kellogg publishes a monograph on potential theory

    consolidation

    Kellogg, O. D., Foundations of potential theory, 1929

    Monograph covering the variational (Dirichlet) approach to potential problems.

    Dating or attribution note

    Monograph chosen as the broad consolidation of potential theory; coverage of the Dirichlet principle not checked page by page.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists the monograph with first publication year 1929 Open Library works OL10480305W
  7. Date unknown

    Earlier uses of the variational principle in potential theory

    precursor

    Gauss, Green and Thomson (Kelvin), earlier work in potential theory

    MacTutor notes that Gauss, Green and Thomson had all made use of the minimum principle before Riemann used it for function theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor's Riemann biography says the principle was not new with Riemann because Gauss, Green and Thomson had used it MacTutor biography of Riemann
