1851 · Complex analysis

Dirichlet's principle

Riemann used a variational minimum principle from potential theory to guarantee existence of functions on surfaces; Weierstrass's criticism led to Hilbert's repaired form.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1839–1856

    Earlier uses of the minimum principle in potential theory (Gauss 1839, Thomson 1847, Dirichlet's lectures to 1856)

    precursor

    Gauss, Green and Thomson (Kelvin), earlier work in potential theory

    MacTutor notes that Gauss, Green and Thomson had all made use of the minimum principle before Riemann used it for function theory.

    Evidence (3)
    • MacTutor's Riemann biography says the principle was not new with Riemann because Gauss, Green and Thomson had used it MacTutor biography of Riemann
    • Read: Dirichlet is said to have given the principle in his lectures on forces acting inversely as the square of the distance, 'wohl durch einen aehnlichen Gedanken von Gauss veranlasst'. Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, Gesammelte Werke (1876)
    • Read: Hilbert names Gauss (1839), Thomson (1847) and Dirichlet (1856) as having applied the argument to boundary value problems. Hilbert, Über das Dirichletsche Prinzip, Math. Ann. 59 (1904) 161-186 (Internet Archive sim_mathematische-annalen_1904_59)
  2. 1851

    Riemann uses the principle in his thesis

    first statement

    Riemann, Grundlagen für eine allgemeine Theorie der Functionen einer veränderlichen complexen Grösse, doctoral thesis, Göttingen, 1851

    Riemann relied on a minimum principle for Dirichlet's integral to guarantee that functions with prescribed boundary behaviour exist, and he is credited with naming it after Dirichlet.

    Evidence (3)
    • Wikipedia: the name 'Dirichlet's principle' is due to Riemann, who applied it to complex analytic functions and assumed the minimum is attained English Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
    • MacTutor says the principle was used in Riemann's doctoral thesis and again in 1857 MacTutor biography of Riemann
    • Read: Art. 16 'Lehrsatz' on the minimum of the Dirichlet integral and Art. 17 'Begruendung ... mittelst der Grenzmethode' in the thesis, reprinted as paper I of the Werke, p. 30ff.; the title line reads 'Inauguraldissertation, Goettingen 1851'. Bernhard Riemann's Gesammelte mathematische Werke (Teubner 1876), Internet Archive gesammeltemath1876riemuoft
  3. 1857

    Riemann reuses the principle for Abelian functions

    key development

    Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, Crelle 54, 115-155, 1857

    The principle again underpins the existence results for functions on Riemann surfaces, exposing it to Weierstrass' later criticism.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref record of the paper Crossref 10.1515/crll.1857.54.115
    • MacTutor says the principle was used again for the results of this 1857 paper MacTutor biography of Riemann
    • Read: 'ein Princip, welches Dirichlet ... in seinen Vorlesungen ueber die dem umgekehrten Quadrat der Entfernung proportional wirkenden Kraefte seit einer Reihe von Jahren zu geben pflegt' and later 'nach dem Dirichlet'schen Princip' (the page reference 'S. 92'). Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, in Gesammelte Werke (1876)
  4. 1857

    Riemann names 'Dirichlet's principle' and credits Dirichlet's lectures

    naming

    Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, J. reine angew. Math. 54 (1857) 115-155

    Riemann uses the term Dirichlet'sches Princip in the text and attributes the idea to Dirichlet's lectures on potential theory, the earliest naming found.

    Evidence (2)
    • Read: the paper calls the method 'das Dirichlet'sche Princip' and attributes it to Dirichlet's lectures, not to his own earlier thesis. Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, Gesammelte Werke (1876), pp. 91-92
    • Crossref record of the paper (Crelle 54, 115-155, 1857). Crossref 10.1515/crll.1857.54.115
  5. 1870

    Weierstrass criticises the principle

    key development

    Weierstrass, Über das sogenannte Dirichlet'sche Princip, Monatsberichte der Berliner Akademie, 1870

    Weierstrass gave a functional whose greatest lower bound is not attained, showing Riemann's assumption that the minimum exists was not justified.

    Evidence (3)
    • Wikipedia: Weierstrass published the first criticism in 1870 with a functional whose infimum is not attained English Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
    • Crossref lists reprints of the note (Mathematische Werke, vol. 2, pp. 49-54) Crossref 10.1017/cbo9781139567817.004
    • Read: heading 'Ueber das sogenannte Dirichlet'sche Princip (Gelesen in der Koenigl. Akademie der Wissenschaften am 14. Juli 1870)' and the opening paragraph on Dirichlet's lectures, Riemann's use and the doubts. Karl Weierstrass, Mathematische Werke II, Internet Archive mathematischewer02weieuoft
  6. 1870

    Weber titles a note on Riemann's proof of Dirichlet's principle

    naming

    Weber, H., A note on Riemann's proof of the Dirichlet principle, 1870

    The earliest zbMATH title found that names the Dirichlet principle.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH record: title with "Dirichlet principle", Weber, 1870 zbMATH Open doc 2721338
    • Crossref: 'Note zu Riemanns Beweis der Dirichletschen Princips', Crelle's Journal 1870, pp. 29-39. Crossref 10.1515/crll.1870.71.29
    • Read: title, text and dating (Heidelberg, Juni 1869) of Weber's note in vol. 71 (1870). Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 71 (1870) (Internet Archive sim_journal-fuer-die-reine-und-angewandte-mathematik_1870_71)
  7. 1900

    Hilbert proposes to revive the principle (lecture of 1899, printed 1900)

    key development

    Hilbert, Über das Dirichlet'sche Princip, Jahresbericht der Deutschen Mathematiker-Vereinigung 8 (1900) 184-188 (lecture at the Munich meeting, text dated Göttingen 12 October 1899)

    Hilbert recasts the principle as the claim that every variational problem has a solution under suitable restrictions, and sketches rigorous existence proofs (shortest curve on a surface) as a way to revive it after Weierstrass.

    Evidence (3)
    • Read: Hilbert's note, text dated 12 October 1899, printed in vol. 8; proposes the general variational form of the principle. Jahresbericht der Deutschen Mathematiker-Vereinigung 8 (1900) 184-188 (Internet Archive bub_gb_jb8GAAAAYAAJ)
    • zbMATH 31.0418.01: Hilbert, 'On the Dirichlet principle', JDMV 8 (1900) pp. 184-188. zbMATH Open, document 31.0418.01
    • Read: Hilbert in 1899 'reestablished' the method; reference JDMV 8 (1900) p. 184. Kellogg, Foundations of potential theory (1929), Internet Archive foundationsofpot037624mbp
  8. 1901

    Hilbert's rigorous proof of the principle (Göttingen Festschrift 1901, reprinted 1904)

    key development

    Hilbert, Über das Dirichletsche Prinzip, Festschrift zur Feier des 150jährigen Bestehens der Königl. Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen, 1901; reprinted Mathematische Annalen 59 (1904) 161-186

    Hilbert proves the existence of the minimising potential function on a Riemann surface (finitely many sheets and branch points) by a direct variational construction, closing the gap Weierstrass had found.

    Evidence (3)
    • Read: footnote 'Abdruck aus der Festschrift ... Göttingen 1901', statement of the principle with Gauss 1839, Thomson 1847, Dirichlet 1856, Weierstrass, and the sections of the proof. Hilbert, Über das Dirichletsche Prinzip, Math. Ann. 59 (1904) 161-186 (Internet Archive sim_mathematische-annalen_1904_59)
    • Crossref record of the Annalen printing (vol. 59, pp. 161-186, 1904). Crossref 10.1007/bf01444753
    • A report in the Jahresbericht lists Hilbert's 'Über das Dirichletsche Princip' among the contents of the Göttingen Festschrift for the 150th anniversary. Jahresbericht der Deutschen Mathematiker-Vereinigung (Internet Archive bub_gb_eV5aAAAAYAAJ)
  9. 1929

    Kellogg publishes a monograph on potential theory

    consolidation

    Kellogg, O. D., Foundations of potential theory, 1929

    Monograph covering the variational (Dirichlet) approach to potential problems.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists the monograph with first publication year 1929 Open Library works OL10480305W
    • Read: Kellogg's discussion of the Dirichlet principle, Weierstrass' 1870 example and Hilbert's 1899 revival. Kellogg, Foundations of potential theory, Springer 1929 (Internet Archive foundationsofpot037624mbp)
  10. 1950

    Courant: Dirichlet's Principle, Conformal Mapping, and Minimal Surfaces

    consolidation

    R. Courant, Dirichlet's Principle, Conformal Mapping, and Minimal Surfaces, Interscience, New York, 1950

    A monograph whose title and subject are the principle, with the direct methods, conformal mapping and minimal surfaces treated through the Dirichlet integral.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library: Courant, Dirichlet's principle, conformal mapping, and minimal surfaces, first published 1950 (Interscience). Open Library work OL811189W
    • Crossref lists the book (Springer reprint 1977) and chapters dated 1950. Crossref 10.1007/978-1-4612-9917-2
