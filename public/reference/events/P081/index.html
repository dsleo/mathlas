≈ 2004 · Number theory

Higher composition laws and arithmetic statistics

Gauss's composition of quadratic forms is extended to cubes and higher spaces via prehomogeneous representations, and geometry of numbers counts the resulting number fields and ranks.

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2004

    First higher composition-law paper published

    key development

    M. Bhargava, Higher composition laws I: A new view on Gauss composition, and quadratic generalizations, Ann. Math. 159 (2004)

    The paper generalizes Gauss composition using higher-dimensional parameterizations.

    Higher composition laws IV: quintic rings

    key development

    Bhargava, Higher composition laws IV: the parametrization of quintic rings, Ann. of Math. (2008)

    Extends the parametrization of rings by composition laws to quintic rings, a main later result of the programme.

    Composition framework applied to elliptic-curve statistics

    key development

    The paper counts quartic forms in a study of average ranks of elliptic curves.
