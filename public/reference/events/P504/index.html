1976 · Combinatorics

Ellipsoid method in combinatorial optimisation

Khachiyan's ellipsoid algorithm is used to prove polynomial solvability of combinatorial problems via separation oracles.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1970

    Shor's space-dilation method

    precursor

    Shor, N. Z., Verwendung der Operation der Raumdehnung in Aufgaben der Minimisierung konvexer Funktionen, Kibernetika (Kiev) 1 (1970) 6-12

    An earlier published space-dilation method for minimising convex functions, which Wikipedia names as a forerunner; whether it is the same idea as the ellipsoid method is not established here.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH: Shor, 'Verwendung der Operation der Raumdehnung...', Kibernetika 1970, No. 1, 6-12 zbMATH Open 3385445
  2. 1976

    Ellipsoid-type methods for convex problems

    first statement

    Yudin, D. B., Nemirovskii, A. S., Informational complexity and efficient methods for the solution of convex extremal problems, 1976

    The published treatment of ellipsoid-type methods for convex extremal problems, the earliest verified publication in this line.

    Evidence (2)
    • Informational complexity and effective methods for the solution of convex extremal problems, 1976 zbMATH record 3516928
    • Nemirovski and Yudin studied an approximation algorithm for convex minimisation in 1972 Wikipedia, Ellipsoid method
  3. 1979

    Polynomial-time linear programming

    first application

    Khachiyan, L. G., A polynomial algorithm in linear programming, Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR, 1979

    Applies the ellipsoid method to linear programming and proves polynomial solvability, the first targeted use of the method for complexity.

    Evidence (3)
    • A polynomial algorithm in linear programming, 1979 zbMATH record 3644821
    • The English translation appeared in USSR Computational Mathematics and Mathematical Physics in 1980 Crossref record
    • Khachiyan's four-page February 1979 paper gave a polynomial implementation Wikipedia, Leonid Khachiyan
  4. 1981

    Ellipsoid method and combinatorial optimisation

    key development

    Grotschel, M., Lovasz, L., Schrijver, A., The ellipsoid method and its consequences in combinatorial optimization, Combinatorica, 1981

    Shows that the ellipsoid method with separation oracles yields polynomial solvability of many combinatorial optimisation problems.

    Evidence (2)
    • The ellipsoid method and its consequences in combinatorial optimization, Combinatorica, 1981 Crossref record
    • The authors received the Fulkerson Prize for this paper MacTutor biography of Laszlo Lovasz
  5. 1988

    Book on geometric algorithms

    consolidation

    Grotschel, M., Lovasz, L., Schrijver, A., Geometric Algorithms and Combinatorial Optimization, Springer, 1988

    The monograph that systematises the ellipsoid method and its applications to combinatorial optimisation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Geometric Algorithms and Combinatorial Optimization, Springer, 1988 Open Library record
