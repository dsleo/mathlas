1971 · Category & homological algebra

Elementary topos theory

Categories with subobject classifiers served as generalised universes of sets.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1964

    Elementary theory of the category of sets

    precursor

    F. William Lawvere, An elementary theory of the category of sets, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 1964

    Lawvere axiomatises the category of sets in categorical terms, the first step toward elementary topoi.

    Dating or attribution note

    Precursor: the category of sets, not yet a topos. The definition of the elementary topos (Lawvere-Tierney) is not verified in a record, so no first_statement is set.

    Evidence (1)
    • PNAS paper 'An elementary theory of the category of sets', 1964 Crossref
  2. 1971

    Lawvere announces the axioms of an elementary topos

    first statement

    F. W. Lawvere, Quantifiers and sheaves, Actes du Congres International des Mathematiciens (Nice, 1970), Tome 1, Gauthier-Villars, Paris, 1971, pp. 329-334

    Lawvere's ICM lecture defines a topos by finite limits and colimits, cartesian closure and a subobject classifier, with no reference to sites; it credits joint work with Tierney.

    Evidence (3)
    • The paper defines a topos by finite limits, colimits, cartesian closure and subobject classifier and frames topologies as a modal operator. Lawvere, Quantifiers and sheaves (PDF on nLab)
    • zbMATH dates the paper 1971. zbMATH Open document 3411260
    • Wikipedia attributes the axioms with a subobject classifier to Lawvere and Tierney. Wikipedia, History of topos theory
  3. 1972

    'Topos' in SGA 4

    naming

    A. Grothendieck, J.-L. Verdier, Topos, in Theorie des topos et cohomologie etale des schemas (SGA 4), Lecture Notes in Mathematics, 1972

    Grothendieck's topos (category of sheaves on a site) is the source of the term used for the elementary notion.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: chapter 'Topos' by Grothendieck and Verdier in Lecture Notes in Mathematics (SGA 4), 1972 Crossref
  4. 1977

    Topos Theory monograph

    consolidation

    Peter T. Johnstone, Topos Theory, Academic Press, 1977

    Johnstone's monograph gives the first systematic treatment of elementary topos theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: 'Topos theory' by Johnstone, Academic Press, first publication 1977 Open Library
  5. 1992

    Sheaves in Geometry and Logic

    consolidation

    Saunders Mac Lane, Ieke Moerdijk, Sheaves in Geometry and Logic, Springer, 1992

    A standard textbook on sheaves and toposes that brings the logical and geometric sides together.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: 'Sheaves in geometry and logic' by Mac Lane, Springer, 1992 Open Library
