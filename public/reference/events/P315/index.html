2001 · Algorithms & complexity

Geometric complexity theory

Mulmuley-Sohoni proposed attacking P versus NP via orbit closures and representation theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2001

    Geometric complexity theory programme proposed

    first statement

    K. Mulmuley and M. Sohoni, Geometric Complexity Theory I: An Approach to the P vs. NP and Related Problems, SIAM J. Comput. 31 (2001)

    The paper proposes a representation-theoretic geometry programme for P versus NP and related complexity questions.

    Evidence (1)
    • SIAM record identifies the named 2001 programme paper. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2007

    Graduate lecture notes on GCT

    consolidation

    K. Mulmuley and M. Sohoni, Geometric Complexity Theory: Introduction, arXiv:0709.0746 (2007)

    Public lecture notes systematically introduce the GCT programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the lecture notes to 2007. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2008

    GCT II: explicit obstructions

    key development

    Mulmuley, K. D., Sohoni, M., Geometric Complexity Theory II: Towards explicit obstructions for embeddings among class varieties, SIAM J. Comput. (2008)

    Lays out the explicit-obstruction program of GCT, the concrete route the programme later pursued.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, SIAM J. Comput. (2008) Crossref
  4. 2011

    Mulmuley's survey on P vs NP and GCT

    consolidation

    Mulmuley, K. D., On P vs. NP and geometric complexity theory, Journal of the ACM (2011)

    An expository account of the GCT programme and its goals for the P versus NP problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, JACM (2011) Crossref
