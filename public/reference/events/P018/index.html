The probabilistic method in combinatorics

Existence of a combinatorial object is proved by showing a random object has the property with positive probability.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1943

    Szele's tournament argument

    precursor

    T. Szele, Kombinatorische Untersuchungen über den gerichteten vollständigen Graphen (1943)

    A prior random-choice existence argument anticipates the later combinatorial method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Szele, Kombinatorische Untersuchungen ueber den gerichteten vollstaendigen Graphen, Mat. Fiz. Lapok 1943 zbMATH Open record
  2. 1947

    Erdős's graph existence proof

    first statement

    P. Erdős, Some remarks on the theory of graphs, Bull. AMS 53 (1947)

    A published paper makes the random-construction method explicit for graph existence.

    Evidence (1)
    • Erdős's graph paper appeared in the Bulletin in 1947. AMS original article record
  3. 1947

    Random graphs give Ramsey bounds

    first application

    P. Erdős, Some remarks on the theory of graphs, Bull. AMS 53 (1947)

    A random graph argument proves a Ramsey-type existence bound.

    Evidence (1)
    • Erdos, Some remarks on the theory of graphs, Bull. AMS 53 (1947) 292-294 Crossref record
  4. 1974

    Probabilistic Methods in Combinatorics

    consolidation

    P. Erdős and J. Spencer, Probabilistic Methods in Combinatorics (1974)

    A named monograph consolidates the method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Erdos and Spencer, Probabilistic Methods in Combinatorics (book), 1974 zbMATH Open record
