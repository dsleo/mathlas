1910 · Topology

Brouwer topology

Brouwer used simplicial approximation and degree to prove invariance of dimension and the fixed-point theorem.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1857

    Riemann uses the analysis situs name in a title

    precursor

    Riemann, B., Lehrsatze aus der analysis situs fur die Theorie der Integrale von zweigliedrigen vollstandigen Differentialen, 1857

    The earliest zbMATH title found that contains "analysis situs", the name of the field before "topology".

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for the 1857 Riemann paper with analysis situs in the title zbMATH Open doc 2750362
  2. 1904

    Bohl proves the three-dimensional case

    precursor

    Piers Bohl, Ueber die Bewegung eines mechanischen Systems in der Naehe einer Gleichgewichtslage, Crelle 127, 1904

    Early fixed-point result for the three-dimensional ball, arising from stability of mechanical systems.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Crelle 127 (1904), pp. 179-276. Crossref 10.1515/crll.1904.127.179
    • The n=3 case was first proved by Bohl in 1904. Wikipedia, Brouwer fixed-point theorem
  3. 1910

    Brouwer's fixed point on surfaces

    first statement

    Brouwer, L. E. J., Uber eineindeutige, stetige Transformationen von Flachen in sich, Mathematische Annalen (1910)

    Brouwer's 1910 paper proves a fixed-point result for homeomorphisms of surfaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper with this title, journal and year Crossref
    • Says Brouwer proved the n=3 case in 1909 and Hadamard the general case in 1910; Brouwer's general continuous case is dated 1911. Wikipedia, Brouwer fixed-point theorem
  4. 1910

    Hadamard proves the differentiable case for balls

    key development

    Jacques Hadamard, appendix to Tannery, Introduction a la theorie des fonctions, 1910

    Proves the fixed-point theorem for differentiable maps of the ball in any dimension using the Kronecker index.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hadamard proved the case of differentiable maps of the closed ball in 1910. Wikipedia, Brouwer fixed-point theorem
  5. 1911

    Invariance of dimension

    first application

    Brouwer, L. E. J., Beweis der Invarianz der Dimensionenzahl, Mathematische Annalen (1911)

    Brouwer uses simplicial approximation to prove invariance of dimension.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper with this title, journal and year Crossref
  6. 1912

    Degree and maps of manifolds

    key development

    Brouwer, L. E. J., Uber Abbildung von Mannigfaltigkeiten, Mathematische Annalen (1912)

    Brouwer's 1912 paper develops the degree of maps and the general fixed-point theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper with this title, journal and year Crossref
  7. 1930

    Schauder's fixed-point theorem in function spaces

    key development

    Juliusz Schauder, Der Fixpunktsatz in Funktionalraeumen, Studia Math. 2, 1930

    Extends Brouwer's theorem to Banach spaces, opening nonlinear analysis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Studia Math. 2 (1930), pp. 171-180. Crossref 10.4064/sm-2-1-171-180
    • Brouwer's theorem was generalised to Banach spaces in 1930. Wikipedia, Brouwer fixed-point theorem
  8. 1935

    Alexandroff and Hopf publish the first volume of Topologie

    consolidation

    Alexandroff, P., Hopf, H., Topologie I, 1935

    Monograph treatment of combinatorial and general topology that covers fixed-point and degree methods.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists Topologie I with first publication year 1935 Open Library works OL21235036W
