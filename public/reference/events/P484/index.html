1964 · Algebra

Deformation theory of rings and algebras (Gerstenhaber)

Kodaira-Spencer deformation ideas are carried to associative algebras, with Hochschild cohomology classifying infinitesimal deformations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1945

    Hochschild cohomology of associative algebras

    precursor

    Hochschild, On the cohomology groups of an associative algebra, Annals of Math. 46 (1945)

    Hochschild defines the cohomology of associative algebras that later classifies infinitesimal deformations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hochschild 1945, Annals, DOI 10.2307/1969145 Crossref
  2. 1958

    Kodaira-Spencer deformations of complex structures

    precursor

    Kodaira, Spencer, On deformations of complex analytic structures I, Annals of Math. 67 (1958)

    The deformation theory of complex structures, the model that Gerstenhaber transfers to algebras.

    Evidence (2)
    • Kodaira-Spencer I, Annals 1958, DOI 10.2307/1970009 Crossref
    • Kodaira-Spencer deformation theory of complex structures is the model for algebra deformations Crossref title record (complex structures)
  3. 1963

    Gerstenhaber's cohomology structure

    precursor

    Gerstenhaber, The cohomology structure of an associative ring, Annals of Math. 78 (1963)

    Gerstenhaber shows that Hochschild cohomology carries a rich algebraic structure (the bracket), which the deformation theory uses.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gerstenhaber 1963, Annals, DOI 10.2307/1970343 Crossref
  4. 1964

    Deformations of rings and algebras defined

    first statement

    Gerstenhaber, On the deformation of rings and algebras, Annals of Math. 79 (1964)

    The first public statement of deformations of associative algebras classified by Hochschild cohomology.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gerstenhaber 1964, Annals, DOI 10.2307/1970484 Crossref
  5. 1964

    Hochschild cohomology classifies infinitesimal deformations

    first application

    Gerstenhaber 1964 (as above)

    The same 1964 paper first applies Hochschild cohomology to classify deformations, the target problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gerstenhaber 1964, Annals, DOI 10.2307/1970484 Crossref
  6. 1964

    Gerstenhaber, 'On the deformation of rings and algebras'

    naming

    M. Gerstenhaber, 'On the deformation of rings and algebras', Annals of Mathematics 79 (1964), DOI 10.2307/1970484

    Gerstenhaber's 1964 Annals paper names the field in its title: deformations of rings and algebras.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record gives title 'On the Deformation of Rings and Algebras', author Gerstenhaber, container The Annals of Mathematics, year 1964 Crossref, DOI 10.2307/1970484
  7. 1968

    Deformations of rings and algebras III

    key development

    Gerstenhaber, On the deformation of rings and algebras: III, Annals of Math. 88 (1968)

    A continuation of the series that extends the deformation theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content of part III was not read; recorded only as a continuation in the series.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gerstenhaber III, Annals 1968, DOI 10.2307/1970553 Crossref
