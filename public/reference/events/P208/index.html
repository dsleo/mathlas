1938 · Algorithms & complexity

Shannon switching circuits

Boole's algebra was applied to analyse and simplify relay circuits.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1854

    Boole's algebra of logic

    precursor

    Boole, An Investigation of the Laws of Thought, 1854

    Boole's algebra of logic is the algebra that Shannon applied to relay circuits.

    Dating or attribution note

    Earlier Boole (1847) work not checked; precursor only.

    Evidence (2)
    • Boole, An Investigation of the Laws of Thought (1854) Open Library search record (first publish year 1854)
    • Shannon's master's thesis used Boole's algebra to analyse and optimise relay switching circuits MacTutor, Claude Shannon biography
  2. 1938

    Shannon's symbolic analysis of relay and switching circuits

    first statement

    Shannon, A symbolic analysis of relay and switching circuits, Electrical Engineering 57 (1938) 713-723

    Shannon stated the switching algebra and used it to analyse relay circuits.

    Evidence (2)
    • Shannon, A symbolic analysis of relay and switching circuits, Electrical Engineering 57 (1938) 713-723 Crossref DOI 10.1109/ee.1938.6431064
    • Shannon's master's thesis is the symbolic analysis of relay and switching circuits MacTutor, Claude Shannon biography
  3. 1938

    Boolean algebra used to analyse and simplify relay circuits

    first application

    Shannon, A symbolic analysis of relay and switching circuits, Electrical Engineering 57 (1938) 713-723

    Boole's algebra was used to analyse and simplify relay circuits, the first application of the switching algebra.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same paper as the first_statement milestone (see above). Independent Soviet work by Shestakov (1941, per Wikipedia) is not counted; Nakashima's earlier Japanese work was not checked.

    Evidence (2)
    • Shannon, A symbolic analysis of relay and switching circuits, Electrical Engineering 57 (1938) 713-723 Crossref DOI 10.1109/ee.1938.6431064
    • Shannon used Boole's algebra to analyse and simplify relay switching circuits MacTutor, Claude Shannon biography
  4. 1949

    Shannon's synthesis of two-terminal switching circuits

    key development

    Shannon, The Synthesis of Two-Terminal Switching Circuits, Bell System Technical Journal 28 (1949) 59-98

    Shannon gave a method for synthesising switching circuits from a given function, not only analysing them.

    Evidence (2)
    • Shannon, The Synthesis of Two-Terminal Switching Circuits, Bell System Technical Journal 28 (1949) 59-98 Crossref DOI 10.1002/j.1538-7305.1949.tb03624.x
    • Shannon worked on synthesis and simplification of relay switching circuits after 1938 MacTutor, Claude Shannon biography
  5. 1958

    Caldwell's Switching Circuits and Logical Design

    consolidation

    Caldwell, Switching Circuits and Logical Design, Wiley (1958)

    A book-length exposition of switching circuits and logical design, treating the subject as a design discipline.

    Evidence (1)
    • Caldwell, Switching Circuits and Logical Design, Wiley (1958) Open Library search record (first publish year 1958)
