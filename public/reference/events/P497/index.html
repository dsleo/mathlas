KP hierarchy and the infinite Grassmannian (Sato)

Soliton hierarchies are viewed as flows on an infinite-dimensional Grassmannian, tying integrable equations to representation theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1970

    KP equation written down

    precursor

    Kadomtsev and Petviashvili write the KP equation as a 2+1-dimensional generalisation of KdV, the equation Sato's theory later explains.

    Evidence (1)
    • The KP equation was first written in 1970 by Kadomtsev and Petviashvili Wikipedia: Kadomtsev-Petviashvili equation
  2. 1981

    Soliton equations as dynamical systems on the infinite Grassmannian

    first statement

    Sato, M., Soliton equations as dynamical systems on infinite dimensional Grassmann manifolds, RIMS Kokyuroku 439 (1981) 30-46

    Sato reads soliton hierarchies as flows on an infinite-dimensional Grassmannian, the central idea of the event.

    Evidence (4)
    • zbMATH: Sato, 'Soliton equations as dynamical systems on an infinite dimensional Grassmann manifolds', RIMS Kokyuroku 439 (1981) 30-46 zbMATH Open 3800630
    • RIMS Kokyuroku 439 contents page lists Sato's paper at page 30 (symposium of January 1981) RIMS Kokyuroku No. 439 contents
    • Book chapter in North-Holland Mathematics Studies, 1983 (DOI 10.1016/s0304-0208(08)72096-6) Crossref record
    • Sato's soliton work with the use of infinite-dimensional Grassmannians Wikipedia: Mikio Sato
  3. 1982

    Transformation groups for soliton equations

    first application

    Date, E., Jimbo, M., Kashiwara, M., Miwa, T., Transformation groups for soliton equations, Physica D 4, 1982

    The Physica D paper applies the transformation-group picture to soliton equations including KP, the first purposeful use of the Sato picture.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in Physica D, 1982 (DOI 10.1016/0167-2789(82)90041-0) Crossref record
  4. 1982

    Date, Jimbo, Kashiwara and colleagues title a paper with the KP hierarchy

    naming

    Date, E., Jimbo, M., Kashiwara, M., et al., Transformation groups for soliton equations. Euclidean Lie algebras and reduction of the KP hierarchy, 1982

    Earliest zbMATH title found that uses the KP hierarchy name.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record with KP hierarchy in the title, first author Date, year 1982 zbMATH Open doc 3910949
  5. 1985

    Loop groups and equations of KdV type

    key development

    Segal, G., Wilson, G., Loop groups and equations of KdV type, Publ. Math. IHÉS 61, 1985

    Segal and Wilson give a loop-group and Grassmannian account of KdV-type equations, the main extension of the Sato picture.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in Publ. Math. IHÉS, 1985 (DOI 10.1007/bf02698802) Crossref record
