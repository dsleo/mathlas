1958 · Category & homological algebra

Kodaira-Spencer deformation theory

Deformations of complex structures were studied by cohomology.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1958

    Deformations of complex analytic structures I

    first statement

    Kodaira and Spencer, On deformations of complex analytic structures I, Ann. of Math. 67 (1958) 328-401

    Kodaira and Spencer set up the deformation theory of complex structures using sheaf cohomology.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: On Deformations of Complex Analytic Structures I, Annals of Mathematics, 1958 Crossref
  2. 1958

    Cohomological method applied to deformations

    first application

    Kodaira and Spencer, On deformations of complex analytic structures I (1958)

    The same paper applies the cohomological method to deformations of compact complex structures.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia Spencer: deformation theory of structures from differential geometry; zbMATH record of the 1958 paper zbMATH
  3. 1958

    Existence of deformations of complex analytic structures

    key development

    Kodaira, Nirenberg and Spencer, On the existence of deformations of complex analytic structures, Ann. of Math. 68 (1958) 450-459

    The existence theorem shows that every small deformation of a compact complex structure is realised, which made the theory matter.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: On the Existence of Deformations of Complex Analytic Structures, Annals of Mathematics, 1958 Crossref
  4. 1960

    Stability theorems for complex structures

    key development

    Kodaira and Spencer, On deformations of complex analytic structures III: stability theorems, Ann. of Math. 71 (1960) 43-76

    Stability theorems show that small deformations of a stable complex structure keep the structure, the main extension of the programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for part III, Annals of Mathematics 71 (1960) zbMATH
  5. 1986

    Monograph on complex manifolds and deformations

    consolidation

    Kodaira, Complex Manifolds and Deformation of Complex Structures, Springer (1986; later English reprint 2006)

    A systematic treatment of the theory after the 1958-60 papers.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: Springer-Verlag 1986 edition under the Japanese title, and Springer London 2006 English reprint Open Library
