1928 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Hilbert-space quantum mechanics

Matrix and wave mechanics were unified in the Hilbert-space framework by Weyl, Dirac and von Neumann.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1912

    Hilbert's general theory of linear integral equations

    precursor

    D. Hilbert, Grundzuege einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen, Teubner, 1912

    Hilbert's spectral theory of integral equations supplied the infinite-dimensional tool later used in quantum mechanics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Gronwall's 1914 Bulletin AMS review of the 1912 book exists Crossref DOI 10.1090/s0002-9904-1914-02494-2
    • MacTutor Hilbert biography: his integral equation work led to functional analysis MacTutor Hilbert biography
  2. 1928

    Hilbert, Nordheim and von Neumann on the foundations of quantum mechanics

    first statement

    D. Hilbert, L. Nordheim, J. von Neumann, 'Ueber die Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik', Math. Ann. 98, 1928

    The paper set out quantum mechanics with Hilbert space and self-adjoint operators as the public mathematical framework.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hilbert-Nordheim-von Neumann Math. Ann. paper exists with this title and year 1928 Crossref DOI 10.1007/bf01451579
  3. 1928

    Hilbert-space framework applied to quantum theory

    first application

    D. Hilbert, L. Nordheim, J. von Neumann, 'Ueber die Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik', Math. Ann. 98, 1928

    The same paper applied the Hilbert-space and operator framework to the physical theory of quantum mechanics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same paper as the first statement Crossref DOI 10.1007/bf01451579
  4. 1930

    Dirac's transformation theory of quantum states

    key development

    P. A. M. Dirac, The Principles of Quantum Mechanics, Clarendon Press, 1930

    Dirac's treatment of quantum states as abstract vectors and transformations extended the Hilbert-space framework to a physics textbook.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists the book with first publication 1930 Open Library search
    • Lennard-Jones's 1931 Mathematical Gazette review gives 1930 Oxford Crossref DOI 10.2307/3605591
  5. 1932

    von Neumann's Mathematische Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik

    consolidation

    J. von Neumann, Mathematische Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik, Springer, 1932

    Von Neumann's monograph put quantum theory on a rigorous Hilbert-space basis and became the standard systematic treatment.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists the book with first publication 1932 (Springer) Open Library search
    • MacTutor says von Neumann put quantum theory on a firm theoretical basis in 1932 MacTutor Quantum age topic (saved text)
