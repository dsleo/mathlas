1799 · Algebra

Impossibility of solving the general quintic by radicals

Ruffini used permutation groups and Abel gave a complete proof of unsolvability of quintic.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1770

    Lagrange's analysis of methods for solving equations

    precursor

    J.-L. Lagrange, Reflexions sur la resolution algebrique des equations, Nouv. Mem. Acad. Berlin, c. 1770-71

    Lagrange unified known solution methods through resolvents and permutations of roots, hinting that degree five might be out of reach.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia dates Lagrange's groundwork to around 1770 and calls it a precursor of Galois theory without a proof of impossibility. English Wikipedia, Abel-Ruffini theorem (History)
    • MacTutor stresses Lagrange still hoped to solve the quintic and that Ruffini built on his reflections. MacTutor, Ruffini biography
  2. 1799

    Ruffini's Teoria generale delle equazioni

    first statement

    Paolo Ruffini, Teoria generale delle equazioni, in cui si dimostra impossibile la soluzione algebraica delle equazioni generali di grado superiore al quarto, Bologna, 1799

    Ruffini published the first claimed proof that the general equation of degree above four has no solution by radicals; the proof had a gap.

    Evidence (3)
    • Library record for the book, Bologna 1799. Internet Archive item teoriageneraled00ruffgoog
    • MacTutor: Ruffini's 1799 book claimed unsolvability of the quintic by radicals; one gap remains. MacTutor, Ruffini biography
    • Wikipedia: first nearly proved by Ruffini in 1799. English Wikipedia, Abel-Ruffini theorem
  3. 1799

    Permutation-group reasoning applied to the quintic

    first application

    Paolo Ruffini, Teoria generale delle equazioni, 1799

    The same book first used order, cycle structure, conjugacy and primitivity of permutation groups to attack an equation-solving question.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor states Ruffini used group theory, having to invent it, and lists the notions he introduced. MacTutor, Ruffini biography
  4. 1813–1815

    Ruffini's later proofs and Cauchy's permutation work

    key development

    P. Ruffini, further proofs 1808 and 1813; A.-L. Cauchy, memoirs on permutations c. 1813-1815

    Ruffini refined the argument and Cauchy, influenced by him, developed permutation groups and endorsed the result.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: further proofs in 1808 and 1813; the 1813 one resembles what later was called Wantzel's modification; Cauchy wrote on permutation groups 1813-1815 and called the proof complete. MacTutor, Ruffini biography
  5. 1824

    Abel's privately printed proof

    key development

    N. H. Abel, Memoire sur les equations algebriques ou l'on demontre l'impossibilite de la resolution de l'equation generale du cinquieme degre, Christiania, 1824

    Abel proved the impossibility for the general fifth-degree equation in a short pamphlet printed at his own expense.

    Evidence (3)
    • MacTutor: in 1824 he proved the impossibility, publishing in French at his own expense. MacTutor, Abel biography
    • Wikipedia gives Abel a proof in 1824. English Wikipedia, Abel-Ruffini theorem
    • A German translation of this title appears in an 1889 collection (confirms the title, not the 1824 date). Crossref DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-52006-8_1
  6. 1826

    Abel's proof in Crelle's Journal

    key development

    N. H. Abel, Beweis der Unmoglichkeit, algebraische Gleichungen von hoheren Graden als dem vierten allgemein aufzulosen, J. reine angew. Math. 1 (1826) 65-84

    A clearer version of the proof appeared in the first volume of Crelle's new journal, making it widely accessible.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Journal fur die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 1826, pages 65-84. Crossref DOI 10.1515/crll.1826.1.65
    • MacTutor: Crelle encouraged a clearer version, which appeared in his journal. MacTutor, Abel biography
  7. 1845

    Wantzel's simplified proof

    key development

    P. L. Wantzel, new proof of the impossibility of solving all algebraic equations by radicals, 1845

    Wantzel gave a tighter version of Abel's argument, citing both Abel and Ruffini.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor: in 1845 Wantzel gave a new proof, remarking that Abel's was correct but too complicated and Ruffini's insufficient. MacTutor, Wantzel biography
    • MacTutor Ruffini page says the 1813 Ruffini proof is essentially Wantzel's modification published in 1845. MacTutor, Ruffini biography
  8. 1870

    Jordan publishes a systematic treatise on substitutions and algebraic equations

    consolidation

    Jordan, C., Traite des substitutions et des equations algebriques, 1870

    Monograph that develops group-theoretic methods for algebraic equations, including the unsolvability of the general quintic.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists the treatise with first publication year 1870 Open Library works OL228903W
