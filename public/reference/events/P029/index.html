1973 · Number theory

Random matrix statistics and the zeros of zeta

The pair correlation of zeta zeros matches that of eigenvalues of random unitary matrices, suggesting a spectral interpretation of the zeros.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Mehta's random-matrix statistics

    precursor

    Mehta, Random Matrices, Academic Press (1967)

    The statistical theory of eigenvalue spacings in random matrix ensembles, the background into which the zeta correlation fits.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: Madan Lal Mehta, Random Matrices, Elsevier, first published 1967 Open Library
  2. 1973

    Montgomery's pair correlation of zeta zeros

    first statement

    Montgomery, The pair correlation of zeros of the zeta function, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 24 (1973)

    The public statement of the conjectural pair-correlation formula for zeros of the Riemann zeta function.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics, 1973, The pair correlation of zeros of the zeta function Crossref
  3. 1987

    Odlyzko's numerical spacings of zeta zeros

    first application

    Odlyzko, On the distribution of spacings between zeros of the zeta function, Math. Comp. 48 (1987)

    Large-scale numerical computation of zero spacings confirms the random-matrix (GUE) prediction, the first purposeful use of the correspondence on data.

    Dating or attribution note

    The numerical-confirmation claim is from the paper's subject; the abstract was not read in full.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Mathematics of Computation, 1987 Crossref
  4. 1996

    Rudnick-Sarnak n-level correlations for L-functions

    key development

    Rudnick, Sarnak, Zeros of principal L-functions and random matrix theory, Duke Math. J. 81 (1996)

    Extends the random-matrix correspondence from the Riemann zeta function to families of L-functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Duke Mathematical Journal, 1996, Zeros of principal L-functions and random matrix theory Crossref
  5. 1999

    Katz-Sarnak monograph

    consolidation

    Katz, Sarnak, Random Matrices, Frobenius Eigenvalues and Monodromy, AMS Colloquium Publications 45 (1999)

    Systematic monograph linking zeros of L-functions over function fields with random-matrix ensembles.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref: AMS Colloquium Publications volume 45, DOI 10.1090/coll/045 (Crossref dates items 1998) Crossref
    • zbMATH: Katz and Sarnak, Random Matrices, Frobenius Eigenvalues, and Monodromy, AMS Colloquium Publications 45 (1999) zbMATH Open 1242982
    • Open Library: first publication year 1999 for Random Matrices, Frobenius Eigenvalues, and Monodromy Open Library search
  6. Date unknown

    Dyson's remark on the pair correlation

    precursor

    Dyson pointed out to Montgomery that the pair correlation of the zeros matches that of random unitary matrices, an observation not in a dated record.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: Dyson pointed out the connection to Montgomery Wikipedia, Montgomery's pair correlation conjecture
