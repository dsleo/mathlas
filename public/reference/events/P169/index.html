1731 · Differential geometry

Clairaut: curves in space

Analytic geometry and calculus were used to study curves of double curvature.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1729

    Clairaut completes research on double-curvature curves

    precursor

    Clairaut finished his study of space curves in 1729, which led to his proposed election to the Academy.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: he completed research on double curvature curves in 1729 and was proposed for the Paris Academy on 4 September 1729. MacTutor, Clairaut
  2. 1731

    Recherches sur les courbes a double courbure

    first statement

    Clairaut, Recherches sur les courbes a double courbure, Paris: Nyon, 1731

    A treatise applying analytic geometry and calculus to curves in three-dimensional space.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive holds the 1731 Nyon edition. Internet Archive
    • Wikipedia, Alexis Clairaut: treatise on tortuous curves, published 1731, which led to his admission to the Academy. English Wikipedia, Alexis Clairaut
  3. 1767

    Euler on the curvature of surfaces

    key development

    Euler, Recherches sur la courbure des surfaces, Mem. Acad. Berlin 16, 1767 (written 1763), E333

    Euler extended the study from curves in space to the curvature of surfaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • Euler Archive E333: published 1767, written 1763, Berlin Memoires vol. 16. Euler Archive
  4. 1851

    Serret's formulas for curves of double curvature

    key development

    J. A. Serret, Sur quelques formules relatives a la theorie des courbes a double courbure, J. Math. Pures Appl. 16 (1851)

    The Frenet-Serret formulas give a moving frame for space curves; Frenet had them in his 1847 thesis.

    Evidence (1)
    • Formulas discovered independently by Frenet (thesis of 1847) and Serret (1851). English Wikipedia, Frenet-Serret formulas
