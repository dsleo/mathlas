1977 · Algebraic topology

Intersection cohomology

Goresky and MacPherson extend Poincare duality to singular spaces.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1974

    Discovery in private work

    precursor

    Goresky and MacPherson find the idea of intersection homology in the fall of 1974, before any publication.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia Intersection homology: discovered in the fall of 1974 Wikipedia: Intersection homology
  2. 1977

    Poincare duality for singular spaces (note)

    first statement

    Goresky, MacPherson, La dualite de Poincare pour les espaces singuliers, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 284 (1977)

    Short note announcing the Poincare-duality property for intersection homology of singular spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia Intersection homology: references the 1977 Comptes Rendus note Wikipedia: Intersection homology
  3. 1979

    Kazhdan-Lusztig proof uses intersection cohomology

    first application

    Kazhdan, Lusztig, Representations of Coxeter groups and Hecke algebras, Invent. Math. (1979)

    Intersection cohomology is used to prove the Kazhdan-Lusztig conjectures, an early purposeful application.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Intersection homology: IH was used to prove the Kazhdan-Lusztig conjectures Wikipedia: Intersection homology
  4. 1980

    Intersection homology theory

    key development

    Goresky, MacPherson, Intersection homology theory, Topology 19 (1980)

    Full definition of intersection homology with allowable chains, the main development.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  5. 1983

    Intersection homology II: sheaf-theoretic approach

    key development

    Goresky, MacPherson, Intersection homology II, Invent. Math. 72 (1983)

    Gives the sheaf-theoretic approach to intersection cohomology, the form used in later work.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Intersection homology: sheaf-theoretic approach in the 1983 paper Wikipedia: Intersection homology
  6. 1984

    Sheaf-theoretic intersection cohomology (Borel et al.)

    consolidation

    Borel et al., Intersection Cohomology, Progress in Mathematics, Birkhauser (1984)

    The book-length treatment of intersection cohomology in the sheaf-theoretic framework.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chapter record in Crossref: 'Sheaf Theoretic Intersection Cohomology', year 1984 Crossref
