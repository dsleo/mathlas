1867 · Topology

Vortex atoms and the origin of knot tabulation

Thomson's proposal that atoms are knotted vortex tubes motivated Tait's classification of knots and links, helped by Kirkman and Little, giving the first knot tables.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1858

    Helmholtz on vortex motion

    precursor

    H. Helmholtz, Ueber Integrale der hydrodynamischen Gleichungen, welche den Wirbelbewegungen entsprechen, Crelle 55, 1858

    Helmholtz showed vortex filaments in an ideal fluid persist and can form closed rings.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Crelle 55 (1858). Crossref
    • Helmholtz showed vortex cores are filaments forming rings, with no link to matter theory. Wikipedia, Vortex theory of the atom
  2. 1867

    Thomson proposes vortex atoms

    first statement

    W. Thomson, On vortex atoms, Phil. Mag. 34, 1867

    Thomson proposed atoms as stable knotted vortex rings in the aether, so that kinds of knot would give kinds of atom.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Phil. Mag. (4) 34 (1867), 'On vortex atoms'. Crossref
    • Published in Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh in 1867 per the history page. Wikipedia, Vortex theory of the atom
  3. 1877

    Tait's first paper On Knots

    first application

    P. G. Tait, On Knots, Trans. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 28, 1877

    Tait took up the knot question raised by vortex atoms and began enumerating knots.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Trans. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 28 (1877), 'On Knots'. Crossref
    • Tait was drawn in by Thomson's idea and began a classification of knots. MacTutor, Tait biography
  4. 1884

    Kirkman's enumeration of knots

    key development

    T. P. Kirkman, The enumeration, description and construction of knots of fewer than ten crossings, Trans. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 32, 1884

    Kirkman, working with Tait, enumerated knots up to nine and then ten crossings, extending the first knot tables.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Trans. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh 32 (1884). Crossref
    • Kirkman published his first knot paper in 1884 and with Tait produced tables up to 10 crossings. MacTutor, Kirkman biography
  5. 1928

    Alexander's invariants of knots and links

    key development

    J. W. Alexander, Topological invariants of knots and links, Trans. AMS 30, 1928

    Alexander introduced a polynomial invariant, moving knot theory from tabulation towards topology.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Trans. AMS 30 (1928). Crossref
    • Early 20th century topologists used the knot group and Alexander polynomial. Wikipedia, Knot theory
  6. 1932

    Reidemeister's Knotentheorie

    consolidation

    K. Reidemeister, Knotentheorie, Ergebnisse der Mathematik, Berlin: Springer, 1932

    A monograph giving the topological theory of knots under the name Knotentheorie.

    Evidence (1)
    • Reviews in 1933 record the 1932 Springer volume. Crossref (review records)
  7. 1992

    Journal of Knot Theory and Its Ramifications

    institution

    J. Knot Theory Ramifications, vol. 1, 1992

    A dedicated journal for the field appears.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref lists volume 1 (1992). Crossref
