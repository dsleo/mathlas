Kac-Moody algebras

Generalised Cartan matrices gave infinite-dimensional algebras.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1966

    Serre's presentation of finite-dimensional simple Lie algebras

    precursor

    Serre showed that the Chevalley-Harish-Chandra relations present simple Lie algebras from the Cartan matrix, which is the setting the generalisation extends.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia says Serre showed in 1966 that the relations give a defining presentation Wikipedia, Kac-Moody algebra (History)
  2. 1967

    Lie algebras from generalised Cartan matrices

    first statement

    Moody, R. V., Lie algebras associated with generalized Cartan matrices, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 73 (1967) 217-221

    Associates an infinite-dimensional Lie algebra to a Cartan matrix that is no longer positive definite.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. (1967), DOI 10.1090/s0002-9904-1967-11688-4, pages 217-221 Crossref; zbMATH 0154.27303
    • Wikipedia says Kac and Moody independently developed the construction in 1967-68 Wikipedia, Kac-Moody algebra (lead and History)
  3. 1968

    Classification of graded Lie algebras of finite growth

    first application

    Kac, V. G., Simple irreducible graded Lie algebras of finite growth, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR (1968)

    Uses the Cartan-matrix construction on a target problem: classifying graded Lie algebras of finite growth.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper in Mathematics of the USSR-Izvestiya (1968), DOI 10.1070/im1968v002n06abeh000729 Crossref
  4. 1968

    Moody's new class of Lie algebras

    key development

    Moody, R. V., A new class of Lie algebras, J. Algebra (1968), pages 221-230 as indexed by zbMATH

    Gives the full structure of the algebras and shows that the class contains the known examples.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper in J. Algebra (1968), DOI 10.1016/0021-8693(68)90096-3; zbMATH 0191.03005 indexes pages 221-230 Crossref; zbMATH
  5. 1983

    Kac's textbook Infinite dimensional Lie algebras

    consolidation

    Kac, V. G., Infinite Dimensional Lie Algebras, first edition (1983); later edition Cambridge University Press (1990)

    A textbook treatment of Kac-Moody algebras; the first edition year is taken from Open Library.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists 'Infinite dimensional Lie algebras' by Victor G. Kac with first publication 1983 Open Library search
    • Later edition DOI 10.1017/cbo9780511626234 (1990) Crossref
