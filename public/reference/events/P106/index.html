2009 · Mathematical physics

Dynamical systems and spectral theory of Schrodinger operators

Renormalisation and cocycle dynamics are used to describe the spectrum of quasi-periodic Schrodinger operators, solving the Ten Martini type problems.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2005

    Ten Martini problem solved

    precursor

    A. Avila and S. Jitomirskaya, The Ten Martini Problem (2005)

    An earlier dynamical/spectral result concerns the almost Mathieu operator, not the later global theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents ten martini problem solved in 2005 arXiv
  2. 2009

    Global one-frequency theory posted

    first statement

    A. Avila, Global theory of one-frequency Schrodinger operators I: stratified analyticity of the Lyapunov exponent and the boundary of nonuniform hyperbolicity (2009)

    Avila defines a stratified analytic framework for Lyapunov exponents and critical energies.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents global one-frequency theory posted in 2009 arXiv
  3. 2009

    Cocycle dynamics applied to critical spectral energies

    first application

    A. Avila, Global theory of one-frequency Schrodinger operators I: stratified analyticity of the Lyapunov exponent and the boundary of nonuniform hyperbolicity (2009)

    The paper uses complexified cocycle dynamics to show the critical set is typically thin.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents cocycle dynamics applied to critical spectral energies in 2009 arXiv
  4. 2015

    Global theory published in Acta Mathematica

    consolidation

    A. Avila, Global theory of one-frequency Schrödinger operators (2015)

    The journal article gives a later durable publication anchor.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents global theory published in acta mathematica in 2015 publisher / Crossref
