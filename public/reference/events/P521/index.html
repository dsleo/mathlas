1967 · Mathematical physics

Constructive quantum field theory

Quantum fields in low dimensions are built rigorously from Markov random fields and reflection-positivity.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Glimm's Yukawa coupling in two dimensions

    first statement

    Glimm, J., Yukawa coupling of quantum fields in two dimensions I, Communications in Mathematical Physics (1967)

    Constructs a quantum field model rigorously in two spacetime dimensions, the first constructive existence result of this type.

    Dating or attribution note

    Glimm 1967 is the earliest constructive 2D paper found; it is the first_statement here. Whether a prior construction exists was not checked. Nelson's 1966 quartic paper (conference volume) was not verified.

    Evidence (2)
    • Communications in Mathematical Physics record for Glimm's Yukawa paper, year 1967 Crossref
    • Wikipedia CQFT article: Glimm and Jaffe showed that many examples exist for dimension below 4 Wikipedia, Constructive quantum field theory
  2. 1968

    Glimm's boson fields with nonlinear self-interaction

    key development

    Glimm, J., Boson fields with nonlinear self-interaction in two dimensions, Communications in Mathematical Physics 8 (1968)

    Constructs boson fields with a nonlinear self-interaction in two dimensions with the renormalisation carried out rigorously.

    Evidence (1)
    • Communications in Mathematical Physics record for Glimm's 1968 paper Crossref
  3. 1973

    Nelson's construction from Markov fields

    key development

    Nelson, E., Construction of quantum fields from Markoff fields, Journal of Functional Analysis 12 (1973)

    Builds quantum fields in low dimensions from Markov random fields using reflection positivity.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal of Functional Analysis record for Nelson's paper, year 1973 Crossref
  4. 1974

    Simon's monograph on the P(phi)_2 Euclidean field theory

    consolidation

    Simon, B., The P(φ)_2 Euclidean (Quantum) Field Theory, Princeton University Press (1974)

    The first systematic treatment of the two-dimensional constructive theory, as a book.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record for The P(phi)2 Euclidean quantum field theory, first published 1974 Open Library
