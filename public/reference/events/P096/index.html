1975 · Number theory

Superrigidity and arithmeticity of lattices

Ergodic-theoretic arguments on homogeneous spaces force lattices in higher rank semisimple groups to be arithmetic, tying Lie theory, ergodic theory and number theory.

  1. 1975

    Margulis's superrigidity theorem announced

    first statement

    Margulis announces the superrigidity theorem for lattices in higher-rank semisimple groups.

  2. 1978

    Quotient groups and measure theory (superrigidity paper)

    key development

    Margulis, Quotient groups of discrete subgroups and measure theory, Funct. Anal. Appl. (1978)

    The published treatment of superrigidity via measure theory and quotient groups.

  3. 1984

    Arithmeticity of irreducible lattices

    key development

    Margulis, Arithmeticity of the irreducible lattices in the semi-simple groups of rank greater than 1, Invent. Math. (1984)

    Shows that irreducible lattices in higher-rank semisimple groups are arithmetic, the arithmeticity half of the event.

    Monograph: Ergodic Theory and Semisimple Groups

    consolidation

    Zimmer, Ergodic Theory and Semisimple Groups, Birkhauser (1984)

    Systematic account of superrigidity and arithmeticity via ergodic theory, the first textbook treatment.

  5. 1991

    Margulis's monograph on discrete subgroups

    consolidation

    Margulis, Discrete Subgroups of Semisimple Lie Groups, Springer (1991)

    Full monograph treatment of superrigidity, arithmeticity and related rigidity theorems.

  6. 1992

    p-adic superrigidity via harmonic maps

    key development

    Gromov, Schoen, Harmonic maps into singular spaces and p-adic superrigidity for lattices in groups of rank one, Publ. Math. IHES 76 (1992)

    Extends superrigidity to p-adic targets and rank-one groups using harmonic maps.
