1165 · Algebra

Sharaf al-Din al-Tusi

Existence of roots of cubics was analysed through maxima of polynomials.

Also known as: Sharaf al-Din al-Muzaffar al-Tusi

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1070–1074

    Khayyam's conic treatment of cubics

    precursor

    Omar Khayyam, Treatise on Demonstration of Problems of Algebra

    Geometric classification of cubics that Sharaf al-Din's algebraic approach contrasts with.

    Evidence (1)
    • Khayyam's treatise classified cubics with conic solutions. MacTutor, Khayyam biography
  2. 1165–1213

    Sharaf al-Din al-Tusi, Treatise on equations

    first statement

    Sharaf al-Din al-Tusi, al-Mu'adalat (Treatise on equations)

    Treated 25 types of equation of degree at most three and decided the existence of positive roots of cubics through maxima of the polynomial.

    Evidence (3)
    • Equations of degree at most three divided into 25 types; maximum of bx - x^3 found at x = sqrt(b/3), giving a discriminant condition. MacTutor, Al-Tusi Sharaf biography
    • He gave the maximum of the cubic for five types and concluded the number of positive solutions. English Wikipedia, Sharaf al-Din al-Tusi
    • Hogendijk analyses how Sharaf al-Din counts positive roots of cubics. Crossref 10.1016/0315-0860(89)90099-2, Hist. Math. 16 (1989)
  3. 1974

    Rashed's study linking Sharaf al-Din and Viete

    recognition

    Roshdi Rashed, Resolution des equations numeriques et algebre: Saraf-al-Din al-Tusi, Viete, Arch. Hist. Exact Sci. 12 (1974)

    Modern recognition of the treatise's numerical method and its relation to later European algebra.

    Evidence (1)
    • Rashed 1974, 244-290. zbMATH 3531316
  4. 1989

    Hogendijk on positive roots of cubics

    recognition

    Jan P. Hogendijk, Hist. Math. 16 (1989) 69-85

    Reassessment of what the treatise shows about roots and the discriminant.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hogendijk 1989. zbMATH 4099214
