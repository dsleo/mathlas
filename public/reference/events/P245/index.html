1935 · Combinatorics

Matroid theory and combinatorial geometry

Whitney's abstraction of dependence unites linear algebra and graphs.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1935

    Whitney's axioms for linear dependence

    first statement

    Whitney, 'On the abstract properties of linear dependence', American Journal of Mathematics 57 (1935)

    Whitney gave the independence axioms that define a matroid and noted that they abstract both linear dependence and graph dependence.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record: Am. J. Math. 1935 Crossref
    • Whitney introduced matroids in the American Journal of Mathematics in 1935 MacTutor: Tutte biography
  2. 1935

    The term 'matroid' coined

    naming

    Same paper: Whitney (1935)

    Whitney called any structure satisfying his axioms a matroid, the first use of the name.

    Evidence (2)
    • Whitney defined structures satisfying the axioms to be matroids Wikipedia: Matroid (history)
    • Paper record for the coinage Crossref
  3. 1935

    Nakasawa's first paper on linear-dependence axioms

    precursor

    T. Nakasawa, Zur Axiomatik der linearen Abhängigkeit I, Sci. Rep. Tokyo Bunrika Daigaku A 2 (1935) 235-255

    Nakasawa published matroid-type axioms independently in 1935, with parts II and III in 1936; as a precursor it sets no derived date.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Zur Axiomatik der linearen Abhängigkeit I, Sci. Rep. Tokyo Bunrika Daigaku A 2 (1935) 235-255 zbMATH record for Nakasawa I
    • Nakasawa independently discovered matroids; his work was forgotten Wikipedia: Matroid (history)
  4. 1958

    Tutte's homotopy theorem and excluded minors

    key development

    Tutte, 'A Homotopy Theorem for Matroids I and II', Transactions of the AMS 88 (1958)

    Tutte's homotopy theorem and his excluded-minor characterisations of binary, regular and graphic matroids gave the subject its main tools.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper records: Trans. AMS 1958 I and II Crossref
    • Tutte's characterisations and the homotopy theorem in the 1950s Wikipedia: Matroid (history)
  5. 1976

    Welsh's Matroid Theory

    consolidation

    Welsh, Matroid Theory (Academic Press, 1976)

    A book-length systematic treatment of matroid theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: 'Matroid theory' by D. J. A. Welsh, first published 1976 Open Library search
