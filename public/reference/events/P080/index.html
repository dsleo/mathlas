1946 · Differential equations

Landau damping: plasma physics to nonlinear PDE

A prediction of plasma physics from 1946 is proved for the nonlinear Vlasov equation in Gevrey regularity, using analytic norms and a new treatment of echoes.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1946

    Landau's vibrations of the electronic plasma

    first statement

    Landau, L. D., On the vibrations of the electronic plasma, J. Phys. USSR 10 (1946) 25-34, zbMATH 0063.03439

    Predicts that plasma waves damp without collisions, through resonant particle interaction.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH record: 'On the vibrations of the electronic plasma', J. Phys. USSR 10 (1946) zbMATH API (Zbl 0063.03439)
    • zbMATH record of the same paper in Zh. Eksp. Teor. Fiz. 16 (1946) 574-586, Zbl 0063.03438 zbMATH API (Zbl 0063.03438)
    • Wikipedia: Landau damping is a mechanism of non-collisional damping; named after Landau Wikipedia: Landau damping
  2. 1961

    Montgomery and Gorman, 'Landau damping to all orders'

    naming

    D. Montgomery, D. Gorman, Landau damping to all orders, Phys. Rev. (2) 124 (1961) 1309-1313

    The earliest zbMATH title found that uses the term 'Landau damping', published in 1961.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'Landau damping to all orders', Montgomery and Gorman, Phys. Rev. 124 (1961) 1309-1313, Zbl 0106.43802 zbMATH Open API
  3. 1962

    Lynden-Bell's analogue in galactic dynamics

    key development

    Lynden-Bell, D., The stability and vibrations of a gas of stars, Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. (1962), as cited in Wikipedia

    Argues that a similar damping mechanism occurs in galactic dynamics, which shows the idea is general.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: Lynden-Bell argued a similar phenomenon occurs in galactic dynamics (reference year 1962) Wikipedia: Landau damping
  4. 1964

    Malmberg and Wharton's experimental confirmation

    key development

    Malmberg, J. H., Wharton, C. B., Collisionless damping of electrostatic plasma waves, Phys. Rev. Lett. 13 (1964) 184, doi 10.1103/physrevlett.13.184

    Confirms experimentally that Landau damping exists, almost two decades after the prediction.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'Collisionless Damping of Electrostatic Plasma Waves', PRL, 1964 Crossref API
    • Wikipedia: proved to exist experimentally by Malmberg and Wharton in 1964 Wikipedia: Landau damping
  5. 2011

    Mouhot and Villani's nonlinear Landau damping

    first application

    Mouhot, C., Villani, C., On Landau damping, Acta Math. 207 (2011) 29-201, zbMATH 1239.82017

    Proves Landau damping for the nonlinear Vlasov equation in analytic-type regularity, applying the idea to a PDE.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'On Landau damping', Acta Math. 207 (2011) zbMATH API (Zbl 1239.82017)
    • Wikipedia: Mouhot and Villani's paper 'On Landau damping' in Acta Mathematica Wikipedia: Landau damping
  6. 2011

    Villani's ICM survey 'Landau damping'

    consolidation

    C. Villani, Landau damping, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians, Hyderabad 2010, 2011

    A survey article on Landau damping by an author of the modern proof, published with the ICM proceedings.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'Landau damping', Villani, in Proceedings of the ICM 2010 (Hyderabad), Zbl 1230.82042 (record 5971077) zbMATH Open API
  7. 2016

    Bedrossian, Masmoudi, Mouhot: paraproducts and Gevrey regularity

    key development

    Bedrossian, J., Masmoudi, N., Mouhot, C., Landau damping: paraproducts and Gevrey regularity, Ann. PDE 2 (2016), zbMATH 1402.35058

    Extends the nonlinear Landau damping theory to Gevrey regularity, using paraproducts.
