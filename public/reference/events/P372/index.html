1916 · Differential geometry

Affine connections (parallel transport, Weyl and Cartan)

Levi-Civita introduced parallel transport, Weyl generalised to gauge geometry, and Cartan developed connections and moving frames.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1916

    Levi-Civita introduces parallel transport

    first statement

    T. Levi-Civita, 'Nozione di parallelismo in una varieta qualunque', Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 42, 173-204 (Crossref print date 1916; volume dated 1917)

    Levi-Civita defines parallel displacement on a general manifold and the associated curvature, the basis of the affine connection.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record in Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo Crossref record
    • Crossref: Levi-Civita, Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 42, pp. 173-204, print date 1916. Crossref 10.1007/bf03014898
  2. 1918

    Weyl's scale geometry and affine connection in Raum-Zeit-Materie and 'Gravitation und Elektrizität'

    key development

    Weyl, Raum-Zeit-Materie (1918); Gravitation und Elektrizität (1918)

    Weyl uses an affine connection with scale (gauge) structure to unify gravitation and electromagnetism.

    Evidence (2)
    • Weyl's Raum-Zeit-Materie first appeared in 1918, with a gauge metric in later editions MacTutor: Weyl biography
    • Weyl's 1918 gauge geometry is the origin of the gauge concept Wikipedia: Gauge theory
  3. 1918

    Connection applied to electromagnetism

    first application

    Weyl, Gravitation und Elektrizität (1918)

    Weyl applies the affine (scale) connection to electromagnetic phenomena, the first physical target problem for connections.

    Evidence (1)
    • Weyl's 1918 geometric attempt to include electromagnetism Wikipedia: Gauge theory
  4. 1923

    Cartan's general affine connections and moving frames

    key development

    Cartan, 'Sur les variétés à connexion affine et la théorie de la relativité généralisée', Annales de l'ENS 40 (1923)

    Cartan develops connections on general manifolds with moving frames, the second part appearing in 1924-1925.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cartan 1923 Annales ENS record (part 1); parts 2 and 3 are 1924 and 1925 Crossref record
  5. 1927

    Eisenhart's Non-Riemannian Geometry

    consolidation

    Eisenhart, Non-Riemannian Geometry (1927)

    The 1927 monograph gives a systematic account of affine and other non-Riemannian connections.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1927 book record, Non-Riemannian geometry Open Library search
