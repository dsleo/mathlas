1987 · Algebraic geometry

Geometric Langlands and its physics

The arithmetic correspondence is reformulated for curves over the complex numbers with sheaves in place of functions, and electric-magnetic duality in gauge theory is identified as its physical origin.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Langlands' letter to Weil

    precursor

    Langlands, letter to A. Weil (1967)

    A 1967 letter to Weil, unpublished at the time, that contains the core ideas of the Langlands program; treated as private precursor.

    Evidence (1)
    • Langlands wrote a letter to Weil in 1967 containing profound ideas MacTutor Langlands biography
  2. 1983

    Drinfeld's GL(2) Langlands over function fields

    precursor

    Drinfeld, Two-dimensional l-adic representations of the fundamental group of a curve over a finite field and automorphic forms on GL(2), Amer. J. Math. (1983)

    Proves the Langlands correspondence for GL(2) over function fields by arithmetic means; an arithmetic precursor, not the geometric version.

    Evidence (2)
    • Amer. J. Math. 1983 paper (DOI 10.2307/2374382) Crossref / publisher record
    • Drinfeld proved the GL(2) case of the Langlands conjecture over function fields MacTutor Drinfeld biography
  3. 1987

    Geometric Langlands correspondence for function fields

    first statement

    Laumon, Correspondance de Langlands geometrique pour les corps de fonctions, Duke Math. J. (1987)

    Formulates the geometric Langlands correspondence for curves over finite fields, with sheaves in place of functions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Duke Math. J. 1987 paper (DOI 10.1215/s0012-7094-87-05418-4) Crossref / publisher record
    • Geometric Langlands for GL(n) over function fields formulated by Drinfeld and Laumon in 1987 Wikipedia: Geometric Langlands correspondence
  4. 1987

    'Geometrique' in a title

    naming

    Laumon (1987), see above

    The 1987 title uses the phrase 'Langlands geometrique'.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1987 Duke paper Crossref / publisher record
  5. 2007

    Electric-magnetic duality and geometric Langlands

    key development

    Kapustin, Witten, Electric-magnetic duality and the geometric Langlands program, Commun. Number Theory Phys. (2007)

    Links the geometric Langlands correspondence to S-duality of gauge theory, the physics origin named in the input summary.

    Evidence (3)
    • Commun. Number Theory Phys. 2007 paper (DOI 10.4310/cntp.2007.v1.n1.a1) Crossref / publisher record
    • arXiv hep-th/0604151 (2006), same paper arXiv record
    • Kapustin and Witten's 2007 paper links geometric Langlands to S-duality Wikipedia: Geometric Langlands correspondence
  6. 2007

    Frenkel's monograph on loop groups

    consolidation

    Frenkel, Langlands Correspondence for Loop Groups, Cambridge University Press (2007)

    Book-length treatment of the Langlands correspondence for loop groups, including geometric aspects.

    Dating or attribution note

    Coverage of the geometric side is medium; a general geometric Langlands textbook was not verified.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record of Frenkel's 2007 book Crossref / publisher record
  7. 2024

    Claimed proof of the geometric Langlands conjecture

    key development

    Gaitsgory, Raskin and collaborators, Proof of the geometric Langlands conjecture I: construction of the functor, arXiv:2405.03599 (2024)

    Claimed proof of the categorical unramified geometric Langlands conjecture in several papers.

    Evidence (2)
    • arXiv 2405.03599 (2024), first of the series arXiv record
    • Claimed proof announced May 2024, contained in more than 1000 pages across five papers Wikipedia: Geometric Langlands correspondence
