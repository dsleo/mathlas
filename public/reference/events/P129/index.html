1501 · Analysis

Kerala series (Madhava-Gregory-Leibniz school)

Madhava and followers derived series for sine, cosine and arctangent.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1400

    Madhava finds the series

    precursor

    Madhava of Sangamagrama, lost writings, about 1400

    Series equal to Maclaurin expansions of sine, cosine and arctangent found about 1400; his own texts on this are lost.

    Evidence (1)
    • Madhava discovered the series around 1400; known through later Keralese authors. MacTutor, Madhava biography
  2. 1501

    Tantrasangraha of Nilakantha (1501)

    first statement

    Nilakantha Somayaji, Tantrasangraha, about 1500

    Earliest surviving Sanskrit work that reports the series.

    Evidence (3)
    • Madhava's mathematics is known through reports by Nilakantha, about 100 years later. MacTutor, Madhava biography
    • Modern translation of Tantrasangraha is indexed. zbMATH 1211.01006 (Ramasubramanian and Sriram, 2011)
    • MacTutor: the Tantrasamgraha is Nilakantha's major astronomy treatise, written in 1501 MacTutor, Nilakantha
  3. 1550

    Yuktibhasa gives derivations

    key development

    Jyesthadeva, Yuktibhasa (Ganita-yukti-bhasa), about 1550

    Malayalam text that explains how Madhava's series were found.

    Evidence (2)
    • Jyesthadeva wrote Yukti-Bhasa around 1550 and explains Madhava's series. MacTutor, Madhava biography
    • Edition with translation catalogued (Hindustan Book Agency, 2008). Open Library OL18719019W
  4. 1834

    First Western report

    recognition

    Charles Whish, On the Hindu quadrature of the circle, Trans. Royal Asiatic Society, 1834

    Brought the Kerala series to European attention.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper record. Crossref doi:10.1017/S0950473700001221
  5. 1973

    Gupta titles the series after Madhava and Gregory

    naming

    Gupta, R. C., The Madhava-Gregory series, 1973

    The earliest zbMATH title found that carries Madhava's name in the series name.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: title "The Madhava-Gregory series", author Gupta, year 1973 zbMATH Open doc 3513719
