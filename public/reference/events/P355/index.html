1888 · Differential geometry

Tensor calculus (absolute differential calculus of Ricci and Levi-Civita)

Christoffel and Ricci developed coordinate-independent calculus of tensors, systematised by Ricci and Levi-Civita.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1869

    Christoffel on transformation of quadratic differential forms

    precursor

    E. B. Christoffel, Ueber die Transformation der homogenen Differentialausdrucke zweiten Grades, Crelle 70, 1869

    Christoffel solved when two quadratic differential forms are equivalent, introducing the symbols that define covariant differentiation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Crelle 70 (1869). Crossref
    • Christoffel's paper was the main influence on Ricci's 1884 start; symbols fundamental in tensor analysis. MacTutor, Ricci-Curbastro and Christoffel biographies
  2. 1883

    Ricci's theory of quadratic differential forms

    precursor

    G. Ricci, Principii di una teoria delle forme differenziali quadratiche, Annali di Matematica 12, 1883-84

    Ricci began his work on invariants of quadratic differential forms, the seed of the absolute differential calculus.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref issue date 1883 (volume 12 spans 1883-84). Crossref
    • Ricci began investigations on quadratic differential forms in 1884. MacTutor, Ricci-Curbastro biography
  3. 1888

    Ricci's systematic presentation of absolute differential calculus

    first statement

    G. Ricci, Delle derivazioni covarianti e controvarianti e del loro uso nella analisi applicata, in Studi editi dalla Universita di Padova (Bologna centenary), vol. 3, 1888

    Ricci first set out the covariant and contravariant technique systematically, completing four publications by 1892.

    Evidence (2)
    • Systematic presentation in 1888, with four publications between 1888 and 1892. MacTutor, Ricci-Curbastro biography
    • zbMATH 20.0282.01: Ricci, 'Delle derivazioni covarianti e controvarianti...', Studi editi dalla Universita di Padova, vol. 3, 1888. zbMATH Open, document 20.0282.01
  4. 1898

    Voigt's modern use of 'tensor'

    naming

    W. Voigt, Die fundamentalen physikalischen Eigenschaften der Krystalle in elementarer Darstellung, 1898

    Voigt introduced the contemporary meaning of the word tensor in crystal physics; Ricci's own term was 'systems'.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref records the 1898 book. Crossref
    • The contemporary usage was introduced by Voigt in 1898. Wikipedia, Tensor
  5. 1900

    Ricci and Levi-Civita, Methodes de calcul differentiel absolu

    consolidation

    G. Ricci, T. Levi-Civita, Methodes de calcul differentiel absolu et leurs applications, Math. Ann. 54, 1900

    The joint exposition that made the calculus accessible and showed applications to geometry and mechanics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Math. Ann. 54 (1900). Crossref
    • Described as the 1900 classic that made tensor calculus accessible. Wikipedia, Tensor
  6. 1916

    Levi-Civita's parallelism

    key development

    T. Levi-Civita, Nozione di parallelismo in una varieta qualunque, Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 42 (Crossref print date 1916; volume dated 1917)

    Levi-Civita gave a geometric meaning to covariant differentiation as parallel transport on a manifold.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref lists vol. 42 with issued date 1916. Crossref
  7. 1916

    Einstein's general relativity written in tensor calculus

    key development

    A. Einstein, Die Grundlage der allgemeinen Relativitatstheorie, Ann. Phys. 354, 1916

    Einstein's field theory made absolute differential calculus the standard language of geometry and physics.

    Dating or attribution note

    The earlier Einstein-Grossmann Entwurf (1913) is the first physical use but was not verified in a record.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Ann. Phys. 354 (1916). Crossref
