    Finite-field sum–product estimate

    key development

    J. Bourgain, N. Katz and T. Tao, A sum-product estimate in finite fields, and applications, GAFA 14 (2004)

    The paper develops a major additive–multiplicative tool with applications.

    Additive Combinatorics monograph

    consolidation

    T. Tao and V. H. Vu, Additive Combinatorics, Cambridge University Press (print 2006)

    A named textbook organizes the field under additive combinatorics.

    Evidence (1)

    Green-Tao primes in long arithmetic progressions

    key development

    Green and Tao, The primes contain arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions, Annals of Mathematics, 2008, pp. 481-547

    A main application of additive-combinatorial tools to primes, pages 481-547.
