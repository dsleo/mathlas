1757 · Fluid mechanics

Euler formulates the equations of fluid motion

Euler expressed fluid motion through general analytic equations and examined velocity fields and boundary conditions, helping establish mathematical fluid mechanics.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1757

    General equations for moving fluids published

    first statement

    L. Euler, Principes généraux du mouvement des fluides, Mémoires de l’académie des sciences de Berlin 11 (1757)

    Euler presents general analytic equations for fluid motion in the memoir published in 1757.

    Evidence (1)
    • The Euler Archive dates the original memoir to 1757 and identifies its analytic treatment of fluid motion University of the Pacific Euler Archive
  2. 1757

    Vortex flows and boundary conditions examined

    first application

    L. Euler, Principes généraux du mouvement des fluides, Mémoires de l’académie des sciences de Berlin 11 (1757)

    The same memoir studies vortex-flow examples and the role of boundary conditions.

    Evidence (1)
    • The Euler Archive summarizes the memoir’s vortex examples and early boundary-condition remarks University of the Pacific Euler Archive
