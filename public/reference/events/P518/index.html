1960 · Probability

Furstenberg: random matrix products and boundaries of Lie groups

Random products of matrices have Lyapunov exponents controlled by a boundary on which the group acts, joining probability, Lie groups and ergodic theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1960

    Furstenberg-Kesten: products of random matrices

    first statement

    Harry Furstenberg and Harry Kesten, Products of random matrices, Ann. Math. Statist. 31 (1960) 457-469

    Establishes limiting behaviour of random matrix products, the starting point of the theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Furstenberg-Kesten 1960 record Crossref
    • Furstenberg as pioneer of products of random matrices MacTutor, Furstenberg biography
  2. 1963

    Furstenberg: noncommuting random products

    key development

    Harry Furstenberg, Noncommuting random products, Trans. AMS 108 (1963)

    Extends the random-products theory to noncommuting matrices, the setting that the boundary theory then controls.

    Dating or attribution note

    Scope inferred from the title and the MacTutor description of his matrix work; the body was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Furstenberg 1963 Trans. AMS record Crossref
  3. 1963

    Furstenberg: Poisson formula for semi-simple Lie groups

    key development

    Harry Furstenberg, A Poisson formula for semi-simple Lie groups, Annals of Math. 77 (1963)

    Introduces the boundary of a semi-simple Lie group and a Poisson formula for random walks on it.

    Evidence (2)
    • Furstenberg 1963 Annals record Crossref
    • Series of articles beginning in 1963 with this paper Wikipedia, Hillel Furstenberg
  4. 1985

    Bougerol and Lacroix, Products of Random Matrices

    consolidation

    Philippe Bougerol and Jean Lacroix, Products of Random Matrices with Applications to Schrodinger Operators, Birkhauser (1985)

    The systematic monograph on random matrix products and their applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Bougerol-Lacroix 1985 record Crossref
