1979 · Computer science

Counting complexity (Valiant's #P)

Counting solutions defines a complexity class, and computing the permanent is shown complete for it.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1812

    Cauchy names the permanent

    precursor

    The permanent of a matrix (a determinant without signs) receives its name, an earlier object that later became the #P-complete example.

    Evidence (1)
    • Word 'permanent' is said to originate with Cauchy in 1812 Wikipedia: Permanent (mathematics)
  2. 1979

    Permanent shown #P-complete; #P defined

    first statement

    Valiant, L. G., The complexity of computing the permanent, Theoretical Computer Science, 1979

    Valiant defines the counting class #P in the context of computing the permanent and proves that the permanent is complete for it.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Theoretical Computer Science, 1979 Crossref record
    • #P first defined by Valiant in a 1979 article on the permanent Wikipedia: Sharp-P (♯P), History
  3. 1979

    Enumeration and reliability problems as counting

    first application

    Valiant, L. G., The complexity of enumeration and reliability problems, SIAM Journal on Computing 8, 1979

    Valiant applies counting complexity to enumeration and reliability problems, a target beyond the permanent.

    Dating or attribution note

    Title-level check only (enumeration and reliability problems); the content linking it to #P was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in SIAM Journal on Computing, 1979 (DOI 10.1137/0208032) Crossref record
  4. 1991

    PP and the polynomial-time hierarchy

    key development

    Toda, S., PP is as hard as the polynomial-time hierarchy, SIAM Journal on Computing, 1991

    Toda's result connects counting classes to the polynomial hierarchy, making #P-type classes central to complexity theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in SIAM Journal on Computing, 1991 (DOI 10.1137/0220053); title states PP hardness vs polynomial hierarchy Crossref record
  5. 1997

    Fortnow's 'Counting complexity' title

    naming

    L. Fortnow, Counting complexity, 1997

    The earliest zbMATH-indexed title that names the subject 'counting complexity'.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Fortnow, 'Counting complexity', year 1997. zbMATH Open, API ti:"counting complexity" search
