1971 · Complex analysis

Real-variable methods in complex analysis (Hardy spaces)

Real-variable characterisations of Hardy spaces and the duality of H^1 and BMO replace complex-variable methods in harmonic analysis.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1952

    Calderon-Zygmund real-variable singular integrals

    precursor

    Calderón, Zygmund, On the existence of certain singular integrals, Acta Math. 88 (1952)

    Real-variable estimates for singular integrals, the technique later used to characterise Hardy spaces without complex analysis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: journal article, Acta Mathematica, 1952 Crossref
    • MacTutor: Alberto Calderón's biography covers his work in harmonic analysis with Zygmund MacTutor, Alberto Calderón
  2. 1970

    Stein, Singular Integrals and Differentiability Properties of Functions

    precursor

    Stein, Singular Integrals and Differentiability Properties of Functions, Princeton UP (1970)

    The first systematic treatment of real-variable methods for singular integrals and function spaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record: Elias M. Stein, Singular integrals and differentiability properties of functions, Princeton UP, 1970 Open Library
    • MacTutor: Stein published this book in 1970; his Hardy-space work transformed the subject MacTutor, Elias Stein
  3. 1971

    Fefferman's characterisations of BMO

    first statement

    Fefferman, Characterizations of bounded mean oscillation, Bull. AMS 77 (1971)

    Real-variable characterisations of BMO that underlie the H^1-BMO duality used for Hardy spaces.

    Dating or attribution note

    The announced duality is taken from the standard account; the 1971 note itself was not read in full.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: journal article, Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society, 1971 Crossref
    • MacTutor: Fefferman's early work was supervised by Stein at Princeton MacTutor, Charles Fefferman
  4. 1972

    Fefferman-Stein, H^p spaces of several variables

    first application

    Fefferman, Stein, H^p spaces of several variables, Acta Math. 129 (1972)

    Applies the real-variable H^p theory to several variables, a purposeful extension of the 1971 characterisations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Acta Mathematica, 1972, H^p spaces of several variables Crossref
