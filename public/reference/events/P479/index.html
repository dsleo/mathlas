1990 · Combinatorics

Analytic combinatorics: generating functions as complex-analytic objects

Singularities of generating functions determine asymptotics of combinatorial classes, in a systematic symbolic-to-analytic method.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1918

    Hardy and Ramanujan's asymptotics for partitions

    precursor

    Hardy and Ramanujan, Asymptotic formulae in combinatory analysis, Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 17 (1918) 75-115

    Hardy and Ramanujan applied analytic methods, first a Tauberian argument and then the circle method, to the partition function.

    Evidence (2)
    • Hardy, Ramanujan, Asymptotic formulae in combinatory analysis, Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 17 (1918) 75-115 Crossref DOI 10.1112/plms/s2-17.1.75
    • Hardy and Ramanujan's work on partitions starting in 1918 is one of the earliest analytic enumeration uses Wikipedia, Analytic combinatorics
  2. 1956

    Hayman's generalisation of Stirling's formula

    precursor

    Hayman, A generalisation of Stirling's formula, J. reine angew. Math. 196 (1956) 67-95

    Hayman's paper is one of the earliest examples of the saddle-point method for coefficient asymptotics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Hayman, A generalisation of Stirling's formula, J. reine angew. Math. 196 (1956) 67-95 Crossref DOI 10.1515/crll.1956.196.67
    • Hayman's 1956 paper is considered one of the earliest examples of the saddle-point method Wikipedia, Analytic combinatorics
  3. 1990

    Flajolet and Odlyzko's singularity analysis

    first statement

    Flajolet, Odlyzko, Singularity analysis of generating functions, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 3 (1990) 216-240

    Flajolet and Odlyzko set out singularity analysis: coefficient asymptotics read from the singularities of generating functions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Flajolet, Odlyzko, Singularity analysis of generating functions, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 3 (1990) 216-240 Crossref DOI 10.1137/0403019
    • In 1990 Flajolet and Odlyzko developed the theory of singularity analysis Wikipedia, Analytic combinatorics
  4. 2009

    Flajolet and Sedgewick's Analytic Combinatorics

    consolidation

    Flajolet, Sedgewick, Analytic Combinatorics, Cambridge University Press (2009)

    The monograph that presents analytic combinatorics as a unified method.

    Evidence (2)
    • Flajolet, Sedgewick, Analytic Combinatorics, Cambridge University Press (2009) Crossref DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511801655
    • In 2009 Flajolet and Sedgewick wrote Analytic Combinatorics, presenting the field with their viewpoint Wikipedia, Analytic combinatorics
