2025 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Chowla cosine problem via Cayley graph eigenvalues and MaxCut

Showing that clique-free Cayley graphs must have very negative eigenvalues gave the first power-of-N lower bounds for the minimum of a sum of cosines, a problem posed in the 1950s-60s.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1965

    Chowla's cosine problem posed

    precursor

    Chowla, S. (1965), original paper not located in Crossref or zbMATH

    A problem about the largest possible minimum of sums of cosines of distinct positive integers, posed by Chowla.

    Evidence (1)
    • The problem is described as Chowla's cosine problem (1965) arXiv:2509.03490 abstract (Jin, Milojevic, Tomon, Zhang)
  2. 2010

    Sanders titles the paper Chowla's cosine problem

    precursor

    Sanders, T., Chowla's cosine problem, 2010

    Earliest zbMATH title found that uses the Chowla name for the cosine problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: "Chowla's cosine problem", Sanders, 2010 zbMATH Open doc 5823080
  3. 2025

    First polynomial bound for the Chowla cosine problem

    first statement

    Jin, Z.; Milojevic, A.; Tomon, I.; Zhang, S., From small eigenvalues to large cuts, and Chowla's cosine problem, arXiv:2509.03490 (2025)

    Shows that graphs with small least eigenvalue contain large cliques, which gives the bound that the minimum of the cosine sum is at most minus |A| to the power 1/10 minus o(1).

    Evidence (1)
    • Abstract states the first polynomial bound for Chowla's problem with exponent 1/10 minus o(1) arXiv abstract 2509.03490v2 (submitted 3 Sept 2025)
  4. 2025

    Spectral-clique method applied to Chowla

    first application

    Jin, Z.; Milojevic, A.; Tomon, I.; Zhang, S., From small eigenvalues to large cuts, and Chowla's cosine problem, arXiv:2509.03490 (2025)

    The first use of the graph-eigenvalue method on the cosine problem, as the abstract presents it.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same preprint as the first_statement milestone arXiv:2509.03490
  5. 2025

    Polynomial bound with exponent 1/5

    key development

    Bedert, B., Polynomial bounds for the Chowla cosine problem, arXiv:2509.05260 (2025)

    Proves min over x of the cosine sum is at most minus n to the power 1/5 minus o(1), an improved polynomial exponent.

    Evidence (1)
    • Abstract: min f_A(x) <= -n^(1/5 - o(1)) for n = |A| distinct positive integers arXiv abstract 2509.05260v3 (submitted 5 Sept 2025)
  6. 2026

    Refined least-eigenvalue estimates for Chowla's problem

    key development

    Yip, F., Lower bounds for the magnitude of the minimum eigenvalue of graphs with applications to MaxCut and Chowla's cosine problem, arXiv:2610.01657 (2026)

    Refines the Jin-Milojevic-Tomon-Zhang recursive estimate and applies it to Chowla's problem.

    Dating or attribution note

    Recent preprint; the authors list was taken from the arXiv entry as retrieved (only one name appeared in the record parsed) and the exponent claim was not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Abstract says the refinement yields improved estimates for Chowla's cosine problem arXiv abstract 2610.01657v2 (2026-10-01)
