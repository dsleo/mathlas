1953 · Statistics & decision theory

Markov chain Monte Carlo and the Bayesian revival

Metropolis-type samplers, via Gibbs sampling, make posterior computation feasible.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1949

    Monte Carlo method (Metropolis and Ulam)

    precursor

    Metropolis and Ulam, The Monte Carlo method, 1949

    The Monte Carlo idea of random sampling that the Metropolis sampler later refined.

    Evidence (1)
    • Popular description of the Monte Carlo method dated 1949 OSTI record
  2. 1953

    Equation of state calculations by fast computing machines

    first statement

    Metropolis, Rosenbluth, Rosenbluth, Teller, Teller, Equation of state calculations by fast computing machines, J. Chem. Phys. 21 (1953) 1087-1092

    The first public statement of Metropolis-type acceptance sampling, applied to an equation-of-state calculation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • Metropolis algorithm designed for high-dimensional integration on early computers, 1953 Wikipedia: Markov chain Monte Carlo
  3. 1953

    Equation-of-state application (same paper)

    first application

    Metropolis et al., J. Chem. Phys. 21 (1953) 1087-1092 (as above)

    The same paper uses the sampler to compute an equation of state, the first purposeful application.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same publication as first_statement Crossref
  4. 1970

    Monte Carlo sampling using Markov chains (Hastings)

    key development

    Hastings, Monte Carlo sampling methods using Markov chains and their applications, Biometrika 57 (1970) 97-109

    Generalises the Metropolis sampler to non-symmetric proposals, the Metropolis-Hastings form.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • W. K. Hastings's 1970 generalisation Wikipedia: Markov chain Monte Carlo
  5. 1984

    Gibbs sampling named in image processing

    naming

    Geman and Geman, Stochastic relaxation, Gibbs distributions, and the Bayesian restoration of images, IEEE TPAMI 6 (1984) 721-741

    The formal naming of Gibbs sampling in image processing, per Wikipedia.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • Formal naming of Gibbs sampling in image processing by Geman and Geman (1984) Wikipedia: Markov chain Monte Carlo
  6. 1990

    Sampling-based marginal densities (Bayesian revival)

    key development

    Gelfand and Smith, Sampling-based approaches to calculating marginal densities, JASA 85 (1990)

    Shows sampling methods give practical Bayesian posterior computation, the basis of the statistical revival.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal and year verified Crossref
    • Revolution in mainstream statistics followed demonstrations of Gibbs sampling Wikipedia: Markov chain Monte Carlo
  7. 1999

    Monte Carlo Statistical Methods (book)

    consolidation

    Robert and Casella, Monte Carlo Statistical Methods, Springer, 1999

    Book-length treatment of Monte Carlo and MCMC methods for statistics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book dated 1999 (Springer) Open Library record
