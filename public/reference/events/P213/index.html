1959 · Algebraic topology

Topological K-theory

Vector bundle classes formed a generalised cohomology theory, used on Hopf invariant and index problems.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1957

    Grothendieck's K_0 and Riemann-Roch for varieties

    precursor

    Grothendieck, Sur quelques points d'algebre homologique, Tohoku Math. J. (1957)

    Grothendieck's group of coherent sheaves (K_0) for algebraic varieties, the algebraic precursor of the topological theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Sur quelques points d'algebre homologique, Tohoku Mathematical Journal, 1957 Crossref
    • Wikipedia K-theory: the subject is said to begin with Grothendieck (1957), who used it for Riemann-Roch Wikipedia K-theory
  2. 1959

    Topological K(X) from vector bundles

    first statement

    Atiyah and Hirzebruch, Riemann-Roch theorems for differentiable manifolds, Bull. AMS 65 (1959) 276-281

    Atiyah and Hirzebruch define K(X) for topological spaces from vector bundles, the first public version of the theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Riemann-Roch theorems for differentiable manifolds, Bull. AMS, 1959 Crossref
    • Wikipedia K-theory: Atiyah and Hirzebruch defined K(X) for topological spaces in 1959 Wikipedia K-theory
  3. 1959

    Riemann-Roch for differentiable manifolds

    first application

    Atiyah and Hirzebruch, Riemann-Roch theorems for differentiable manifolds, Bull. AMS 65 (1959)

    The same paper uses K-theory on a target problem, a Riemann-Roch theorem for differentiable manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record for the Bull. AMS paper (title names the Riemann-Roch application) Crossref
  4. 1959

    Bott periodicity

    key development

    Bott, The stable homotopy of the classical groups, Ann. of Math. 70 (1959)

    Bott periodicity makes topological K-theory a generalised cohomology theory with a periodic structure.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: The Stable Homotopy of the Classical Groups, Annals of Mathematics, 1959 Crossref
    • Wikipedia K-theory: Bott periodicity is one of the main tools of the K-theory approach Wikipedia K-theory
  5. 1961

    K-theory of homogeneous spaces

    key development

    Atiyah and Hirzebruch, Vector bundles and homogeneous spaces, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 3 (1961)

    Applies topological K-theory to homogeneous spaces, showing the theory computes useful invariants.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record for the 1961 Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics volume 3 Crossref
  6. 1964

    Atiyah's book K-theory

    consolidation

    Atiyah, K-theory, W. A. Benjamin (1964)

    The first book-length treatment of topological K-theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: K-theory, Atiyah, W. A. Benjamin, first publish year 1964 Open Library
