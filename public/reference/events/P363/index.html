Metric spaces (Frechet)

Frechet unified earlier function-space work by Cantor, Volterra, Arzela and Hadamard into abstract metric spaces.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1905

    Frechet, La notion d'ecart dans le calcul fonctionnel

    first statement

    M. Frechet, La notion d'ecart dans le calcul fonctionnel, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 140 (1905) 772-774

    A note introducing the ecart (distance) on abstract sets of functions, ahead of the thesis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Note indexed in zbMATH (checked: ti:ecart py:1905) zbMATH Open 2650525 / JFM 36.0449.03
    • Historical analysis quotes the 1905 note defining an abstract écart for arbitrary elements; it distinguishes that note from the full 1906 thesis Bélanger, Université de Montréal thesis, pp. 32–33
  2. 1906

    Frechet's thesis on functional calculus

    key development

    M. Frechet, Sur quelques points du calcul fonctionnel, Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 22 (1906) 1-74

    The thesis builds abstract spaces with a distance, unifying function-space work of Cantor, Volterra, Arzela and Hadamard, with compactness results.

    Dating or attribution note

    A, B and D. Role split: the 1906 paper is also the first application (functionals on function spaces); the same paper is kept under one role only, as the method and statement coincide. Do not infer 'metric space' appears in the title.

    Evidence (2)
    • Thesis indexed in Crossref and zbMATH Crossref / zbMATH 2647192
    • MacTutor: introduced the metric space concept in the 1906 thesis MacTutor Frechet
  3. 1906

    Abstract distance applied to functionals and compactness in function spaces

    first application

    M. Frechet, Sur quelques points du calcul fonctionnel, Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 22 (1906)

    The thesis itself uses the abstract distance on function spaces to study functionals and compactness.

    Evidence (1)
    • Fréchet's Sur quelques points du calcul fonctionnel appeared in Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo 22 (1906), pp. 1–72. Springer, Fréchet original article
  4. 1910

    Frechet, Les ensembles abstraits et le calcul fonctionnel

    key development

    M. Frechet, Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 30 (1910)

    Follow-up on abstract sets and functional calculus.

    Evidence (1)
    • Follow-up paper Crossref
  5. 1914

    Hausdorff, Grundzuege der Mengenlehre: metrischer Raum

    naming

    F. Hausdorff, Grundzuege der Mengenlehre, Veit, Leipzig (1914)

    Hausdorff coins the name metric space (metrischer Raum) and treats metric and topological spaces systematically.

    Evidence (3)
    • Monograph with a theory of metric and topological spaces zbMATH JFM 45.0123.01
    • MacTutor: the name metric space is due to Hausdorff MacTutor Frechet
    • Full text uses metrischer Raum archive.org
  6. 1928

    Frechet, Les espaces abstraits

    consolidation

    M. Frechet, Les espaces abstraits et leur theorie consideree comme introduction a l'analyse generale, Gauthier-Villars (1928)

    Frechet's own monograph on abstract spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • Monograph indexed zbMATH JFM 54.0614.02 / 2577807
