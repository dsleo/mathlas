1973 · Differential equations

Heat equation proof of index theorem

Heat kernels give local proofs of the index formula.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1963

    Atiyah-Singer index theorem announced

    precursor

    Atiyah, Singer, The index of elliptic operators on compact manifolds, Bull. AMS 69 (1963)

    The index theorem that the heat method later reproves, announced in 1963.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Atiyah-Singer article: theorem announced in 1963 Wikipedia: Atiyah-Singer index theorem
  2. 1973

    Heat-equation proof of the index theorem

    first statement

    Atiyah, Bott, Patodi, On the heat equation and the index theorem, Invent. Math. 19 (1973)

    Gives a proof of the Atiyah-Singer formula by small-time asymptotics of heat kernels, the first public heat-kernel proof.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Atiyah-Singer article: 1973 new proof using the heat equation Wikipedia: Atiyah-Singer index theorem
  3. 1973

    Heat method applied to the index formula

    first application

    Atiyah, Bott, Patodi, Invent. Math. 19 (1973)

    The same paper applied the heat kernel directly to the index problem, which is what the derived methodological date uses.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same paper as the first_statement milestone (rule 2 note). Wikipedia says a related paper by Melrose described it; that is not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  4. 1986

    Local index theorem via supersymmetry

    key development

    Getzler, A short proof of the local Atiyah-Singer index theorem, Topology 25 (1986)

    Short heat-kernel proof of the local index theorem for Dirac-type operators using supersymmetry, the route later standard.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Atiyah-Singer article: Getzler's short proof, motivated by Witten and Alvarez-Gaume Wikipedia: Atiyah-Singer index theorem
  5. 1992

    Monograph: Heat kernels and Dirac operators

    consolidation

    Berline, Getzler, Vergne, Heat Kernels and Dirac Operators, Springer (1992)

    Systematic book-length treatment of heat kernels and Dirac operators, the textbook form of the method.
