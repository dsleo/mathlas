1960 · Algebraic geometry

Scheme theory

Algebraic geometry is rebuilt on arbitrary commutative rings so that geometry over the integers and over finite fields is treated uniformly with complex varieties.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1960

    EGA I states the scheme language

    first statement

    A. Grothendieck, Éléments de géométrie algébrique I: Le langage des schémas, Publ. Math. IHÉS 4 (1960)

    The first checked public systematic definition of schemes appears in EGA I.

    Evidence (1)
    • EGA I published in 1960 with the language of schemes Numdam / IHÉS
  2. 1960

    EGA I consolidates the language

    consolidation

    A. Grothendieck, Éléments de géométrie algébrique I: Le langage des schémas, Publ. Math. IHÉS 4 (1960)

    The book-length treatment makes schemes the working language of algebraic geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • EGA I published in 1960 with the language of schemes Numdam / IHÉS
  3. 1961

    EGA II applies schemes to morphisms

    first application

    A. Grothendieck, EGA II: Étude globale élémentaire de quelques classes de morphismes, Publ. Math. IHÉS 8 (1961)

    The second installment develops global classes of morphisms using the scheme framework.

    Evidence (1)
    • EGA II published 28 December 1961 Numdam / IHÉS
