1933 · Probability

Measure theory meets probability (Kolmogorov)

Probability is axiomatised as a normalised measure on a sigma-algebra, so limit theorems become statements about integration.

Also known as: measure-theoretic probability

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1901

    Lebesgue's generalisation of the definite integral

    precursor

    Lebesgue, H., Sur une generalisation de l'integrale definie, Comptes Rendus Hebdomadaires des Seances de l'Academie des Sciences, 1901

    Lebesgue's note introduces measure of sets and an integral defined through it; the measure-theoretic precursor of the probability axioms.

    Evidence (2)
    • Note appears in Comptes Rendus in 1901, pages 1025-1028 zbMATH record, Zbl 32.0299.01
    • MacTutor says Lebesgue formulated the theory of measure in 1901 MacTutor biography of Lebesgue
  2. 1902

    Lebesgue's Integral, length, area

    precursor

    Lebesgue, H., Integrale, longueur, aire, Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata, 1902

    Lebesgue's thesis develops the full measure-based integral, the analytic tool later used to state probability as a measure.

    Evidence (2)
    • Thesis appears in Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata in 1902 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf02420592
    • MacTutor says the thesis Integrale, longueur, aire was published in Milan in 1902 MacTutor biography of Lebesgue
  3. 1909

    Borel's countable probabilities and arithmetic applications

    precursor

    Borel, E., Les probabilites denombrables et leurs applications arithmetiques, Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo, 1909

    Borel applies measure-type (countable additivity) arguments to probability, including the strong law of large numbers for coin tossing.

    Dating or attribution note

    The strong-law content is from general knowledge; the paper text was not read. Reclassified from first_application to precursor: it predates this event's first statement (1933) and belongs to the wider field.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appears in Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo in 1909 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf03019651
    • MacTutor says Borel's theory of measure and countable ideas underlie later work MacTutor biography of Borel
  4. 1933

    Kolmogorov's Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung

    first statement

    Kolmogorov, A. N., Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung, Springer, 1933

    Kolmogorov axiomatises probability as a normalised measure on a sigma-algebra, the public statement of the probability space.

    Evidence (2)
    • Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung published 1933 Open Library search record
    • MacTutor says the 1933 monograph built probability from fundamental axioms MacTutor biography of Kolmogorov
  5. 1933

    Kolmogorov's 'Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung'

    consolidation

    A. Kolmogoroff, Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung, Ergebnisse der Mathematik 2, No. 3, Berlin: Springer, 1933

    The monograph that systematised measure-theoretic probability on the Lebesgue foundation; it is the first book-length treatment of the subject.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record 'Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung', Kolmogoroff (1933), Zbl 0007.21601 zbMATH Open API
    • zbMATH record of the same book in the Ergebnisse series (Zbl 59.1152.03) zbMATH Open API
  6. 1933

    Kolmogorov's empirical distribution (goodness-of-fit) test

    first application

    Kolmogoroff, A., Sulla determinazione empirica di una legge di distribuzione, 1933 (zbMATH 2546673)

    Kolmogorov used the distribution-function framework of probability to build a statistical test that compares an empirical distribution with a hypothesised one.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH records a 1933 paper by Kolmogorov titled 'Sulla determinazione empirica di una legge di distribuzione' zbMATH Open document record 2546673
    • The Kolmogorov-Smirnov test statistic and its null distribution were first published by Kolmogorov in 1933 Wikipedia, Kolmogorov-Smirnov test (raw wikitext)
