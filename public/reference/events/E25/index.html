1928 · Game theory & economics

Game theory

Strategic interaction is given a mathematical model, with minimax and equilibrium existence proved by convexity and fixed-point arguments.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1928

    Von Neumann formulates minimax games

    first statement

    J. von Neumann, Zur Theorie der Gesellschaftsspiele, Math. Ann. 100 (1928)

    The article gives a mathematical framework for two-person zero-sum games.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published minimax article in 1928 Mathematische Annalen / Crossref
  2. 1928

    Minimax applied to strategic games

    first application

    J. von Neumann, Zur Theorie der Gesellschaftsspiele, Math. Ann. 100 (1928)

    Von Neumann uses the minimax principle on the target class of strategic games.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published minimax article in 1928 Mathematische Annalen / Crossref
  3. 1944

    Theory of Games and Economic Behavior

    consolidation

    J. von Neumann and O. Morgenstern, Theory of Games and Economic Behavior (1944)

    Von Neumann and Morgenstern establish a named book-length treatment of game theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • First edition of the named book, 1944 Open Library
  4. 1950

    Nash's n-person equilibrium

    key development

    J. Nash, Equilibrium points in n-person games, PNAS 36 (1950)

    Nash extends strategic equilibrium beyond two-person zero-sum games.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published n-person equilibrium paper, 1950 PNAS / Crossref
