1799 · Geometry

Monge's descriptive geometry

Monge turned military engineering projections into a geometric discipline.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1525

    Dürer's published projection methods

    precursor

    The earliest known publication on projection technique is Dürer's 1525 treatise, long before Monge.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia says the earliest known publication on the technique is Dürer's of 1525 English Wikipedia, Descriptive geometry
  2. 1765

    Monge's first discoveries as a draftsman

    precursor

    Monge worked out methods while a draftsman for military fortifications at Mézières; the results were published much later.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia says his first discoveries were in 1765 and published later English Wikipedia, Descriptive geometry
    • MacTutor says he was appointed draftsman at the École Royale du Génie in 1765 MacTutor, Monge
  3. 1794

    Monge becomes instructor in descriptive geometry

    precursor

    Monge was appointed on 9 November 1794 as instructor in descriptive geometry at the new school that became the École Polytechnique, and also taught a course at the École Normale.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor gives 9 November 1794 for the appointment and mentions the École Normale course MacTutor, Monge
  4. 1799

    Géométrie descriptive published

    first statement

    G. Monge, Géométrie descriptive: leçons données aux écoles normales, l'an 3 de la République, Paris, 1799

    A book from Monge's École Normale lectures, assembled by Hachette and printed without Monge, is the first public statement.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive item: Géométrie descriptive: leçons données aux écoles normales, l'an 3 de la République, 1799 archive.org geometriedescrip00mong
    • MacTutor says the memoir was published in 1799 from Monge's École Normale lectures, put together by Hachette MacTutor, Monge
  5. 1799

    First systematic treatise

    consolidation

    Monge, Géométrie descriptive, 1799

    The book is the first systematic account of the subject.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia calls Monge the father of descriptive geometry credited with its invention English Wikipedia, Descriptive geometry
