2005 · Algebraic geometry

Gauge-theory heuristics for rational points (non-abelian Chabauty)

Kim's programme uses spaces of path-collections on fundamental groups of curves, modelled on gauge-theory fields, in the hope of a least-action principle for finding rational solutions of Diophantine equations.

  1. 2005

    Unipotent Albanese and Selmer-variety approach

    first statement

    M. Kim, The unipotent Albanese map and Selmer varieties for curves, arXiv:math/0510441 (2005)

    The public preprint relates p-adic path torsors and Selmer varieties to Diophantine finiteness questions.

  2. 2019

    Explicit Chabauty-Kim for the split Cartan modular curve of level 13

    key development

    Balakrishnan, Dogra, Muller, Tuitman, Vonk, Explicit Chabauty-Kim for the split Cartan modular curve of level 13, Annals (2019)

    Implements the non-abelian Chabauty-Kim method to determine rational points on a modular curve, the first major application of the 2005 approach.
