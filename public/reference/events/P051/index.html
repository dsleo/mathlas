2023 · Combinatorics

Entropy proves the polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa conjecture

Entropy of random variables, rather than sets, is used with a doubling-style argument to settle the polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa conjecture in characteristic 2.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2023

    Entropy proof of PFR posted

    first statement

    W. T. Gowers, B. Green, F. Manners and T. Tao, On a conjecture of Marton, arXiv:2311.05762 (2023)

    The public preprint proves the polynomial Freiman–Ruzsa conjecture in characteristic two by an entropy-based argument.

    Evidence (2)
    • ArXiv dates the proof to November 2023 and states the characteristic-two result. Original publication or repository record
    • Coauthor Tao describes the entropy argument used in the new proof. Terence Tao, author commentary
  2. 2024

    Marton's conjecture for bounded torsion

    key development

    Gowers, Green, Manners, Tao, Marton's Conjecture in abelian groups with bounded torsion, arXiv:2404.02244

    The method is extended from the PFR setting to abelian groups with bounded torsion, giving a later main result on the same line.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record for this title, posted 2024-04-02, authors Gowers, Green, Manners, Tao arXiv
