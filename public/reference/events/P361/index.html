Hilbert space and integral equations

Hilbert and Riesz made sequence and function spaces into an infinite-dimensional geometry with spectral theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1903

    Fredholm's integral equations

    precursor

    Fredholm, Sur une classe d'equations fonctionnelles, Acta Math. 27 (1903)

    Solves a class of linear integral equations, the starting point for Hilbert's spectral theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1903. Crossref
  2. 1907

    Riesz's geometry of function spaces

    first statement

    Riesz, F., 'Sur une espèce de géométrie analytique des systèmes de fonctions sommables', C. R. Acad. Sci. (1907)

    Riesz treats square-summable function systems as a geometry, the first public statement of the function-space picture.

    Evidence (1)
    • The historical bibliography identifies Riesz's paper in Comptes rendus 144 (1907), pp. 1409–1411. Revue d'histoire des mathématiques, Numdam
  3. 1908

    Schmidt on infinitely many linear equations

    key development

    Erhard Schmidt, Ueber die Aufloesung linearer Gleichungen mit unendlich vielen Unbekannten, Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 25 (1908)

    Geometric treatment of sequence space, close to the abstract Hilbert space picture.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1908. Crossref
  4. 1912

    Hilbert's general theory of linear integral equations

    consolidation

    Hilbert, D., Grundzüge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen (1912)

    The book presents the infinite-dimensional spectral theory of integral equations, the systematic treatment that gives the event its name.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'Grundzüge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen', year 1912 zbMATH API, query 'Hilbert Grundzuge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen'
  5. 1929

    von Neumann's abstract Hilbert space

    naming

    von Neumann, J., 'Allgemeine Eigenwerttheorie Hermitescher Funktionaloperatoren', Math. Ann. (1929)

    Von Neumann coins the term 'abstract Hilbert space' in his work on Hermitian operators.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Allgemeine Eigenwerttheorie Hermitescher Funktionaloperatoren', year 1929 zbMATH API, query 'von Neumann Allgemeine Eigenwerttheorie'
    • Von Neumann coined the term 'abstract Hilbert space' in his 1929 work Wikipedia, Hilbert space (saved wikitext)
  6. 1932

    Stone's Linear Transformations in Hilbert Space

    consolidation

    Marshall H. Stone, Linear Transformations in Hilbert Space and Their Applications to Analysis, AMS, 1932

    Systematic book treatment of operators on Hilbert space.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book first published 1932. Open Library
