1931 · Probability

Kolmogorov's diffusion theory and parabolic PDE

Continuous-time Markov processes were linked to parabolic equations, founding diffusion theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1900

    Bachelier models Brownian motion in finance

    precursor

    L. Bachelier, Théorie de la spéculation, Ann. Sci. École Norm. Sup. (1900)

    Early random-walk model of prices, a precursor of continuous-time diffusion.

    Evidence (1)
    • Reprint record "Louis Bachelier's Theory of Speculation" (2011) Crossref
  2. 1931

    Kolmogorov links Markov processes to diffusion equations

    first statement

    A. Kolmogorov, Über die analytischen Methoden in der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung, Math. Ann. 104 (1931)

    Founded the diffusion theory of Markov processes by deriving the forward and backward equations, which are parabolic PDEs.

    Evidence (3)
    • DOI record: Math. Ann. 104 (1931) Crossref
    • The results of 1931 mark the beginning of diffusion theory MacTutor: Andrey Kolmogorov
    • Equations named after Kolmogorov for his 1931 foundational work Wikipedia: Kolmogorov equations
  3. 1952

    Feller links parabolic equations to semigroups of transformations

    key development

    W. Feller, The parabolic differential equations and the associated semi-groups of transformations, Ann. of Math. (2) 55 (1952)

    Connected the parabolic equations to semigroups of transformations, the operator framework for the diffusion theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    Significance is from the title and general knowledge; the paper was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • DOI record: Ann. of Math. 55 (1952) Crossref
  4. 1953

    Doob: Stochastic Processes

    consolidation

    J. L. Doob, Stochastic Processes (1953)

    First broad monograph covering stochastic processes, including Markov diffusions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record (1953) Open Library
