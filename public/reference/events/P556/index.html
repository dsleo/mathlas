2007 · Algebraic geometry

Tropical geometry transferred to Brill-Noether theory

Degenerating curves to graphs and playing chip-firing games gave new proofs and extensions of classical statements about how many equations are needed to embed a curve.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2007

    Specialization from curves to graphs

    first statement

    M. Baker, Specialization of linear systems from curves to graphs (2007)

    Baker formulates the tropical specialization method for linear systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents specialization from curves to graphs in 2007 arXiv
  2. 2007

    Graph divisors constrain algebraic-curve linear series

    first application

    M. Baker, Specialization of linear systems from curves to graphs (2007)

    The paper uses specialization to transfer divisor-rank information to curves.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents graph divisors constrain algebraic-curve linear series in 2007 arXiv
  3. 2012

    Cools-Draisma-Payne-Robeva tropical proof of Brill-Noether

    key development

    Cools, Draisma, Payne, Robeva, A tropical proof of the Brill-Noether theorem, Advances in Mathematics, 2012, pp. 759-776

    Gives a tropical proof of the Brill-Noether theorem, the main result showing the tropical method applies to the classical problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for this title, year 2012, pages 759-776 zbMATH Open
  4. 2017

    Fixed-gonality Brill–Noether theorem

    key development

    D. Jensen and D. Ranganathan, Brill-Noether theory for curves of a fixed gonality (2017)

    Jensen and Ranganathan use tropical divisor theory and logarithmic maps.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents fixed-gonality brill–noether theorem in 2017 arXiv
  5. 2018

    Tropical methods reach maximal rank cases

    key development

    D. Jensen and S. Payne, On the strong maximal rank conjecture in genus 22 and 23 (2018)

    Tropicalized linear series prove two cases of the strong maximal rank conjecture.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents tropical methods reach maximal rank cases in 2018 arXiv
