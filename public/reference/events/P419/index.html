1896 · Group & representation theory

Burnside's use of character theory for finite group theory

Burnside applied Frobenius characters to prove the p^a q^b solvability theorem.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1896

    Frobenius' group characters

    precursor

    Frobenius, G., Ueber Gruppencharaktere, Sitzungsberichte der Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, 1896

    Frobenius' group characters are the tool Burnside adopted; this is the precursor for the event.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper on group characters, 1896, pages 985-1021 zbMATH record, Zbl 27.0092.01
  2. 1896

    Frobenius introduces group characters for finite groups

    first statement

    G. Frobenius, Ueber Gruppencharaktere, Sitzungsberichte der Koniglich Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, 1896, pp. 985-1021

    Frobenius defines characters of arbitrary finite groups, expecting them to enrich finite group theory, the tool Burnside later used for his solvability theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH lists the paper in the 1896 Berlin Academy proceedings, pp. 985-1021. zbMATH Open document 2675572
    • Presented to the Berlin Academy on 16 July 1896; Frobenius expected the concept to enrich group theory; Burnside soon used it. MacTutor, Frobenius and Burnside biographies
  3. 1904

    Burnside's p^a q^b theorem by characters

    first application

    Burnside, W., On groups of order p^alpha q^beta, Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society (2) 1, 1904

    Burnside applies Frobenius characters to prove that groups of order p^a q^b are solvable.

    Dating or attribution note

    Use of characters in the 1904 proof is stated by MacTutor; the paper text was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appears in PLMS 1904, pages 388-392 zbMATH record, Zbl 35.0162.01
    • MacTutor says Burnside began to use character theory and the p^m q^n result appeared in 1904 MacTutor biography of Burnside
  4. 1905

    Second paper on groups of order p^a q^b

    key development

    Burnside, W., On groups of order p^alpha q^beta (second paper), Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society (2), 1905

    Burnside's second paper on the same theorem extends and sharpens the 1904 argument.

    Dating or attribution note

    The sense in which it extends the 1904 result is not verified; the paper text was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Second paper appears in PLMS in 1905, pages 432-437 zbMATH record, Zbl 36.0198.02
  5. 1911

    Second edition of Burnside's Theory of Groups

    consolidation

    Burnside, W., Theory of Groups of Finite Order, 2nd ed., Cambridge, 1911

    The 1911 edition systematically includes Frobenius' character theory and Burnside's own character results.

    Evidence (2)
    • Theory of groups of finite order, 1911 edition Open Library search record
    • MacTutor says the 1911 edition contains a systematic development including Frobenius's character theory MacTutor biography of Burnside
