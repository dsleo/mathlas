1637 · Geometry

Analytic geometry (Descartes, Fermat)

Geometric problems were translated into algebraic equations, solved and classified by degree.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1629–1636

    Fermat's restoration of Apollonius' Plane loci

    precursor

    Fermat, Ad locos planos et solidos isagoge (circulated in manuscript)

    Fermat translated loci problems into equations in the 1620s-30s; the Isagoge circulated only in manuscript and was printed after his death.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gave a copy of his Plane loci restoration in Bordeaux in 1629; the Isagoge appears among his works. MacTutor biography of Fermat
  2. 1637

    Descartes, La Geometrie

    first statement

    Descartes, La Geometrie, appendix to Discours de la methode, Leiden, 1637

    Descartes applied algebra to geometry, solving construction problems through equations and classifying curves by degree.

    Evidence (2)
    • La Geometrie published at Leiden in 1637 and gave Cartesian geometry. MacTutor biography of Descartes
    • Record of the 1637 Discours de la methode volume. Internet Archive record: Discours de la methode, 1637
  3. 1649

    Van Schooten's Latin edition of the Geometria

    consolidation

    van Schooten, Geometria a Renato Des Cartes (Latin ed.), 1649/1659

    Van Schooten's annotated Latin version spread Descartes' method across Europe.

    Evidence (1)
    • Records show the Latin Geometria with commentary (1659 and 1683 editions found). Internet Archive record: Geometria a Renato Des Cartes, 1659 edition
  4. 1679

    Fermat's Isagoge printed in Varia opera

    consolidation

    Fermat, Varia opera mathematica, 1679

    Fermat's analytic geometry reached print in the posthumous collected works.

    Dating or attribution note

    That the Isagoge sits in the 1679 volume is background knowledge, not checked in the scan.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record of the 1679 Varia opera mathematica. Internet Archive record: Varia opera mathematica, 1679
    • MacTutor lists the first page of Ad locos planos et solidos isagoge among Fermat's materials. MacTutor biography of Fermat
  5. 1748

    Euler, Introductio in analysin infinitorum

    consolidation

    Euler, Introductio in analysin infinitorum, 1748

    Euler's textbook systematically treats curves through equations and functions, fixing modern analytic geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record of the 1748 Introductio. Internet Archive record: Introductio in analysin infinitorum, 1748
