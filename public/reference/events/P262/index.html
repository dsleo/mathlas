1967 · Logic & foundations

Automata and formal verification

Floyd and Hoare apply logic to program correctness.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1962

    Buchi's automata decision method

    precursor

    Buchi, J. R., On a decision method in restricted second order arithmetic, in Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science (1962)

    Uses finite automata on infinite words to decide a logic, the mathematical backbone later used for automata-based verification.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper indexed by zbMATH as 0147.25103 (1962) zbMATH
  2. 1967

    Floyd's assertions for programs

    first statement

    Floyd, R. W., Assigning meanings to programs, Proc. Sympos. Appl. Math. 19 (1967)

    Proposes attaching assertions to flowchart points to prove program correctness.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in Proc. Sympos. Appl. Math. 19 (1967), DOI 10.1090/psapm/019/0235771 Crossref; zbMATH 0189.50204
    • Wikipedia says Hoare logic was seeded by Floyd's similar system for flowcharts Wikipedia, Hoare logic (lead)
  3. 1969

    Hoare's axiomatic basis and Hoare triples

    key development

    Hoare, C. A. R., An axiomatic basis for computer programming, Comm. ACM 12 (1969) 576-580

    Gives the Hoare triple and axioms for language constructs, which made the logic a usable rule system.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in Comm. ACM 12 (1969), pages 576-580 zbMATH 0179.23105
    • Wikipedia gives 1969 as the year the logic was proposed Wikipedia, Hoare logic (lead)
  4. 1977

    Pnueli's temporal logic of programs

    key development

    Pnueli, A., The temporal logic of programs, 18th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (1977)

    Introduces temporal logic for specifying and verifying concurrent programs, the specification language later used for model checking.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in FOCS 1977 (DOI 10.1109/sfcs.1977.32) Crossref
    • Wikipedia says Pnueli pioneered temporal logic in computing science Wikipedia, Model checking (History)
  5. 1981

    Apt's survey Ten years of Hoare's logic

    consolidation

    Apt, K. R., Ten years of Hoare's logic: a survey, Part I (1981), zbMATH 0471.68006, pages 431-483

    A survey that consolidates Hoare's logic for a wider audience ten years after the axioms.

    Evidence (1)
    • Survey indexed by zbMATH as 0471.68006, pages 431-483 (1981) zbMATH
  6. 1982

    Model checking of synchronisation skeletons

    first application

    Clarke, E. M.; Emerson, E. A., Design and synthesis of synchronization skeletons using branching time temporal logic, in Logics of Programs, LNCS 131 (1982)

    Applies a temporal-logic model checker to automatically verify concurrent synchronisation skeletons, the first purposeful automatic verification of this kind in the records consulted.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in LNCS 131 (1982), DOI 10.1007/bfb0025774; zbMATH 0546.68014 (1982) Crossref; zbMATH
    • Wikipedia says model checking began with Clarke, Emerson, Queille and Sifakis Wikipedia, Model checking (History)
  7. 1999

    Clarke-Grumberg-Peled monograph Model Checking

    consolidation

    Clarke, E. M.; Grumberg, O.; Peled, D., Model Checking, MIT Press (1999)

    A textbook treatment of model checking that consolidated the strand after its first decades.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists 'Model checking' (1999) by Edmund M. Clarke Jr. and Orna Grumberg Open Library search
