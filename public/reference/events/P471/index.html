1959 · Differential equations

Lojasiewicz-Simon: real-analytic gradient inequalities control geometric flows

A gradient inequality from real-analytic geometry gives convergence to a unique limit for gradient-like evolution equations such as harmonic map flow and mean curvature flow.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1959

    Lojasiewicz's inequality in the division paper

    first statement

    Lojasiewicz, Sur le probleme de la division, Studia Math. 18 (1959) 87-136

    Lojasiewicz proves the inequality bounding the distance to a zero set of a real-analytic function.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lojasiewicz, Sur le probleme de la division, Studia Math., DOI 10.4064/sm-18-1-87-136 Crossref
    • The Lojasiewicz inequality is named after Lojasiewicz (distance to zero set bound) Wikipedia: Lojasiewicz inequality
  2. 1963

    Polyak's gradient inequality

    precursor

    Polyak, Gradient methods for the minimisation of functionals, USSR Comput. Math. Math. Phys. 3(4) (1963) 864-878

    A special case of the Lojasiewicz inequality for minimisation, a precursor in optimisation rather than a priority claim.

    Evidence (2)
    • Polyak 1963, DOI 10.1016/0041-5553(63)90382-3 Crossref
    • A special case of the Lojasiewicz inequality, due to Polyak Wikipedia: Lojasiewicz inequality
  3. 1983

    Simon's asymptotics for geometric evolution equations

    first application

    Simon, Asymptotics for a class of nonlinear evolution equations, with applications to geometric problems, Annals of Math. 118 (1983)

    Simon adapts the analytic inequality to gradient-like evolution equations, giving convergence for geometric variational problems.

    Dating or attribution note

    The title states the geometric applications; the specific flows covered were not checked in the text.

    Evidence (1)
    • Simon 1983, Annals of Mathematics, DOI 10.2307/2006981 Crossref
  4. 2003

    Abstract gradient inequality for evolution equations

    key development

    Chill, On the Lojasiewicz-Simon gradient inequality, J. Funct. Anal. 201 (2003)

    Chill gives an abstract, functional-analytic form of the Lojasiewicz-Simon inequality for gradient-like flows.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chill 2003, J. Funct. Anal., DOI 10.1016/s0022-1236(02)00102-7 Crossref
  5. 2019

    General Lojasiewicz-Simon theory on Banach spaces

    consolidation

    Feehan, Maridakis, Lojasiewicz-Simon gradient inequalities for analytic and Morse-Bott functions on Banach spaces, J. reine angew. Math. (2019)

    A general, systematic treatment of the inequality on Banach spaces, covering analytic and Morse-Bott functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Feehan-Maridakis 2019, Crelle, DOI 10.1515/crelle-2019-0029 Crossref
