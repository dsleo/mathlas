1974 · Number theory

Function-field Langlands: Drinfeld and Lafforgue

Moduli of Drinfeld modules and shtukas, objects of algebraic geometry over finite fields, are used to prove the Langlands correspondence for function fields.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1974

    Drinfeld, Elliptic modules

    first statement

    V. G. Drinfeld, Elliptic modules, Mat. Sb. 94 (1974) (transl. Math. USSR Sb. 23)

    Introduces Drinfeld modules (elliptic modules), the tool for function-field arithmetic.

    Evidence (1)
    • Mat. Sb. 1974; zbMATH Zbl 0321.14014 Crossref
  2. 1977

    Drinfeld, Proof of the global Langlands conjecture for GL(2) over a function field

    first application

    V. G. Drinfeld, Funct. Anal. Appl. 11 (1977)

    Announces the proof for GL(2) using elliptic modules and shtukas.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title names the conjecture over a function field Crossref / zbMATH 0392.12007
  3. 1979

    Kazhdan, An introduction to Drinfeld's Shtuka

    naming

    D. Kazhdan, 1979 (zbMATH 3637986)

    Earliest indexed title using the term shtuka.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title search ti:shtuka (checked) zbMATH Open 3637986
  4. 1983

    Drinfeld, Two-dimensional l-adic representations of the fundamental group of a curve over a finite field

    key development

    V. G. Drinfeld, Amer. J. Math. 105 (1983)

    Full application of the geometric machinery to two-dimensional representations over a finite field.

    Evidence (1)
    • Amer. J. Math. 105 Crossref
  5. 2002

    L. Lafforgue, Chtoucas de Drinfeld et correspondance de Langlands

    key development

    L. Lafforgue, Invent. Math. 147 (2002) 1-241

    Proves the Langlands correspondence for GL(n) over function fields.

    Evidence (1)
    • Invent. Math. 147 Crossref
  6. 2002

    Fields Medal to L. Lafforgue (ICM Beijing)

    recognition

    ICM Beijing, 20 August 2002

    Fields Medal marking acceptance of the function-field result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Fields Medal to L. Lafforgue, ICM Beijing 2002 MacTutor Lafforgue
  7. 2018

    V. Lafforgue, Chtoucas pour les groupes reductifs et parametrisation de Langlands globale

    key development

    V. Lafforgue, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 31 (2018)

    Constructs the global Langlands parametrisation for reductive groups over function fields.

    Evidence (1)
    • JAMS 31 Crossref
