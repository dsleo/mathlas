1869 · Logic & foundations

Arithmetization of analysis and the construction of the real numbers

Weierstrass, Cantor and Dedekind defined real numbers from rationals via limits or cuts, removing reliance on geometric intuition.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1858

    Dedekind devises cuts (unpublished)

    precursor

    Dedekind worked out his definition of real numbers by cuts of the rationals but kept it private for years.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cuts were worked out in 1858 and published only in 1872. MacTutor, Real numbers 2
  2. 1865

    Weierstrass lectures on real numbers (unpublished)

    precursor

    Weierstrass, Berlin lectures from 1865 (not published at the time)

    Weierstrass gave his own theory of the real numbers in Berlin lectures that circulated through students, not in print.

    Evidence (1)
    • His theory was given in lectures beginning in 1865 and not published. MacTutor, Real numbers 2
  3. 1869

    Meray publishes an arithmetical theory of irrationals

    first statement

    Meray, Remarques sur la nature des quantites definies par la condition de servir de limites a des variables donnees, Revue des Societes savantes, 1869

    Meray defined irrational numbers as limits of convergent rational sequences, the first such theory to appear in print.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cited as the first published arithmetical theory of irrational numbers (1869). MacTutor, Meray biography
  4. 1872

    Heine's construction by fundamental sequences

    key development

    E. Heine, Die Elemente der Functionenlehre, J. reine angew. Math. 74, 1872

    Heine built real numbers from Cauchy-type rational sequences with arithmetic and order, following Cantor's idea.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref lists the paper in volume 74 (1872). Crossref
    • Heine introduced arithmetic operations and an order on his sequences. MacTutor, Real numbers 2
  5. 1872

    Cantor's real numbers from fundamental sequences

    key development

    G. Cantor, Ueber die Ausdehnung eines Satzes aus der Theorie der trigonometrischen Reihen, Math. Ann. 5, 1872

    Cantor defined real numbers as classes of rational Cauchy sequences within a paper on trigonometric series.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref records the paper in Math. Ann. 5 (1872). Crossref
    • Cantor published a version in 1872 using Cauchy sequences of rationals. MacTutor, Real numbers 2
  6. 1872

    Dedekind's cuts published

    key development

    R. Dedekind, Stetigkeit und irrationale Zahlen, Braunschweig: Vieweg, 1872

    Dedekind published his definition of the reals by partitions of the rationals, showing continuity without geometric appeal.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: Stetigkeit und irrationale Zahlen, Vieweg, 1872. Internet Archive
    • Dedekind published after realising Heine and Cantor were about to. MacTutor, Real numbers 2
  7. 1895

    Klein's 'On the arithmetization of mathematics' address

    naming

    F. Klein, On the arithmetization of mathematics, Nachr. Gottingen, Geschaeftl. Mitt. 1895, 82-91 (zbMATH 26.0467.01)

    Klein's 1895 Gottingen address has 'arithmetization' in its title; the earliest title use of the term found.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH 26.0467.01: Klein, 'On the arithmetization of mathematics', Gott. Nachr. Geschaeftl. Mitt. 1895, 82-91. zbMATH Open, document 26.0467.01
  8. 1930

    Landau's Grundlagen der Analysis

    consolidation

    E. Landau, Grundlagen der Analysis, 1930

    A textbook building naturals, rationals, reals and complexes step by step from Peano axioms.

    Evidence (1)
    • Contemporary review in Amer. Math. Monthly 37 (1930). Crossref (review record)
