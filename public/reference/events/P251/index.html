1933 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Haar measure and abstract harmonic analysis (Weil)

Haar measure and Pontryagin duality were systematised in a general setting.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1897

    Hurwitz invariant integral for Lie groups

    precursor

    Hurwitz introduced an invariant integral for Lie groups, the special case later generalised by Haar.

    Dating or attribution note

    Precursor from Wikipedia; the Hurwitz paper was not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Special case for Lie groups introduced by Hurwitz in 1897 as an invariant integral Wikipedia: Haar measure
  2. 1927

    Peter–Weyl completeness of representations of compact groups

    precursor

    F. Peter, H. Weyl, Die Vollständigkeit der primitiven Darstellungen einer geschlossenen kontinuierlichen Gruppe, Math. Ann. 97 (1927)

    Completeness of representations for compact groups, the compact precursor of harmonic analysis on groups.

    Dating or attribution note

    Significance (compact precursor) is from general knowledge; the paper was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • DOI record: Math. Ann. 97 (1927) Crossref
  3. 1933

    Haar introduces invariant measure on locally compact groups

    first statement

    A. Haar, Der Massbegriff in der Theorie der kontinuierlichen Gruppen, Ann. of Math. (2) 34 (1933)

    Introduced the invariant measure on locally compact topological groups, now called Haar measure.

    Evidence (3)
    • DOI record: Ann. of Math. 34 (1933) Crossref
    • Introduced by Haar in 1933 Wikipedia: Haar measure
    • In 1932 he introduced invariant measure, called Haar measure; paper in Annals 1933 MacTutor: Alfréd Haar
  4. 1934

    Pontryagin duality for locally compact abelian groups

    key development

    L. S. Pontryagin, The theory of topological commutative groups, Ann. of Math. (2) 35 (1934)

    Developed the character theory of locally compact abelian groups, giving the duality theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • DOI record: Ann. of Math. 35 (1934) Crossref
    • In 1934 Pontryagin introduced characters on locally compact abelian groups MacTutor: Lev Pontryagin
  5. 1934

    Pontryagin solves Hilbert's fifth problem for abelian groups

    first application

    L. S. Pontryagin, The theory of topological commutative groups, Ann. of Math. (2) 35 (1934)

    Used the character theory to settle Hilbert's fifth problem for abelian groups, the first target use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hilbert's Fifth Problem proved for abelian groups in 1934 using characters MacTutor: Lev Pontryagin
  6. 1940

    Weil: L'intégration dans les groupes topologiques et ses applications

    consolidation

    A. Weil, L'intégration dans les groupes topologiques et ses applications, Hermann (1940)

    First systematic monograph on integration on topological groups and its application to harmonic analysis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record (1940) Open Library
    • Book title in the Weil bibliography MacTutor: André Weil
  7. 1963

    Hewitt and Ross: Abstract Harmonic Analysis

    naming

    E. Hewitt, K. A. Ross, Abstract Harmonic Analysis I, Springer (1963)

    Title uses the field name "abstract harmonic analysis", later the standard name.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record (1963) Open Library
