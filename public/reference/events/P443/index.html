Elliptic integrals from lemniscate and elastica (Bernoulli)

Arc length of ellipses and spirals and the equation of a compressed elastic rod led Jacob Bernoulli to the lemniscate integral, treated by analogy with the arcsine.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1691

    Jakob Bernoulli poses the elastica problem

    precursor

    Bernoulli asks for the shape of a flexible rod bent by a weight, a problem later solved with elliptic functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia's lemniscate-functions article says Bernoulli posed this problem in 1691. Wikipedia, Lemniscate elliptic functions
  2. 1694

    Bernoulli introduces the lemniscate

    first statement

    Jakob Bernoulli, Acta Eruditorum, 1694

    Bernoulli conceived the figure-eight curve in 1694 as a variant of the ellipse, the curve whose arc length gives the lemniscate integral.

    Evidence (3)
    • MacTutor says the lemniscate of Bernoulli was first conceived by Jakob Bernoulli in 1694. MacTutor: Jacob Bernoulli
    • The curve was first studied in 1694 by Jakob Bernoulli as a modification of an ellipse. Wikipedia: Lemniscate of Bernoulli
    • Archive.org holds the 1694 volume of the Acta Eruditorum. archive.org item bub_gb_Wa8J4qKCS-0C
  3. 1718–1723

    Fagnano's work on the lemniscate arc

    key development

    Giulio Fagnano, work on the lemniscate, 1718-1723

    Fagnano studied the arc of the lemniscate and gave the first treatment of the associated elliptic functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • The lemniscate elliptic functions were first studied by Fagnano in 1718. Wikipedia: Lemniscate elliptic functions
  4. 1750

    Fagnano and Euler found the addition theorem for elliptic integrals

    key development

    Fagnano; Euler, c. 1750

    The sources date the first study of elliptic integrals to Fagnano and Euler around 1750.

    Evidence (1)
    • Elliptic integrals were first studied by Fagnano and Euler, c. 1750. Wikipedia: Elliptic integral
  5. 1750

    Fagnano's Produzioni matematiche

    key development

    Giulio Carlo Fagnano, Produzioni matematiche, Pesaro, 1750

    Collected papers, printing Fagnano's work on dividing the lemniscate and its arc.

    Dating or attribution note

    That the lemniscate papers are in this volume is taken from the catalogue title and Wikipedia's account of Fagnano; contents were not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Produzioni matematiche del marchese Giulio Carlo de' Toschi di Fagnano, 1750. Internet Archive record bub_gb_NA0NVGqz4V8C
  6. 1811

    Legendre's Exercices de calcul intégral

    consolidation

    Adrien-Marie Legendre, Exercices de calcul intégral, Paris, 1811-1817

    Systematic treatment of elliptic integrals, reduced to three standard (Legendre) forms.

    Evidence (2)
    • Exercices de calcul intégral sur divers ordres de transcendantes, 1811. Internet Archive record exercicescalculi01legerich
    • Every elliptic integral reduces to the three Legendre canonical forms. Wikipedia, Elliptic integral
  7. 1827

    Abel's Recherches sur les fonctions elliptiques

    key development

    N. H. Abel, Recherches sur les fonctions elliptiques, Journal fuer die reine und angewandte Mathematik 2, 1827

    Studies the inverse functions of elliptic integrals, turning the lemniscate integral into a doubly periodic function.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, vol. 2 (1827), pp. 101-181. Crossref 10.1515/crll.1827.2.101
    • Wikipedia dates Abel's work 1827-1828. Wikipedia, Lemniscate elliptic functions
  8. 1829

    Jacobi's Fundamenta nova

    key development

    C. G. J. Jacobi, Fundamenta nova theoriae functionum ellipticarum, Koenigsberg, 1829

    Builds the theory of elliptic functions systematically from the inverse functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Digitised record dated 1829. Internet Archive record fundamentanovat00jacogoog
