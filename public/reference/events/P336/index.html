Paracontrolled distributions

Paraproduct calculus gave an alternative to regularity structures for singular SPDEs.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2003

    Controlled rough paths

    precursor

    M. Gubinelli, Controlling Rough Paths, arXiv:math/0306433 (2003)

    Controlled rough paths supply one of the ingredients later adapted to singular PDEs.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted June 2003 arXiv
  2. 2012

    Paracontrolled distributions introduced

    first statement

    M. Gubinelli, P. Imkeller and N. Perkowski, Paracontrolled distributions and singular PDEs, arXiv:1210.2684 (2012)

    The paper combines paradifferential calculus with controlled rough paths for singular PDEs.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted October 2012 arXiv
  3. 2012

    Paracontrolled method applied to model SPDEs

    first application

    M. Gubinelli, P. Imkeller and N. Perkowski, Paracontrolled distributions and singular PDEs, arXiv:1210.2684 (2012)

    The authors treat fractional-Brownian, Burgers-type and parabolic-Anderson model problems.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract explicitly lists the model problems arXiv
  4. 2015

    Paracontrolled distributions and singular PDEs

    key development

    Gubinelli, Imkeller, Perkowski, Paracontrolled distributions and singular PDEs, Forum Math. Pi (2015)

    Develops the paracontrolled calculus into a full solution method for singular PDEs, a main later development.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2015 Forum Math. Pi paper on paracontrolled distributions and singular PDEs zbMATH Open record
