1994 · Topology

Seiberg-Witten invariants

Witten's physical dual to Donaldson theory gave monopole equations that quickly proved the Thom conjecture.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1994

    Monopole four-manifold invariants

    first statement

    Monopoles and Four-Manifolds (1994)

    Witten’s public preprint proposes using monopole equations for smooth four-manifold invariants.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated record and abstract support this 1994 milestone. Original publication or repository record
  2. 1996

    The Seiberg-Witten equations and applications to the topology of smooth four-manifolds

    consolidation

    Morgan, The Seiberg-Witten Equations and Applications to the Topology of Smooth Four-Manifolds, Princeton (1996)

    The first book-length treatment of the Seiberg-Witten equations and their application to four-manifold topology.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 1996 book by Morgan on the Seiberg-Witten equations and four-manifolds zbMATH Open record
  3. 1996

    Seiberg-Witten implies Gromov (SW => Gr)

    key development

    Taubes, SW => Gr: From the Seiberg-Witten equations to pseudo-holomorphic curves, JAMS (1996)

    Proves that Seiberg-Witten invariants equal Gromov invariants, linking the new invariants to pseudo-holomorphic curves.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 1996 JAMS paper by Taubes, SW => Gr zbMATH Open record
  4. 2007

    Monopoles and three-manifolds

    key development

    Kronheimer, Mrowka, Monopoles and Three-Manifolds, Cambridge University Press (2007)

    A monograph applying monopole (Seiberg-Witten) Floer theory to three-manifold topology.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2007 Cambridge monograph by Kronheimer and Mrowka on monopoles and three-manifolds zbMATH Open record
