Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2006

    Seiberg–Witten proof of Weinstein posted

    first statement

    C. H. Taubes, The Seiberg-Witten equations and the Weinstein conjecture (2006)

    Taubes presents the three-dimensional closed-Reeb-orbit theorem using gauge-theoretic methods.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents seiberg–witten proof of weinstein posted in 2006 arXiv
  2. 2006

    Gauge theory proves closed Reeb orbit

    first application

    C. H. Taubes, The Seiberg-Witten equations and the Weinstein conjecture (2006)

    The Seiberg–Witten equations are used intentionally to prove the Weinstein conjecture in dimension three.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents gauge theory proves closed reeb orbit in 2006 arXiv
  3. 2008

    ECH–Seiberg–Witten bridge strengthens Weinstein results

    key development

    M. Hutchings and C. H. Taubes, The Weinstein conjecture for stable Hamiltonian structures (2008)

    Hutchings and Taubes apply the ECH/Floer equivalence to stable Hamiltonian structures.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents ech–seiberg–witten bridge strengthens weinstein results in 2008 arXiv
  4. 2010

    Embedded contact homology survey

    consolidation

    M. Hutchings, Embedded contact homology and its applications (2010)

    Hutchings surveys the Floer-theoretic bridge and Reeb-dynamics applications.
