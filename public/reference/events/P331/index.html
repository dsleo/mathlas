2010 · Number theory

Inverse theorem for Gowers norms and Mobius-nilsequences

Green-Tao-Ziegler proved nilsequences control Gowers norms, settling linear equations in primes counts.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2006

    Prime-pattern strategy is stated conditionally

    precursor

    B. Green and T. Tao, Linear Equations in Primes (2006)

    The 2006 paper makes the prime-counting goal conditional on the inverse theorem and Möbius–nilsequence conjecture.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents prime-pattern strategy is stated conditionally in 2006 arXiv
  2. 2008

    Möbius orthogonality to nilsequences proved

    precursor

    B. Green and T. Tao, The Mobius function is strongly orthogonal to nilsequences (2008)

    One of the two needed inputs is established independently in 2008.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents möbius orthogonality to nilsequences proved in 2008 arXiv
  3. 2010

    General Gowers inverse theorem announced

    first statement

    B. Green, T. Tao and T. Ziegler, An inverse theorem for the Gowers U^{s+1}[N]-norm (announcement) (2010)

    The June 2010 public announcement states nilsequence control for all higher Gowers norms.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents general gowers inverse theorem announced in 2010 arXiv
  4. 2010

    Inverse theorem yields prime-pattern asymptotics

    first application

    B. Green, T. Tao and T. Ziegler, An inverse theorem for the Gowers U^{s+1}[N]-norm (announcement) (2010)

    The announcement identifies unconditional consequences for finite-complexity linear equations in primes.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents inverse theorem yields prime-pattern asymptotics in 2010 arXiv
  5. 2010

    Full inverse-theorem proof posted

    key development

    B. Green, T. Tao and T. Ziegler, An inverse theorem for the Gowers U^{s+1}[N]-norm (2010)

    The September 2010 preprint supplies the detailed proof following the June announcement.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents full inverse-theorem proof posted in 2010 arXiv
  6. 2014

    Nilmanifold-dynamics survey

    consolidation

    T. Ziegler, Linear equations in primes and dynamics of nilmanifolds (2014)

    A survey explains the number-theoretic and ergodic-theoretic chain.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents nilmanifold-dynamics survey in 2014 arXiv
