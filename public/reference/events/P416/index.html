1920 · Applied mathematics

Mathematical biology

Volterra and Lotka modeled predator-prey dynamics.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1910

    Lotka's autocatalytic oscillation model

    precursor

    Lotka first wrote the interacting-rate equations in a chemical (autocatalytic) setting before the biological use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia reports the Lotka-Volterra model was first proposed by Lotka for autocatalytic chemical reactions in 1910 Wikipedia: Lotka-Volterra equations (raw wikitext)
  2. 1920

    Lotka's analytical note on rhythmic relations in organic systems

    first statement

    A. J. Lotka, 'Analytical Note on Certain Rhythmic Relations in Organic Systems', Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 6(7), 410-415, 1920

    Lotka stated coupled equations for an oscillating organic system of a plant and an herbivore, which is the earliest public form of the model.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lotka 1920 PNAS article exists with this title and year Crossref DOI 10.1073/pnas.6.7.410
    • Wikipedia says Lotka extended the model in 1920 to organic systems with a plant and an herbivore Wikipedia: Lotka-Volterra equations (raw wikitext)
  3. 1926

    Volterra's predator-prey fluctuations

    first application

    V. Volterra, 'Fluctuations in the Abundance of a Species considered Mathematically', Nature 118, 558-560, 1926

    Volterra applied the coupled equations to two interacting animal species, the first purposeful predator-prey use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Volterra 1926 Nature article with this title exists Crossref DOI 10.1038/118558a0
  4. 1931

    Volterra's Lecons on the mathematical theory of the struggle for life

    consolidation

    V. Volterra, Lecons sur la theorie mathematique de la lutte pour la vie, Gauthier-Villars, 1931

    Volterra's monograph gathered the predator-prey and competition theory into a systematic book.

    Evidence (2)
    • Book with this title, publisher and year 1931 listed Open Library search (first_publish_year 1931, Gauthier-Villars)
    • Doob's 1936 Bulletin AMS review of the book exists Crossref DOI 10.1090/s0002-9904-1936-06292-0
