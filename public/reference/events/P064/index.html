1976 · Computer science

Public-key cryptography

Modular exponentiation, discrete logarithms, factoring and later elliptic curves are used to build secure protocols, turning classical number theory into infrastructure.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1970

    Ellis's idea of non-secret encryption

    precursor

    Ellis, J. H., The Possibility of Secure Non-secret Digital Encryption, GCHQ internal paper, January 1970

    An internal GCHQ paper conceived the possibility of non-secret encryption but no practical method, and was not published until 1997.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ellis conceived non-secret encryption in 1970 at GCHQ Wikipedia, Public-key cryptography (citing the NSA archive copy of Ellis's paper)
  2. 1973

    Cocks's internal RSA-type scheme

    precursor

    A GCHQ internal scheme implementing a practical non-secret encryption method, unpublished until declassification.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cocks implemented a method later known as RSA in 1973 while at GCHQ Wikipedia, Public-key cryptography
  3. 1976

    Diffie-Hellman public key agreement

    first statement

    Diffie, W., Hellman, M. E., New directions in cryptography, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 1976

    The first published method of public key agreement over an open channel, using exponentiation in a finite field.

    Evidence (2)
    • New directions in cryptography, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory, 1976 Crossref record
    • The scheme was published by Diffie and Hellman in 1976 Wikipedia, Diffie-Hellman key exchange
  4. 1978

    RSA encryption and signature system

    first application

    Rivest, R. L., Shamir, A., Adleman, L., A method for obtaining digital signatures and public-key cryptosystems, Communications of the ACM, 1978

    A practical public-key encryption and signature system based on factoring, the first purposeful use of the idea for encryption.

    Evidence (2)
    • A method for obtaining digital signatures and public-key cryptosystems, CACM, 1978 Crossref record
    • RSA was published in 1978 and is an implementation of public-key cryptography Wikipedia, Public-key cryptography
  5. 1978

    Merkle's public key distribution puzzles

    precursor

    Merkle, R. C., Secure communications over insecure channels, Communications of the ACM, 1978

    Merkle's public-key distribution method influenced Diffie-Hellman; the work was conceived earlier, but its publication year is used here.

    Evidence (2)
    • Secure communications over insecure channels, CACM, 1978 Crossref record
    • Merkle's work influenced Diffie-Hellman, and Diffie-Hellman named his protocol in 1978 Wikipedia, Diffie-Hellman key exchange
  6. 1978

    Rivest, Shamir and Adleman, 'A method for obtaining digital signatures and public-key cryptosystems'

    naming

    R. L. Rivest, A. Shamir, L. Adleman, 'A method for obtaining digital signatures and public-key cryptosystems', Communications of the ACM, pages 120-126, zbMATH record 3575463, 1978

    The 1978 RSA paper uses the term 'public-key cryptosystems' in its title, the earliest exact-term title found in the searched window.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record gives title 'A method for obtaining digital signatures and public-key cryptosystems', three authors, year 1978, pages 120-126, ISSN 0001-0782 (Communications of the ACM) zbMATH Open API, document 3575463
  7. 1986

    Elliptic curves proposed for cryptography

    key development

    Miller, V. S., Use of elliptic curves in cryptography, CRYPTO 1985, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, 1986

    Proposes elliptic curves over finite fields as a basis for public-key systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Use of elliptic curves in cryptography, LNCS (CRYPTO 1985 proceedings), 1986 Crossref record (DOI 10.1007/3-540-39799-X_31) and zbMATH record 3948147
  8. 1987

    Elliptic curve cryptosystems

    key development

    Koblitz, N., Elliptic curve cryptosystems, Mathematics of Computation, 1987

    Develops elliptic-curve cryptosystems in a form that became the standard route for public-key elliptic-curve cryptography.

    Dating or attribution note

    Later uses are not checked; the 'standard route' claim is editorial.

    Evidence (1)
    • Elliptic curve cryptosystems, Math. Comp., 1987 Crossref record
