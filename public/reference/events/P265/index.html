1939 · Optimization & control

Kantorovich linear programming

Production-planning problems were posed as linear optimisation with multipliers, with solution methods.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1938–1939

    Plywood-industry allocation problem

    precursor

    Kantorovich's consultation with a plywood laboratory posed machine allocation as a linear optimisation problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • In 1938-1939 Kantorovich worked with a plywood-industry laboratory on allocating machines to products MacTutor, Leonid Kantorovich biography
  2. 1939

    Kantorovich's Mathematical Methods of Organizing and Planning Production

    first statement

    Kantorovich, Mathematical Methods of Organizing and Planning Production (Leningrad, 1939); English translation Management Science 6 (1960) 366-422

    Kantorovich set out linear optimisation for production planning, the first public statement of the method.

    Evidence (2)
    • Kantorovich, Mathematical Methods of Organizing and Planning Production, Leningrad (1939); English translation Management Science 6 (1960) 366-422 Crossref DOI 10.1287/mnsc.6.4.366 (1960 translation); 1939 year from history pages
    • Kantorovich devised the technique now known as linear programming in 1939 Wikipedia, Leonid Kantorovich
  3. 1947

    Dantzig's unpublished Air Force planning model and simplex method

    precursor

    Dantzig formulated the general linear programming model and the simplex method for US Air Force planning, without publishing it at the time.

    Evidence (1)
    • From 1946 to 1947 Dantzig developed the linear programming formulation for US Air Force planning; the simplex method was invented in 1947 Wikipedia, Linear programming (history section)
  4. 1949

    Koopmans's transportation-system optimisation

    key development

    Koopmans, Optimum utilization of the transportation system, Econometrica 17 (suppl.) (1949) 136

    Koopmans formulated classical economic problems as linear programs, showing the method applied to economics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Koopmans, Optimum utilization of the transportation system, Econometrica 17 (suppl.) (1949) 136 Crossref DOI 10.2307/1907301
    • Koopmans formulated classical economic problems as linear programs Wikipedia, Linear programming (history section)
  5. 1951

    Dantzig's public statement in the Cowles activity-analysis volume

    key development

    Dantzig, Maximization of a linear function of variables subject to linear inequalities, in Koopmans (ed.), Activity Analysis of Production and Allocation (1951)

    Dantzig's linear-programming statement appeared in the Cowles Commission volume edited by Koopmans in 1951.

    Evidence (2)
    • Activity Analysis of Production and Allocation (Cowles Commission, 1951), ed. Koopmans; chapter by Dantzig Open Library search record (first publish year 1951)
    • Dantzig's chapter 'Maximization of a linear function of variables subject to linear inequalities' is cited for the simplex method Wikipedia, Linear programming (references)
  6. 1953

    Charnes, Cooper and Henderson's An Introduction to Linear Programming

    consolidation

    Charnes, Cooper, Henderson, An Introduction to Linear Programming, Wiley (1953)

    An early textbook presenting linear programming as a teachable subject.

    Dating or attribution note

    Wikipedia calls it the first textbook; that priority claim was not checked against other books.

    Evidence (2)
    • Charnes, Cooper, Henderson, An Introduction to Linear Programming, Wiley (1953) Open Library search record (first publish year 1953)
    • In 1953 Charnes and Cooper published the first textbook on linear programming Wikipedia, Operations research (history section)
  7. 1975

    Nobel Memorial Prize shared by Kantorovich and Koopmans

    recognition

    Kantorovich and Koopmans shared the 1975 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for optimum allocation of resources.

    Evidence (1)
    • The 1975 economics prize was shared by Kantorovich and Koopmans Wikipedia, Leonid Kantorovich; MacTutor biography
