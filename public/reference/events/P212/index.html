1960 · Algebraic geometry

Etale cohomology

Grothendieck topologies gave cohomology theories for varieties over finite fields as required by Weil.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1949

    Weil's conjectures on zeta functions over finite fields

    precursor

    Weil, Numbers of solutions of equations in finite fields, Bull. AMS 55 (1949) 497-508

    Weil states the conjectures that a cohomology theory for varieties over finite fields was meant to prove.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record for Weil 1949 in Bull. AMS Crossref
    • MacTutor Weil: Weil proved the Riemann hypothesis for curves over finite fields and the conjectures were posed MacTutor Weil biography
  2. 1960

    Grothendieck proposes a Weil cohomology from unramified coverings

    first statement

    A. Grothendieck, The cohomology theory of abstract algebraic varieties, Proc. International Congress of Mathematicians (Edinburgh, 1958), Cambridge Univ. Press, 1960, pp. 103-118

    Grothendieck proposes defining the Weil cohomology from Galois cohomology and local triviality on unramified coverings, the starting point of etale cohomology.

    Evidence (3)
    • The text says the Weil cohomology must come from a different approach, suggested by sheaf and Galois cohomology and unramified coverings, and that this opens a route to the Weil conjectures. Copy of the paper text (ICM 1958 lecture)
    • zbMATH dates the paper 1960. zbMATH Open document 3194966
    • Wikipedia gives Grothendieck (1960) as the introduction of etale cohomology. Wikipedia, Etale cohomology
  3. 1962

    Artin's Harvard seminar notes on Grothendieck topologies

    precursor

    M. Artin, Grothendieck topologies, Notes on a seminar, Spring 1962, Harvard University, Dept. of Mathematics (mimeographed)

    Artin's mimeographed seminar notes develop Grothendieck topologies and the cohomology of schemes in a form that circulated before SGA 4.

    Evidence (2)
    • Reference [1] of Bourbaki exp. 279: Artin, Grothendieck topologies, Notes on a seminar, Spring 1962, Harvard University (multigraphie). Numdam, Seminaire Bourbaki exp. 279, bibliography
    • Wikipedia: Grothendieck and Artin worked out the foundations, published as (Artin 1962) and SGA 4. Wikipedia, Etale cohomology
  4. 1966

    Lefschetz formula and rationality of L-functions via etale methods

    first application

    Grothendieck, Lefschetz formula and rationality of L-functions, Seminaire Bourbaki 1964/65 (published 1966)

    Grothendieck applies etale cohomology to the Lefschetz formula and the rationality of L-functions, the first purposeful use on the Weil problems.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH record 'Lefschetz formula and rationality of L-functions' by Grothendieck, dated 1966 zbMATH
    • Wikipedia: Grothendieck used etale cohomology to prove some of the Weil conjectures Wikipedia Etale cohomology
    • Bourbaki exp. 279, dated December 1964, 1964/65, printed 1966: proof of rationality of L-functions via etale cohomology and the Lefschetz formula. Numdam, Seminaire Bourbaki vol. 9, exp. 279
  5. 1972

    SGA 4: systematic foundations of etale cohomology

    consolidation

    Artin, Grothendieck, Verdier (eds.), Theorie des topos et cohomologie etale des schemas, SGA 4, Springer LNM 269 (1972)

    The seminar notes give the first systematic treatment of etale cohomology and its topos foundations.

    Evidence (3)
    • Open Library record for the Springer 1972 volume SGA 4 (Michael Artin as author of the entry) Open Library
    • Crossref: Theorie des Topos et Cohomologie Etale des Schemas (Springer LNM), 1972. Crossref API
    • Crossref: Theorie des Topos et Cohomologie Etale des Schemas, second record, 1972. Crossref API
  6. 1974

    Deligne's proof of the Weil conjectures (l-adic cohomology)

    key development

    Deligne, La conjecture de Weil I, Publ. Math. IHES 43 (1974)

    Deligne proves the remaining Riemann hypothesis part of the Weil conjectures using l-adic etale cohomology.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: La conjecture de Weil I, Publ. Math. IHES, 1974 Crossref
    • Wikipedia: the Riemann-hypothesis analogue was proved by Deligne in 1974 using l-adic cohomology Wikipedia Etale cohomology
  7. 1980

    Milne's textbook on etale cohomology

    consolidation

    Milne, Etale Cohomology, Princeton University Press (1980)

    The first monograph treatment at textbook level after SGA 4.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record: Etale cohomology, Princeton University Press, 1980 Open Library
    • Crossref: Etale Cohomology (PMS-33), Princeton University Press, 1980. Crossref API
