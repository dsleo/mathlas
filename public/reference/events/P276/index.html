Blum-Shub-Smale real computation

A machine model over the reals combined Turing computability with numerical analysis.

  2. 1989

    NP-completeness of quartic-root existence in the real BSS model

    first application

    Blum, Shub, Smale, 'On a theory of computation and complexity over the real numbers: NP-completeness, recursive functions and universal machines', Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 21 (1989) 1-46

    The new real-number machine model is used to prove that deciding the existence of a root of a quartic polynomial is NP-complete over the reals.

    Evidence (2)
    • The BSS model is used to show NP-completeness of the existence of roots of quartic polynomials (1989 paper). Wikipedia, Blum-Shub-Smale machine (raw wikitext), history/theory section and citation of zbMATH 0681.03020
    • Bibliographic record: Blum, Shub, Smale, real-number computation NP-completeness paper, year 1989, pages 1-46 (Bull. AMS). zbMATH Open record 4114604
  3. 1998

    Monograph on real computation

    consolidation

    Complexity and Real Computation (1998)

    The Springer book gives a systematic treatment of real computation.
