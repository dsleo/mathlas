1977 · Topology

Rational homotopy theory via differential forms

Homotopy types are encoded by commutative differential graded algebras of polynomial forms, so rational homotopy becomes homological algebra.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1951

    Rational homotopy groups of spheres

    precursor

    Serre computed the rational homotopy groups of spheres, an early computation the later theory generalised.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia (Rational homotopy theory): rational homotopy groups of spheres computed by Serre in 1951 Wikipedia
  2. 1969

    Rational homotopy via DG Lie algebras

    precursor

    Daniel Quillen, Rational homotopy theory, Annals of Mathematics, 1969

    Quillen gives a parallel public founding of rational homotopy theory using differential graded Lie algebras, not differential forms.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals of Mathematics paper 'Rational Homotopy Theory', 1969 Crossref
  3. 1970

    Sullivan's MIT notes on localisation and minimal models

    precursor

    Dennis Sullivan, Geometric topology: localization, periodicity and Galois symmetry, MIT notes, 1970

    Sullivan's unpublished 1970 notes contain the differential-form and localisation ideas that later appear publicly.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: Sullivan produced notes in 1970 on localisation and periodicity; published in 2005 MacTutor
  4. 1976

    Closed geodesics via rational homotopy

    precursor

    Dennis Sullivan, Micheline Vigue-Poirrier, The homology theory of the closed geodesic problem, Journal of Differential Geometry, 1976

    Sullivan and Vigue-Poirrier use the rational model to prove that many manifolds have infinitely many closed geodesics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal of Differential Geometry paper with this title, 1976 Crossref
    • Wikipedia (Rational homotopy theory): geodesic theorem by Sullivan and Vigue-Poirrier (1976) Wikipedia
  5. 1977

    Minimal models from differential forms

    first statement

    Dennis Sullivan, Infinitesimal computations in topology, Publications Mathematiques IHES, 1977

    Sullivan's paper gives the public forms-based model: commutative differential graded algebras of polynomial forms encode rational homotopy types.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publications Mathematiques IHES paper 'Infinitesimal computations in topology', 1977 Crossref
  6. 2001

    Graduate-text treatment of rational homotopy

    consolidation

    Yves Felix, Stephen Halperin, Jean-Claude Thomas, Rational Homotopy Theory, Springer (GTM 205), 2001

    A standard graduate text treating the rational homotopy theory systematically.

    Evidence (1)
    • Springer book 'Rational Homotopy Theory' (2001), Crossref record for a chapter in that volume Crossref
