1895 · Analysis

Borel sets and measure

Borel introduced a hierarchy of sets generated from open sets and applied countable additivity.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1895

    Borel's thesis on sets and functions

    first statement

    Borel, E., Sur quelques points de la theorie des fonctions, Annales Scientifiques de l'Ecole Normale Superieure, 1895

    Borel's thesis introduces the sets obtained from open sets by countable operations, the first appearance of the Borel class.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appears in Annales de l'ENS in 1895, pages 9-55 zbMATH record, Zbl 26.0429.03
    • MacTutor says the ideas of measure theory first appear in this paper MacTutor biography of Borel
  2. 1898

    Borel's Lecons sur la theorie des fonctions

    consolidation

    Borel, E., Lecons sur la theorie des fonctions, Gauthier-Villars, 1898

    Borel's lectures give the first systematic treatment of the theory of sets and measure in his setting.

    Dating or attribution note

    The systematic-treatment role is from general knowledge of the book; the text was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lectures on the theory of functions, 1898 (English title in zbMATH) zbMATH record, Zbl 29.0336.01
  3. 1901

    Lebesgue's measure

    key development

    Lebesgue, H., Sur une generalisation de l'integrale definie, Comptes Rendus, 1901

    Lebesgue builds measure on Borel sets, making countable additivity central to analysis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Note appears in Comptes Rendus in 1901, pages 1025-1028 zbMATH record, Zbl 32.0299.01
    • MacTutor says Lebesgue formulated measure in 1901 building on Borel MacTutor biography of Lebesgue
  4. 1909

    Borel's countable probabilities

    first application

    Borel, E., Les probabilites denombrables et leurs applications arithmetiques, Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo, 1909

    Borel applies countable additivity to probability, the first purposeful use of the measure idea outside analysis.

    Dating or attribution note

    Its use of countable additivity is from general knowledge; the paper text was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper appears in Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo in 1909 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf03019651
