1954 · Topology

Cobordism: homotopy computes manifold invariants

Manifolds up to cobordism are classified by homotopy groups of Thom spaces, turning geometric questions into computations in stable homotopy theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1954

    Thom's global properties of differentiable manifolds

    first statement

    Thom, Quelques proprietes globales des varietes differentiables, Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici 28 (1954) 17-86

    Thom's public statement that classifies manifolds up to cobordism through homotopy of Thom spaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • In 1954 Thom proved the unoriented cobordism ring is a polynomial algebra over F2 Wikipedia: Cobordism
  2. 1954

    Unoriented cobordism ring computed (same paper)

    first application

    Thom, Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici 28 (1954) 17-86 (as above)

    The same paper computes the unoriented cobordism ring, the first purposeful use of the idea on a manifold invariant.

    Evidence (2)
    • Computation of the unoriented cobordism ring in 1954 Wikipedia: Cobordism
    • Same publication as first_statement Crossref
  3. 1960

    Complex analogue of cobordism

    key development

    Milnor, On the cobordism ring Omega* and a complex analogue, Part I, American Journal of Mathematics 82 (1960) 505

    Introduces the complex analogue of cobordism, the start of complex cobordism theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title, journal, volume, page and year verified Crossref
  4. 1968

    Notes on Cobordism Theory (book)

    consolidation

    Stong, Notes on Cobordism Theory, Princeton University Press, 1968

    Book-length exposition of cobordism theory.
