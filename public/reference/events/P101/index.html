Virtual fundamental cycles via homotopical algebra

Homotopy-coherent methods give a rigorous construction of virtual fundamental cycles on moduli spaces of pseudoholomorphic curves, underpinning Floer and Fukaya theory.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1996

    Intrinsic normal cone and virtual class

    precursor

    K. Behrend and B. Fantechi, The intrinsic normal cone, arXiv:alg-geom/9601010 (1996)

    An earlier algebraic construction of virtual classes provides a distinct foundation.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted January 1996 arXiv
  2. 2013

    Pardon introduces implicit-atlas VFC package

    first statement

    J. Pardon, An algebraic approach to virtual fundamental cycles on moduli spaces of pseudo-holomorphic curves, arXiv:1309.2370 (2013)

    The preprint presents an algebraic, sheaf-theoretic virtual-cycle construction using implicit atlases.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted September 2013 arXiv
  3. 2013

    Implicit-atlas package applied to symplectic invariants

    first application

    J. Pardon, An algebraic approach to virtual fundamental cycles on moduli spaces of pseudo-holomorphic curves, arXiv:1309.2370 (2013)

    The paper gives definitions of Gromov–Witten invariants and Hamiltonian Floer homology and an S1-localization calculation.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract explicitly names these applications arXiv
  4. 2016

    Pardon's algebraic construction of virtual cycles

    key development

    Pardon, J., An algebraic approach to virtual fundamental cycles on moduli spaces of pseudo-holomorphic curves, Geometry & Topology 20 (2016)

    Gives an algebraic route to virtual fundamental cycles on moduli spaces of pseudo-holomorphic curves.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, G&T 20 (2016) Crossref
  5. 2019

    Virtual cycles applied to contact homology

    key development

    Pardon, J., Contact homology and virtual fundamental cycles, Journal of the AMS (2019)

    Applies the virtual-cycle machinery to define contact homology in general, a main later application.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, JAMS (2019) Crossref
