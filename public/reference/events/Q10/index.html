2025 · Algebraic geometry

Non-rationality of cubic fourfolds attacked via mirror-symmetry-inspired Hodge atoms

Curve counts on a fourfold, an idea inspired by mirror symmetry, split its Hodge structure into atoms and show that degree-3 five-variable equations cannot be parametrised.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2023

    Iritani's quantum-cohomology blowup theorem

    precursor

    H. Iritani, Quantum cohomology of blowups, arXiv:2307.13555 (2023)

    The later Hodge-atoms paper explicitly says its blowup behavior accords with this theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted in July 2023 arXiv
  2. 2025

    Hodge atoms and cubic-fourfold result posted

    first statement

    L. Katzarkov, M. Kontsevich, T. Pantev and T. Y. Yu, Birational Invariants from Hodge Structures and Quantum Multiplication, arXiv:2508.05105 (2025)

    The public preprint introduces Hodge atoms and states irrationality for very general cubic fourfolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Version 1 was public in August 2025 and contains this claim arXiv
  3. 2025

    Hodge atoms obstruct rationality

    first application

    L. Katzarkov, M. Kontsevich, T. Pantev and T. Y. Yu, Birational Invariants from Hodge Structures and Quantum Multiplication, arXiv:2508.05105 (2025)

    The authors apply the new invariants to prove that a very general cubic fourfold is not rational.

    Evidence (2)
    • Version 1 was public in August 2025 arXiv
    • Version 1 explicitly states and proves the very-general-cubic-fourfold result Original preprint, version 1
