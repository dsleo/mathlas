1976 · Analysis

Pattern theory and variational image models (Grenander, Mumford)

Statistical and variational models of images and signals are developed as a mathematical theory of perception and vision.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1976

    Grenander's Pattern Synthesis

    first statement

    Grenander, U., Pattern Synthesis, Lectures in Pattern Theory vol. I, Springer, 1976

    Introduces pattern theory as a framework for patterns in signals and images, the first public statement of the idea.

    Evidence (2)
    • Pattern synthesis. Lectures in pattern theory, vol. I, 1976 zbMATH record 3523118 and Open Library (Pattern synthesis, Springer-Verlag, 1976)
    • The term Pattern Theory was introduced by Grenander in the 1970s MacTutor biography of David Mumford
  2. 1976

    Series title 'Lectures in Pattern Theory'

    naming

    Grenander, U., Pattern Synthesis, Lectures in Pattern Theory vol. I, Springer, 1976

    The name 'pattern theory' first appears in the series title of the same 1976 volume.

    Evidence (1)
    • The 1976 volume is titled Lectures in Pattern Theory vol. I zbMATH record 3523118
  3. 1989

    Mumford-Shah variational segmentation model

    first application

    Mumford, D., Shah, J., Optimal approximations by piecewise smooth functions and associated variational problems, Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics, 1989

    A variational model for image segmentation with piecewise smooth approximations, the first purposeful variational image model of this kind.

    Dating or attribution note

    Whether it is the first variational image model overall is not verified.

    Evidence (1)
    • Optimal approximations by piecewise smooth functions and associated variational problems, CPAM, 1989 Crossref record
  4. 1994

    Pattern theory as a unifying perspective

    key development

    Mumford, D., Pattern Theory: A Unifying Perspective, First European Congress of Mathematics, 1994

    Mumford's statement that pattern theory unifies probabilistic and variational models of vision, extending the programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Pattern theory: a unifying perspective, First European Congress of Mathematics, 1994 Crossref record
  5. 2010

    Mumford and Desolneux book

    consolidation

    Mumford, D., Desolneux, A., Pattern Theory, CRC Press, 2010

    A book-length treatment of pattern theory and its stochastic models of signals.

    Evidence (1)
    • Pattern Theory, CRC Press, 2010, by Mumford and Desolneux Open Library record
