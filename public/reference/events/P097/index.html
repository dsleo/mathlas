Supersymmetry and Morse theory

Supersymmetric quantum mechanics gives an analytic proof of the Morse inequalities, a template for later physics-inspired invariants.

Also known as: Supersymmetric Morse theory

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1981

    Dynamical breaking of supersymmetry

    precursor

    Witten, Dynamical breaking of supersymmetry, Nucl. Phys. B (1981)

    Supersymmetric field theory work that is the background to the supersymmetric quantum mechanics used in 1982.

    Evidence (1)
    • Nucl. Phys. B 1981 paper Crossref / publisher record
  2. 1982

    Analytic proof of the Morse inequalities

    first statement

    Witten, Supersymmetry and Morse theory, J. Differential Geom. (1982)

    Shows that a supersymmetric deformed de Rham complex gives an analytic proof of the Morse inequalities.

    Evidence (2)
    • J. Differential Geom. 1982 paper 'Supersymmetry and Morse theory' (DOI 10.4310/jdg/1214437492) Crossref / publisher record
    • In 1982 Witten developed an analytic approach to the Morse inequalities Wikipedia: Morse theory
  3. 1982

    Morse inequalities from perturbed de Rham complex

    first application

    Witten, Supersymmetry and Morse theory, J. Differential Geom. (1982)

    The same 1982 paper applies the construction to the Morse inequalities, which is the first purposeful use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1982 paper Crossref / publisher record
  4. 1982

    Witten, 'Supersymmetry and Morse theory'

    naming

    E. Witten, 'Supersymmetry and Morse theory', Journal of Differential Geometry 17 (1982), DOI 10.4310/jdg/1214437492

    Witten's 1982 paper names both fields, supersymmetry and Morse theory, in its title, the field name of this event.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record gives title 'Supersymmetry and Morse theory', author Witten, container Journal of Differential Geometry, year 1982 Crossref, DOI 10.4310/jdg/1214437492
  5. 1988

    Floer theory from Witten's approach

    key development

    Floer, Morse theory for Lagrangian intersections, J. Differential Geom. (1988)

    Builds infinite-dimensional Morse theory for Lagrangian intersections, extending the analytic idea.

    Evidence (1)
    • J. Differential Geom. 1988 paper Crossref / publisher record
