2022 · Number theory

Moment problem for class-group statistics (Sawin-Wood)

A general theorem on when moments determine a distribution of group-valued random objects was used to recover class-group distributions and to correct earlier predictions.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1984

    Cohen-Lenstra heuristics on class groups

    precursor

    Cohen, Lenstra, Heuristics on class groups of number fields, Lecture Notes in Math. 1052 (1984) 26-36

    Gives the heuristic distributions of class groups that moment problems are later used to test and match.

    Dating or attribution note

    Moments are part of the Cohen-Lenstra framework; whether the 1984 paper is the first statement of moment-based class group statistics was not checked against its text.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cohen and Lenstra's class-group heuristics appeared in 1984; this predates the Sawin–Wood moment theorem. Cohen and Lenstra, Springer chapter DOI
  2. 2019

    Moments of random integral matrices match Cohen-Lenstra

    precursor

    Wood, Random integral matrices and the Cohen-Lenstra heuristics, Amer. J. Math. (2019)

    Uses moments of cokernels of random integral matrices to support the Cohen-Lenstra distributions.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content described from the title only; the abstract was not read in this pass.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
  3. 2022

    General theorem: moments determine distributions for random objects

    first statement

    Sawin, Wood, The moment problem for random objects in a category, arXiv:2210.06279 (2022)

    Gives conditions under which moments determine a distribution of random objects in a category, the general theorem behind the event.

    Evidence (1)
    • Sawin and Wood state the moment problem and their general existence and uniqueness theorem in the 2022 preprint. Sawin and Wood, original paper
  4. 2026

    Class-group conjectures for extensions containing roots of unity

    key development

    Sawin, Wood, Conjectures for distributions of class groups of extensions of number fields containing roots of unity, Math. Ann. 394 (2026), no. 4, Paper 81

    Applies the moment theory to class-group distributions over extensions with roots of unity.

    Dating or attribution note

    The input summary says the work corrects earlier predictions; that claim was not verified here.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, volume and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
