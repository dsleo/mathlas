1968 · Algebraic geometry

Variation of Hodge structure and period domains (Griffiths)

Periods of integrals on algebraic manifolds vary holomorphically with transversality, giving an analytic tool for moduli and cycle questions.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1958

    Deformation theory of complex structures

    precursor

    K. Kodaira, D. C. Spencer, On deformations of complex analytic structures I, Annals of Mathematics, 1958

    Kodaira-Spencer deformation theory supplies the moduli viewpoint in which Hodge structures later vary.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals of Mathematics paper 'On deformations of complex analytic structures I', 1958 Crossref
  2. 1968

    Periods vary with the parameter

    first statement

    Phillip A. Griffiths, Periods of integrals on algebraic manifolds I, American Journal of Mathematics, 1968

    Griffiths constructs the modular variation of periods of integrals on algebraic manifolds.

    Evidence (2)
    • American Journal of Mathematics paper 'Periods of Integrals on Algebraic Manifolds I', 1968 Crossref
    • Wikipedia (Hodge structure): variation of Hodge structure given by Griffiths 1968, 1968a, 1970 Wikipedia
  3. 1968

    Local study of the period mapping

    first application

    Phillip A. Griffiths, Periods of integrals on algebraic manifolds II: local study of the period mapping, American Journal of Mathematics, 1968

    Griffiths studies the period map locally, which is the purposeful use of variation to control moduli of algebraic manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • American Journal of Mathematics paper 'Periods of Integrals on Algebraic Manifolds II', 1968 Crossref
  4. 1970

    Global differential-geometric properties of the period map

    key development

    Phillip A. Griffiths, Periods of integrals on algebraic manifolds III, Publications Mathematiques IHES 38, 1970

    Part III adds global geometric properties of period domains and the transversality that defines variations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publications Mathematiques IHES paper 'Periods of integrals on algebraic manifolds III', 1970 Crossref
  5. 1978

    Griffiths and Harris, 'Principles of algebraic geometry'

    consolidation

    P. Griffiths, J. Harris, Principles of Algebraic Geometry, Pure and Applied Mathematics, New York: Wiley-Interscience, 1978

    A textbook on algebraic geometry and Hodge theory with a systematic treatment of period maps and variations of Hodge structure.

    Dating or attribution note

    Textbook role from the record; the chapter on variations of Hodge structure was not read in this pass. Griffiths' own 1968-1970 'Periods of integrals' papers are already in the record.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'Principles of algebraic geometry', Griffiths and Harris, Wiley 1978, Zbl 0408.14001 zbMATH Open API
