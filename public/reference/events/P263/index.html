1951 · Algebraic topology

Serre's spectral sequence and homotopy groups

Leray's spectral sequence applied to fibrations gave homotopy results for spheres.

Also known as: Leray-Serre spectral sequence

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1942

    Lefschetz, 'Algebraic topology' (AMS Colloquium Publications)

    precursor

    S. Lefschetz, Algebraic Topology, American Mathematical Society Colloquium Publications vol. 27, 1942, zbMATH record 3102166

    A 1942 monograph titled 'Algebraic topology' is the earliest title found with the field's name in the searched window.

  2. 1951

    Homologie singuliere des espaces fibres

    first statement

    Serre, Homologie singuliere des espaces fibres, Annals of Mathematics 54 (1951)

    Serre's thesis develops the spectral sequence for fibre spaces in singular homology.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal and year verified Crossref
    • Doctorate from the Sorbonne in 1951 on this thesis MacTutor biography of Serre
  3. 1953

    Homotopy groups via Serre classes

    first application

    Serre, Groupes d'homotopie et classes de groupes abeliens, Annals of Mathematics 58 (1953)

    Applies the fibration method to compute homotopy groups, the purpose MacTutor gives for the extension.

    Dating or attribution note

    Claim that this is the first such application rests on MacTutor's account and was not checked against other histories.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal and year verified Crossref
    • Serre found that Leray's theory could be extended to compute homotopy groups MacTutor biography of Serre (paraphrased)
  4. 1953

    Borel on fibred spaces and homogeneous spaces

    key development

    A. Borel, Sur la cohomologie des espaces fibres principaux et des espaces homogenes de groupes de Lie compacts, Ann. of Math. 57 (1953)

    Uses the Leray-Serre spectral sequence to compute the cohomology of classifying and homogeneous spaces.

    Dating or attribution note

    Use of the spectral sequence is inferred from the title and general knowledge.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1953. Crossref
  5. 1953

    Serre's mod 2 cohomology of Eilenberg-MacLane complexes

    key development

    Serre, Cohomologie modulo 2 des complexes d'Eilenberg-MacLane, Comment. Math. Helv. 27 (1953)

    Computes the mod 2 cohomology of Eilenberg-MacLane spaces with the spectral sequence, giving further homotopy-group results.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1953. Crossref
  6. 2001

    McCleary's A User's Guide to Spectral Sequences

    consolidation

    John McCleary, A User's Guide to Spectral Sequences, Cambridge UP, 2nd ed. 2001

    A textbook devoted to spectral sequences with the Serre sequence as a main example.

    Evidence (1)
    • Work listed (2000 record, first publication 1985 for the 1st edition per general knowledge). Open Library
  7. Date unknown

    Leray's spectral sequence for fibrations

    precursor

    Leray's spectral sequence for fibre spaces, which Serre extended to homotopy.

    Evidence (1)
    • Serre's early work extended Leray's spectral sequence theory; Leray invented spectral sequences MacTutor biography of Serre
