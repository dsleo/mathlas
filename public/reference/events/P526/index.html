2006 · Combinatorics

Positive Grassmannian linking combinatorics, exclusion processes and KP solitons

Total positivity extended to the Grassmannian produced cell-counting polynomials that also give probabilities in exclusion processes and describe soliton solutions of the KP equation.

    Williams counts positive Grassmann cells

    precursor

    A combinatorial study precedes Postnikov's network framework.

    Postnikov posts Grassmannian network theory

    first statement

    A. Postnikov, Total positivity, Grassmannians, and networks, arXiv:math/0609764 (2006)

    A public preprint connects total positivity, Grassmannians and networks.

    Evidence (1)

  3. 2006

    ASEP tableaux linked to Grassmannian combinatorics

    first application

    S. Corteel and L. K. Williams, Tableaux combinatorics for the asymmetric exclusion process, arXiv:math/0602109 (2006)

    A public preprint applies the combinatorial structure to an exclusion process.

    Evidence (1)
    • Corteel and Williams's public preprint connects Grassmannian-derived tableaux to exclusion-process probabilities. arXiv preprint record
  4. 2013

    KP solitons from the real Grassmannian

    key development

    Y. Kodama and L. K. Williams, Combinatorics of KP solitons from the real Grassmannian (2013)

    The positive Grassmannian links to another integrable-systems target.

    Evidence (1)
    • Kodama and Williams, Combinatorics of KP solitons from the real Grassmannian, Abel Symposia (2013) Crossref

    consolidation

    Postnikov, A., Positive Grassmannian and polyhedral subdivisions, in: Proceedings of the ICM 2018, DOI 10.1142/9789813272880_0177

    An ICM survey presenting the positive Grassmannian as a combinatorial framework for total positivity.
