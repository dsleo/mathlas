1991 · Geometry

Two-dimensional gravity and the moduli space of curves

A prediction of topological gravity about intersection numbers on moduli spaces of curves is proved by combinatorial (ribbon graph) and hyperbolic-geometric routes.

  5. 2013

    Growth of Weil-Petersson volumes for large genus

    key development

    Mirzakhani, M., Growth of Weil-Petersson volumes and random hyperbolic surfaces of large genus, J. Differential Geom. 94 (2013) 267-300, DOI 10.4310/jdg/1367438650

    Mirzakhani extends the volume recursion to large-genus asymptotics, which is the route by which the intersection-number side reaches random hyperbolic geometry.
