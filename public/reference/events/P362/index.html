1922 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Banach spaces (complete normed linear spaces)

Banach defined complete normed spaces and proved the key theorems on operators, forming the Lwow school.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1922

    Banach's thesis on operations on abstract sets

    first statement

    Banach, 'Sur les opérations dans les ensembles abstraits et leur application aux équations intégrales', Fundamenta Mathematicae 3 (1922)

    Banach works on abstract sets with operations and proves a fixed-point theorem in complete normed settings; the paper is the origin of functional analysis as a field.

    Dating or attribution note

    The complete-normed-space content and the fixed-point theorem are from general knowledge of the paper, not read in full.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1922 Fundamenta Mathematicae record Crossref record
    • The thesis 'On Operations on Abstract Sets and their Application to Integral Equations' is said to mark the birth of functional analysis MacTutor: Banach biography
  2. 1922

    Same paper applied to integral equations

    first application

    Banach (1922), same paper

    The paper applies the abstract operator theory to integral equations, its stated target; same work as the first_statement.

    Evidence (1)
    • Thesis title names integral equations as application MacTutor: Banach biography
  3. 1927

    Banach-Steinhaus uniform boundedness principle

    key development

    Banach & Steinhaus, 'Sur le principe de la condensation de singularités', Fundamenta Mathematicae 9 (1927)

    The uniform boundedness principle for families of bounded operators on Banach spaces, a main result of the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1927 Fund. Math. record Crossref record
  4. 1932

    Banach's Théorie des opérations linéaires

    consolidation

    Banach, Théorie des opérations linéaires (Warsaw, 1932)

    The monograph (French version of a 1931 Polish volume) is the first systematic treatment of the theory of linear operations on Banach spaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1932 book record Open Library search
    • French version of a 1931 Polish volume, a classic MacTutor: Banach biography
