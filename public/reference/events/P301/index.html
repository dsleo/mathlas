1970 · Algebraic topology

Bloch-Kato / norm residue

Motivic Steenrod operations proved the norm residue isomorphism in all primes.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1970

    Milnor proposes the mod 2 Milnor K-theory and Galois cohomology isomorphism

    first statement

    J. Milnor, Algebraic K-theory and quadratic forms, Invent. Math. 9 (1970), 318-344

    Milnor suggests that Milnor K-theory mod 2 of a field is isomorphic to its Galois cohomology with Z/2 coefficients, the l = 2 case of the norm residue statement.

    Dating or attribution note

    Covers only l = 2; the all-primes statement is Bloch-Kato 1986, held as precursor in the record. Text of the 1970 paper not read.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH lists the paper in Invent. Math. 9 (1970), pp. 318-344, with an appendix by Tate. zbMATH Open document 3318401
    • The Milnor conjecture is a proposal of Milnor from 1970; the general case is the Bloch-Kato conjecture. Wikipedia, Milnor conjecture (K-theory)
    • Crossref: 'Algebraic K-theory and quadratic forms', Invent. Math. 9 (1970) 318-344, issued December 1970 Crossref 10.1007/bf01425486
  2. 1986

    p-adic étale cohomology (Bloch-Kato)

    precursor

    Bloch, S., Kato, K., p-adic étale cohomology, Publ. Math. IHÉS 63, 1986

    The Bloch-Kato paper is the standard reference that the conjecture is named after; its precise conjectural statement was not checked, so it is only a precursor here.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publ. Math. IHÉS, 1986 (DOI 10.1007/bf02831624); title partly garbled in the Crossref record Crossref record
  3. 2000

    Survey: Bloch-Kato conjecture and motivic cohomology with finite coefficients

    consolidation

    Suslin, A., Voevodsky, V., Bloch-Kato conjecture and motivic cohomology with finite coefficients, in The Arithmetic and Geometry of Algebraic Cycles, 2000

    A survey by Suslin and Voevodsky that sets out the conjecture for finite coefficients and the motivic approach.

    Dating or attribution note

    Recorded as a survey; whether it is the first systematic treatment was not verified.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chapter in The Arithmetic and Geometry of Algebraic Cycles, 2000 (DOI 10.1007/978-94-011-4098-0_5) Crossref record
  4. 2003

    Milnor conjecture (mod 2) via motivic cohomology

    first application

    Voevodsky, V., Motivic cohomology with Z/2-coefficients, Publ. Math. IHÉS 98, 2003

    Voevodsky uses motivic cohomology to prove the 2-primary (mod 2) case of the Milnor conjecture, the first purposeful use on the norm-residue question.

    Evidence (2)
    • Publ. Math. IHÉS, 2003 (DOI 10.1007/s10240-003-0010-6) Crossref record
    • Voevodsky's proof of the Milnor conjecture, with 1995-96 preprints earlier Wikipedia: Norm residue isomorphism theorem
  5. 2006

    Norm varieties completed

    key development

    Suslin, A., Joukhovitski, S., Norm varieties, J. Pure Appl. Algebra, 2006

    Suslin and Joukhovitski prove the properties of Rost's norm varieties needed for the general norm residue theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in J. Pure Appl. Algebra, 2006 (DOI 10.1016/j.jpaa.2005.12.012) Crossref record
    • Proof that the varieties have the required properties was completed by Suslin and Joukhovitski in 2006 Wikipedia: Norm residue isomorphism theorem
  6. 2011

    Motivic cohomology with Z/l-coefficients

    key development

    Voevodsky, V., On motivic cohomology with Z/l-coefficients, Annals of Mathematics 174, 2011

    Voevodsky's Annals paper extends the argument to all primes, giving the general norm residue isomorphism theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Annals of Mathematics, 2011 (DOI 10.4007/annals.2011.174.1.11) Crossref record
    • Voevodsky announced a proof of the Bloch-Kato conjectures in 2009 MacTutor: Voevodsky
