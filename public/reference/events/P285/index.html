1970 · Statistics & decision theory

Global analysis and mathematical economics

Topological and dynamical methods were applied to equilibrium existence and price adjustment.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1954

    Arrow-Debreu existence of equilibrium

    precursor

    K. Arrow and G. Debreu, Existence of an equilibrium for a competitive economy, Econometrica 22 (1954)

    Proves existence of general equilibrium with fixed-point methods, the setting that later differentiable methods refine.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1954. Crossref
  2. 1959

    Debreu's Theory of Value

    precursor

    Gerard Debreu, Theory of Value, Wiley, 1959

    Axiomatic treatment of general equilibrium theory, the base for later global-analysis work.

    Evidence (1)
    • Theory of value, 1959. Open Library
  3. 1970

    Debreu, Economies with a finite set of equilibria

    first statement

    Debreu, Economies with a finite set of equilibria, Econometrica 38 (1970)

    Debreu applies Sard's theorem and Baire-category arguments to show that general equilibrium economies generically have finitely many equilibria.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article record: Econometrica, 1970, Debreu, Economies with a finite set of equilibria Crossref
    • Wikipedia credits Debreu and Smale with the global-analysis proof method for equilibrium existence Wikipedia, General equilibrium theory
  4. 1970

    Debreu applies Sard's lemma to equilibria

    first application

    Debreu, Economies with a finite set of equilibria, Econometrica 38 (1970)

    The first application of differential-topological tools to a target problem in economic equilibrium theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same bibliographic record as the first_statement milestone Crossref
  5. 1973

    'Global Analysis and Economics I' in a title

    naming

    Smale, Global Analysis and Economics I: Pareto Optimum and a Generalization of Morse Theory, in Dynamical Systems (Peixoto ed.), 1973

    Smale's chapter is the earliest title found that uses the phrase 'global analysis and economics'; it generalises Morse theory to Pareto optima.

    Evidence (2)
    • Book-chapter record dated 1973 with the title 'Global Analysis and Economics I' Crossref
    • MacTutor: Smale worked on economics during much of the 1970s, injecting topology and dynamics into general equilibrium MacTutor, Stephen Smale
  6. 1974

    Smale, Global analysis and economics IV

    key development

    Smale, Global analysis and economics IV: finiteness and stability of equilibria with general consumption sets and production, J. Math. Econ. 1 (1974)

    Extends finiteness and stability of equilibria to general consumption sets and production.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1974. Crossref
  7. 1985

    Mas-Colell's differentiable approach

    consolidation

    Andreu Mas-Colell, The Theory of General Economic Equilibrium: A Differentiable Approach, Cambridge UP, 1985

    Monograph on equilibrium theory by differentiable methods.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book listed in Open Library. Open Library search, The theory of general economic equilibrium
