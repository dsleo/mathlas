1978 · Combinatorics

Topology proves Kneser's conjecture

The chromatic number of Kneser graphs is bounded below via the Borsuk-Ulam theorem applied to a topological space built from the graph.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1955

    Kneser states the coloring conjecture

    precursor

    The combinatorial problem predates its topological solution.

    Evidence (1)
    • Kneser's 1955 Aufgabe 360 is cited in later mathematical literature Kneser problem bibliography
  2. 1978

    Lovász proves Kneser's conjecture

    first statement

    L. Lovász, Kneser's conjecture, chromatic number, and homotopy, JCTA 25 (1978)

    The paper states the graph-coloring result through a topological argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1978 published proof using homotopy Journal of Combinatorial Theory / Crossref
  3. 1978

    Homotopy used on Kneser graphs

    first application

    L. Lovász, Kneser's conjecture, chromatic number, and homotopy, JCTA 25 (1978)

    Lovász applies the topology of a graph complex to the chromatic-number problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1978 published proof using homotopy Journal of Combinatorial Theory / Crossref
  4. 1978

    Bárány's short topological proof

    key development

    I. Bárány, A short proof of Kneser's conjecture, JCTA 25 (1978)

    A different short proof follows using Borsuk–Ulam ideas.

    Evidence (1)
    • Short proof in JCTA 25, 1978 Journal of Combinatorial Theory / Crossref
  5. 2003

    Matousek's book on Borsuk-Ulam methods in combinatorics

    consolidation

    Matousek, Using the Borsuk-Ulam Theorem, Springer Lecture Notes, 2003

    A book-length treatment that teaches the topological method behind Kneser-type results.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record gives this title, ISBN 3-540-00362-2, year 2003 zbMATH Open
