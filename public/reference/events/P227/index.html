1965 · Logic & foundations

Fuzzy sets

Graded membership generalised set theory for imprecise concepts.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1937

    Max Black on vagueness

    precursor

    Black, M., Vagueness, Philosophy of Science 4 (1937)

    A philosophical analysis of vague predicates that anticipates graded membership without the mathematical structure.

    Evidence (1)
    • Black's Vagueness paper, Philosophy of Science 1937 (DOI 10.1086/286476) Crossref
  2. 1965

    Zadeh introduces fuzzy sets

    first statement

    Zadeh, L. A., Fuzzy sets, Information and Control 8 (1965)

    Defines sets whose elements have degrees of membership in the unit interval.

    Evidence (2)
    • Zadeh's Fuzzy sets, Information and Control (1965), DOI 10.1016/s0019-9958(65)90241-x Crossref; zbMATH
    • Wikipedia states fuzzy sets were introduced by Zadeh in 1965 Wikipedia, Fuzzy set
  3. 1965

    Term 'fuzzy sets' in the title

    naming

    Zadeh, L. A., Fuzzy sets (1965)

    The name of the field is coined in this title.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title of the 1965 paper Crossref
  4. 1967

    Goguen's L-fuzzy sets

    key development

    Goguen, J. A., L-fuzzy sets, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications 18 (1967) 145-174

    Replaces the unit interval by a general lattice, making fuzzy structures an algebraic setting.

    Evidence (2)
    • Goguen, L-fuzzy sets, 1967, pages 145-174; the container ISSN 0022-247X is J. Math. Anal. Appl. zbMATH 0145.24404
    • Wikipedia notes a more general L-relation structure studied in the same period Wikipedia, Fuzzy set
  5. 1975

    Fuzzy logic controller experiment

    first application

    Mamdani, E. H.; Assilian, S., An experiment in linguistic synthesis with a fuzzy logic controller, Int. J. Man-Machine Studies 7 (1975) 1-13

    Uses fuzzy rules as a controller on a target engineering problem, the first purposeful application in the records consulted.

    Evidence (1)
    • Mamdani-Assilian paper, Int. J. Man-Machine Studies 1975 (DOI 10.1016/s0020-7373(75)80002-2), pages 1-13 Crossref; zbMATH 0301.68076
  6. 1980

    Dubois treatise Fuzzy sets and systems

    consolidation

    Dubois, D.; Prade, H., Fuzzy Sets and Systems: Theory and Applications, Academic Press (1980)

    A book-length systematic treatment of fuzzy sets and their applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists 'Fuzzy sets and systems' with first publication 1980 under Didier Dubois Open Library search
