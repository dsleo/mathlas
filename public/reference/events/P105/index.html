1960 · Topology

Handle decompositions and the high-dimensional Poincare (h-cobordism) theorem

Gradient flows of Morse functions give handle decompositions, and cancellation of handles proves the generalised Poincare conjecture in dimensions at least five.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1934

    Morse's calculus of variations in the large

    precursor

    Morse, The calculus of variations in the large, AMS Colloquium Publications 18 (1934)

    Morse theory relates critical points of a function to the topology of a manifold, the basis of handle decompositions.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Morse, The calculus of variations in the large (1934) zbMATH
  2. 1960

    Announcement of the generalised Poincare conjecture in higher dimensions

    first statement

    Smale, The generalized Poincare conjecture in higher dimensions, Bull. AMS 66 (1960) 373-375

    Smale announces the high-dimensional Poincare conjecture, with dimension range first n at least 7 and later extended to 5.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: The generalized Poincare conjecture in higher dimensions, Bull. AMS, 1960 Crossref
    • Wikipedia Generalized Poincare conjecture: Smale proved n at least 7 in 1960 and extended to n at least 5 Wikipedia Generalized Poincare conjecture
  3. 1960

    Polyhedral homotopy-spheres (Stallings's parallel proof)

    key development

    Stallings, Polyhedral homotopy-spheres, Bull. AMS 66 (1960) 485-488

    Stallings gives a parallel PL proof for dimensions at least 7 using engulfing, a different route to the same result.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Polyhedral homotopy-spheres, Bull. AMS, 1960 Crossref
    • Wikipedia Generalized Poincare conjecture: Stallings gave a different proof in dimensions at least 7 using engulfing Wikipedia Generalized Poincare conjecture
  4. 1961

    Handle cancellation proves the Poincare conjecture for dimensions at least 5

    first application

    Smale, Generalized Poincare's conjecture in dimensions greater than four, Ann. of Math. 74 (1961) 391-406

    Smale uses handle decompositions and cancellation of handles to prove the generalised Poincare conjecture in dimensions at least 5.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Generalized Poincare's Conjecture in Dimensions Greater Than Four, Annals of Mathematics, 1961 Crossref
    • MacTutor Smale: Smale proved the higher dimensional Poincare conjecture in 1961 for n at least 5 MacTutor Smale biography
  5. 1962

    Structure of manifolds: the h-cobordism theorem

    key development

    Smale, On the structure of manifolds (1962)

    Smale proves the h-cobordism theorem for high dimensions, which underlies the Poincare results.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Smale, On the structure of manifolds, 1962 zbMATH
    • Wikipedia H-cobordism: the theorem was first proved by Smale Wikipedia H-cobordism
  6. 1965

    Milnor's Lectures on the h-cobordism theorem

    consolidation

    Milnor, Lectures on the h-cobordism theorem, Princeton Mathematical Notes (1965)

    Lecture notes giving the first systematic treatment of the h-cobordism theorem for smooth manifolds.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record for Milnor's Lectures on the h-cobordism theorem, 1965 zbMATH
    • Crossref record: Lectures on the H-Cobordism Theorem (Princeton, 1965) Crossref
