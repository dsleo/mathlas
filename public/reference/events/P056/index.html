≈ 2006 · Number theory

O-minimality in diophantine geometry (Pila-Wilkie, Pila-Zannier)

Counting theorems for rational points on definable sets in o-minimal structures are combined with functional transcendence to prove Manin-Mumford and cases of Andre-Oort.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2006

    Counting rational points on definable sets

    key development

    J. Pila and A. J. Wilkie, The rational points of a definable set, Duke Math. J. 133 (2006)

    The counting theorem supplies the rational-point bound later used in diophantine applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher record identifies the 2006 Pila–Wilkie paper. Original bibliographic record
  2. 2008

    Counting method applied to Manin–Mumford

    first application

    J. Pila and U. Zannier, Rational points in periodic analytic sets and the Manin-Mumford conjecture, arXiv:0802.4016 (2008)

    Pila and Zannier explicitly combine rational-point upper bounds with torsion-point lower bounds to reprove Manin–Mumford.

    Evidence (1)
    • The 2008 abstract states the target theorem and counting method. Original bibliographic record
  3. 2011

    Pila's André-Oort via o-minimality

    key development

    Pila, J., O-minimality and the André-Oort conjecture for C^n, Annals of Mathematics 173 (2011)

    Uses o-minimal counting to prove the André-Oort conjecture for products of modular curves, extending the Pila-Wilkie method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Annals 173 (2011) Crossref
  4. 2015

    Survey chapter on o-minimality and Diophantine geometry

    consolidation

    Pila, J., Functional transcendence via o-minimality, in: G. O. Jones and A. J. Wilkie (eds.), O-Minimality and Diophantine Geometry, LMS Lecture Note Series, 2015

    An expository chapter in a volume that surveys the o-minimal approach to diophantine problems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref chapter record, 2015, in the O-Minimality and Diophantine Geometry volume Crossref
