1974 · Algebra

Buildings: geometry of algebraic groups

Simplicial complexes attached to groups of Lie type encode their parabolic subgroups, giving a geometric language for finite simple groups and p-adic groups.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1964

    Tits' abstract simple groups and BN-pairs

    precursor

    Tits, J., Algebraic and abstract simple groups, Annals of Mathematics 80 (1964)

    Introduces the group-theoretic axioms (Tits systems) from which the building geometry is later derived.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annals of Mathematics record for Tits' paper, year 1964 Crossref
    • Wikipedia Building article: buildings were initially introduced by Tits to describe isotropic reductive groups Wikipedia, Building (mathematics)
  2. 1972

    Bruhat-Tits buildings for p-adic reductive groups

    precursor

    Bruhat, F. and Tits, J., Groupes réductifs sur un corps local I, Publ. Math. IHES 41 (1972)

    Applies building-type structures (valued root data) to reductive groups over local fields: the first purposeful use on a target problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Publications Mathématiques IHES record for Bruhat-Tits I, year 1972 Crossref
    • Wikipedia Building article: the Bruhat-Tits theory of buildings plays a role for p-adic Lie groups Wikipedia, Building (mathematics)
  3. 1974

    Tits' monograph defining buildings

    first statement

    Tits, J., Buildings of Spherical Type and Finite BN-Pairs, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 386, Springer (1974)

    Defines buildings as simplicial complexes with a group action, and proves the classification via finite BN-pairs.

    Evidence (2)
    • Springer LNM record for Buildings of Spherical Type and Finite BN-Pairs, year 1974 Crossref
    • Open Library record for the 1974 Springer edition Open Library
  4. 1974

    Tits' Lecture Notes volume on buildings

    consolidation

    Tits, J., Buildings of Spherical Type and Finite BN-Pairs, LNM 386 (1974)

    The first systematic treatment of buildings and BN-pairs.

    Evidence (1)
    • Springer LNM record for LNM 386, year 1974 Crossref
