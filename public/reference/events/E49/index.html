1999 · Probability

Schramm-Loewner evolution

Scaling limits of planar lattice models are described by random curves generated by Loewner's equation driven by Brownian motion, giving rigorous access to conformal field theory predictions.

Also known as: stochastic Loewner evolution

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1999

    Schramm proposes stochastic Loewner evolution

    first statement

    O. Schramm, Scaling limits of loop-erased random walks and uniform spanning trees, arXiv:math/9904022 (1999)

    The public preprint identifies a conformally invariant scaling-limit framework.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint submitted in 1999 arXiv
  2. 1999

    Scaling limits targeted at LERW and UST

    first application

    O. Schramm, Scaling limits of loop-erased random walks and uniform spanning trees, arXiv:math/9904022 (1999)

    The construction is directed at loop-erased walks and uniform spanning trees.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint submitted in 1999 arXiv
  3. 2001

    LERW and UST conformal invariance proved

    key development

    G. Lawler, O. Schramm and W. Werner, Conformal invariance of planar loop-erased random walks and uniform spanning trees, arXiv:math/0112234 (2001)

    Lawler, Schramm and Werner identify the LERW scaling limit with SLE(2).

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint submitted in 2001 arXiv
  4. 2005

    Lawler's monograph on conformally invariant processes

    consolidation

    Lawler, G. F., Conformally Invariant Processes in the Plane, AMS Mathematical Surveys and Monographs, 2005

    A book treating SLE and related conformally invariant planar processes in a systematic way.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library work record, first publish 2005 Open Library search API
    • zbMATH record Lawler 2005 zbMATH Open API
  5. 2005

    Basic properties of SLE established

    key development

    Rohde, S., Schramm, O., Basic properties of SLE, Annals of Mathematics 161 (2005)

    Establishes the basic regularity and continuity properties of SLE traces that let the process be used as a tool.
