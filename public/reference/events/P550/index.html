2012 · Group & representation theory

Umbral moonshine and pariah moonshine

Mathieu moonshine and 23 further umbral moonshines link groups to mock modular forms, proved in 2015, and a pariah group (O'Nan) was tied to weight-3/2 modular forms in 2017.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2010

    K3–Mathieu observation

    precursor

    T. Eguchi, H. Ooguri and Y. Tachikawa, Notes on the K3 Surface and the Mathieu group M_24 (2010)

    The elliptic genus of K3 is related to Mathieu group representation dimensions.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents k3–mathieu observation in 2010 arXiv
  2. 2012

    Umbral moonshine conjecture formulated

    first statement

    M. C. N. Cheng, J. F. R. Duncan and J. A. Harvey, Umbral Moonshine (2012)

    The 2012 paper relates mock modular forms to a family of finite groups.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents umbral moonshine conjecture formulated in 2012 arXiv
  3. 2012

    Umbral moonshine named in title

    naming

    M. C. N. Cheng, J. F. R. Duncan and J. A. Harvey, Umbral Moonshine (2012)

    The paper title gives a dated term-use anchor.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents umbral moonshine named in title in 2012 arXiv
  4. 2012

    Mock modular forms linked to umbral groups

    first application

    M. C. N. Cheng, J. F. R. Duncan and J. A. Harvey, Umbral Moonshine (2012)

    The founding paper deliberately uses the conjectured group correspondence to organize mock modular forms.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents mock modular forms linked to umbral groups in 2012 arXiv
  5. 2015

    Umbral moonshine conjecture proved

    key development

    J. F. R. Duncan, M. J. Griffin and K. Ono, Proof of the Umbral Moonshine Conjecture (2015)

    The proof constructs and identifies the predicted moonshine modules.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents umbral moonshine conjecture proved in 2015 arXiv
  6. 2017

    O'Nan moonshine links a pariah group and arithmetic

    key development

    J. F. R. Duncan, M. H. Mertens and K. Ono, O'Nan moonshine and arithmetic (2017)

    A separate 2017 paper constructs a graded O'Nan module whose series are weight-3/2 modular forms.
