Approximate groups and the model theory of pseudofinite structures

A Lie-model theorem built with nonstandard methods describes the structure of finite approximate subgroups.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2009

    Stable model theory meets approximate subgroups

    first statement

    E. Hrushovski, Stable group theory and approximate subgroups (2009)

    Hrushovski’s public preprint develops a model-theoretic stabilizer method for approximate subgroups.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents stable model theory meets approximate subgroups in 2009 arXiv
  2. 2009

    Model-theoretic method applied to finite-set growth

    first application

    E. Hrushovski, Stable group theory and approximate subgroups (2009)

    The paper treats approximate subsets of linear groups and draws nilpotence consequences.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents model-theoretic method applied to finite-set growth in 2009 arXiv
  3. 2011

    General structure theorem for approximate groups

    key development

    E. Breuillard, B. Green and T. Tao, The structure of approximate groups (2011)

    Breuillard, Green and Tao use nonstandard analysis and a Lie model to classify approximate groups.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents general structure theorem for approximate groups in 2011 arXiv
  4. 2014

    Breuillard's introduction to approximate groups

    consolidation

    Breuillard, E., A brief introduction to approximate groups, in: Thin Groups and Superstrong Approximation (2014)

    An expository chapter introducing approximate groups and their structure theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref chapter record, 2014 Crossref
