Perfectoid spaces and tilting (Scholze)

Characteristic p and characteristic 0 p-adic fields are related by an equivalence of Galois groups extended to geometry, moving results from one side to the other.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1979

    Fontaine-Wintenberger field-of-norms notes

    precursor

    Fontaine, J.-M.; Wintenberger, J.-P., Le corps des normes de certaines extensions algebriques de corps locaux, and Extensions algebriques et corps des normes des extensions APF des corps locaux, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris Ser. A 288 (1979) 367-370 and 441-444

    Two short Comptes Rendus notes that introduce the field of norms, an earlier announcement of the construction rather than the full theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH: 'Le corps des normes de certaines extensions algebriques de corps locaux', C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris Ser. A 288 (1979) 367-370 zbMATH Open 3745320
    • zbMATH: 'Extensions algebriques et corps des normes des extensions APF des corps locaux', C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris Ser. A 288 (1979) 441-444 zbMATH Open 3625519
  2. 1983

    Field of norms: Galois-group equivalence for local fields

    precursor

    Wintenberger, Le corps des normes de certaines extensions infinies de corps locaux; applications, Ann. Sci. ENS (1983)

    The field-of-norms construction gives an equivalence of Galois groups between characteristic 0 and characteristic p fields, which tilting later generalises.

    Evidence (2)
    • Field of norms paper, author and year Crossref
    • Tilting moves results between characteristic p and 0 p-adic fields via Galois groups Input summary (unverified; Wikipedia: Perfectoid space)
  3. 2011

    Perfectoid spaces and tilting introduced

    first statement

    P. Scholze, Perfectoid spaces, arXiv:1111.4914 (2011)

    The preprint defines perfectoid rings and spaces and their characteristic-changing tilting operation.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted November 2011 arXiv
  4. 2011

    Tilting applied to weight-monodromy cases

    first application

    P. Scholze, Perfectoid spaces, arXiv:1111.4914 (2011)

    The paper deduces cases of the weight-monodromy conjecture by reduction to equal characteristic.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract states the target application arXiv
  5. 2012

    Perfectoid spaces and tilting introduced

    first statement

    Scholze, Perfectoid spaces, Publications Math. IHES 116 (2012)

    Scholze introduces perfectoid spaces and the tilting equivalence in 2012.

    Evidence (2)
    • Notions introduced in 2012 by Peter Scholze; tilting equivalence Wikipedia: Perfectoid space
    • Publications IHES paper, author and year Crossref
  6. 2012

    Survey of perfectoid spaces

    consolidation

    Scholze, Perfectoid spaces: a survey, Current Developments in Mathematics (2012)

    A survey gives the first systematic exposition of the perfectoid framework beyond the original paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • Survey, author and year Crossref
  7. 2012

    Perfectoid spaces used in rigid-analytic p-adic Hodge theory

    key development

    P. Scholze, p-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties, arXiv:1205.3463 (2012)

    A comparison theorem for rigid-analytic varieties explicitly relies on perfectoid spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted May 2012 arXiv
  8. 2013

    Perfectoid spaces used to construct Galois representations on torsion cohomology

    first application

    Scholze, P., On torsion in the cohomology of locally symmetric varieties, arXiv:1306.2070 (submitted 9 June 2013); Ann. of Math. (2) 182 (2015), 945-1066

    Scholze used the perfectoid space of level-p structures and its Hodge-Tate period map to build Galois representations attached to torsion classes in the cohomology of GL_n locally symmetric varieties.

    Evidence (2)
    • arXiv 1306.2070 by Peter Scholze, 'On torsion in the cohomology of locally symmetric varieties', submitted 9 June 2013 arXiv abstract page
    • Annals of Mathematics 182 (2015), 945-1066; the review says the infinite level cover is a perfectoid space with a Hodge-Tate period map, used to realise torsion classes zbMATH Open document record 6514750 (review text)
  9. 2013

    Perfectoid-space survey

    consolidation

    P. Scholze, Perfectoid Spaces: A survey, arXiv:1303.5948 (2013)

    The survey organizes the new theory, applications and open problems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public survey preprint posted March 2013 arXiv
  10. 2020

    Berkeley Lectures on p-adic geometry: perfectoid chapter

    consolidation

    Scholze, Weinstein, Perfectoid spaces, in Berkeley Lectures on p-adic Geometry, Princeton (2020)

    The Berkeley lectures treat perfectoid spaces in book form.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chapter 'Perfectoid spaces' in the 2020 book, co-authors Scholze and Weinstein Crossref
