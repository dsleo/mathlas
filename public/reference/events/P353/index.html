2013 · Group & representation theory

Kadison-Singer via interlacing polynomials

Marcus-Spielman-Srivastava solved Kadison-Singer using interlacing families of polynomials, a method arising from graph sparsification.

Also known as: Kadison-Singer problem

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1959

    Kadison–Singer problem posed

    precursor

    R. V. Kadison and I. M. Singer, Extensions of Pure States, American Journal of Mathematics 81 (1959)

    The original operator-algebra problem predates its interlacing-polynomial solution.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher record dates the original article to 1959 JSTOR / Crossref
  2. 2013

    Interlacing families I establishes method

    precursor

    A. Marcus, D. A. Spielman and N. Srivastava, Interlacing Families I, arXiv:1304.4132 (2013)

    An April preprint develops the interlacing method for Ramanujan graphs before the Kadison–Singer transfer.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted April 2013 arXiv
  3. 2013

    Kadison–Singer solution posted

    first statement

    A. Marcus, D. A. Spielman and N. Srivastava, Interlacing Families II: Mixed Characteristic Polynomials and the Kadison-Singer Problem, arXiv:1306.3969 (2013)

    The public preprint states a positive solution through interlacing and mixed characteristic polynomials.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted June 2013 arXiv
  4. 2013

    Mixed characteristic polynomials prove paving bounds

    first application

    A. Marcus, D. A. Spielman and N. Srivastava, Interlacing Families II: Mixed Characteristic Polynomials and the Kadison-Singer Problem, arXiv:1306.3969 (2013)

    The method proves Weaver's KS2 and explicit paving consequences implying Kadison–Singer.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract identifies these target conjectures arXiv
  5. 2016

    Stevens' SpringerBrief on the Kadison-Singer problem

    consolidation

    Stevens, A., The Kadison-Singer Problem, SpringerBriefs in Mathematical Physics (2016)

    A short monograph reviewing the Kadison-Singer problem and its solution through interlacing polynomials.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref chapter record, SpringerBriefs 2016 Crossref
  6. 2018

    Bownik's survey of the Kadison-Singer problem

    consolidation

    Bownik, M., The Kadison-Singer problem, Contemporary Mathematics 706 (2018)

    A survey of the Kadison-Singer problem after its solution.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Contemp. Math. 706 (2018) Crossref
