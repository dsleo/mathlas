1909 · Topology

Abstract topological spaces from point-set analysis

Cantor's derived and open sets, Weierstrass's neighbourhoods and Frechet's metric spaces led Riesz and Hausdorff to topological spaces defined by neighbourhood axioms without distance.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1906

    Frechet's abstract metric spaces

    precursor

    Frechet, M., Sur quelques points du calcul fonctionnel (1906)

    Frechet's 1906 paper introduces abstract spaces with a distance, a precursor of topological spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper with this title and year zbMATH Open
  2. 1909

    Riesz on continuity and abstract set theory

    first statement

    Riesz, F., Stetigkeit und abstrakte Mengenlehre (1909)

    Riesz introduces abstract topological spaces defined by axioms rather than a distance.

    Evidence (1)
    • Riesz's contribution appears in the printed proceedings of the 1908 Rome congress, issued in 1909. International Mathematical Union, ICM 1908 proceedings
  3. 1914

    Hausdorff's Grundzuge der Mengenlehre

    consolidation

    Hausdorff, F., Grundzuge der Mengenlehre (1914)

    Hausdorff's 1914 book systematises topological spaces defined by neighbourhood axioms.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book with this title and year zbMATH Open
  4. 1922

    Kuratowski's closure operation

    key development

    Kuratowski, C., Sur l'operation A de l'analysis situs (1922)

    Kuratowski's 1922 paper gives axioms for the closure operation, a key step toward general topology.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper with this title and year zbMATH Open
