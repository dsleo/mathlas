1958 · Analysis

Entropy of dynamical systems (Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy)

Shannon's entropy is turned into an isomorphism invariant of measure-preserving transformations, settling that Bernoulli shifts with different entropy are not isomorphic.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1948

    Shannon's entropy of a message source

    precursor

    Shannon, A Mathematical Theory of Communication, Bell System Technical Journal, 1948

    Shannon defines entropy for a source of messages; the dynamical invariant is later built from this quantity.

    Evidence (1)
    • BSTJ article with this title dated 1948 Crossref
  2. 1958

    Kolmogorov's metric invariant of dynamical systems

    first statement

    Kolmogorov, A new metric invariant of transitive dynamical systems and automorphisms of Lebesgue spaces, Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR 119 (1958) 861-864

    Kolmogorov introduces entropy of a measure-preserving transformation as an isomorphism invariant.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH review of the 1986 reprint cites this 1958 Doklady paper (Zbl 0083.106) zbMATH
  3. 1959

    Sinai's theorems make entropy computable

    first application

    Sinai, On the concept of entropy of a dynamical system, Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR (1959)

    Sinai's first 1959 paper gives theorems that make the entropy calculable for a large class of systems, which is the first purposeful use of the invariant.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor: Sinai's 1959 paper gives theorems for calculating entropy for a large variety of systems MacTutor Sinai biography
    • zbMATH record of 'On the concept of entropy of a dynamical system' dated 1959 zbMATH
  4. 1959

    Generator theorem: supremum attained on generating partitions

    key development

    Sinai, On the concept of entropy of a dynamical system, Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR (1959)

    Sinai shows the supremum defining the entropy is attained on generating partitions, which makes the invariant computable.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia: a theorem of Sinai in 1959 shows the supremum is attained on generators Wikipedia Measure-preserving dynamical system
    • zbMATH record for Sinai 1959 zbMATH
  5. 1970

    Ornstein: equal entropy implies Bernoulli isomorphism

    key development

    Ornstein, Bernoulli shifts with the same entropy are isomorphic, Advances in Mathematics 4 (1970)

    Ornstein proves that two Bernoulli shifts with equal entropy are isomorphic, so entropy is a complete invariant for Bernoulli shifts.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: article title and Advances in Mathematics, 1970 Crossref
    • Wikipedia Bernoulli scheme: the Ornstein isomorphism theorem states equal entropy implies isomorphic Wikipedia Bernoulli scheme
  6. 1989

    Survey of entropy theory

    consolidation

    Cornfeld and Sinai, Entropy Theory of Dynamical Systems, Encyclopaedia of Mathematical Sciences (1989)

    A survey chapter that systematises the entropy theory after the Ornstein results.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: chapter 'Entropy Theory of Dynamical Systems' in Encyclopaedia of Mathematical Sciences, 1989 Crossref
