2008 · Algebra

Additive combinatorics proves expansion in groups

Sum-product and product-set growth estimates show that Cayley graphs of SL2(Fp) are expanders, a spectral gap statement about groups.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2003

    Finite-field sum-product preprint

    precursor

    J. Bourgain, N. Katz and T. Tao, A sum-product estimate in finite fields and applications, arXiv:math/0301343 (2003)

    Bourgain, Katz and Tao establish a tool later used in expansion arguments.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint submitted in 2003 arXiv
  2. 2008

    Bourgain–Gamburd expansion theorem

    first statement

    J. Bourgain and A. Gamburd, Uniform expansion bounds for Cayley graphs of SL₂(Fp), Ann. Math. 167 (2008)

    The paper establishes uniform expansion bounds for Cayley graphs of SL₂(Fp).

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals article, volume 167 (2008), pages 625–642 Annals of Mathematics / Crossref
  3. 2008

    Growth tools applied to SL₂ expansion

    first application

    J. Bourgain and A. Gamburd, Uniform expansion bounds for Cayley graphs of SL₂(Fp), Ann. Math. 167 (2008)

    The proof deliberately transfers sum-product and group-growth estimates to expander graphs.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals article, volume 167 (2008), pages 625–642 Annals of Mathematics / Crossref
  4. 2011

    Approximate subgroups of linear groups

    key development

    Breuillard, Green, Tao, Approximate subgroups of linear groups, GAFA 21 (2011)

    Classifies approximate subgroups of linear groups, giving the growth-to-structure step that underlies later expansion results in groups.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2011 GAFA paper on approximate subgroups of linear groups zbMATH Open record
  5. 2015

    Tao's book on expansion in finite simple groups

    consolidation

    Tao, Expansion in Finite Simple Groups of Lie Type, AMS Graduate Studies in Mathematics 164 (2015)

    A book-length treatment of expansion in finite simple groups of Lie type, which collects the sum-product and growth methods used for the Bourgain-Gamburd expansion theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2015 book by Tao on expansion in finite simple groups of Lie type zbMATH Open record
