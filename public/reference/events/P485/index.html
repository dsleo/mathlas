1994 · Algebraic geometry

Noncommutative projective geometry (Artin-Schelter-Zhang)

Graded noncommutative rings are studied through the quasi-coherent sheaves on a 'projective scheme' built from their modules.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1987

    Regular graded algebras of dimension 3 as noncommutative P^2

    precursor

    Artin, Schelter, Graded algebras of global dimension 3, Adv. Math. 66 (1987) 171-216

    Classifies three-dimensional regular graded algebras, the noncommutative analogues of P^2 used later.

  2. 1990

    Point modules and automorphisms of elliptic curves

    precursor

    Artin, Tate, Van den Bergh, Some algebras associated to automorphisms of elliptic curves, in The Grothendieck Festschrift vol. 1 (1990)

    Constructs algebras from automorphisms of elliptic curves and studies their projective geometry.

    Evidence (2)

  3. 1991

    Modules over regular algebras of dimension 3

    precursor

    Artin, Tate, Van den Bergh, Modules over regular algebras of dimension 3, Invent. Math. 106 (1991) 335-388

    Studies modules over three-dimensional regular algebras and their point-module geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
  4. 1994

    Noncommutative projective schemes

    first statement

    Artin, Zhang, Noncommutative projective schemes, Adv. Math. 109 (1994) 228-287

    Defines the projective scheme of a noncommutative graded algebra as a quotient of graded modules by torsion modules.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref

  5. 2001

    Survey of noncommutative curves and surfaces

    consolidation

    Stafford, Van den Bergh, Noncommutative curves and noncommutative surfaces, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 38 (2001)

    Survey of the noncommutative projective theory in dimensions one and two.
