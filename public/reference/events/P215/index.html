1950 · Game theory & economics

Nash equilibrium: existence via fixed points

Equilibria of non-cooperative games were shown to exist via fixed-point arguments.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1941

    Kakutani's fixed-point theorem

    precursor

    Kakutani, A generalization of Brouwer's fixed point theorem, Duke Mathematical Journal 8 (1941)

    Fixed-point tool that Nash used to prove existence in 1950; a tool, not the event's start.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title, journal and year verified Crossref
  2. 1944

    von Neumann-Morgenstern mixed strategies

    precursor

    von Neumann and Morgenstern, Theory of Games and Economic Behavior, 1944

    Introduced mixed-strategy equilibrium, but their analysis was restricted to zero-sum games.

    Evidence (1)
    • Mixed-strategy equilibrium introduced in the 1944 book; restricted to zero-sum games Wikipedia: Nash equilibrium
  3. 1950

    Equilibrium points in n-person games (PNAS)

    first statement

    Nash, Equilibrium points in n-person games, PNAS 36 (1950) 48-49

    Nash's public statement of the equilibrium concept with an existence proof.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • Nash used the Kakutani fixed-point theorem in the 1950 paper Wikipedia: Nash equilibrium
  4. 1950

    Kakutani fixed point applied to game existence (same paper)

    first application

    Nash, PNAS 36 (1950) 48-49 (as above)

    Applies a fixed-point theorem to prove existence of equilibria in finite games.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same publication; existence proof via Kakutani Wikipedia: Nash equilibrium
  5. 1951

    Non-cooperative games: mixed equilibria via Brouwer

    key development

    Nash, Non-cooperative games, Annals of Mathematics 54 (1951) 286-295

    Defines mixed-strategy equilibrium for any finite game and proves existence using the simpler Brouwer theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume and year verified Crossref
    • 1951 paper defines mixed-strategy equilibrium and uses Brouwer Wikipedia: Nash equilibrium
  6. 1957

    Games and Decisions (textbook)

    consolidation

    Luce and Raiffa, Games and Decisions, Wiley, 1957

    Textbook-scale treatment of non-cooperative equilibrium, used to consolidate the concept for later readers.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book dated 1957 (Wiley) Open Library record
