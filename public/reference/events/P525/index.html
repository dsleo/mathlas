Ramsey lower bounds from random sphere geometry

Colouring edges of a complete graph by distances between random points on a high-dimensional sphere gave the first improvement in about fifty years on Erdos's 1947 bound for near-diagonal Ramsey numbers.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1947

    Erdos's probabilistic lower bound for diagonal Ramsey numbers

    precursor

    Erdos P., Some remarks on the theory of graphs, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 53, 292-294, 1947

    Erdos's random-colouring argument gave the classical exponential lower bound and introduced the probabilistic method in combinatorics; the baseline for the 2025 result.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Bull. AMS 53, 292-294, 1947 Crossref
    • Wikipedia states the exponential lower bound was given by Erdos in 1947 Wikipedia, Ramsey's theorem

    Chvatal and Harary, 'Generalized Ramsey theory for graphs'

    precursor

    V. Chvatal and F. Harary, 'Generalized Ramsey theory for graphs', Bulletin of the AMS (ISSN 0002-9904), pages 423-426, zbMATH record 3370375, 1972

    A 1972 paper with 'Ramsey theory' in its title is the earliest title found in the searched window that uses the field's name.

    Evidence (1)

  4. 2025

    Ma, Shen, Xie: exponential improvement on the Erdos bound for r(l, Cl)

    first statement

    Ma J., Shen W., Xie S., An exponential improvement for Ramsey lower bounds, arXiv:2507.12926, 2025

    The first exponential improvement over Erdos's 1947 lower bound for off-diagonal Ramsey numbers r(l, Cl), for every C > 1 and large l.

    Evidence (2)
    • arXiv record 2507.12926, posted 17 July 2025; abstract states the first exponential improvement over Erdos 1947 arXiv
    • A later paper says Ma, Shen and Xie broke the Erdos barrier for R(l, Cl) arXiv 2605.25843 (Lin and Niu)
  5. 2025

    Random sphere construction applied to off-diagonal Ramsey lower bounds

    first application

    Ma J., Shen W., Xie S., arXiv:2507.12926, 2025

    The same paper is the first use of random high-dimensional sphere graphs to lower-bound r(l, Cl).

    Evidence (2)
    • arXiv record 2507.12926 (same as the first-statement record) arXiv
    • Attwa et al. describe the Ma-Shen-Xie approach as a random sphere graph model arXiv 2602.02155
  6. 2025

    Hunter, Milojevic, Sudakov: Gaussian random graph proof

    key development

    Hunter Z., Milojevic A., Sudakov B., Gaussian random graphs and Ramsey numbers, arXiv:2512.17718, 2025

    A simpler proof of the Ma-Shen-Xie bound, using Gaussian random graphs, with better quantitative bounds.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record 2512.17718, posted 19 December 2025; abstract describes the simplified proof arXiv
  7. 2026

    Attwa, Lopez Vidal, Morris: multicolour extension via random sphere graphs

    key development

    Attwa Y., Lopez Vidal A., Morris P., A note on multicolour Ramsey numbers and random sphere graphs, arXiv:2602.02155, 2026

    Extends the random sphere graph approach to multicolour Ramsey numbers r(t; l), giving an exponential improvement over a previous exponent.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record 2602.02155, posted 2 February 2026; abstract describes the multicolour improvement arXiv
  8. 2026

    Lin and Niu: sharper exponent via refined Gaussian estimates

    key development

    Lin Q., Niu L., Sharper Ramsey lower bounds from refined Gaussian estimates, arXiv:2605.25843, 2026

    A further improvement of the exponent in the off-diagonal lower bound by a sharper cumulant generating function estimate.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record 2605.25843, posted 25 May 2026; abstract describes the sharper exponent arXiv
