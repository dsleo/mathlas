1900 · Differential equations

Riemann-Hilbert problem and monodromy

Riemann asked for a differential equation with prescribed monodromy group, and Plemelj attacked it with singular integral equations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1857

    Riemann's paper on functions of the Gauss series

    precursor

    Riemann, Beiträge zur Theorie der durch die Gauss'sche Reihe F(α, β, γ, x) darstellbaren Functionen, Abhandlungen der Königlichen Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen 7, 1857

    Riemann characterised hypergeometric functions by their behaviour under analytic continuation, the viewpoint Hilbert later said Riemann probably had in mind for the existence problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Hilbert's problem text, as quoted by Wikipedia, says Riemann himself may have had the problem in mind English Wikipedia, Hilbert's twenty-first problem
    • Crossref lists the title in Riemann's Collected Works reprint Crossref 10.1017/cbo9781139568050.005
  2. 1895

    Schlesinger's partial proof

    precursor

    Schlesinger, 1895 (proof in the unit-modulus case, using Poincaré's Fuchsian zeta-functions)

    Schlesinger proved existence of the equation when the local monodromy has eigenvalues of absolute value one, the only rigorous result Hilbert cited.

    Evidence (2)
    • Hilbert's text, as quoted by Wikipedia, credits Schlesinger 1895 with the proof in that special case English Wikipedia, Hilbert's twenty-first problem
    • Plemelj 1909 (zbMATH 2637289) is a note disputing Schlesinger's proof of the existence of such function systems zbMATH 2637289
  3. 1900

    Hilbert's twenty-first problem

    first statement

    Hilbert, Mathematische Probleme, lecture at the International Congress, Paris 1900; Nachrichten Göttingen 1900, 253-297

    Hilbert stated the problem of showing that a Fuchsian equation exists for any given singular points and monodromy group.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH lists Hilbert, Mathematische Probleme, Nachr. Ges. Wiss. Göttingen 1900 zbMATH 2664770
    • Wikipedia reproduces the statement of the problem from 1900 English Wikipedia, Hilbert's twenty-first problem
  4. 1905

    Hilbert reduces the problem to an integral equation

    first application

    Hilbert, Grundzüge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen (3rd communication), Nachrichten Göttingen, 1905

    Hilbert turned Riemann's problem into a singular integral equation, the tool Plemelj then used.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor's Plemelj biography says Hilbert reduced the problem to an integral equation in 1905 MacTutor biography of Plemelj
    • zbMATH lists the third communication, Nachr. Göttingen 1905 zbMATH 2650501
  5. 1908

    Plemelj's solution

    key development

    Plemelj, Riemannsche Funktionenscharen mit gegebener Monodromiegruppe, Monatshefte für Mathematik und Physik 19, 211-245, 1908

    Plemelj solved the existence problem by singular integral equations, giving a regular system with the prescribed monodromy; for decades it was accepted as a full solution.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record of the 1908 paper Crossref 10.1007/bf01736697
    • Wikipedia: Plemelj published a solution in 1908 that was long accepted, with a gap found in the early 1980s English Wikipedia, Hilbert's twenty-first problem
  6. 1957

    Röhrl's paper on the Riemann-Hilbert problem

    naming

    Röhrl, Das Riemann-Hilbertsche Problem der Theorie der linearen Differentialgleichungen, Mathematische Annalen 133, 1-25, 1957

    The earliest title I found that uses the name Riemann-Hilbert problem, treating the problem on Riemann surfaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record of Röhrl 1957, Math. Ann. 133, 1-25 Crossref 10.1007/bf01343983
    • Wikipedia notes Röhrl treated the problem in one complex dimension English Wikipedia, Hilbert's twenty-first problem
  7. 1989

    Bolibrukh's counterexample

    key development

    Bolibrukh, The Riemann-Hilbert problem on the complex projective line, Matematicheskie Zametki 46, 118-120, 1989

    Bolibrukh showed that in the Fuchsian-system form of the problem some monodromy groups cannot be realised, giving a negative answer.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH lists Bolibrukh's 1989 note zbMATH 4125736
    • Wikipedia dates the counterexample to 1989 and explains the three readings of the problem English Wikipedia, Hilbert's twenty-first problem
  8. 1994

    Anosov and Bolibruch monograph

    consolidation

    Anosov and Bolibruch, The Riemann-Hilbert problem, Aspects of Mathematics E22, Vieweg, 1994

    A book-length account of the problem, its history and the counterexample.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH lists the Vieweg volume (1994) zbMATH 589488
