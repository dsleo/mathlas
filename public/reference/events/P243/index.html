1974 · Geometry

Lattice gauge theory and Yang-Mills instantons (geometry)

Atiyah-Hitchin-Singer-Drinfeld-Manin construct instantons.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1974

    Lattice formulation and confinement of quarks

    first statement

    Wilson, Confinement of quarks, Physical Review D 10 (1974)

    Wilson gives the lattice formulation of gauge theory (Wilson loops) and uses it to address confinement.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wilson's 1974 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Wilson loops named after Kenneth G. Wilson in lattice gauge action Wikipedia: Lattice gauge theory
  2. 1974

    Lattice gauge theory applied to quark confinement

    first application

    Wilson, Confinement of quarks, Physical Review D 10 (1974)

    The lattice gauge framework is put to work on the confinement problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1974 paper addresses confinement of quarks Crossref
  3. 1975

    BPST pseudoparticle (first instanton)

    first statement

    Belavin, Polyakov, Schwarz, Tyupkin, Pseudoparticle solutions of the Yang-Mills equations, Physics Letters B 59 (1975)

    The BPST solution introduces the instanton (topological charge one) of Yang-Mills theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • BPST discoverers named in Wikipedia; Crossref lists author 'Schwartz' for Schwarz Wikipedia: Instanton
    • 1975 Physics Letters B paper, title and year Crossref
  4. 1978

    ADHM construction classifies all instantons

    key development

    Atiyah, Drinfeld, Hitchin, Manin, Construction of instantons, Physics Letters A 65 (1978)

    The ADHM construction reduces all instantons to linear-algebra data (monad construction).

    Evidence (2)
    • ADHM authors and paper title; all instantons built by linear algebra Wikipedia: ADHM construction
    • 1978 Physics Letters A paper, title and year Crossref
  5. 1979

    Atiyah's Geometry of Yang-Mills fields lectures

    consolidation

    Atiyah, Geometry of Yang-Mills fields, Lezioni Fermiane (Pisa, 1979)

    Atiyah's lecture notes give the first systematic geometric treatment of Yang-Mills fields and instantons.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lists 'Geometry on Yang-Mills fields (1979)' among Atiyah's books MacTutor: Michael Atiyah
    • Crossref has the 2008 Lecture Notes in Physics reprint of the text, not the 1979 original Crossref
