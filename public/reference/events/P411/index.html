1901 · Group & representation theory

Schur: polynomial representations of GL(n) and invariant theory

Schur's thesis used Frobenius methods to classify rational representations of the general linear group, tying them to symmetric functions.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1896

    Frobenius' group characters (1896)

    precursor

    G. Frobenius, Ueber Gruppencharaktere, Berl. Ber. 1896, 985-1021

    Frobenius's 1896 paper on group characters, the precursor of the character method used for symmetric groups.

  2. 1900

    Young's quantitative substitutional analysis

    precursor

    Alfred Young, On quantitative substitutional analysis, Proc. London Math. Soc. 33, 1900

    Introduces the diagrams (Young tableaux) used to index irreducible representations of the symmetric group and later Schur functors.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Proc. LMS s1-33 (1900), pp. 97-145. Crossref 10.1112/plms/s1-33.1.97
    • Schur functors are indexed by Young diagrams. Wikipedia, Schur functor
  3. 1901

    Schur's thesis on a class of matrices

    first statement

    Schur, I., 'Über eine Klasse von Matrizen, die sich einer gegebenen Matrix zuordnen lassen', Dissertation (1901)

    Schur's dissertation on matrix classes gives the first application of the representation approach that leads to GL(n).

    Dating or attribution note

    The thesis content is inferred from the title and the input summary; the review was not read, so the claim that it classifies GL(n) reps is not confirmed here.

  4. 1925

    Weyl's theory of representations of semisimple groups

    key development

    Hermann Weyl, Theorie der Darstellung kontinuierlicher halb-einfacher Gruppen durch lineare Transformationen I, Math. Z. 23, 1925

    Extends the ideas of Hurwitz and Schur to a general character formula for compact groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Math. Z. 23 (1925), pp. 271-309. Crossref 10.1007/bf01506234
    • Says Weyl took forward ideas introduced by Hurwitz and Schur. MacTutor, Weyl biography
  5. 1927

    Rational representations of the general linear group

    key development

    Schur, I., 'Über die rationalen Darstellungen der allgemeinen linearen Gruppe', Sitzungsber. Preuss. Akad. (1927)

    Gives the classification of rational representations of GL(n) through symmetric functions, the central result of this event.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Über die rationalen Darstellungen der allgemeinen linearen Gruppe', year 1927 zbMATH API, query 'Schur Uber die rationalen Darstellungen der allgemeinen linearen Gruppe'

  6. 1939

    Weyl's The Classical Groups

    consolidation

    Hermann Weyl, The Classical Groups: Their Invariants and Representations, Princeton, 1939

    Monograph that presents the Schur-Weyl treatment of invariants and representations of GL(n).

    Evidence (1)
    • First Princeton edition 1939. Open Library record OL8018944W
  7. 1979

    Macdonald's Symmetric Functions and Hall Polynomials

    consolidation

    I. G. Macdonald, Symmetric Functions and Hall Polynomials, Oxford, 1979

    Standard systematic treatment of Schur functions and their link to representations of GL(n).

    Evidence (1)
    • First published 1979, Oxford University Press. Open Library record OL2690889W
