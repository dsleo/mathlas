1985 · Computer science

Quantum computation: Shor's algorithm

A quantum Fourier transform finds periods and hence factors integers in polynomial time, tying quantum mechanics to complexity and cryptography.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1982

    Simulating physics with computers

    precursor

    Feynman, Simulating physics with computers, Int. J. Theor. Phys. (1982)

    Proposes that quantum systems could be simulated by quantum computing devices.

    Evidence (1)
    • Int. J. Theor. Phys. 1982 paper Crossref / publisher record
  2. 1985

    Universal quantum computer

    first statement

    Deutsch, Quantum theory, the Church-Turing principle and the universal quantum computer, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A (1985)

    Defines a universal quantum computer model, the first public statement of quantum computation as a computing theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 1985 paper Crossref / publisher record
  3. 1994

    Quantum period-finding (Simon's problem)

    precursor

    Simon, On the power of quantum computation, FOCS (1994)

    Oracle period-finding with quantum queries; the direct inspiration for Shor's period-finding approach to factoring.

    Evidence (2)
    • FOCS 1994 paper (DOI 10.1109/sfcs.1994.365701) Crossref / publisher record
    • Shor's work followed Simon's problem MacTutor Shor biography
  4. 1994

    Shor's factoring algorithm

    first application

    Shor, Algorithms for quantum computation: discrete logarithms and factoring, FOCS (1994)

    Uses the quantum Fourier transform to find periods and hence factor integers in polynomial time.

    Evidence (2)
    • FOCS 1994 proceedings paper (DOI 10.1109/sfcs.1994.365700) Crossref / publisher record
    • Shor published the algorithm known as Shor's algorithm in 1994 MacTutor Shor biography
  5. 1997

    Journal version of factoring algorithm

    key development

    Shor, Polynomial-time algorithms for prime factorization and discrete logarithms on a quantum computer, SIAM J. Comput. (1997)

    Full journal version with discrete logarithms and prime factorization.

    Dating or attribution note

    An arXiv preprint (quant-ph/9508027, 1995) exists per background knowledge; the arXiv record was not verified in this session because of rate limits.

    Evidence (1)
    • SIAM J. Comput. 1997 paper (DOI 10.1137/s0097539795293172) Crossref / publisher record
  6. 2000

    Nielsen-Chuang textbook

    consolidation

    Nielsen, Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, Cambridge University Press (2000)

    Standard textbook presenting quantum computation and factoring.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record of Nielsen and Chuang, 2000 Crossref / publisher record
