Learning with errors and lattice cryptography

Regev's LWE gave worst-case to average-case reductions and a quantum reduction from lattice problems.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1996

    Hard lattice instances from random instances

    precursor

    Generating hard instances of lattice problems (extended abstract) (1996)

    Ajtai supplied an earlier worst-case to average-case lattice connection, before LWE.

  2. 2005

    Learning with errors and cryptography

    first statement

    On lattices, learning with errors, random linear codes, and cryptography (2005)

    The STOC paper introduces LWE, its lattice hardness connection, and a cryptosystem.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated publication record and abstract support the 2005 learning with errors and cryptography milestone. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2013

    Ideal lattices and learning with errors over rings

    key development

    Lyubashevsky, V.; Peikert, C., On ideal lattices and learning with errors over rings, J. ACM 60 (2013) art. 43, DOI 10.1145/2535925

    Introduces ring-LWE, the structured variant that made LWE practical for efficient cryptography.
