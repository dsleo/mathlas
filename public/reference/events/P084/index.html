1984 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Wavelets

Multiresolution ideas from signal processing and quantum field theory are merged with harmonic analysis to give orthonormal bases of compactly supported functions.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1910

    Haar's orthogonal system

    precursor

    Haar, Zur Theorie der orthogonalen Funktionensysteme, Math. Ann. (1910)

    Haar's orthogonal system of step functions, the first compactly supported orthonormal wavelet; not called a wavelet at the time.

    Evidence (2)
    • Math. Ann. 1910 paper on orthogonal function systems Crossref / publisher record
    • Haar wavelet as the first wavelet (Haar 1909 dissertation); timeline in Wikipedia Wikipedia: Wavelet
  2. 1982

    Morlet et al. wave propagation and sampling

    precursor

    Morlet, Arens, Fourgeau, Giard, Wave propagation and sampling theory, Part I, Geophysics (1982)

    Uses wavelet-type time-frequency analysis on seismic signals, the first purposeful use of the wavelet idea as a tool.

    Evidence (2)
    • Geophysics 1982 Part I (DOI 10.1190/1.1441328) Crossref / publisher record
    • Morlet's work on seismic data leading to the continuous wavelet transform Wikipedia: Wavelet transform
  3. 1984

    Grossmann-Morlet wavelet decomposition

    first statement

    Grossmann, Morlet, Decomposition of Hardy functions into square integrable wavelets of constant shape, SIAM J. Math. Anal. (1984)

    Formulates the continuous wavelet transform as a decomposition into dilates and translates of one function.

    Evidence (2)
    • SIAM J. Math. Anal. 1984 paper Crossref / publisher record
    • Continuous wavelet transform credited to Grossmann and Morlet Wikipedia: Wavelet transform
  4. 1984

    'Wavelets' in a mathematical title

    naming

    Grossmann, Morlet (1984), see above

    The 1984 title is the first title in this record set to use the term 'wavelets' in the mathematical sense.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1984 SIAM paper; title contains 'square integrable wavelets' Crossref / publisher record
  5. 1988

    Compactly supported orthonormal wavelets

    key development

    Daubechies, Orthonormal bases of compactly supported wavelets, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. (1988)

    Constructs smooth compactly supported orthonormal wavelet bases, making wavelets practical for signal processing.

    Evidence (2)
    • Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 1988 paper Crossref / publisher record
    • Daubechies' 1988 orthonormal compactly supported bases MacTutor Daubechies biography
  6. 1989

    Multiresolution and the fast wavelet transform

    key development

    Mallat, A theory for multiresolution signal decomposition: the wavelet representation, IEEE Trans. PAMI (1989)

    Introduces multiresolution analysis and the fast wavelet transform, linking wavelets to filter banks.

    Evidence (2)
    • IEEE Trans. PAMI 1989 paper Crossref / publisher record
    • Fast wavelet transform introduced by Mallat and Meyer in 1988/89 Wikipedia: Multiresolution analysis
  7. 1992

    Daubechies' lectures

    consolidation

    Daubechies, Ten Lectures on Wavelets, SIAM (CBMS-NSF 61) (1992)

    Textbook-length treatment of wavelet theory and construction, the standard systematic reference.

    Evidence (1)
    • SIAM 1992 lectures (DOI 10.1137/1.9781611970104) Crossref / publisher record
