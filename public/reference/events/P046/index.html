Sphere packing in dimensions 8 and 24

A modular-form construction produces the magic function required by the Fourier linear programming bound, matching the E8 and Leech lattice packing densities.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2003

    Linear-programming upper bounds

    precursor

    H. Cohn and N. Elkies, New upper bounds on sphere packings I, Ann. Math. 157 (2003)

    The upper-bound method preceded the exact optimality proofs.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals record dates the bounds paper to 2003. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2016

    Dimension-eight optimality proved

    first statement

    M. Viazovska, The sphere packing problem in dimension 8, arXiv:1603.04246 (2016)

    Viazovska proved the E8 lattice packing optimal in eight dimensions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint dated March 2016 states the optimality theorem. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2016

    Dimension-twenty-four optimality proved

    key development

    H. Cohn et al., The sphere packing problem in dimension 24, arXiv:1603.06518 (2016)

    A second public paper proved the Leech lattice optimal in twenty-four dimensions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint dated March 2016 states the 24-dimensional result. Original publication or repository record
  4. 2022

    Universal optimality of E8 and Leech lattices

    key development

    Cohn, Kumar, Miller, Radchenko, Viazovska, Universal optimality of the E8 and Leech lattices and interpolation formulas, pp. 983-1082

    The interpolation method extends the dimension-8 and dimension-24 optimality results to universal optimality.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for this title, year 2022, pages 983-1082 zbMATH Open
