1939 · Information & cryptography

Wiener-Kolmogorov filtering

Optimal prediction of stationary processes was solved by spectral methods.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1939

    Kolmogorov's note on interpolation and extrapolation of stationary sequences

    first statement

    Kolmogoroff, Sur l'interpolation et extrapolation des suites stationnaires, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 208 (1939) 2043-2045

    Kolmogorov announced the solution of the interpolation and extrapolation problem for stationary sequences by least squares, with the limiting error expressed through the spectral density.

    Evidence (2)
    • Kolmogoroff, Sur l'interpolation et extrapolation des suites stationnaires, C. R. 208 (1939) 2043-2045 zbMATH 2511388
    • Review of the 1941 paper: the interpolation problem is solved 'as in Kolmogoroff, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 208 (1939), 2043-2045'. zbMATH 2503221 (review by H. Wold)
  2. 1941

    Kolmogorov's interpolation and extrapolation of stationary sequences

    key development

    Kolmogorov, Interpolation und Extrapolation von stationaren zufalligen Folgen, Bull. Acad. Sci. URSS Ser. Math. 5 (1941) 3-14

    Kolmogorov's fuller account in the Izvestiya of the discrete-time interpolation and extrapolation problem, with the formula for the prediction error; proofs are only sketched and refer to a longer paper (Bull. Univ. Moscow 2, no. 6, 1941).

    Evidence (3)
    • Kolmogorov, Interpolation und Extrapolation von stationaren zufalligen Folgen, Bull. Acad. Sci. URSS (Izv. Ser. Mat.) (1941) 3-14 zbMATH record (1941, pp. 3-14)
    • The discrete-time equivalent of Wiener's work was derived independently by Kolmogorov and published in 1941 Wikipedia, Wiener filter
    • Wold's review describes the prediction-error formula and states that proofs are only indicated, with a reference to the 1939 note. zbMATH 2503221 (review by H. Wold)
  3. 1942

    Wiener's restricted wartime report on filtering

    precursor

    Wiener, The interpolation, extrapolation and smoothing of stationary time series, NDRC report (DIC-6037, MIT), February 1942

    Wiener developed continuous-time filtering in a restricted wartime report in 1942, before the public book.

    Evidence (3)
    • Wiener proposed the filter during the 1940s and published it in 1949 Wikipedia, Wiener filter
    • Report of the Services 19, Research Project DIC-6037, MIT, February 1942; published 1949 by MIT Press. Wikipedia, Wiener filter (references)
    • Wiener's 1942 classified report, nicknamed the 'yellow peril', published postwar in 1949. Wikipedia, Norbert Wiener
  4. 1949

    Wiener's Extrapolation, Interpolation, and Smoothing of Stationary Time Series

    consolidation

    Wiener, Extrapolation, Interpolation, and Smoothing of Stationary Time Series, with Engineering Applications (1949)

    The published monograph that presents the continuous-time least-squares filter as a complete theory.

    Evidence (3)
    • MIT Press record, Extrapolation, Interpolation, and Smoothing of Stationary Time Series, 1949. Crossref 10.7551/mitpress/2946.001.0001
    • Wiener, Extrapolation, Interpolation, and Smoothing of Stationary Time Series (1949) Open Library search record (first publish year 1949)
    • The filter was published in 1949 Wikipedia, Wiener filter
  5. 1950

    Bode and Shannon's simplified derivation

    key development

    Bode, Shannon, A simplified derivation of linear least square smoothing and prediction theory, Proc. IRE 38 (1950) 417-425

    Bode and Shannon gave a short engineering derivation of least-squares smoothing and prediction, which made the theory usable.

    Evidence (1)
    • Bode, Shannon, A simplified derivation of linear least square smoothing and prediction theory, Proc. IRE 38 (1950) 417-425 Crossref DOI 10.1109/jrproc.1950.231821
