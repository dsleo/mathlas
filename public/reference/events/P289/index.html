1955 · Algebraic geometry

Serre GAGA

Analytic and algebraic coherent sheaf cohomology on projective varieties were shown to agree.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1949

    Chow's theorem

    precursor

    W.-L. Chow, On compact complex analytic varieties, Amer. J. Math. 71 (1949)

    Shows compact analytic subvarieties of projective space are algebraic, an early analytic-algebraic comparison.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1949. Crossref
  2. 1950–1953

    Foundations for analytic-algebraic comparison in the early 1950s

    precursor

    Foundations relating complex-analytic and algebraic geometry were put in place in the early 1950s, including the Oka coherence theorem for analytic structure sheaves.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia GAGA article: foundations were put in place during the early part of the 1950s, and the Oka coherence theorem is cited Wikipedia, Algebraic geometry and analytic geometry
  3. 1950

    Cartan on ideals and modules of analytic functions

    precursor

    H. Cartan, Ideaux et modules de fonctions analytiques de variables complexes, Bull. SMF 78 (1950)

    Develops coherence-type theory for analytic functions on which Serre's sheaf comparison depends.

    Dating or attribution note

    Role inferred from title and general knowledge. Reclassified from key_development to precursor: it predates this event's first statement (1955) and belongs to the wider field.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1950. Crossref
  4. 1955

    Serre's Faisceaux algébriques cohérents

    first statement

    Serre, J.-P., Faisceaux algébriques cohérents, Annals of Mathematics 61 (1955)

    Coherence of the structure sheaf and cohomology of coherent sheaves on algebraic varieties: the algebraic side of the comparison.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annals of Mathematics record for Faisceaux algébriques cohérents, year 1955 Crossref
    • Wikipedia GAGA article: the algebraic structure sheaf's coherence was proved in FAC (1955) Wikipedia, Algebraic geometry and analytic geometry
  5. 1956

    Serre's GAGA comparison theorems

    key development

    Serre, J.-P., Géométrie algébrique et géométrie analytique, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 6 (1956)

    Proves that coherent sheaf cohomology of projective varieties agrees with the analytic one, making algebraic and analytic methods interchangeable.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annales de l'Institut Fourier record for GAGA, year 1956 Crossref
    • Wikipedia GAGA article: GAGA is the major paper consolidating the theory Wikipedia, Algebraic geometry and analytic geometry
  6. 1956

    Hirzebruch applies topological methods

    key development

    F. Hirzebruch, Neue topologische Methoden in der algebraischen Geometrie, Springer, 1956

    A book using sheaf cohomology and the comparison of analytic and algebraic viewpoints.

    Dating or attribution note

    Link to GAGA inferred from general knowledge.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book recorded in Crossref, 1956. Crossref
  7. 1956

    Serre, 'Geometrie algebrique et geometrie analytique'

    naming

    J.-P. Serre, 'Geometrie algebrique et geometrie analytique', Annales de l'Institut Fourier 6 (1955-56), DOI 10.5802/aif.59

    Serre's paper, whose French title names the two fields compared in GAGA, is the first title that uses this field name.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record gives title 'Geometrie algebrique et geometrie analytique', author Serre, container Annales de l'Institut Fourier, year 1956 Crossref, DOI 10.5802/aif.59
  8. 1971

    Grothendieck's SGA 1 extends GAGA

    key development

    Grothendieck, Revetements etales et groupe fondamental (SGA 1), 1960-61, published 1971

    The comparison is extended to proper schemes over the complex numbers, with consequences for fundamental groups.

    Evidence (1)
    • SGA 1 listed in Open Library. Open Library
