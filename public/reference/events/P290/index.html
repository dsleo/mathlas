1938 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Sobolev spaces and generalised solutions

Weak derivatives and function spaces were introduced to treat generalised solutions of wave and elliptic equations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1933

    Nikodym's absolute-continuity characterisation

    precursor

    Nikodym, 'Sur une classe de fonctions considerée dans l'etude du probleme de Dirichlet', Fundamenta Mathematicae 21 (1933)

    Nikodym established the ACL characterisation of the class later used in the Sobolev-space description.

    Dating or attribution note

    Precursor: a characterisation of a related class, not the definition of the spaces.

    Evidence (3)
    • Paper record: Fund. Math. 1933 Crossref
    • ACL characterisation of Sobolev spaces established by Nikodym (1933) Wikipedia: Sobolev space
    • zbMATH 2543418: Nikodym, Sur une classe de fonctions consideree dans l'etude du probleme de Dirichlet, Fundamenta Mathematicae, 1933. zbMATH Open 2543418
  2. 1936

    Sobolev's new method for the Cauchy problem for hyperbolic equations

    precursor

    S. Soboleff, Méthode nouvelle à résoudre le problème de Cauchy pour les équations linéaires hyperboliques normales, Rec. Math. Moscou (2) 1 (1936) 39-72

    Sobolev's 1936 paper applies a new method to the Cauchy problem for hyperbolic equations, the first purposeful use in the sources checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH 0014.05902: Méthode nouvelle à résoudre le problème de Cauchy..., 1936, pp. 39-72 zbMATH 0014.05902
  3. 1936

    Sobolev's estimates for functions with square-integrable derivatives

    precursor

    S. Sobolev, Sur quelques evaluations concernant les familles de fonctions ayant des derivees a carre integrable, C. R. (Doklady) Acad. Sci. URSS 1 (1936)

    A short note on estimates for families of functions whose derivatives are square-integrable, close in subject to the later function spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH 2525918: Sobolev, Sur quelques evaluations concernant les familles de fonctions ayant des derivees a carre integrable, Comptes Rendus (Doklady) Acad. Sci. URSS, 1936. zbMATH Open 2525918
  4. 1938

    Sobolev's function spaces

    first statement

    Soboleff, S., Sur un theoreme d'analyse fonctionnelle, Mat. Sb. 4 (46) (1938) 471-497 (English: On a theorem of functional analysis, AMS Transl. (2) 34 (1963) 39-68)

    Sobolev introduced function spaces with a norm combining Lp-norms of a function and its derivatives, so that generalised solutions of PDE lie in them.

    Evidence (4)
    • zbMATH 3035784: Soboleff, Sur un theoreme d'analyse fonctionnelle, Recueil Mathematique, 1938. zbMATH Open 3035784
    • English translation (AMS, 1963) cited as a translation of Mat. Sb. 4 (1938) Wikipedia: Sobolev space (references)
    • Sobolev function spaces introduced in the 1930s MacTutor: Sobolev biography
    • AMS translation of the paper, issued 1963 (pp. 39-68). Crossref DOI 10.1090/trans2/034/02
  5. 1950

    Sobolev's Applications of Functional Analysis in Mathematical Physics

    consolidation

    Sobolev, Some Applications of Functional Analysis in Mathematical Physics (Leningrad, 1950; AMS translation 1963)

    The monograph that collected the applications of the function spaces to PDE and mathematical physics.

    Evidence (2)
    • AMS translation record (1963) of the book Crossref
    • Book published 1950 in Russian, English 1963 MacTutor: Sobolev biography
  6. 1975

    Adams' Sobolev Spaces

    consolidation

    Adams, Sobolev Spaces (Academic Press, 1975)

    A systematic monograph on Sobolev spaces that became a standard reference.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library work record: 'Sobolev spaces' by R. A. Adams, first published 1975. Open Library work OL7009871W
