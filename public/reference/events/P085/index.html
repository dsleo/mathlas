1956 · Combinatorics

Shannon capacity of graphs and the Lovász theta function

A coding-theory quantity for graphs is bounded by a semidefinite programme, giving the theta function and its exact polynomial-time computation for perfect graphs.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1956

    Zero-error capacity of a noisy channel

    first statement

    Shannon, The zero error capacity of a noisy channel, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory (1956)

    Shannon defines the zero-error capacity of a noisy channel, the setting in which the graph capacity arises.

    Dating or attribution note

    Attribution of the graph-capacity concept to this 1956 paper is from the literature and was not checked in the text; the Wikipedia Shannon capacity page does not cite 1956.

    Evidence (2)
    • Shannon 1956 IEEE IT paper, author and year Crossref
    • Shannon's information-theory work and zero-error setting MacTutor: Claude Shannon
  2. 1979

    Theta function introduced; bound on Shannon capacity

    key development

    Lovász, On the Shannon capacity of a graph, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 25 (1979)

    Lovász introduces the theta function as a semidefinite upper bound on the Shannon capacity of a graph.

    Evidence (2)
    • Theta function first introduced in Lovász's 1979 paper Wikipedia: Lovász number
    • 1979 IEEE IT paper, author and year Crossref
  3. 1979

    Theta applied to Shannon's problem on the pentagon

    first application

    Lovász, On the Shannon capacity of a graph, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 25 (1979)

    Lovász applies theta to Shannon's capacity problem, the target that motivates the bound.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lovász solved a long-standing problem of Shannon in coding theory MacTutor: László Lovász
  4. 1981

    Polynomial-time computation of theta via the ellipsoid method

    key development

    Grötschel, Lovász, Schrijver, The ellipsoid method and its consequences in combinatorial optimization, Combinatorica 1 (1981)

    The ellipsoid-method paper shows theta is computable in polynomial time, making the bound usable.

    Evidence (2)
    • Theta computable in polynomial time by SDP and the ellipsoid method Wikipedia: Lovász number
    • Combinatorica 1981 paper, authors and year Crossref
  5. 1988

    Geometric Algorithms and Combinatorial Optimization (monograph)

    consolidation

    Grötschel, Lovász, Schrijver, Geometric Algorithms and Combinatorial Optimization, Springer (1988)

    The monograph gives the first systematic treatment of the ellipsoid and semidefinite methods that include theta.

    Dating or attribution note

    Systematic treatment is an inference; the book is the standard reference for theta's algorithmic use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book title, authors, year 1988 Open Library
  6. 1994

    Knuth's Sandwich Theorem exposition

    key development

    Knuth, The Sandwich Theorem, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics (1994)

    Knuth gives a survey-style account of theta and the sandwich theorem, which Wikipedia attributes to Lovász.

    Evidence (2)
    • Sandwich Theorem, author and year Crossref
    • Lovász sandwich theorem for theta Wikipedia: Lovász number
