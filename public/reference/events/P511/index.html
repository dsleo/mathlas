2004 · Probability

Random planar maps and the Brownian map

Bijections for planar maps allow scaling limits to a random metric sphere, linking combinatorics, probability and two-dimensional quantum gravity.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2004

    Brownian map named as quadrangulation limit

    first statement

    J.-F. Marckert and A. Mokkadem, Limit of normalized quadrangulations: The Brownian map, arXiv:math/0403398 (2004)

    The public preprint names a random scaling-limit object the Brownian map.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the preprint to 2004 and the abstract explicitly names the Brownian map. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2011

    Universality proved for planar maps

    key development

    J.-F. Le Gall, Uniqueness and universality of the Brownian map, arXiv:1105.4842 (2011)

    The public paper proves convergence of multiple random-map families to a universal Brownian-map limit.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the preprint to 2011 and states the universality theorem. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2013

    Le Gall: uniqueness and universality of the Brownian map

    key development

    Le Gall, Uniqueness and universality of the Brownian map, Ann. Probab., 2013, pp. 2880-2960

    Proves uniqueness of the Brownian map limit and its universality among planar maps.

    Dating or attribution note

    Journal version of a result also listed in the existing 2011 entry; this is the published characterisation.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for this title, year 2013, pages 2880-2960 zbMATH Open
  4. 2013

    Miermont: Brownian map as scaling limit of quadrangulations

    key development

    Miermont, The Brownian map is the scaling limit of uniform random plane quadrangulations, Acta Math., 2013, pp. 319-401

    Shows the Brownian map is the scaling limit of uniform random plane quadrangulations.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for this title, year 2013, pages 319-401 zbMATH Open
