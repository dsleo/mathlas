2001 · Statistics & decision theory

Smirnov conformal invariance

Smirnov proved conformal invariance of critical Ising and percolation using discrete holomorphicity.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2001

    Conformal invariance of triangular-lattice percolation

    first statement

    S. Smirnov, Critical percolation in the plane: conformal invariance, Cardy’s formula, scaling limits, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 333 (2001)

    The published paper establishes Cardy’s formula and conformal invariance for critical site percolation on the triangular lattice.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher record dates the paper to August 2001 and states its result. Original bibliographic record
  2. 2006

    Towards conformal invariance of 2D lattice models (ICM talk)

    consolidation

    Smirnov, S., Towards conformal invariance of 2D lattice models, Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians, Madrid 2006 (arXiv 0708.0032)

    An ICM survey of the conformal-invariance programme for 2D lattice models, with percolation as the leading example.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: ICM Madrid 2006 proceedings contribution zBMATH Open record (search API)
  3. 2007

    Holomorphic fermions in the Ising model

    key development

    S. Smirnov, Conformal invariance in random cluster models. I. Holomorphic fermions in the Ising model, arXiv:0708.0039 (2007)

    The public preprint constructs discrete holomorphic observables for the critical Ising model.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv records the 2007 preprint and describes the Ising construction. Original bibliographic record
