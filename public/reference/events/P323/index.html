Natural proofs and algebrization barriers

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1994

    Natural proofs barrier

    first statement

    Natural Proofs (1994)

    The dated public report identifies a conditional barrier to broad classes of circuit lower-bound proofs.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated record and abstract support this 1994 milestone. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2008

    Algebrization barrier

    key development

    Algebrization: A New Barrier in Complexity Theory (2008)

    The public report describes a further barrier beyond relativization and natural proofs.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated record and abstract support this 2008 milestone. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2008

    Cracks in the defenses: survey of circuit lower-bound approaches

    consolidation

    Allender, E., Cracks in the defenses: scouting out approaches on circuit lower bounds, in: Computer Science - Theory and Applications (CSR 2008), Springer LNCS

    A survey that discusses natural proofs among other barriers to circuit lower bounds.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: CSR 2008 proceedings survey zBMATH Open record (search API)
  4. 2017

    Algebraic natural proofs barrier

    key development

    Grochow, J. A.; Kumar, M., Towards an algebraic natural proofs barrier via polynomial identity testing, arXiv 1701.01717 (2017)

    Shows that a natural-proofs-type barrier also constrains algebraic approaches, extending the 1994 barrier to the algebraic setting.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: arXiv 1701.01717 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  5. 2018

    Affine relativization unifies algebrization and relativization

    key development

    Aydinlioğlu, B.; Bach, E., Affine relativization: unifying the algebrization and relativization barriers, ACM Trans. Comput. Theory 10 (2018) art. 1, DOI 10.1145/3170704

    Combines the algebrization and relativization barriers into one framework, a direct refinement of the 2008 barrier.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: ACM TOCT 10 (2018) art. 1 zBMATH Open record (search API)
