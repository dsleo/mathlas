2007 · Algebraic geometry

Topological recursion and enumerative geometry

Eynard-Orantin recursion recovers Gromov-Witten and Weil-Petersson volumes.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2007

    Eynard–Orantin recursion formulated

    first statement

    B. Eynard and N. Orantin, Invariants of algebraic curves and topological expansion (2007)

    The 2007 preprint defines algebraic-curve invariants and recursive topological expansion.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents eynard–orantin recursion formulated in 2007 arXiv
  2. 2007

    Recursion applied to Kontsevich integral

    first application

    B. Eynard and N. Orantin, Invariants of algebraic curves and topological expansion (2007)

    The founding paper applies its invariants to the Kontsevich integral and gives a KdV result.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents recursion applied to kontsevich integral in 2007 arXiv
  3. 2007

    Recursion applied to Weil–Petersson volumes

    key development

    B. Eynard and N. Orantin, Weil-Petersson volume of moduli spaces, Mirzakhani's recursion and matrix models (2007)

    A later 2007 paper identifies Mirzakhani volume recursion with matrix-model loop equations.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents recursion applied to weil–petersson volumes in 2007 arXiv
  4. 2012

    Topological recursion proves BKMP conjecture

    key development

    B. Eynard and N. Orantin, Computation of open Gromov-Witten invariants for toric Calabi-Yau 3-folds by topological recursion, a proof of the BKMP conjecture (2012)

    The method computes toric Calabi–Yau open Gromov–Witten invariants in general.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents topological recursion proves bkmp conjecture in 2012 arXiv
  5. 2016

    Eynard's Counting Surfaces lectures

    consolidation

    Eynard, Counting Surfaces, CRM Aisenstadt Chair Lectures, Birkhäuser, 2016

    A monograph-length treatment of topological recursion as a tool for counting surfaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for Counting surfaces, CRM Aisenstadt chair lectures, year 2016, ISBN 978-3-7643-8796-9 zbMATH Open
