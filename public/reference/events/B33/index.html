Kakeya sets in three dimensions

A volume estimate for unions of convex sets obtained from incidence geometry and multi-scale arguments settles the Kakeya set conjecture in R^3.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2024

    Wang–Zahl prove a partial 3D Kakeya dimension result

    precursor

    H. Wang and J. Zahl, The Assouad dimension of Kakeya sets in R³, arXiv:2401.12337 (2024)

    The precursor establishes full Assouad dimension and special Hausdorff-dimension cases.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted in January 2024 arXiv
  2. 2025

    Three-dimensional Kakeya theorem posted

    first statement

    H. Wang and J. Zahl, Volume estimates for unions of convex sets, and the Kakeya set conjecture in three dimensions, arXiv:2502.17655 (2025)

    The public preprint states the Hausdorff-dimension-three result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted 24 February 2025 arXiv
  3. 2025

    Volume estimates applied to Kakeya sets

    first application

    H. Wang and J. Zahl, Volume estimates for unions of convex sets, and the Kakeya set conjecture in three dimensions, arXiv:2502.17655 (2025)

    Multi-scale volume estimates for convex-set unions close the three-dimensional Kakeya target.

    Evidence (1)
    • The paper title and abstract identify the volume-estimate application arXiv
  4. 2026

    Streamlined proof with Guth

    key development

    L. Guth, H. Wang and J. Zahl, A streamlined proof of the Kakeya set conjecture in R³, arXiv:2601.14411 (2026)

    A later public preprint gives a streamlined proof of the theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted in January 2026 arXiv
