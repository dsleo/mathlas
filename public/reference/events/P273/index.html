1986 · Number theory

Ribet and modularity

Ribet shows Frey curve would contradict modularity.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1982

    Frey notices the non-modularity of the Frey curve

    precursor

    Frey draws attention to the unusual properties of the curve tied to a Fermat triple, before any publication.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: in 1982 Frey called attention to the curve Wikipedia: Ribet's theorem
  2. 1986

    Frey's link between elliptic curves and Diophantine equations

    first statement

    Frey, Links between stable elliptic curves and certain Diophantine equations, Ann. Univ. Sarav. Ser. Math. 1 (1986)

    Publishes the Frey-curve argument linking a counterexample to Fermat with non-modular elliptic curves.

    Evidence (1)
    • The university library catalogue records Frey's 40-page Links between stable elliptic curves and certain diophantine equations in 1986. Karlsruhe Institute of Technology library
  3. 1987

    Serre's epsilon-type conjecture

    first statement

    Serre, Sur les representations modulaires de degre 2 de Gal(Qbar/Q), Duke Math. J. 54 (1987) 179-230

    States the conjecture on modularity of mod-p Galois representations that Ribet later proved in the required case.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content of the conjecture from Wikipedia and the title; the conjecture's exact statement was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
    • Wikipedia: Ribet's theorem was proposed by Serre Wikipedia: Ribet's theorem
  4. 1990

    Epsilon conjecture proved: Frey curve not modular

    first application

    Ribet, On modular representations of Gal(Qbar/Q) arising from modular forms, Invent. Math. 100 (1990) 431-476

    Proves the epsilon conjecture, so a Frey curve from a Fermat counterexample would contradict modularity.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
    • Wikipedia: the theorem was proven by Ken Ribet, a step toward Fermat's Last Theorem Wikipedia: Ribet's theorem
  5. 1990

    Expository account from Taniyama-Shimura to Fermat

    consolidation

    Ribet, From the Taniyama-Shimura conjecture to Fermat's last theorem, Ann. Fac. Sci. Toulouse 11 (1990) 116-139

    Survey explaining the chain from modularity to Fermat's Last Theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
  6. 1995

    Modularity for semistable elliptic curves

    key development

    Wiles, Modular elliptic curves and Fermat's Last Theorem, Ann. of Math. 141 (1995) 443-551

    Proves modularity for semistable elliptic curves, the input needed to close the Frey-Ribet argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
