Twistor theory

Penrose encodes spacetime fields as complex geometry.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Penrose's twistor algebra

    first statement

    Penrose, R., Twistor algebra, J. Math. Phys. 8 (1967) 345-366

    Introduces twistor space as the spinor-like setting for massless fields in spacetime.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in J. Math. Phys. 8 (1967), DOI 10.1063/1.1705200, pages 345-366 Crossref; zbMATH 0163.22602
    • Wikipedia says twistor theory was proposed by Penrose in 1967, citing this paper Wikipedia, Twistor theory (lead)
  2. 1967

    Term 'twistor' in a title

    naming

    Penrose, R., Twistor algebra (1967)

    The term 'twistor' is fixed in this paper title.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title of the 1967 paper Crossref
  3. 1976

    Nonlinear gravitons and curved twistor theory

    key development

    Penrose, R., Nonlinear gravitons and curved twistor theory, General Relativity and Gravitation 7 (1976)

    Extends twistor methods to curved space and gives the nonlinear graviton construction for self-dual gravitational fields.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper in General Relativity and Gravitation (1976), DOI 10.1007/bf00762011 Crossref
  4. 1977

    Twistors applied to instantons

    first application

    Atiyah, M. F.; Ward, R. S., Instantons and algebraic geometry, Comm. Math. Phys. 55 (1977) 117-124

    Applies the twistor correspondence to classify self-dual Yang-Mills instantons through algebraic geometry on twistor space.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper in Comm. Math. Phys. (1977), DOI 10.1007/bf01626514, zbMATH 0362.14004, pages 117-124 Crossref; zbMATH
  5. 1978

    Self-duality in four-dimensional Riemannian geometry

    key development

    Atiyah, M. F.; Hitchin, N. J.; Singer, I. M., Self-duality in four-dimensional Riemannian geometry, Proc. Roy. Soc. London A 362 (1978) 425-461

    Computes the dimension of moduli spaces of self-dual connections by the index theorem; the twistor methods handle the classification.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper in Proc. Roy. Soc. A (1978), DOI 10.1098/rspa.1978.0143, zbMATH 0389.53011, pages 425-461 Crossref; zbMATH
  6. 1990

    Ward and Wells textbook on twistor geometry

    consolidation

    Ward, R. S.; Wells, R. O. jun., Twistor Geometry and Field Theory, Cambridge University Press (1990)

    A systematic textbook treatment of twistor geometry and its field-theoretic applications.

    Evidence (2)
    • Book in Cambridge University Press (1990), DOI 10.1017/cbo9780511524493 Crossref
    • zbMATH indexes the book as 0714.53059 (1989 entry) zbMATH
