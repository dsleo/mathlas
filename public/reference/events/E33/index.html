1963 · Geometry

Atiyah-Singer index theorem

The analytic index of an elliptic operator is computed from topological data, connecting PDE, characteristic classes and algebraic geometry.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1960

    Gel'fand poses the index problem

    precursor

    Gel'fand asks for topological information about the index of elliptic operators.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gel'fand's 1960 index question Atiyah's historical account
  2. 1963

    Atiyah–Singer theorem announced

    first statement

    M. Atiyah and I. Singer, The index of elliptic operators on compact manifolds, Bull. AMS 69 (1963)

    Atiyah and Singer publish the equality of analytic and topological indices.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published theorem announcement in 1963 AMS / Crossref
  3. 1963

    Topological index computes analytic index

    first application

    M. Atiyah and I. Singer, The index of elliptic operators on compact manifolds, Bull. AMS 69 (1963)

    The index formula is applied to elliptic operators on compact manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published theorem announcement in 1963 AMS / Crossref
  4. 1965

    Palais seminar volume on the index theorem

    consolidation

    Palais (ed.), Seminar on the Atiyah-Singer Index Theorem, Annals of Mathematics Studies 57, 1965

    A collected seminar volume that gives the first systematic exposition of the Atiyah-Singer theorem, with contributions by Atiyah, Borel, Floyd, Seeley and others.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for the 1965 seminar volume, with Palais as editor and Atiyah among contributors zbMATH Open record
  5. 1968

    Detailed proof in the Annals

    key development

    M. Atiyah and I. Singer, The index of elliptic operators I, Ann. Math. 87 (1968)

    The first paper in the index-operator series gives the detailed K-theoretic proof.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals paper published in 1968 Annals / JSTOR
