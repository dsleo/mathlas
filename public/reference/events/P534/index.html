2015 · Probability

Liouville quantum gravity, the Brownian map and SLE

Two models of random two-dimensional surfaces, one measuring area and one distance, were shown to describe the same object using SLE curves as the link.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1999

    Schramm's LERW/UST scaling-limit work posted

    precursor

    O. Schramm, Scaling limits of loop-erased random walks and uniform spanning trees, arXiv:math/9904022 (1999)

    SLE-related scaling-limit methods precede the later random-surface metric construction.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted April 1999 arXiv
  2. 2015

    QLE metric on Liouville quantum gravity proposed

    first statement

    J. Miller and S. Sheffield, Liouville quantum gravity and the Brownian map I: The QLE(8/3,0) metric, arXiv:1507.00719 (2015)

    The first series paper constructs a metric on a dense subset of the Liouville quantum gravity sphere.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted July 2015 arXiv
  3. 2016

    LQG metric identified with the Brownian map

    first application

    J. Miller and S. Sheffield, Liouville quantum gravity and the Brownian map II: geodesics and continuity of the embedding, arXiv:1605.03563 (2016)

    The QLE metric is extended continuously to the sphere and its metric-measure law is identified with the Brownian map.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted May 2016; abstract states the identification arXiv
  4. 2021

    Liouville quantum gravity and the Brownian map II: geodesics and continuity

    key development

    Miller, J.; Sheffield, S., Liouville quantum gravity and the Brownian map II: geodesics and continuity of the embedding, Ann. Probab. 49 (2021) 2732-2829, DOI 10.1214/21-AOP1506

    Studies geodesics and continuity of the Brownian-map embedding, extending the metric identification.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: Ann. Probab. 49 (2021) 2732-2829 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  5. 2021

    An axiomatic characterization of the Brownian map

    key development

    Miller, J.; Sheffield, S., An axiomatic characterization of the Brownian map, J. Éc. Polytech. Math. 8 (2021) 609-731, DOI 10.5802/jep.155

    Characterizes the Brownian map axiomatically, a structural step that reinforces the identification.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: J. Éc. polytech. Math. 8 (2021) 609-731 zBMATH Open record (search API)
