1939 · Statistics & decision theory

Statistical decision theory

Estimation and testing recast as games.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1939

    Wald's decision-theoretic unification of estimation and testing

    first statement

    Wald, Contributions to the theory of statistical estimation and testing hypotheses, Ann. Math. Statist. 10 (1939) 299-326

    Wald showed that estimation and hypothesis testing are special cases of a general decision problem with loss and risk.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wald, Contributions to the theory of statistical estimation and testing hypotheses, Ann. Math. Statist. 10 (1939) 299-326 Crossref DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177732144
    • Wald's 1939 paper showed hypothesis testing and parameter estimation are special cases of the general decision problem Wikipedia, Decision theory (history section)
  2. 1944

    von Neumann and Morgenstern's Theory of Games and Economic Behavior

    key development

    von Neumann, Morgenstern, Theory of Games and Economic Behavior, Princeton University Press (1944)

    The game-theoretic and expected-utility framework gave decision theory its game setting.

    Evidence (2)
    • von Neumann, Morgenstern, Theory of Games and Economic Behavior (1944) Open Library search record (first publish year 1944)
    • von Neumann and Morgenstern formalised expected utility in the 1940s Wikipedia, Decision theory (history section)
  3. 1945

    Wald's minimax statistical decision functions

    key development

    Wald, Statistical decision functions which minimize the maximum risk, Annals of Mathematics 46 (1945) from p. 265

    Wald's decision functions minimising the maximum risk made minimax the central criterion of statistical decision theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wald, Statistical decision functions which minimize the maximum risk, Ann. of Math. 46 (1945) from p. 265 Crossref DOI 10.2307/1969022
  4. 1950

    Wald's Statistical Decision Functions

    consolidation

    Wald, Statistical Decision Functions, Wiley (1950)

    A monograph setting out decision functions, loss, risk and minimax as one theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    The phrase 'decision theory' is attributed by Wikipedia to E. L. Lehmann in 1950; the Lehmann record was not checked, so no naming milestone is recorded.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wald, Statistical Decision Functions, Wiley (1950) Open Library search record (first publish year 1950)
