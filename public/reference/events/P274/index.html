Vertex operator algebras

Borcherds and FLM axiomatise vertex operators to build the moonshine module.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1986

    Vertex algebra axioms

    first statement

    R. E. Borcherds, Vertex algebras, Kac-Moody algebras, and the Monster, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 83 (1986) 3068-3071

    Axiomatises the vertex operators attached to a lattice, giving the vertex algebra notion.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref); zbMATH lists volume 84, Crossref volume 83 used Crossref
    • Wikipedia: the vertex algebra notion was introduced by Borcherds in 1986 Wikipedia: Vertex operator algebra
  2. 1988

    Vertex operator algebra named in a monograph title

    naming

    Frenkel, Lepowsky, Meurman, Vertex Operator Algebras and the Monster, Academic Press (Pure and Applied Mathematics 134), 1988

    Monograph that introduces the modification of vertex algebra to vertex operator algebra with Virasoro action.

    Evidence (3)
    • Book title, series and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
    • Wikipedia: the VOA notion was introduced as a modification of vertex algebra by FLM in 1988 Wikipedia: Vertex operator algebra
    • Open Library lists Vertex operator algebras and the Monster, first published 1988. Open Library search
  3. 1992

    Borcherds' proof of monstrous moonshine

    first application

    R. E. Borcherds, Monstrous moonshine and monstrous Lie superalgebras, Invent. Math. 109 (1992) 405-444

    Borcherds proves the Conway-Norton moonshine conjecture using vertex algebras and the FLM moonshine module.

    Dating or attribution note

    First purposeful use of the notion on a major target problem as far as found; the paper was not read, and Wikipedia only names moonshine as an application and the module V-natural as the object with monster symmetry. Same paper may be recorded under the moonshine event.

    Evidence (3)
    • Monstrous moonshine and monstrous Lie superalgebras, Invent. Math. 109 (1992) 405-444. Crossref record
    • Same paper, Invent. Math. 109, 405-444 (1992). zbMATH Open, Zbl 0799.17014
    • Names monstrous moonshine as an application and the moonshine module with monster symmetry as the key example. Wikipedia: Vertex operator algebra
  4. 1996

    Modular invariance of characters of VOAs

    key development

    Zhu, Modular invariance of characters of vertex operator algebras, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 9 (1996) 237-302

    Proves modular invariance of characters for rational vertex operator algebras, a central tool afterwards.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, volume, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
    • Title and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
  5. 1997

    Introductory textbook on vertex algebras

    consolidation

    V. Kac, Vertex algebras for beginners, AMS University Lecture Series 10 (1997; second edition 1998)

    Accessible systematic treatment of vertex algebras and their axioms.

    Evidence (2)
    • Book title, series and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
    • Open Library lists Vertex algebras for beginners, first published 1997, editions 1997 and 1998. Open Library search
