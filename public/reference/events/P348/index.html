Hodge theory via o-minimality

Bakker-Klingler-Tsimerman used o-minimal definability for Ax-Schanuel and algebraicity of period maps.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2017

    Ax–Schanuel for Hodge variations

    precursor

    B. Bakker and J. Tsimerman, The Ax-Schanuel conjecture for variations of Hodge structures, arXiv:1712.05088 (2017)

    A related period-map transcendence theorem predates the tame-topology paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted December 2017 arXiv
  2. 2018

    Arithmetic quotients shown o-minimally definable

    first statement

    B. Bakker, B. Klingler and J. Tsimerman, Tame topology of arithmetic quotients and algebraicity of Hodge loci, arXiv:1810.04801 (2018)

    The paper develops the tame-topology framework for arithmetic quotients and period maps.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted October 2018 arXiv
  3. 2018

    Definability applied to Hodge loci

    first application

    B. Bakker, B. Klingler and J. Tsimerman, Tame topology of arithmetic quotients and algebraicity of Hodge loci, arXiv:1810.04801 (2018)

    The authors derive algebraicity statements for Hodge loci using o-minimal definability.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract names the Hodge-locus application arXiv
  4. 2023

    o-minimal GAGA and a conjecture of Griffiths

    key development

    Bakker, B.; Brunebarbe, Y., o-minimal GAGA and a conjecture of Griffiths, Invent. Math. 232 (2023) 163-228, DOI 10.1007/s00222-022-01166-1

    Uses o-minimal definability to prove an algebraicity result for period maps, extending the Hodge-loci programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: Invent. Math. 232 (2023) 163-228 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  5. 2024

    Definability of mixed period maps

    key development

    Bakker, B.; Brunebarbe, Y., Definability of mixed period maps, J. Eur. Math. Soc. 26 (2024) 2191-2209, DOI 10.4171/JEMS/1319

    Extends the definability results to mixed period maps, widening the reach of the tame-topology method.

    Evidence (1)
