Linear programming and the simplex method

An algorithm moving along vertices of a polyhedron solved linear programs efficiently.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1827

    Fourier's method for linear inequalities

    precursor

    Fourier published a method for solving systems of linear inequalities, the origin of the later Fourier-Motzkin elimination.

    Evidence (1)
    • Fourier published a method for linear inequalities in 1827 Wikipedia, Linear programming (History)
  2. 1939

    Kantorovich's pamphlet on organising and planning production

    first statement

    Kantorovich L. V., Mathematical methods of organizing and planning production, Leningrad State University, 1939 (English: Management Science 6, 366-422, 1960)

    Kantorovich published a pamphlet with linear-programming-type models for allocating machine time and planning production.

    Evidence (2)
    • Pamphlet published in July 1939 by Leningrad State University MacTutor, Kantorovich biography
    • English version in Management Science 6(4), 1960, found in Crossref Crossref
  3. 1941

    Hitchcock's transportation problem as a linear program

    precursor

    Hitchcock F. L., The distribution of a product from several sources to numerous localities, J. Math. Phys. 20, 224-230, 1941

    Hitchcock formulated the transportation problem as a linear program and gave a solution close to the later simplex method.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: J. Math. Phys. 20, 224-230, 1941 Crossref
    • Hitchcock formulated transportation problems as linear programs in 1941 Wikipedia, Linear programming (History)
  4. 1947

    Simplex method applied to US Air Force planning and to a least-cost diet

    first application

    Dantzig applied the simplex method to Air Force planning problems and, in 1947, to a least-cost diet calculation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Dantzig's planning work for the US Air Force and the 1947 diet calculation MacTutor, George Dantzig biography
  5. 1948

    Koopmans proposes the term 'linear programming'

    naming

    Tjalling Koopmans proposed the term 'linear programming' during a 1948 visit by Dantzig to RAND.

    Evidence (1)
    • The term 'linear programming' was proposed by Koopmans during a 1948 visit to RAND MacTutor, George Dantzig biography
  6. 1951

    Dantzig's public statement of the simplex method

    first statement

    Dantzig G. B., Maximization of a linear function of variables subject to linear inequalities, in Koopmans T. C. (ed.), Activity Analysis of Production and Allocation, Wiley, 1951

    Dantzig's simplex method first appears in print in the 1951 Cowles volume on activity analysis.

    Evidence (1)
    • The 1951 Cowles volume lists Dantzig's chapter on maximization at pp. 339–347 and a separate simplex-method application chapter. Cowles Foundation, Activity Analysis contents
  7. 1963

    Dantzig, Linear Programming and Extensions

    consolidation

    Dantzig G. B., Linear Programming and Extensions, Princeton University Press, 1963

    A large textbook-monograph that systematised the simplex method and the theory of linear programming.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record: Princeton University Press, 1963 Open Library
    • MacTutor cites the 1963 book as Dantzig's main monograph MacTutor, George Dantzig biography
  8. 1975

    Shared Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences

    recognition

    Kantorovich and Koopmans shared the 1975 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for work on optimal resource allocation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Kantorovich was a joint 1975 Nobel laureate with Koopmans MacTutor, Kantorovich biography
