1952 · Differential equations

Calderon-Zygmund singular integrals in PDE

Singular integral operators from harmonic analysis give L^p estimates and uniqueness results for elliptic and parabolic equations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1952

    On the existence of certain singular integrals

    first statement

    Calderon and Zygmund, On the existence of certain singular integrals, Acta Mathematica 88 (1952)

    Public statement of the singular integral theory on L^p, the harmonic-analysis base for later PDE use.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume and year verified Crossref
    • Calderon and Zygmund's collaboration on singular integrals MacTutor biography of Calderon
  2. 1956

    On singular integrals (general theory)

    key development

    Calderon and Zygmund, On singular integrals, American Journal of Mathematics 78 (1956)

    General theory of singular integral operators, which the PDE application uses.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title, journal, volume and year verified Crossref
  3. 1957

    Singular integral operators and differential equations

    first application

    Calderon and Zygmund, Singular integral operators and differential equations, American Journal of Mathematics 79 (1957)

    Applies singular integral operators to differential equations, the first PDE use named in the title.

    Dating or attribution note

    PDE use is taken from the title; the body text was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title, journal, volume and year verified Crossref
  4. 1970

    Singular Integrals and Differentiability Properties of Functions

    consolidation

    Stein, Singular Integrals and Differentiability Properties of Functions, Princeton University Press, 1970

    Book-length treatment of singular integrals and their differentiability applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book dated 1970 (Princeton University Press) Open Library record
