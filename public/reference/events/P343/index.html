    Crystal bases at q = 0

    first statement

    Crystalizing the q-analogue of universal enveloping algebras (1990)

    The 1990 paper introduces crystal bases for quantum-group representations.

    The Duke article develops crystal-base theory in detail.

    Evidence (1)

  3. 1993

    Kashiwara's global crystal bases

    key development

    Kashiwara, M., Global crystal bases of quantum groups, Duke Mathematical Journal (1993)

    Constructs global crystal bases for quantum groups, extending the local theory to the whole algebra.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Duke (1993) Crossref
  4. 2002

    Hong-Kang book on quantum groups and crystal bases

    consolidation

    Hong, J., Kang, S.-J., Introduction to Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases, AMS (2002)

    A textbook introducing quantum groups and the crystal-basis theory built around Kashiwara's construction.
