1966 · Probability

Large deviation theory (Donsker-Varadhan framework)

Variational principles for rare events of Markov processes, modelled on free energy computations, become a general theory of large deviations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1938

    Cramér's rare-event theorem

    precursor

    H. Cramér, Sur un nouveau théorème-limite de la théorie des probabilités (1938)

    Earlier probability result, narrower than the later general theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cramer 1938 limit theorem for rare deviations of sums (precursor, insurance context) zbMATH JFM 64.0529.01
  2. 1966

    Varadhan's general asymptotic framework

    first statement

    S. R. S. Varadhan, Asymptotic probabilities and differential equations, CPAM 19 (1966)

    A public paper formulates the general large-deviation approach used here.

    Evidence (1)
    • Varadhan, Asymptotic probabilities and differential equations, CPAM 19 (1966): paper that began a general theory of large deviations Crossref record
  3. 1975

    Donsker–Varadhan apply variational asymptotics to Markov processes

    first application

    M. D. Donsker and S. R. S. Varadhan, Asymptotic evaluation of certain Markov process expectations for large time I (1975)

    The framework is deliberately applied to a Markov-process target.

    Evidence (1)
    • Donsker and Varadhan, Asymptotic evaluation of certain Markov process expectations for large time, I, CPAM 28 (1975) Crossref record
  4. 1984

    Varadhan's large-deviations lecture monograph

    consolidation

    S. R. S. Varadhan, Large Deviations and Applications (1984)

    A named book-length treatment consolidates the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published CBMS-NSF lecture monograph, 1984 zbMATH Open
