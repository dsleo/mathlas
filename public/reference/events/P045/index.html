2002 · Topology

Perelman's proof of the Poincare conjecture

Ricci flow with surgery, controlled by an entropy functional and Alexandrov-space compactness, classifies three-manifolds and gives the Poincare and geometrisation conjectures.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1904

    Poincare conjecture posed

    precursor

    Poincare's question whether a simply connected closed 3-manifold is the 3-sphere.

    Evidence (1)
    • Poincare conjecture first conjectured in 1904 Wikipedia: Poincare conjecture (infobox)
  2. 1982

    Thurston's geometrization conjecture

    precursor

    Thurston, Three-dimensional manifolds, Kleinian groups and hyperbolic geometry, Bull. AMS (1982)

    States that every closed 3-manifold splits into pieces with one of eight geometries; implies the Poincare conjecture.

    Evidence (2)
    • Bull. AMS 1982 announcement of the geometrization framework Crossref / publisher record
    • Thurston proposed the geometrization conjecture in 1982 Wikipedia: Geometrization conjecture
  3. 1982

    Hamilton introduces Ricci flow

    precursor

    Hamilton, Three-manifolds with positive Ricci curvature, J. Differential Geom. (1982)

    Introduces the Ricci flow equation for Riemannian metrics and uses it to prove a 3-manifold theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • J. Differential Geom. 1982 paper on positive Ricci curvature in dimension 3 Crossref / publisher record
    • Ricci flow introduced by Richard Hamilton Wikipedia: Ricci flow
  4. 1982

    Ricci flow applied to 3-manifold topology

    precursor

    Hamilton, Three-manifolds with positive Ricci curvature, J. Differential Geom. (1982)

    The same 1982 paper is the first use of the flow on a topological target (3-manifolds with positive Ricci curvature).

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1982 paper as the first statement Crossref / publisher record
  5. 2002

    Perelman's entropy formula

    first statement

    Perelman, The entropy formula for the Ricci flow and its geometric applications, arXiv math/0211159 (2002)

    Introduces a monotone entropy functional for the Ricci flow and derives geometric consequences; the first of Perelman's three preprints.

    Evidence (2)
    • arXiv posting of the entropy formula preprint, November 2002 arXiv record
    • Perelman posted the entropy paper in November 2002 MacTutor Perelman biography
  6. 2003

    Ricci flow with surgery on three-manifolds

    first application

    Perelman, Ricci flow with surgery on three-manifolds, arXiv math/0303109 (2003)

    Constructs Ricci flow with surgery and completes the programme behind the Poincare and geometrization conjectures.

    Evidence (2)
    • arXiv posting of the surgery preprint, 2003 arXiv record
    • Perelman's 2002 and 2003 papers complete Hamilton's programme Wikipedia: Poincare conjecture
  7. 2007

    Morgan-Tian monograph

    consolidation

    Morgan, Tian, Ricci Flow and the Poincare Conjecture, Clay Mathematics Monographs (AMS, 2007)

    First book-length exposition of the proof of the Poincare conjecture from Perelman's papers.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref/Open Library record of Morgan's 2007 monograph on Ricci flow and the Poincare conjecture Crossref / publisher record
  8. 2008

    Kleiner-Lott lecture notes

    consolidation

    Kleiner, Lott, Notes on Perelman's papers, Geometry & Topology (2008)

    Detailed notes on Perelman's three preprints, the standard reference for checking the argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • Geometry & Topology 2008 notes on Perelman's papers Crossref / publisher record
