1901 · Analysis

Lebesgue measure and integral: set theory enters analysis

Lebesgue defined measure in 1901 and the integral in 1902 using Cantor-style set concepts, showing analysis required set-theoretic methods.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1895

    Borel sets

    precursor

    Borel, E., Sur quelques points de la theorie des fonctions, Annales Scientifiques de l'Ecole Normale Superieure, 1895

    Borel's classes of sets are the precursor on which Lebesgue builds measure.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper appears in Annales de l'ENS in 1895 zbMATH record, Zbl 26.0429.03
  2. 1901

    Lebesgue's measure and generalised integral

    first statement

    Lebesgue, H., Sur une generalisation de l'integrale definie, Comptes Rendus Hebdomadaires des Seances de l'Academie des Sciences, 1901

    Lebesgue states the measure of sets and a generalised integral in a short Comptes Rendus note.

    Evidence (2)
    • Note appears in Comptes Rendus in 1901, pages 1025-1028 zbMATH record, Zbl 32.0299.01
    • MacTutor says Lebesgue formulated the theory of measure in 1901 MacTutor biography of Lebesgue
  3. 1902

    Lebesgue's thesis on the integral

    key development

    Lebesgue, H., Integrale, longueur, aire, Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata, 1902

    The thesis gives the full measure-based theory of the integral, the form now standard.

    Evidence (2)
    • Thesis appears in Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata in 1902 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf02420592
    • MacTutor says the thesis was presented in 1902 and published in Milan in the Annali MacTutor biography of Lebesgue
  4. 1904

    Lebesgue's Lecons sur l'integration

    consolidation

    Lebesgue, H., Lecons sur l'integration et la recherche des fonctions primitives, Gauthier-Villars, 1904

    Lebesgue's lecture book gives the first systematic treatment of measure and integral.

    Dating or attribution note

    Systematic-treatment role is from general knowledge of the book; the text was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lessons on integration and the investigation of primitive functions, 1904 (English title in zbMATH) zbMATH record, Zbl 35.0377.01
