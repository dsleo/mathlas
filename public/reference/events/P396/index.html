1940 · Algebraic geometry

Rigorous foundations of algebraic geometry via commutative algebra

Van der Waerden, Zariski and Weil used valuation and ideal theory to rebuild the Italian school's results rigorously.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1935

    Zariski's Algebraic Surfaces

    precursor

    O. Zariski, Algebraic Surfaces, Ergebnisse der Mathematik, 1935

    Zariski's monograph on surfaces was a classical-language survey of the Italian results that were then rebuilt with commutative algebra.

    Evidence (2)
    • Mathematical Gazette 1935 review of Algebraic Surfaces (Ergebnisse) exists Crossref DOI 10.2307/3605895
    • MacTutor Zariski biography describes the rebuilding of algebraic geometry with commutative algebra MacTutor Zariski biography
  2. 1939

    van der Waerden's Einfuehrung in die algebraische Geometrie

    precursor

    B. L. van der Waerden, Einfuehrung in die algebraische Geometrie, Springer, 1939

    Van der Waerden's textbook gave the first systematic rigorous treatment of algebraic geometry on commutative-algebra foundations.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists the book with first publication 1939 (J. Springer) Open Library search
    • Pedoe's 1939 Mathematical Gazette review exists Crossref DOI 10.2307/3606190
  3. 1940

    Zariski's algebraic varieties over ground fields of characteristic zero

    first statement

    O. Zariski, 'Algebraic Varieties over Ground Fields of Characteristic Zero', Amer. J. Math. 62, 1940

    Zariski defined algebraic varieties over an arbitrary ground field by valuation and ideal theory, which is the rigorous version.

    Evidence (1)
    • Zariski 1940 American Journal of Mathematics paper with this title exists Crossref DOI 10.2307/2371447
  4. 1946

    Weil's Foundations of Algebraic Geometry

    key development

    A. Weil, Foundations of Algebraic Geometry, AMS Colloquium Publications 29, 1946

    Weil built abstract varieties over arbitrary fields with a general foundation, the framework used for later algebraic geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref lists Weil 1946 Colloquium Publications 29 Crossref DOI 10.1090/coll/029
