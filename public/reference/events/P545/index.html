2016 · Mathematical physics

Undecidability in quantum games via combinatorial group theory

Systems of equations encoded as games were used to show that unlimited entanglement can win certain nonlocal games with certainty and that finite-dimensional strategies cannot.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2016

    Solution-group embedding and game undecidability

    first statement

    W. Slofstra, Tsirelson's problem and an embedding theorem for groups arising from non-local games, arXiv:1606.03140 (2016)

    The paper embeds finitely presented groups into solution groups and states undecidability for perfect commuting-operator strategies.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted June 2016 arXiv
  2. 2016

    Group embedding applied to nonlocal games

    first application

    W. Slofstra, Tsirelson's problem and an embedding theorem for groups arising from non-local games, arXiv:1606.03140 (2016)

    The solution-group construction gives nonlocal games separating commuting and tensor-product strategies.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract states this application arXiv
  3. 2017

    Quantum correlation set shown non-closed

    key development

    W. Slofstra, The set of quantum correlations is not closed, arXiv:1703.08618 (2017)

    A linear-system game separates a limit of finite-dimensional strategies from any finite-dimensional perfect strategy.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted March 2017 arXiv
  4. 2020

    MIP*=RE gives stronger undecidability

    key development

    Z. Ji et al., MIP*=RE, arXiv:2001.04383 (2020)

    The result reduces the halting problem to deciding the entangled value of a nonlocal game.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted January 2020 arXiv
  5. 2025

    Undecidability of constant-sized quantum correlation membership

    key development

    Fu, H., Miller, C. A., Slofstra, W., The Membership Problem for Constant-Sized Quantum Correlations is Undecidable, Communications in Mathematical Physics (2025)

    Extends the undecidability results for quantum correlation sets to a constant-sized setting.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, CMP (2025) Crossref
