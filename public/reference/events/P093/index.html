1983 · Number theory

Faltings: Mordell's conjecture via moduli and heights

Finiteness of rational points on curves of genus at least 2 is proved by combining the Tate conjecture for abelian varieties, moduli of abelian varieties and height theory.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1922

    Mordell's conjecture

    precursor

    Mordell, On the rational solutions of the indeterminate equations of the third and fourth degrees, Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. (1922)

    States that a curve of genus at least 2 has only finitely many rational points, the problem Faltings solved.

    Evidence (2)
    • Mordell conjectured finiteness of rational points for curves of genus > 1 in 1922 Wikipedia: Faltings' theorem (raw)
    • Mordell's 1922 work and his later career MacTutor Mordell biography
  2. 1968

    Parshin's function-field curves

    precursor

    Parshin, Algebraic curves over function fields I, Izv. AN SSSR (1968)

    Function-field work on curves that is an analogue of the finiteness problem used in the Mordell-Shafarevich route.

    Evidence (1)
    • Math. USSR Izvestiya 1968 paper (DOI 10.1070/im1968v002n05abeh000723) Crossref / publisher record
  3. 1983

    Finiteness theorems for abelian varieties

    first statement

    Faltings, Endlichkeitssatze fur abelsche Varietaten uber Zahlkorpern, Invent. Math. (1983)

    Proves finiteness of rational points on curves of genus at least 2 via moduli of abelian varieties and heights, establishing Mordell's conjecture.

    Evidence (2)
    • Invent. Math. 1983 paper (DOI 10.1007/bf01388432) Crossref / publisher record
    • Faltings proved the Mordell conjecture in 1983 MacTutor Faltings biography
  4. 1983

    Mordell's conjecture proved

    first application

    Faltings, Endlichkeitssatze fur abelsche Varietaten uber Zahlkorpern, Invent. Math. (1983)

    The same paper applies the moduli and height approach to the target problem (Mordell's conjecture).

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1983 paper Crossref / publisher record
  5. 1991

    Alternative proof via Nevanlinna theory

    key development

    Vojta, Siegel's theorem in the compact case, Ann. of Math. (1991)

    Gives a different route to finiteness results of Siegel and Mordell type, a key development in the subject.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ann. of Math. 1991 paper (DOI 10.2307/2944318) Crossref / publisher record
  6. 2000

    Hindry-Silverman textbook

    consolidation

    Hindry, Silverman, Diophantine Geometry: An Introduction, Graduate Texts in Mathematics (2000)

    Standard textbook covering Faltings' theorem and heights.

    Evidence (1)
    • Springer GTM 201 (DOI 10.1007/978-1-4612-1210-2) Crossref / publisher record
