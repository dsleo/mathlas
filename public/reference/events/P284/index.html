1950 · Applied mathematics

Operations research

Wartime scientific analysis of radar and convoys becomes a quantitative decision discipline.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1937

    Operational studies at Bawdsey Research Station

    precursor

    Operational research began at Bawdsey as an initiative to study how radar was used in practice.

    Evidence (1)
    • Modern operational research originated at Bawdsey Research Station in 1937, on an initiative of A. P. Rowe and Robert Watson-Watt Wikipedia, Operations research (history section)
  2. 1941

    Blackett's Coastal Command convoy analysis

    precursor

    Blackett's team found that convoy losses depended largely on the number of escorts, and changed tactics on that basis.

    Evidence (1)
    • Blackett's Coastal Command team found convoy losses depended on the number of escorts, and the Operational Research Section was set up in 1941 Wikipedia, Operations research (history section)
  3. 1950

    Blackett's article titled 'Operational Research' (first issue of Operational Research Quarterly)

    first statement

    P. M. S. Blackett, Operational Research, Operational Research Quarterly 1 (1950), DOI 10.2307/3006455

    The earliest article found in Crossref with the field's name as its title; it states the idea of the new field in public, after the wartime work.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record 'Operational Research', author Blackett, Operational Research Quarterly (1950-1952), issued March 1950, DOI 10.2307/3006455 Crossref API
  4. 1951

    Morse and Kimball's Methods of Operations Research

    consolidation

    Morse and Kimball, Methods of Operations Research, Wiley (1951)

    A wartime-derived methods book that presents operations research as a general quantitative method.

    Dating or attribution note

    Treated as the first systematic textbook of the method; priority over other 1950s books not checked.

    Evidence (2)
    • Morse and Kimball, Methods of Operations Research, Wiley (1951) Open Library search record (first publish year 1951)
    • Morse led the US wartime operations research and later was first president of ORSA Wikipedia, Operations research (history section)
  5. 1952

    Operations Research Society of America and its journal

    institution

    The US Operations Research Society was founded in 1952, with the journal Operations Research.

    Evidence (1)
    • Operations Research Society of America (ORSA) founded in 1952 Wikipedia, Operations research (history section)
