1866 · Differential equations

Fuchs-Frobenius theory of regular singular points (Frobenius method)

Fuchs and Frobenius used series in the complex domain to solve linear ODEs with regular singular points.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1866

    Fuchs, Zur Theorie der linearen Differentialgleichungen mit veraenderlichen Coefficienten

    first statement

    L. Fuchs, J. reine angew. Math. 66 (1866) 121-160

    Fuchs identifies the class of linear ODEs with regular singular points and the behaviour of solutions near them.

    Evidence (2)

    Frobenius, Ueber die Integration der linearen Differentialgleichungen durch Reihen

    first application

    F. G. Frobenius, J. reine angew. Math. 76 (1873) 214-235

    Systematic use of power series at regular singular points to integrate linear ODEs.
