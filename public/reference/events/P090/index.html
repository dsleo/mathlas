1980 · Algebra

D-modules and the Riemann-Hilbert correspondence

Systems of linear PDE (D-modules) are matched with constructible sheaves, and localisation matches D-modules with representations of Lie algebras.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1972

    Analytic continuation with respect to a parameter

    precursor

    Bernstein, I. N., The analytic continuation of generalized functions with respect to a parameter, Functional Analysis and Its Applications, 1972

    Bernstein applies D-module-style methods to analytic continuation, the tool later called the Bernstein-Sato polynomial method.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Functional Analysis and Its Applications, 1972 (DOI 10.1007/bf01077645) Crossref record
    • D-module theory built up after 1970 from Sato's ideas and the Sato-Bernstein work Wikipedia: D-module
  2. 1980

    Hilbert-Riemann problem for regular holonomic systems

    first statement

    Mebkhout, Z., Sur le problème de Hilbert-Riemann, 1980

    Mebkhout states the Riemann-Hilbert correspondence in the holonomic setting, independently of Kashiwara.

    Evidence (2)
    • Sur le problème de Hilbert-Riemann, 1980 (zbMATH 0444.32003 and 0431.32009) zbMATH record
    • Kashiwara (1980, 1984) and Mebkhout (1980, 1984) independently prove the holonomic Riemann-Hilbert result Wikipedia: Riemann-Hilbert correspondence
  3. 1984

    The Riemann-Hilbert problem for holonomic systems

    key development

    Kashiwara, M., The Riemann-Hilbert problem for holonomic systems, Publ. RIMS 20, 1984

    Kashiwara's paper gives the full correspondence between holonomic D-modules and perverse sheaves, a main theorem for the subject.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Publ. RIMS, Kyoto, 1984 (DOI 10.2977/prims/1195181610) Crossref record
    • Kashiwara 1980 and 1984 papers on regular holonomic D-modules Wikipedia: Riemann-Hilbert correspondence
  4. 2008

    Textbook D-modules, perverse sheaves and representation theory

    consolidation

    Hotta, R., Takeuchi, K., Tanisaki, T., D-Modules, Perverse Sheaves, and Representation Theory, Birkhäuser (Progress in Math. 236)

    The monograph gives the first systematic treatment of coherent and holonomic D-modules and their perverse-sheaf side.

    Evidence (3)
    • Book chapters in Birkhäuser Progress in Mathematics series (DOI 10.1007/978-0-8176-4523-6) Crossref record
    • Book record gives first publication 2004 for an edition, conflicting with the 2008 date Open Library record
    • Crossref record for D-Modules, Perverse Sheaves, and Representation Theory (Progress in Mathematics), issued 2008 Crossref 10.1007/978-0-8176-4523-6
  5. Date unknown

    Hilbert's 21st problem

    precursor

    Hilbert poses the problem of finding a linear differential equation with prescribed monodromy, the question the correspondence answers.

    Evidence (1)
    • Classically, David Hilbert posed his twenty-first problem, referencing Riemann Wikipedia: Riemann-Hilbert correspondence
