1979 · Topology

Operator K-theory and KK-theory

Kasparov links K-homology to elliptic operators.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1973

    BDF announce extensions as K-homology

    precursor

    Brown, Douglas, Fillmore, Extensions of C*-algebras, operators with compact self-commutators, and K-homology, Bull. AMS (1973)

    Announces that extensions of C*-algebras give a homology theory, the K-homology side that KK later unifies with K-theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  2. 1977

    BDF full paper on extensions and K-homology

    precursor

    Brown, Douglas, Fillmore, Extensions of C*-algebras and K-homology, Ann. of Math. 105 (1977)

    Full treatment of the extension-theoretic K-homology that Kasparov's KK generalises.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia KK-theory: KK was influenced by BDF's 1977 classification of extensions Wikipedia: KK-theory
  3. 1979

    Kasparov's short note on the K-functor in extension theory

    first statement

    Kasparov, The K-functor in the theory of extensions of C*-algebras, Funct. Anal. Appl. (1979)

    Short announcement by Kasparov of the K-functor approach to extensions, the earliest candidate for public KK.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked; content not read Crossref
  4. 1980

    Kasparov defines KK (Izvestiya)

    key development

    Kasparov, The operator K-functor and extensions of C*-algebras, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR 44 (1980); English transl. Math. USSR-Izv. 16 (1981)

    Full definition of the bivariant K-functor KK(A,B) and the Kasparov product, which made the theory matter for index theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia KK-theory: introduced by Kasparov in 1980 (Izv. 44) Wikipedia: KK-theory
    • Crossref record is the 1981 English translation of the Izvestiya paper Crossref
  5. 1983

    Cuntz titles a paper with the KK-theory name

    naming

    Cuntz, J., Generalized homomorphisms between C*-algebras and KK-theory, 1983

    Earliest zbMATH title found that contains KK-theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: title with KK-theory, Cuntz, 1983 zbMATH Open doc 3893799
  6. 1986

    Blackadar publishes K-theory for operator algebras

    consolidation

    Blackadar, B., K-theory for operator algebras, 1986

    Monograph on K-theory of C*-algebras, the standard reference that treats the operator K-theory setting.

    Dating or attribution note

    Chosen as the monograph in the operator K-theory area; whether KK-theory is treated in full was not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists the monograph with first publication year 1986 Open Library works OL1940858W
  7. 1987

    Cuntz's quasihomomorphism picture of KK

    key development

    Cuntz, A new look at KK-theory, K-Theory 1 (1987)

    Gives an equivalent definition of KK using homotopy classes of *-homomorphisms, removing the Fredholm module data.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia KK-theory: Cuntz's definition is one of the equivalent forms Wikipedia: KK-theory
  8. 1988

    Equivariant KK and the Novikov conjecture

    first application

    Kasparov, Equivariant KK-theory and the Novikov conjecture, Invent. Math. 91 (1988)

    First major use of KK-theory on a target problem, the Novikov conjecture.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia KK-theory: KK was essential to the Baum-Connes conjecture and noncommutative topology Wikipedia: KK-theory
