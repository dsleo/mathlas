1998 · Algebraic geometry

Donaldson-Thomas theory and wall-crossing

Counting sheaves on Calabi-Yau threefolds gives invariants whose jumps across walls follow universal formulas.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1998

    Holomorphic Casson invariant preprint

    first statement

    R. P. Thomas, A holomorphic Casson invariant for Calabi–Yau 3-folds, and bundles on K3 fibrations, arXiv:math/9806111 (1998)

    Thomas’s public preprint defines a deformation-invariant holomorphic Casson invariant on Calabi–Yau threefolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the preprint to June 1998 and describes the invariant. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2008

    Motivic DT wall-crossing formalism

    key development

    M. Kontsevich and Y. Soibelman, Stability structures, motivic Donaldson–Thomas invariants and cluster transformations, arXiv:0811.2435 (2008)

    The preprint develops motivic DT invariants and wall-crossing formulas.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the public paper to November 2008 and states the wall-crossing results. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2013

    Wall-crossing formula of DT invariants without Chern–Simons functional

    key development

    Kiem, Y.-H.; Li, J., A wall crossing formula of Donaldson-Thomas invariants without Chern-Simons functional, Asian J. Math. 17 (2013) 63-94, DOI 10.4310/AJM.2013.v17.n1.a4

    Proves a wall-crossing formula for DT invariants in a setting that avoids the Chern–Simons functional.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: Asian J. Math. 17 (2013) 63-94 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  4. 2014

    Wall-crossing structures in Donaldson–Thomas invariants, integrable systems and mirror symmetry

    consolidation

    Kontsevich, M.; Soibelman, Y., Wall-crossing structures in Donaldson-Thomas invariants, integrable systems and mirror symmetry, in: Homological Mirror Symmetry and Tropical Geometry, Lect. Notes Unione Mat. Ital. 15 (2014), DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-06514-4_6

    A systematic account of the wall-crossing structure attached to Donaldson–Thomas invariants.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: LNUMI 15 (2014) chapter zBMATH Open record (search API)
