1868 · Geometry

Beltrami and Klein models of non-Euclidean geometry

Beltrami realised hyperbolic geometry on a surface in Euclidean space and Klein completed models using Cayley's distance, reducing consistency of non-Euclidean geometry to Euclidean.

Also known as: Beltrami-Klein model

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1859

    Cayley's projective definition of distance

    precursor

    A. Cayley, A sixth memoir upon quantics, Phil. Trans. 149, 1859

    Cayley defined metric notions inside projective geometry via an absolute conic, the tool Klein would later use.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Phil. Trans. 1859. Crossref
    • Cayley's 1859 cross-ratio definition of angle became the Cayley metric. Wikipedia, Beltrami-Klein model
  2. 1868

    Beltrami's Saggio: hyperbolic geometry on surfaces

    first statement

    E. Beltrami, Saggio di interpretazione della geometria non-euclidea, Giornale di Matematiche 6, 1868

    Beltrami realised Lobachevsky's plane on surfaces of constant negative curvature, linking it to Riemann's geometry.

    Evidence (2)
    • The 1868 essay gives a concrete realisation using the pseudosphere. MacTutor, Beltrami biography
    • Citation: Giornale di Matematiche 6 (1868). Wikipedia, Eugenio Beltrami
  3. 1868

    Beltrami's Teoria fondamentale: higher-dimensional and projective model

    key development

    E. Beltrami, Teoria fondamentale degli spazii di curvatura costante, Annali di Matematica (2) 2, 1868

    The general n-dimensional treatment, in which the model with straight lines inside a ball first appears.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Annali di Matematica 2 (1868). Crossref
    • The model first appeared in two 1868 memoirs of Beltrami. Wikipedia, Beltrami-Klein model
  4. 1868

    Beltrami's 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie non-euclidienne'

    naming

    E. Beltrami, 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie non-euclidienne' (in French; Italian original in Giornale di Matematiche, 1868), zbMATH record 2720244

    Beltrami's 1868 paper on an interpretation of non-Euclidean geometry, with 'non-Euclidean geometry' in the title, is the earliest title found that uses the field's name.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 2720244, year 1868, original title 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie non-euclidienne'; the English title field and source note are muddled, citing Giornale di Matematiche and Hoffmann Z. 130-132 (1868) zbMATH Open API, document 2720244
  5. 1869

    Houel translates Beltrami and notes independence of the parallel postulate

    first application

    J. Houel, French translation of Beltrami's Essai (Italian Saggio), Annales scientifiques de l'Ecole normale (1) 6, 251-288, 1869

    Houel saw that Beltrami's interpretation shows the parallel postulate cannot be derived from the others.

    Evidence (2)
    • Houel translated both works in 1870 and noted the independence. MacTutor, Beltrami biography
    • Crossref: Houel's translation 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie noneuclidienne' in Ann. Sci. ENS 6 (1869), pp. 251-288. Crossref 10.24033/asens.60
  6. 1871

    Klein's Cayley-metric models of non-Euclidean geometry

    key development

    F. Klein, Ueber die sogenannte Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie, Math. Ann. 4, 1871

    Klein used Cayley's projective distance to build models of hyperbolic and elliptic geometry inside projective geometry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Math. Ann. 4 (1871). Crossref
    • Klein realised Cayley's ideas give a projective model of the non-Euclidean plane. Wikipedia, Beltrami-Klein model
