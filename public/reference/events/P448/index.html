1868 · Geometry

Beltrami and Klein models of non-Euclidean geometry

Beltrami realised hyperbolic geometry on a surface in Euclidean space and Klein completed models using Cayley's distance, reducing consistency of non-Euclidean geometry to Euclidean.

Also known as: Beltrami-Klein model

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1859

    Cayley's projective definition of distance

    precursor

    A. Cayley, A sixth memoir upon quantics, Phil. Trans. 149, 1859

    Cayley defined metric notions inside projective geometry via an absolute conic, the tool Klein would later use.

    Evidence (4)
    • Crossref: Phil. Trans. 1859. Crossref
    • Cayley's 1859 cross-ratio definition of angle became the Cayley metric. Wikipedia, Beltrami-Klein model
    • Read: the introduction (notion of distance upon descriptive principles) and 'On the Theory of Distance, Nos. 209 to 229' with the Absolute. Cayley, A sixth memoir upon quantics, Collected Mathematical Papers vol. 2, no. 158 (Internet Archive collectedmathema02cayl)
    • Read: Klein's footnote cites 'Sixth Memoir upon Quantics, Phil. Transactions t. 149, 1859' for the projective measure. Klein, Ueber die sogenannte Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie, Math. Ann. 4 (1871) 573-625 (Internet Archive sim_mathematische-annalen_1871_4)
  2. 1865

    Beltrami: surfaces whose geodesics are straight lines

    precursor

    E. Beltrami, Risoluzione del problema: riportare i punti di una superficie sopra un piano in modo che le linee geodetiche vengano rappresentate da linee rette, Annali di Matematica (1) 7 (1865) 185-204

    Beltrami finds that a surface can be mapped onto the plane with geodesics going to straight lines only if its curvature is constant, the property he later extends to spaces of constant curvature.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata, series 1, vol. 7, pp. 185-204 (1865). Crossref 10.1007/bf03198517
    • Read: Beltrami's opening of the Teoria fondamentale refers to this memoir (vol. VII, 1865, p. 185; Opere vol. I, p. 262). Beltrami, Opere matematiche vol. 1, no. 25 (Internet Archive operematematiche01beltuoft)
  3. 1868

    Beltrami's Saggio: hyperbolic geometry on surfaces

    first statement

    E. Beltrami, Saggio di interpretazione della geometria non-euclidea, Giornale di Matematiche 6, 1868

    Beltrami realised Lobachevsky's plane on surfaces of constant negative curvature and also represented it inside a limit circle whose chords are the geodesics, linking it to Riemann's geometry.

    Evidence (4)
    • The 1868 essay gives a concrete realisation using the pseudosphere. MacTutor, Beltrami biography
    • Citation: Giornale di Matematiche 6 (1868). Wikipedia, Eugenio Beltrami
    • The essay maps the region onto the inside of a limit circle in which geodesics correspond to chords. Numdam, Beltrami-Houel translation (1869)
    • Read: contents list gives the Saggio in Giornale di Matematiche vol. VI (1868) pp. 284-312 and the Teoria fondamentale in Annali di Matematica (2) 2 (1868-69) pp. 232-255. Beltrami, Opere matematiche vol. 1 (Internet Archive operematematiche01beltuoft)
  4. 1868

    Beltrami's Teoria fondamentale: higher-dimensional and projective model

    key development

    E. Beltrami, Teoria fondamentale degli spazii di curvatura costante, Annali di Matematica (2) 2, 1868

    The general n-dimensional treatment, in which the model with straight lines inside a ball first appears.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref: Annali di Matematica 2 (1868). Crossref
    • The model first appeared in two 1868 memoirs of Beltrami. Wikipedia, Beltrami-Klein model
    • Read: introduction, the linear representation of geodesics for constant curvature, first-order surfaces, and the negative-curvature case. Beltrami, Teoria fondamentale degli spazii di curvatura costante, Opere matematiche vol. 1, no. 25 (Internet Archive operematematiche01beltuoft)
  5. 1868

    Beltrami's Saggio di interpretazione della geometria non-euclidea

    naming

    E. Beltrami, Saggio di interpretazione della geometria non-euclidea, Giornale di Matematiche 6 (1868); French title 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie non-euclidienne'

    The 1868 essay carries 'non-Euclidean geometry' in its title; it is the earliest such title found, not the result of a systematic search.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record 2720244, year 1868, original title 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie non-euclidienne'; the English title field and source note are muddled, citing Giornale di Matematiche and Hoffmann Z. 130-132 (1868) zbMATH Open API, document 2720244
    • Translation headed 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie non euclidienne ... Extrait du Giornale di Matematiche, t. VI, 1868'. Numdam ASENS_1869_1_6__251_0
  6. 1869

    Houel's French translation of Beltrami's Essai

    key development

    J. Houel, French translation of Beltrami's Essai (Italian Saggio), Annales scientifiques de l'Ecole normale (1) 6, 251-288, 1869

    Houel's translation, with Beltrami's text unchanged, made the interpretation known in France.

    Evidence (3)
    • Houel translated both works in 1870 and noted the independence. MacTutor, Beltrami biography
    • Crossref: Houel's translation 'Essai d'interpretation de la geometrie noneuclidienne' in Ann. Sci. ENS 6 (1869), pp. 251-288. Crossref 10.24033/asens.60
    • Scan of the translation, headed 'Extrait du Giornale di Matematiche, t. VI, 1868', Annales scientifiques de l'E.N.S. 1re serie, t. 6 (1869), pp. 251-288. Numdam ASENS_1869_1_6__251_0
  7. 1871

    Klein's Cayley-metric models of non-Euclidean geometry

    key development

    F. Klein, Ueber die sogenannte Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie, Math. Ann. 4, 1871

    Klein used Cayley's projective distance to build models of hyperbolic and elliptic geometry inside projective geometry.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref: Math. Ann. 4 (1871). Crossref
    • Klein realised Cayley's ideas give a projective model of the non-Euclidean plane. Wikipedia, Beltrami-Klein model
    • Read: Klein's introduction, footnotes citing Cayley (1859) and Beltrami (Giornale di Matematiche 1868; Annali (2) 2, 1868/69) and the remark on what Beltrami had already shown. Klein, Ueber die sogenannte Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie, Math. Ann. 4 (1871) 573-625 (Internet Archive sim_mathematische-annalen_1871_4)
