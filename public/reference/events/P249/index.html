1949 · Information & cryptography

Shannon: communication theory of secrecy

Information-theoretic concepts were used to define and bound secrecy of ciphers.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1945

    Shannon's Bell Labs cryptography memorandum

    precursor

    Shannon, C. E., A Mathematical Theory of Cryptography, Bell Labs memorandum (1945), unpublished at the time

    A private Bell Labs memorandum that anticipated the 1949 public paper; it is a precursor, not the public start.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia reference: 'A Mathematical Theory of Cryptography', 1945, Bell Laboratories (IACR museum copy) Wikipedia: Communication theory of secrecy systems
  2. 1948

    Shannon's mathematical theory of communication

    precursor

    Shannon, C. E., A mathematical theory of communication, Bell Syst. Tech. J. 27 (1948) 379-423, 623-656, zbMATH 1154.94303

    Creates the information-theoretic framework on which the 1949 secrecy analysis rests.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'A mathematical theory of communication', BSTJ 27 (1948) zbMATH API (Zbl 1154.94303)
  3. 1949

    Shannon's communication theory of secrecy systems

    first statement

    Shannon, C. E., Communication theory of secrecy systems, Bell Syst. Tech. J. 28(4) (1949) 656-715, doi 10.1002/j.1538-7305.1949.tb00928.x

    The first public treatment of cryptography in information-theoretic terms, defining and bounding secrecy.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'Communication Theory of Secrecy Systems', BSTJ, 1949 Crossref API
    • MacTutor: Communication theory of secrecy systems, published 1949 MacTutor Biography: Claude Shannon
  4. 1976

    Diffie and Hellman's new directions

    key development

    Diffie, W., Hellman, M. E., New directions in cryptography, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 22 (1976) 644-654, zbMATH 0435.94018

    Introduces public-key cryptography, moving security from information-theoretic bounds towards computational hardness.

    Dating or attribution note

    Public-key significance is from general knowledge of the paper's title; the text was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'New directions in cryptography', IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 22 (1976) zbMATH API (Zbl 0435.94018)
  5. 1997

    Handbook of Applied Cryptography

    consolidation

    Menezes, A. J., van Oorschot, P. C., Vanstone, S. A., Handbook of Applied Cryptography, CRC Press (1997), zbMATH 0868.94001

    A reference handbook covering the mathematics of cryptography as applied.

    Dating or attribution note

    This is a handbook, not the first systematic textbook; the first textbook (Stinson 1995, not verified) is not used.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'Handbook of applied cryptography', CRC Press zbMATH API (Zbl 0868.94001)
