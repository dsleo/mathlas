1965 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Pseudodifferential operators

Symbol calculus extended Calderon-Zygmund theory to PDE, then to propagation of singularities.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1952

    Calderon-Zygmund singular integral operators

    precursor

    Calderon, A. P.; Zygmund, A., On the existence of certain singular integrals, Acta Mathematica 88 (1952)

    Singular integral theory on which the later symbol calculus was built; a precursor, not a pseudodifferential calculus.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper published in Acta Mathematica 1952 (DOI 10.1007/bf02392130), zbMATH 0047.10201, authors Calderon and Zygmund Crossref; zbMATH
    • Calderon-Zygmund singular integral theory is the root of the calculus and its later extension MacTutor, Calderon biography
  2. 1965

    Kohn-Nirenberg algebra of pseudo-differential operators

    first statement

    Kohn, J. J.; Nirenberg, L., An algebra of pseudo-differential operators, Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics 18 (1965)

    Introduces the operator algebra with symbols in the form that became the standard pseudodifferential calculus.

    Evidence (3)
    • Title and 1965 year of the Kohn-Nirenberg paper (DOI 10.1002/cpa.3160180121) Crossref
    • Wikipedia dates the start of the field to the mid 1960s work of Kohn, Nirenberg, Hormander, Unterberger and Bokobza Wikipedia, Pseudo-differential operator (History)
    • zbMATH: Unterberger-Bokobza, 'Les operateurs pseudo-differentiels d'ordre variable', C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 261 (1965) 2271-2273; the 1964 note 3231475 has no such term in its title zbMATH Open 3231478, 3231475
  3. 1965

    Term 'pseudo-differential operators' in a title

    naming

    Kohn, J. J.; Nirenberg, L., An algebra of pseudo-differential operators (1965)

    The field's name appears in the title of the 1965 Kohn-Nirenberg paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title of the 1965 paper uses the term Crossref
  4. 1967

    Pseudo-differential operators applied to hypoelliptic equations

    first application

    Hormander, L., Pseudo-differential operators and hypoelliptic equations, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 10 (1967)

    Uses the symbol calculus on a target problem, the hypoellipticity of differential equations.

    Dating or attribution note

    Earliest verified application on a target problem in the records consulted; the applications inside the 1965 Kohn-Nirenberg paper were not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hormander 1967 paper in Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 10 (DOI 10.1090/pspum/010/0383152) Crossref; zbMATH 0167.09603
  5. 1972

    Fourier integral operators II (propagation of singularities)

    key development

    Duistermaat, J. J.; Hormander, L., Fourier integral operators II, Acta Mathematica 128 (1972) 183-269

    Extends the calculus to Fourier integral operators, the setting in which singularities propagate along bicharacteristics.

    Dating or attribution note

    The input summary names propagation of singularities as the outcome; this paper is the standard record of that step, but the wording of the result was not checked against the paper itself.

    Evidence (1)
    • Duistermaat-Hormander, Fourier integral operators II, Acta Math. 1972, pages 183-269 zbMATH 0232.47055
  6. 1981

    Taylor's monograph Pseudodifferential Operators

    consolidation

    Taylor, M. E., Pseudodifferential Operators, Princeton University Press (1981)

    A textbook treatment that systematised the calculus for a wider readership.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book title and 1981 year (Princeton, Mathematical Notes series PMS-34, DOI 10.1515/9781400886104) Crossref
