1979 · Group & representation theory

Canonical bases and character sheaves: geometry in representation theory

Intersection cohomology and perverse sheaves give the Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials and canonical bases of quantum groups.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1979

    Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials and cells

    first statement

    Kazhdan, Lusztig, Representations of Coxeter groups and Hecke algebras, Inventiones Mathematicae 53 (1979)

    Kazhdan and Lusztig introduce integral polynomials attached to Coxeter groups, the basis of the later geometric theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • KL polynomials studied in spring 1978 and introduced in the 1979 paper Wikipedia: Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomial
    • Inventiones 1979 paper, authors and year Crossref
  2. 1980

    Intersection cohomology interpretation of KL polynomials

    first application

    Kazhdan, Lusztig, Schubert varieties and Poincaré duality, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 36 (1980)

    The KL polynomials are shown to compute local intersection cohomology of Schubert varieties, the geometric application.

    Evidence (2)
    • KL polynomials relate to intersection cohomology; the 1980 geometric paper MacTutor: David Kazhdan
    • PSPM 1980 paper, authors and year Crossref
  3. 1982

    Perverse sheaves named

    naming

    Beilinson, Bernstein, Deligne, Gabber, Faisceaux pervers, Astérisque 100 (1982)

    The notion of perverse sheaves is introduced and named in Faisceaux pervers, the institutional name for the geometric framework.

    Evidence (2)
    • Perverse sheaves introduced in BBD and Gabber (1982) Wikipedia: Perverse sheaf
    • Book record of Faisceaux pervers (2018 reprint of the Astérisque volume) Open Library
  4. 1985

    Character sheaves I

    key development

    Lusztig, Character sheaves I, Advances in Mathematics 56 (1985)

    Lusztig begins the character-sheaf theory, the geometric analogue of characters for reductive groups, in the first of the series.

    Evidence (2)
    • Advances 1985 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Lusztig's role in the KL conjecture and representation theory MacTutor: David Kazhdan
  5. 1990

    Canonical bases for quantized enveloping algebras

    key development

    Lusztig, Canonical bases arising from quantized enveloping algebras, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 3 (1990)

    Lusztig constructs canonical bases of quantized enveloping algebras using perverse sheaves, a key result of the programme.

    Evidence (2)
    • JAMS 1990 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Canonical bases are part of the input summary's claim (perverse sheaves give canonical bases) Input summary; consistent with MacTutor: Kazhdan
