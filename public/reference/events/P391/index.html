1927 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Representations of compact and continuous groups (Schur, Weyl, Peter-Weyl)

Schur and Weyl used invariant integration to extend finite-group representation theory to compact Lie groups.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1925

    Weyl's representation theory of semisimple Lie groups

    precursor

    Weyl, H., Theorie der Darstellung kontinuierlicher halb-einfacher Gruppen durch lineare Transformationen I, Mathematische Zeitschrift, 1925

    Weyl applies representation-theoretic methods from finite groups to continuous semisimple groups, the first purposeful transfer of the method.

    Dating or attribution note

    The input summary's claim about invariant integration in Weyl's and Schur's work is not verified against the paper text. Schur's role is not counted here because no Schur record for compact groups was verified. Reclassified from first_application to precursor: it predates this event's first statement (1927) and belongs to the wider field.

    Evidence (2)
    • Part I appears in Mathematische Zeitschrift in 1925 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf01506234
    • MacTutor says Weyl's 1925-26 papers developed group representations from general relativity MacTutor biography of Weyl
  2. 1927

    Peter-Weyl completeness of primitive representations

    first statement

    Peter, F. and Weyl, H., Die Vollstaendigkeit der primitiven Darstellungen einer geschlossenen kontinuierlichen Gruppe, Mathematische Annalen, 1927

    Peter and Weyl prove the completeness of the matrix coefficients of irreducible representations of a compact group, the Peter-Weyl theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper Die Vollstaendigkeit der primitiven Darstellungen appears in Mathematische Annalen in 1927 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf01447892
  3. 1930

    Cartan on linear representations of closed groups

    key development

    Elie Cartan, Sur les representations lineaires des groupes clos, Comment. Math. Helv. 2 (1930)

    Extends the representation theory of compact Lie groups in the style of Weyl.

    Dating or attribution note

    Contribution inferred from the title; the paper was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, Comment. Math. Helv. 1930. Crossref
  4. 1933

    Haar's invariant measure

    key development

    Alfred Haar, Der Massbegriff in der Theorie der kontinuierlichen Gruppen, Ann. of Math. 34 (1933)

    Gives invariant integration on general locally compact groups, so Peter-Weyl-type results extend to all compact groups.

    Dating or attribution note

    The extension role comes from the Wikipedia statement of the theorem; the historical chain was not checked.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, Ann. Math. 1933. Crossref
    • Peter-Weyl theorem stated for compact groups using the Haar measure. English Wikipedia, Peter-Weyl theorem
  5. 1939

    Weyl's The Classical Groups

    consolidation

    Weyl, H., The Classical Groups, Princeton University Press, 1939

    Weyl's book systematically treats representations of classical compact groups, consolidating the theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • The Classical Groups published 1939 Open Library search record
    • MacTutor lists The classical groups (1939) among Weyl's major texts MacTutor biography of Weyl
