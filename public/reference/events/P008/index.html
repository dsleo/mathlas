1868 · Differential geometry

Riemannian geometry and manifolds

Intrinsic geometry of surfaces is generalised to n-dimensional spaces with a metric given by a quadratic form, with the question of physical space in view.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1828

    Gauss's general investigations of curved surfaces

    precursor

    C. F. Gauss, Disquisitiones generales circa superficies curvas, 1828

    Gauss developed intrinsic curvature of surfaces, the starting point for Riemann's generalisation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Contemporary 1828 account of the Gauss paper with this title. Crossref DOI 10.1080/14786442808674658 (Philosophical Magazine 3, 1828)
    • Wikipedia calls Riemannian geometry a broad generalisation of the differential geometry of surfaces in R^3. English Wikipedia, Riemannian geometry
  2. 1854

    Riemann's habilitation lecture

    precursor

    B. Riemann, Ueber die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen, lecture of 10 June 1854

    Riemann introduced n-dimensional manifolds with a metric from a quadratic form in an oral lecture chosen by Gauss.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor: delivered 10 June 1854 after Gauss chose it among three lectures. MacTutor, Riemann biography
    • Wikipedia says Riemannian manifolds were first conceptualised by Riemann in 1854. English Wikipedia, Riemannian manifold (saved lead)
  3. 1868

    Riemann's lecture is printed

    first statement

    B. Riemann, Ueber die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen, Abhandlungen der Koniglichen Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Gottingen 13, printed 1868

    The lecture, written in 1854, reached print only in 1868, two years after Riemann's death.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor image caption: written in 1854 but not published until 1868. MacTutor, Riemann biography
    • Gottingen Abhandlungen volume 13 record (dated 1866-67 by the library item). Internet Archive item
  4. 1869

    Christoffel's transformation of quadratic differential forms

    key development

    E. B. Christoffel, Ueber die Transformation der homogenen Differentialausdruecke zweiten Grades, J. reine angew. Math. 70 (1869) 46-70

    Christoffel supplied the invariant machinery for quadratic differential expressions that underlies covariant differentiation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Crelle's Journal 1869, pages 46-70. Crossref DOI 10.1515/crll.1869.70.46
  5. 1900

    Ricci and Levi-Civita's absolute differential calculus

    consolidation

    G. Ricci and T. Levi-Civita, Methodes de calcul differentiel absolu et leurs applications, Math. Ann. 54 (1900) 125-201

    The paper organised tensor calculus on Riemannian spaces into a systematic tool.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Mathematische Annalen 54, 1900, pages 125-201. Crossref DOI 10.1007/bf01454201
  6. 1916

    General relativity formulated in Riemannian terms

    key development

    A. Einstein, Die Grundlage der allgemeinen Relativitaetstheorie, Ann. Phys. 354 (1916) 769-822

    Einstein used the geometry of a four-dimensional metric manifold to formulate gravity.

    Dating or attribution note

    Earlier Einstein-Grossmann work (1913) not checked and so not listed.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Annalen der Physik 354, 1916, pages 769-822. Crossref DOI 10.1002/andp.19163540702
    • Wikipedia: Riemannian geometry enabled the formulation of Einstein's general relativity. English Wikipedia, Riemannian geometry
