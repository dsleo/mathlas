1815 · Functional equations

Babbage develops a calculus of functional equations

Babbage investigated how to recover unknown functions from equations relating their values, giving functional equations a systematic treatment.

  1. 1815

    Babbage develops a calculus of functional equations

    first statement

    C. Babbage, An essay towards the calculus of functions, Philosophical Transactions (1815)

    Babbage studies unknown functions defined by equations relating function values.

    Second part extends the calculus of functions

    key development

    C. Babbage, An essay towards the calculus of functions, Part II, Philosophical Transactions (1816)

    The continuation develops the treatment of functional equations with more than one variable.
