1979 · Algebra

Monstrous moonshine

Dimensions of representations of the Monster group match coefficients of the modular function j, and a proof requires a new algebraic structure from string theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1978

    McKay's numerical observation

    precursor

    John McKay observes that coefficients of the j-function match dimensions of Monster representations; this is a private numerical observation, not a publication.

    Evidence (1)
    • McKay's first numerical observation in 1978 Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine
  2. 1979

    Conway-Norton conjecture: 'Monstrous Moonshine'

    first statement

    Conway, Norton, Monstrous Moonshine, Bulletin London Math. Soc. 11 (1979)

    Conway and Norton state the conjecture linking Monster characters with McKay-Thompson series of modular functions.

    Evidence (3)
    • Bulletin LMS 1979 paper, authors and year Crossref
    • Phrase coined by Conway and Norton in 1979 Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine
    • Title and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
  3. 1979

    Coinage of 'moonshine'

    naming

    Conway, Norton, Monstrous Moonshine, Bulletin London Math. Soc. 11 (1979)

    The term 'monstrous moonshine' appears in the title of the 1979 Bulletin paper, which names the field.

    Evidence (2)
    • Phrase coined by Conway and Norton in 1979 Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine
    • Title 'Monstrous Moonshine' in the Bulletin paper Crossref
  4. 1984

    Graded Monster module with J as character

    first application

    Frenkel, Lepowsky, Meurman, A natural representation of the Fischer-Griess Monster with the modular function J as character, PNAS 81 (1984) 3256-3260

    Constructs a graded representation of the Monster whose graded dimension is the j-function, the first explicit test of the correspondence.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref); title read from record Crossref
    • Wikipedia notes the moonshine module construction by FLM Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine
  5. 1988

    Moonshine module built as a vertex operator algebra

    first application

    Frenkel, Lepowsky, Meurman, Vertex Operator Algebras and the Monster, Academic Press (1988)

    The vertex operator algebra construction builds the moonshine module with a Monster action, a purposeful application of the new tool.

    Evidence (2)
    • Book title, authors, year 1988 Open Library
    • Preface of the book (Pure and Applied Mathematics vol. 134) dated 1988 Crossref
  6. 1988

    Monograph treating the moonshine module

    consolidation

    Frenkel, Lepowsky, Meurman, Vertex Operator Algebras and the Monster, Academic Press, 1988

    Systematic treatment of the moonshine module and the Monster symmetry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book title and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
  7. 1992

    Borcherds proves the Conway-Norton conjecture

    key development

    Borcherds, Monstrous moonshine and monstrous Lie superalgebras, Inventiones Mathematicae 109 (1992)

    Borcherds proves the moonshine conjectures for the moonshine module using vertex algebras and the no-ghost theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Borcherds proved the Conway-Norton conjectures in 1992 Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine
    • Inventiones paper, author and year Crossref
  8. 1992

    Borcherds proves monstrous moonshine

    key development

    Borcherds, Monstrous moonshine and monstrous Lie superalgebras, Invent. Math. 109 (1992) 405-444

    Proof of the Conway-Norton conjecture using the Monster vertex algebra and generalised Kac-Moody algebras.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
    • Wikipedia: Borcherds proved the Conway-Norton conjecture for the moonshine module in 1992 Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine
  9. 1998

    Fields Medal to Borcherds

    recognition

    Fields Medal citing the solution of the Conway-Norton conjecture among other work.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: Borcherds won the Fields Medal in 1998, partly for the moonshine solution Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine
