Ramsey theory

Ramsey proved a combinatorial theorem on homogeneous subsets while studying a decision problem in logic, and van der Waerden proved a related Ramsey-type theorem on arithmetic progressions.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1927

    van der Waerden proves an arithmetic-progression colouring theorem

    precursor

    B. L. van der Waerden, Beweis einer Baudetschen Vermutung, Nieuw Archief voor Wiskunde (2) 15 (1927) 212-216

    A colouring theorem on arithmetic progressions, of Ramsey type though not Ramsey's own result, was published in 1927.

    Evidence (2)
    • Publication record: Nieuw Archief (2) 15, 212-216 (1927) zbMATH Open
    • Proof of Baudet's conjecture, a Ramsey-type partition result Wikipedia: Van der Waerden's theorem
  2. 1930

    Ramsey's partition theorem published

    first statement

    F. P. Ramsey, On a problem of formal logic, Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 30 (1930).

    Ramsey published the combinatorial theorem on homogeneous subsets while treating a decision problem in logic.

    Evidence (2)
    • DOI record: Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 30 (1930), 264-286 Crossref
    • Read to the LMS on 13 Dec 1928; published 1930 MacTutor: Frank Ramsey
  3. 1930

    Ramsey's combinatorial theorem applied to a decision problem in logic

    first application

    F. P. Ramsey, On a problem of formal logic, Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 30 (1930).

    Ramsey used the combinatorial theorem to attack a decision problem for a class of logical formulas, which is the purpose of the paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title and purpose: a problem of formal logic Crossref record and MacTutor: Frank Ramsey
  4. 1952

    Erdős and Rado partition theorems

    key development

    P. Erdős, R. Rado, Combinatorial theorems on classifications of subsets of a given set, Proc. London Math. Soc. (3) 2 (1952)

    Extended the partition-type Ramsey theorems to general classifications of subsets.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publication record: Proc. London Math. Soc. (3) 2 (1952) Crossref
  5. 1971

    Graham–Rothschild theorem for n-parameter sets

    key development

    R. L. Graham, B. L. Rothschild, Ramsey's theorem for n-parameter sets, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 159 (1971)

    Generalised Ramsey's partition theorem to colourings of structured sets (n-parameter sets).

    Evidence (2)
    • Publication record: Trans. AMS 159 (1971) Crossref
    • Named as a Ramsey-theory generalisation in the classic reference list Wikipedia: Van der Waerden's theorem / Ramsey theory references
  6. 1980

    Graham, Rothschild and Spencer: Ramsey Theory (monograph)

    consolidation

    R. L. Graham, B. L. Rothschild, J. H. Spencer, Ramsey Theory, Wiley (1980)

    First book-length systematic treatment of Ramsey theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record: "Ramsey theory" (1980) Open Library
    • Classic reference for Ramsey theory (Graham, Rothschild, Spencer) Wikipedia: Ramsey theory
