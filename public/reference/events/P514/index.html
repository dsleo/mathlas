1982 · Algebraic geometry

Teichmuller dynamics: homogeneous dynamics on moduli spaces

Ratner-style measure rigidity is adapted to the SL(2,R) action on moduli of abelian differentials, proving the magic wand theorem.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1982

    Ergodicity of the Teichmuller geodesic flow

    first statement

    Masur, Interval exchange transformations and measured foliations, Ann. of Math. (1982)

    Relates interval exchanges and the Teichmuller flow and shows the flow's ergodic behaviour.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ann. of Math. 1982 paper (DOI 10.2307/1971341) Crossref / publisher record
  2. 1982

    Gauss measure for interval exchanges

    first statement

    Veech, Gauss measures for transformations on the space of interval exchange maps, Ann. of Math. (1982)

    Independently constructs an invariant measure for the Teichmuller flow on interval exchange space.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ann. of Math. 1982 paper (DOI 10.2307/1971391) Crossref / publisher record
  3. 1986

    Veech's geodesic flow paper

    key development

    Veech, The Teichmuller geodesic flow, Ann. of Math. (1986)

    Develops the geodesic flow on the moduli space of flat surfaces, a basis of later Teichmuller dynamics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ann. of Math. 1986 paper (DOI 10.2307/2007091) Crossref / publisher record
  4. 1991

    Ratner measure rigidity

    precursor

    Ratner, On Raghunathan's measure conjecture, Ann. of Math. (1991)

    Measure rigidity for unipotent actions on homogeneous spaces, the source technique later adapted to moduli spaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • Ann. of Math. 1991 paper (DOI 10.2307/2944357) Crossref / publisher record
    • Ratner's measure rigidity as the source of the orbit-closure approach MacTutor Ratner biography
  5. 2001

    Asymptotic formulas on flat surfaces

    first application

    Eskin, Masur, Asymptotic formulas on flat surfaces, Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems (2001)

    Uses the Teichmuller flow to count closed geodesics and saddle connections on flat surfaces, a first purposeful use for counting.

    Dating or attribution note

    Judgement that this is the first counting application; not verified against earlier work.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 2001 paper (DOI 10.1017/s0143385701001225) Crossref / publisher record
  6. 2003

    Billiards in rectangles with barriers

    key development

    Eskin, Masur, Schmoll, Billiards in rectangles with barriers, Duke Math. J. (2003)

    Applies Teichmuller and translation-surface dynamics to billiards in rectangles with barriers.

    Dating or attribution note

    The magic wand attribution is not verified in the paper's abstract in this session; the milestone is kept on the record only.

    Evidence (1)
    • Duke Math. J. 2003 paper (DOI 10.1215/s0012-7094-03-11832-3) Crossref / publisher record
  7. 2012

    Forni titles a paper with the Teichmuller dynamics name

    naming

    Forni, G., On the Brin Prize work of Artur Avila in Teichmuller dynamics and interval-exchange transformations, 2012

    Earliest zbMATH title found with Teichmuller dynamics in the name.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record with Teichmuller dynamics in the title, Forni, 2012 zbMATH Open doc 6094022
  8. 2015

    Orbit closures are affine invariant submanifolds

    key development

    Eskin, Mirzakhani, Mohammadi, Isolation, equidistribution, and orbit closures for the SL(2,R) action on moduli space, Ann. of Math. (2015)

    Classifies orbit closures and proves equidistribution for the SL(2,R) action on moduli space, using Ratner-style rigidity.

    Evidence (3)
    • Ann. of Math. 2015 paper (DOI 10.4007/annals.2015.182.2.7) Crossref / publisher record
    • arXiv preprint 1305.3015 (2013) with the same title arXiv record
    • Mirzakhani and Eskin collaboration with Mohammadi on moduli space dynamics MacTutor Mirzakhani biography
