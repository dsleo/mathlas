1591 · Algebra

Viete: symbolic algebra (the analytic art)

Letters were used for both unknown and known quantities in a systematic method for equations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1572

    Bombelli's L'algebra

    precursor

    Rafael Bombelli, L'algebra, Bologna, 1572

    A major Italian algebra treatise of the generation before Viete, written without letters for general known quantities.

    Evidence (2)
    • L'algebra first printed 1572. Open Library, L'algebra
    • Page gives 1572 for L'Algebra and describes it as the rhetoric that preceded symbolic notation. English Wikipedia, Rafael Bombelli
  2. 1591

    In artem analyticem isagoge

    first statement

    Francois Viete, In artem analyticem isagoge, Tours 1591

    Introduces letters for unknown and known quantities in a general method for equations (vowels for unknowns, consonants for knowns).

    Evidence (3)
    • Introduced the first systematic algebraic notation in the Isagoge, Tours 1591. MacTutor, Viete biography
    • 1591 Isagoge edition scan. Internet Archive bub_gb_BWTyywN39KEC
    • Dover translation The Analytic Art. Open Library OL8254364W
  3. 1591

    'Species logistic' named in the Isagoge

    naming

    Viete, In artem analyticem isagoge, Tours, 1591

    Viete calls his calculus on symbols the logistic of species (logistica speciosa).

    Evidence (2)
    • Viete called his systematic theory the species logistic, art of calculation on symbols (1591). English Wikipedia, Francois Viete
    • Isagoge scan catalogued. Internet Archive bub_gb_BWTyywN39KEC
  4. 1593

    Zeteticorum libri quinque: Diophantine problems solved with the analytic art

    first application

    Francisci Vietae, Zeteticorum libri quinque, Tours: Mettayer, 1593

    Viete applies his symbolic analytic art, introduced in 1591, to a collection of problems from Diophantus.

    Evidence (2)
    • Zeteticorum libri quinque (1593) is a collection of problems from Diophantus solved using the analytical art. Wikipedia, Francois Viete (raw wikitext), works list 1593
    • Bibliographic record for Viete's Zeteticorum (Zeteticorum liber I), dated 1593. Open Library search, title 'zeteticorum' (work OL44076239W)
  5. 1631

    Harriot's Artis analyticae praxis

    key development

    Thomas Harriot, Artis analyticae praxis, London, 1631 (posthumous)

    Harriot's posthumously printed algebra shows an independent English line of letter-based symbolic algebra.

    Evidence (2)
    • Executors published Artis Analyticae Praxis in 1631. English Wikipedia, Thomas Harriot
    • Artis analyticae praxis, 1631. Open Library
  6. 1637

    Descartes' convention

    key development

    Descartes, La Geometrie, 1637

    Later convention of early letters for knowns and late letters for unknowns replaced Viete's vowels.

    Evidence (2)
    • The convention was introduced later by Descartes in La Geometrie. MacTutor, Viete biography
    • La Geometrie published in 1637 as an appendix to the Discours de la methode. English Wikipedia, La Geometrie
  7. 1646

    Opera mathematica

    consolidation

    Viete, Opera mathematica, ed. van Schooten, Leiden 1646

    Collected works gave the analytic art wide circulation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Opera Mathematica, Leiden 1646, cited in MacTutor's references. MacTutor, Viete biography
