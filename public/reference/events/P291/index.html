1967 · Number theory

Tate's thesis and adelic harmonic analysis

Fourier analysis on idele groups gave a new proof of functional equations for Hecke L-functions.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1950

    Tate's Princeton thesis (unpublished)

    precursor

    Tate, Fourier analysis in number fields and Hecke's zeta-functions, PhD thesis, Princeton, 1950

    Tate's thesis proved the functional equation of Hecke L-functions by Fourier analysis on the ideles, unpublished until 1967.

    Evidence (2)
    • Doctorate in 1950 under Artin on this thesis MacTutor biography of Tate
    • Thesis proved Hecke's functional equation via Fourier analysis on idele groups; published as a chapter in 1967 Wikipedia: Tate's thesis
  2. 1967

    Tate's thesis in print (Cassels-Frohlich volume)

    first statement

    Tate, Fourier analysis in number fields and Hecke's zeta-functions, in Cassels and Frohlich (eds.), Algebraic Number Theory, 1967

    The first public statement of the thesis, in the 1967 volume.

    Evidence (2)
    • Book dated 1967 (Algebraic Number Theory, Cassels ed.) Open Library record
    • Thesis published as a book chapter in 1967 Wikipedia: Tate's thesis
  3. 1967

    Functional equation of Hecke L-functions (same publication)

    first application

    Tate, in Cassels and Frohlich (eds.), 1967 (as above)

    The thesis applies Fourier analysis on ideles to Hecke's functional equation, its first purposeful use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Thesis gave a new proof of the functional equation of Hecke L-series MacTutor biography of Tate
  4. 1967

    Cassels and Frohlich publish Algebraic number theory with Tate's thesis

    consolidation

    Cassels, J. W. S., Frohlich, A. (eds.), Algebraic number theory, 1967

    Book-length treatment that includes Tate's adelic thesis among the chapters.

    Dating or attribution note

    The thesis chapter is a known contents item; the chapter itself was not checked in this run.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists Algebraic number theory (Cassels and Frohlich) with first publication year 1967 Open Library works OL22471132W
  5. 1972

    Generalisation to GL(n)

    key development

    Godement and Jacquet, Zeta Functions of Simple Algebras, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 260 (1972)

    Extends Iwasawa-Tate theory to GL(n) over number fields and automorphic representations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Godement and Jacquet extended Iwasawa-Tate theory to GL(n) in 1972 Wikipedia: Tate's thesis
