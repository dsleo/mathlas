1663 · Probability

Cardano: first treatise on chance (Liber de ludo aleae)

Dice outcomes were analysed by counting favourable cases; the book appeared posthumously.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1563

    Liber de ludo aleae completed

    precursor

    Cardano, Liber de ludo aleae, probably completed by 1563 (manuscript)

    Analyses dice outcomes by counting favourable cases, unpublished in his lifetime.

    Evidence (1)
    • Probably completed by 1563; first foray into probability. MacTutor, Cardan biography
  2. 1654

    Pascal and Fermat correspond on dividing stakes

    precursor

    Private letters work out how to share a stake in an interrupted game, the problem Huygens then took up.

    Dating or attribution note

    Private correspondence, so a precursor; the letters themselves were not read here.

    Evidence (1)
    • Huygens learnt of probability work done in a correspondence between Pascal and Fermat. MacTutor, Huygens biography
  3. 1657

    Huygens' De ratiociniis in ludo aleae

    precursor

    Christiaan Huygens, De ratiociniis in ludo aleae, 1657

    First printed treatise on calculating chances.

    Dating or attribution note

    Huygens' tract is the first printed treatment, earlier in print than Cardano's book (1663); the record is stated by MacTutor, not checked against earlier prints. Reclassified from consolidation to precursor: it predates this event's first statement (1663) and belongs to the wider field.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1657 printing (within van Schooten's Exercitationes). Internet Archive DeRatiociniisInLudoAleae
    • Describes the tract as the first printed work on the calculus of probabilities, written after Huygens learnt of the Pascal-Fermat correspondence. MacTutor, Huygens biography
  4. 1663

    Printed in Cardano's Opera omnia

    first statement

    Cardano, Opera omnia, Lyon 1663, including Liber de ludo aleae

    First public appearance of the book.

    Evidence (3)
    • Published 1663. MacTutor, Cardan biography
    • Opera omnia 1663 scan. Internet Archive bub_gb_npDfaPp-3Y0C
    • Ore's translation and study. zbMATH 0051.24306
  5. 1713

    Jakob Bernoulli's Ars conjectandi

    key development

    Jakob Bernoulli, Ars conjectandi, Basel, 1713 (posthumous)

    Posthumous treatise on the art of conjecture that extends the calculus of chances well beyond games.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ars conjectandi, opus posthumum, printed 1713. Internet Archive record jacobibernoulli00bern
  6. 1718

    De Moivre's Doctrine of Chances

    key development

    Abraham de Moivre, The Doctrine of Chances, London, 1718

    First English treatise on chance, developing Huygens' methods with new analytic tools.

    Evidence (2)
    • First edition dated 1718. Internet Archive record b30412390
    • Biography of the author. MacTutor, De Moivre biography
