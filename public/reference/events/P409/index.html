Decision problems for groups (Dehn)

Dehn posed word, conjugacy and isomorphism problems for finitely presented groups, motivated by topology.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1908

    Tietze's isomorphism problem

    precursor

    H. Tietze, Ueber die topologischen Invarianten mehrdimensionaler Mannigfaltigkeiten, Monatsh. Math. Phys. 19 (1908)

    Tietze's work on finitely presented groups and manifold invariants is the usual earlier statement of the isomorphism question.

    Dating or attribution note

    Role as precursor of the isomorphism problem is background knowledge; the paper was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1908. Crossref
  2. 1911

    Dehn poses the word problem

    first statement

    Dehn, M., Uber unendliche diskontinuierliche Gruppen, Mathematische Annalen (1911)

    Dehn poses the word, conjugacy and isomorphism problems for finitely presented groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper with this title, journal and year Crossref
    • Dehn poses the word problem for finitely presented groups in 1911 Wikipedia: Word problem (mathematics), timeline
  3. 1912

    Dehn solves the word problem for surface groups

    key development

    Dehn, Transformation der Kurven auf zweiseitigen Flaechen, Math. Ann. 72 (1912)

    Gives an algorithm for words in surface groups, the first solved case.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1912. Crossref
    • Dehn posed the word problem in 1911; MacTutor biography. MacTutor, Dehn
  4. 1930

    Magnus' Freiheitssatz

    key development

    W. Magnus, Ueber diskontinuierliche Gruppen mit einer definierenden Relation (Der Freiheitssatz), J. reine angew. Math. 163 (1930) 141-165

    Magnus proves the Freiheitssatz for groups with one defining relation, a tool used for the identity problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Magnus, 'Ueber diskontinuierliche Gruppen mit einer definierenden Relation (Der Freiheitssatz)', Crelle 163 (1930), pp. 141-165. Crossref 10.1515/crll.1930.163.141
  5. 1932

    Magnus solves the identity problem for one-relator groups

    key development

    Magnus, Das Identitaetsproblem fuer Gruppen mit einer definierenden Relation, Math. Ann. 106 (1932)

    Solves Dehn's word problem for groups with a single defining relation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1932. Crossref
  6. 1958

    Novikov on algorithmic insolvability of the word problem

    key development

    Novikov, P. S., On the algorithmic insolvability of the word problem in group theory (1958)

    Novikov shows the word problem for groups is algorithmically unsolvable, settling the decision question negatively.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper with this title and year 1958 zbMATH Open
  7. 1959

    Boone proves unsolvability independently

    key development

    W. W. Boone, The word problem, Ann. of Math. 70 (1959)

    A second proof that the word problem is unsolvable for some finitely presented group.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1959. Crossref
  8. 1977

    Lyndon and Schupp, Combinatorial Group Theory

    consolidation

    Lyndon and Schupp, Combinatorial Group Theory, Springer, 1977

    Standard monograph covering the decision problems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book first published 1977. Open Library
