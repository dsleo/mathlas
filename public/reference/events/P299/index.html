1994 · Probability

Rough paths

Lyons gave a pathwise integration theory beyond bounded variation using iterated integrals.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1994

    Rough-signal differential equation theory begins

    first statement

    T. Lyons, Differential equations driven by rough signals (I): an extension of an inequality of L. C. Young, Math. Res. Lett. 1 (1994)

    The 1994 paper publicly develops an approach to differential equations driven by rough signals.

    Evidence (1)
    • Oxford’s bibliographic record identifies the 1994 paper. Original publication or repository record
  2. 1998

    Systematic rough-path treatment

    consolidation

    T. Lyons, Differential equations driven by rough signals, Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 14 (1998)

    A long paper systematically treats differential equations under rough driving signals.

    Evidence (1)
    • EMS journal record identifies the 1998 treatment. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2004

    Gubinelli's controlled rough paths

    key development

    Gubinelli, M., Controlling rough paths, Journal of Functional Analysis (2004)

    Introduces controlled rough paths, the calculus used to solve equations driven by rough signals.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, JFA (2004) Crossref
  4. 2014

    Hairer's regularity structures

    key development

    Hairer, M., A theory of regularity structures, Inventiones mathematicae (2014)

    Extends the rough-path idea to singular stochastic PDEs through regularity structures.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Inventiones (2014) Crossref
  5. 2014

    Friz-Hairer textbook on rough paths

    consolidation

    Friz, P. K., Hairer, M., A Course on Rough Paths, Universitext, Springer (2014)

    A textbook treatment of rough path theory from the basics through integration.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library work record, 2014 Open Library search API
    • Crossref chapter record, Universitext 2014 Crossref
