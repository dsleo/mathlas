1829 · Number theory

Jacobi: theta functions and elliptic function theory

Elliptic functions were rebuilt on theta functions in a systematic treatise.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1827

    Jacobi's letter to Legendre

    precursor

    letter of C. G. J. Jacobi to A.-M. Legendre, 5 August 1827

    A private letter first told Legendre of Jacobi's new advances on elliptic functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: on 5 August 1827 Jacobi wrote to Legendre, who saw at once that a fundamental advance had been made. MacTutor, Jacobi biography
  2. 1827–1828

    Jacobi's first printed announcement

    precursor

    C. G. J. Jacobi, Extraits de deux lettres de Mr. Jacobi a l'editeur, Astronomische Nachrichten 6, pp. 33-37

    Extracts of two letters printed in Schumacher's journal announced Jacobi's results on elliptic functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Astronomische Nachrichten volume 6, pages 33-37 (record date January 1828). Crossref DOI 10.1002/asna.18280060302
  3. 1828

    Note sur les fonctions elliptiques

    precursor

    C. G. J. Jacobi, Note sur les fonctions elliptiques, J. reine angew. Math. 3 (1828) 192-195

    A short note in Crelle's Journal continued the announcement of the new theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Crelle's Journal 1828, pages 192-195. Crossref DOI 10.1515/crll.1828.3.192
  4. 1829

    Fundamenta nova theoriae functionum ellipticarum

    first statement

    C. G. J. Jacobi, Fundamenta nova theoriae functionum ellipticarum, Regiomonti, 1829

    Jacobi's treatise built elliptic function theory on four theta functions.

    Evidence (3)
    • Library records for the 1829 book. Internet Archive items bub_gb_M-O9C0fKiWMC, fundamentanovat00jacogoog
    • MacTutor: the theory was based on four theta functions in this paper of 1829. MacTutor, Jacobi biography
    • Wikipedia attaches the name Jacobi to the theta function. English Wikipedia, Theta function
  5. 1829

    Fundamenta nova as the systematic treatise

    consolidation

    C. G. J. Jacobi, Fundamenta nova theoriae functionum ellipticarum, 1829

    The same book is the first systematic account of the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor describes the work and its later supplements as fundamental contributions to elliptic function theory. MacTutor, Jacobi biography
