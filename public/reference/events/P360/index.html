1887 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Volterra functionals

Volterra introduced functionals as functions of functions, and Hadamard named the field in 1910.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1887

    Volterra: functions that depend on other functions

    first statement

    Volterra, Sopra le funzioni che dipendono da altre funzioni, Rend. Accad. Lincei (4), 1887

    Volterra defined quantities determined by an entire function, extending calculus-of-variations functionals to a general notion.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record dated 1887. zbMATH Open
    • Wikipedia credits Volterra with introducing the general functional concept in 1887. Wikipedia: Functional analysis
  2. 1890

    Functional calculus applied to Hamilton-Jacobi theory

    first application

    Volterra, c. 1890

    He used the calculus of functionals to carry the Hamilton-Jacobi integration theory over to mathematical physics.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor dates this to 1890. MacTutor Biographies/Volterra
  3. 1910

    Hadamard replaces Volterra's term with 'functional'

    naming

    Hadamard, Lecons sur le calcul des variations, 1910

    Hadamard's lectures introduced the word 'functional', which replaced Volterra's earlier terminology.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor: Hadamard later introduced the word functional, replacing Volterra's terminology. MacTutor Biographies/Volterra
    • Internet Archive record, 1910 edition. Internet Archive
  4. 1913

    Volterra's Sorbonne course on functions of lines

    key development

    Volterra, Lecons sur les fonctions de lignes, Gauthier-Villars, 1913

    A systematic course (given 1912, edited by Peres) presenting the calculus of functions of lines.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record, 1913. zbMATH Open
    • Internet Archive record, 1913. Internet Archive
  5. 1922

    Levy extends nonlinear functional theory

    key development

    Levy, Lecons d'analyse fonctionnelle, 1922

    Levy, a student in Hadamard's circle, continued the theory of nonlinear functionals.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record, 1922. zbMATH Open
    • Wikipedia says nonlinear functional theory was continued by Frechet and Levy. Wikipedia: Functional analysis
  6. 1930

    Volterra's Theory of Functionals (English edition)

    consolidation

    Volterra, Theory of Functionals and of Integral and Integro-Differential Equations, Blackie, 1930

    Volterra's own summary of the theory of functionals, edited by Fantappie and translated into English.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record, 1930. zbMATH Open
    • Internet Archive record dated 1930. Internet Archive
