Von Neumann algebras

Weakly closed self-adjoint operator algebras on Hilbert space became a theory with Murray's classification of factors.

Also known as: W*-algebras (Murray–von Neumann)

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1930

    von Neumann introduces weakly closed operator algebras

    first statement

    J. von Neumann, Zur Algebra der Funktionaloperationen und Theorie der normalen Operatoren, Math. Ann. 102 (1930) 370-427

    Von Neumann's Math. Ann. paper introduces weakly closed operator algebras; the volume issue is dated 1930, and the 1929 on history pages is the earlier date.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref: Math. Ann. 102, pp. 370-427, issued December 1930 Crossref
    • Introduced in 1929 in Mathematische Annalen MacTutor: John von Neumann
    • First studied by von Neumann in 1929 Wikipedia: Von Neumann algebra
  2. 1936

    Murray and von Neumann begin the rings-of-operators series

    key development

    F. J. Murray, J. von Neumann, On Rings of Operators, Ann. of Math. (2) 37 (1936)

    Opened the joint series that founded the systematic theory; the factor-type classification is developed in this series.

    Evidence (2)
    • Publication record: Annals of Math. (2) 37 (1936) Crossref
    • Foundations laid in a fundamental series of papers from the late 1930s and early 1940s MacTutor: John von Neumann
  3. 1943

    Murray-von Neumann: Rings of operators IV

    key development

    F. J. Murray and J. von Neumann, 'On rings of operators IV', Annals of Mathematics 44 (1943) 716-808

    The fourth paper constructs the hyperfinite II1 factor, proves its uniqueness and exhibits non-isomorphic II1 factors.

    Evidence (1)
    • Murray, von Neumann, On Rings of Operators IV, Ann. of Math. 44 (1943) 716, DOI 10.2307/1969107 Crossref
  4. 1957

    Dixmier monograph on operator algebras in Hilbert space

    consolidation

    J. Dixmier, Les algèbres d'opérateurs dans l'espace hilbertien (algèbres de von Neumann), Gauthier-Villars (1957)

    First monograph treating these algebras systematically.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record (1957) Open Library
    • Dixmier's 1957 monograph is the source of the name MacTutor: John von Neumann
  5. 1957

    Dixmier calls them "von Neumann algebras"

    naming

    J. Dixmier, Les algèbres d'opérateurs dans l'espace hilbertien (algèbres de von Neumann), Gauthier-Villars (1957)

    Dixmier's 1957 monograph gives the name "von Neumann algebras" to the field.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor: Dixmier in 1957 called them von Neumann algebras MacTutor: John von Neumann
    • Open Library record (1957) Open Library
