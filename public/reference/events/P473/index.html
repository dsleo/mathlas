1952 · Geometry

Mathematical general relativity: nonlinear PDE for Einstein's equations

Well-posedness of the Einstein equations as a hyperbolic system is followed by global stability of Minkowski space and the analysis of shock formation.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1915

    Einstein's field equations of general relativity

    precursor

    Einstein's 1915 field equations are the nonlinear PDE system whose analysis this event concerns.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor General relativity topic: in the second half of 1915 Einstein put the theory in place MacTutor General relativity topic (saved text)
  2. 1952

    Choquet-Bruhat's existence theorem for the Einstein equations

    first statement

    Y. Fourès-Bruhat (Choquet-Bruhat), 'Theoreme d'existence pour certains systemes d'equations aux derivees partielles non lineaires', Acta Math. 88, 1952

    Choquet-Bruhat proved local existence for the Einstein equations as a hyperbolic system, the first well-posedness statement.

    Evidence (2)
    • Acta Mathematica 1952 paper with this title exists Crossref DOI 10.1007/bf02392131
    • MacTutor Choquet-Bruhat biography covers her 1952 existence work MacTutor Choquet-Bruhat biography
  3. 1989

    Christodoulou's nonlinear stability of Minkowski metric

    key development

    D. Christodoulou, 'The nonlinear stability of the Minkowski metric in general relativity', Lecture Notes in Mathematics, 1989

    Christodoulou's announced stability of Minkowski space started the global-existence program for the Einstein equations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lecture Notes in Mathematics chapter with this title exists Crossref DOI 10.1007/bfb0083872
  4. 1993

    Christodoulou and Klainerman, The Global Nonlinear Stability of the Minkowski Space

    consolidation

    D. Christodoulou and S. Klainerman, The Global Nonlinear Stability of the Minkowski Space, Princeton University Press, 1993

    The monograph gave the full proof of stability of Minkowski space and is the systematic treatment of the subject.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists the book with first publication 1993 (Princeton) Open Library search
    • Crossref indexes the Princeton 1994 volume record (PMS-41) Crossref DOI 10.1515/9781400863174
  5. 2007

    Christodoulou's shock formation in 3-dimensional fluids

    key development

    D. Christodoulou, The Formation of Shocks in 3-Dimensional Fluids, EMS Monographs in Mathematics, 2007

    Christodoulou's shock-formation theory for fluids is the analytic method that the shock-formation program for Einstein's equations later extended.

    Dating or attribution note

    The verified work is the fluid monograph. The Einstein-equation shock program (Christodoulou's later work on gravitational shocks) was not verified bibliographically, so this milestone is about the fluid analogue only.

    Evidence (1)
    • EMS monograph with this title and year 2007 exists Crossref DOI 10.4171/031
