1977 · Combinatorics

Furstenberg: ergodic theory proves Szemeredi's theorem

Density statements about integers are translated into recurrence statements for measure-preserving systems and proved there.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1975

    Szemeredi's combinatorial theorem

    precursor

    Szemeredi, On sets of integers containing k elements in arithmetic progression, Acta Arith. 27 (1975)

    The theorem that the ergodic proof reproves: dense sets of integers contain arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Szemeredi's theorem: general case settled in 1975 Wikipedia: Szemeredi's theorem
  2. 1977

    Ergodic proof of Szemeredi's theorem

    first statement

    Furstenberg, Ergodic behavior of diagonal measures and a theorem of Szemeredi on arithmetic progressions, J. Analyse Math. 31 (1977)

    Translates density statements into recurrence statements for measure-preserving systems, giving an ergodic proof of the theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Szemeredi's theorem: Furstenberg's 1977 ergodic proof Wikipedia: Szemeredi's theorem
  3. 1977

    Ergodic method proves the theorem

    first application

    Furstenberg, J. Analyse Math. 31 (1977)

    The same paper applies ergodic recurrence to the density problem, which is what the methodological date uses.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  4. 1978

    Multidimensional ergodic Szemeredi theorem

    key development

    Furstenberg, Katznelson, An ergodic Szemeredi theorem for commuting transformations, J. Analyse Math. 34 (1978)

    Extends the ergodic proof to commuting transformations, the multidimensional version of the theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref

  5. 1981

    Monograph: Recurrence in ergodic theory

    consolidation

    Furstenberg, Recurrence in Ergodic Theory and Combinatorial Number Theory, Princeton (1981)

    Book-length treatment of recurrence and the ergodic approach to combinatorial number theory.

    Survey: the ergodic theoretical proof

    consolidation

    Furstenberg, Katznelson, Ornstein, The ergodic theoretical proof of Szemeredi's theorem, Bull. AMS (1982)

    Survey of the ergodic proof, consolidating the method for a wider audience.
