1961 · Topology

Surgery theory (controlled modification of manifolds)

Surgery classifies high-dimensional manifolds via algebraic obstruction groups.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1961

    Milnor's surgery procedure for killing homotopy groups

    first statement

    John Milnor, A procedure for killing homotopy groups of differentiable manifolds, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 3 (1961)

    Milnor introduces the cutting-and-gluing operation that defines surgery.

    Evidence (2)
    • Milnor 1961 Proc. Symp. Pure Math. record Crossref
    • Surgery technique introduced by Milnor in 1961 Wikipedia, Surgery theory
  2. 1961

    Milnor names the technique 'surgery'

    naming

    John Milnor, A procedure for killing homotopy groups of differentiable manifolds (1961)

    Milnor calls the technique surgery; Wallace called it spherical modification.

    Evidence (1)
    • Milnor called the technique surgery Wikipedia, Surgery theory
  3. 1963

    Kervaire-Milnor: groups of homotopy spheres (exotic spheres)

    first application

    Michel Kervaire and John Milnor, Groups of homotopy spheres I, Annals of Math. 77 (1963)

    Applies surgery to classify exotic spheres; Wikipedia says this classification led to surgery as a major tool.

    Evidence (2)
    • Kervaire-Milnor 1963 Annals record Crossref
    • Classification of exotic spheres led to surgery as a tool Wikipedia, Surgery theory
  4. 1969

    Browder: Kervaire invariant of framed manifolds

    key development

    William Browder, The Kervaire invariant of framed manifolds and its generalization, Annals of Math. 90 (1969) 157-186

    Browder reduces the Kervaire invariant problem to stable homotopy theory and shows it vanishes outside dimensions 2^k-2.

    Evidence (2)
    • Browder 1969 Annals record Crossref
    • Browder's reduction and dimension restriction Wikipedia, Kervaire invariant
  5. 1970

    Wall, Surgery on Compact Manifolds

    consolidation

    C. T. C. Wall, Surgery on Compact Manifolds, Academic Press (1970)

    The first systematic monograph on surgery theory for compact manifolds.

    Evidence (3)
    • Wall 1970 book record Open Library
    • AMS reprint (1999) in the Surveys series Crossref
    • Wall's book listed as 1970 MacTutor, Wall biography
  6. 1972

    Browder, Surgery on Simply-Connected Manifolds

    consolidation

    William Browder, Surgery on Simply-Connected Manifolds, Ergebnisse 65, Springer (1972)

    Systematic treatment of surgery for simply-connected manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Browder 1972 book record Crossref
