1974 · Differential equations

Finite-gap integration of soliton equations

Theta functions of algebraic curves give explicit periodic solutions of KdV and related soliton equations.

Also known as: finite-zone integration

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Inverse scattering method for KdV

    precursor

    Gardner, Greene, Kruskal, Miura, Method for solving the Korteweg-de Vries equation, Phys. Rev. Lett. 19 (1967)

    Introduces the inverse scattering transform for KdV, the method on which finite-gap solutions are built.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  2. 1974

    Periodic problem for KdV (finite-gap origin)

    first statement

    Novikov, The periodic problem for the Korteweg-de Vries equation, Funct. Anal. Appl. 8 (1974)

    Treats the periodic Korteweg-de Vries problem, where finite-gap (finite-zone) solutions first appear as the periodic analogue of the soliton theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  3. 1975

    Hill's operators with finitely many gaps

    first application

    Its, Matveev, Hill's operator with finitely many gaps, Funct. Anal. Appl. 9 (1975)

    Uses theta-function formulas for Hill operators with finitely many gaps, the first explicit finite-gap integration.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  4. 1976

    KdV-type equations, finite-zone operators and abelian varieties

    key development

    Dubrovin, Matveev, Novikov, Non-linear equations of Korteweg-de Vries type, finite-zone linear operators, and Abelian varieties, Russian Math. Surveys 31 (1976)

    Establishes the algebro-geometric (theta-function) solutions of KdV with finite-zone operators on Jacobians of curves.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  5. 1977

    Krichever survey: algebraic geometry methods

    consolidation

    Krichever, Methods of algebraic geometry in the theory of non-linear equations, Russian Math. Surveys 32 (1977)

    Survey that systematises the algebro-geometric method for nonlinear equations, the consolidated treatment.
