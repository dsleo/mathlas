2004 · Geometry

Optimal transport and synthetic Ricci curvature

Lower Ricci curvature bounds are defined on non-smooth spaces by convexity of entropy along optimal transport geodesics, giving a stable notion under Gromov-Hausdorff limits.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1991

    Polar factorization through optimal transport

    precursor

    Y. Brenier, Polar factorization and monotone rearrangement of vector-valued functions, CPAM 44 (1991)

    Brenier established polar factorization using a Monge–Kantorovich problem, a precursor to the later metric-measure curvature definitions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher gives June 1991 and describes the Monge–Kantorovich method. Original bibliographic record
  2. 2004

    Ricci bounds defined through optimal transport

    first statement

    J. Lott and C. Villani, Ricci curvature for metric-measure spaces via optimal transport, arXiv:math/0412127 (2004)

    The public preprint defines synthetic lower Ricci curvature bounds for measured length spaces via displacement convexity.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv records the December 2004 public submission and states the definition in the abstract. Original bibliographic record
  3. 2006

    Sturm's metric measure space curvature bounds

    key development

    Sturm, K.-T., On the geometry of metric measure spaces I, Acta Mathematica (2006)

    Defines curvature-dimension bounds for metric measure spaces by convexity of entropy along optimal transport, a parallel route to Lott-Villani.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record for Acta Math 2006 Crossref
  4. 2009

    Lott-Villani curvature via optimal transport

    key development

    Lott, J., Villani, C., Ricci curvature for metric-measure spaces via optimal transport, Annals of Mathematics 169 (2009)

    Gives the transport-based synthetic Ricci lower bound for metric-measure spaces that became the standard definition.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Annals 169 (2009) Crossref
  5. 2009

    Villani's Optimal Transport: Old and New

    consolidation

    Villani, C., Optimal Transport: Old and New, Springer (Grundlehren series), 2009

    A large survey-monograph that systematizes optimal transport and its curvature-based viewpoint.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref book record, 2009 Crossref
