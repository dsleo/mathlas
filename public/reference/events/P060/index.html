1900 · Probability

Brownian motion: from physics and finance to measure on path space

A random motion modelled by physicists and by Bachelier is given a rigorous measure on continuous paths, which then solves boundary value problems for harmonic functions.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1876

    Gerardin uses the Brownian-movement phrase in a title

    precursor

    Gerardin, A., Traitement des eaux industrielles, mouvement brownien, 1876

    Earliest title found that contains the phrase "mouvement brownien".

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists the title with first publication year 1876 Open Library works OL43484664W
  2. 1900

    Bachelier's model of speculative prices

    first statement

    Bachelier, L., 'Théorie de la spéculation', Ann. Sci. ENS (1900)

    Bachelier models price fluctuations with a Brownian-type stochastic process in his doctoral thesis.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Théorie de la spéculation', year 1900 zbMATH API, query 'Bachelier Theorie de la speculation'
    • Bachelier modelled the stochastic process in his 1900 thesis Wikipedia, Brownian motion (saved wikitext)
  3. 1905

    Einstein's kinetic theory of Brownian motion

    first statement

    Einstein, A., 'Über die von der molekularkinetischen Theorie der Wärme geforderte Bewegung...', Ann. Phys. (1905)

    Einstein derives the diffusion law for particles suspended in a fluid, from a physical model of molecular motion.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Annalen der Physik, year 1905 Crossref, DOI 10.1002/andp.19053220806
  4. 1923

    Differential space: Wiener measure on continuous paths

    key development

    Wiener, N., 'Differential-space', J. Math. Phys. (1923)

    Wiener constructs a measure on continuous paths, the basis of the rigorous Brownian motion.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'Differential-space', year 1923 zbMATH API, query 'Wiener Differential space'
  5. 1944

    Brownian motion and harmonic functions in the plane

    first application

    Kakutani, S., 'Two-dimensional Brownian motion and harmonic functions', Proc. Imp. Acad. Tokyo (1944)

    Uses Brownian paths to study harmonic functions, the step that connects the path measure to boundary value problems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Proc. Japan Acad., year 1944 Crossref, DOI 10.3792/pia/1195572706
  6. 1948

    Levy publishes a monograph on stochastic processes and Brownian motion

    consolidation

    Levy, P., Processus stochastiques et mouvement brownien, 1948

    Book-length treatment of Brownian motion as a stochastic process.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists the monograph with first publication year 1948 Open Library works OL1332342W
