Liouville quantum gravity and mating of trees

Duplantier-Sheffield-Miller-Gwynne built random surfaces from Gaussian free field and linked them to SLE.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2008

    Probabilistic LQG and KPZ relation

    precursor

    B. Duplantier and S. Sheffield, Liouville Quantum Gravity and KPZ (2008)

    The 2008 work constructs a Gaussian-free-field LQG measure and KPZ interpretation.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents probabilistic lqg and kpz relation in 2008 arXiv
  2. 2014

    Mating-of-trees LQG construction posted

    first statement

    B. Duplantier, J. Miller and S. Sheffield, Liouville quantum gravity as a mating of trees (2014)

    The 2014 preprint canonically embeds a pair of continuum random trees as an LQG sphere.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents mating-of-trees lqg construction posted in 2014 arXiv
  3. 2014

    Tree mating identifies SLE-decorated LQG

    first application

    B. Duplantier, J. Miller and S. Sheffield, Liouville quantum gravity as a mating of trees (2014)

    The paper relates the continuum tree pair to SLE and decorated random-planar-map scaling limits.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents tree mating identifies sle-decorated lqg in 2014 arXiv
  4. 2018

    SLE as a mating of trees in Euclidean geometry

    key development

    Holden, Sun, SLE as a mating of trees in Euclidean geometry, Comm. Math. Phys. (2018)

    Uses the mating-of-trees bijection to describe SLE in Euclidean geometry, a main later application of the construction.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2018 CMP paper by Holden and Sun on SLE as a mating of trees zbMATH Open record
  5. 2023

    Mating of trees for random planar maps and LQG: a survey

    consolidation

    Gwynne, Holden, Sun, Mating of trees for random planar maps and Liouville quantum gravity: a survey (2023)

    A survey that presents the mating-of-trees theory for random planar maps and Liouville quantum gravity.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2023 survey by Gwynne, Holden and Sun on mating of trees for planar maps and LQG zbMATH Open record
