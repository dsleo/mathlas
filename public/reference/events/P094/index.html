1941 · Number theory

The large sieve: harmonic analysis in analytic number theory

Duality for trigonometric polynomials gives inequalities on sums over characters, producing the Bombieri-Vinogradov theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1941

    Linnik's large sieve inequality

    first statement

    Linnik, Yu. V., The large sieve, Comptes Rendus (Doklady) de l'Academie des Sciences de l'URSS (N.S.) 30 (1941) 292-294

    Linnik bounds how many integers can survive when residue classes are removed modulo many primes, the first form of the sieve.

    Evidence (2)
    • Zentralblatt record: title 'The large sieve', Dokl. Acad. Sci. URSS 30, pages 292-294 (1941) zbMATH API (Zbl 0024.29302)
    • MacTutor: in a 1941 paper Linnik introduced the large sieve method MacTutor Biography: Yuri Linnik
  2. 1941

    Term 'large sieve' coined and used in a title

    naming

    Linnik (1941), as above

    The term was Linnik's own and first appears in the title of his 1941 note.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH title 'The large sieve' (1941) zbMATH API (Zbl 0024.29302)
    • MacTutor: 'large sieve', which was Linnik's own term MacTutor Biography: Yuri Linnik
  3. 1950

    Renyi's probabilistic reworking of Linnik's sieve

    key development

    Renyi, A., On the large sieve of Ju. V. Linnik (1950), zbMATH 0034.02403

    Renyi developed Linnik's large sieve further with probability methods, which kept the method in circulation.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'On the large sieve of Ju. V. Linnik', 1950 zbMATH API (Zbl 0034.02403)
    • Wikipedia: early history traces to Linnik 1941, then Renyi used probability methods Wikipedia: Large sieve, History
  4. 1965

    Bombieri's large sieve and the Bombieri-Vinogradov theorem

    first application

    Bombieri, E., On the large-sieve, Mathematika (1965), doi 10.1112/s0025579300005313

    Bombieri applies the sieve to primes in arithmetic progressions, yielding the Bombieri-Vinogradov theorem.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref record: 'On the large sieve', Mathematika, 1965 Crossref API
    • Wikipedia: Bombieri and Roth formulated the sieve in the early 1960s; Bombieri-Vinogradov proved in the mid-1960s as a major application Wikipedia: Large sieve, History
    • MacTutor: Bombieri's theorem on primes in progressions is obtained by the large sieve MacTutor Biography: Enrico Bombieri
  5. 1968

    Montgomery's note on the large sieve

    key development

    Montgomery, H. L., A note on the large sieve, J. London Math. Soc. 43 (1968) 93-98, zbMATH 0254.10043

    A short reworking of the sieve that made the duality argument accessible.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'A note on the large sieve', J. Lond. Math. Soc. 43 (1968) zbMATH API (Zbl 0254.10043)
  6. 1971

    Gallagher's larger sieve

    key development

    Gallagher, P. X., A larger sieve, Acta Arithmetica 18 (1971) 77-81, doi 10.4064/aa-18-1-77-81

    Gallagher removes arbitrarily many residue classes and simplifies the key estimates of the large sieve.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'A larger sieve', Acta Arithmetica, 1971 Crossref API
    • Wikipedia: Gallagher simplified many key ingredients in the late 1960s and early 1970s Wikipedia: Large sieve, History
  7. 1978

    Montgomery's survey of the analytic principle

    consolidation

    Montgomery, H. L., The analytic principle of the large sieve, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 84 (1978), doi 10.1090/s0002-9904-1978-14497-8

    A survey that gathers the duality principle and applications into one systematic account.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: 'The analytic principle of the large sieve', Bull. AMS, 1978 Crossref API
