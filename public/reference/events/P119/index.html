Archimedean statics and hydrostatics

Archimedes treated equilibrium and floating bodies by geometric proof.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1586

    Stevin's published statics treatise

    key development

    S. Stevin, De Beghinselen der Weeghconst (1586)

    A documented early modern development of the statics tradition.

    Evidence (1)
    • Stevin, De Beghinselen der Weeghconst (1586), Dutch treatise on statics (weeghconst). Open Library
  2. 1897

    Heath publishes English Archimedes edition

    consolidation

    T. L. Heath, The Works of Archimedes (1897)

    The edition consolidates access to the ancient statics corpus.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: Heath, The Works of Archimedes (1897), standard English edition. Open Library
  3. Date unknown

    Archimedes' statics treatises, undated

    precursor

    Archimedes, On the Equilibrium of Planes and On Floating Bodies

    The ancient treatises combine mechanical questions with geometric arguments.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: On plane equilibriums sets out principles of mechanics using geometry methods; order of Archimedes writings uncertain; lifetime 287-212 BC. MacTutor, Archimedes biography
