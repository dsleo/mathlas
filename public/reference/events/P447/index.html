1829 · Geometry

Non-Euclidean geometry: the independence of the parallel postulate

After Saccheri and Lambert explored denials of the fifth postulate, Gauss privately, then Bolyai and Lobachevsky openly, developed a consistent geometry with many parallels.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1829

    Lobachevsky's geometry appears in print

    first statement

    N. Lobachevsky, Kazan Messenger account of non-Euclidean geometry (1829)

    A published account develops geometry without Euclid's parallel postulate.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lobachevsky's account, the first in print, appeared in 1829 in the Kazan Messenger MacTutor, Lobachevsky biography
  2. 1832

    Bolyai's independent Appendix

    key development

    J. Bolyai, Appendix scientiam spatii absolute veram exhibens (1832)

    An independent public construction extends the non-Euclidean programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Bolyai's Appendix appeared in 1832 MacTutor
  3. 1837

    Lobachevsky titles his work Géométrie imaginaire

    naming

    N. Lobachevsky, Géométrie imaginaire, J. reine angew. Math. 17 (1837)

    A published title names the alternative geometry, though not with the later non-Euclidean term.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lobachevsky, 'Geometrie imaginaire', J. reine angew. Math. 17 (1837) 295-320 Crossref record
  4. 1868

    Beltrami models the alternative geometry

    first application

    E. Beltrami, Saggio di interpretazione della geometria non-euclidea (1868)

    A model addresses the consistency and interpretation of non-Euclidean geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Beltrami's 1868 'Saggio di interpretazione della geometria non-euclidea' gave his famous model MacTutor, Beltrami biography
