2022 · Algorithms & complexity

AlphaTensor: reinforcement learning for matrix multiplication algorithms

Matrix multiplication was cast as a single-player tensor decomposition game solved by AlphaGo-style training, and conventional search then refined its mod-2 algorithms within days.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1969

    Strassen's faster matrix multiplication

    precursor

    V. Strassen, Gaussian elimination is not optimal, Numer. Math. 13 (1969)

    A classical faster algorithm is the benchmark later surpassed for particular small matrices.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published 1969 Numerische Mathematik article Springer / Crossref
  2. 2022

    AlphaTensor research article

    first statement

    A. Fawzi et al., Discovering faster matrix multiplication algorithms with reinforcement learning, Nature 610 (2022)

    The published paper describes a reinforcement-learning game for exact matrix-multiplication tensor decompositions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Nature article published 5 October 2022 Nature / Crossref
  3. 2022

    AlphaTensor finds improved multiplication algorithms

    first application

    A. Fawzi et al., Discovering faster matrix multiplication algorithms with reinforcement learning, Nature 610 (2022)

    The agent finds exact decompositions, including a 4×4 mod-2 algorithm using 47 multiplications.

    Evidence (1)
    • The article reports the 4×4 finite-field result Nature / Crossref
  4. 2022

    Kauers–Moosbauer improve a mod-2 record

    key development

    M. Kauers and J. Moosbauer, The FBHHRBNRSSSHK-Algorithm for Multiplication in Z₂^{5×5} Is Still Not the End of the Story, arXiv:2210.04045 (2022)

    An independent search rapidly improves a finite-field multiplication record.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted in October 2022 arXiv
  5. 2023

    Flip graphs for matrix multiplication

    key development

    Kauers, Moosbauer, Flip graphs for matrix multiplication, ISSAC 2023

    Develops a search method for matrix multiplication schemes that produces new small schemes, a later development of the computer-search line that AlphaTensor opened.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2023 ISSAC paper by Kauers and Moosbauer on flip graphs for matrix multiplication zbMATH Open record
