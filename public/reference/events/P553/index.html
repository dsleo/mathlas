2023 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Random hyperbolic surfaces have maximal spectral gap: random-graph technique transfer

Friedman's method for handling problematic paths in random graphs was adapted to hyperbolic surfaces and Mirzakhani-style geodesic counts to prove the optimal spectral gap of 1/4 for random surfaces.
