1685 · Algebra

Seki: resultants, determinants, elimination

Seki Takakazu found resultant and determinant in elimination.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1674

    Seki's Hatsubi Sanpo solves 15 problems by elimination

    precursor

    Seki Takakazu, Hatsubi Sanpo, 1674

    Seki gave single-variable equations that result from elimination for fifteen set problems, but did not reveal the process.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor says Seki published Hatsubi Sanpo in 1674 solving fifteen problems posed four years earlier. MacTutor, Takakazu Seki
    • Wikipedia says the 1674 book gave only the resulting single-variable equations with no account of the process or symbols. English Wikipedia, Seki Takakazu
  2. 1683

    Kaifukudai no Ho: resultant and determinant in manuscript

    precursor

    Seki Takakazu, Kaifukudai no Ho, 1683 (manuscript)

    Seki pushed elimination theory using resultants and devised determinants to express them, in a manuscript whose 5x5 formula was wrong.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia: in 1683 Seki developed the determinant to express the resultant in Kaifukudai no Ho; the 5x5 formula in the manuscript is wrong. English Wikipedia, Seki Takakazu
    • MacTutor: Seki was the first person to study determinants, in 1683, ten years before Leibniz. MacTutor, Takakazu Seki
  3. 1683–1710

    Taisei Sankei compiled: complete determinant formula

    precursor

    Seki, Takebe brothers, Taisei Sankei (compiled 1683-1710)

    The school's encyclopaedia, assembled over decades, contains a correct general determinant expansion.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia: Taisei Sankei, written 1683-1710, contains a correct general formula equivalent to Laplace's expansion; the complete version is no later than 1710. English Wikipedia, Seki Takakazu
    • MacTutor biography of Takebe: Seki's project to record mathematics launched 1683, carried on by the Takebe brothers. MacTutor, Takebe Katahiro
  4. 1685

    Takebe's Hatsubi Sanpo Genkai shows elimination in detail

    first statement

    Takebe Katahiro, Hatsubi Sanpo Genkai, 1685

    A pupil of Seki published notes showing the elimination process in full using the new algebraic symbols, the first open account of the method.

    Dating or attribution note

    Earliest public statement found; Seki's own priority rests on manuscripts. Tanaka Yoshizane's 1678 Sanpo Meiki appears to contain related elimination results; not checked against a primary source.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: in 1685 Takebe Katahiro published notes on Hatsubi Sanpo showing the process of elimination in detail with algebraic symbols. English Wikipedia, Seki Takakazu
