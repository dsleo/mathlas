1963 · Combinatorics

Hales–Jewett theorem and structured Ramsey partitions

Ramsey's partition theorem is generalised to structured colourings.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1930

    Ramsey's partition theorem (cross-event precursor)

    precursor

    F. P. Ramsey, On a problem of formal logic, Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 30 (1930).

    Ramsey's 1930 partition theorem is the ancestor of the structured colouring results. Same paper as the Ramsey theory event.

    Evidence (1)
    • DOI record: Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 30 (1930) Crossref
  2. 1963

    Hales and Jewett prove the colouring theorem on high-dimensional cubes

    first statement

    A. W. Hales, R. I. Jewett, Regularity and positional games, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 106 (1963)

    Proved that colourings of a high-dimensional n-by-n-by-n cube contain a monochromatic combinatorial line.

    Evidence (2)
    • DOI record: Trans. AMS 106 (1963) Crossref
    • Named after Hales and Jewett; statement of the theorem Wikipedia: Hales–Jewett theorem
  3. 1963

    Theorem applied to regularity of positional games

    first application

    A. W. Hales, R. I. Jewett, Regularity and positional games, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 106 (1963)

    The same paper uses the combinatorial result to study positional games, which is its target problem.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same paper as the first_statement; it plays both roles. The application to positional games is from the title; the content was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Title: Regularity and positional games Crossref record
  4. 1971

    Graham–Rothschild extend partition theorems to structured sets

    key development

    R. L. Graham, B. L. Rothschild, Ramsey's theorem for n-parameter sets, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 159 (1971)

    Generalised Ramsey's partition theorem to n-parameter sets; this is the structured-colouring extension named in the event.

    Evidence (1)
    • DOI record: Trans. AMS 159 (1971) Crossref
  5. 1980

    Graham, Rothschild and Spencer: Ramsey Theory (monograph)

    consolidation

    R. L. Graham, B. L. Rothschild, J. H. Spencer, Ramsey Theory, Wiley (1980)

    Monograph that treats the Hales–Jewett and structured-colouring results with the rest of Ramsey theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: "Ramsey theory" (1980) Open Library
  6. 1991

    Furstenberg–Katznelson density version of Hales–Jewett

    key development

    H. Furstenberg, Y. Katznelson, A density version of the Hales–Jewett theorem, J. Analyse Math. 57 (1991)

    Proved a density (quantitative positive-density) version of the Hales–Jewett theorem using ergodic methods.

    Evidence (2)
    • DOI record: J. Analyse Math. 57 (1991) Crossref
    • Cited as a density version of the Hales–Jewett theorem Wikipedia: Ramsey theory references
