1963 · Algebra

Forcing: independence results enter algebra and analysis

Cohen's method of forcing shows statements such as the continuum hypothesis are undecidable, and Shelah uses related techniques to show Whitehead's problem on abelian groups is independent.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1940

    Godel's consistency of CH

    precursor

    Godel, The Consistency of the Continuum Hypothesis, Annals of Mathematics Studies 3, Princeton, 1940

    Godel's inner-model proof of the consistency of CH is the background against which Cohen developed forcing.

    Evidence (2)
    • Godel, Consistency of the Continuum Hypothesis, DOI 10.1515/9781400881635 Crossref
    • Cohen developed forcing after reading Godel's book on the consistency of CH MacTutor: Paul Cohen
  2. 1963

    Cohen proves independence of CH

    first statement

    Cohen, The independence of the continuum hypothesis, PNAS 50 (1963) 1143-1148

    Cohen's forcing method first establishes the independence of the continuum hypothesis from ZF.

    Dating or attribution note

    The name 'forcing' is attributed to this work; the wording in the text was not checked.

    Evidence (2)
    • Cohen, The independence of the continuum hypothesis, PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.50.6.1143 Crossref
    • Forcing was first used by Cohen in 1963 Wikipedia: Forcing (mathematics)
  3. 1964

    Cohen proves independence of AC and CH

    key development

    Cohen, The independence of the continuum hypothesis II, PNAS 51 (1964) 105-110

    The second paper extends the method to independence of the axiom of choice and the continuum hypothesis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Cohen, The independence of the continuum hypothesis II, DOI 10.1073/pnas.51.1.105 Crossref
    • Cohen used forcing to prove the independence of AC and CH from ZF MacTutor: Paul Cohen
  4. 1966

    Cohen's monograph Set Theory and the Continuum Hypothesis

    consolidation

    Cohen, Set Theory and the Continuum Hypothesis, W. A. Benjamin, New York, 1966

    Cohen's book is the first systematic presentation of forcing.

    Evidence (1)
    • Review in Science gives Benjamin, New York, 1966, DOI 10.1126/science.157.3791.914 Crossref
  5. 1974

    Shelah: Whitehead problem independent of ZFC

    first application

    S. Shelah, 'Infinite abelian groups, Whitehead problem and some constructions', Israel J. Math. 18 (1974) 243-256

    Shelah shows that every Whitehead group is free under V=L but not under Martin's axiom with not-CH, a consistency obtained by forcing, so an algebra problem becomes independent of ZFC.

    Dating or attribution note

    That the MA + not-CH half rests on forcing is from the standard account of the result; the paper text was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Shelah, Israel J. Math. 18 (1974) 243-256, DOI 10.1007/bf02757281 Crossref
    • Shelah proved in 1974 that the Whitehead problem is independent of ZFC. Wikipedia, Whitehead problem
  6. 1980

    Whitehead groups need not be free even under CH

    key development

    Shelah, Whitehead groups may not be free even assuming CH, II, Israel J. Math. (1980)

    Shelah shows that CH alone does not decide the Whitehead problem, refining his 1974 independence result.

    Dating or attribution note

    The paper's method (forcing versus a related set-theoretic argument) was not checked in the text.

    Evidence (2)
    • Shelah, Whitehead groups may not be free even assuming CH II, DOI 10.1007/bf02760652 Crossref
    • Shelah 1980 is the second paper of the series on the Whitehead problem and CH Crossref title record
