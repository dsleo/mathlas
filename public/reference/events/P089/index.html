1979 · Probability

Spin glasses: the Parisi formula for the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model

A replica-symmetry-breaking prediction for the free energy of the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model is proved by interpolation arguments and ultrametricity.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1975

    Sherrington-Kirkpatrick solvable spin-glass model

    precursor

    Sherrington, Kirkpatrick, Solvable model of a spin-glass, Physical Review Letters 35 (1975)

    The SK model is introduced as an exactly solvable mean-field spin glass; the formula concerns its free energy.

    Evidence (2)
    • SK model introduced in 1975 Wikipedia: Spin glass
    • PRL 1975 paper, authors and year Crossref
  2. 1979

    Replica-symmetry breaking formula for the SK free energy

    first statement

    Parisi, Infinite number of order parameters for spin-glasses, Physical Review Letters 43 (1979)

    Parisi finds the equilibrium solution of the SK model by infinitely many order parameters, the formula later proved rigorous.

    Evidence (2)
    • Equilibrium solution of SK model found by Parisi in 1979 with replica method Wikipedia: Spin glass
    • PRL 1979 paper, author and year Crossref
  3. 1979

    RSB solution applied to the SK model

    first application

    Parisi, Infinite number of order parameters for spin-glasses, Physical Review Letters 43 (1979)

    The 1979 paper applies the replica-symmetry-breaking idea to the SK model, its target problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Parisi's 1979 solution applies to the SK model Wikipedia: Spin glass
  4. 1980

    Order parameter as a function on [0,1]

    key development

    Parisi, The order parameter for spin glasses: a function on the interval 0-1, J. Phys. A 13 (1980)

    Parisi's 1980 paper gives the explicit functional order parameter, the form the formula is written in.

    Evidence (1)
    • J. Phys. A 1980 paper, author and year Crossref
  5. 1987

    Spin Glass Theory and Beyond (monograph)

    consolidation

    Mézard, Parisi, Virasoro, Spin Glass Theory and Beyond, World Scientific (1987)

    The 1987 monograph is the first systematic treatment of spin-glass theory with replica symmetry breaking.

    Dating or attribution note

    Systematic-treatment claim is an inference; it precedes the Talagrand 2011 monograph.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book title, authors, year 1987 Open Library
  6. 2003

    Broken replica symmetry bounds via interpolation

    key development

    Guerra, Broken replica symmetry bounds in the mean field spin glass model, Communications in Mathematical Physics (2003)

    Guerra's interpolation method gives rigorous bounds on the free energy consistent with broken replica symmetry.

    Dating or attribution note

    The claim that Guerra's bound is the first rigorous step is not checked on a fetched page.

    Evidence (2)
    • CMP 2003 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Broken replica symmetry bounds in the mean-field model are the first rigorous step Input summary (interpolation); not checked on a fetched page
  7. 2006

    Proof of the Parisi formula

    key development

    Talagrand, The Parisi formula, Annals of Mathematics 163 (2006)

    Talagrand proves the Parisi formula for the free energy of the SK model using ultrametricity.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annals 2006 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Talagrand's work on rigorous results for spin glasses MacTutor: Michel Talagrand
