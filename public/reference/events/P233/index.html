1965 · Logic & foundations

Ax-Kochen

Ultraproducts showed Artin's conjecture on p-adic forms holds for large primes.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1965

    Ax-Kochen complete axioms for p-adic fields

    first statement

    Ax, J.; Kochen, S., Diophantine problems over local fields I, II, Amer. J. Math. 87 (1965) 605-648

    Gives a complete axiomatisation of the p-adic fields and shows that truth transfers through ultraproducts over large primes.

    Evidence (2)
    • Part I and II, Amer. J. Math. 87 (1965), pages 605-630 and 631-648, DOI 10.2307/2373065 zbMATH 0136.32805; Crossref
    • The proof uses ultraproducts over primes and Lang's theorem for Laurent series over finite fields Wikipedia, Ax-Kochen theorem (proof section)
  2. 1965

    Finite exceptional set for degree-d forms over p-adic fields

    first application

    Ax, J.; Kochen, S., Diophantine problems over local fields I, II, Amer. J. Math. 87 (1965)

    Applies the transfer to show that homogeneous forms of degree d have p-adic zeros for all but finitely many primes in enough variables, answering an Artin-type question.

    Evidence (2)
    • The theorem states a finite exceptional set of primes for each degree d, with at least d squared plus 1 variables Wikipedia, Ax-Kochen theorem (lead)
    • Same 1965 paper as the first_statement milestone zbMATH 0136.32805
  3. 1966

    Decidability of p-adic fields

    key development

    Ax, J.; Kochen, S., Diophantine problems over local fields III: Decidable fields, Annals of Mathematics 83 (1966) 437-456

    Proves that the theory of p-adic fields is decidable, showing the method yields a full decision result and not only a transfer.

    Evidence (1)
    • Part III, Annals of Mathematics (1966), pages 437-456, DOI 10.2307/1970476 zbMATH 0223.02050; Crossref
  4. 1978

    Prestel-Ziegler model-theoretic methods for topological fields

    consolidation

    Prestel, A.; Ziegler, M., Model-theoretic methods in the theory of topological fields, J. reine angew. Math. 299/300 (1978) 318-341

    A systematic model-theoretic treatment of fields that extends the Ax-Kochen method to topological and valued settings.

    Evidence (1)
    • Prestel-Ziegler, Crelle 299/300 (1978), pages 318-341, DOI 10.1515/crll.1978.299-300.318 Crossref; zbMATH 0367.12014
