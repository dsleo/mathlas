Gromov polynomial growth via geometry

Large-scale geometry shows polynomial growth implies virtual nilpotence.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1972

    Bass, 'The degree of polynomial growth of finitely generated nilpotent groups'

    precursor

    H. Bass, 'The degree of polynomial growth of finitely generated nilpotent groups', Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society, DOI 10.1112/plms/s3-25.4.603, 1972

    Bass's 1972 paper uses 'polynomial growth' for finitely generated groups in its title, the earliest such title found in the searched window.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record gives title 'The Degree of Polynomial Growth of Finitely Generated Nilpotent Groups', author Bass, container Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society, year 1972 Crossref, DOI 10.1112/plms/s3-25.4.603
  2. 1981

    Groups of polynomial growth and expanding maps

    first statement

    Gromov, M., Groups of polynomial growth and expanding maps, Publ. Math. IHÉS 53, 1981 (with an appendix by Tits)

    Gromov proves that polynomial growth implies a nilpotent subgroup of finite index, using limits of rescaled metrics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Publ. Math. IHÉS, 1981 (DOI 10.1007/bf02698687) Crossref record
    • Gromov's proof of the polynomial-growth conjecture MacTutor: Mikhael Gromov
  3. 2009

    A new proof of Gromov's theorem

    key development

    Kleiner, B., A new proof of Gromov's theorem on groups of polynomial growth, J. AMS 23, 2009

    Kleiner gives a new proof of the theorem using harmonic functions and geometry of balls, simplifying the original argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in J. AMS, 2009 (DOI 10.1090/s0894-0347-09-00658-4) Crossref record
  4. 2010

    A finitary version of Gromov's theorem

    key development

    Shalom, Y., Tao, T., A finitary version of Gromov's polynomial growth theorem, GAFA 2010

    Shalom and Tao prove a quantitative finitary version, a further development of the theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in Geom. Funct. Anal., 2010 (DOI 10.1007/s00039-010-0096-1) Crossref record
  5. Date unknown

    Growth of nilpotent groups (Wolf, Guivarc'h, Bass)

    precursor

    Earlier results show nilpotent groups have polynomial growth and compute its order, the input to Gromov's converse.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wolf showed f.g. nilpotent groups have polynomial growth; Guivarc'h and Bass computed the order Wikipedia: Gromov's theorem on groups of polynomial growth
