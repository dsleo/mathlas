1970 · Number theory

The Langlands programme: automorphic forms and Galois representations over number fields

Galois representations are conjecturally matched with automorphic representations, linking arithmetic, representation theory and harmonic analysis.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Langlands' letter to Weil

    precursor

    R. P. Langlands, letter to A. Weil, January 1967 (17 pages)

    A private letter sketching the conjectures, circulated in typed copies before any publication.

    Evidence (2)
    • 17-page letter to Weil, January 1967 MacTutor Langlands
    • IAS Langlands archive IAS publications
  2. 1970

    Langlands, Problems in the theory of automorphic forms

    first statement

    R. P. Langlands, Problems in the theory of automorphic forms, Lecture Notes in Math. 170 (1970)

    The first published statement of the conjectures matching automorphic representations with Galois representations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Springer record; zbMATH Zbl 0225.14022 Crossref / zbMATH
  3. 1970

    Jacquet and Langlands, Automorphic Forms on GL(2)

    first application

    H. Jacquet, R. P. Langlands, Automorphic Forms on GL(2), Lecture Notes in Math. 114 (1970)

    The first large-scale use of representation theory to realise the correspondence in the GL(2) case.

    Evidence (1)
    • Springer record Crossref
  4. 1977

    Drinfeld proves global Langlands for GL(2) over function fields

    key development

    V. G. Drinfeld, Proof of the global Langlands conjecture for GL(2) over a function field, Funct. Anal. Appl. 11 (1977)

    First complete proof of the conjecture in a global setting (function fields; see P057).

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper title names the conjecture Crossref
  5. 1979

    Corvallis proceedings, Automorphic forms, representations and L-functions

    institution

    Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 33 (1979)

    The 1977 summer institute volume, the first dedicated volume on the programme, including Langlands' 'Ein Maerchen'.

    Dating or attribution note

    A (volume) and B (Maerchen). MSC codes not verified.

    Evidence (2)
    • Proceedings volume zbMATH Zbl 0403.00002
    • Langlands, Ein Maerchen, in the same volume Crossref
  6. 1980

    Langlands, Base change for GL(2); Tunnell 1981

    key development

    R. P. Langlands, Base change for GL(2), Princeton (1980); J. Tunnell, Artin's conjecture for representations of octahedral type, Bull. AMS 5 (1981)

    Base change for GL(2) and its use by Tunnell to prove Artin's conjecture for octahedral representations: the first number-field use of the correspondence on an outside target problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Base change for GL(2) indexed (1980) zbMATH Open 3693585
    • Tunnell 1981 treats an Artin L-function case Crossref / zbMATH 3745316
  7. 1981

    Tunnell's octahedral Artin case

    key development

    J. Tunnell, Artin's conjecture for representations of octahedral type, Bull. AMS 5 (1981) 173–175

    Tunnell proves the octahedral Artin case, a later substantive application of the Langlands programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Bulletin of the AMS 5(2), September 1981, pages 173–175 AMS / Crossref article record
  8. 1984

    Gelbart, An elementary introduction to the Langlands program

    naming

    S. Gelbart, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 10 (1984) 177-219

    Earliest indexed title using the phrase Langlands program.

    Evidence (1)
    • Bull. AMS 10 (1984) zbMATH Zbl 0539.12008
