≈ 2021 · Differential geometry

Floer homology with Morava K-theory proves Arnold bounds in characteristic p

Floer's orbit-counting method was combined with chromatic homotopy theory to count fixed points of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms using generalised cohomology, proving Arnold's conjecture in cases classical Floer theory could not reach.
