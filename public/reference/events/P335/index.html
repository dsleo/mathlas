  2. 2000

    Topological modular functor shown universal for quantum computation

    first application

    Freedman, M. H., Larsen, M., Wang, Z., A modular functor which is universal for quantum computation, arXiv:quant-ph/0001108 (submitted 29 January 2000); Comm. Math. Phys. 227 (2002), DOI 10.1007/s002200200645

    The authors showed that the topological modular functor of Chern-Simons theory can approximate any quantum circuit computation, applying anyonic topology to the target problem of universal quantum computation.

    Evidence (3)
