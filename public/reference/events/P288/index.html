1959 · Number theory

Iwasawa theory

Class groups in infinite towers are studied with module theory over Iwasawa algebras.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1959

    Iwasawa publishes the cyclotomic-tower framework

    first statement

    K. Iwasawa, On the Theory of Cyclotomic Fields, Ann. Math. (1959)

    A public paper states the arithmetic tower framework.

    Evidence (1)
    • Iwasawa's On the Theory of Cyclotomic Fields was published in 1959. Annals original article record
  2. 1959

    Class groups in cyclotomic towers

    first application

    K. Iwasawa, On the Theory of Cyclotomic Fields, Ann. Math. (1959)

    The framework is used to study class-group growth.

    Evidence (1)
    • The paper studies class-group growth in cyclotomic towers. Annals original article record
  3. 1973

    Iwasawa extends the Z-l tower theory

    key development

    K. Iwasawa, On Z-l-extensions of algebraic number fields, Ann. Math. 98 (1973)

    A later paper develops the arithmetic theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Iwasawa, On Z_l-extensions of algebraic number fields, Ann. of Math. 98 (1973) Crossref record
  4. 1977

    Iwasawa theory appears in a paper title

    naming

    J. Coates, p-adic L-functions and Iwasawa theory (1977)

    The named title provides a reproducible institutional anchor.

    Evidence (1)
    • Earliest zbMATH title with the name: Coates, p-adic L-functions and Iwasawa theory, Durham symposium 1977 zbMATH 0393.12027
