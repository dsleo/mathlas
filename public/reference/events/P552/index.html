2011 · Combinatorics

Arc spaces of singularities and partition identities

Arc spaces of simple singularities were found to be described by partition identities, one of several fields where the Rogers-Ramanujan identities reappear and are re-proved with local tools.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2011

    Arc-space/partition correspondence posted

    first statement

    C. Bruschek, H. Mourtada and J. Schepers, Arc Spaces and Rogers-Ramanujan Identities, arXiv:1101.4950 (2011)

    The paper links Hilbert-Poincare series of arc spaces to partition generating functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted January 2011 arXiv
  2. 2011

    Double-point arc space yields Rogers-Ramanujan identity

    first application

    C. Bruschek, H. Mourtada and J. Schepers, Arc Spaces and Rogers-Ramanujan Identities, DMTCS Proceedings AO (2011)

    The 2011 proceeding applies the arc-space series of a double point to integer partitions without equal or consecutive parts.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published 2011 proceeding and abstract identify the double-point application DMTCS / Crossref
  3. 2020

    Differential-ideal Groebner bases extend the partition connection

    key development

    P. Afsharijoo and H. Mourtada, Partition identities and application to infinite dimensional Groebner basis and viceversa, arXiv:2006.09147 (2020)

    A later paper relates differential-ideal Groebner bases to Rogers-Ramanujan-type partition identities.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted June 2020 arXiv
  4. 2020

    Afsharijoo-Mourtada: partition identities and Gröbner bases

    consolidation

    Afsharijoo, P., Mourtada, H., Partition Identities and Application to Infinite-Dimensional Gröbner Basis and Vice Versa, in: Arc Schemes and Singularities (World Scientific, 2020)

    An expository chapter in a volume on arc schemes that relates partition identities to infinite-dimensional Gröbner bases.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref chapter record, 2020, in the Arc Schemes and Singularities volume Crossref
  5. 2022

    Mohsen-Mourtada: numerators of Rogers-Ramanujan identities

    key development

    Mohsen, O., Mourtada, H., Neighborly partitions and the numerators of Rogers-Ramanujan identities, International Journal of Number Theory (2022)

    Extends the arc-space partition correspondence to the numerators in the Rogers-Ramanujan identities.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, IJNT (2022) Crossref
