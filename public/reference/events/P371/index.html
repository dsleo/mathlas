1899 · Differential geometry

Cartan exterior differential calculus

Cartan developed the exterior derivative and used it with Pfaffian systems and moving frames to unify differential geometry with Lie theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1844

    Grassmann's Ausdehnungslehre

    precursor

    H. Grassmann, Die lineale Ausdehnungslehre, Leipzig 1844

    Grassmann's exterior product is the algebra underlying differential forms.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive item: Grassmann's Ausdehnungslehre, 1844. Internet Archive 10081845bsb
    • MacTutor biography of Grassmann. MacTutor, biography of Grassmann
  2. 1877

    Frobenius on the Pfaffian problem

    precursor

    G. Frobenius, Über das Pfaffsche Problem, J. reine angew. Math. 82 (1877)

    Frobenius gives the algebraic treatment of Pfaff's problem that Cartan's 1899 paper recasts with differential forms.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor biography of Frobenius, who treated the Pfaff problem. MacTutor, biography of Frobenius
  3. 1899

    Exterior derivative in its current form

    first statement

    Cartan, E., 'Sur certaines expressions différentielles et le problème de Pfaff', Ann. Sci. ENS 16 (1899)

    Cartan introduces the exterior derivative of differential forms and applies it to the Pfaff problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: title and year 1899, Annales ENS Crossref, DOI 10.24033/asens.467
    • Exterior derivative first described in its current form by Cartan in 1899 Wikipedia, Exterior derivative (saved lead)
  4. 1901

    Integration of Pfaff systems of character two

    key development

    Cartan, E., 'Sur l'intégration de certains systèmes de Pfaff de caractère deux', Bull. SMF (1901)

    Extends the Pfaff-system method to systems of character two, one step in building the theory of exterior differential systems.

    Dating or attribution note

    Assessed as a development step from the bibliographic record only; full text not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Cartan, Bull. SMF, year 1901 Crossref, DOI 10.24033/bsmf.647
  5. 1922

    Cartan's Leçons sur les invariants intégraux

    key development

    É. Cartan, Leçons sur les invariants intégraux, Paris 1922

    Cartan's course uses exterior forms systematically for integral invariants.

    Evidence (1)
    • Jahrbuch record: Leçons sur les invariants intégraux, Paris, Hermann, 1922. zbMATH record 48.0538.02
  6. 1934

    Kähler's differential systems

    key development

    E. Kähler, Einführung in die Theorie der Systeme von Differentialgleichungen, Leipzig 1934

    Kähler extends Cartan's exterior systems (Cartan-Kähler theory).

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Kähler, Einführung in die Theorie der Systeme von Differentialgleichungen, 1934. zbMATH record 0011.16103
  7. 1945

    Cartan's exterior differential systems

    consolidation

    É. Cartan, Les systèmes différentiels extérieurs et leurs applications géométriques, Paris 1945

    Book-length treatment of exterior differential systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Cartan, Exterior differential systems and their geometric applications, Actualités Sci. Ind. 994, 1945. zbMATH record 0063.00734
  8. Date unknown

    Pfaff's problem on differential forms

    precursor

    Pfaffian expressions and the Pfaff problem were the setting in which Cartan later worked; the 19th-century origin is not dated here.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cartan's work revives the Pfaff problem for differential expressions Wikipedia, Exterior derivative (wikitext, saved)
