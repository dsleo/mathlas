1927 · Algebra

Group representations in quantum mechanics

Symmetry groups of atoms and spectra are analysed by decomposing the state space into irreducible representations, which also drives the general theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1896

    Frobenius' group characters (1896)

    precursor

    G. Frobenius, Ueber Gruppencharaktere, Berl. Ber. 1896, 985-1021

    Frobenius developed the character theory of finite groups before any physical use, the mathematical base of the later method.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor says Frobenius and Netto carried group theory forward; no year given on the saved page MacTutor Development of group theory topic (saved text)
    • zbMATH 27.0092.01: Frobenius, 'Ueber Gruppencharaktere', Berl. Ber. 1896, 985-1021. zbMATH Open, document 27.0092.01
  2. 1927

    Weyl's quantum mechanics and group theory

    first statement

    H. Weyl, 'Quantenmechanik und Gruppentheorie', Zeitschrift fuer Physik 46, 1927

    Weyl stated that symmetry groups and their representations are the proper tool for quantum mechanics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Weyl 1927 Zeitschrift fuer Physik article exists Crossref DOI 10.1007/bf02055756
  3. 1927

    Wigner's term structure of atomic spectra

    first application

    E. Wigner, 'Einige Folgerungen aus der Schroedingerschen Theorie fuer die Termstrukturen', Zeitschrift fuer Physik 43, 1927

    Wigner used representations of the symmetry group to classify atomic energy levels, a purposeful application to spectra.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wigner 1927 Zeitschrift fuer Physik article exists Crossref DOI 10.1007/bf01397327
    • MacTutor Wigner biography says he was told to study group theory for quantum mechanics MacTutor Wigner biography
  4. 1928

    Weyl's Gruppentheorie und Quantenmechanik

    consolidation

    H. Weyl, Gruppentheorie und Quantenmechanik, S. Hirzel, Leipzig, 1928

    Weyl's book was the first systematic treatment of group theory for quantum physics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library lists the book with first publication 1928 Open Library search
    • A 1929 review of the book in Angewandte Chemie exists Crossref DOI 10.1002/ange.19290421908
  5. 1931

    Wigner's Gruppentheorie und ihre Anwendung auf die Quantenmechanik der Atomspektren

    key development

    E. Wigner, Gruppentheorie und ihre Anwendung auf die Quantenmechanik der Atomspektren, Springer/Vieweg, 1931

    Wigner's monograph extended the method to atomic spectra and made group-theoretic quantum mechanics standard in physics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Springer book chapters 1931 exist Crossref DOI 10.1007/978-3-663-02555-9
