1870 · Topology

Betti numbers and early combinatorial topology

Riemann's connectivity of surfaces was extended by Betti to higher-dimensional spaces via numerical invariants.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1847

    Listing's Vorstudien zur Topologie

    precursor

    Listing, Vorstudien zur Topologie, Göttingen, 1847

    Listing's study used the word topology in a title; he had used it in letters for ten years.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor says Listing was the first to use the word topology and wrote the paper in 1847 MacTutor, A history of Topology
    • Internet Archive holds a copy of Vorstudien zur Topologie dated 1848 archive.org vorstudienzurto00listgoog
  2. 1847

    Kirchhoff's cyclomatic number

    precursor

    Kirchhoff, 1847 network paper (cyclomatic number)

    For a graph the first Betti number equals the cyclomatic number, a quantity that Kirchhoff had introduced in his circuit analysis before Betti's paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia states the cyclomatic number was named by Kirchhoff before Betti's paper English Wikipedia, Betti number
  3. 1851

    Riemann studies connectivity of surfaces

    precursor

    Riemann, doctoral dissertation 1851

    Riemann examined how many cuts are needed to make a surface simply connected, the first numerical measure of connectivity that Betti extended.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor says Listing was not the first to examine connectivity: Riemann studied it in 1851 and 1857 MacTutor, A history of Topology
  4. 1870–1871

    Betti's spaces of any number of dimensions

    first statement

    Betti, Sopra gli spazi di un numero qualunque di dimensioni, Annali di Matematica pura ed applicata (2) 4, 140-158, 1870-71

    Betti extended Listing's and Riemann's connectivity ideas to n-dimensional spaces and defined the numbers later named after him.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Betti, Sopra gli spazi di un numero qualunque di dimensioni, Annali 4, 140-158 (issued 1870) Crossref 10.1007/bf02420029
    • MacTutor dates the paper to 1871 and says it contained what we now call the Betti numbers MacTutor biography of Betti
  5. 1895

    Poincaré's Analysis situs

    consolidation

    Poincaré, Analysis situs, Journal de l'École Polytechnique, 1895

    Poincaré put connectivity on a rigorous basis, introduced homology and gave the Betti numbers a precise definition; the name 'Betti numbers' is credited to him.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH lists Poincaré, Analysis situs, 1895 zbMATH 2678889
    • MacTutor says Poincaré's 1895 papers made connectivity rigorous and introduced homology and the Betti numbers precisely MacTutor, A history of Topology
    • MacTutor's Betti biography says Poincaré named the numbers; Wikipedia agrees MacTutor biography of Betti
  6. 1920–1926

    Noether's modern formulation

    key development

    Emmy Noether, work on Betti groups in the 1920s

    The modern formulation of Betti numbers as ranks of homology groups is credited to Noether.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia attributes the modern formulation to Emmy Noether and points to her second epoch (1920-1926) English Wikipedia, Betti number
