2013 · Topology

Triangulation conjecture disproved via Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer theory

Equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology of homology 3-spheres, via Galewski-Stern reduction, showed that manifolds of every dimension at least five need not be triangulable.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1978

    Galewski-Stern classification of triangulations

    precursor

    D. Galewski, R. Stern, Simplicial triangulations of topological manifolds, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 32, Part 2 (1978) 7-12; T. Matumoto, Triangulation of manifolds, ibid. 3-6

    Galewski and Stern, and independently Matumoto, relate the triangulability of high-dimensional topological manifolds to homology 3-spheres of Rokhlin invariant one.

    Dating or attribution note

    The arXiv abstract of Manolescu's paper says that by previous work of Galewski-Stern and Matumoto, the absence of homology spheres Y of Rokhlin invariant one with Y # Y bounding an acyclic smooth 4-manifold implies non-triangulable high-dimensional manifolds. The 1978 papers themselves were not read. The detailed classification is Galewski-Stern, Ann. of Math. 111 (1980) 1-34.

    Evidence (4)
    • Galewski-Stern 1978 paper exists (title, venue, year) Crossref
    • Triangulation conjecture is the background of the high-dimensional disproof Wikipedia: Triangulation (topology)
    • zbMATH: Galewski, Stern, Simplicial triangulations of topological manifolds, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 32 (1978) 7-12; Matumoto, Triangulation of manifolds, 3-6. zbMATH Open, Zbl 0511.57015 and Zbl 0509.57010
    • Manolescu states that the non-existence result implies non-triangulable manifolds by previous work of Galewski-Stern and Matumoto. arXiv:1303.2354 (abstract), Manolescu, Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and the Triangulation Conjecture
  2. 1980

    Galewski-Stern classification of simplicial triangulations

    precursor

    D. Galewski, R. Stern, Classification of simplicial triangulations of topological manifolds, Ann. of Math. 111 (1980) 1-34

    The full classification result that reduces the triangulation conjecture in dimension at least five to a question on homology 3-spheres.

    Evidence (1)
    • Classification of Simplicial Triangulations of Topological Manifolds, Ann. Math. 111 (1980). Crossref record
  3. 2003

    Seiberg-Witten Floer stable homotopy type

    precursor

    Manolescu, Seiberg-Witten-Floer stable homotopy type of three-manifolds with b1=0, Geometry & Topology 7 (2003)

    Manolescu constructs the Seiberg-Witten Floer stable homotopy type that the later Pin(2)-equivariant argument uses as a tool.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Geometry & Topology 7 (2003) 889-932. Crossref
    • Wikipedia: Manolescu's thesis topic was a spectrum-valued TQFT from the Seiberg-Witten equations. Wikipedia: Ciprian Manolescu
  4. 2013

    Preprint disproving the triangulation conjecture in dimension 5 and higher

    first statement

    arXiv:1303.2354 (abstract), Manolescu, Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and the Triangulation Conjecture (submitted 10 March 2013)

    Manolescu releases a preprint disproving the triangulation conjecture for manifolds of dimension five and higher.

  5. 2013

    Pin(2) Floer tool applied to triangulability

    first application

    Manolescu, Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and the triangulation conjecture (2013 preprint)

    The Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer tool is applied to the non-triangulable homology-sphere argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • Abstract: defines Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and applies it to Rokhlin-invariant-one homology spheres. arXiv:1303.2354 (abstract), Manolescu, Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and the Triangulation Conjecture
