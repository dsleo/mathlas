2013 · Topology

Triangulation conjecture disproved via Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer theory

Equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology of homology 3-spheres, via Galewski-Stern reduction, showed that manifolds of every dimension at least five need not be triangulable.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1978

    Galewski-Stern classification of triangulations

    precursor

    Galewski, Stern, Simplicial triangulations of topological manifolds, Proc. Symp. Pure Math. 32 (1978)

    Galewski and Stern open the classification of triangulations of topological manifolds that frames the triangulation question.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content of the reduction (triangulability versus homology spheres) was not checked on a fetched text; Galewski-Stern 1980 Annals paper (DOI 10.2307/1971215) also exists. Matumoto's work not checked.

    Evidence (2)
    • Galewski-Stern 1978 paper exists (title, venue, year) Crossref

  2. 2003

    Seiberg-Witten Floer stable homotopy type

    precursor

    Manolescu, Seiberg-Witten-Floer stable homotopy type of three-manifolds with b1=0, Geometry & Topology 7 (2003)

    Manolescu constructs the Seiberg-Witten Floer stable homotopy type that the later Pin(2)-equivariant argument uses as a tool.

    Evidence (2)
    • G&T 2003 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Manolescu's thesis topic: spectrum-valued TQFT from Seiberg-Witten equations Wikipedia: Ciprian Manolescu
  3. 2013

    Preprint disproving the triangulation conjecture in dimension 5 and higher

    first statement

    Manolescu, Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and the triangulation conjecture, preprint (2013)

    Manolescu releases a preprint disproving the triangulation conjecture for manifolds of dimension five and higher.

  4. 2013

    Pin(2) Floer tool applied to triangulability

    first application

    Manolescu, Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and the triangulation conjecture (2013 preprint)

    The Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer tool is applied to the non-triangulable homology-sphere argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 2013 preprint applies the Floer tool to triangulability Wikipedia: Ciprian Manolescu
  5. 2016

    Published proof in JAMS

    key development

    Manolescu, Pin(2)-equivariant Seiberg-Witten Floer homology and the Triangulation Conjecture, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 29 (2016)

    The disproof appears in the Journal of the American Mathematical Society, the peer-reviewed version of the argument.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal of the American Mathematical Society paper, author and volume Crossref
    • Disproof of triangulation conjecture for dimension 5 and higher Wikipedia: Ciprian Manolescu
  6. 2025

    Manolescu's survey 'Triangulation and Floer homology'

    consolidation

    C. Manolescu, Triangulation and Floer homology, Proceedings of the International Conference of Basic Science, 2025

    A survey by the author of the Pin(2)-equivariant result, placing the triangulation disproof within Floer theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    A survey by the same author as the key development, so it is not an independent consolidation. Survey and proceedings type from the record; content not read. No earlier survey of this result was found.
