1941 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Gelfand theory of Banach algebras

Maximal ideals of normed rings gave a topological representation.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1938

    Gelfand's D.Sc. thesis on commutative normed rings

    precursor

    Gelfand's thesis developed the theory of commutative normed rings and maximal ideals.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gelfand's theory of commutative normed rings was developed in his D.Sc. thesis submitted in 1938 MacTutor, Israel Gelfand biography
  2. 1941

    Gelfand's 'Normierte Ringe'

    first statement

    Gelfand, Normierte Ringe, Mat. Sbornik (Nov. Ser.) 9(51) (1941) 3-24

    Gelfand represented commutative Banach algebras through their maximal ideals, the first public statement of the theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Gelfand, Normierte Ringe, Mat. Sbornik Nov. Ser. 9(51) (1941) 3-24 zbMATH record (1941, pp. 3-24)
    • Gelfand brought to light the fundamental concept of a maximal ideal in normed rings MacTutor, Israel Gelfand biography
  3. 1943

    Gelfand-Naimark embedding of C*-algebras

    key development

    Gel'fand, Naimark, On the imbedding of normed rings into the ring of operators in Hilbert space, Mat. Sb. Nov. Ser. 12(54) (1943) 197-213

    Gelfand and Naimark embedded normed *-algebras isometrically into operators on Hilbert space, extending the theory beyond commutative algebras.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gel'fand, Naimark, On the imbedding of normed rings into the ring of operators in Hilbert space, Mat. Sb. Nov. Ser. 12(54) (1943) 197-213 zbMATH record (1943, pp. 197-213)
  4. 1956

    Naimark's Normed Rings (Russian edition)

    consolidation

    Naimark, Normed Rings, Noordhoff (1959); Russian edition 1956

    A monograph on normed rings and Banach algebras that systematised the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Naimark, Normirovannye koltsa (Normed rings), 1956; English edition Normed Rings, Noordhoff (1959) Open Library search record (first publish year 1956 for the Russian edition; English 1959)
