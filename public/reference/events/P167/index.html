1839 · Geometry

Pluecker: line geometry and algebraic curves

Lines became elements of space and numerical characteristics of curves were related.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1831

    Homogeneous line coordinates in the plane

    precursor

    J. Pluecker, Analytisch-geometrische Entwickelungen, vol. 2, 1831

    The plane analytic geometry already treats conics as envelopes of lines using line coordinates, the dual viewpoint that later carries over to space.

    Evidence (1)
    • Biography describes volume 2 as using homogeneous line coordinates and conics as envelopes. MacTutor: Plucker biography
  2. 1839

    Theorie der algebraischen Curven and the Pluecker equations

    first statement

    J. Pluecker, Theorie der algebraischen Curven, Bonn, 1839

    Pluecker studies singular and infinite points of plane curves and relates the order and class of a curve in a set of equations now named after him.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive catalogue lists the 1839 book. Internet Archive scan
    • Biography names the Pluecker equations relating order and class in this work. MacTutor: Plucker biography
  3. 1865

    On a new geometry of space

    key development

    J. Pluecker, On a new geometry of space, Phil. Trans. R. Soc. 155 (1865) 725-791

    Pluecker presents the straight line as the element of space, giving line geometry in the Royal Society's journal.

    Dating or attribution note

    Whether the 1846 System der Geometrie des Raumes (archive.org item systemdergeometr00pluoft) introduced line coordinates in space was not verified, so no earlier first_statement is claimed for line geometry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal and pages 725-791 confirmed, dated 1865. Crossref record 10.1098/rstl.1865.0017
    • Biography says his research returned to mathematics in 1865 and describes the lines-as-points idea of his final memoir. MacTutor: Plucker biography
  4. 1868

    Neue Geometrie des Raumes (part 1; part 2 by Klein 1869)

    consolidation

    J. Pluecker, Neue Geometrie des Raumes, gegruendet auf die Betrachtung der geraden Linie als Raumelement, Leipzig, 1868

    The posthumous book treats the line as a space element, noting the four-dimensional space of lines; Klein completed the second part from Pluecker's plan.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive catalogue lists the 1868 book. Internet Archive catalogue
    • Biography says part 1 appeared in 1868, Klein finished part 2 in 1869, and quotes Gindikin on the four-dimensional space of lines. MacTutor: Plucker biography
