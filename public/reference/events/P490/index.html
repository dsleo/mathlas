2001 · Topology

Heegaard Floer homology

Holomorphic-disc counts in symmetric products of Heegaard surfaces give invariants of three-manifolds and knots with strong genus bounds.

Also known as: Heegaard Floer theory

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2001

    Heegaard Floer invariants introduced

    first statement

    P. Ozsváth and Z. Szabó, Holomorphic disks and topological invariants for closed three-manifolds, arXiv:math/0101206 (2001)

    The public preprint defines three-manifold invariants from Heegaard splittings and holomorphic disks.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the preprint to January 2001 and describes the invariants. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2004

    Holomorphic disks and three-manifold invariants: properties and applications

    key development

    Ozsvath, Szabo, Holomorphic disks and three-manifold invariants: properties and applications, Ann. of Math. (2004)

    Establishes the basic properties of Heegaard Floer invariants and computes them for key classes of three-manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2004 Annals paper by Ozsvath and Szabo on holomorphic disks and three-manifold invariants zbMATH Open record
  3. 2006

    Lectures on Heegaard Floer homology

    consolidation

    Ozsvath, Szabo, Lectures on Heegaard Floer Homology, Clay Math. Proc. (2006)

    A lecture-notes monograph that presents Heegaard Floer homology as a self-contained theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2006 lecture notes by Ozsvath and Szabo on Heegaard Floer homology zbMATH Open record
