1899 · Number theory

p-adic numbers

Hensel treated integers like power series in a prime, giving p-adic numbers, and Kuerschak and Hasse later turned this into valuation theory and local-global principles.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1899

    Hensel's new foundation of algebraic number theory

    first statement

    Hensel, K., Ueber eine neue Begruendung der Theorie der algebraischen Zahlen, Jahresbericht der Deutschen Mathematiker-Vereinigung, 1899

    Hensel introduces p-adic numbers, treating integers like power series in a prime p.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper Ueber eine neue Begruendung der Theorie der algebraischen Zahlen, Jahresbericht DMV 1899, pages 83-88 zbMATH record, Zbl 30.0096.03
    • MacTutor says Hensel invented p-adic numbers in 1897 from Weierstrass power-series methods MacTutor biography of Hensel
  2. 1908

    Hensel's Theorie der algebraischen Zahlen

    consolidation

    Hensel, K., Theorie der algebraischen Zahlen, Leipzig, 1908

    Hensel's monograph gives the first systematic treatment of the p-adic theory of algebraic numbers.

    Evidence (1)
    • Theorie der algebraischen Zahlen published 1908 by Kurt Hensel Open Library search record
  3. 1913

    Kurschak's general field theory

    key development

    Kurschak, J., Ueber Limesbildung und allgemeine Koerpertheorie, Journal fuer die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 1913

    Kurschak gives an abstract axiomatic treatment of absolute values on fields, a step toward valuation theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    The axiomatic content is from general knowledge of the field; the paper text was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper appears in Crelle's Journal in 1913 Crossref record, DOI 10.1515/crll.1913.142.211
  4. 1916

    Ostrowski's classification of absolute values

    key development

    Ostrowski, A., Ueber einige Loesungen der Funktionalgleichung psi(x) psi(y) = psi(xy), Acta Mathematica, 1916

    Ostrowski's paper is the source of the classical result that every absolute value on the rationals is either archimedean or p-adic.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper appears in Acta Mathematica in 1916 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf02422947
  5. 1923

    Hasse's local-global principle for quadratic forms

    first application

    Hasse, H., Ueber die Darstellbarkeit von Zahlen durch quadratische Formen im Koerper der rationalen Zahlen, Journal fuer die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 1923

    Hasse uses p-adic and real completions to decide representability of rational numbers by quadratic forms, a purposeful application of p-adic methods.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appears in Crelle's Journal in 1923 Crossref record, DOI 10.1515/crll.1923.152.129
    • MacTutor says Hasse discovered the local-global principle in October 1920 and it forms part of his thesis MacTutor biography of Hasse
