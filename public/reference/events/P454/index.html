1918 · Mathematical physics

Noether's theorems (symmetries and conservation laws)

Hilbert's variational derivation of gravitation led to identities that Noether tied to symmetries, giving her 1918 theorem.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1915

    Hilbert's variational route to gravitation

    precursor

    Hilbert, Die Grundlagen der Physik, 1915

    Noether began her invariance work in 1915 while helping Klein and Hilbert with general relativity.

    Dating or attribution note

    Hilbert's paper itself was not verified; the milestone records the setting.

    Evidence (1)
    • Noether's work on the invariance theorem began in 1915 helping Klein and Hilbert. English Wikipedia, Noether's theorem
  2. 1918

    Noether's theorem on invariant variational problems

    first statement

    Noether, 'Invariante Variationsprobleme', Nachrichten der Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen (1918)

    Noether proves that each continuous symmetry of an action gives a conservation law, and the converse relation for gauge-type symmetries.

    Evidence (1)
    • Noether's 1918 paper on invariant variational problems, cited in the MacTutor Noether biography MacTutor: Noether biography
  3. 1918

    Same 1918 paper applied to energy in general relativity

    first application

    Noether, 'Invariante Variationsprobleme' (1918)

    The 1918 paper also applies the invariance results to energy-momentum conservation in general relativity; it plays both roles.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same work under two roles, as allowed by the note rule. The content of the application was not checked against the paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1918 paper cited; general relativity application recorded in the Noether biography (not read in full) MacTutor: Noether biography
  4. 1921

    Bessel-Hagen extends the theorem

    key development

    Bessel-Hagen, Ueber die Erhaltungssaetze der Elektrodynamik, Math. Ann. 84 (1921)

    Applies and extends Noether's theorem to the conservation laws of electrodynamics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1921. Crossref
  5. 1951

    Hill's review in Reviews of Modern Physics

    recognition

    E. L. Hill, Hamilton's principle and the conservation theorems of mathematical physics, Rev. Mod. Phys. 23 (1951)

    Brings the theorem to physicists writing in English.

    Dating or attribution note

    The role is inferred from the title and background knowledge.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1951. Crossref
  6. 1971

    English translation by Tavel

    key development

    Noether, Invariant variation problems (transl. M. A. Tavel), Transport Theory and Statistical Physics 1 (1971)

    Makes the 1918 paper widely readable.

    Evidence (1)
    • Translation recorded in Crossref, 1971. Crossref
  7. 1980

    Olver's Applications of Lie Groups to Differential Equations

    consolidation

    Peter J. Olver, Applications of Lie Groups to Differential Equations (first edition 1980 per library record)

    Textbook treatment of Noether's theorem within symmetry methods.

    Evidence (1)
    • Work first published 1980 per library record. Open Library
  8. 2011

    Kosmann-Schwarzbach's history and treatment of the Noether theorems

    consolidation

    Kosmann-Schwarzbach, The Noether Theorems (Springer, 2011)

    The 2011 monograph treats both Noether theorems and their history, the first book-length treatment found.

    Evidence (1)
    • 2011 Springer record Crossref record
