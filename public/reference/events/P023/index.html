1949 · Number theory

Weil conjectures

Counting points on varieties over finite fields is predicted to behave like Betti numbers, and a cohomology theory built for that purpose proves it.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1949

    Weil: numbers of solutions of equations in finite fields

    first statement

    Weil A., Numbers of solutions of equations in finite fields, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 55, 497-508, 1949

    Weil stated the conjectures on the zeta function of varieties over finite fields, with the analogy to topology.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Bull. AMS 55, 497-508, 1949 Crossref
    • MacTutor says Weil raised the conjectures in 1949 MacTutor, Andre Weil biography
  2. 1960

    Dwork: rationality of the zeta function

    key development

    Dwork B., On the rationality of the zeta function of an algebraic variety, Amer. J. Math. 82, 631-648, 1960

    Dwork proved the rationality part of the Weil conjectures, using p-adic methods.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Amer. J. Math. 82, 631-648, 1960 Crossref
    • Wikipedia says the rationality was proved first by Dwork using p-adic methods Wikipedia, Weil conjectures
  3. 1965

    Grothendieck: cohomology proof of the functional equation (Lefschetz formula)

    key development

    Grothendieck A., Formule de Lefschetz et rationalite des fonctions L, Seminaire Bourbaki 279, 1964/65

    Grothendieck's cohomological framework gave the functional equation of the zeta function, using the Lefschetz trace formula.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia cites Grothendieck (1965) for the functional equation Wikipedia, Weil conjectures
  4. 1968

    Kleiman titles a survey with the Weil conjectures name

    naming

    Kleiman, S. L., Algebraic cycles and the Weil conjectures, 1968

    Earliest zbMATH title found that uses the Weil conjectures name.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: "Algebraic cycles and the Weil conjectures", Kleiman, 1968 zbMATH Open doc 3315839
  5. 1974

    Deligne: the Riemann hypothesis analogue for Weil conjectures

    key development

    Deligne P., La conjecture de Weil I, Publ. Math. IHES 43, 273-307, 1974

    Deligne proved the analogue of the Riemann hypothesis for varieties over finite fields, completing the Weil conjectures.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Publ. Math. IHES 43, 273-307, 1974 Crossref
    • Wikipedia says the Riemann hypothesis analogue was proved by Deligne Wikipedia, Weil conjectures
  6. Date unknown

    Gauss's antecedent on counting points on curves

    precursor

    Wikipedia names Gauss's work as the earliest antecedent, in the section of his Disquisitiones on congruences.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia says the earliest antecedent is by Gauss Wikipedia, Weil conjectures (Background)
