1989 · Topology

Contact topology and the h-principle (Eliashberg)

Overtwisted contact structures are classified by homotopy theory, separating flexible from rigid phenomena.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1969

    Gromov's stable maps of foliations

    precursor

    Gromov, Stable mappings of foliations into manifolds, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR (1969)

    Early h-principle-type statement for foliations, the origin of the homotopy principle used by Eliashberg.

    Evidence (2)
    • Math. USSR Izvestiya 1969 paper (DOI 10.1070/im1969v003n04abeh000796) Crossref / publisher record
    • The h-principle was introduced by Gromov in the late 1960s and early 1970s MacTutor Eliashberg biography
  2. 1971

    Contact structures in dimension 3

    precursor

    Martinet, Formes de contact sur les varietes de dimension 3, Lecture Notes in Math. (1971)

    Early work on contact structures on 3-manifolds, the setting of the classification.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1971 (DOI 10.1007/bfb0068901) Crossref / publisher record
  3. 1985

    Pseudo-holomorphic curves

    precursor

    Gromov, Pseudo holomorphic curves in symplectic manifolds, Invent. Math. (1985)

    Introduces holomorphic-curve methods for symplectic rigidity, the rigid counterpart to the flexible h-principle.

    Evidence (1)
    • Invent. Math. 1985 paper (DOI 10.1007/bf01388806) Crossref / publisher record
  4. 1989

    Classification of overtwisted contact structures

    first statement

    Eliashberg, Classification of overtwisted contact structures on 3-manifolds, Invent. Math. (1989)

    Classifies overtwisted contact structures on 3-manifolds by homotopy theory, showing they are flexible.

    Evidence (2)
    • Invent. Math. 1989 paper (DOI 10.1007/bf01393840) Crossref / publisher record
    • Eliashberg's work on contact and h-principle topology MacTutor Eliashberg biography
  5. 1989

    h-principle applied to contact topology

    first application

    Eliashberg, Classification of overtwisted contact structures on 3-manifolds, Invent. Math. (1989)

    The same paper is the first use of the h-principle to classify contact structures.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1989 paper Crossref / publisher record
  6. 2002

    Eliashberg-Mishachev monograph

    consolidation

    Eliashberg, Mishachev, Introduction to the h-principle, Graduate Studies in Mathematics (2002)

    Book-length treatment of the h-principle and its applications to contact and symplectic geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • AMS Graduate Studies 48 (DOI 10.1090/gsm/048) Crossref / publisher record
