1945 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Distributions

Generalised functions were defined as continuous linear functionals on test functions, unifying Dirac delta, weak derivatives and Fourier transforms.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1927

    Dirac introduces the delta function

    precursor

    Dirac, P. A. M., The physical interpretation of the quantum dynamics, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 113 (1927) 621-641

    Dirac uses the delta function as a physical tool in quantum mechanics, before any rigorous theory of such objects existed.

    Evidence (2)
    • Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 113, pp. 621-641, issued 1 January 1927. Crossref DOI 10.1098/rspa.1927.0012
    • Wikipedia: Dirac introduced the delta function in a 1927 paper, popularised in his 1930 book. English Wikipedia, Dirac delta function (History)
  2. 1930

    Dirac's delta function in quantum mechanics

    precursor

    Dirac, P. A. M., The Principles of Quantum Mechanics, Oxford: Milford (1930), zbMATH 56.0745.05

    The delta function is used as a physical tool, before a rigorous theory of distributions existed.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'The principles of quantum mechanics', Milford 1930 zbMATH API (Zbl 56.0745.05)
    • Wikipedia: distributions are used in physics and engineering, e.g. the Dirac delta function Wikipedia: Distribution (mathematical analysis)
  3. 1936

    Sobolev's generalised solutions of the Cauchy problem

    precursor

    Soboleff, S., Methode nouvelle a resoudre le probleme de Cauchy pour les equations lineaires hyperboliques normales, Rec. Math. Moscou 1 (1936) 39-72, zbMATH 62.0568.01

    Uses generalised derivatives to treat the Cauchy problem for hyperbolic equations, the first purposeful use of the idea.

    Dating or attribution note

    The link between this 1936 paper and the later theory is from the Wikipedia history; the paper's content was not read. Reclassified from first_application to precursor: it predates this event's first statement (1945) and belongs to the wider field.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH record: Sobolev, Methode nouvelle a resoudre le probleme de Cauchy, Rec. Math. Moscou (1936) zbMATH API (Zbl 62.0568.01)
    • Wikipedia: the ideas began in Sobolev's work on second-order hyperbolic PDEs Wikipedia: Distribution (mathematical analysis)
    • MacTutor: Sobolev's generalised functions concern generalised solutions of boundary value problems MacTutor Biography: Sobolev
  4. 1945

    Schwartz's generalisation of the notion of function

    first statement

    Schwartz, L., Generalisation de la notion de fonction, de derivation, de transformation de Fourier et applications mathematiques et physiques, Ann. Univ. Grenoble 21 (1945) 57-74, zbMATH 0060.27504

    The first public statement of generalised functions as an extension of functions and derivatives.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: Schwartz, Generalisation de la notion de fonction ..., Grenoble (1945) zbMATH API (Zbl 0060.27504)
    • MacTutor names 'Generalisation de la notion de fonction, de derivation, de transformation de Fourier et applications mathematiques et physiques' as the first publication presenting Schwartz's ideas, but dates it 1948 (zbMATH and the journal volume give 1945). MacTutor Biography: Laurent Schwartz
  5. 1948

    Schwartz's title 'Theorie des distributions'

    naming

    Schwartz, L., Theorie des distributions et transformation de Fourier, Ann. Univ. Grenoble (2) 23 (1948) 7-24, zbMATH 0030.12601

    The first title in the searched index that uses the term 'distributions' for the theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Theorie des distributions et transformation de Fourier', 1948 zbMATH API (Zbl 0030.12601)
    • Wikipedia: Schwartz introduced the term 'distribution' by analogy with electrical charge Wikipedia: Distribution (mathematical analysis)
  6. 1950

    Schwartz's Theorie des distributions, Tome I

    consolidation

    Schwartz, L., Theorie des distributions, Tome I, Actualites Scientifiques et Industrielles 1091, Hermann (1950), zbMATH 0037.07301

    The first systematic treatise of the theory, which established distributions as a field.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH record: 'Theorie des distributions. Tome I', Hermann 1950 zbMATH API (Zbl 0037.07301)
    • Open Library: Theorie des distributions, Hermann, first published 1950 Open Library search
    • MacTutor: Schwartz's Vancouver lectures of 1949 became the two-volume treatise Theorie des distributions (1950, 1951). MacTutor Biography: Laurent Schwartz
