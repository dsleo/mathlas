2015 · Combinatorics

Erdos discrepancy problem solved by multiplicative-function methods

Tao used Polymath5's reduction and Fourier/entropy methods for multiplicative functions to settle the Erdos discrepancy problem; SAT solvers had done the c=2 case earlier.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2010

    Polymath5 publicly explores discrepancy

    precursor

    T. Gowers, Erdős discrepancy problem as a forthcoming Polymath project (2010)

    A public research discussion develops directions that precede Tao's proof.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gowers' January 2010 blog post proposing EDP as a Polymath project discusses multiplicative sequences Gowers's Weblog
  2. 2014

    SAT resolves a finite discrepancy case

    precursor

    B. Konev and A. Lisitsa, A SAT attack on the Erdős discrepancy conjecture (2014)

    A finite computational result precedes the general theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Konev and Lisitsa, A SAT attack on the Erdos discrepancy conjecture, SAT 2014, LNCS Crossref record
  3. 2015

    Tao posts the full discrepancy proof

    first statement

    T. Tao, The Erdős discrepancy problem, arXiv:1509.05363 (2015)

    The public preprint states and proves the unbounded-discrepancy result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Tao released the full proof as a public preprint on 17 September 2015. arXiv preprint record
  4. 2015

    Multiplicative-function method closes the problem

    first application

    T. Tao, The Erdős discrepancy problem, arXiv:1509.05363 (2015)

    Multiplicative and Fourier-analytic tools solve the target discrepancy problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • The proof uses Polymath5 reductions and multiplicative-function methods to settle discrepancy. arXiv preprint record
  5. 2016

    Logarithmically averaged Chowla and Elliott conjectures

    key development

    Tao, T., The logarithmically averaged Chowla and Elliott conjectures for two-point correlations, Forum Math. Pi 4 (2016) e8, DOI 10.1017/fmp.2016.6

    Extends the entropy-based method of the discrepancy proof to two-point correlations of multiplicative functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: Forum Math. Pi 4 (2016) e8 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  6. 2018

    Survey of Tao's resolution of the discrepancy problem

    consolidation

    Soundararajan, K., Tao's resolution of the Erdős discrepancy problem, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. (N.S.) 55 (2018) 81-92, DOI 10.1090/bull/1598

    An expository account explaining the multiplicative-function argument that settles the problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: BAMS 55 (2018) 81-92 zBMATH Open record (search API)
