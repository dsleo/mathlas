1906 · Probability

Markov chains

Markov studied dependent trials and showed the law of large numbers extends to chains, later applying them to letter sequences.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1906

    Markov's first paper on chains

    first statement

    Markov, A. A., Rasprostranenie zakona bol'shikh chisel na velichiny, zavisyashchie drug ot druga, Izvestiya Fiziko-matematicheskogo obshchestva pri Kazanskom universitete, 2nd series, 15 (1906) 135-156

    Markov shows that the law of large numbers extends to a chain of dependent trials.

    Evidence (1)
    • First paper on Markov chains published 1906; reference cited Wikipedia: Markov chain, History
  2. 1913

    Markov's statistical study of Eugene Onegin

    first application

    Markov, A. A., Essai d'une recherche statistique sur le texte du roman 'Eugene Onegin' illustrant la liaison des epreuves en chaine, Bulletin de l'Academie Imperiale des Sciences de St.-Petersbourg, 6th series, 7(3) (1913) 153-162

    Markov applies the chain model to the letter sequence of a literary text.

    Evidence (2)
    • The original Onegin statistical study appeared in the Imperial Academy bulletin 7(3) (1913), pp. 153–162. Math-Net.Ru digitized original
    • 2006 translation of a lecture of 23 January 1913: first application of the theory of dependent trials to empirical material (the text of Onegin) zbMATH Open id 5229321 (Science in Context 19(4), 591-600)
  3. 1931

    Kolmogorov's analytical methods for Markov processes

    key development

    Kolmogorov, A., Uber die analytischen Methoden in der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung, Mathematische Annalen (1931)

    Kolmogorov's 1931 analytic method for Markov processes underpins the continuous-time theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper exists with this title, journal and year Crossref
    • Review: processes in which the state at t0 determines the distribution of later states, with continuous time zbMATH Open id 3000412
  4. 1936

    Kolmogorov: foundations of Markov chains with infinitely many states

    key development

    Kolmogorov, A., Anfangsgruende der Theorie der Markoffschen Ketten mit unendlich vielen moeglichen Zustaenden, Recueil mathematique (Matematicheskii sbornik) 1 (43) (1936) 607-610

    Kolmogorov begins the classification of states of a countable Markov chain (essential and inessential states, communication classes).

    Evidence (1)
    • Review: definition of essential and inessential states and the classification of states of a chain with infinitely many states zbMATH Open id 2526907 (Rec. math. Moscou (2) 1, 607-610)
  5. 1953

    Doob's Stochastic processes

    consolidation

    Doob, J. L., Stochastic processes (1953)

    Doob's 1953 monograph gives a systematic treatment of Markov processes within stochastic process theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Monograph with this title and year zbMATH Open
    • Stochastic processes, Wiley, 654 p. (1953) zbMATH Open id 3085434
