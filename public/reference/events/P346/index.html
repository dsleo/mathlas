Differential privacy

Dwork-McSherry-Nissim-Smith defined privacy through noise calibration.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2006

    Differential privacy formally defined

    first statement

    C. Dwork, F. McSherry, K. Nissim and A. Smith, Calibrating Noise to Sensitivity in Private Data Analysis, TCC (2006)

    The conference paper characterizes privacy via transcript indistinguishability and calibrates noise to query sensitivity.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher dates the paper to 2006 and its abstract states the definition and mechanism. Original bibliographic record
  2. 2009

    Differentially private recommender systems (McSherry and Mironov)

    first application

    McSherry, F., Mironov, I., Differentially private recommender systems, Proc. 15th ACM SIGKDD (2009), DOI 10.1145/1557019.1557090

    McSherry and Mironov applied differential privacy to a recommender system, a concrete data-analysis target problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'Differentially private recommender systems', KDD 2009, published 28 June 2009, authors McSherry and Mironov Crossref REST API (bibliographic query for the title)
    • Differential privacy was introduced in 2006 by Dwork, McSherry, Nissim and Smith, which is the first_statement and the context for this application Wikipedia, Differential privacy (raw wikitext)
  3. 2014

    Algorithmic foundations monograph

    consolidation

    C. Dwork and A. Roth, The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy, Foundations and Trends in TCS 9 (2014)

    A monograph systematizes differential-privacy algorithms and theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher record identifies the 2014 monograph. Original bibliographic record
