2002 · Algebraic geometry

Higher topos theory

Quasicategory-based infinity-topoi and derived geometry reframed homotopy theory and moduli problems.

Also known as: infinity-topos theory

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2002

    Higher topoi developed in homotopical algebraic geometry

    first statement

    B. Toën and G. Vezzosi, Homotopical Algebraic Geometry I: Topos theory, arXiv:math/0207028 (2002)

    The public preprint investigates higher topoi using simplicially enriched categories and develops higher sites and stacks.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv gives the public July 2002 submission and the abstract explicitly describes higher topoi. Original arXiv record
  2. 2006

    Lurie’s higher-topos manuscript

    key development

    J. Lurie, Higher Topos Theory, arXiv:math/0608040 (2006)

    The public manuscript develops higher versions of Grothendieck topoi using quasicategories.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the public manuscript to August 2006 and states its purpose. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2009

    Higher Topos Theory book published

    consolidation

    J. Lurie, Higher Topos Theory, Princeton University Press (2009)

    The Princeton monograph gives systematic treatment of quasicategories and higher topoi.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher metadata identifies the 2009 book. Original publication or repository record
