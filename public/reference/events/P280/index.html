1965 · Complex analysis

Several complex variables via PDE (L2 methods for the d-bar equation)

L2 estimates for the d-bar equation solved problems about pseudoconvex domains.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1963

    Kohn's harmonic integrals on strongly pseudoconvex manifolds

    precursor

    Kohn, Harmonic integrals on strongly pseudo-convex manifolds I, Ann. of Math. 78 (1963)

    Kohn solves the d-bar problem on strongly pseudoconvex manifolds by harmonic integrals, a PDE method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Harmonic Integrals on Strongly Pseudo-Convex Manifolds I, Annals of Mathematics, 1963 Crossref
  2. 1965

    L2 estimates and existence theorems for the d-bar operator

    first statement

    Hormander, L2 estimates and existence theorems for the d-bar operator, Acta Math. 113 (1965) 89-152

    Hormander proves L2 estimates for the d-bar operator, giving existence theorems on pseudoconvex domains by PDE methods.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: L2 estimates and existence theorems for the d-bar operator, Acta Mathematica, 1965 Crossref
  3. 1965

    Existence theorems on pseudoconvex domains

    first application

    Hormander, L2 estimates and existence theorems for the d-bar operator, Acta Math. 113 (1965)

    The same paper applies the L2 estimates to existence of holomorphic functions on pseudoconvex domains, the problem the input names.

    Dating or attribution note

    Same paper as first_statement; kept under the one-paper-two-roles rule. Its content on pseudoconvex domains is from the input summary and not checked line by line.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia Pseudoconvexity: pseudoconvex domains defined by plurisubharmonic exhaustion; the 1965 paper is the record for the application Wikipedia Pseudoconvexity
  4. 1965

    Carleman estimates on complex manifolds

    key development

    Andreotti and Vesentini, Carleman estimates for the Laplace-Beltrami equation on complex manifolds, Publ. Math. IHES 25 (1965) 81-130

    A parallel L2 (Carleman-type) method for complex manifolds that supports the same PDE-based program.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Carleman estimates for the Laplace-Beltrami equation on complex manifolds, Publ. Math. IHES, 1965 Crossref
  5. 1966

    Introduction to complex analysis in several variables

    consolidation

    Hormander, An Introduction to Complex Analysis in Several Variables, Van Nostrand (1966)

    The first textbook treatment of the L2 method together with the basic theory of several complex variables.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: An introduction to complex analysis in several variables, Van Nostrand, 1966 Open Library
