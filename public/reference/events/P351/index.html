2006 · Number theory

Kapustin-Witten: geometric Langlands as S-duality

Twisted N=4 super Yang-Mills was used to derive Hecke eigensheaves from branes.

Also known as: Kapustin-Witten correspondence

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2006

    Geometric Langlands interpreted via S-duality

    first statement

    A. Kapustin and E. Witten, Electric-Magnetic Duality And The Geometric Langlands Program (2006)

    The 2006 public preprint sets out the N=4 gauge-theory interpretation.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents geometric langlands interpreted via s-duality in 2006 arXiv
  2. 2006

    Branes yield Hecke-eigensheaf dictionary

    first application

    A. Kapustin and E. Witten, Electric-Magnetic Duality And The Geometric Langlands Program (2006)

    The paper applies electric–magnetic duality and brane constructions to objects of geometric Langlands.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents branes yield hecke-eigensheaf dictionary in 2006 arXiv
  3. 2007

    Kapustin–Witten paper published

    key development

    A. Kapustin and E. Witten, Electric-Magnetic Duality And The Geometric Langlands Program (2007)

    The peer-reviewed version appeared in 2007.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents kapustin–witten paper published in 2007 publisher / Crossref
  4. 2010

    Geometric Langlands from six dimensions (Witten)

    consolidation

    Witten, Geometric Langlands from six dimensions (2010)

    An expository account of the geometric Langlands correspondence as S-duality in six dimensions, following the 2007 paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2010 expository article by Witten on geometric Langlands from six dimensions zbMATH Open record
  5. 2015

    Outline of the proof of the geometric Langlands conjecture for GL(2)

    key development

    Gaitsgory, Outline of the proof of the geometric Langlands conjecture for GL(2), Asterisque (2015)

    Outlines the proof of the geometric Langlands conjecture for GL(2), a major later result about the same correspondence, though not via S-duality.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2015 Asterisque outline by Gaitsgory on the GL(2) geometric Langlands conjecture zbMATH Open record
