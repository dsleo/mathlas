1980 · Group & representation theory

Permutation-group algorithms meet complexity theory

Complexity-theoretic analysis was applied to permutation groups, giving polynomial-time versions of Sylow-type and quotient computations that fed back into practical systems.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1970

    Sims' computational methods for permutation groups

    precursor

    Sims, C. C., Computational methods in the study of permutation groups, in Computational Problems in Abstract Algebra, Pergamon, 1970

    Sims develops methods for handling large permutation groups, the non-complexity starting point of the field.

    Evidence (2)
    • Chapter in Computational Problems in Abstract Algebra (Pergamon, 1970) Crossref record
    • Sims' methods for big permutation groups were presented at the 1967 Oxford conference MacTutor: History of Computational Group Theory
  2. 1980

    Polynomial-time algorithms for permutation groups

    first statement

    Furst, M., Hopcroft, J., Luks, E., Polynomial-time algorithms for permutation groups, FOCS 1980

    The FOCS 1980 paper gives polynomial-time algorithms for permutation groups, the complexity-theoretic start.

    Evidence (2)
    • Proceedings of FOCS 1980 (DOI 10.1109/sfcs.1980.34) Crossref record
    • Early CGT results came from theoretical discussions of the complexity of permutation group algorithms MacTutor: History of Computational Group Theory
  3. 1980

    Graph isomorphism of bounded valence

    first application

    Luks, E., Isomorphism of graphs of bounded valence can be tested in polynomial time, FOCS 1980 (JCSS 25, 1982)

    Luks applies polynomial-time permutation group algorithms to graph isomorphism, the first target problem outside group theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    The use of permutation group algorithms is inferred from the title and not read in the paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • FOCS 1980 proceedings (DOI 10.1109/sfcs.1980.24); JCSS 1982 (DOI 10.1016/0022-0000(82)90009-5) Crossref record
  4. 1983

    Canonical labelling of graphs

    key development

    Babai, L., Luks, E., Canonical labeling of graphs, STOC 1983

    Babai and Luks use permutation-group machinery for canonical labelling of graphs, a main application.

    Evidence (1)
    • STOC 1983 proceedings (DOI 10.1145/800061.808746) Crossref record
  5. 1985

    Sylow subgroups in polynomial time

    key development

    Kantor, W. M., Polynomial-time algorithms for finding elements of prime order and Sylow subgroups, J. Algorithms 6, 1985

    Kantor gives polynomial-time algorithms for Sylow-type subgroups, the direct analogue of the complexity programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in J. Algorithms, 1985 (DOI 10.1016/0196-6774(85)90029-x) Crossref record
  6. 1988

    Fast management of permutation groups

    key development

    Babai, L., Luks, E., Seress, Á., Fast management of permutation groups, FOCS 1988 (SIAM J. Comput. 1997)

    The FOCS 1988 paper, expanded in SIAM J. Comput. 1997, gives efficient randomised management of permutation groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • FOCS 1988 proceedings (DOI 10.1109/sfcs.1988.21943) Crossref record
    • Expanded version in SIAM J. Comput. 1997 (DOI 10.1137/s0097539794229417) Crossref record
  7. 2003

    Textbook Permutation Group Algorithms

    consolidation

    Seress, Á., Permutation Group Algorithms, Cambridge University Press, 2003

    Seress's monograph gives a systematic treatment of polynomial-time and randomised permutation-group algorithms.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book published by Cambridge University Press, 2003 Open Library record
