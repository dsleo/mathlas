1970 · Algorithms & complexity

Knuth-Bendix rewriting: universal algebra meets word problems

Rewriting-system methods from universal algebra were applied to word problems, giving a computational route to normal forms in presented algebraic structures.

Also known as: Knuth-Bendix completion

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1914

    Thue poses the semigroup word problem

    precursor

    Thue's 1914 problem on rewriting strings by rules anticipates the word-problem setting of rewriting.

    Evidence (1)
    • Timeline: Thue poses the word problem for finitely presented semigroups (1914) Wikipedia
  2. 1942

    Newman on combinatorial definitions of equivalence

    precursor

    Max H. A. Newman, On theories with a combinatorial definition of 'equivalence', Annals of Mathematics, 1942

    Newman's paper treats equivalence defined by rewriting rules, an early route to confluence arguments.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper exists with this title in the Annals of Mathematics, 1942 Crossref
  3. 1970

    Knuth-Bendix completion stated

    first statement

    Donald E. Knuth, Peter B. Bendix, Simple word problems in universal algebras, in Computational Problems in Abstract Algebra (Pergamon), 1970

    Knuth and Bendix give the completion procedure that turns equations into a confluent rewriting system, solving word problems in some algebras.

    Evidence (1)
    • Chapter 'Simple Word Problems in Universal Algebras' in Computational Problems in Abstract Algebra, 1970 Crossref
  4. 1970

    Completion applied to the group axioms

    first application

    Knuth and Bendix (1970), as above

    The same paper runs the procedure on the group axioms to obtain a confluent system for groups.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia's Knuth-Bendix article cites the group-axiom example from Knuth and Bendix (1970) Wikipedia
  5. 1980

    Confluence criteria for rewriting

    key development

    Gerard Huet, Confluent reductions: abstract properties and applications to term rewriting systems, Journal of the ACM, 1980

    Huet's abstract analysis of confluence and termination makes completion a general method for term rewriting.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal of the ACM paper with this title, 1980 Crossref
  6. 1998

    Textbook treatment of term rewriting

    consolidation

    Franz Baader, Tobias Nipkow, Term Rewriting and All That, Cambridge University Press, 1998

    A standard textbook that systematises rewriting, completion and the word problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Monograph 'Term Rewriting and All That', Cambridge University Press, 1998 Crossref
