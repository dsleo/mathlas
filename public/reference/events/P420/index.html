1882 · Group & representation theory

Combinatorial group theory

Presentations by generators and relations arose from geometric studies of discrete groups, then Poincare's fundamental group, Tietze's transformations and the Dehn and Nielsen-Schreier line made group presentations a topological tool.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1856

    Hamilton's icosian calculus

    precursor

    Hamilton, icosian calculus, 1856

    Hamilton described the icosahedral group via the dodecahedron's edge graph.

    Evidence (1)
    • Notes a proto-form in Hamilton's 1856 icosian calculus. Wikipedia: Combinatorial group theory
  2. 1882

    von Dyck's group-theoretic studies

    first statement

    von Dyck, Gruppentheoretische Studien, Math. Ann. 20, 1-44, 1882

    First systematic study of groups by generators and relations.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: 1882. Crossref
    • Says Dyck laid the foundations in the early 1880s. Wikipedia: Combinatorial group theory
  3. 1895

    Poincare's fundamental group

    key development

    Poincare, Analysis situs, 1895

    Poincare introduced the fundamental group, giving presentations a topological role.

    Evidence (1)
    • Says he introduced the fundamental group in his 1894 paper and the Analysis situs was published 1895. MacTutor biography of Poincare
  4. 1908

    Tietze presents the fundamental group

    first application

    Tietze, Ueber die topologischen Invarianten mehrdimensionaler Mannigfaltigkeiten, Monatsh. Math. Phys. 19, 1-118, 1908

    Tietze produced finite presentations of fundamental groups and the transformations showing they are invariants.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: 1908. Crossref
    • Describes the finite presentation and Tietze transformations. MacTutor biography of Tietze
  5. 1911

    Dehn's infinite discontinuous groups

    key development

    Dehn, Ueber unendliche diskontinuierliche Gruppen, Math. Ann. 71, 116-144, 1911

    Dehn posed the word and isomorphism problems.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: 1911. Crossref
    • Says he formulated the word and isomorphism problems. MacTutor biography of Dehn
  6. 1924–1927

    Nielsen and Schreier on free groups

    key development

    Nielsen, Math. Ann. 91, 1924; Schreier, Abh. Hamburg 5, 1927

    Nielsen's method and Schreier's subgroup theorem for free groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: 1924. Crossref
    • Record: 1927. Crossref
  7. 1959

    Undecidability of the word problem

    key development

    Boone, The word problem, Annals of Math. 70, 207, 1959

    Boone proved a finitely presented group with unsolvable word problem exists.

    Dating or attribution note

    Novikov proved the same independently around 1955; Novikov's record not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record: 1959. Crossref
  8. 1966

    Magnus-Karrass-Solitar

    consolidation

    Magnus, Karrass, Solitar, Combinatorial Group Theory, 1966

    First systematic text.

    Evidence (1)
    • Archive.org record: 1966. Internet Archive
