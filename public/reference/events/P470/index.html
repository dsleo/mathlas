1976 · Fluid mechanics

Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg partial regularity for Navier-Stokes

Hausdorff-measure covering arguments show that suitable weak solutions of the 3D Navier-Stokes equations have a singular set of one-dimensional parabolic measure zero.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1934

    Leray's Navier-Stokes solutions

    precursor

    Jean Leray, Sur le mouvement d'un liquide visqueux emplissant l'espace, Acta Math. 63, 193-248, 1934

    Leray's 1934 work on the Navier-Stokes equations laid the weak-solution framework that the partial regularity theory later refines.

    Evidence (3)
    • In 1934 Jean Leray proved existence of smooth globally defined solutions under a smallness assumption on the initial velocity Wikipedia, Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness
    • Crossref record, Acta Math. 63 (1934), pp. 193-248. Crossref 10.1007/bf02547354
    • Studied the 3D Navier-Stokes initial value problem and showed solutions stay smooth only for a finite time. MacTutor, Leray biography
  2. 1976

    Scheffer's partial regularity

    first statement

    Scheffer, V., Partial regularity of solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations, Pacific Journal of Mathematics, 1976

    The first published partial regularity result for the Navier-Stokes equations, as the title records.

    Dating or attribution note

    The content (for example Hausdorff-measure bounds on the singular set) was not read in this pass; the claim rests on the title. Scheffer's first name was not verified.

    Evidence (1)
    • Partial regularity of solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations, Pacific J. Math., 1976 Crossref record
  3. 1977

    Scheffer's Hausdorff measure and Navier-Stokes

    key development

    Vladimir Scheffer, Hausdorff measure and the Navier-Stokes equations, Comm. Math. Phys. 55, 1977

    Bounds the Hausdorff dimension of the singular set of weak solutions, the idea CKN sharpened.

    Dating or attribution note

    Relation to CKN inferred from titles and the Hausdorff-measure framing.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Comm. Math. Phys. 55 (1977), pp. 97-112. Crossref 10.1007/bf01626512
  4. 1977

    Temam's 'Navier-Stokes equations: theory and numerical analysis'

    consolidation

    R. Temam, Navier-Stokes Equations: Theory and Numerical Analysis, Studies in Mathematics and its Applications 2, Amsterdam: North-Holland, 1977

    A systematic monograph on the Navier-Stokes equations, covering the weak-solution theory on which partial regularity results are built.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'Navier-Stokes equations. Theory and numerical analysis', Temam, North-Holland 1977, Zbl 0383.35057 zbMATH Open API
  5. 1982

    CKN partial regularity theorem

    key development

    Caffarelli, L., Kohn, R., Nirenberg, L., Partial regularity of suitable weak solutions of the Navier-Stokes equations, Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics, 1982

    Proves that suitable weak solutions have a singular set of one-dimensional parabolic Hausdorff measure zero.

    Evidence (3)
    • Partial regularity of suitable weak solutions of the Navier-Stokes equations, CPAM, 1982 Crossref record
    • Caffarelli and Nirenberg worked on fluid dynamics and Navier-Stokes at the Courant Institute in the 1980s MacTutor biography of Luis Caffarelli
    • Caffarelli says the speed can be infinite at most on a set of zero one-dimensional measure. MacTutor, Caffarelli biography
  6. 1998

    Lin's new proof of the CKN theorem

    key development

    Fanghua Lin, A new proof of the Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg theorem, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 51, 1998

    Shorter proof of the partial regularity theorem that made it widely teachable.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, CPAM 51 (1998), pp. 241-257. Crossref 10.1002/(sici)1097-0312(199803)51:3<241::aid-cpa2>3.0.co;2-a
