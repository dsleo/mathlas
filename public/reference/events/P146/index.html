Fermat's adequality (method of maxima, minima and tangents)

An algebraic adequality rule found extrema and tangents of curves.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1629–1631

    Early maxima and minima work in Bordeaux

    precursor

    In Bordeaux Fermat produced maxima-and-minima work, passed to a local mathematician, before the Methodus circulated.

    Evidence (1)
    • Places his first work on maxima and minima in Bordeaux, with Plane loci given c. 1629. MacTutor biography of Fermat
  2. 1636

    Methodus circulated in manuscript

    precursor

    Fermat, Methodus ad disquirendam maximam et minimam (circulated c. 1636)

    Fermat equated p(x) and p(x+e) approximately, divided by e and dropped remaining e-terms to find extrema and tangents; the text circulated privately.

    Evidence (1)
    • States the treatise circulated in France c. 1636 and describes the method. English Wikipedia: Adequality
  3. 1642

    Hérigone prints Fermat's maxima and minima method

    first statement

    Hérigone, Supplementum cursus mathematici, Paris, 1642 (Supplementum algebrae, prop. XXVI, De maximis et minimis)

    Hérigone's course added a supplement carrying Fermat's method, its first public appearance.

    Evidence (3)
    • Says Hérigone added a supplement with Fermat's methods of maxima and minima to his Cursus mathematicus. MacTutor biography of Fermat
    • Internet Archive scan: title page 'Supplementum Cursus Mathematici ... Par Pierre Herigone ... A Paris M.DC.XLII'; Supplementum algebrae, prop. XXVI, De maximis et minimis, with the remark that the same method finds tangents. Internet Archive: Hérigone, Supplementum Cursus mathematici (1642)
    • The 1679 Varia opera quotes 'P. Herigonius, tom. 6. Cursus Mathematici p. 68. De Maximis & minimis' and names Fermat as the inventor of the method. Internet Archive: Fermat, Varia opera mathematica (1679)
  4. 1679

    Varia opera mathematica prints Methodus

    consolidation

    Fermat, Varia opera mathematica, Toulouse, 1679

    The posthumous collected works first gave the Methodus and related tracts in print in full.

    Evidence (3)
    • Record of the 1679 Varia opera mathematica. Internet Archive record: Varia opera mathematica, 1679
    • Open Library lists Varia opera 1679. Open Library search: Varia opera entries
    • Internet Archive scan of the 1679 Toulouse volume shows the heading 'Methodus ad disquirendam maximam & minimam' and, after it, the tangent and centre-of-gravity applications. Internet Archive: Fermat, Varia opera mathematica (1679)
