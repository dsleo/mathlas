2002 · Algorithms & complexity

AKS primality

Agrawal, Kayal and Saxena gave an unconditional deterministic polynomial-time primality test using polynomial congruences.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2002

    PRIMES is in P posted publicly

    first statement

    M. Agrawal, N. Kayal and N. Saxena, PRIMES is in P, IIT Kanpur public preprint (2002)

    The publicly posted paper gives an unconditional deterministic polynomial-time primality algorithm.

    Evidence (1)
    • Original paper is dated 6 August 2002; IIT and contemporary accounts confirm public posting. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2004

    Annals version published

    key development

    M. Agrawal, N. Kayal and N. Saxena, PRIMES is in P, Ann. Math. 160 (2004)

    The journal version publishes the AKS result in Annals of Mathematics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals records September 2004 online publication. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2004

    Primality testing in polynomial time (monograph)

    consolidation

    Dietzfelbinger, Primality Testing in Polynomial Time, LNCS 3000 (2004)

    A monograph that presents the AKS algorithm and its background, from randomized primality tests to PRIMES is in P.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2004 LNCS monograph by Dietzfelbinger on primality testing in polynomial time zbMATH Open record
