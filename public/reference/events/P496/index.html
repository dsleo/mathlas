1967 · Differential equations

Inverse scattering transform and Lax pairs for soliton equations

The KdV equation from water waves and plasma physics is solved exactly through scattering data of a Schroedinger operator, and Lax recasts it as an isospectral flow.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1951

    Gelfand-Levitan inverse spectral problem

    precursor

    Gelfand, I. M.; Levitan, B. M., On the determination of a differential equation from its spectral function, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR 15 (1951) 309-360

    Reconstructs a Schrodinger operator from spectral data, the linear core of the later scattering-data method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Gelfand-Levitan paper, pages 309-360, zbMATH 0044.09301 (1951) zbMATH
  2. 1965

    Zabusky-Kruskal numerical soliton collisions

    precursor

    Zabusky, N. J.; Kruskal, M. D., Interaction of 'solitons' in a collisionless plasma and the recurrence of initial states, Phys. Rev. Lett. 15 (1965) 240-243

    Numerically finds that solitary waves emerge from collisions with their shape restored, which motivated the exact method.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in Phys. Rev. Lett. 15 (1965), DOI 10.1103/physrevlett.15.240 Crossref; zbMATH 1201.35174
    • Wikipedia credits Zabusky and Kruskal's numerical work with the particle-like soliton collisions that led to the method Wikipedia, Inverse scattering transform (History)
  3. 1967

    Gardner-Greene-Kruskal-Miura method for the KdV equation

    first statement

    Gardner, C. S.; Greene, J. M.; Kruskal, M. D.; Miura, R. M., Method for solving the Korteweg-de Vries equation, Phys. Rev. Lett. 19 (1967) 1095-1097

    Introduces the inverse scattering transform for solving the KdV equation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper in Phys. Rev. Lett. 19 (1967), DOI 10.1103/physrevlett.19.1095 Crossref; zbMATH 1061.35520
    • Wikipedia credits this group with introducing the inverse scattering transform for KdV Wikipedia, Inverse scattering transform (History)
  4. 1967

    Exact solution of the KdV initial-value problem

    first application

    Gardner, C. S.; Greene, J. M.; Kruskal, M. D.; Miura, R. M., Method for solving the Korteweg-de Vries equation, Phys. Rev. Lett. 19 (1967)

    The same paper first applies the transform to the KdV equation, which is the target problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same 1967 paper as the first_statement milestone Crossref
  5. 1968

    Lax pair and isospectral flow

    key development

    Lax, P. D., Integrals of nonlinear equations of evolution and solitary waves, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 21 (1968) 467-490

    Recasts nonlinear evolution equations as isospectral flows of an operator pair, which gives the conserved quantities and the general structure.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lax's paper in CPAM 21 (1968), pages 467-490, DOI 10.1002/cpa.3160210503 Crossref; zbMATH 0162.41103
    • Wikipedia says Lax generalised the KdV approach to other equations Wikipedia, Inverse scattering transform (History)
  6. 1974

    Zakharov-Shabat scheme for nonlinear Schrodinger and general scattering problems

    key development

    Zakharov, V. E.; Shabat, A. B., A scheme for integrating the nonlinear equations of mathematical physics by the method of the inverse scattering problem, Funct. Anal. Appl. 8 (1974) 226-235

    Extends the scheme to a new nonlinear equation (the nonlinear Schrodinger equation), which is the step beyond KdV.

    Evidence (3)
    • Zakharov-Shabat scheme indexed by zbMATH 0303.35024, pages 226-235 (1974) zbMATH
    • Wikipedia lists the nonlinear Schrodinger equation among equations solved by the generalised approach Wikipedia, Inverse scattering transform (History)
    • zbMATH 3474158: Funct. Anal. Appl. 8 (1974) 226-235, translation of Funkts. Anal. Prilozh. 8, No. 3 (1974) 43-53 zbMATH Open 3474158
  7. 1974

    Name 'inverse scattering transform' in a title

    naming

    Ablowitz, M. J.; Kaup, D. J.; Newell, A. C.; Segur, H., The inverse scattering transform-Fourier analysis for nonlinear problems, Studies in Applied Mathematics 53 (1974) 249-315

    Uses the name of the method in the title and gives the general AKNS framework.

    Evidence (1)
    • AKNS paper, Stud. Appl. Math. 53 (1974), DOI 10.1002/sapm1974534249, pages 249-315 Crossref; zbMATH 0408.35068
  8. 1981

    Ablowitz-Segur monograph on solitons and IST

    consolidation

    Ablowitz, M. J.; Segur, H., Solitons and the Inverse Scattering Transform, SIAM (1981)

    The first book-length treatment of the method and its soliton applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Monograph in SIAM (1981), DOI 10.1137/1.9781611970883 Crossref; zbMATH 0472.35002
