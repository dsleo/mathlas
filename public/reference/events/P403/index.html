1903 · Analysis

Fredholm integral equations

Fredholm treated integral equations as limits of linear algebraic systems and solved the Dirichlet problem.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1897

    Volterra on inversion of definite integrals

    precursor

    V. Volterra, Sopra alcune questioni di inversione di integrali definiti, Ann. Mat. Pura Appl. (1897)

    Volterra solves what are now called Volterra integral equations by iteration, the model for Fredholm's method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Volterra, Sopra alcune questioni di inversione di integrali definiti, Annali di Matematica, 1897. Crossref, DOI 10.1007/bf02580504
  2. 1900

    Fredholm's solution of the Dirichlet problem

    precursor

    Fredholm, I., 'Sur une nouvelle méthode pour la résolution du problème de Dirichlet' (1900)

    Fredholm applies an integral-equation method to the Dirichlet problem, the first purposeful use of the approach.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Sur une nouvelle méthode pour la résolution du problème de Dirichlet', year 1900 zbMATH API, query 'Fredholm Sur une nouvelle methode pour la resolution du probleme de Dirichlet'
    • Fredholm treated integral equations as limits of linear systems and solved the Dirichlet problem Wikipedia, Fredholm integral equation (saved lead) and MacTutor Fredholm biography (live)
  3. 1903

    Fredholm's classes of functional equations

    first statement

    Fredholm, I., 'Sur une classe d'équations fonctionnelles', Acta Math. 27 (1903)

    Gives the general integral-equation theory with the Fredholm determinant, the first systematic public statement.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Acta Mathematica, year 1903 Crossref, DOI 10.1007/bf02421317
  4. 1907

    Schmidt on linear and nonlinear integral equations

    key development

    E. Schmidt, Zur Theorie der linearen und nichtlinearen Integralgleichungen, Math. Ann. 63 (1907)

    Schmidt gives an elementary eigenfunction approach to Fredholm theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record of Schmidt's Teil III in Math. Ann. (1908); Teil I appeared in 1907. Crossref, DOI 10.1007/bf01456418
  5. 1909

    Bôcher's Cambridge tract

    consolidation

    M. Bôcher, An introduction to the study of integral equations, Cambridge 1909

    Early English-language systematic introduction to Volterra and Fredholm theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Jahrbuch record of the 1909 Cambridge tract. zbMATH record 40.0395.02
    • Internet Archive copy. Internet Archive introductiontost00bcuoft
  6. 1912

    Hilbert's general theory of linear integral equations

    consolidation

    Hilbert, D., Grundzüge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen (1912)

    Book-length treatment of linear integral equations and their spectral theory, which systematises the Fredholm approach.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'Grundzüge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen', year 1912 zbMATH API, query 'Hilbert Grundzuge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen'
  7. 1916

    Riesz on linear functional equations

    key development

    F. Riesz, Über lineare Funktionalgleichungen, Acta Math. 41 (1916)

    Riesz recasts Fredholm theory for compact operators on function spaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Riesz, Über lineare Funktionalgleichungen, Acta Mathematica, 1916. Crossref, DOI 10.1007/bf02422940
