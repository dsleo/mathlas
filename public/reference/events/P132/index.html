1805 · Statistics & decision theory

Least squares

Fitting observations by minimising squared errors predicted Ceres.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1795

    Gauss's later claim of possession since 1795

    precursor

    Gauss said in 1809 that he had used the method since 1795, which is a private claim, not a publication.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia says that in 1809 Gauss claimed to have been in possession of the method since 1795 English Wikipedia, Least squares
  2. 1801

    Gauss's prediction of Ceres

    precursor

    Gauss's Ceres orbit prediction in 1801; MacTutor says he used least squares but did not reveal his methods then.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor says Gauss did not disclose his methods in 1801 but used least squares MacTutor, Gauss
  3. 1805

    Legendre's appendix on least squares

    first statement

    A.-M. Legendre, Nouvelles méthodes pour la détermination des orbites des comètes, Paris, 1805

    Legendre published the method as an appendix to his memoir on comet orbits, the first clear exposition.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive item for Legendre's 1805 Nouvelles méthodes pour la détermination des orbites des comètes archive.org nouvellesmthode00legegoog
    • MacTutor says Legendre gave the least squares method in an appendix and that Gauss published his version in 1809 acknowledging Legendre MacTutor, Legendre
  4. 1805

    Legendre applies it to comet orbits

    first application

    Legendre, Nouvelles méthodes, 1805

    In the same work Legendre fitted data for two comets to produce more equations than unknowns.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor says Legendre applied his methods to data on two comets and had more equations than unknowns MacTutor, Legendre
  5. 1808

    Adrain's independent formulation

    key development

    The American Robert Adrain independently formulated least squares in 1808.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia says Adrain independently formulated the idea in 1808 English Wikipedia, Least squares
  6. 1809

    Gauss's Theoria motus

    key development

    C. F. Gauss, Theoria motus corporum coelestium in sectionibus conicis solem ambientium, Hamburg, 1809

    Gauss published his orbit-determination method with least squares and claimed priority, acknowledging that Legendre had printed it first.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive item for Theoria motus corporum coelestium (1809) archive.org bub_gb_ORUOAAAAQAAJ
    • MacTutor, Legendre, says Gauss published in 1809 and still claimed priority MacTutor, Legendre
  7. 1863

    Least squares named in a title (Chauvenet's manual appendix)

    naming

    William Chauvenet, A manual of spherical and practical astronomy ... with an appendix on the method of least squares, Lippincott, 1863

    The phrase 'method of least squares' first appears in this title as an appendix topic, before Chauvenet's stand-alone treatise.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: Chauvenet's Manual of spherical and practical astronomy with an appendix on the method of least squares, first publication year 1863, Lippincott. Open Library search API
  8. 1868

    Chauvenet's Treatise on the Method of Least Squares

    consolidation

    William Chauvenet, A Treatise on the Method of Least Squares, Trubner, 1868

    Chauvenet's stand-alone treatise is the first book-length systematic treatment of least squares among the Open Library records checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: A Treatise on the Method of Least Squares, first publication year 1868, Trubner; a later 1879 Lippincott edition has the same title. Open Library search API
