1953 · Geometry

Kodaira vanishing via harmonic integrals

Hodge theory (harmonic forms) is applied to compact Kahler manifolds to prove vanishing theorems and characterise projective varieties.

Also known as: Kodaira vanishing theorem

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1941

    Hodge's harmonic integrals book

    precursor

    Hodge, The Theory and Applications of Harmonic Integrals, Cambridge University Press, 1941

    Hodge's theory of harmonic integrals supplied the analytic tools Kodaira later applied to vanishing.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hodge's book is dated 1941 (Cambridge University Press) Open Library record
  2. 1949

    Kodaira's thesis on harmonic fields

    precursor

    Kodaira, Harmonic fields in Riemannian manifolds, Annals of Mathematics, 1949

    Kodaira's doctoral work on harmonic fields on Riemannian manifolds, which he later carried into complex geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Thesis published as an 80-page paper in the Annals in 1949 MacTutor biography of Kodaira
  3. 1953

    Kodaira's differential-geometric method (PNAS)

    first statement

    Kodaira, On a differential-geometric method in the theory of analytic stacks, PNAS 39 (1953) 1268-1273

    Kodaira's public statement of the harmonic-form method for vanishing on complex manifolds.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume, pages and year verified Crossref
    • Attribution of the vanishing theorem to this paper MacTutor biography of Kodaira (vanishing work with harmonic forms)
  4. 1953

    Vanishing theorem from harmonic forms (same paper)

    first application

    Kodaira, PNAS 39 (1953) 1268-1273 (as above)

    The same 1953 paper applies the harmonic-form method to the vanishing theorem for line bundles.

    Evidence (1)
    • Same publication as the first statement Crossref
  5. 1954

    Kodaira's intrinsic characterisation of algebraic varieties

    key development

    Kodaira, On Kahler varieties of restricted type (an intrinsic characterization of algebraic varieties), Annals of Mathematics 60 (1954)

    Uses harmonic-form methods to characterise projective varieties intrinsically, a result that made the vanishing method central.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, journal, volume and year verified Crossref
    • Kodaira's 1954 work on algebraic varieties and Fields Medal nomination MacTutor biography of Kodaira
  6. 1971

    Kodaira and Morrow's 'Complex manifolds'

    consolidation

    J. Morrow, K. Kodaira, Complex Manifolds, Athena Series, New York: Holt, Rinehart and Winston, 1971

    A textbook on complex manifolds written by Kodaira, in which the vanishing theorems of his method are presented in systematic form.

    Dating or attribution note

    Textbook role from the record; the vanishing-theorem chapter was not read. Hodge's 1941 'Theory and applications of harmonic integrals' (Zbl 0024.39703) is the earlier monograph on the harmonic-integral method; it is already the precursor in the record and is not repeated.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'Complex manifolds', Morrow and Kodaira, Holt, Rinehart and Winston 1971, Zbl 0325.32001 zbMATH Open API
