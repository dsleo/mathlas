1982 · Topology

Hyperbolic geometry in three-manifold topology (Thurston)

Three-manifolds are decomposed into pieces carrying one of eight model geometries, hyperbolic being generic, so topology is studied with geometric tools.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1978–1981

    Princeton lecture notes on three-manifolds

    precursor

    Thurston, W., The geometry and topology of three-manifolds, Princeton lecture notes, 1978-1981

    The course notes in which hyperbolic structures and the geometrization picture circulated before publication.

    Evidence (2)
    • Thurston gave the Princeton course in 1978 and the notes circulated widely MacTutor biography of William Thurston
    • The notes are listed with dates 1978-1981 Wikipedia, William Thurston references
  2. 1982

    Hyperbolic geometry and Kleinian groups announced in print

    first statement

    Thurston, W., Three-dimensional manifolds, Kleinian groups and hyperbolic geometry, Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society, 1982

    The first printed survey in which the geometrization picture for three-manifolds appears.

    Evidence (1)
    • Three-dimensional manifolds, Kleinian groups and hyperbolic geometry, Bull. AMS, 1982 Crossref record
  3. 1986

    Deformation theory of hyperbolic structures (Part I)

    key development

    Thurston, W., Hyperbolic structures on 3-manifolds I: Deformation of acylindrical manifolds, Annals of Mathematics, 1986

    Develops the deformation theory behind hyperbolic Dehn surgery and the hyperbolisation of Haken manifolds.

    Dating or attribution note

    Wikipedia does not say which part contains the surgery theorem; the link between Part I and the surgery theorem was not checked from the text.

    Evidence (2)
    • Hyperbolic structures on 3-manifolds I: Deformation of acylindrical manifolds, Ann. Math., 1986 Crossref record
    • Thurston proved the hyperbolic Dehn surgery theorem and the hyperbolisation theorem for Haken manifolds Wikipedia, William Thurston
  4. 1997

    Published Princeton notes

    consolidation

    Thurston, W. P., Three-Dimensional Geometry and Topology, Princeton University Press, 1997

    The published book version of the Princeton notes, the systematic treatment of the subject.

    Evidence (1)
    • Three-Dimensional Geometry and Topology, Princeton University Press, 1997 Open Library record
