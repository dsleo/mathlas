1896 · Group & representation theory

Representation theory of finite groups

Frobenius, Burnside and Schur studied groups through matrix representations, and character tables proved structural theorems such as Burnside's p^a q^b.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1896

    Frobenius' group characters

    first statement

    Frobenius, G., Ueber Gruppencharaktere, Sitzungsberichte der Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, 1896

    Frobenius introduces group characters, the central invariant of representation theory of finite groups.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper on group characters, Sitzungsberichte 1896, pages 985-1021 zbMATH record, Zbl 27.0092.01
    • MacTutor says Frobenius started representation theory and character theory in 1896 MacTutor biography of Frobenius
  2. 1897

    Representations of finite groups by linear substitutions

    first statement

    Frobenius, G., Ueber die Darstellung der endlichen Gruppen durch lineare Substitutionen, Sitzungsberichte der Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, 1897

    Frobenius gives the matrix (linear substitution) representations of finite groups, the matrix form of the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • ETH's archive digitizes Frobenius's original paper; the AMS historical account dates the representation-theory development to 1897. ETH Library digitization and AMS history
  3. 1904

    Burnside's p^a q^b theorem

    first application

    Burnside, W., On groups of order p^alpha q^beta, Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society (2) 1, 1904

    Burnside uses Frobenius characters to prove that groups of order p^a q^b are solvable, the first major application of the character method.

    Dating or attribution note

    Use of characters in the 1904 proof is stated by MacTutor; the paper text was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper On groups of order p^alpha q^beta, PLMS 1904, pages 388-392 zbMATH record, Zbl 35.0162.01
    • MacTutor says Burnside began to use character theory and the p^m q^n solubility result appeared in 1904 MacTutor biography of Burnside
  4. 1904

    Schur on representations of finite groups by fractional linear substitutions

    key development

    Schur, I., Ueber die Darstellung der endlichen Gruppen durch gebrochen lineare Substitutionen, Journal fuer die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 1904

    Schur extends the theory to projective representations of finite groups, a major development of the character method.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appears in Crelle's Journal in 1904 Crossref record, DOI 10.1515/crll.1904.127.20
    • MacTutor says Schur worked on projective representations and group characters between 1904 and 1907 MacTutor biography of Schur
  5. 1911

    Second edition of Theory of Groups of Finite Order

    consolidation

    Burnside, W., Theory of Groups of Finite Order, 2nd ed., Cambridge, 1911

    The second edition of Burnside's book includes a systematic development of Frobenius' character theory; the first edition (1897) predates the character method.

    Evidence (2)
    • Theory of groups of finite order published with a 1911 edition Open Library search record
    • MacTutor says the 1911 edition contains a systematic development including Frobenius's character theory MacTutor biography of Burnside
