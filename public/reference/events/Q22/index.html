2024 · Combinatorics

Integer distance sets via the determinant method

Lifting planar integer-distance sets into higher dimensions and applying the determinant method showed that all but a sparse subset of a large set lies on a line or circle.

Also known as: integral distance sets

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1945

    Anning–Erdős integral distances

    precursor

    N. H. Anning and P. Erdős, Integral distances, Bull. AMS 51 (1945)

    A classical paper establishes an earlier structural restriction on infinite integral-distance sets.

    Evidence (1)
    • Published 1945 Bulletin article AMS / Crossref
  2. 2024

    Greenfeld–Iliopoulou–Peluse post structural theorem

    first statement

    R. Greenfeld, M. Iliopoulou and S. Peluse, On integer distance sets, arXiv:2401.10821 (2024)

    The public preprint states that nearly all points of a planar integer-distance set lie on a line or circle.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted in January 2024 arXiv
  3. 2024

    Determinant method applied to integer-distance structure

    first application

    R. Greenfeld, M. Iliopoulou and S. Peluse, On integer distance sets, arXiv:2401.10821 (2024)

    The authors use the determinant method to cover lifted points by curves and derive structural and diameter bounds.

    Evidence (2)
    • Public preprint posted in January 2024 arXiv
    • Section 1.4 explicitly uses the determinant method to cover lifted points by algebraic curves Original paper, section 1.4
