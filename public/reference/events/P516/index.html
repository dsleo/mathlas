≈ 1998 · Algebraic geometry

Motivic integration

Measures on arc spaces are valued in a Grothendieck ring of varieties, generalising p-adic integration with model-theoretic tools.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1998

    Arc spaces and motivic integration

    key development

    Germs of arcs on singular algebraic varieties and motivic integration (1998)

    The public preprint develops motivic integration on arc spaces of singular varieties.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated publication record and abstract support the 1998 arc spaces and motivic integration milestone. Original publication or repository record
  2. 1999

    Non-Archimedean integrals and stringy Euler numbers of log-terminal pairs

    key development

    Batyrev, Non-Archimedean integrals and stringy Euler numbers of log-terminal pairs, JEMS (1999)

    Introduces non-archimedean (arc-space) integrals and stringy Euler numbers, an early main application of the motivic-integration idea.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 1999 JEMS paper by Batyrev on non-archimedean integrals and stringy Euler numbers zbMATH Open record
  3. 2001

    Geometry on arc spaces of algebraic varieties

    consolidation

    Denef, Loeser, Geometry on arc spaces of algebraic varieties (Panoramas and Syntheses series)

    A survey of arc spaces and motivic integration that gives a systematic account after the 1998 statement.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2001 survey by Denef and Loeser on arc spaces of algebraic varieties zbMATH Open record
