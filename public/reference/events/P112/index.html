1639 · Geometry

Desargues' projective geometry

Properties invariant under projection, points at infinity and involution gave a unified theory of conics.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1639

    Brouillon project d'une atteinte aux evenemens des rencontres du cone avec un plan

    first statement

    Desargues, Brouillon project, Paris, 1639

    Desargues introduced pencils, ranges, involution and points at infinity, treating conics through properties invariant under projection.

    Evidence (3)
    • Describes the 1639 text: involutions, infinite points, unified theory of conics. MacTutor biography of Desargues
    • Cites the 1639 'Rough draft for an essay on plane sections of a cone'. English Wikipedia: Girard Desargues
    • Modern edition record (Oeuvres de Desargues, 2011). Crossref record: Brouillon project chapter
  2. 1648

    Desargues' perspective theorem first published

    key development

    Bosse, work on perspective, 1648

    The theorem on triangles in perspective appeared in print through Bosse's perspective book.

    Evidence (2)
    • Perspective theorem first published in 1648 in a work by Bosse. MacTutor biography of Desargues
    • Records of Bosse's Desargues-based books. Internet Archive record: La maniere universelle de Mr Desargues, 1643
  3. 1822

    Poncelet's Traite des proprietes projectives

    consolidation

    Poncelet, Traite des proprietes projectives des figures, Paris, 1822

    First systematic textbook-style development of projective geometry as a subject.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record shows 1865 reprint of the Traite. Internet Archive record: Traite des proprietes projectives, 1865 reprint
  4. 1864

    Poudra edition of Desargues' works

    key development

    Poudra, Oeuvres de Desargues, 1864

    The collected works were republished, bringing Desargues back after La Hire's copy resurfaced.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record of the 1864 Poudra edition. Internet Archive record: Oeuvres de Desargues, 1864
    • Work rediscovered and republished 1864. English Wikipedia: Girard Desargues
