1955 · Differential geometry

Singularity theory (Whitney, Thom, Mather, Arnold)

Stable singularities of smooth maps are classified by algebra and topology, then applied to caustics, wavefronts and bifurcations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1955

    Whitney on singularities of plane-to-plane maps

    first statement

    Whitney, H., On singularities of mappings of Euclidean spaces I, Annals of Mathematics 62 (1955)

    Whitney classifies the generic singularities of maps of the plane into the plane (folds and cross-caps).

    Evidence (2)
    • Annals of Mathematics record for Whitney's 'On singularities of mappings of Euclidean spaces I' with year 1955 Crossref
    • MacTutor biography lists Whitney's work on singularities among his main topics MacTutor, Hassler Whitney
  2. 1956

    Thom's paper on singularities of differentiable maps

    key development

    Thom, R., Les singularités des applications différentiables, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 6 (1956)

    Thom develops the general theory of singularities of differentiable maps, building on Whitney and linking them to cobordism and transversality.

    Evidence (2)
    • Annales de l'Institut Fourier record for Thom's paper, year 1956 Crossref
    • MacTutor says Thom's work on transversality and characteristic classes led to his Fields Medal in 1958 MacTutor, René Thom
  3. 1972

    Thom's catastrophe theory applied to morphogenesis

    first application

    Thom, R., Stabilité structurelle et morphogénèse, Benjamin (1972)

    Thom applies stable-singularity classification to natural phenomena (morphogenesis, bifurcations), the usual 'first application' outside mathematics.

    Dating or attribution note

    Application to morphogenesis is the 1972 book. Whether an earlier application exists (for example caustics in optics) was not checked.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record for Stabilité structurelle et morphogénèse, first published 1972 Open Library
    • MacTutor notes catastrophe theory as Thom's applied development of the topic MacTutor, René Thom
  4. 1973

    Golubitsky-Guillemin monograph on stable mappings

    consolidation

    Golubitsky, M. and Guillemin, V., Stable Mappings and Their Singularities, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 14, Springer (1973)

    A graduate text giving the systematic treatment of stable mappings and their singularities.

    Evidence (1)
    • Springer Graduate Texts record for Stable Mappings and Their Singularities, year 1973 Crossref
