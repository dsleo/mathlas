1906 · Differential geometry

Lorentz group and Minkowski spacetime: group theory enters physics

Poincare showed Lorentz transformations form a group, Einstein derived them from two postulates, and Minkowski gave a tensor form of electrodynamics and showed Newtonian gravity incompatible.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1905

    Einstein derives the transformations from two postulates

    precursor

    Einstein, A., Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Korper, Annalen der Physik (1905)

    Einstein uses the transformations, derived from two postulates, to describe kinematics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Einstein's 1905 paper derives kinematics from the postulates Wikipedia: Minkowski spacetime, History
  2. 1906

    Poincare on Lorentz transformations as invariance group

    first statement

    Poincare, H., Sur la dynamique de l'electron, Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo (1906)

    Poincare's 1906 Palermo paper treats the Lorentz transformations as a group of invariance transformations.

    Dating or attribution note

    The group statement is in the input summary; the paper's text was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper exists with this title, journal and year Crossref
    • Poincare described invariants of the transformations Wikipedia: Minkowski spacetime, History
  3. 1909

    Minkowski's four-dimensional space-time lecture (published)

    first statement

    Minkowski, H., Raum und Zeit, Physikalische Zeitschrift (1909)

    Minkowski's Cologne lecture, published 1909, presents space and time as one four-dimensional geometry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lecture 'Raum und Zeit' held at the 80th Naturforscher-Versammlung, Cologne; published 1909 zbMATH Open
    • Minkowski's 1908 lecture introduced spacetime geometry Wikipedia: Minkowski spacetime, History
  4. 1910

    Minkowski's tensor electrodynamics (posthumous)

    key development

    Minkowski, H., Die Grundgleichungen fur die elektromagnetischen Vorgange in bewegten Korpern, Mathematische Annalen (1910)

    Minkowski's posthumous 1910 paper gives the electrodynamics of moving bodies in tensor form.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper exists with this title, journal and year Crossref
  5. 1925

    Brill titles a note on the Lorentz group

    naming

    Brill, J., Note on the Lorentz group, 1925

    Earliest zbMATH title found that names the Lorentz group.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: "Note on the Lorentz group", Brill, 1925 zbMATH Open doc 2591967
  6. 1963

    English edition of the Gel'fand, Minlos and Shapiro monograph

    consolidation

    Gel'fand, I. M., Minlos, R. A., Shapiro, Z. Ya., Representations of the rotation and Lorentz groups and their applications, 1963

    Systematic monograph on the representations of the Lorentz group and its applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library lists the English edition with first publication year 1963 Open Library works OL10785799W
