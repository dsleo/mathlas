1948 · Algebraic topology

Lie algebra cohomology

Cohomology of compact Lie groups was computed algebraically via Lie algebras.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1948

    Chevalley and Eilenberg: cohomology theory of Lie groups and Lie algebras

    first statement

    Chevalley C., Eilenberg S., Cohomology theory of Lie groups and Lie algebras, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 63, 85-124, 1948

    The algebraic cohomology of a Lie algebra, defined for the first time with the Chevalley-Eilenberg complex.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Trans. AMS, 1948, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-1948-0024908-8 Crossref
    • MacTutor says Eilenberg and Chevalley gave an algebraic approach to the cohomology of Lie groups in 1948 MacTutor, Eilenberg biography
  2. 1948

    Algebraic computation of the cohomology of compact Lie groups

    first application

    Chevalley C., Eilenberg S., Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 63, 85-124, 1948

    The same 1948 paper computes the cohomology of compact Lie groups from the Lie algebra, the first purposeful use of the theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor says the 1948 paper gave an algebraic approach to Lie-group cohomology via Lie algebras MacTutor, Eilenberg biography
    • Crossref record for the 1948 paper Crossref
  3. 1950

    Koszul: homology and cohomology of Lie algebras (modules)

    key development

    Koszul J.-L., Homologie et cohomologie des algebres de Lie, Bull. Soc. Math. France 78, 65-127, 1950

    Koszul extended the cohomology theory to arbitrary Lie modules.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Bull. SMF 78, 65-127, 1950 Crossref
    • Wikipedia says the theory was extended by Koszul to coefficients in an arbitrary Lie module Wikipedia, Lie algebra cohomology
  4. 1953

    Hochschild and Serre: cohomology of Lie algebras

    key development

    Hochschild G., Serre J.-P., Cohomology of Lie algebras, Ann. of Math. 57, 591-603, 1953

    Hochschild and Serre gave a spectral-sequence treatment of the cohomology of Lie algebras relative to subalgebras.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content claim paraphrased from the standard result; the text was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Ann. of Math. 57, 591-603, 1953 Crossref
    • MacTutor Hochschild biography lists the 1953 Annals paper with Serre MacTutor, Gerhard Hochschild biography
  5. 1962

    Jacobson, Lie Algebras

    consolidation

    Jacobson N., Lie Algebras, Interscience, 1962

    A textbook treatment of Lie algebras, including their cohomology, consolidating the theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    Priority as the first textbook on this subject was not checked; the record is a general Lie-algebra text.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: Interscience, 1962 Open Library
