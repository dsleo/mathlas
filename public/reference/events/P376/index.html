1921 · Statistics & decision theory

Fisher's statistical inference

Fisher unified chi-squared, small-sample t and variance analysis into a framework tested on Rothamsted crop data.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1908

    Student's t-distribution for small samples

    precursor

    Student (Gosset), 'The Probable Error of a Mean', Biometrika 6 (1908)

    Gosset derives the distribution of the mean for small samples, which Fisher later used in inference.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1908 Biometrika record Crossref record
    • Gosset invented the t-test for small samples MacTutor: Gosset biography
  2. 1912

    Fisher's absolute criterion for fitting frequency curves

    precursor

    R. A. Fisher, On an absolute criterion for fitting frequency curves, Messenger of Mathematics (2) 41 (1912) 155-160

    Fisher proposes choosing the curve that maximises the sum of the logarithms of the ordinate probabilities, the criterion later called maximum likelihood.

    Evidence (1)
    • Review: the curve is chosen to maximise log P' = sum of log p, with P' proportional to the probability of the observations zbMATH Open id 2625372 (Messenger (2) 41, 155-160)
  3. 1918

    Fisher: Correlation between relatives (variance and its analysis)

    precursor

    R. A. Fisher, The Correlation between Relatives on the Supposition of Mendelian Inheritance, Transactions of the Royal Society of Edinburgh 52 (1918) 399-433

    Fisher introduces the term variance and proposes the analysis of the variance into components, in a paper on population genetics.

    Evidence (2)
    • Fisher introduced the term variance and proposed its formal analysis in a 1918 article on theoretical population genetics Wikipedia, Analysis of variance
    • Trans. R. Soc. Edinburgh 52(2), 399-433; Crossref issued date 1919 Crossref 10.1017/S0080456800012163
  4. 1921

    Crop-yield variation analysed on Rothamsted data

    first application

    Fisher, 'Studies in crop variation. I.', The Journal of Agricultural Science 11 (1921)

    Fisher analyses long-term Broadbalk wheat yields at Rothamsted with variance methods, the first purposeful test on field data.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1921 J. Agric. Sci. record on Broadbalk yields Crossref record
    • His first application of the analysis of variance to data was published in 1921, Studies in Crop Variation I Wikipedia, Analysis of variance
  5. 1921

    Fisher's likelihood interval (Metron paper)

    first statement

    R. A. Fisher, "On the probable error of a coefficient of correlation deduced from a small sample", Metron 1 (1921) 3-32

    Fisher's 1921 paper introduces the likelihood interval, the earliest public form of the likelihood idea found.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: the 1921 Metron paper introduced what is today called the 'likelihood interval'; the 1922 paper introduced the term 'method of maximum likelihood'. Wikipedia, Likelihood function
  6. 1922

    Fisher's foundations paper (likelihood, consistency, efficiency)

    key development

    Fisher, 'On the mathematical foundations of theoretical statistics', Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 222 (1922)

    Fisher introduces the maximum-likelihood framework and criteria for estimators as the basis of statistical inference.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1922 Phil. Trans. record Crossref record
    • Biography says Fisher introduced likelihood in 1921 MacTutor: Fisher biography
  7. 1925

    Fisher's Statistical Methods for Research Workers

    consolidation

    Fisher, Statistical Methods for Research Workers (Oliver & Boyd, 1925)

    The 1925 book is the first systematic treatment of Fisher's methods, including analysis of variance, for working scientists.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1925 book record Open Library search
