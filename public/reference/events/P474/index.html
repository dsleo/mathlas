Hida theory: p-adic families of modular forms

Ordinary cusp forms are organised into p-adic analytic families whose Galois representations vary in a Hecke algebra.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1973

    Serre's p-adic modular forms and zeta functions

    precursor

    Serre, Formes modulaires et fonctions zeta p-adiques, in Modular Functions of One Variable III, LNM 350 (1973)

    Introduces p-adic modular forms and p-adic L-functions, the setting in which Hida's families live.

    precursor

    Katz, p-adic properties of modular schemes and modular forms, in Modular Functions of One Variable III, LNM 350 (1973)

  3. 1981

    Hida's congruences of cusp forms and zeta values

    precursor

    Hida, Congruences of cusp forms and special values of their zeta functions, Invent. Math. (1981)

    Congruences between cusp forms and special values, the arithmetic input behind ordinary families.

  4. 1986

    Galois representations over GL2 of Iwasawa algebra

    first statement

    Hida, Galois representations into GL2(Zp[[X]]) attached to ordinary cusp forms, Invent. Math. (1986)

    Shows that ordinary cusp forms in a family give Galois representations varying over a p-adic power series ring, the core of Hida theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Haruzo Hida: Hida's work on p-adic L-functions and p-adic Hecke rings Wikipedia: Haruzo Hida
  5. 1986

    Ordinary Hecke algebra as a family over Iwasawa algebra

    key development

    Hida, Iwasawa modules attached to congruences of cusp forms, Ann. Sci. ENS (1986)

    Shows that the ordinary Hecke algebra of a tower of levels is finite over the Iwasawa algebra, giving the p-adic families of forms.

    Hida's book on L-functions and Eisenstein series

    consolidation

    Hida, Elementary Theory of L-functions and Eisenstein Series, LMS Student Texts 26, Cambridge UP (1993)

    Textbook account of the elementary theory of L-functions and Eisenstein series underlying Hida theory.
