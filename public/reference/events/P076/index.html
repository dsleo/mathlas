1983 · Analysis

Sullivan's dictionary: rational maps and Kleinian groups

Iteration of rational maps is matched term by term with the theory of Kleinian groups, so results and techniques of one side suggest and prove statements on the other.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1918

    Julia on iteration of rational functions

    precursor

    G. Julia, Mémoire sur l'itération des fonctions rationnelles, J. Math. Pures Appl. (8) 1 (1918)

    Founding memoir on iteration of rational maps and their Julia sets.

    Evidence (1)
    • Jahrbuch record: Julia, Mémoire sur l'itération des fonctions rationnelles, 1918. zbMATH record 46.0520.06
  2. 1919

    Fatou on functional equations

    precursor

    P. Fatou, Sur les équations fonctionnelles, Bull. Soc. Math. France 47 (1919)

    Parallel founding work on iteration of analytic maps.

    Evidence (1)
    • Jahrbuch record: Fatou, Sur les équations fonctionnelles, Bull. SMF 47, 1919. zbMATH record 47.0921.02
  3. 1964

    Ahlfors on finitely generated Kleinian groups

    precursor

    L. Ahlfors, Finitely generated Kleinian groups, Amer. J. Math. 86 (1964)

    Finiteness theorem for Kleinian groups, one side of the later dictionary.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Ahlfors, Finitely generated Kleinian groups, Am. J. Math. 86, 413-429, 1964. zbMATH record 0133.04201
  4. 1981

    Sullivan on ergodic theory at infinity

    precursor

    D. Sullivan, On the ergodic theory at infinity of an arbitrary discrete group of hyperbolic motions, Ann. of Math. Studies 97 (1981)

    Ergodic methods for Kleinian limit sets, which Sullivan then carries to rational maps.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Sullivan, On the ergodic theory at infinity..., 1981. Crossref, DOI 10.1515/9781400881550-035
  5. 1983

    Conformal dynamical systems

    first statement

    Sullivan, Conformal dynamical systems, in Geometric Dynamics (Rio de Janeiro 1981), Lecture Notes in Math. 1007 (1983)

    Sullivan's conformal dynamics paper; the dictionary between Kleinian groups and rational maps is attributed to this line of work.

    Evidence (1)
    • Sullivan lists Conformal Dynamical Systems in Geometric Dynamics, LNM 1007 (1983), pp. 725–752. Dennis Sullivan, publications list
  6. 1985

    No wandering domains for rational maps

    key development

    Sullivan, Quasiconformal homeomorphisms and dynamics I. Solution of the Fatou-Julia problem on wandering domains, Ann. of Math. 122 (1985) 401-418

    Solves the Fatou-Julia problem on wandering domains, a rational-map result of the kind the dictionary transfers from Kleinian groups.

    Dating or attribution note

    The link to the dictionary is interpretive; the paper's Kleinian-group analogue was not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Journal, volume, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
  7. 1995

    McMullen's Complex dynamics and renormalization

    consolidation

    C. McMullen, Complex Dynamics and Renormalization, Ann. of Math. Studies 135, Princeton 1994

    Monograph treatment of rational dynamics using the Teichmüller-theoretic side of the dictionary.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: McMullen, Complex dynamics and renormalization, 1995. zbMATH record 0822.30002
  8. 1998

    McMullen and Sullivan: Teichmüller space of a dynamical system

    key development

    C. McMullen, D. Sullivan, Quasiconformal homeomorphisms and dynamics III, Adv. Math. 135 (1998)

    Builds a Teichmüller space for holomorphic dynamical systems, making the analogy with Kleinian groups precise.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: McMullen, Sullivan, Quasiconformal homeomorphisms and dynamics III, Adv. Math., 1998. Crossref, DOI 10.1006/aima.1998.1726
