Compressed sensing

Sparse signals are recovered exactly from few random measurements by l1 minimisation, using harmonic analysis and random matrices.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2004

    Sparse recovery from incomplete Fourier samples

    first statement

    E. Candès, J. Romberg and T. Tao, Robust Uncertainty Principles: Exact Signal Reconstruction from Highly Incomplete Frequency Information, arXiv:math/0409186 (2004)

    The preprint proves sparse-signal recovery from incomplete frequency information using convex minimization.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the public submission to September 2004 and describes the recovery theorem. Original bibliographic record
  2. 2006

    Compressed sensing paper published

    key development

    D. Donoho, Compressed Sensing, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 52 (2006)

    Donoho’s named treatment helped define the compressed-sensing programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • IEEE DOI identifies the 2006 publication. Original bibliographic record
  3. 2013

    Foucart-Rauhut compressive sensing textbook

    consolidation

    Foucart and Rauhut, A Mathematical Introduction to Compressive Sensing, Birkhäuser, 2013

    The standard textbook on compressed sensing and sparse recovery.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record for this title, year 2013, ISBN 978-0-8176-4947-0 zbMATH Open
