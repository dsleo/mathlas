2014 · Number theory

Kakeya and Bourgain-Guth, decoupling for Vinogradov

Bourgain-Demeter-Guth proved the main Vinogradov mean-value conjecture using l^2 decoupling from harmonic analysis.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2010

    Bourgain–Guth multilinear restriction argument

    precursor

    J. Bourgain and L. Guth, Bounds on oscillatory integral operators based on multilinear estimates, arXiv:1012.3760 (2010)

    Multilinear estimates improve restriction bounds and connect to curved Kakeya problems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public preprint posted December 2010 arXiv
  2. 2014

    Sharp l2 decoupling proved

    first statement

    J. Bourgain and C. Demeter, The proof of the l2 Decoupling Conjecture, arXiv:1403.5335 (2014)

    The public paper proves the decoupling conjecture for positive-curvature hypersurfaces and cones.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted March 2014 arXiv
  3. 2015

    Decoupling proves Vinogradov mean-value conjecture

    first application

    J. Bourgain, C. Demeter and L. Guth, Proof of the main conjecture in Vinogradov's mean value theorem for degrees higher than three, arXiv:1512.01565 (2015)

    A sharp decoupling inequality for curves yields the main conjecture in Vinogradov's mean-value theorem for degrees above three.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted December 2015; abstract identifies the method arXiv
  4. 2016

    Decoupling study guide

    consolidation

    J. Bourgain and C. Demeter, A study guide for the l2 Decoupling Theorem, arXiv:1604.06032 (2016)

    A self-contained proof organizes the decoupling theorem for readers.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public guide posted April 2016 arXiv
