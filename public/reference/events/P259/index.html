1974 · Algebraic geometry

Deligne proof of Weil conjectures

Etale cohomology plus Lefschetz pencils and Rankin methods proved the Riemann hypothesis for varieties over finite fields.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1949

    Weil conjectures stated

    precursor

    Weil, Numbers of solutions of equations in finite fields, Bull. AMS 55 (1949)

    States the conjectures on zeta functions of varieties over finite fields, including the Riemann hypothesis analogue.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Weil conjectures: Weil's conjectures and the three parts Wikipedia: Weil conjectures
  2. 1960

    Rationality of the zeta function (Dwork)

    precursor

    Dwork, On the rationality of the zeta function of an algebraic variety, Amer. J. Math. 82 (1960)

    First proof of the rationality part of the Weil conjectures, by p-adic methods, not the Riemann hypothesis part.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Weil conjectures: rationality proved by Dwork Wikipedia: Weil conjectures
  3. 1965

    Grothendieck proves all but the Riemann hypothesis part

    precursor

    Grothendieck, Formule de Lefschetz et rationalite des fonctions L, Seminaire Bourbaki (1965); SGA 5, LNM 589 (1977)

    Via etale cohomology and the Lefschetz formula, Grothendieck with Artin and Verdier proves the zeta-function formula and all but the Riemann hypothesis part.

    Evidence (2)
    • Wikipedia Weil conjectures: Grothendieck with Artin and Verdier proved all but the RH part (1965) Wikipedia: Weil conjectures
    • SGA 5 volume record (1977) containing the Lefschetz formula chapter Crossref
  4. 1974

    Deligne's proof of the Riemann hypothesis part (Weil I)

    first statement

    Deligne, La conjecture de Weil I, Publ. Math. IHES 43 (1974)

    Completes the proof of the Weil conjectures, including the Riemann hypothesis, using Lefschetz pencils and Rankin methods.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • MacTutor Deligne: solution of the three Weil conjectures in 1974 MacTutor: Deligne
  5. 1980

    Deligne's Weil II: general theorems

    key development

    Deligne, La conjecture de Weil II, Publ. Math. IHES 52 (1980)

    Extends the Weil I methods with further general theorems and the weight machinery.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
  6. 1988

    Freitag and Kiehl, 'Etale cohomology and the Weil conjecture'

    consolidation

    E. Freitag, R. Kiehl, Etale Cohomology and the Weil Conjecture, Ergebnisse der Math. 3. Folge 13, Berlin: Springer, 1988 (with a historical introduction by J. A. Dieudonne)

    A monograph giving a systematic account of etale cohomology and the proof of the Weil conjectures, after Deligne.

    Evidence (1)
