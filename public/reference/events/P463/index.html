1934 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Leray-Schauder degree and topological methods for nonlinear PDE

Brouwer's degree is extended to compact perturbations of the identity in Banach spaces, giving existence results for nonlinear partial differential equations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1912

    Brouwer's degree of maps

    precursor

    Brouwer, 'Uber Abbildung von Mannigfaltigkeiten', Mathematische Annalen 71 (1912)

    Brouwer's degree for maps between finite-dimensional manifolds is the ancestor of the Leray-Schauder degree.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper record: Math. Ann. 1912 (Crossref also has a 1911 record under the same title) Crossref
  2. 1930

    Schauder's fixed point theorem in Banach spaces

    precursor

    Schauder, 'Der Fixpunktsatz in Funktionalraumen', Studia Mathematica 2 (1930) 171-180

    Schauder's fixed point theorem for compact maps in Banach spaces is the infinite-dimensional step that Leray and Schauder generalised to a degree.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record: Studia Mathematica 1930 Crossref
    • Schauder published fixed point theorems for Banach spaces in 1930 MacTutor: Schauder biography
  3. 1934

    Leray-Schauder degree defined

    first statement

    Leray and Schauder, 'Topologie et equations fonctionnelles', Annales scientifiques de l'Ecole Normale Superieure 51 (1934)

    The degree is defined for maps of the form identity minus a compact map on balls in a Banach space, and used for existence results.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record: Ann. Sci. ENS 1934 Crossref
    • 1934 paper defines what is now the Leray-Schauder degree MacTutor: Leray biography
  4. 1934

    Existence for nonlinear PDE via the degree

    first application

    Same paper: Leray and Schauder (1934)

    The degree was invented to prove existence results for partial differential equations, the first purposeful use.

    Evidence (2)
    • The degree was invented to prove existence for PDEs Wikipedia: Leray-Schauder degree (lead)
    • 1934 paper is on topology and partial differential equations MacTutor: Leray biography
  5. 1985

    Deimling's Nonlinear Functional Analysis

    consolidation

    Deimling, Nonlinear Functional Analysis (Springer, 1985)

    A standard textbook treatment of degree theory in Banach spaces and its applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book record: Springer 1985 Crossref
