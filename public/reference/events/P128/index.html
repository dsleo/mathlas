1825 · Complex analysis

Cauchy and the beginnings of complex analysis

Integrals along paths in the complex plane gave theorems and the calculus of residues.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1814

    Cauchy's memoir on definite integrals

    precursor

    A.-L. Cauchy, memoir on definite integrals presented 1814

    A memoir on evaluating definite integrals became the basis of Cauchy's later theory of complex functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: in 1814 he published the memoir on definite integrals that later became the basis of his theory of complex functions. MacTutor, Cauchy biography
  2. 1825

    Memoire sur les integrales definies prises entre des limites imaginaires

    first statement

    A.-L. Cauchy, Memoire sur les integrales definies, prises entre des limites imaginaires, Paris, de Bure, 1825

    Cauchy treated integrals along paths between complex limits, the setting of his integral theorem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Library records dated 1825 for a memoir with this title. Internet Archive items mmoiresurlesin00cauc and bub_gb_Hx0paIDkUbQC
  3. 1826

    Calculus of residues

    key development

    A.-L. Cauchy, Sur un nouveau genre de calcul analogue au calcul infinitesimal, 1826

    Cauchy began the calculus of residues, a systematic way to evaluate integrals using singularities.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor: he began a study of the calculus of residues in 1826 in this work. MacTutor, Cauchy biography
  4. 1829

    Complex function of a complex variable defined

    key development

    A.-L. Cauchy, Lecons sur le calcul differentiel, 1829

    Cauchy gave, for the first time per MacTutor, a definition of a complex function of a complex variable.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor attributes the first definition of a complex function of a complex variable to the 1829 lectures. MacTutor, Cauchy biography
  5. 1829

    Cauchy's Lecons sur le calcul differentiel

    consolidation

    A.-L. Cauchy, Lecons sur le calcul differentiel, Paris, 1829

    Textbook in which Cauchy defines a complex function of a complex variable, so it also consolidates the subject.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record of the 1829 Lecons sur le calcul differentiel. Open Library work record
    • Cauchy defined a complex function of a complex variable for the first time in a textbook in 1829. Wikipedia, Augustin-Louis Cauchy
  6. 1900

    Goursat removes the continuity hypothesis

    key development

    E. Goursat, Sur la definition generale des fonctions analytiques, d'apres Cauchy, Trans. AMS 1 (1900) 14-16

    Goursat proved the integral theorem assuming only that the complex derivative exists.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record, Transactions of the AMS 1, 1900, pages 14-16. Crossref DOI 10.1090/s0002-9947-1900-1500519-7
    • Wikipedia: Goursat showed the theorem holds with only a derivative existing. English Wikipedia, Cauchy's integral theorem
