2023 · Number theory

Relative Langlands duality and electromagnetic duality

Periods and L-functions were indexed by dual Hamiltonian spaces, guided by electromagnetic duality in physics, to extend the Langlands correspondence between periods and L-functions in both directions.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2008

    Gauge-theory boundary S-duality

    precursor

    D. Gaiotto and E. Witten, S-Duality of Boundary Conditions In N=4 Super Yang-Mills Theory (2008)

    The 2008 physics paper develops the boundary-condition duality used as analogy.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents gauge-theory boundary s-duality in 2008 arXiv
  2. 2012

    Spherical-variety period framework

    precursor

    Y. Sakellaridis and A. Venkatesh, Periods and harmonic analysis on spherical varieties (2012)

    The 2012 work develops period and dual-group questions preceding the three-author proposal.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents spherical-variety period framework in 2012 arXiv
  3. 2023

    Relative Langlands proposal circulates publicly

    first statement

    An author-hosted first version gives the Hamiltonian-space duality and electric–magnetic analogy.

    Evidence (2)
    • The source documents relative langlands proposal circulates publicly in 2023 Venkatesh author site / first draft
    • A dated July 2023 account links the already circulating first draft Columbia mathematics blog
  4. 2024

    Proposal receives arXiv record

    key development

    D. Ben-Zvi, Y. Sakellaridis and A. Venkatesh, Relative Langlands Duality (2024)

    The named proposal has a dated public bibliographic record in September 2024.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents proposal receives arxiv record in 2024 arXiv
  5. 2025

    Oberwolfach Arbeitsgemeinschaft names the subject

    institution

    D. Ben-Zvi, Y. Sakellaridis and A. Venkatesh, Arbeitsgemeinschaft: Relative Langlands Duality (2025)

    A named workshop report treats relative Langlands duality systematically.
