1922 · Logic & foundations

Hilbert's programme

Hilbert proposed to prove consistency of mathematics by finitary methods.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1900

    Hilbert's Problem 2: consistency of arithmetic axioms

    precursor

    Hilbert, D., Mathematische Probleme, Nachrichten von der Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Goettingen, 1900

    Hilbert's Paris lecture asks for a proof of the consistency of the arithmetical axioms, the problem the programme later addresses without yet giving a method.

    Evidence (1)
    • Mathematische Probleme appears in the Nachrichten Goettingen in 1900, pages 253-297 zbMATH record, Zbl 31.0068.03
  2. 1905

    Hilbert's Heidelberg lecture on the foundations of logic and arithmetic

    precursor

    Hilbert, D., Ueber die Grundlagen der Logik und der Arithmetik, Verhandlungen des Dritten Internationalen Mathematiker-Kongresses, 1905

    Hilbert's lecture for the 1904 congress sketches a proof-theoretic route to consistency; this is the earliest public statement of the approach found.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lecture published in the Verhandlungen of the Third International Congress in 1905 (given 1904) zbMATH record, Zbl 36.0084.03
    • SEP: Hilbert presented a sketch of a consistency proof in his Heidelberg talk (1905); his own programme was presented in Hamburg talks of 1921, published 1922. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Hilbert's Program
  3. 1922

    Hilbert's Neubegruendung der Mathematik

    first statement

    Hilbert, D., Neubegruendung der Mathematik. Erste Mitteilung, Abhandlungen aus dem Mathematischen Seminar der Hamburgischen Universitaet, 1922

    Hilbert names and formulates the programme: prove the consistency of mathematics with finitary (intuitive) methods.

    Evidence (3)
    • Neubegruendung der Mathematik, Erste Mitteilung, Hamburg Abhandlungen 1922 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf02940589
    • Wikipedia says Hilbert's programme was formulated in the early 1920s Wikipedia, Hilbert's program
    • SEP: Hilbert presented a sketch of a consistency proof in his Heidelberg talk (1905); his own programme was presented in Hamburg talks of 1921, published 1922. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Hilbert's Program
  4. 1931

    Godel's incompleteness theorems

    key development

    Goedel, K., Ueber formal unentscheidbare Saetze der Principia Mathematica und verwandter Systeme I, Monatshefte fuer Mathematik und Physik, 1931

    Godel's incompleteness theorems show that most of the programme's goals cannot be reached in their obvious form.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appears in Monatshefte fuer Mathematik und Physik in 1931 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf01700692
    • Wikipedia says Godel's incompleteness theorems published in 1931 showed the programme unattainable for key areas Wikipedia, Hilbert's program
  5. 1934

    Grundlagen der Mathematik

    consolidation

    Hilbert, D. and Bernays, P., Grundlagen der Mathematik, vol. 1, Springer, 1934

    The Hilbert-Bernays volumes give the first systematic treatment of the proof theory of the programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Grundlagen der Mathematik published 1934 under Hilbert's name Open Library search record
  6. 1936

    Gentzen's consistency proof of arithmetic

    first application

    Gentzen, G., Die Widerspruchsfreiheit der reinen Zahlentheorie, Mathematische Annalen, 1936

    Gentzen proves the consistency of elementary number theory by transfinite induction, the first purposeful consistency proof for arithmetic in the programme's spirit.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper appears in Mathematische Annalen in 1936 Crossref record, DOI 10.1007/bf01565428
    • MacTutor quotes Gentzen's aim to prove the consistency of elementary number theory MacTutor biography of Gentzen
