1851 · Complex analysis

Riemann surfaces

Riemann treated multi-valued algebraic functions as single-valued functions on a covering surface, bringing topology into complex analysis.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1850

    Puiseux on algebraic functions and branch points

    precursor

    Victor Puiseux, Recherches sur les fonctions algebriques, J. Math. Pures Appl. 15, 1850

    Analyses algebraic functions near branch points, the local picture Riemann's surfaces assemble.

    Evidence (2)
    • Numdam record: Recherches sur les fonctions algebriques, J. Math. Pures Appl. 15. Numdam JMPA_1850_1_15__365_0
    • Riemann's thesis builds on Cauchy and on Puiseux's ideas of branch points. MacTutor, Riemann biography
  2. 1851

    Riemann's doctoral dissertation

    first statement

    Riemann, Grundlagen für eine allgemeine Theorie der Functionen einer veränderlichen complexen Grösse, doctoral thesis, Göttingen, 1851

    Riemann's thesis studied analytic functions through geometric surfaces on which many-valued functions become single-valued, introducing topology into complex function theory.

    Evidence (3)
    • MacTutor says the thesis studied what we now call Riemann surfaces and was examined on 16 December 1851, building on Puiseux's branch points MacTutor biography of Riemann
    • MacTutor's topology history says Riemann studied connectivity in 1851 and introduced his surfaces for the equation f(w,z)=0 MacTutor, A history of Topology
    • Neumann's 1865 preface cites Riemann's doctoral dissertation (Goettingen 1851) as introducing, for multivalued functions, surfaces made of several linked sheets, which Neumann calls a completely new idea. Neumann, Vorlesungen ueber Riemann's Theorie der Abel'schen Integrale (1865), Vorwort
  3. 1857

    Riemann's theory of Abelian functions

    key development

    Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 54, 115-155, 1857

    Riemann treated many-valued functions as single-valued on a special surface and solved general inversion problems of Abelian integrals, showing the surface concept carried real theorems.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Riemann, Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen, Crelle 54, 115-155 (1857) Crossref 10.1515/crll.1857.54.115
    • MacTutor says this paper developed the Riemann surface idea and solved inversion problems for Abelian integrals MacTutor biography of Riemann
  4. 1865

    C. Neumann's lectures on Riemann's theory

    consolidation

    Carl Neumann, Vorlesungen ueber Riemann's Theorie der Abel'schen Integrale, Leipzig, 1865

    An early textbook account of Riemann's surfaces and Abelian integrals, written fourteen years after the thesis.

    Evidence (1)
    • Internet Archive scan of the 1865 Teubner edition, title page and preface. Internet Archive 10082480bsb
  5. 1882

    Klein's Riemann's theory of algebraic functions

    consolidation

    Felix Klein, Ueber Riemann's Theorie der algebraischen Functionen und ihrer Integrale, Leipzig: Teubner, 1882

    A book-length account of Riemann's theory of algebraic functions and their integrals through Riemann surfaces, offered as a supplement to the usual presentations.

    Dating or attribution note

    Title, subtitle ('Eine Ergaenzung der gewoehnlichen Darstellungen') and 1882 imprint checked on records; the book was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library work record: Klein, Ueber Riemann's Theorie der Algebraischen Functionen und ihrer Integrale, first published 1882. Open Library work OL3783629W
    • Internet Archive scan of the 1882 Teubner edition. Internet Archive ueberriemannsthe00kleirich
  6. 1913

    Weyl's book on the idea of a Riemann surface

    consolidation

    Weyl, Die Idee der Riemannschen Fläche, Teubner, Leipzig, 1913

    Weyl gave a rigorous, abstract definition of a Riemann surface as a manifold with a complex structure, severing it from covering of the plane.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH lists Weyl, Die Idee der Riemannschen Fläche (1913) zbMATH 2621710
    • Internet Archive copy of the 1913 first edition archive.org dieideederrieman00weyluoft
    • Wikipedia's article on Riemann surfaces cites Weyl 1913 as the standard foundational text English Wikipedia, Riemann surface
  7. 1925

    Rado shows Riemann surfaces have countable bases

    key development

    T. Rado, Ueber den Begriff der Riemannschen Flaeche, Acta Szeged 2, 1925

    Settles the abstract definition of a Riemann surface, showing the surfaces are not too large.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record, Acta Szeged 2, 101-121 (1925). zbMATH 2591746
    • Wikipedia: Rado's theorem, proved by Tibor Rado (1925), states that every connected Riemann surface is second-countable. English Wikipedia, Rado's theorem (Riemann surfaces)
