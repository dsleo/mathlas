1918 · Geometry

Hausdorff dimension and fractal dimension

Hausdorff allowed non-integer dimensions defined metrically, Besicovitch developed the theory, and Mandelbrot's fractal definition set it against topological dimension.

Also known as: Hausdorff-Besicovitch dimension

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1914

    Carathéodory's outer measure construction

    precursor

    Carathéodory, outer measure construction (1914)

    Carathéodory's construction of outer measure, which Hausdorff generalised to define fractional dimensions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Hausdorff's construction generalises one introduced five years earlier by Carathéodory MacTutor: Hausdorff biography
  2. 1918

    Hausdorff introduces dimension via outer measure

    first statement

    F. Hausdorff, 'Dimension und aeusseres Mass', Mathematische Annalen 79 (1918) 157-179

    Hausdorff defines a metric dimension that can take non-integer values, the basis of later fractal dimension.

    Evidence (4)
    • Hausdorff's 1918 communication and 1919 publication of Dimension und äusseres Mass MacTutor: Fractal geometry
    • Introduced the notion of Hausdorff dimension in 'Dimension und äusseres Mass' (1919) MacTutor: Hausdorff biography
    • Reprint in Gesammelte Werke (the original Math. Ann. record was not returned by Crossref) Crossref record
    • Crossref: 'Dimension und äußeres Maß', Math. Ann. 79, issue 1-2, pp. 157-179, print date 1918. Crossref 10.1007/bf01457179
  3. 1935

    Besicovitch applies Hausdorff measure to non-differentiability sets

    first application

    Besicovitch, 'Sets of points of non-differentiability of absolutely continuous functions', Mathematische Annalen (1935)

    Besicovitch uses fractional-dimension measures on sets of non-differentiability, a first purposeful use of the dimension on a target problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Besicovitch 1935 Math. Ann. record (title truncated in Crossref) Crossref record
    • Besicovitch wrote three papers on Hausdorff's dimension 1934-1937 MacTutor: Fractal geometry
  4. 1967

    Coastline length measured by fractional dimension

    first application

    Mandelbrot, 'How Long Is the Coast of Britain?', Science 156 (1967)

    Mandelbrot applies statistical self-similarity and fractional dimension to measure the coastline of Britain.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1967 Science record Crossref record
  5. 1975

    Mandelbrot coins 'fractal' in Les objets fractals

    naming

    Mandelbrot, Les objets fractals: forme, hasard et dimension (1975)

    Mandelbrot names the sets fractals and defines them as sets whose Hausdorff dimension exceeds their topological dimension.

    Evidence (2)
    • 1975 book record, Les objets fractals Open Library search
    • Mandelbrot defined fractals in 1975 via Hausdorff dimension MacTutor: Fractal geometry
