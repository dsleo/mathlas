Vector analysis

Gibbs and Heaviside extracted vector methods from quaternions.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1844

    Grassmann's extension theory

    precursor

    Grassmann, Die lineale Ausdehnungslehre, 1844

    An algebra of vector-like quantities that later vector analysts drew on.

    Evidence (1)
    • Archive.org record: 1844. Internet Archive
  2. 1853

    Hamilton's Lectures on Quaternions

    precursor

    Hamilton, Lectures on Quaternions, 1853

    Quaternions, with their scalar and vector parts, were the starting point of vector analysis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: 1853. Internet Archive
    • Says vector calculus was developed from quaternion theory by Gibbs and Heaviside. Wikipedia: Vector calculus
  3. 1881–1884

    Gibbs's Elements of Vector Analysis

    first statement

    Gibbs, Elements of Vector Analysis, New Haven, 1881-1884 (privately printed)

    Gibbs extracted vector methods from quaternions in a pamphlet for his students, apparently the first text of its kind.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: 1881. Internet Archive
    • Archive.org record: 1884 edition. Internet Archive
  4. 1885–1887

    Heaviside's vector treatment of electromagnetism

    first application

    Heaviside, Electromagnetic induction and its propagation, The Electrician, 1885-1887

    Heaviside used vector methods to restate Maxwell's theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Says his vector methods overshadowed his electromagnetism results. MacTutor biography of Heaviside
    • Record: reprint in Electrical Papers. Crossref
  5. 1891

    Gibbs defends vectors against quaternionists

    key development

    Gibbs, Quaternions and the Ausdehnungslehre, Nature 44, 79-82, 1891

    A public exchange in Nature made the case for vector analysis over quaternions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Nature 44 (1891) 79-82. Crossref
    • Tait's reply, Nature 44 (1891) 105-106. Crossref
  6. 1901

    Gibbs-Wilson Vector Analysis

    consolidation

    Gibbs and Wilson, Vector Analysis, Yale, 1901

    The textbook that fixed the notation and terminology.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: 1901. Internet Archive
    • Says most notation was established by the 1901 book. Wikipedia: Vector calculus
