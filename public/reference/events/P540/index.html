1985 · Probability

Probabilistic construction of Liouville conformal field theory

Polyakov's path integral for Liouville theory was made rigorous via the Gaussian free field and Kahane's multiplicative chaos, and its correlation functions were shown to agree with the physicists' bootstrap and the DOZZ formula.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1981

    Polyakov's quantum geometry of bosonic strings

    precursor

    Polyakov, A. M., Quantum geometry of bosonic strings, Phys. Lett. B 103, 1981

    Polyakov writes the path integral over metrics that gives the Liouville action, the physics object the probabilistic construction later makes rigorous.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Physics Letters B, 1981 (DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(81)90743-7) Crossref record
    • Liouville theory's path integral was difficult to define and compute; its rigorous construction came in the 2010s Wikipedia: Liouville field theory
  2. 1985

    Multiplicative chaos

    first statement

    Kahane, J.-P., Sur le chaos multiplicatif, Ann. Sci. Math. Québec 9, 1985

    Kahane introduces multiplicative chaos, the random measure construction used to define the Liouville measure.

    Evidence (1)
    • The journal's own table of contents records Kahane's Sur le chaos multiplicatif in volume 9(2), December 1985, pp. 105–150. Annales des sciences mathématiques du Québec
  3. 2016

    Liouville quantum gravity on the Riemann sphere

    first application

    David, F., Kupiainen, A., Rhodes, R., Vargas, V., Liouville quantum gravity on the Riemann sphere, Comm. Math. Phys., 2016

    The 2016 paper constructs the Liouville path integral probabilistically on the sphere, the first purposeful use of the chaos tools on the physics object.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Comm. Math. Phys., 2016 (DOI 10.1007/s00220-016-2572-4) Crossref record
    • A rigorous probabilistic construction of the path integral was found in the 2010s Wikipedia: Liouville field theory
  4. 2016

    Kupiainen's 'Constructive Liouville Conformal Field Theory'

    naming

    A. Kupiainen, Constructive Liouville Conformal Field Theory, 2016

    The earliest zbMATH-indexed title using the phrase 'Liouville conformal field theory' in the constructive sense.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: Kupiainen, 'Constructive Liouville Conformal Field Theory', year 2016. zbMATH Open, API ti:"Liouville conformal field theory" search
  5. 2018

    The DOZZ formula from the path integral

    key development

    Kupiainen, A., Rhodes, R., Vargas, V., The DOZZ formula from the path integral, JHEP, 2018

    This paper derives the DOZZ structure constants from the probabilistic path integral.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in JHEP, 2018 (DOI 10.1007/jhep05(2018)094) Crossref record
  6. 2020

    Integrability of Liouville theory: proof of the DOZZ formula

    key development

    Kupiainen, A., Rhodes, R., Vargas, V., Integrability of Liouville theory: proof of the DOZZ formula, Annals of Mathematics 191, 2020

    The Annals paper proves the DOZZ formula and the conformal bootstrap for Liouville theory, the main theorem of the event.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Annals of Mathematics, 2020 (DOI 10.4007/annals.2020.191.1.2) Crossref record
    • Rigorous construction led to a proof of the DOZZ formula and the conformal bootstrap Wikipedia: Liouville field theory
