2015 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Fractal uncertainty principle and quantum chaos

A Fourier uncertainty principle for fractal-like sets was proved in one dimension and used to show waves cannot be trapped on a hyperbolic surface, then extended to higher dimensions.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2015

    Fractal uncertainty principle named in spectral-gap work

    first statement

    S. Dyatlov and J. Zahl, Spectral gaps, additive energy, and a fractal uncertainty principle, arXiv:1504.06589 (2015)

    The public paper introduces a fractal uncertainty principle in its spectral-gap argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted in April 2015 arXiv
  2. 2015

    Fractal uncertainty used for hyperbolic spectral gaps

    first application

    S. Dyatlov and J. Zahl, Spectral gaps, additive energy, and a fractal uncertainty principle, arXiv:1504.06589 (2015)

    The method yields an essential spectral gap for convex co-compact hyperbolic manifolds under the paper's dimension conditions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Abstract explicitly identifies the spectral-gap application arXiv
  3. 2016

    Spectral gaps without the pressure condition

    key development

    J. Bourgain and S. Dyatlov, Spectral gaps without the pressure condition, arXiv:1612.09040 (2016)

    A stronger fractal uncertainty result gives gaps for all convex co-compact hyperbolic surfaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted in December 2016; abstract names fractal uncertainty as the new ingredient arXiv
  4. 2019

    Survey of fractal uncertainty and quantum chaos

    consolidation

    S. Dyatlov, An introduction to fractal uncertainty principle, arXiv:1903.02599 (2019)

    The survey systematically presents the principle and its quantum-chaos applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public survey preprint posted in March 2019 arXiv
