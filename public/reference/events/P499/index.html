1971 · Algebraic geometry

Mixed Hodge theory (Deligne)

Cohomology of singular or open varieties carries a weight filtration, extending Hodge theory and mirroring weights in the Weil conjectures.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1960

    Virtual Betti numbers and the Weil conjectures

    precursor

    In the 1960s, after the Weil conjectures, Serre noticed that singular and non-proper varieties should carry virtual Betti numbers, the motivation for mixed structures.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia (Hodge structure): Serre's virtual Betti numbers noticed in the 1960s Wikipedia
  2. 1971

    Weight filtration for smooth varieties

    first statement

    Pierre Deligne, Theorie de Hodge II, Publications Mathematiques IHES 40, 1971

    Deligne introduces the weight filtration on the cohomology of smooth varieties and constructs mixed Hodge structures for that case.

    Evidence (2)
    • Publications Mathematiques IHES paper 'Theorie de Hodge II', 1971 Crossref
    • Wikipedia (Hodge structure): mixed Hodge structure for smooth varieties shown by Deligne (1971) Wikipedia
  3. 1974

    Mixed Hodge theory for arbitrary varieties

    key development

    Pierre Deligne, Theorie de Hodge III, Publications Mathematiques IHES 44, 1974

    Deligne extends the mixed Hodge structure to singular and non-proper complex varieties, which is the general theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Publications Mathematiques IHES paper 'Theorie de Hodge III', 1974 Crossref
    • Wikipedia (Hodge structure): general case by Deligne (1974) Wikipedia
  4. 2008

    Monograph on mixed Hodge structures

    consolidation

    Chris A. M. Peters, Joseph H. M. Steenbrink, Mixed Hodge Structures, Springer, 2008

    A systematic monograph on mixed Hodge structures and their applications.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: 'Mixed Hodge Structures' by Peters, Springer, 2008 Open Library
