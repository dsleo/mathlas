1050 · Combinatorics

Binomial coefficient triangle in China

A table of binomial coefficients up to the eighth power appeared as the 'ancient method' of expanding powers.

Also known as: Pascal's triangle in China

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 250 BCE

    Pingala's Meruprastara

    precursor

    Pingala, Chandahshastra

    A counting array for metres later read as binomial coefficients.

    Evidence (1)
    • Pingala (3rd/2nd century BCE) arranged counts of metres in a form later called Pascal's triangle. English Wikipedia, Pascal's triangle
  2. 975

    Halayudha's commentary on Pingala

    precursor

    Halayudha, Mritasanjivani, c. 975

    Described the Meruprastara as the arithmetic triangle.

    Evidence (1)
    • Halayudha (c. 975) clearly described the Meruprastara as what is now known as the arithmetic triangle. English Wikipedia, Pascal's triangle
  3. 1050

    Jia Xian's table of binomial coefficients

    first statement

    Jia Xian, Shi suo suan shu (lost)

    A table of coefficients of (a+b)^n up to n=6, built by adding the two entries above, used for root extraction.

    Evidence (2)
    • Jia Xian gave a table of binomial coefficients like Pascal's triangle up to row 1 6 15 20 15 6 1 and understood the additive generation rule. MacTutor, Jia Xian biography
    • Jia Xian described Pascal's triangle in the 11th century and used it for extracting roots. English Wikipedia, Jia Xian
  4. 1261

    Yang Hui, Xiangjie jiuzhang suanfa

    key development

    Yang Hui, Xiangjie jiuzhang suanfa (1261)

    The surviving source for the triangle, up to the sixth row, attributed to Jia Xian.

    Evidence (1)
    • In 1261 Yang wrote the Detailed analysis; he gives the triangle up to the sixth row, saying he learnt it from Jia Xian's treatise. MacTutor, Yang Hui biography
  5. 1303

    Zhu Shijie's diagram to the eighth power

    key development

    Zhu Shijie, Siyuan yujian (1303)

    The diagram gives coefficients up to the eighth power, called the 'ancient method' table.

    Evidence (1)
    • The book opens with Pascal's triangle giving coefficients up to the eighth power; Zhu makes no claim of originality. MacTutor, Zhu Shijie biography
  6. 1665

    Pascal, Traite du triangle arithmetique

    consolidation

    Blaise Pascal, Traite du triangle arithmetique (1665)

    The first systematic treatise on the triangle in Europe, with applications to probability.

    Evidence (2)
    • Open Library record, first published 1665. Open Library
    • Pascal collected known results and used them in probability, published posthumously in 1665. English Wikipedia, Pascal's triangle
  7. 1708

    Montmort names it for Pascal

    naming

    Pierre Raymond de Montmort (1708)

    The European name 'Pascal's triangle' dates from Montmort's 'table de M. Pascal'.

    Evidence (1)
    • Triangle later named for Pascal by Montmort (1708). English Wikipedia, Pascal's triangle
