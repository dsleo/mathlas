p-adic Hodge theory and Fontaine’s period rings

Fontaine’s period rings and filtered modules relate p-adic Galois representations to de Rham and crystalline invariants, creating a bridge between arithmetic and cohomology.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1979

    Filtered modules and Barsotti–Tate rings

    precursor

    J.-M. Fontaine, Modules galoisiens, modules filtrés et anneaux de Barsotti-Tate, Astérisque 65 (1979)

    An earlier filtered-module framework for p-adic Galois representations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Astérisque 65 published Fontaine’s filtered-module work in 1979 Numdam
  2. 1982

    Fontaine publishes the period-ring framework

    first statement

    J.-M. Fontaine, Sur certains types de représentations p-adiques du groupe de Galois d’un corps local, Ann. Math. 115 (1982)

    Constructs a Barsotti–Tate period ring and develops classes of p-adic local Galois representations.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals published Fontaine’s period-ring paper in 1982 Annals of Mathematics
  3. 1982

    Fontaine–Laffaille construct p-adic representations

    first application

    J.-M. Fontaine and G. Laffaille, Construction de représentations p-adiques, Ann. Sci. ENS 15 (1982)

    Uses filtered Dieudonné modules to explicitly construct p-adic Galois representations under stated hypotheses.

    Evidence (1)
    • The 1982 paper constructs representations from filtered modules; its abstract and introduction state the conditions and applications Numdam
  4. 1994

    Bures seminar published as Périodes p-adiques

    consolidation

    J.-M. Fontaine, Périodes p-adiques (Séminaire de Bures, 1988), Astérisque 223 (1994)

    The published seminar volume assembles period rings, representation classes, and comparisons among p-adic cohomology theories.

    Evidence (1)
    • The SMF describes the 1994 Astérisque volume and its 1988 seminar origin Société Mathématique de France
