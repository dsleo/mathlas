1971 · Functional & harmonic analysis

Fourier integral operators and microlocal analysis

Fourier transforms and symplectic geometry of the cotangent bundle are combined to study singularities of solutions of linear PDE.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1965

    Kohn-Nirenberg algebra of pseudo-differential operators

    precursor

    Kohn, Nirenberg, An algebra of pseudo-differential operators, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 18 (1965)

    Pseudo-differential operators with symbols give the calculus on which Fourier integral operators were later built.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics, 1965 Crossref
  2. 1971

    Hörmander, Fourier integral operators I

    first statement

    Hörmander, Fourier integral operators. I, Acta Math. 127 (1971)

    The first systematic definition and calculus of Fourier integral operators with phase functions and symbols.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: journal article, Acta Mathematica, 1971, Fourier integral operators. I Crossref
    • MacTutor: Hörmander's pseudo-differential and FIO theory is the basis of his PDE work MacTutor, Lars Hörmander
  3. 1972

    Duistermaat-Hörmander, Fourier integral operators II

    key development

    Duistermaat, Hörmander, Fourier integral operators. II, Acta Math. 128 (1972)

    Extends the FIO calculus to the composition and singularity analysis that makes FIOs usable on PDE problems.

    Dating or attribution note

    The specific propagation-of-singularities result is not checked in the record; the role rests on the title and the sequel's place in the programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Acta Mathematica, 1972, Fourier integral operators. II Crossref
  4. 1980

    Treves, Introduction to pseudodifferential and Fourier integral operators

    consolidation

    Treves, Introduction to Pseudodifferential and Fourier Integral Operators, Plenum (1980)

    A systematic textbook treatment of pseudo-differential and Fourier integral operators.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: François Treves, Introduction to pseudodifferential and Fourier integral operators, Plenum, first published 1980 Open Library
