1956 · Number theory

Selberg trace formula links spectra of surfaces to number theory

A formula equates sums over Laplace eigenvalues with sums over closed geodesics, mirroring the explicit formulas of prime number theory and later generalised by Arthur.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1956

    Selberg's trace formula for Riemann surfaces

    first statement

    A. Selberg, Harmonic analysis and discontinuous groups in weakly symmetric Riemannian spaces with applications to Dirichlet series, J. Indian Math. Soc. 20, 47-87, 1956

    Selberg's initial publication relates sums over Laplace eigenvalues to sums over closed geodesics, in analogy with prime-number explicit formulas.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH record: J. Indian Math. Soc. 20, 47-87 (1956). zbMATH 3120273
    • Initial publication in 1956 dealt with compact Riemann surfaces and the analogy with prime-number explicit formulas. Wikipedia, Selberg trace formula
    • Calls the trace formula Selberg's best and most important work. MacTutor, Selberg biography
  2. 1959

    Huber on hyperbolic space forms and geodesics

    first application

    Heinz Huber, Zur analytischen Theorie hyperbolischer Raumformen und Bewegungsgruppen, Math. Ann. 138, 1959

    Uses the trace-formula method to relate the spectrum to lengths of closed geodesics.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content not read; the application is inferred from the title and the standard account of Huber's geodesic counting. Treat as a candidate.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Math. Ann. 138 (1959), pp. 1-26. Crossref 10.1007/bf01369663
  3. 1972

    McKean's exposition for compact surfaces

    key development

    H. P. McKean, Selberg's trace formula as applied to a compact Riemann surface, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 25, 1972

    Gives a self-contained account of the formula and the zeta function for compact surfaces.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, CPAM 25 (1972), pp. 225-246. Crossref 10.1002/cpa.3160250302
  4. 1976

    Hejhal's Selberg trace formula for PSL(2,R), Volume I

    consolidation

    Hejhal, D., The Selberg Trace Formula for PSL(2,R), Volume I, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 548 (1976)

    The first monograph-length systematic treatment of the Selberg trace formula for PSL(2,R).

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record for Hejhal's chapter in LNM 548 (Volume I), year 1976 Crossref
  5. 1978

    Arthur's trace formula for reductive groups

    key development

    Arthur, J., A trace formula for reductive groups I, Duke Mathematical Journal 45 (1978)

    Generalises the Selberg trace formula to reductive groups, the framework behind Arthur's classification.

    Evidence (1)
    • Duke Mathematical Journal record for Arthur's trace formula paper, year 1978 Crossref
