1973 · Algebraic topology

Higher algebraic K-theory

Homotopy-theoretic constructions (plus construction, classifying spaces) define K-groups of rings in all degrees, importing topology into algebra and number theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1957

    Grothendieck's K-group construction

    precursor

    Grothendieck, Classes de faisceaux et théorème de Riemann-Roch, mimeographed notes, Princeton (1957)

    The K_0 group of a scheme in the Grothendieck-Riemann-Roch theorem, from which the name 'K-theory' is taken.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: the subject takes its name from a 1957 construction of Grothendieck Wikipedia, Algebraic K-theory
  2. 1957

    Grothendieck's K_0 in intersection theory

    precursor

    Grothendieck, Classes de faisceaux et theoreme de Riemann-Roch, mimeographed notes, Princeton, 1957

    Grothendieck defines K_0 of varieties in unpublished mimeographed notes, the origin named in later histories.

    Evidence (1)
    • K-theory takes its name from a 1957 construction of Grothendieck, in unpublished notes Wikipedia: Algebraic K-theory
  3. 1958

    Grothendieck-Riemann-Roch published by Borel and Serre

    precursor

    Borel, Serre, Le theoreme de Riemann-Roch, Bull. Soc. Math. France 86 (1958)

    The published exposition of Grothendieck's theorem, the first purposeful use of K_0 on a target problem (Riemann-Roch).

    Evidence (2)
    • Borel-Serre paper on Riemann-Roch, Bull SMF 1958, DOI 10.24033/bsmf.1500 Crossref
    • GRR is the main application of Grothendieck's K_0 Wikipedia: Algebraic K-theory
  4. 1968

    Bass, Algebraic K-theory

    precursor

    Bass, Algebraic K-theory, W. A. Benjamin (1968)

    The first book title using the name 'algebraic K-theory', covering the lower K-groups.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record: Hyman Bass, Algebraic K-theory, W.A. Benjamin, first published 1968 Open Library
  5. 1968

    Bass's monograph 'Algebraic K-theory'

    precursor

    Bass, Algebraic K-theory, W. A. Benjamin, New York, 1968

    A monograph titled with the field's name, the first title found in these records.

    Dating or attribution note

    Records show the 1968 title; the phrase may have appeared earlier in talks, which were not checked. Reclassified from naming to precursor: it predates this event's first statement (1973) and belongs to the wider field.

    Evidence (2)
    • Bass, Algebraic K-theory, 1968 (book record) Open Library search
    • Bass' book 'Algebraic K-theory' is cited in the K-theory history Wikipedia: Algebraic K-theory
  6. 1971

    Milnor's Introduction to Algebraic K-theory

    precursor

    Milnor, Introduction to Algebraic K-Theory, Annals of Mathematics Studies 72, Princeton, 1971

    A systematic treatment of the lower K-groups (K_0, K_1, K_2), the first textbook-level account.

    Evidence (3)
    • Milnor's Introduction to Algebraic K-Theory, DOI 10.1515/9781400881796 (Annals Studies 72) Crossref
    • zbMATH record: Milnor, Introduction to algebraic K-theory, Annals of Math. Studies 72, Princeton, 1971 zbMATH Open 3375711
    • Open Library: first publication year 1971 for Milnor's Introduction to Algebraic K-Theory Open Library search
  7. 1972

    Quillen computes higher K-groups of finite fields

    precursor

    Quillen, On the cohomology and K-theory of the general linear groups over a finite field, Annals of Math. 96 (1972)

    The first computation of higher K-groups of finite fields by the plus-construction method, a purposeful use on a target problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: journal article, The Annals of Mathematics, 1972 Crossref
    • MacTutor: Quillen was the principal architect of higher algebraic K-theory in 1972 MacTutor, Daniel Quillen
  8. 1973

    Quillen, Higher algebraic K-theory I

    first statement

    Quillen, Higher algebraic K-theory: I, Lecture Notes in Math. 341 (1973)

    The public definition of higher K-groups of rings through the plus construction and the Q-construction.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref: Lecture Notes in Mathematics, 1973, Higher algebraic K-theory: I Crossref
    • MacTutor: the 63-page LNM paper built higher K-theory from first principles MacTutor, Daniel Quillen
    • Quillen found the higher K-theory construction in 1972 and it was highly successful Wikipedia: Algebraic K-theory
  9. 1973

    Finite generation of K_i of rings of integers

    key development

    Quillen, Finite generation of the groups K_i of rings of algebraic integers, Lecture Notes in Math. 341 (1973)

    Quillen proves finite generation of higher K-groups for rings of integers, a main theorem showing the theory bites on number theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Quillen, Finite generation of the groups K_i..., DOI 10.1007/bfb0067056 Crossref
