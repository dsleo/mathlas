Kalman filter (recursive state-space estimation)

State-space recursive estimation unified filtering and control.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1880

    Thiele's recursive estimation procedure

    precursor

    T. N. Thiele, 1880 (analysed in S. L. Lauritzen, Int. Stat. Rev. 49 (1981) 319-331)

    Thiele derives a recursive procedure for estimating a regression component from a time series.

    Evidence (2)
    • Lauritzen 1981 analysis of Thiele's 1880 contribution Crossref
    • Wikipedia notes Thiele's recursive procedure as earlier Wikipedia, Kalman filter
  2. 1959

    Stratonovich's nonlinear filter

    precursor

    R. L. Stratonovich, Optimum nonlinear systems which select a signal with constant parameters from noise (Russian, 1959); English reprint 1965

    Stratonovich gives a nonlinear filter that the Kalman-Bucy filter is a special case of.

    Evidence (2)
    • English translation chapter record (1965) Crossref
    • Original dated 1959 and linked to the Kalman-Bucy filter Wikipedia, Kalman filter
  3. 1960

    Kalman's 'A New Approach to Linear Filtering and Prediction Problems'

    first statement

    Rudolf E. Kalman, A new approach to linear filtering and prediction problems, J. Basic Eng. 82 (1960) 35-45

    Kalman introduces recursive state-space filtering for linear systems with noise.

    Evidence (2)
    • Kalman 1960 record Crossref
    • Kalman filter attributed to Kalman 1960 and Kalman-Bucy 1961 Wikipedia, Kalman filter
  4. 1961

    Kalman and Bucy: continuous-time filter and Riccati variance equation

    key development

    Rudolf E. Kalman and Richard S. Bucy, New results in linear filtering and prediction theory, J. Basic Eng. 83 (1961) 95-108

    Derives the Riccati variance equation for the optimal filter, giving the continuous-time Kalman-Bucy filter.

    Evidence (2)
    • Kalman-Bucy 1961 record Crossref
    • Kalman-Bucy filter as the continuous form MacTutor, Kalman biography
  5. 1979

    Anderson and Moore, Optimal Filtering

    consolidation

    Brian D. O. Anderson and John B. Moore, Optimal Filtering, Prentice-Hall (1979)

    A systematic monograph treatment of optimal (Kalman-type) filtering.

    Evidence (1)
    • Anderson 1979 book record Open Library
