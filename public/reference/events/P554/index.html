2020 · Probability

Universality of rotational invariance at 2D criticality via coupling and integrability

Rotational invariance at the critical point was proved across a broad class of planar models by coupling lattices with their rotations and using integrability inputs.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2001

    Smirnov on critical percolation

    precursor

    S. Smirnov, Critical percolation in the plane: conformal invariance, Cardy's formula, scaling limits, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 333 (2001)

    Proves conformal invariance for site percolation on the triangular lattice, the model case of the universality programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Smirnov, Critical percolation in the plane, CRAS 2001. Crossref, DOI 10.1016/s0764-4442(01)01991-7
  2. 2010

    Smirnov: fermionic observables in the Ising model

    precursor

    S. Smirnov, Conformal invariance in random cluster models. I. Holomorphic fermions in the Ising model, Ann. of Math. 172 (2010)

    Establishes conformal invariance of the critical FK-Ising model, the lattice-by-lattice analogue of the later rotation-invariance result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Smirnov, Conformal invariance in random cluster models I, Ann. Math., 2010. Crossref, DOI 10.4007/annals.2010.172.1435
  3. 2020

    Rotational invariance in critical planar lattice models (arXiv)

    first statement

    Duminil-Copin, Kozlowski, Krachun, Manolescu, Oulamara, Rotational invariance in critical planar lattice models, arXiv:2012.11672 (2020)

    Rotation invariance at criticality is proved for a class of planar lattice models by coupling a lattice with its rotation and using integrability inputs.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record: title and authors match; first version 21 December 2020 arXiv API
  4. 2022

    Six-vertex free energy (integrability input)

    key development

    Duminil-Copin, Kozlowski, Krachun, Manolescu, Tikhonovskaia, On the six-vertex model's free energy, Commun. Math. Phys. (2022)

    An integrability result for the six-vertex model, the kind of input the rotational-invariance argument is described as using.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: journal article, Communications in Mathematical Physics, 2022, On the Six-Vertex Model's Free Energy Crossref
  5. 2024

    Bourbaki exposé on rotation invariance (after the same authors)

    consolidation

    Duminil-Copin et al., Exposé Bourbaki 1210, Rotation invariance of critical planar percolation, Astérisque (2024)

    An expository treatment by the same group of the rotation-invariance results, presented in Bourbaki's seminar.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Exposé Bourbaki 1210, Astérisque, 2024; authors include the five arXiv authors Crossref
