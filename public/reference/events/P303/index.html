Prismatic cohomology

A single cohomology theory was constructed that specializes to etale, de Rham and crystalline p-adic theories.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2015

    Integral p-adic Hodge theory announced

    precursor

    B. Bhatt, M. Morrow and P. Scholze, Integral p-adic Hodge theory—announcement, arXiv:1507.08129 (2015)

    The announcement sketches an interpolating cohomology theory before prisms were defined.

    Evidence (1)
    • Public announcement posted July 2015 arXiv
  2. 2019

    Prisms and prismatic site introduced

    first statement

    B. Bhatt and P. Scholze, Prisms and Prismatic Cohomology, arXiv:1905.08229 (2019)

    The preprint defines prisms and attaches the prismatic site to p-adic formal schemes.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted May 2019 arXiv
  3. 2019

    Prismatic cohomology proves comparison results

    first application

    B. Bhatt and P. Scholze, Prisms and Prismatic Cohomology, arXiv:1905.08229 (2019)

    The paper applies the theory to almost purity, q-de Rham cohomology and p-adic Tate-twist vanishing.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract lists these applications arXiv
  4. 2022

    Prisms and prismatic cohomology (Annals publication)

    key development

    Bhatt, Scholze, Prisms and prismatic cohomology, Ann. of Math. (2022)

    The main published account of prisms and prismatic cohomology, giving comparison results for the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2022 Annals paper by Bhatt and Scholze on prisms and prismatic cohomology zbMATH Open record
