Kontsevich-Soibelman wall crossing

Wall-crossing formulas for DT invariants unify stability conditions and cluster transformations.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2008

    Motivic DT wall-crossing framework posted

    first statement

    M. Kontsevich and Y. Soibelman, Stability structures, motivic Donaldson-Thomas invariants and cluster transformations (2008)

    Kontsevich and Soibelman define stability structures and motivic invariants in 2008.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents motivic dt wall-crossing framework posted in 2008 arXiv
  2. 2008

    Wall-crossing relates DT and cluster transformations

    first application

    M. Kontsevich and Y. Soibelman, Stability structures, motivic Donaldson-Thomas invariants and cluster transformations (2008)

    The founding work applies its identities to 3-Calabi–Yau categories and cluster collections.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents wall-crossing relates dt and cluster transformations in 2008 arXiv
  3. 2010

    Motivic Donaldson–Thomas invariants: summary of results

    consolidation

    Kontsevich, M.; Soibelman, Y., Motivic Donaldson-Thomas invariants: summary of results, in: Mirror Symmetry and Tropical Geometry, Contemp. Math. 527 (2010), arXiv 0910.4315

    A summary of the motivic DT framework and wall-crossing formulas, written as the programme's overview.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: Contemp. Math. 527 (2010), arXiv 0910.4315 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  4. 2011

    Cohomological Hall algebra and motivic DT invariants

    key development

    Kontsevich, M.; Soibelman, Y., Cohomological Hall algebra, exponential Hodge structures and motivic Donaldson-Thomas invariants, Commun. Number Theory Phys. 5 (2011) 231-352, DOI 10.4310/CNTP.2011.v5.n2.a1

    Builds the cohomological Hall algebra that supplies the algebraic structure behind the wall-crossing formulas.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: CNTP 5 (2011) 231-352 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  5. 2013

    Wall-crossing structures extended

    key development

    M. Kontsevich and Y. Soibelman, Wall-crossing structures in Donaldson-Thomas invariants, integrable systems and Mirror Symmetry (2013)

    The 2013 paper develops wall-crossing structures in DT invariants and integrable systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents wall-crossing structures extended in 2013 arXiv
