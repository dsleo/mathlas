Regularity structures

Ill-posed stochastic PDEs are solved by combining rough-path ideas with a Hopf-algebraic account of renormalisation taken from quantum field theory.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2011

    Pathwise KPZ solution

    precursor

    M. Hairer, Solving the KPZ equation, arXiv:1109.6811 (2011)

    A pathwise solution theory for KPZ precedes the general regularity-structure framework.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public preprint posted September 2011 arXiv
  2. 2013

    Regularity structures introduced

    first statement

    M. Hairer, A theory of regularity structures, arXiv:1303.5113 (2013)

    The public preprint introduces regularity structures as a framework for singular stochastic PDEs.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the preprint to March 2013 and describes the new structure. Original publication or repository record
  3. 2013

    Phi-four-three dynamics constructed

    first application

    M. Hairer, A theory of regularity structures, arXiv:1303.5113 (2013)

    The same preprint applies the framework to construct a Markov process associated with the Phi-four-three model.

    Evidence (1)
    • The abstract explicitly names the Phi-four-three application. Original publication or repository record
  4. 2015

    Introduction to regularity structures

    consolidation

    Hairer, Introduction to regularity structures, Brazilian Journal of Probability and Statistics (2015)

    A survey of the regularity structures framework, giving a systematic introduction to the theory after its first statement.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2015 survey by Hairer, Introduction to regularity structures zbMATH Open record
  5. 2018

    Discretisations of rough stochastic PDEs

    key development

    Hairer, Matetski, Discretisations of rough stochastic PDEs, Ann. Probab. (2018)

    Extends the regularity-structures solution theory to convergent numerical discretisations of singular stochastic PDEs.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2018 Annals of Probability paper by Hairer and Matetski zbMATH Open record
  6. 2020

    Rough-path course incorporates regularity structures

    consolidation

    P. K. Friz and M. Hairer, A Course on Rough Paths, second edition, Springer (2020)

    A systematic second-edition text includes an introduction to regularity structures.

    Evidence (1)
    • Publisher records the 2020 second edition Springer / Crossref
