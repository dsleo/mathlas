1782 · Probability

Laplace's analytic probability

Generating functions and asymptotic approximations were used to solve probability problems.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1718

    De Moivre's The Doctrine of Chances

    precursor

    Abraham de Moivre, The Doctrine of Chances, London, 1718

    An earlier analytic treatise on chances whose methods Laplace extended.

    Dating or attribution note

    That Laplace built on it is background knowledge (Hald); not checked in the pages read.

    Evidence (2)
    • The Doctrine of Chances, 1718. Open Library
    • De Moivre wrote The Doctrine of Chances and proved a special case of the central limit theorem. English Wikipedia, Abraham de Moivre
  2. 1774

    Laplace's memoir on the probability of causes

    precursor

    Laplace, Mémoire sur la probabilité des causes par les événemens, 1774

    An early memoir that treats probability by analytic means; not shown here to use generating functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: Stigler, Laplace's 1774 memoir on inverse probability, Statistical Science 1 (1986) Crossref 10.1214/ss/1177013620
  3. 1779

    Laplace's generating-function method for probabilities

    precursor

    Laplace, paper written in 1779 (generating functions for probabilities)

    Laplace treats probabilities as coefficients of a generating function and recovers them by interpolation and difference equations.

    Evidence (1)
    • The method was indicated by Laplace in a paper written in 1779; the probability-generating function is described. English Wikipedia, Pierre-Simon Laplace
  4. 1782

    Laplace introduces generating functions

    first statement

    P. S. Laplace, Memoire sur les suites, Mem. Acad. Roy. Sci. Paris, annee 1779 (published 1782); Oeuvres completes X, pp. 1-89

    Laplace announces a calculus of generating functions for series and difference equations and says it serves the analysis of chances, the method his later probability theory is built on.

    Evidence (3)
    • The memoir's own introduction names a new calculus of generating functions and says its recurro-recurrent series matter for the analysis of chances. Pulskamp's English translation of the memoir
    • The memoir is dated year 1779, volume published 1782, Oeuvres X pp. 1-89. Header of the Pulskamp translation
    • Wikipedia's Laplace article places the generating-function method in a paper written in 1779. Wikipedia, Pierre-Simon Laplace
  5. 1812

    Théorie analytique des probabilités

    consolidation

    P.-S. Laplace, Théorie analytique des probabilités, Paris, 1812

    Book one is on generating functions and approximations to expressions in probability, book two on probability, expectation, compound events and least squares.

    Evidence (2)
    • Internet Archive item for the 1812 edition archive.org theorieanalytiqu0000mlec
    • MacTutor describes the book's first volume as generating functions and approximations, and the 1812 first edition MacTutor, Laplace
  6. 1812

    'Théorie analytique' in the book title

    naming

    Laplace, Théorie analytique des probabilités, 1812

    The title names the programme of analytic probability that gives the event its name.

    Evidence (1)
    • Archive record title: Théorie analytique des probabilités, 1812 archive.org theorieanalytiqu0000mlec
