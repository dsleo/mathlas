1917 · Complex analysis

Complex dynamics: iteration of rational functions (Julia and Fatou)

Julia and Fatou independently studied iterated rational maps of the plane, introducing attractors, repellers and the sets now named after them.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1917

    Fatou's note on rational substitutions

    first statement

    Fatou, P., Sur les substitutions rationnelles (1917)

    Fatou's 1917 note begins the study of iterated rational maps of the plane.

    Evidence (1)
    • Fatou's bibliography records Sur les substitutions rationnelles in Comptes rendus, 1917. Michèle Audin, University of Strasbourg, Fatou bibliography
  2. 1918

    Julia's memoir on iteration of rational functions

    key development

    Julia, G., Memoire sur l'iteration des fonctions rationnelles (1918)

    Julia's 1918 memoir gives the systematic theory of the Julia set.

    Evidence (1)
    • Memoir with this title and year zbMATH Open
  3. 1919

    Fatou's memoir on functional equations

    key development

    Fatou, P., Sur les equations fonctionnelles (1919)

    Fatou's 1919 memoir develops the Fatou set and the classification of its components.

    Evidence (1)
    • Memoir with this title and year 1919 zbMATH Open
  4. 1927

    Montel's Lecons sur les familles normales

    consolidation

    Montel, P., Lecons sur les familles normales de fonctions analytiques et leurs applications (1927)

    Montel's 1927 lectures give the normal-families framework on which the Julia-Fatou theory rests.

    Dating or attribution note

    Whether the book treats iteration of rational maps is not verified; the consolidation role is provisional.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book with this title and year zbMATH Open
