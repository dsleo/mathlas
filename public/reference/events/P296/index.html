2010 · Geometry

Guth-Katz distinct distances

Guth and Katz used polynomial partitioning and ruled surface results to nearly solve the distinct distances problem.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2010

    Guth–Katz distinct-distances preprint

    first statement

    L. Guth and N. H. Katz, On the Erdos distinct distance problem in the plane (2010)

    The 2010 paper announces the near-sharp planar distance bound.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents guth–katz distinct-distances preprint in 2010 arXiv
  2. 2010

    Polynomial incidence geometry bounds distances

    first application

    L. Guth and N. H. Katz, On the Erdos distinct distance problem in the plane (2010)

    Polynomial partitioning and ruled-surface geometry are applied to the Erdős distance problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents polynomial incidence geometry bounds distances in 2010 arXiv
  3. 2015

    Distinct-distances proof appears in Annals

    key development

    L. Guth and N. H. Katz, On the Erdős distinct distances problem in the plane (2015)

    The 2015 journal publication supplies a later formal record.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents distinct-distances proof appears in annals in 2015 publisher / Crossref
  4. 2016

    Polynomial methods in combinatorics

    consolidation

    Guth, Polynomial Methods in Combinatorics, AMS University Lecture Series 64 (2016)

    A monograph on the polynomial method, including polynomial partitioning used in the distinct distances argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2016 AMS University Lecture Series monograph by Guth zbMATH Open record
