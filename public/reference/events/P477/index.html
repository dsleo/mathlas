1973 · Graph theory

Explicit expander graphs from property (T) (Margulis)

Kazhdan's property (T) for lattices in Lie groups gives the first explicit infinite families of expanding graphs.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1967

    Kazhdan's property (T) introduced

    precursor

    Kazhdan, Connection of the dual space of a group with the structure of its closed subgroups, Funct. Anal. Appl. 1 (1967)

    Introduces property (T), the representation-theoretic source of Margulis's explicit expanders.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Kazhdan's property (T): introduced by Kazhdan in 1967 Wikipedia: Kazhdan's property (T)
  2. 1973

    Margulis gives the first explicit construction of concentrators

    first statement

    G. A. Margulis, Explicit constructions of concentrators, Probl. Peredachi Inf. 9:4 (1973), 71-80; Problems Inform. Transmission 9:4 (1973), 325-332

    Margulis uses group representation theory to build concentrators (expanders) explicitly, where existence had been shown only by probabilistic arguments.

    Dating or attribution note

    Use of property (T) rests on secondary sources; the paper text was not read. Pinsker's 1973 probabilistic existence proof is not checked. The 1988 Margulis paper (mathnet ppi686) is a different work.

    Evidence (2)
    • Mathnet record: 1973, vol. 9 no. 4, pp. 71-80; abstract says group representations solve the explicit construction. Math-Net.Ru record ppi925
    • Secondary sources credit this paper as the first explicit expander construction via Kazhdan's property (T). Web search results (IPAM workshop page, survey literature)
  3. 1981

    Explicit linear-sized expanders from the Margulis graph

    first application

    Gabber, Galil, Explicit constructions of linear-sized superconcentrators, JCSS (1981)

    Analyses the explicit Z_n x Z_n graph attributed to Margulis and proves its expansion, giving an explicit linear-sized expander family.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Expander graph: Margulis's construction analysed by Gabber and Galil Wikipedia: Expander graph
  4. 1988

    Ramanujan graphs: optimal explicit expanders

    key development

    Lubotzky, Phillips, Sarnak, Ramanujan graphs, Combinatorica 8 (1988)

    Gives explicit families of optimal spectral expanders via Ramanujan-type bounds, a standard reference for explicit expanders.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record title, journal and year checked Crossref
    • Wikipedia Expander graph: Ramanujan graphs constructed explicitly by Lubotzky-Phillips-Sarnak and Margulis Wikipedia: Expander graph
  5. 2006

    Survey: Expander graphs and their applications

    consolidation

    Hoory, Linial, Wigderson, Expander graphs and their applications, Bull. AMS 43 (2006)

    The standard survey of expanders, their constructions and applications.
