1964 · Group & representation theory

Golod-Shafarevich: nil algebras produce infinite periodic groups

Using a construction on nil algebras, Golod and Shafarevich built a finitely generated infinite periodic group, answering the general Burnside question negatively by algebraic means.

Also known as: Golod-Shafarevich theorem

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1964

    Golod-Shafarevich class field tower theorem

    first statement

    Golod, Shafarevich, On the class field tower, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR 28 (1964)

    The Golod-Shafarevich theorem that some class field towers are infinite, from which the group construction proceeds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Golod and Shafarevich proved the theorem named after them in 1964 Wikipedia: Golod-Shafarevich theorem
  2. 1964

    Infinite periodic group answers the general Burnside problem

    first application

    Golod, On nil-algebras and finitely approximable p-groups, AMS Transl. (2) 48 (1965); Izv. 28 (1964) 273-276

    Golod's nil-algebra construction gives an infinite, finitely generated periodic group, a negative answer to the general Burnside problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Golod 1965 translation, DOI 10.1090/trans2/048/06 Crossref
    • Golod and Shafarevich provided a counter-example to the general Burnside problem, an infinite finitely generated periodic group, in 1964 MacTutor: A history of the Burnside problem
  3. 1968

    Adian-Novikov infinite periodic groups

    key development

    Adian, Novikov, On infinite periodic groups I-III, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR 32 (1968)

    Adian and Novikov show that B(m,n) is infinite for odd n above a large bound, developing the bounded Burnside problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Adian-Novikov 1968 infinite periodic groups, with B(m,n) infinite for odd n >= 4381 MacTutor: A history of the Burnside problem
  4. 1979

    Adian's monograph on the Burnside problem

    consolidation

    Adian, The Burnside Problem and Identities in Groups, Springer (1979; Russian original 1975)

    Adian's monograph collects the Burnside-problem results, the first systematic treatment in the event's record.

    Evidence (2)
    • Adian, The Burnside Problem and Identities in Groups, DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-66932-3 Crossref
    • Adian's Burnside monograph (Moscow, 1975) cited in MacTutor MacTutor: A history of the Burnside problem
