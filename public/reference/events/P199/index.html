1931 · Dynamics

Ergodic theory from statistical mechanics

Koopman's operator on function spaces let von Neumann prove the first rigorous ergodic theorem.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1898

    Boltzmann introduces "ergoden" in his gas theory lectures

    precursor

    Boltzmann's ergodic hypothesis is the statistical-mechanics ancestor of the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ergodic hypothesis originally due to Boltzmann; citation to Vorlesungen über Gastheorie (1898) Wikipedia: Ergodic hypothesis
  2. 1931

    Koopman: Hamiltonian flows as operators on Hilbert space

    first statement

    B. O. Koopman, Hamiltonian systems and transformations in Hilbert space, PNAS 17 (1931)

    Showed Hamiltonian flows can be studied through unitary operators on Hilbert space of functions, the operator route to ergodic theorems.

    Evidence (3)
    • DOI record: PNAS 17 (1931) Crossref
    • Koopman operator on function spaces underlies von Neumann's ergodic theorem Wikipedia: Ergodic theory
    • Crossref: PNAS 17 (1931). Crossref
  3. 1931

    Birkhoff proves the pointwise ergodic theorem

    key development

    G. D. Birkhoff, Proof of the ergodic theorem, PNAS 17 (1931) 656-660

    Proved that time averages along trajectories converge almost everywhere, the main ergodic theorem.

    Evidence (3)
    • DOI record: PNAS 17 (1931) Crossref
    • Birkhoff's ergodic theorem of 1931 Wikipedia: Ergodic theory
    • Birkhoff discovered the ergodic theorem in 1931 MacTutor: George Birkhoff
  4. 1931

    Koopman: Hamiltonian systems as unitary operators on Hilbert space

    first application

    B. O. Koopman, Hamiltonian systems and transformation in Hilbert space, PNAS 17, 1931

    Koopman represented classical flows by unitary operators on Hilbert space, the idea von Neumann then used.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: PNAS 17 (1931). Crossref
    • Koopman's idea of operators on a function space stimulated von Neumann's ergodic theorem. MacTutor, von Neumann biography
  5. 1932

    von Neumann proves the quasi-ergodic hypothesis

    first application

    J. von Neumann, Proof of the quasi-ergodic hypothesis, PNAS 18 (1932) 70-82

    Applied the new mean ergodic theorem to the quasi-ergodic hypothesis of statistical mechanics, which is the target problem.

    Evidence (2)
    • DOI record: PNAS 18 (1932) Crossref
    • Operator approach to the ergodic theorem for statistical mechanics Wikipedia: Ergodic theory
  6. 1932

    von Neumann's mean ergodic theorem

    key development

    J. von Neumann, Proof of the quasi-ergodic hypothesis, PNAS 18, 1932

    Von Neumann proved the mean ergodic theorem using Hilbert space methods, giving a rigorous basis to Boltzmann's hypothesis.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: PNAS 18 (1932). Crossref
    • Described as the first rigorous proof of an ergodic theorem. MacTutor, von Neumann biography
  7. 1932

    Birkhoff and Koopman survey of recent ergodic theory

    consolidation

    G. D. Birkhoff, B. O. Koopman, Recent contributions to the ergodic theory, PNAS 18, 1932

    An early survey of the new theorems and their connections.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: PNAS 18 (1932). Crossref
  8. 1937

    Hopf's Ergodentheorie

    consolidation

    E. Hopf, Ergodentheorie, Ergebnisse der Mathematik, Berlin: Springer, 1937

    A monograph presenting the theory, including geodesic flow on surfaces of negative curvature.

    Dating or attribution note

    'First monograph' not verified.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Springer 1937, Ergodentheorie. Crossref
  9. 1956

    Halmos: Lectures on Ergodic Theory

    consolidation

    P. R. Halmos, Lectures on Ergodic Theory (1956)

    Early textbook-level treatment of ergodic theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    Halmos's lectures are one textbook choice; other early treatments were not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library record (1956) Open Library
  10. 1977

    Furstenberg's ergodic proof of Szemeredi's theorem

    key development

    H. Furstenberg, Ergodic behavior of diagonal measures and a theorem of Szemeredi on arithmetic progressions, J. Anal. Math. 31, 1977

    Ergodic methods were used to prove a deep combinatorial theorem, extending the field's reach.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: J. Analyse Math. 31 (1977). Crossref
  11. 1981

    Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems journal

    institution

    Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems, vol. 1, 1981

    A journal named after the field starts.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref lists volume 1 (1981). Crossref
