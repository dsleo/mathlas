1880 · Dynamics

Dynamical systems from celestial mechanics (Poincare to Birkhoff)

Poincare studied orbits geometrically rather than by formulae, uncovering homoclinic phenomena in the three-body problem, and Birkhoff built on this.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1880

    Poincare's note on curves defined by a differential equation

    first statement

    H. Poincare, Sur les courbes definies par une equation differentielle, 1880 (zbMATH 2708648)

    Poincare announces the geometric, qualitative study of the orbits of differential equations rather than their formulae.

    Evidence (3)
    • Poincare note of 1880 with this title is indexed zbMATH Open 2708648
    • Memoir of same title in J. Math. Pures Appl. 1881 and 1882 zbMATH Open 2707188, 2705837
    • Published note in Comptes rendus 90 (1880), pages 673–675; distinct from the withdrawn longer memoir Henri Poincaré Papers bibliography
  2. 1881

    Poincare's memoir on curves defined by differential equations

    key development

    H. Poincare, Memoire sur les courbes definies par une equation differentielle, J. Math. Pures Appl. (3) 7 (1881) 375-422

    The full memoir lays out the qualitative classification of singular points and limit cycles in the plane.

    Evidence (1)
    • Numdam record of the 1881 memoir (first Sur les courbes paper per D) Numdam JMPA 1881
  3. 1890

    Poincare's three-body memoir (Acta Math. 13)

    first application

    H. Poincare, Sur le probleme des trois corps et les equations de la dynamique, Acta Math. 13 (1890) 1-270

    Poincare applies geometric methods to the restricted three-body problem and finds homoclinic intersections.

    Evidence (2)
    • The full memoir is catalogued in Acta Mathematica 13 (1890), pages 1–270 Henri Poincaré Papers bibliography
    • Prize memoir; chaotic behaviour first described MacTutor Poincare
  4. 1892–1899

    Les methodes nouvelles de la mecanique celeste, vol. I

    consolidation

    H. Poincare, Les methodes nouvelles de la mecanique celeste, Gauthier-Villars (1892-1899)

    Poincare's three-volume treatise systematises the new qualitative methods; homoclinic solutions are treated in volume III (1899).

    Evidence (2)
    • Tome I (1892) indexed in JFM zbMATH / JFM 24.1130.01
    • Tome III (1899) discusses doubly asymptotic solutions archive.org full text
  5. 1912

    Birkhoff, Quelques theoremes sur le mouvement des systemes dynamiques

    naming

    G. D. Birkhoff, Bull. Soc. Math. France 40 (1912)

    Earliest indexed title naming dynamical systems as the object of study in the Poincare tradition.

    Evidence (1)
    • Earliest indexed title with systemes dynamiques as object zbMATH Open 2627123
  6. 1913

    Birkhoff proves Poincare's last geometric theorem

    key development

    G. D. Birkhoff, Proof of Poincare's geometric theorem, Trans. AMS 14 (1913) 14-22

    The first major result by the successor, making the Poincare programme a theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Trans. AMS 14 (1913) zbMATH JFM 44.0761.01
  7. 1920

    Birkhoff's AMS Colloquium lectures on dynamical systems

    institution

    AMS Colloquium Lectures 1920 (Birkhoff)

    A named lecture series on dynamical systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • Birkhoff gave the 1920 AMS Colloquium lectures on Dynamical Systems MacTutor Birkhoff
  8. 1927

    Birkhoff, Dynamical Systems

    consolidation

    G. D. Birkhoff, Dynamical Systems, AMS Colloquium Publications 9 (1927)

    The first monograph titled Dynamical Systems.

    Evidence (1)
    • AMS Colloquium Publications vol. 9 Crossref
