1881 · Number theory

Automorphic functions and uniformisation

Poincare and Klein linked discrete groups of Mobius transformations, hyperbolic geometry and differential equations, and uniformised algebraic curves.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1866

    Fuchs on linear differential equations

    precursor

    Fuchs, Zur Theorie der linearen Differentialgleichungen mit veraenderlichen Coefficienten, Crelle 66, 121-160, 1866

    Fuchs's study of linear equations with singular points supplied the problem that Poincare's functions were built to solve.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Crelle 66 (1866) 121-160. Crossref
    • Says Poincare named the new functions Fuchsian after Fuchs. MacTutor biography of Poincare
  2. 1881

    Poincare's first notes on Fuchsian functions

    first statement

    Poincare, notes in Comptes Rendus, 1881

    Poincare began publishing his theory of Fuchsian functions in 1881, linking their groups to non-Euclidean geometry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Says Poincare began publishing an outline of automorphic function theory in 1881. MacTutor biography of Klein
    • Describes the insight that Fuchsian transformations are those of non-Euclidean geometry. MacTutor biography of Poincare
  3. 1882

    Theory of Fuchsian groups

    naming

    Poincare, Theorie des groupes fuchsiens, Acta Math. 1, 1-62, 1882

    Poincare's Acta paper names and develops the groups of the theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Acta Math. 1 (1882) 1-62. Crossref
    • Says he named the functions Fuchsian after Fuchs; later called automorphic. MacTutor biography of Poincare
  4. 1882

    Memoir on Fuchsian functions

    first application

    Poincare, Memoire sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, Acta Math. 1, 193-294, 1882

    The memoir uses the new functions to solve linear differential equations.

    Dating or attribution note

    That the memoir integrates linear equations is from MacTutor's account; the paper was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Acta Math. 1 (1882) 193-294. Crossref
    • Says the functions arose from the search for solutions of differential equations. MacTutor biography of Poincare
  5. 1883

    Klein on Riemann function theory

    key development

    Klein, Neue Beitraege zur Riemann'schen Functionentheorie, Math. Ann. 21, 141-218, 1883

    Klein linked automorphic functions to Riemann surfaces and framed the uniformisation programme.

    Dating or attribution note

    Paper contents not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Math. Ann. 21 (1883) 141-218. Crossref
    • Describes the Klein-Poincare race to formulate a grand uniformization theorem. MacTutor biography of Klein
  6. 1897

    Fricke-Klein lectures on automorphic functions

    consolidation

    Fricke and Klein, Vorlesungen ueber die Theorie der automorphen Functionen, vol. 1, 1897

    First systematic textbook treatment under the name automorphic functions.

    Evidence (1)
    • Archive.org record: 1897. Internet Archive
  7. 1907

    Koebe: rigorous uniformisation (Gottingen 1907)

    key development

    P. Koebe, Ueber die Uniformisierung beliebiger analytischer Kurven, Gott. Nachr. 1907, 191-210

    Poincare and Koebe independently gave the first rigorous proofs of the general uniformisation theorem.

    Evidence (2)
    • Says the first rigorous proofs were by Poincare and Koebe, completed by 1907. Wikipedia: Uniformization theorem
    • zbMATH 38.0454.01: Koebe, 'Ueber die Uniformisierung beliebiger analytischer Kurven', Gott. Nachr. 1907, 191-210. zbMATH Open, document 38.0454.01
  8. 1908

    Poincare's uniformisation memoir (Acta Mathematica)

    key development

    H. Poincare, Sur l'uniformisation des fonctions analytiques, Acta Math. 31 (1908) 1-63

    Poincare's memoir on the uniformisation of analytic functions; its Crossref print date is 1908.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Poincare, 'Sur l'uniformisation des fonctions analytiques', Acta Math. 31, pp. 1-63, print date 1908. Crossref 10.1007/bf02415442
