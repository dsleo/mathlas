1881 · Number theory

Automorphic functions and uniformisation

Poincare and Klein linked discrete groups of Mobius transformations, hyperbolic geometry and differential equations, and uniformised algebraic curves.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1866

    Fuchs on linear differential equations

    precursor

    Fuchs, Zur Theorie der linearen Differentialgleichungen mit veraenderlichen Coefficienten, Crelle 66, 121-160, 1866

    Fuchs's study of linear equations with singular points supplied the problem that Poincare's functions were built to solve.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Crelle 66 (1866) 121-160. Crossref
    • Says Poincare named the new functions Fuchsian after Fuchs. MacTutor biography of Poincare
  2. 1881

    Poincaré's first note on Fuchsian functions

    first statement

    Poincaré, Sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 92 (14 February 1881) 333-335

    Poincare began publishing his theory of Fuchsian functions in 1881, linking their groups to non-Euclidean geometry.

    Evidence (4)
    • Says Poincare began publishing an outline of automorphic function theory in 1881. MacTutor biography of Klein
    • Describes the insight that Fuchsian transformations are those of non-Euclidean geometry. MacTutor biography of Poincare
    • Bibliography lists the note as Sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, C. R. 92 (14 February 1881) 333-335 Numdam, Klein's notes about Poincaré's early publications (PHSC 1997)
    • Printed note, C. R. 92 pp. 333-334: aim, definitions of groupe fuchsien and fonction fuchsienne, tiling of the fundamental circle by non-Euclidean polygons. Poincare, Sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 92 (14 Feb 1881), archive.org scan
  3. 1881

    Fuchsian functions named in the title of Poincaré's first note

    naming

    Poincaré, Sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 92 (14 February 1881) 333-335

    The first note already carries the name Fuchsian functions, which MacTutor says honours Fuchs; Klein's term automorphic came later.

    Evidence (4)
    • Bibliography gives the title Sur les fonctions fuchsiennes for the note of 14 February 1881 Numdam, Klein's notes about Poincaré's early publications (PHSC 1997)
    • Poincaré first called automorphic functions Fuchsian after Lazarus Fuchs MacTutor biography of Poincare
    • Poincare: "auxquelles j'ai donne le nom de fonctions fuchsiennes, en l'honneur de M. Fuchs". Poincare, C. R. 92 (14 Feb 1881) p. 333, archive.org scan
    • Klein coined the term automorphic function about 1890 (footnote 8, citing Klein's collected works). Adelmann and Gerbracht, Letters from William Burnside to Robert Fricke, arXiv:0712.4296
  4. 1881

    Poincare integrates linear differential equations by Fuchsian functions

    first application

    Poincare, Sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 92 (21 February 1881) 395-398

    Poincare shows that every Fuchsian function integrates an associated linear differential equation, with the hypergeometric series and the modular functions as examples.

    Evidence (2)
    • Klein's notes on C. R. 92, p. 395: every Fuchsian function integrates an associated differential equation; hypergeometric series and modular functions as examples. Bruning, Klein's notes about Poincare's early publications (Numdam)
    • Printed note, C. R. 92 pp. 395-396: two theorems, the second integrating linear equations with algebraic coefficients by Fuchsian functions. Poincare, Sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 92 (21 Feb 1881), archive.org scan
  5. 1882

    Théorie des groupes fuchsiens

    key development

    Poincare, Theorie des groupes fuchsiens, Acta Math. 1, 1-62, 1882

    Poincaré's Acta paper develops the Fuchsian groups of the theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Record: Acta Math. 1 (1882) 1-62. Crossref
  6. 1882

    Memoir on Fuchsian functions

    key development

    Poincare, Memoire sur les fonctions fuchsiennes, Acta Math. 1, 193-294, 1882

    The memoir uses the new functions to solve linear differential equations.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Acta Math. 1 (1882) 193-294. Crossref
    • Says the functions arose from the search for solutions of differential equations. MacTutor biography of Poincare
  7. 1882

    Klein's notes on single-valued functions with linear transformations

    key development

    Klein, Ueber eindeutige Functionen mit linearen Substitutionen in sich, Math. Ann. 20, 49-51, 1882; and Math. Ann. 19, 565-568, 1882

    Klein's own short notes enter the theory in the year of Poincaré's Acta memoirs.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: Klein, Math. Ann. 20, 49-51, 1882 Crossref 10.1007/bf01443325
    • Crossref record: Klein, Math. Ann. 19, 565-568, 1882 Crossref 10.1007/bf01446671
  8. 1883

    Klein on Riemann function theory

    key development

    Klein, Neue Beitraege zur Riemann'schen Functionentheorie, Math. Ann. 21, 141-218, 1883

    Klein linked automorphic functions to Riemann surfaces and framed the uniformisation programme.

    Dating or attribution note

    Paper contents not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Record: Math. Ann. 21 (1883) 141-218. Crossref
    • Describes the Klein-Poincare race to formulate a grand uniformization theorem. MacTutor biography of Klein
  9. 1897

    Fricke-Klein lectures on automorphic functions

    consolidation

    Fricke and Klein, Vorlesungen ueber die Theorie der automorphen Functionen, vol. 1, 1897

    First systematic textbook treatment under the name automorphic functions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: 1897. Internet Archive
    • Hutchinson's review of Fricke-Klein, Vorlesungen uber die Theorie der automorphen Functionen, Bull. AMS 9 (1903). Crossref API
  10. 1907

    Koebe: rigorous uniformisation (Gottingen 1907)

    key development

    P. Koebe, Ueber die Uniformisierung beliebiger analytischer Kurven, Gott. Nachr. 1907, 191-210

    Koebe's first Göttingen note on uniformisation of arbitrary analytic curves; Poincaré's own memoir follows as the 1908 entry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Says the first rigorous proofs were by Poincare and Koebe, completed by 1907. Wikipedia: Uniformization theorem
    • zbMATH 38.0454.01: Koebe, 'Ueber die Uniformisierung beliebiger analytischer Kurven', Gott. Nachr. 1907, 191-210. zbMATH Open, document 38.0454.01
  11. 1908

    Poincare's uniformisation memoir (Acta Mathematica)

    key development

    H. Poincare, Sur l'uniformisation des fonctions analytiques, Acta Math. 31 (1908) 1-63

    Poincare's memoir on the uniformisation of analytic functions; its Crossref print date is 1908.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Poincare, 'Sur l'uniformisation des fonctions analytiques', Acta Math. 31, pp. 1-63, print date 1908. Crossref 10.1007/bf02415442
