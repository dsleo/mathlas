Rigid analytic spaces (Tate; analytic geometry over non-archimedean fields)

Complex-analytic ideas (coherent sheaves, analytic continuation) are made to work over complete non-archimedean fields, giving p-adic uniformisation of elliptic curves.

Also known as: rigid analytic geometry

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1962

    Tate's unpublished notes on rigid spaces

    precursor

    Tate introduces rigid spaces in 1962 as part of his work on uniformising p-adic elliptic curves with bad reduction; the notes are unpublished.

    Evidence (1)
    • Rigid spaces introduced by Tate in 1962 Wikipedia, Rigid analytic space
  2. 1971

    Tate: 'Rigid analytic spaces'

    first statement

    John Tate, Rigid analytic spaces, Invent. Math. 12 (1971) 257-289

    Tate's first published definition of rigid analytic spaces via affinoid covers.

    Evidence (2)
    • Tate 1971 Inventiones record Crossref
    • Original notes dated 1962, published 1971 Wikipedia, Rigid analytic space
  3. 1971

    Name 'rigid analytic space' in a title

    naming

    John Tate, Rigid analytic spaces, Invent. Math. 12 (1971)

    The term 'rigid analytic space' appears in the title of the 1971 paper.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1971 title 'Rigid analytic spaces' Crossref
  4. 1974

    Raynaud: rigid spaces as generic fibres of formal schemes

    key development

    Michel Raynaud, Geometrie analytique rigide d'apres Tate, Kiehl, ..., Bull. Soc. Math. France Mem. 39/40 (1974) 319-327

    Raynaud (around 1970) interprets rigid spaces as generic fibres of formal schemes over a valuation ring, the key bridge to algebraic geometry.

    Evidence (1)
    • Raynaud 1974 Bull. SMF exposé cited in Wikipedia Wikipedia, Rigid analytic space
  5. 1984

    Bosch, Guntzer, Remmert: Non-Archimedean Analysis

    consolidation

    Siegfried Bosch, Ulrich Guntzer, Reinhold Remmert, Non-Archimedean Analysis, Grundlehren 261, Springer (1984)

    The standard systematic monograph on rigid and non-archimedean analysis.

    Evidence (1)
    • Bosch-Guntzer-Remmert 1984 record Crossref
  6. 1990

    Berkovich: spectral theory over non-archimedean fields

    key development

    Vladimir Berkovich, Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean Fields, AMS Math. Surveys 33 (1990)

    Reformulates rigid analytic geometry via spectra of Banach algebras, giving compact, connected non-archimedean spaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • AMS Surveys 33 record (2012 reprint in Crossref) Crossref
    • Berkovich reformulated the theory in the late 1980s Wikipedia, Rigid analytic space
