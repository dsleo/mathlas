1935 · Numerical analysis

Kantorovich's functional analysis for numerical methods

Ordered vector spaces and normed spaces were applied to approximation and equation solving.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1935

    Kantorovich: semi-ordered linear spaces (K-spaces)

    first statement

    L. V. Kantorovich, On semiordered linear spaces and their applications in the theory of linear operators, Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR 4 (1935) no. 1-2, 11-14

    Kantorovich defined a new type of ordered linear space, now called a K-space, which became the basis for applying ordered spaces to analysis.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor says Kantorovich defined K-spaces in 1935 MacTutor, Kantorovich biography
    • Citation of Kantorovich's first 1935 paper on semiordered linear spaces, described as his first paper on the new topic. Kutateladze, article on Kantorovich (arXiv 1502.06494)
  2. 1936

    Semi-ordered spaces applied to numerical methods

    first application

    L. V. Kantorovich, On a class of functional equations, C. R. (Dokl.) Acad. Sci. URSS (N.S.) 1936, no. 4, 219-224

    Kantorovich applied semi-ordered spaces to numerical methods, the first purposeful use of the functional-analytic idea for approximation.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor says the 1936 paper On one class of functional equations applied semi-ordered spaces to numerical methods MacTutor, Kantorovich biography
    • zbMATH entry for Kantorovich, On a class of functional equations, 1936. zbMATH Open
  3. 1948

    Newton-Kantorovich theorem on Newton's method

    key development

    L. V. Kantorovich, Functional analysis and applied mathematics, Uspekhi Mat. Nauk 3 (1948) no. 6(28), 89-185 (a 1948 Doklady note on Newton's method is also cited; not verified)

    A semi-local convergence theorem for Newton's method, the functional-analytic result for which Kantorovich is best known in numerical analysis.

    Evidence (3)
    • The Kantorovich theorem was first stated by Kantorovich in 1948. Wikipedia, Kantorovich theorem
    • Bibliographic record of Functional analysis and applied mathematics, Uspekhi Mat. Nauk 3 (6), 1948, pp. 89-185. Mathnet.ru
    • Kantorovich carried Newton's method to functional spaces in 1948. Polyak, Newton-Kantorovich method and its global convergence, J. Math. Sci. 133 (2006)
  4. 1959

    Kantorovich and Akilov, Functional Analysis in Normed Spaces (Russian original)

    consolidation

    L. V. Kantorovich, G. P. Akilov, Functional Analysis in Normed Spaces, Moscow, 1959 (English edition Pergamon, 1964)

    The main textbook on functional analysis in normed spaces and its numerical applications; the English edition followed in 1964.

    Evidence (2)
    • MacTutor lists Functional Analysis in Normed Spaces (Russian) written with G. P. Akilov, 1959. MacTutor, Kantorovich biography
    • Open Library: Kantorovich and Akilov, Functional analysis in normed spaces, first published 1964 (English edition). Open Library
