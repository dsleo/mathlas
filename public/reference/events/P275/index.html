1954 · Statistics & decision theory

Mathematical economics via fixed-point theorems

Arrow-Debreu equilibrium existence by fixed-point theorem.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1941

    Kakutani's set-valued fixed-point theorem

    precursor

    Kakutani, A generalization of Brouwer's fixed point theorem, Duke Mathematical Journal, 1941

    Kakutani's extension of Brouwer's theorem to set-valued maps becomes the standard tool for equilibrium existence; a tool precursor, not an economics result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article record, Duke Math. J. 1941 Crossref
  2. 1950

    Nash equilibrium proved by a fixed-point argument

    precursor

    Nash, Equilibrium points in n-person games, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 1950

    Nash uses a fixed-point argument to show existence of equilibrium in finite non-cooperative games, the earliest use of the tool on an equilibrium problem of economic agents found here.

    Dating or attribution note

    Game theory rather than general equilibrium; the use of Kakutani's theorem in the 1950 paper was not checked in fetched text. Included as the earliest economics-adjacent application, with this caveat. Reclassified from first_application to precursor: it predates this event's first statement (1954) and belongs to the wider field.

    Evidence (1)
    • Reprint record cites PNAS 36 (1950) 48-49 for the paper Crossref (Classics in Game Theory reprint)
  3. 1952

    Debreu's social equilibrium existence theorem

    precursor

    Debreu, A social equilibrium existence theorem, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 1952

    Debreu proves an equilibrium existence theorem for an abstract social/economic system by a fixed-point method, a step toward the 1954 competitive result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article record, PNAS 1952 Crossref
  4. 1954

    Arrow-Debreu existence of competitive equilibrium

    first statement

    Arrow and Debreu, Existence of an equilibrium for a competitive economy, Econometrica, 1954

    Arrow and Debreu prove existence of competitive equilibrium with a fixed-point argument, the public statement of the general-equilibrium use.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article record, Econometrica 1954 Crossref
  5. 1954

    McKenzie's equilibrium in Graham's model of world trade

    key development

    McKenzie, On equilibrium in Graham's model of world trade and other competitive systems, Econometrica, 1954

    McKenzie gives an independent existence proof of competitive equilibrium for a trade model, reinforcing the fixed-point route.

    Dating or attribution note

    Method inferred from the literature, not verified in fetched text.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article record, Econometrica 1954 Crossref
  6. 1959

    Debreu's Theory of Value

    consolidation

    Debreu, Theory of Value: An Axiomatic Analysis of Economic Equilibrium, Wiley, 1959

    The first systematic axiomatic monograph on competitive equilibrium with fixed-point existence results.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH 0193.20205 record, book 1959 zbMATH Open
