2015 · Combinatorics

Hodge theory proves log-concavity conjectures for matroids

Heron-Rota-Welsh log-concavity of chromatic polynomials and Rota's conjecture are proved using Hodge-Riemann relations on Chow rings of matroid-like objects.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2010

    Huh posts a graph log-concavity preprint

    precursor

    J. Huh, Milnor numbers of projective hypersurfaces and the chromatic polynomial of graphs, arXiv:1008.4749 (2010)

    The preprint uses geometry to establish graph chromatic-polynomial log-concavity.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv preprint 1008.4749 first posted 27 Aug 2010 arXiv API record
  2. 2012

    Huh–Katz matroid coefficients

    precursor

    J. Huh and E. Katz, Log-concavity of characteristic polynomials and the Bergman fan of matroids (2012)

    Geometric methods reach characteristic-polynomial coefficients for representable matroids.

    Evidence (1)
    • Huh and Katz apply geometric methods to log-concavity of matroid characteristic polynomials. Springer original article record
  3. 2015

    Hodge theory for combinatorial geometries posted

    first statement

    K. Adiprasito, J. Huh and E. Katz, Hodge theory for combinatorial geometries, arXiv:1511.02888 (2015)

    The public preprint states the general matroid Hodge-theoretic construction.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv 1511.02888 posted 9 Nov 2015; abstract mentions the Mason-Welsh conjecture arXiv API record
  4. 2015

    Hodge theory proves matroid log-concavity

    first application

    K. Adiprasito, J. Huh and E. Katz, Hodge theory for combinatorial geometries, arXiv:1511.02888 (2015)

    The construction settles log-concavity questions for general matroids.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv 1511.02888 posted 9 Nov 2015; abstract mentions the Mason-Welsh conjecture arXiv API record
  5. 2018

    Hodge theory in combinatorics (survey)

    consolidation

    Baker, M., Hodge theory in combinatorics, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. (N.S.) 55 (2018) 57-80, DOI 10.1090/bull/1599

    A survey that explains how Hodge-theoretic methods give log-concavity and related inequalities for matroids and beyond.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: BAMS 55 (2018) 57-80 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  6. 2021

    Log-concave polynomials and matroid base counting

    key development

    Anari, N.; Oveis Gharan, S.; et al., Log-concave polynomials I: entropy and a deterministic approximation algorithm for counting bases of matroids, Duke Math. J. 170 (2021) 3459-3504, DOI 10.1215/00127094-2020-0091

    A polynomial-method extension of matroid log-concavity that turns it into an algorithmic counting tool.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: Duke Math. J. 170 (2021) 3459-3504 zBMATH Open record (search API)
