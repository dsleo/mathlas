1975 · Analysis

Derivation of the Boltzmann equation from hard-sphere dynamics

Hilbert's sixth-problem programme of deriving kinetic equations from Newtonian particle systems is carried to long times for hard spheres.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1872

    Boltzmann's kinetic equation

    precursor

    L. Boltzmann, Weitere Studien uber das Warmegleichgewicht unter Gasmolekulen, Wiener Berichte, 1872

    Boltzmann derived the kinetic equation for gas density from collisions, with a molecular chaos assumption.

    Evidence (1)
    • The Boltzmann equation was devised by Boltzmann in 1872. Wikipedia, Boltzmann equation
  2. 1900

    Hilbert's sixth problem

    precursor

    D. Hilbert, Mathematical problems, address 1900; Bull. AMS 8, 1902

    Hilbert asked for rigorous axiomatisation of mechanics and a treatment of kinetic gas theory limits.

    Evidence (2)
    • Hilbert's sixth problem asks for rigorous methods of mean values in kinetic gas theory. Wikipedia, Hilbert's sixth problem
    • Bull. AMS 8 (1902) English version. Crossref
  3. 1972

    Cercignani on the rigid-sphere Boltzmann equation

    precursor

    C. Cercignani, On the Boltzmann equation for rigid spheres, Transport Theory Statist. Phys. 2, 1972

    A study of the Boltzmann equation for hard spheres, the model later treated by Lanford.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Transport Theory Stat. Phys. 2 (1972). Crossref
  4. 1975

    Lanford's derivation for short times

    first statement

    O. E. Lanford, Time evolution of large classical systems, Lecture Notes in Physics 38, 1975

    Lanford first derived the Boltzmann equation from hard-sphere Newtonian dynamics for a short fraction of a collision time.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Lecture Notes in Physics (1975). Crossref
    • Lanford was first to derive the equation for small times in 1975. Wikipedia, Hilbert's sixth problem
  5. 1986

    Illner and Pulvirenti: global validity for a rare gas in vacuum

    key development

    R. Illner, M. Pulvirenti, Global validity of the Boltzmann equation for a two-dimensional rare gas in vacuum, Comm. Math. Phys. 105, 1986

    Extended validity to all times for rarefied gases expanding in vacuum.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: CMP 105 (1986). Crossref
  6. 2014

    Gallagher, Saint-Raymond, Texier: From Newton to Boltzmann

    consolidation

    I. Gallagher, L. Saint-Raymond, B. Texier, From Newton to Boltzmann: hard spheres and short-range potentials, EMS, 2014

    A lecture-notes monograph giving a full proof of the derivation and extensions to short-range potentials.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: EMS Zurich Lectures in Advanced Mathematics. Crossref
  7. 2024

    Deng, Hani, Ma: long-time derivation

    key development

    Y. Deng, Z. Hani, X. Ma, Long time derivation of the Boltzmann equation from hard sphere dynamics, arXiv:2408.07818, 2024

    Extended the derivation from hard-sphere dynamics from a fraction of a collision time to arbitrarily long times.

    Dating or attribution note

    Preprint; peer-review status not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record submitted 2024-08-14. arXiv
  8. 2026

    Fields Medal to Yu Deng

    recognition

    Wikipedia links Deng's Hilbert sixth-problem work to a 2026 Fields Medal.

    Evidence (1)
    • The sixth-problem work led Deng to win the 2026 Fields Medal. Wikipedia, Hilbert's sixth problem
