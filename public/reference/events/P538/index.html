2014 · Category & homological algebra

Higher-form and non-invertible symmetries in quantum field theory

Symmetry in field theory was extended from groups to extended operators and then to non-invertible symmetries described by fusion categories, explaining pion decay rates and phases of matter.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2014

    Higher-form symmetries defined

    first statement

    D. Gaiotto et al., Generalized Global Symmetries, arXiv:1412.5148 (2014)

    The paper defines q-form global symmetries using extended charged operators.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted in December 2014 arXiv
  2. 2021

    Non-invertible topological operators extend the framework

    key development

    B. Heidenreich et al., Non-Invertible Global Symmetries and Completeness of the Spectrum, arXiv:2104.07036 (2021)

    Non-invertible operators are used to connect charge completeness and absence of global symmetries.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted in April 2021; abstract states the extension arXiv
  3. 2022

    Non-invertible symmetries applied to QED and QCD

    first application

    Y. Choi, H. T. Lam and S.-H. Shao, Non-invertible Global Symmetries in the Standard Model, arXiv:2205.05086 (2022)

    The paper applies non-invertible operators to QED and QCD selection rules and reframes the neutral-pion decay coupling.

    Evidence (1)
    • First public version posted in May 2022; abstract specifies QED, QCD and pion coupling arXiv
  4. 2023

    Schafer-Nameki lectures on non-invertible symmetries

    consolidation

    Schafer-Nameki, ICTP Lectures on (Non-)Invertible Generalized Symmetries, arXiv:2305.18296, 2023

    A lecture-notes survey of higher-form and non-invertible symmetries.

    Evidence (1)
    • arXiv record for this title, posted 2023-05-29, author Schafer-Nameki arXiv
