2002 · Algebraic geometry

Derived algebraic geometry

Toen-Vezzosi and Lurie combined algebraic geometry with homotopical algebra to build derived schemes.

Also known as: homotopical algebraic geometry

  1. 2002

    Derived moduli spaces in homotopical algebraic geometry

    From HAG to DAG: derived moduli spaces (2002)

    The 2002 public notes define derived stacks and study derived moduli problems.

  3. 2005

    Homotopical algebraic geometry I: topos theory

    key development

    Toen, Vezzosi, Homotopical algebraic geometry I: topos theory, Adv. Math. (2005)

    Builds the topos-theoretic foundations of homotopical algebraic geometry, the framework that underlies derived algebraic geometry.

    Evidence (1)

    Derived algebraic geometry (EMS survey)

    consolidation

    A survey that presents derived algebraic geometry and its main structures after the 2002 statement.
