1972 · Dynamics

Thermodynamic formalism for hyperbolic dynamics (Sinai, Ruelle, Bowen)

Gibbs states from statistical mechanics describe invariant measures of hyperbolic systems, with pressure replacing free energy.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1902

    Gibbs ensembles in statistical mechanics

    precursor

    Josiah Willard Gibbs, Elementary Principles in Statistical Mechanics, 1902

    Gibbs' equilibrium ensembles are the statistical-mechanical source of the Gibbs states used in dynamics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: 'Elementary principles in statistical mechanics' first publication 1902 Open Library
  2. 1972

    Gibbs measures in ergodic theory

    first statement

    Yakov Sinai, Gibbs measures in ergodic theory, Russian Mathematical Surveys, 1972

    Sinai shows that Gibbs states give invariant measures for dynamical systems, with pressure in the role of free energy.

    Evidence (1)
    • Russian Mathematical Surveys paper 'Gibbs measures in ergodic theory', 1972 Crossref
  3. 1975

    Equilibrium states for Axiom A diffeomorphisms

    consolidation

    Rufus Bowen, Equilibrium States and the Ergodic Theory of Anosov Diffeomorphisms, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 470, 1975

    Bowen's monograph treats equilibrium states for Axiom A systems as a systematic theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Lecture Notes in Mathematics monograph with this title, 1975 Crossref
  4. 1976

    SRB measures for attractors

    first application

    David Ruelle, A measure associated with axiom-A attractors, American Journal of Mathematics, 1976

    Ruelle builds a natural invariant measure on axiom-A attractors, a purposeful use of the Gibbs-state idea on hyperbolic dynamics.

    Dating or attribution note

    Judged as the application from the title and Wikipedia's account; the text was not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • American Journal of Mathematics paper 'A measure associated with axiom-A attractors', 1976 Crossref
  5. 1978

    'Thermodynamic formalism' in a title

    naming

    David Ruelle, Thermodynamic Formalism, 1978

    Ruelle's book gives the field its name in the title and treats pressure and equilibrium states.

    Dating or attribution note

    The title is confirmed by the record; that it is the first use of the phrase is not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: 'Thermodynamic formalism' by Ruelle, first publication 1978 Open Library
