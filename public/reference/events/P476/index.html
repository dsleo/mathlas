2009 · Number theory

Thin groups and the affine sieve (Bourgain-Gamburd-Sarnak)

Expansion in Cayley graphs from sum-product theory powers a sieve on orbits of non-arithmetic subgroups of SL_n(Z).

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 2008

    Cayley-graph expansion for SL2

    precursor

    J. Bourgain and A. Gamburd, Uniform expansion bounds for Cayley graphs of SL2(Fp) (2008)

    Expansion estimates prepare the sieve transfer.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents cayley-graph expansion for sl2 in 2008 publisher / Crossref
  2. 2009

    Affine sieve paper published online

    first statement

    J. Bourgain, A. Gamburd and P. Sarnak, Affine linear sieve, expanders, and sum-product (2009)

    The publisher dates the first online publication 26 November 2009; the issue is March 2010.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents affine sieve paper published online in 2009 publisher / Crossref
  3. 2009

    Expansion and sum-product sieve thin orbits

    first application

    J. Bourgain, A. Gamburd and P. Sarnak, Affine linear sieve, expanders, and sum-product (2009)

    The paper applies expander bounds to almost-prime values along affine orbits.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents expansion and sum-product sieve thin orbits in 2009 publisher / Crossref
  4. 2009

    Affine sieve strengthened through spectral gap

    key development

    J. Bourgain, A. Gamburd and P. Sarnak, Generalization of Selberg's 3/16 Theorem and Affine Sieve (2009)

    A companion preprint gives sharp sieve upper bounds using a different orbit ordering.

    Evidence (1)
    • The source documents affine sieve strengthened through spectral gap in 2009 arXiv
  5. 2010

    Affine linear sieve, expanders, and sum-product

    key development

    Bourgain, Gamburd, Sarnak, Affine linear sieve, expanders, and sum-product, Invent. Math. (2010)

    Proves the affine linear sieve for thin orbits using expansion and sum-product, a main development of the 2009 statement.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2010 Inventiones paper by Bourgain, Gamburd and Sarnak zbMATH Open record
  6. 2014

    Thin groups and superstrong approximation (MSRI volume)

    consolidation

    Breuillard, Oh (eds.), Thin Groups and Superstrong Approximation, MSRI Publications 61 (2014)

    A volume of papers on thin groups and superstrong approximation, collecting the field after the 2009 programme.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 2014 MSRI volume edited by Breuillard and Oh on thin groups zbMATH Open record
