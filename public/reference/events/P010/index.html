1872 · Geometry

Klein's Erlangen programme

Each geometry is characterised by a transformation group and its invariants, giving a dictionary between geometric and algebraic statements and ordering the projective, affine, Euclidean and non-Euclidean geometries.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1859

    Cayley: metric geometry inside projective geometry

    precursor

    A. Cayley, A sixth memoir upon quantics, Phil. Trans. 149, 1859

    Cayley showed Euclidean metric geometry can be defined from projective geometry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref: Phil. Trans. 1859. Crossref
    • 1859 definition of distance via cross-ratio became the Cayley metric. Wikipedia, Beltrami-Klein model
  2. 1871

    Klein's non-Euclidean models from projective geometry

    precursor

    F. Klein, Ueber die sogenannte Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie, Math. Ann. 4, 1871

    Klein related the geometries by projective means, preparing the group-theoretic classification.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Math. Ann. 4 (1871). Crossref
  3. 1872

    Klein's Vergleichende Betrachtungen (Erlangen programme)

    first statement

    F. Klein, Vergleichende Betrachtungen uber neuere geometrische Forschungen, Erlangen: Deichert, 1872

    Klein proposed classifying geometries by the group of transformations whose invariants they study.

    Evidence (2)
    • Archive.org record: Erlangen, A. Deichert, 1872. Internet Archive
    • The Erlanger Programm (1872) unified geometry as study of group invariants. MacTutor, Klein biography
  4. 1893

    Programme reprinted in Math. Annalen

    key development

    F. Klein, Vergleichende Betrachtungen uber neuere geometrische Forschungen, Math. Ann. 43, 1893

    Reprinted with a note, giving it wide circulation.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Math. Ann. 43 (1893). Crossref
  5. 1893

    English translation by Haskell

    key development

    F. Klein (tr. M. W. Haskell), A comparative review of recent researches in geometry, Bull. NY Math. Soc. 2, 1893

    The English translation spread the programme in the English-speaking world.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref: Bull. AMS (formerly Bull. NY Math. Soc.) 2 (1893). Crossref
  6. 1921

    'Zum Erlanger Programm' appears in the title of Klein's collected papers, vol. 1

    naming

    F. Klein, Gesammelte mathematische Abhandlungen, Erster Band, Berlin: Springer, 1921 (subtitle 'Zum Erlanger Programm')

    The earliest zbMATH title containing the phrase 'Erlanger Programm' before 1922; the phrase is used in the subtitle of a volume of collected papers.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record of Klein, Gesammelte mathematische Abhandlungen, Erster Band, with 'Zum Erlanger Programm' in the title (Zbl 48.0012.02) zbMATH Open API
