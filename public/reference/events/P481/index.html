1990 · Combinatorics

Wilf-Zeilberger method: rational-function certificates for hypergeometric identities

Rational-function certificates let a computer prove hypergeometric summation identities automatically.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1978

    Gosper's decision procedure for indefinite summation

    precursor

    Gosper, Decision procedure for indefinite hypergeometric summation, PNAS 75 (1978)

    Gosper's algorithm decides indefinite hypergeometric summation and is the tool that finds WZ counterparts.

    Evidence (2)
    • Gosper's algorithm provides the WZ counterpart Wikipedia: Wilf-Zeilberger pair
    • PNAS paper, author and year Crossref
  2. 1990

    WZ pairs certify combinatorial identities

    first statement

    Wilf, Zeilberger, Rational functions certify combinatorial identities, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 3 (1990)

    Wilf and Zeilberger introduce WZ pairs and rational-function certificates that prove hypergeometric identities.

    Evidence (2)
    • Joint paper 'Rational functions certify combinatorial identities' (1990) MacTutor: Herbert Wilf
    • JAMS paper, authors and year Crossref
  3. 1990

    Zeilberger's fast algorithm for terminating hypergeometric identities

    first application

    Zeilberger, A fast algorithm for proving terminating hypergeometric identities, Discrete Mathematics 80 (1990)

    Zeilberger's algorithm gives an automatic computer proof procedure for terminating hypergeometric identities, the purposeful use of the method on identities.

    Evidence (2)
    • Zeilberger's algorithm: automated proofs of identities Wikipedia: Doron Zeilberger
    • Discrete Mathematics paper, author and year Crossref
  4. 1992

    Certification of multisum, integral and q-identities

    key development

    Wilf, Zeilberger, Rational function certification of multisum/integral/q identities, Bull. AMS (1992)

    The 1992 Bulletin paper extends rational-function certificates to multisums, integrals and q-identities.

    Evidence (2)
    • Bulletin AMS 1992 paper, authors and year Crossref
    • WZ method extended to multisums and q-analogues MacTutor: Herbert Wilf
  5. 1996

    Petkovsek, Wilf and Zeilberger, 'A=B'

    consolidation

    M. Petkovsek, H. S. Wilf, D. Zeilberger, A=B, Wellesley, MA: A. K. Peters, 1996

    A book that presents the WZ method and its algorithmic certificates for hypergeometric identities in systematic form.

    Dating or attribution note

    The title names the identities the method proves; the book is the standard book-length treatment. Its content was not read in this pass.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'A=B', Petkovsek, Wilf and Zeilberger, A. K. Peters 1996, Zbl 0848.05002 zbMATH Open API
