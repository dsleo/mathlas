1987 · Algebraic topology

Elliptic cohomology and topological modular forms

Elliptic curves and modular forms are used to build cohomology theories that refine genera and are tied to string theory; Hopkins reported on them at the 2002 congress.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1983

    Witten's index conjecture for loop spaces

    precursor

    Witten conjectured an index-theoretic result tied to elliptic genera before the cohomology theory existed.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: in 1983 Witten conjectured an index result for circle actions Wikipedia: Elliptic cohomology
  2. 1987

    Ochanine's elliptic genus

    first application

    Ochanine, Sur les genres multiplicatifs definis par des integrales elliptiques, Topology 26 (1987)

    Multiplicative genera defined from elliptic integrals: the first topological use of elliptic curves in cobordism theory.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
    • Wikipedia: Ochanine introduced elliptic genera Wikipedia: Elliptic cohomology
  3. 1987

    Elliptic genera and quantum field theory

    first statement

    Witten, Elliptic genera and quantum field theory, Commun. Math. Phys. 109 (1987) 525-536

    Relates elliptic genera to two-dimensional quantum field theory and index theory on loop spaces.

    Evidence (2)
    • Journal, pages and year verified (Crossref) Crossref
    • Wikipedia: Witten related elliptic genera to index theory on loop spaces Wikipedia: Elliptic cohomology
  4. 1988

    Proceedings on elliptic curves and modular forms in topology

    consolidation

    Landweber (ed.), Elliptic Curves and Modular Forms in Algebraic Topology, Lecture Notes in Math. 1326 (1988)

    Conference proceedings collecting the early theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book title, series and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
  5. 2002

    Hopkins ICM on topological modular forms

    key development

    Hopkins, Algebraic topology and modular forms, Proc. ICM Beijing 2002

    ICM survey on tmf and modular forms in homotopy theory, the setting for the Witten genus lift.

    Dating or attribution note

    Wikipedia credits the lift to a series of works; this ICM survey is recorded as the key development but its content was not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Title, congress and year verified (zbMATH) zbMATH
    • Wikipedia: Witten genus lifted to tmf by Hopkins, Ando, Rezk and Strickland Wikipedia: Topological modular forms

    Survey of elliptic cohomology

    consolidation

    Lurie, A survey of elliptic cohomology, in Algebraic topology: the Abel Symposium 2007 (2009)

    Survey of the modern elliptic cohomology theory.
