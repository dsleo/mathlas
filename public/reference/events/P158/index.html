1250 · Astronomy & trigonometry

Al-Tusi: trigonometry as an independent discipline

Al-Tusi's Quadrilateral treated plane and spherical trigonometry separately from astronomy.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 100

    Menelaus' Sphaerica

    precursor

    Menelaus of Alexandria, Sphaerica, about 100 CE

    Earliest surviving work on spherical triangles, framed for astronomy.

    Evidence (2)
    • Menelaus' Sphaerica is the earliest known work on spherical trigonometry (about 100). MacTutor, Trigonometric functions
    • Only Sphaerica of Menelaus survives; it defines the spherical triangle. MacTutor, Menelaus biography
  2. 1250

    Treatise on the Quadrilateral

    first statement

    Nasir al-Din al-Tusi, Shakl al-qatta (Treatise on the Quadrilateral), 13th century

    First surviving exposition of plane and spherical trigonometry as one system, independent of astronomy.

    Evidence (3)
    • Treatise on the quadrilateral is the first extant exposition of the whole system and a branch of pure mathematics. MacTutor, Al-Tusi biography
    • Overview of the treatise (13th century; complete spherical trigonometry with proofs). arXiv 2605.03516 (Papadopoulos)
    • Study of the treatise is indexed. zbMATH 1473.01018 (Papadopoulos, 2018)
  3. 1464

    Regiomontanus' De triangulis omnimodis

    key development

    Regiomontanus, De triangulis omnimodis, written about 1464

    Systematic European treatment of triangle solving; later printed 1533.

    Evidence (2)
    • De triangulis omnimodis dated 1464, printed 1533. MacTutor, Trigonometric functions
    • 1533 Nuremberg printing exists. Internet Archive ARes352131
  4. 1595

    The word 'trigonometry' appears in a title

    naming

    Pitiscus, Trigonometria, 1595

    First use of the word trigonometry in a book title.

    Evidence (3)
    • The term first appears as the title of Pitiscus' Trigonometria (1595). MacTutor, Trigonometric functions
    • Pitiscus coined the word; first published at Heidelberg 1595 inside Scultetus' Sphaericorum libri tres. MacTutor, Pitiscus biography
    • 1600 revised edition is catalogued. Open Library OL18411014W
  5. Date unknown

    Al-Jayyani's Book of unknown arcs of a sphere

    precursor

    Al-Jayyani, Book of unknown arcs of a sphere (11th century)

    Called the first treatise on spherical trigonometry; has the general law of sines and the polar triangle.

    Evidence (1)
    • The book is the first treatise on spherical trigonometry and includes the law of sines and polar triangle. MacTutor, al-Jayyani biography
