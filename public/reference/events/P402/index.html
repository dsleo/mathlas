1900 · Optimization & control

Calculus of variations to Hilbert's direct methods

Hilbert justified Dirichlet's principle with direct variational methods, rescuing Riemann's use of minimisation in complex analysis.

Also known as: direct method in the calculus of variations

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1870

    Weierstrass criticises Dirichlet's principle

    precursor

    Weierstrass, objection to Dirichlet's principle, 1870

    Weierstrass gives a functional with greatest lower bound that is not attained, undermining the assumption that the minimum exists.

    Evidence (1)
    • Weierstrass published the first criticism in 1870 with a counterexample functional. English Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
  2. 1900

    Hilbert announces a justification of Dirichlet's principle

    key development

    Hilbert, Ueber das Dirichletsche Prinzip, Jahresbericht DMV 8 (1900)

    Hilbert shows how the principle can be justified, opening the direct method.

    Evidence (2)
    • In 1900 Hilbert justified Riemann's use of the principle by the direct method. English Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
    • Hilbert, Ueber das Dirichletsche Prinzip, Crelle 129 (1905), 63-67, cited on the page. Crossref
  3. 1900

    Hilbert announces a proof of Dirichlet's principle

    first statement

    D. Hilbert, Ueber das Dirichletsche Princip, Jahresbericht der Deutschen Mathematiker-Vereinigung 8 (1900), 184-188

    Hilbert publishes his first treatment justifying the Dirichlet principle by direct variational methods, four years before the full Mathematische Annalen paper.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH lists the note in Jahresbericht DMV 8 (1900), pp. 184-188, with an EuDML scan. zbMATH Open document 2665638
    • Wikipedia dates Hilbert's justification of Riemann's use of the principle to 1900. Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle
  4. 1904

    Hilbert's justification of Dirichlet's principle

    first application

    Hilbert, D., 'Über das Dirichletsche Prinzip', Math. Ann. 59 (1904)

    Hilbert justifies Dirichlet's principle by the direct method of the calculus of variations, rescuing Riemann's use.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Über das Dirichletsche Prinzip', year 1904 zbMATH API, query 'Hilbert Uber das Dirichletsche Prinzip'
    • Hilbert justified Riemann's use of Dirichlet's principle by developing the direct method Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle (saved wikitext)
  5. 1920

    Tonelli's semicontinuity method

    key development

    Tonelli, La semicontinuita nel calcolo delle variazioni, Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo 44 (1920)

    Lower semicontinuity of functionals becomes the standard route to minimisers by the direct method.

    Dating or attribution note

    Role inferred from title and general knowledge.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper recorded in Crossref, 1920. Crossref
  6. 1950

    Courant's Dirichlet's Principle, Conformal Mapping, and Minimal Surfaces

    consolidation

    Richard Courant, Dirichlet's Principle, Conformal Mapping, and Minimal Surfaces, Interscience, 1950

    A monograph on the variational method and its uses.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book first published 1950. Open Library
  7. 1975

    Monna's history of Dirichlet's principle

    consolidation

    Monna, A. F., Dirichlet's Principle: A Mathematical Comedy of Errors (1975)

    Historical study of the principle and its direct-method repair; cited in the Wikipedia lead.

    Dating or attribution note

    Historical study, not a mathematical consolidation; bibliographic record not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • Cited as the historical source for the Dirichlet principle history Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle (saved wikitext, citing Monna 1975)
  8. Date unknown

    Riemann's use of Dirichlet's principle

    precursor

    Riemann used the minimisation principle in complex analysis, which was later questioned; the date of his use is not verified here.

    Evidence (1)
    • Dirichlet's principle name due to Riemann, who applied it in complex analysis Wikipedia, Dirichlet's principle (saved wikitext)
