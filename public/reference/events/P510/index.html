1992 · Probability

Tracy-Widom distributions and universality in random matrices

The Airy kernel gives the edge fluctuation law of random matrices, later shown to be universal across ensembles.

Also known as: Tracy–Widom law

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1992

    Airy-kernel level-spacing law

    first statement

    Level-Spacing Distributions and the Airy Kernel (1992)

    The public 1992 preprint obtains the Airy-kernel Fredholm-determinant edge distribution.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated publication record and abstract support the 1992 result. Original publication or repository record
  2. 1996

    On orthogonal and symplectic matrix ensembles

    key development

    Tracy, Widom, On orthogonal and symplectic matrix ensembles, Comm. Math. Phys. (1996)

    Extends the Airy-kernel and level-spacing analysis to the orthogonal and symplectic ensembles, a main development of the 1992 statement.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 1996 CMP paper by Tracy and Widom on orthogonal and symplectic matrix ensembles zbMATH Open record
  3. 2010

    Log-gases and random matrices

    consolidation

    Forrester, Log-Gases and Random Matrices, Princeton University Press (2010)

    A monograph that presents the log-gas and random-matrix framework behind the Tracy-Widom distributions and universality.

    Evidence (1)
