≈ 1994 · Category & homological algebra

Categorification as programme

Crane-Frenkel proposed categorifying quantum groups; Khovanov-Lauda and Rouquier gave concrete versions.

≈ marks an approximate map placement; a dated first statement is not established for this event.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1994

    Hopf categories in four-dimensional TQFT

    precursor

    Four dimensional topological quantum field theory, Hopf categories, and the canonical bases (1994)

    The public paper proposes Hopf-category structures and relates them to canonical bases.

    Evidence (1)
    • The dated record and abstract support this 1994 milestone. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2000

    Khovanov's categorification of the Jones polynomial

    key development

    Khovanov, M., A categorification of the Jones polynomial, Duke Mathematical Journal (2000)

    Constructs a homology theory whose Euler characteristic is the Jones polynomial, the first major categorification example.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, Duke (2000) Crossref
  3. 2006

    Khovanov's survey Link homology and categorification

    consolidation

    Khovanov, M., Link homology and categorification (2006)

    A survey of link homology as the categorification of link polynomials.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record 'Link homology and categorification', 2006 zbMATH Open API
  4. 2008

    Diagrammatic categorification of quantum groups

    key development

    A diagrammatic approach to categorification of quantum groups I (2008)

    The preprint constructs categories whose Grothendieck groups recover parts of quantum groups.

    • The dated record and abstract support this 2008 milestone. Original publication or repository record
