1981 · Differential equations

Viscosity solutions: control theory meets PDE

Dynamic-programming value functions, merely continuous, are given a rigorous notion of solution for fully nonlinear first and second order PDE.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1981

    Uniqueness condition for generalized Hamilton-Jacobi solutions

    first statement

    Crandall, M. G., Lions, P.-L., Condition d'unicité pour les solutions généralisées des équations de Hamilton-Jacobi du premier ordre, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 292, 1981

    Crandall and Lions give the first uniqueness criterion for generalized solutions of first-order Hamilton-Jacobi equations, the origin of the viscosity notion.

    Evidence (2)
    • Note in C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 292 (1981), pp. 183-186 (zbMATH 0469.49023) zbMATH record
    • Viscosity solution concept introduced in the early 1980s by Lions and Crandall Wikipedia: Viscosity solution
  2. 1982

    Monograph Generalized solutions of Hamilton-Jacobi equations

    consolidation

    Lions, P.-L., Generalized Solutions of Hamilton-Jacobi Equations, Pitman, 1982

    Lions' monograph is the first book-length treatment of the generalized (viscosity) solutions.

    Dating or attribution note

    Recorded as the first monograph; the claim of being first systematic treatment is judged from the title, not read.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book published by Pitman, 1982 Open Library record
  3. 1983

    Viscosity solutions of Hamilton-Jacobi equations

    key development

    Crandall, M. G., Lions, P.-L., Viscosity solutions of Hamilton-Jacobi equations, Trans. AMS 277, 1983

    The Trans. AMS paper develops existence, uniqueness and stability of viscosity solutions, the main theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article in Trans. AMS, 1983 (DOI 10.1090/s0002-9947-1983-0690039-8) Crossref record
  4. 1984

    Properties of viscosity solutions

    key development

    Crandall, M. G., Evans, L. C., Lions, P.-L., Some properties of viscosity solutions of Hamilton-Jacobi equations, Trans. AMS 282, 1984

    Crandall, Evans and Lions establish further properties and the comparison-based theory of viscosity solutions.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Trans. AMS, 1984 (DOI 10.1090/s0002-9947-1984-0732102-x) Crossref record
    • Refinements in 1984 by Crandall, Evans and Lions Wikipedia: Viscosity solution
  5. 1992

    User's guide to viscosity solutions

    consolidation

    Crandall, M. G., Ishii, H., Lions, P.-L., User's guide to viscosity solutions of second order partial differential equations, Bull. AMS 27, 1992

    The user's guide is the standard survey of viscosity solutions for second-order equations.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article in Bull. AMS, 1992 (DOI 10.1090/s0273-0979-1992-00266-5) Crossref record
    • Crandall, Ishii and Lions' survey has become a standard reference Wikipedia: Pierre-Louis Lions
