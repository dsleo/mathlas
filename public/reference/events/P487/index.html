1984 · Algebraic topology

Chromatic homotopy theory and the nilpotence theorem

Formal groups organise stable homotopy into chromatic layers, and the nilpotence theorem proves the Ravenel conjectures.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1969

    Formal group laws and complex cobordism

    precursor

    Daniel Quillen, On the formal group laws of unoriented and complex cobordism theory, Bulletin of the AMS, 1969

    Quillen links formal group laws to complex cobordism, the algebra that the chromatic picture later uses.

    Evidence (1)
    • Bulletin of the AMS paper with this title, 1969 Crossref
  2. 1984

    Chromatic localisation and the Ravenel conjectures

    first statement

    Douglas C. Ravenel, Localization with respect to certain periodic homology theories, American Journal of Mathematics, 1984

    Ravenel introduces chromatic localisation and poses the nilpotence and periodicity conjectures at the end of the paper.

    Evidence (2)
    • American Journal of Mathematics paper 'Localization with respect to certain periodic homology theories', 1984 Crossref
    • Wikipedia (Ravenel's conjectures): posed at the end of a 1984 paper, circulated earlier in preprint Wikipedia
  3. 1986

    Monograph on complex cobordism and stable homotopy

    consolidation

    Douglas C. Ravenel, Complex Cobordism and Stable Homotopy Groups of Spheres, Academic Press, 1986

    Ravenel's monograph gives the first systematic treatment of the chromatic picture and its algebra.

    Evidence (1)
    • Open Library: 'Complex cobordism and stable homotopy groups of spheres' by Ravenel, Academic Press, 1986 Open Library
  4. 1988

    Nilpotence theorem

    key development

    Devinatz, Hopkins, Smith, Nilpotence and stable homotopy theory I, Annals of Mathematics, 1988

    Devinatz, Hopkins and Smith prove the nilpotence theorem, which resolves the central Ravenel conjecture.

    Evidence (1)
    • Annals of Mathematics paper 'Nilpotence and Stable Homotopy Theory I', 1988 Crossref
