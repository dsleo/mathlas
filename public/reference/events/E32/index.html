1970 · Number theory

Logic solves Hilbert's tenth problem

Recursively enumerable sets are shown to be Diophantine, so no algorithm decides solvability of Diophantine equations.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1961

    Davis–Putnam–Robinson reduction

    precursor

    M. Davis, H. Putnam and J. Robinson, The decision problem for exponential Diophantine equations, Ann. Math. 74 (1961)

    The reduction makes the missing exponential-Diophantine step decisive but does not yet solve Hilbert's tenth problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • 1961 reduction to exponential Diophantine equations Annals of Mathematics / JSTOR
  2. 1970

    Matiyasevich closes Hilbert's tenth problem

    first statement

    Yu. Matiyasevich, The Diophantineness of enumerable sets, Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR 191 (1970)

    The article establishes the Diophantineness result needed for the negative solution.

    Evidence (1)
    • Original Russian article published in 1970 Math-Net.Ru
  3. 1970

    Undecidability follows for Hilbert's tenth

    first application

    Yu. Matiyasevich, The Diophantineness of enumerable sets, Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR 191 (1970)

    The Diophantine representation is used to finish the algorithmic unsolvability argument.

    Evidence (1)
    • Original Russian article published in 1970 Math-Net.Ru
  4. 1972

    Detailed account of Diophantine sets

    key development

    Yu. Matiyasevich, Diophantine sets, Russian Math. Surveys 27 (1972)

    Matiyasevich gives a fuller account of the result and the logic behind it.

    Evidence (1)
    • Detailed survey published in 1972 Math-Net.Ru / journal record
  5. 1984

    Register-machine proof of exponential Diophantine representation

    key development

    Jones, J. P.; Matiyasevich, Yu. V., Register machine proof of the theorem on exponential diophantine representation of enumerable sets, J. Symb. Log. 49 (1984) 818-829

    A shorter and more machine-based proof of the Diophantine representation theorem that underpins the solution of Hilbert's tenth problem.

    Evidence (1)
    • Jones–Matiyasevich 1984 JSL paper on exponential Diophantine representation, DOI 10.2307/2274135 zBMATH Open record (search API)
  6. 1993

    First monograph on Hilbert's tenth problem

    consolidation

    Matiyasevich, Yu. V., Hilbert's tenth problem, Moscow: Nauka (Matematicheskaya Logika i Osnovaniya Matematiki 26), 1993

    Matiyasevich's book-length treatment of the solution and of the Diophantine-set machinery built around it.

    Evidence (1)
    • zBMATH record: monograph 'Hilbert's tenth problem' in the Nauka series, 1993 zBMATH Open record (search API)
