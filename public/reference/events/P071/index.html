Calabi conjecture (Calabi-Yau metrics)

The complex Monge-Ampere equation is solved with a priori estimates, producing Ricci-flat Kahler metrics and showing a topological condition forces a metric.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1954

    Calabi announces the conjecture

    precursor

    Calabi first made the conjecture in 1954 at the International Congress, before the published statement.

    Dating or attribution note

    Conference statement, not the published one; the 1957 paper governs as first_statement. The 1954 Congress record was not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • The Calabi conjecture was made in 1954 MacTutor biography of Shing-Tung Yau (quoting Nirenberg)
  2. 1957

    Calabi conjecture in print

    first statement

    Calabi, E., On Kahler manifolds with vanishing canonical class, in Algebraic Geometry and Topology (Princeton symposium), 1957

    The published statement of the conjecture on Kahler metrics with prescribed Ricci form on compact complex manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • On Kahler manifolds with vanishing canonical class, 1957 Crossref record
  3. 1977

    Yau's proof announced

    key development

    Yau, S.-T., Calabi's conjecture and some new results in algebraic geometry, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 1977

    Announces Yau's solution of the Calabi conjecture by a priori estimates for the complex Monge-Ampere equation.

    Evidence (2)
    • Calabi's conjecture and some new results in algebraic geometry, PNAS, 1977 Crossref record
    • Yau solved the Calabi conjecture, with a priori estimates for the Monge-Ampere equation Wikipedia, Calabi conjecture
  4. 1978

    Full proof of the Calabi conjecture

    key development

    Yau, S.-T., On the Ricci curvature of a compact Kahler manifold and the complex Monge-Ampere equation I, Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics, 1978

    The full proof, which gives the existence of a Ricci-flat Kahler metric in each Kahler class with vanishing first Chern class.

    Evidence (1)
    • On the Ricci curvature of a compact Kahler manifold and the complex Monge-Ampere equation I, CPAM, 1978 Crossref record
  5. 1982

    Aubin's monograph on Monge-Ampere equations

    consolidation

    Aubin, T., Nonlinear Analysis on Manifolds. Monge-Ampere Equations, Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften, Springer, 1982

    A monograph treating the Monge-Ampere equation on Kahler manifolds, including the Calabi conjecture.

    Evidence (2)
    • Complex Monge-Ampere equation on compact Kahler manifolds, a chapter in the Grundlehren volume, 1982 Crossref record for the Grundlehren volume
    • Aubin's monograph gives a proof of the Calabi conjecture Wikipedia, Calabi conjecture (reference list)
  6. 1985

    Calabi-Yau manifolds in superstring vacua

    first application

    Candelas, P., Horowitz, G. T., Strominger, A., Witten, E., Vacuum configurations for superstrings, Nuclear Physics B, 1985

    Uses Ricci-flat Calabi-Yau metrics as compactification spaces for superstring vacua, a first physical use of the theorem.

    Dating or attribution note

    The string-compactification claim is from the field's general knowledge and the title; the paper's text was not read in this pass.

    Evidence (1)
    • Vacuum configurations for superstrings, Nucl. Phys. B, 1985 Crossref record
