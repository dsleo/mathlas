1741 · Topology

Topology from the geometry of position (Euler to Listing)

Euler's measurement-free treatments of bridges and polyhedra, Lhuilier's genus correction and Listing's connectivity studies established a geometry of position whose name was fixed in 1847.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1741

    Euler's Konigsberg bridges paper published

    first statement

    Euler, Solutio problematis ad geometriam situs pertinentis, Commentarii Acad. Sci. Petropol. 8, pp. 128-140 (volume dated 1736, published 1741; written 1735), E53

    Euler solved the bridge problem by pure connectivity and said in the title that it belongs to a geometry where measure plays no role.

    Evidence (2)
    • Euler Archive E53 lists publication 1741, written 1735, journal vol. 8 pp. 128-140. Euler Archive (Scholarly Commons, Pacific)
    • MacTutor notes the title shows Euler knew this was a different geometry in which distance is irrelevant. MacTutor, Topology in mathematics
  2. 1758

    Euler's polyhedron formula papers

    key development

    Euler, Elementa doctrinae solidorum, Novi Comm. Acad. Sci. Petropol. 4, pp. 109-140 (1758; written 1750), E230

    Euler set out the relation between vertices, edges and faces of a polyhedron, the second measurement-free result of the field.

    Evidence (2)
    • Euler Archive E230: published 1758, written 1750, Novi Commentarii vol. 4 pp. 109-140. Euler Archive
    • Wikipedia Euler characteristic: concept originally defined for polyhedra; Euler gave the polyhedron relation. English Wikipedia, Euler characteristic
  3. 1813

    Lhuilier's genus correction of Euler's formula

    key development

    Lhuilier, Memoire sur la polyedrometrie..., Annales de mathematiques pures et appliquees 3 (1812-1813), pp. 169-189

    Lhuilier showed that Euler's relation fails for solids with holes and corrected it to 2 - 2g, bringing in the genus.

    Evidence (2)
    • Numdam record: Annales de mathematiques pures et appliquees, Tome 3 (1812-1813), pp. 169-189, with examination of exceptions to Euler's theorem. Numdam
    • MacTutor says Lhuilier noticed Euler's formula fails for solids with holes and gave v - e + f = 2 - 2g in 1813. MacTutor, Topology in mathematics
  4. 1836

    Listing coins 'topology' in a letter

    precursor

    Listing used the word topology in a private letter to his former teacher, not yet in print.

    Evidence (1)
    • MacTutor says the word topology first appears in a long letter to his school teacher written on the Sicily journey, and dates its first use to 1836. MacTutor, Listing biography
  5. 1847

    Listing's Vorstudien zur Topologie

    naming

    Listing, Vorstudien zur Topologie, Gottinger Studien 1847 (separate print Gottingen, Vandenhoeck und Ruprecht, 1848)

    The word topology appeared in print for the first time in this essay, which defines it as the study of connection and position without regard to measure.

    Evidence (3)
    • Internet Archive holds the book dated 1848 (Vandenhoeck und Ruprecht). Internet Archive
    • MacTutor says Listing wrote the Vorstudien in 1847 and that it was the first printed use of the word. MacTutor, Listing biography
    • Wikipedia Topology: introduced the term in Vorstudien zur Topologie, 1847. English Wikipedia, Topology
  6. Date unknown

    Leibniz's geometry of position (idea only)

    precursor

    Leibniz imagined a geometry of position (geometria situs, analysis situs) in the seventeenth century, before any worked topological result.

    Evidence (1)
    • Topology article says the ideas go back to Leibniz's geometria situs and analysis situs in the 17th century. English Wikipedia, Topology
