1999 · Differential equations

Random matrix universality and Dyson / Tracy-Widom in KPZ

Longest increasing subsequence fluctuations were shown to follow Tracy-Widom, linking random permutations to random matrices.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1999

    Growth fluctuations linked to Tracy–Widom law

    first statement

    K. Johansson, Shape Fluctuations and Random Matrices, arXiv:math/9903134 (1999)

    Johansson’s public paper shows a growth-model fluctuation law converging to the GUE Tracy–Widom distribution.

    Evidence (1)
    • ArXiv dates the preprint to March 1999 and states the Tracy–Widom limit. Original publication or repository record
  2. 2000

    Johansson: shape fluctuations and random matrices

    key development

    Johansson, K., Shape fluctuations and random matrices, Communications in Mathematical Physics (2000)

    Shows Tracy-Widom fluctuations for a growth model, extending the random-matrix universality into KPZ-type growth.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, CMP (2000) Crossref
  3. 2012

    Corwin's survey on the KPZ universality class

    consolidation

    Corwin, I., The Kardar-Parisi-Zhang equation and universality class, Random Matrices: Theory and Applications (2012)

    A survey of the KPZ universality class and its Tracy-Widom limits.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record, RMTA (2012) Crossref
