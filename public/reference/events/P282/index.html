Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1954

    Hirzebruch's Riemann-Roch theorem for algebraic varieties

    first statement

    Hirzebruch, Arithmetic genera and the theorem of Riemann-Roch for algebraic varieties, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 1954

    Hirzebruch states and proves the Riemann-Roch theorem for algebraic varieties of arbitrary dimension using topological methods.

    Evidence (2)
    • Article record, PNAS 40 (1954) 110 Crossref
    • Hirzebruch's 1954 result generalising Riemann-Roch to complex algebraic varieties Wikipedia, Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch theorem
  2. 1956

    Neue topologische Methoden in der algebraischen Geometrie

    consolidation

    Hirzebruch, Neue topologische Methoden in der algebraischen Geometrie, Springer (Ergebnisse), 1956

    The monograph presenting the Riemann-Roch theorem and topological methods in full, a basic text for sheaf-theoretic algebraic geometry.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record for the 1956 Springer book Crossref
    • MacTutor: the monograph sets out the Riemann-Roch theory fully MacTutor, Friedrich Hirzebruch biography
  3. 1958

    Borel-Serre Riemann-Roch theorem

    key development

    Borel and Serre, Le théorème de Riemann-Roch, Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France, 1958

    Borel and Serre give a published general treatment of Riemann-Roch that became a reference point for the subsequent Grothendieck-Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch development.

    Dating or attribution note

    Role inferred from the title and the general literature; not verified in fetched text.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article record, Bull. SMF 1958 Crossref
  4. 1959

    Riemann-Roch for differentiable manifolds via K-theory

    first application

    Atiyah and Hirzebruch, Riemann-Roch theorems for differentiable manifolds, Bulletin of the AMS, 1959

    Atiyah and Hirzebruch apply topological K-theory to extend the Riemann-Roch formula to differentiable manifolds.

    Evidence (1)
    • Article record, Bull. AMS 1959 Crossref
