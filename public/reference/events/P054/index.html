1942 · Algebra

Homological algebra

Chain complexes, derived functors and exact sequences, first tools of algebraic topology, are turned into a general machinery for modules and then for sheaves.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1895

    Poincare's Analysis situs

    precursor

    Poincare, H., Analysis situs, J. Ecole Polytechnique (2) 1 (1895) 1-123, zbMATH 26.0541.07

    Introduces homology in combinatorial topology, the topological origin of the chain-complex machinery.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Analysis situs', J. Ecole Polytech. (2) 1 (1895) zbMATH API (JFM 26.0541.07)
    • Wikipedia: origins in combinatorial topology, chiefly by Poincare and Hilbert Wikipedia: Homological algebra
  2. 1942

    Eilenberg and Mac Lane define homology of groups through extensions

    first statement

    S. Eilenberg, S. Mac Lane, Group extensions and homology, Ann. of Math. (2) 43 (1942) 757-831

    An early algebraic definition of group homology and cohomology via extensions, which is the first public use of homology as an algebraic tool in this search.

    Evidence (4)
    • Group Extensions and Homology appears in Annals of Mathematics 43 (1942), pp. 757–831. Annals of Mathematics/JSTOR article record
    • Crossref: 'Group Extensions and Homology', Ann. of Math. 43, pp. 757-831, issued October 1942. Crossref API
    • Barr's history: the Eilenberg-Mac Lane paper originated from the observation that Steenrod's groups were the groups of the algebraic theory of group extensions. M. Barr, Algebraic cohomology: the early days
    • MacTutor: the 1942 paper introduced Hom and Ext for the first time. MacTutor Biography: Samuel Eilenberg
  3. 1945

    Eilenberg and Mac Lane's natural equivalences

    precursor

    Eilenberg, S., Mac Lane, S., General theory of natural equivalences, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 58 (1945) 231-294, zbMATH 0061.09204

    Introduces the categories, functors and natural transformations in which homological algebra was later stated.

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH record: 'General theory of natural equivalences', TAMS 58 (1945) zbMATH API (Zbl 0061.09204)
    • MacTutor: Eilenberg and Mac Lane introduced functor and natural transformation, and category in 1945 MacTutor Biography: Samuel Eilenberg
    • Crossref: 'General theory of natural equivalences', Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 58 (1945) 231-294. Crossref API
  4. 1955

    Serre's coherent sheaves via homological methods

    first application

    Serre, J.-P., Faisceaux algebriques coherents, Ann. of Math. 61 (1955), doi 10.2307/1969915

    Applies homological methods (derived functors) to coherent sheaves, the first target problem for the theory.

    Evidence (3)
    • Crossref record: 'Faisceaux Algebriques Coherents', Annals, 1955 Crossref API
    • Serre's introduction: section 4 relates the groups H^q(X, F) to the Ext functors introduced by Cartan-Eilenberg. Serre, FAC, English translation (Milne's page)
    • Grothendieck: it is less than four years since cohomological methods (methods of Homological Algebra) were introduced into Algebraic Geometry in Serre's paper. Grothendieck, The cohomology theory of abstract algebraic varieties (ICM 1958, printed 1960)
  5. 1956

    Cartan and Eilenberg's Homological Algebra

    consolidation

    Cartan, H., Eilenberg, S., Homological Algebra, Princeton University Press (1956), doi 10.1515/9781400883844

    The first systematic book on the subject, which consolidated the field.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'Homological Algebra' (PMS-19), Princeton 1956 Crossref API
    • zbMATH record: 'Homological algebra', Princeton 1956 zbMATH API (Zbl 0075.24305)
  6. 1956

    Title 'Homological Algebra'

    naming

    Cartan, H., Eilenberg, S., Homological Algebra (1956), as above

    The book title gives the field its name within the searched index (no earlier title found 1945-1956).

    Evidence (3)
    • zbMATH search for titles with 'homological algebra' 1945-1956 returns only this book among general titles zbMATH API (Zbl 0075.24305)
    • MacTutor: the manuscript of Homological Algebra was finished by 1953 but published in 1956. MacTutor Biography: Samuel Eilenberg
    • nLab: the term 'homological algebra' was first coined in the Cartan-Eilenberg book. nLab: Homological Algebra
  7. 1957

    Grothendieck's abelian-category framework

    key development

    Grothendieck, A., Sur quelques points d'algebre homologique, Tohoku Math. J. 9 (1957), doi 10.2748/tmj/1178244839

    Gives the abelian-category framework for derived functors and sheaf cohomology, the paper that made the field general.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'Sur quelques points d'algebre homologique, I', Tohoku, 1957 Crossref API
    • Wikipedia: the celebrated 1957 paper by Grothendieck using the abelian category concept Wikipedia: Homological algebra
