1967 · Dynamics

Smale's hyperbolic dynamics (Axiom A, structural stability)

Topological methods were applied to structural stability, followed by strange attractors.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1958

    Smale learns of Pontryagin's structural stability work

    precursor

    Smale starts applying topological methods to structural stability after learning Pontryagin's work on structurally stable vector fields.

    Evidence (1)
    • Smale began topological work on structural stability in 1958, after Pontryagin's results MacTutor, Smale biography
  2. 1960

    Morse inequalities for a dynamical system

    precursor

    Stephen Smale, Morse inequalities for a dynamical system, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 66 (1960) 43-49

    Smale applies Morse-theoretic counting to a dynamical system, introducing what became Morse-Smale systems.

    Evidence (2)
    • Smale 1960 Bull. AMS record Crossref
    • Smale introduced Morse-Smale systems, citing the 1960 paper Wikipedia, Stephen Smale
  3. 1967

    Smale's survey 'Differentiable dynamical systems' defines Axiom A

    first statement

    Stephen Smale, Differentiable dynamical systems, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 73 (1967) 747-817

    Smale's survey sets out the axiom A class of dynamical systems, a term that originates with him.

    Evidence (2)
    • Smale 1967 Bull. AMS record Crossref
    • Term 'axiom A' originates with Smale, citing the 1967 survey Wikipedia, Axiom A
  4. 1967

    'Axiom A' term introduced

    naming

    Stephen Smale, Differentiable dynamical systems, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 73 (1967)

    The label 'axiom A' for the class of systems is first used in the same survey.

    Evidence (1)
    • Term 'axiom A' originates with Smale Wikipedia, Axiom A
  5. 1970

    Markov partitions for Axiom A diffeomorphisms

    key development

    Rufus Bowen, Markov partitions for Axiom A diffeomorphisms, Amer. J. Math. 92 (1970) 725-747

    Bowen shows the non-wandering set of an axiom A diffeomorphism carries a Markov partition, so the dynamics is conjugate to a shift of finite type on a generic subset.

    Evidence (2)
    • Bowen 1970 Am. J. Math. record Crossref
    • Markov partition result cited to Bowen 1970 Wikipedia, Axiom A
  6. 1971

    Structural stability theorem (Robbin)

    key development

    Joel Robbin, A structural stability theorem, Annals of Math. 94 (1971)

    Robbin's structural stability theorem is a core result of the programme that Smale outlined.

    Dating or attribution note

    Scope of the theorem not checked line by line; only the record and its place in the programme are established.

    Evidence (1)
    • Robbin 1971 Annals record Crossref
  7. 1971

    Ruelle and Takens: strange attractors and turbulence

    key development

    David Ruelle and Floris Takens, On the nature of turbulence, Comm. Math. Phys. 20 (1971) 167-192

    Ruelle and Takens propose strange attractors as a route to turbulence, bringing the chaotic dynamics of the hyperbolic programme into physics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Ruelle-Takens 1971 record Crossref
  8. 1982

    Palis and de Melo, Geometric Theory of Dynamical Systems

    consolidation

    Jacob Palis and Welington de Melo, Geometric Theory of Dynamical Systems, Springer (1982)

    A textbook treatment of structural stability and hyperbolic dynamics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Palis 1982 book record Open Library
