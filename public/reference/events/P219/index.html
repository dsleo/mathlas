1945 · Logic & foundations

Recursion theory and realizability

Recursive functions were used to interpret intuitionistic arithmetic.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1936

    Kleene's general recursive functions

    precursor

    Kleene, S. C., General recursive functions of natural numbers, Math. Ann. 112 (1936) 727-742, zbMATH 0014.19402

    Defines the general recursive functions that the realizability relation later interprets.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'General recursive functions of natural numbers', Math. Ann. 112 (1936) zbMATH API (Zbl 0014.19402)
  2. 1945

    Kleene's 1945 realizability interpretation

    first statement

    Kleene, S. C., On the interpretation of intuitionistic number theory, J. Symbolic Logic 10 (1945), doi 10.2307/2269016

    Introduces a realizability relation between numbers and formulas of intuitionistic arithmetic, giving the first public statement of the notion.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'On the interpretation of intuitionistic number theory', JSL, 1945 Crossref API
    • Wikipedia: Kleene's 1945-realizability relation for Heyting arithmetic Wikipedia: Realizability, Example
  3. 1959

    Kreisel's interpretation of analysis

    first application

    Kreisel, G., Interpretation of analysis by means of constructive functionals of finite types, in Constructivity in Mathematics (1959) 101-128, zbMATH 0134.01001

    Uses constructive functionals to interpret analysis, extending realizability beyond arithmetic.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Interpretation of analysis by means of constructive functionals of finite types', 1959 zbMATH API (Zbl 0134.01001)
    • Wikipedia: Kreisel's 1959 interpretation of analysis by constructive functionals Wikipedia: Realizability, References
  4. 1969

    Formalized realizability with recursive functionals

    key development

    Kleene, S. C., Formalized recursive functionals and formalized realizability, Mem. Amer. Math. Soc. 89 (1969), zbMATH 0184.02004

    A formal treatment of realizability with recursive functionals, which made it a precise tool for proof theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'Formalized recursive functionals and formalized realizability', Mem. AMS 89 (1969) zbMATH API (Zbl 0184.02004)
  5. 1973

    Kleene's retrospective survey

    consolidation

    Kleene, S. C., Realizability: a retrospective survey, Lecture Notes in Math. 337 (1973), zbMATH 0272.02036

    Surveys the variants of realizability, the systematic treatment cited on Wikipedia.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Realizability: A retrospective survey', LNM (1973) zbMATH API (Zbl 0272.02036)
    • Wikipedia cites Kleene 1973 as a retrospective survey Wikipedia: Realizability, References
