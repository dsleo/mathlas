1983 · Number theory

Fundamental lemma (Langlands programme)

Ngo proved the fundamental lemma by interpreting orbital integrals through the geometry of Hitchin fibration.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1979

    Stable conjugacy and transfer lemmas

    precursor

    Langlands, Stable conjugacy: definitions and lemmas, Canadian J. Math. 31 (1979)

    Langlands develops stable conjugacy and transfer, the setting in which the fundamental lemma is stated.

    Dating or attribution note

    Precursor, not the first statement: the fundamental lemma's own first formulation (possibly Langlands 1983 or later) was not verified.

    Evidence (2)
    • CJM 1979 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Fundamental lemma conjectured in developing the Langlands programme (no year) Wikipedia: Fundamental lemma (Langlands program)
  2. 1983

    Langlands introduces the fundamental lemma

    first statement

    R. P. Langlands, Les debuts d'une formule des traces stable, Publ. Math. Univ. Paris VII 13 (1983; zbMATH dates it 1982)

    Langlands's lecture notes state the fundamental lemma, a combinatorial identity between orbital integrals on a group and its endoscopic groups.

    Evidence (3)
    • Langlands says the fundamental lemma is introduced in these notes. Langlands' commentary, UBC Digital Math Archive
    • zbMATH lists the notes in the Univ. Paris VII publications with year 1982. zbMATH Open document 3844039
    • Wikipedia says Langlands conjectured it in 1983; the SL(2) case is Labesse-Langlands 1979. Wikipedia, Fundamental lemma (Langlands program)
  3. 1987

    Hitchin fibration and integrable systems

    precursor

    Hitchin, Stable bundles and integrable systems, Duke Math. J. 54 (1987)

    Hitchin's integrable systems on moduli of Higgs bundles give the Hitchin fibration used in Ngô's geometric proof.

    Dating or attribution note

    Link from Hitchin to Ngô's proof is from the input summary and literature, not checked in the Wikipedia text.

    Evidence (2)
    • Duke 1987 paper, author and year Crossref
    • Ngô's proof uses the Hitchin fibration (from the input summary) Input summary; not checked on a fetched page
  4. 1990

    Clozel titles a paper on the fundamental lemma for stable base change

    naming

    Clozel, L., The fundamental lemma for stable base change, 1990

    Earliest zbMATH title found with fundamental lemma in the Langlands sense.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: "The fundamental lemma for stable base change", Clozel, 1990 zbMATH Open doc 4208407
  5. 1991

    Waldspurger's reductions for linear groups

    key development

    Waldspurger, Sur les intégrales orbitales tordues pour les groupes linéaires: un lemme fondamental, Canadian J. Math. (1991)

    Waldspurger proves the fundamental lemma in important cases and reduces the general case, as Wikipedia notes.

    Dating or attribution note

    Scope (linear groups) taken from the paper title; not checked in text.

    Evidence (2)
    • Waldspurger's reductions to the Lie algebra case Wikipedia: Fundamental lemma (Langlands program)
    • CJM 1991 paper, author and year Crossref
  6. 2008

    Laumon-Ngô: fundamental lemma for unitary groups

    key development

    Laumon, Ngô, Le lemme fondamental pour les groupes unitaires, Annals of Mathematics 168 (2008)

    Laumon and Ngô prove the fundamental lemma for unitary groups, the first complete case.

    Evidence (2)
    • Fundamental lemma proved for unitary groups by Laumon and Ngô Wikipedia: Fundamental lemma (Langlands program)
    • Annals 2008 paper, authors and year Crossref
  7. 2010

    Ngô proves the fundamental lemma for Lie algebras

    key development

    Ngô, Le lemme fondamental pour les algèbres de Lie, Publ. Math. IHES 111 (2010)

    Ngô proves the fundamental lemma in general for Lie algebras via the Hitchin fibration, leading to the 2010 Fields Medal.

    Evidence (2)
    • Ngô's general proof and the 2010 Fields Medal Wikipedia: Fundamental lemma (Langlands program)
    • Publications IHES 2010 paper, author and year Crossref
