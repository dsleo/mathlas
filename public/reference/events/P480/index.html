Mobius inversion on posets and incidence algebras (Rota)

The Mobius function of the integers is generalised to partially ordered sets, giving a unified framework for inclusion-exclusion, counting and lattice theory.

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1832

    Mobius function for the integers

    precursor

    Mobius's 1832 work on the function now named for him (number-theoretic, not the poset version)

    Mobius introduced the function on the integers that later generalised to posets.

    Evidence (1)
    • Mobius function introduced by Mobius in 1832 Wikipedia: Mobius function (lead)
  2. 1935

    Weisner's abstract inversion

    first statement

    Weisner, 'Abstract theory of inversion of finite series', Transactions of the AMS (1935)

    Weisner gave an abstract inversion formula for finite series, an early general form of Mobius inversion on a lattice-like structure.

    Dating or attribution note

    Content and priority relative to Hall were not checked against the paper text; first_statement role is tentative.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper record: Trans. AMS 1935 Crossref
  3. 1936

    Hall's Eulerian functions of a group

    first application

    Hall, 'The Eulerian functions of a group', Quarterly Journal of Mathematics 7 (1936)

    Hall applied a Mobius-type inversion on the subgroup structure of a finite group to count generators, the first application in group theory.

    Dating or attribution note

    The identification of Hall's Eulerian functions with a Mobius function on the subgroup lattice is recalled from the literature, not checked in the text.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record: Q. J. Math. 1936 Crossref
    • Hall's theorem on the Mobius value of chains is credited in the literature on incidence algebras Wikipedia: Incidence algebra
  4. 1964

    Rota's Mobius functions on locally finite posets

    key development

    Rota, 'On the foundations of combinatorial theory I: Theory of Mobius functions', Zeitschrift fur Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie 2 (1964)

    Rota defined the Mobius function for any locally finite poset and tied it to inclusion-exclusion and counting, making the theory general.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record: Z. Wahrsch. 1964, pp. 340-368 Crossref
    • Rota's treatment of incidence algebras of locally finite posets begins in 1964 Wikipedia: Incidence algebra (literature)
  5. 1972

    Reduced incidence algebras and generating functions

    key development

    Doubilet, Rota and Stanley, 'On the foundations of combinatorial theory VI: the idea of generating function' (1972)

    Reduced incidence algebras were introduced to give generating functions a natural algebraic construction.

    Evidence (2)
    • Paper record: 1972 Crossref
    • Reduced incidence algebras introduced by Doubilet, Rota and Stanley Wikipedia: Incidence algebra
  6. 1986

    Stanley's Enumerative Combinatorics

    consolidation

    Stanley, Enumerative Combinatorics vol. 1 (1986)

    A systematic textbook treatment of incidence algebras and Mobius functions within enumerative combinatorics.

    Evidence (1)
    • Book record: Enumerative Combinatorics 1986 Crossref
