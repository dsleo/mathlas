1944 · Differential geometry

Chern: intrinsic Gauss-Bonnet and characteristic classes

Curvature forms gave topological invariants.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1848

    Bonnet's special case

    precursor

    Bonnet published a special case of the formula for surfaces, the first public version.

    Dating or attribution note

    Title of Bonnet's memoir not checked; not a bibliographic record.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: Bonnet published a special case in 1848 Wikipedia: Gauss-Bonnet theorem
  2. 1943

    Allendoerfer-Weil for Riemannian polyhedra

    precursor

    Allendoerfer, C. B., Weil, A., The Gauss-Bonnet theorem for Riemannian polyhedra, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 53 (1943), doi 10.1090/s0002-9947-1943-0007627-9

    Extends the formula to Riemannian polyhedra, a parallel development preceding Chern's intrinsic proof.

    Evidence (1)
    • Crossref record: 'The Gauss-Bonnet theorem for Riemannian polyhedra', TAMS, 1943 Crossref API
  3. 1944

    Chern's intrinsic proof of Gauss-Bonnet

    first statement

    Chern, S.-S., A simple intrinsic proof of the Gauss-Bonnet formula for closed Riemannian manifolds, Ann. of Math. 45 (1944), doi 10.2307/1969302

    Proves the formula intrinsically for closed Riemannian manifolds, with no embedding.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'A Simple Intrinsic Proof of the Gauss-Bonnet Formula', Annals of Math., 1944 Crossref API
    • MacTutor: Chern gave a now famous proof of the Gauss-Bonnet formula during his 1943-45 Princeton visit MacTutor Biography: Chern
  4. 1945

    Chern's curvatura integra in higher dimensions

    key development

    Chern, S.-S., On the curvatura integra in a Riemannian manifold, Ann. of Math. 46 (1945), doi 10.2307/1969203

    Extends the integral formula beyond surfaces to higher-dimensional Riemannian manifolds.

    Dating or attribution note

    The higher-dimensional content is taken from the Wikipedia reference, not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • Crossref record: 'On the Curvatura Integra in a Riemannian Manifold', Annals, 1945 Crossref API
    • Wikipedia: the Chern theorem (after Chern 1945) generalises Gauss-Bonnet Wikipedia: Gauss-Bonnet theorem
  5. 1946

    Chern's characteristic classes of Hermitian manifolds

    key development

    Chern, S.-S., Characteristic classes of Hermitian manifolds, Ann. of Math. (2) 47 (1946) 85-121, zbMATH 0060.41416

    Builds characteristic classes from curvature of Hermitian manifolds, the origin of the Chern classes.

    Dating or attribution note

    Link to the Chern classes is from the title and MacTutor, not read.

    Evidence (2)
    • zbMATH record: 'Characteristic classes of Hermitian manifolds', Ann. Math. 47 (1946) zbMATH API (Zbl 0060.41416)
    • MacTutor: Chern worked on characteristic classes in 1943-45 and is credited with Chern classes MacTutor Biography: Chern
  6. 1974

    Milnor and Stasheff's Characteristic Classes

    consolidation

    Milnor, J. W., Stasheff, J. D., Characteristic Classes, Annals of Math. Studies 76, Princeton (1974), zbMATH 0298.57008

    The standard textbook treatment of characteristic classes.

    Dating or attribution note

    Described as the first systematic treatment by convention; earlier textbooks were not checked.

    Evidence (1)
    • zbMATH record: 'Characteristic classes', Annals of Math. Studies 76 (1974) zbMATH API (Zbl 0298.57008)
  7. Date unknown

    Gauss's unpublished version

    precursor

    Gauss developed a version of the theorem but did not publish it.

    Evidence (1)
    • Wikipedia: Gauss developed a version but never published it Wikipedia: Gauss-Bonnet theorem
