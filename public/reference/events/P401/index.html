1910 · Algebra

Steinitz's abstract field theory

Steinitz gave the first abstract definition of field and proved every field has an algebraic closure.

Locate this event on the map ↗

Historical record

These milestones show distinct stages of the idea. Dates and priority claims can be uncertain; consult the evidence and notes for each stage.

  1. 1893

    Weber develops an abstract field framework

    precursor

    H. Weber, Die allgemeinen Grundlagen der Galois'schen Gleichungstheorie (1893)

    An earlier field abstraction precedes Steinitz's systematic theory.

    Evidence (1)
    • Weber's paper supplies a checked abstract-field/Galois framework in 1893. Springer original article record
  2. 1910

    Steinitz publishes Algebraische Theorie der Körper

    first statement

    E. Steinitz, Algebraische Theorie der Körper, J. reine angew. Math. 137 (1910)

    The paper gives a systematic abstract theory of fields.

    Evidence (1)
    • Steinitz, Algebraische Theorie der Koerper, J. reine angew. Math. 137 (1910) Crossref record
  3. 1910

    Steinitz proves field-structure results

    first application

    E. Steinitz, Algebraische Theorie der Körper, J. reine angew. Math. 137 (1910)

    The abstract framework yields algebraic-closure and extension results.

    Evidence (1)
    • Algebraic closure and structure results are proved in the 1910 paper itself Crossref record
  4. 1910

    Algebraic theory of fields named in title

    naming

    E. Steinitz, Algebraische Theorie der Körper, J. reine angew. Math. 137 (1910)

    The paper title provides a reproducible named anchor.

    Evidence (1)
    • Paper title 'Algebraische Theorie der Koerper' (1910) zbMATH Open (JFM) record
