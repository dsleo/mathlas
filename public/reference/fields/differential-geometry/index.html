Differential geometry

A curated selection of 17 recorded events in Differential geometry. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

≈ marks an approximate map placement where a dated first statement is not established.

Events in this field

  1. 1673
    Huygens: evolutes and the isochronous pendulum

    The cycloid's tautochrone property and evolute theory tied curve geometry to pendulum clocks.

  2. 1731
    Clairaut: curves in space

    Analytic geometry and calculus were used to study curves of double curvature.

  3. 1732
    Euler: geodesics from mechanics

    Euler derived the geodesic equation for motion on a surface.

  4. 1767
    Differential geometry of surfaces (Clairaut, Euler, Monge, Gauss)

    Clairaut, Euler and Monge studied curvature of curves and surfaces.

  5. 1868
    Riemannian geometry and manifolds

    Intrinsic geometry of surfaces is generalised to n-dimensional spaces with a metric given by a quadratic form, with the question of physical space in view.

  6. 1888
    Tensor calculus (absolute differential calculus of Ricci and Levi-Civita)

    Christoffel and Ricci developed coordinate-independent calculus of tensors, systematised by Ricci and Levi-Civita.

  7. 1899
    Cartan exterior differential calculus

    Cartan developed the exterior derivative and used it with Pfaffian systems and moving frames to unify differential geometry with Lie theory.

  8. 1906
    Lorentz group and Minkowski spacetime: group theory enters physics

    Poincare showed Lorentz transformations form a group, Einstein derived them from two postulates, and Minkowski gave a tensor form of electrodynamics and showed Newtonian gravity incompatible.

  9. 1913
    Abstract Riemann surface (Weyl's manifold definition)

    Weyl gave a rigorous definition of Riemann surface as a topological manifold with charts.

  10. 1916
    Affine connections (parallel transport, Weyl and Cartan)

    Levi-Civita introduced parallel transport, Weyl generalised to gauge geometry, and Cartan developed connections and moving frames.

  11. 1918
    Weyl's gauge theory (local scale invariance)

    Weyl proposed local scale invariance.

  12. 1944
    Chern: intrinsic Gauss-Bonnet and characteristic classes

    Curvature forms gave topological invariants.

  13. 1955
    Singularity theory (Whitney, Thom, Mather, Arnold)

    Stable singularities of smooth maps are classified by algebra and topology, then applied to caustics, wavefronts and bifurcations.

  14. 1985
    Symplectic topology

    The geometry of phase space is studied by rigidity phenomena, using complex-geometric techniques (holomorphic curves) and Morse-theoretic ones (Floer homology).

  15. 2006
    Seiberg–Witten theory and embedded contact homology prove Weinstein-type Reeb dynamics

    Embedded contact homology proved the Weinstein conjecture in dimension 3.

  16. 2020
    Synthetic Lorentzian curvature from optimal transport (non-smooth singularity theorems)

    Triangle comparison with time separation and optimal-transport characterisations of Ricci curvature were combined to define curvature bounds without smoothness and to reprove the Hawking and Penrose singularity theorems there.

  17. ≈ 2021
    Floer homology with Morava K-theory proves Arnold bounds in characteristic p

    Floer's orbit-counting method was combined with chromatic homotopy theory to count fixed points of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms using generalised cohomology, proving Arnold's conjecture in cases classical Floer theory could not reach.
