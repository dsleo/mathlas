Dynamics

A curated selection of 9 recorded events in Dynamics. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Events in this field

  1. 1880
    Dynamical systems from celestial mechanics (Poincare to Birkhoff)

    Poincare studied orbits geometrically rather than by formulae, uncovering homoclinic phenomena in the three-body problem, and Birkhoff built on this.

  2. 1892
    Lyapunov stability

    Lyapunov gave general criteria for stability of equilibria using auxiliary functions.

  3. 1931
    Ergodic theory from statistical mechanics

    Koopman's operator on function spaces let von Neumann prove the first rigorous ergodic theorem.

  4. 1952
    Dynamic programming: Bellman's principle of optimality

    Principle of optimality recast multi-stage decisions as recursion.

  5. 1954
    KAM theory: stability of Hamiltonian systems

    Kolmogorov, Arnold and Moser show that most quasi-periodic motions of perturbed integrable Hamiltonian systems survive, using an iterative scheme that beats small divisors.

  6. 1967
    Smale's hyperbolic dynamics (Axiom A, structural stability)

    Topological methods were applied to structural stability, followed by strange attractors.

  7. 1969
    Catastrophe theory

    Singularity classification was proposed as a model of discontinuous change in morphogenesis.

  8. 1972
    Thermodynamic formalism for hyperbolic dynamics (Sinai, Ruelle, Bowen)

    Gibbs states from statistical mechanics describe invariant measures of hyperbolic systems, with pressure replacing free energy.

  9. 1978
    Feigenbaum universality (period-doubling universality)

    Renormalisation explains universal constants in the period-doubling route.
