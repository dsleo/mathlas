Events in this field

  1. 1757
    Euler formulates the equations of fluid motion

    Euler expressed fluid motion through general analytic equations and examined velocity fields and boundary conditions, helping establish mathematical fluid mechanics.

  2. 1976
    Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg partial regularity for Navier-Stokes

    Hausdorff-measure covering arguments show that suitable weak solutions of the 3D Navier-Stokes equations have a singular set of one-dimensional parabolic measure zero.
