Computer science

A curated selection of 12 recorded events in Computer science. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Locate this field on the map ↗

Events in this field

  1. 1936
    Computer science separates from mathematics

    Computability, switching theory and programming grow into a discipline with its own departments, journals and questions (complexity, algorithms) while keeping heavy mathematical exchange.

  2. 1968
    Analysis of algorithms (Knuth)

    Generating functions and asymptotics are used to compute the running time of programs, creating a mathematical theory of algorithm performance.

  3. 1970
    Computer algebra and Groebner bases

    Groebner bases and CAD give algorithms for polynomial systems.

  4. 1975
    Computational geometry

    Shamos names the field and gives algorithms for geometric problems.

  5. 1976
    Public-key cryptography

    Modular exponentiation, discrete logarithms, factoring and later elliptic curves are used to build secure protocols, turning classical number theory into infrastructure.

  6. 1979
    Counting complexity (Valiant's #P)

    Counting solutions defines a complexity class, and computing the permanent is shown complete for it.

  7. 1985
    Quantum computation: Shor's algorithm

    A quantum Fourier transform finds periods and hence factors integers in polynomial time, tying quantum mechanics to complexity and cryptography.

  8. 1992
    PCP theorem: proofs, coding theory and hardness of approximation

    Low-degree polynomial codes let a verifier check proofs by reading a few bits, which implies that many optimisation problems cannot be approximated.

  9. 1994
    Semidefinite rounding: Goemans-Williamson approximation of MAX-CUT

    A semidefinite relaxation followed by random hyperplane rounding gives the best known ratio for MAX-CUT.

  10. 2000
    Topological data analysis

    Persistent homology gave a computable, stable topological summary of point clouds, with a structure theorem from commutative algebra.

  11. 2018
    Neural tangent kernel

    Infinite-width networks were analysed as kernel machines and mean fields.

  12. 2021
    Machine learning guides conjectures in knot theory and representation theory

    Supervised learning and saliency analysis pointed human mathematicians to a relation between signature and geometric invariants of knots and to a candidate recipe for Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials.
