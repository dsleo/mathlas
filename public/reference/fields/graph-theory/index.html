Graph theory

A curated selection of 7 recorded events in Graph theory. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Locate this field on the map ↗

Events in this field

  1. 1741
    Graph theory from the Königsberg bridges

    A puzzle about walks across bridges is stripped to vertices and edges, and a parity condition settles it, an early instance of 'geometry of position'.

  2. 1845
    Kirchhoff's circuit laws and the graph-theoretic treatment of networks

    Kirchhoff's circuit laws.

  3. 1879
    Map colouring and the four colour problem (Kempe and Heawood)

    A map-colouring question became a vertex-colouring problem on planar graphs, with Kempe's chains and Heawood's bound via Euler characteristic bringing in topology.

  4. 1959
    Random graph theory

    Graphs chosen at random were studied for thresholds of properties.

  5. 1973
    Explicit expander graphs from property (T) (Margulis)

    Kazhdan's property (T) for lattices in Lie groups gives the first explicit infinite families of expanding graphs.

  6. 2013
    Ramanujan graphs / interlacing

    Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava proved existence of bipartite Ramanujan graphs of all degrees via interlacing polynomials, also solving Kadison-Singer.

  7. 2020
    Set-theoretic measurable coloring meets distributed local algorithms

    Efficient local algorithms for colouring networks were shown to translate into measurable colourings of infinite graphs, so thresholds in descriptive set theory and in distributed computing classify the same problems.
