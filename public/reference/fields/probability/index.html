Probability

A curated selection of 24 recorded events in Probability. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Locate this field on the map ↗

Events in this field

  1. 1657
    Probability from games of chance

    The problem of points and dice questions are solved by counting equally likely cases, then generalised into a calculus of chance and, with Laplace, an analytic one.

  2. 1663
    Cardano: first treatise on chance (Liber de ludo aleae)

    Dice outcomes were analysed by counting favourable cases; the book appeared posthumously.

  3. 1665
    Pascal's arithmetical triangle

    The triangle and induction were used to compute fair divisions.

  4. 1707
    De Moivre: probability and the trigonometry-complex number link

    De Moivre extended probability to approximation by analysis and linked powers of complex numbers to trigonometry.

  5. 1713
    Bernoulli's Ars Conjectandi and the law of large numbers

    Bernoulli proved the first law of large numbers.

  6. 1733
    De Moivre: normal approximation to the binomial

    Stirling-type asymptotics were applied to binomial sums, yielding the normal curve.

  7. 1782
    Laplace's analytic probability

    Generating functions and asymptotic approximations were used to solve probability problems.

  8. 1900
    Brownian motion: from physics and finance to measure on path space

    A random motion modelled by physicists and by Bachelier is given a rigorous measure on continuous paths, which then solves boundary value problems for harmonic functions.

  9. 1906
    Markov chains

    Markov studied dependent trials and showed the law of large numbers extends to chains, later applying them to letter sequences.

  10. 1931
    Kolmogorov's diffusion theory and parabolic PDE

    Continuous-time Markov processes were linked to parabolic equations, founding diffusion theory.

  11. 1933
    Measure theory meets probability (Kolmogorov)

    Probability is axiomatised as a normalised measure on a sigma-algebra, so limit theorems become statements about integration.

  12. 1944
    Ito calculus and stochastic differential equations

    A differential and integral calculus for Brownian paths is built and used to solve stochastic differential equations.

  13. 1960
    Furstenberg: random matrix products and boundaries of Lie groups

    Random products of matrices have Lyapunov exponents controlled by a boundary on which the group acts, joining probability, Lie groups and ergodic theory.

  14. 1966
    Large deviation theory (Donsker-Varadhan framework)

    Variational principles for rare events of Markov processes, modelled on free energy computations, become a general theory of large deviations.

  15. 1979
    Spin glasses: the Parisi formula for the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model

    A replica-symmetry-breaking prediction for the free energy of the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model is proved by interpolation arguments and ultrametricity.

  16. 1985
    Probabilistic construction of Liouville conformal field theory

    Polyakov's path integral for Liouville theory was made rigorous via the Gaussian free field and Kahane's multiplicative chaos, and its correlation functions were shown to agree with the physicists' bootstrap and the DOZZ formula.

  17. 1992
    Tracy-Widom distributions and universality in random matrices

    The Airy kernel gives the edge fluctuation law of random matrices, later shown to be universal across ensembles.

  18. 1994
    Rough paths

    Lyons gave a pathwise integration theory beyond bounded variation using iterated integrals.

  19. 1999
    Schramm-Loewner evolution

    Scaling limits of planar lattice models are described by random curves generated by Loewner's equation driven by Brownian motion, giving rigorous access to conformal field theory predictions.

  20. 2004
    Random planar maps and the Brownian map

    Bijections for planar maps allow scaling limits to a random metric sphere, linking combinatorics, probability and two-dimensional quantum gravity.

  21. 2013
    Regularity structures

    Ill-posed stochastic PDEs are solved by combining rough-path ideas with a Hopf-algebraic account of renormalisation taken from quantum field theory.

  22. 2014
    Liouville quantum gravity and mating of trees

    Duplantier-Sheffield-Miller-Gwynne built random surfaces from Gaussian free field and linked them to SLE.

  23. 2015
    Liouville quantum gravity, the Brownian map and SLE

    Two models of random two-dimensional surfaces, one measuring area and one distance, were shown to describe the same object using SLE curves as the link.

  24. 2020
    Universality of rotational invariance at 2D criticality via coupling and integrability

    Rotational invariance at the critical point was proved across a broad class of planar models by coupling lattices with their rotations and using integrability inputs.
