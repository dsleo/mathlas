Algebraic topology

A curated selection of 16 recorded events in Algebraic topology. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

≈ marks an approximate map placement where a dated first statement is not established.

Events in this field

  1. 1895
    Algebraic topology: Betti numbers to homology groups

    Numerical invariants (Betti numbers) of manifolds from the study of integrals and differential equations are replaced by groups, so algebra becomes the language of topology.

  2. 1945
    Axiomatic homology

    Homology theories were characterised by axioms such as excision, allowing comparison and later generalised theories.

  3. 1948
    Lie algebra cohomology

    Cohomology of compact Lie groups was computed algebraically via Lie algebras.

  4. 1951
    Serre's spectral sequence and homotopy groups

    Leray's spectral sequence applied to fibrations gave homotopy results for spheres.

  5. 1954
    Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch theorem

    Characteristic classes expressed Euler characteristic of bundles.

  6. 1959
    Topological K-theory

    Vector bundle classes formed a generalised cohomology theory, used on Hopf invariant and index problems.

  7. 1970
    Bloch-Kato / norm residue

    Motivic Steenrod operations proved the norm residue isomorphism in all primes.

  8. 1973
    Higher algebraic K-theory

    Homotopy-theoretic constructions (plus construction, classifying spaces) define K-groups of rings in all degrees, importing topology into algebra and number theory.

  9. 1977
    Intersection cohomology

    Goresky and MacPherson extend Poincare duality to singular spaces.

  10. 1984
    Chromatic homotopy theory and the nilpotence theorem

    Formal groups organise stable homotopy into chromatic layers, and the nilpotence theorem proves the Ravenel conjectures.

  11. 1987
    Elliptic cohomology and topological modular forms

    Elliptic curves and modular forms are used to build cohomology theories that refine genera and are tied to string theory; Hopkins reported on them at the 2002 congress.

  12. 1987
    Floer homology

    Infinite-dimensional Morse theory proved Arnold's conjecture.

  13. 1988
    Topological and conformal field theory as axioms (Atiyah, Segal, Witten)

    Quantum field theory is recast as a functor from cobordisms to vector spaces, linking invariants of manifolds with physical models.

  14. ≈ 1993
    Floer theory and Fukaya categories (Lagrangian Floer)

    Lagrangian intersection Floer theory was organised into an A-infinity category; Fukaya-Oh-Ohta-Ono gave a rigorous foundation.

  15. 1995
    Cobordism hypothesis

    Fully extended TQFTs were classified by fully dualizable objects of a symmetric monoidal infinity-category.

  16. ≈ 1999
    A^1-homotopy theory and motivic cohomology

    Homotopy theory is rebuilt over schemes with the affine line as the interval, giving motivic cohomology and the proof of the Milnor conjecture.
