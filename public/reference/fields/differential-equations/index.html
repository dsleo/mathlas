Differential equations

A curated selection of 20 recorded events in Differential equations. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Locate this field on the map ↗

Events in this field

  1. 1690
    Differential equations from calculus and mechanics

    Newton solved fluxional equations by series; Leibniz separated variables; Bernoullis solved named equations.

  2. 1749
    D'Alembert and Euler: the wave equation and partial differential equations

    The vibrating string led to the wave equation and series solutions.

  3. 1750
    Vibrating-string controversy and the general function concept

    The dispute between d'Alembert and Euler over admissible initial shapes pushed analysis from formulas toward arbitrary dependence, later made explicit in Euler's definition and Fourier's heat work.

  4. 1785
    Potential theory in celestial mechanics and physics

    The potential function and its equation were introduced for attracting masses, then used in electrostatics.

  5. 1866
    Fuchs-Frobenius theory of regular singular points (Frobenius method)

    Fuchs and Frobenius used series in the complex domain to solve linear ODEs with regular singular points.

  6. 1900
    Riemann-Hilbert problem and monodromy

    Riemann asked for a differential equation with prescribed monodromy group, and Plemelj attacked it with singular integral equations.

  7. 1946
    Landau damping: plasma physics to nonlinear PDE

    A prediction of plasma physics from 1946 is proved for the nonlinear Vlasov equation in Gevrey regularity, using analytic norms and a new treatment of echoes.

  8. 1949
    Feynman-Kac formula

    Path integrals matched with expectations over Brownian paths.

  9. 1952
    Calderon-Zygmund singular integrals in PDE

    Singular integral operators from harmonic analysis give L^p estimates and uniqueness results for elliptic and parabolic equations.

  10. 1955
    Hörmander linear partial differential operators

    Existence and regularity for general PDE are proved by Fourier and distribution methods.

  11. 1959
    Lojasiewicz-Simon: real-analytic gradient inequalities control geometric flows

    A gradient inequality from real-analytic geometry gives convergence to a unique limit for gradient-like evolution equations such as harmonic map flow and mean curvature flow.

  12. 1965
    Fermi-Pasta-Ulam problem and solitons

    Numerical experiments lead to the soliton concept.

  13. 1967
    Inverse scattering transform and Lax pairs for soliton equations

    The KdV equation from water waves and plasma physics is solved exactly through scattering data of a Schroedinger operator, and Lax recasts it as an isospectral flow.

  14. 1967
    Yang-Baxter and solvable lattice models

    Yang-Baxter equation.

  15. 1973
    Heat equation proof of index theorem

    Heat kernels give local proofs of the index formula.

  16. 1974
    Finite-gap integration of soliton equations

    Theta functions of algebraic curves give explicit periodic solutions of KdV and related soliton equations.

  17. 1981
    Viscosity solutions: control theory meets PDE

    Dynamic-programming value functions, merely continuous, are given a rigorous notion of solution for fully nonlinear first and second order PDE.

  18. 1993
    Fourier restriction method for nonlinear dispersive PDE (Bourgain)

    Restriction estimates from harmonic analysis, with lattice-point counts, give well-posedness of nonlinear Schroedinger and KdV equations in rough spaces.

  19. 1999
    Random matrix universality and Dyson / Tracy-Widom in KPZ

    Longest increasing subsequence fluctuations were shown to follow Tracy-Widom, linking random permutations to random matrices.

  20. 2012
    Paracontrolled distributions

    Paraproduct calculus gave an alternative to regularity structures for singular SPDEs.
