Combinatorics

A curated selection of 36 recorded events in Combinatorics. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

≈ marks an approximate map placement where a dated first statement is not established.

Events in this field

  1. 300 BCE
    Jaina permutations and combinations (early Indian combinatorics)

    Jaina texts treated counting of permutations and combinations alongside multi-level infinities.

  2. 300 BCE
    Pingala: combinatorics of poetic metres

    Counting patterns of long and short syllables yielded binomial-type identities and a binary-like device; Halayudha later described the triangle.

  3. 1050
    Binomial coefficient triangle in China

    A table of binomial coefficients up to the eighth power appeared as the 'ancient method' of expanding powers.

  4. 1847
    Kirkman's schoolgirl problem and combinatorial designs

    Kirkman's triple-arrangement puzzle gave an early case of combinatorial designs.

  5. 1900
    Algebraic combinatorics: Young tableaux and symmetric functions

    Symmetric functions and tableaux are linked to representations (Schensted, Stanley).

  6. 1935
    Matroid theory and combinatorial geometry

    Whitney's abstraction of dependence unites linear algebra and graphs.

  7. 1935
    Mobius inversion on posets and incidence algebras (Rota)

    The Mobius function of the integers is generalised to partially ordered sets, giving a unified framework for inclusion-exclusion, counting and lattice theory.

  8. 1936
    Erdős discrepancy problem

    A combinatorial discrepancy question is reduced to correlations of multiplicative functions, then settled with entropy-decrement and short-interval results from analytic number theory.

  9. 1947
    The probabilistic method in combinatorics

    Existence of a combinatorial object is proved by showing a random object has the property with positive probability.

  10. 1953
    Roth's theorem: Fourier-analytic method for 3-term progressions

    A circle-method style Fourier argument with a density-increment iteration shows dense integer sets contain three-term progressions.

  11. 1956
    Shannon capacity of graphs and the Lovász theta function

    A coding-theory quantity for graphs is bounded by a semidefinite programme, giving the theta function and its exact polynomial-time computation for perfect graphs.

  12. 1963
    Hales–Jewett theorem and structured Ramsey partitions

    Ramsey's partition theorem is generalised to structured colourings.

  13. 1964
    Rota combinatorial theory

    Moebius functions of posets unified inversion.

  14. 1976
    Computers enter proof: four colour theorem and the Kepler conjecture

    Exhaustive case analysis by computer is accepted as proof in graph theory, and later whole proofs are re-verified in proof assistants.

  15. 1976
    Ellipsoid method in combinatorial optimisation

    Khachiyan's ellipsoid algorithm is used to prove polynomial solvability of combinatorial problems via separation oracles.

  16. 1977
    Furstenberg: ergodic theory proves Szemeredi's theorem

    Density statements about integers are translated into recurrence statements for measure-preserving systems and proved there.

  17. 1978
    Topology proves Kneser's conjecture

    The chromatic number of Kneser graphs is bounded below via the Borsuk-Ulam theorem applied to a topological space built from the graph.

  18. 1988
    Hardness versus randomness

    Explicit combinatorial designs turn a function hard for small circuits into a generator fooling small circuits, linking circuit lower bounds and derandomisation.

  19. 1990
    Analytic combinatorics: generating functions as complex-analytic objects

    Singularities of generating functions determine asymptotics of combinatorial classes, in a systematic symbolic-to-analytic method.

  20. 1990
    Wilf-Zeilberger method: rational-function certificates for hypergeometric identities

    Rational-function certificates let a computer prove hypergeometric summation identities automatically.

  21. ≈ 2002
    Tropical geometry

    Algebraic varieties were replaced by piecewise-linear shadows, allowing enumerative problems to be solved by counting combinatorial objects.

  22. ≈ 2004
    Additive combinatorics as a field

    Structure-versus-randomness for sets with small sumset consolidated into a discipline with a dedicated textbook.

  23. 2004
    Graph limits and graphons

    Dense graph sequences were given analytic limit objects, unifying extremal combinatorics and statistical physics.

  24. 2006
    Positive Grassmannian linking combinatorics, exclusion processes and KP solitons

    Total positivity extended to the Grassmannian produced cell-counting polynomials that also give probabilities in exclusion processes and describe soliton solutions of the KP equation.

  25. 2007
    Flag algebras

    Razborov turned Turan-type problems into semidefinite programs using limits of graph densities.

  26. ≈ 2008
    The polynomial method in combinatorics

    Low-degree polynomials vanishing on a point set give short proofs of the finite-field Kakeya problem and the Erdos distinct-distances problem in the plane.

  27. 2009
    Approximate groups and the model theory of pseudofinite structures

    A Lie-model theorem built with nonstandard methods describes the structure of finite approximate subgroups.

  28. 2010
    Algorithmic discrepancy theory (Bansal) and the Komlos conjecture

    Designing efficient vector-balancing algorithms, rather than existence arguments, turned out to reproduce and then improve the best known discrepancy bounds, including a new bound towards Komlos.

  29. 2011
    Arc spaces of singularities and partition identities

    Arc spaces of simple singularities were found to be described by partition identities, one of several fields where the Rogers-Ramanujan identities reappear and are re-proved with local tools.

  30. 2015
    Erdos discrepancy problem solved by multiplicative-function methods

    Tao used Polymath5's reduction and Fourier/entropy methods for multiplicative functions to settle the Erdos discrepancy problem; SAT solvers had done the c=2 case earlier.

  31. 2015
    Hodge theory proves log-concavity conjectures for matroids

    Heron-Rota-Welsh log-concavity of chromatic polynomials and Rota's conjecture are proved using Hodge-Riemann relations on Chow rings of matroid-like objects.

  32. 2016
    Cap set

    Croot-Lev-Pach and Ellenberg-Gijswijt used a low-degree polynomial argument to bound cap sets.

  33. 2019
    Lorentzian polynomials and log-concavity

    A class of polynomials unifies log-concavity statements across matroids, Hodge theory and discrete convex analysis, giving the strongest Mason conjecture.

  34. 2023
    Entropy proves the polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa conjecture

    Entropy of random variables, rather than sets, is used with a doubling-style argument to settle the polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa conjecture in characteristic 2.

  35. 2024
    Integer distance sets via the determinant method

    Lifting planar integer-distance sets into higher dimensions and applying the determinant method showed that all but a sparse subset of a large set lies on a line or circle.

  36. 2025
    Ramsey lower bounds from random sphere geometry

    Colouring edges of a complete graph by distances between random points on a high-dimensional sphere gave the first improvement in about fifty years on Erdos's 1947 bound for near-diagonal Ramsey numbers.
