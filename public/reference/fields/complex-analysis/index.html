Complex analysis

A curated selection of 10 recorded events in Complex analysis. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Locate this field on the map ↗

Events in this field

  1. 1714
    Cotes: logarithm of complex arguments

    Cotes showed log(cos+i sin)=i theta.

  2. 1748
    Euler: complex logarithms and exponentials

    Trigonometric and exponential functions were linked through complex arguments and logarithms of negatives.

  3. 1825
    Cauchy and the beginnings of complex analysis

    Integrals along paths in the complex plane gave theorems and the calculus of residues.

  4. 1827
    Abel: elliptic functions as inverses of elliptic integrals

    Inverting elliptic integrals gave doubly periodic functions.

  5. 1851
    Dirichlet's principle

    Riemann used a variational minimum principle from potential theory to guarantee existence of functions on surfaces; Weierstrass's criticism led to Hilbert's repaired form.

  6. 1851
    Riemann surfaces

    Riemann treated multi-valued algebraic functions as single-valued functions on a covering surface, bringing topology into complex analysis.

  7. 1906
    Several complex variables and Hartogs

    Hartogs discovered phenomena, such as automatic extension of holomorphic functions, with no one-variable analogue.

  8. 1917
    Complex dynamics: iteration of rational functions (Julia and Fatou)

    Julia and Fatou independently studied iterated rational maps of the plane, introducing attractors, repellers and the sets now named after them.

  9. 1965
    Several complex variables via PDE (L2 methods for the d-bar equation)

    L2 estimates for the d-bar equation solved problems about pseudoconvex domains.

  10. 1971
    Real-variable methods in complex analysis (Hardy spaces)

    Real-variable characterisations of Hardy spaces and the duality of H^1 and BMO replace complex-variable methods in harmonic analysis.
