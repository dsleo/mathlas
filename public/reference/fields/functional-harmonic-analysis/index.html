Functional & harmonic analysis

A curated selection of 18 recorded events in Functional & harmonic analysis. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Locate this field on the map ↗

Events in this field

  1. 1808
    Fourier series: from heat flow to analysis

    The heat equation leads to arbitrary functions represented as trigonometric series, forcing a redefinition of function and integral.

  2. 1887
    Functional analysis

    Integral equations and the calculus of variations are treated as linear algebra in infinite-dimensional normed spaces with topological structure.

  3. 1887
    Volterra functionals

    Volterra introduced functionals as functions of functions, and Hadamard named the field in 1910.

  4. 1907
    Hilbert space and integral equations

    Hilbert and Riesz made sequence and function spaces into an infinite-dimensional geometry with spectral theory.

  5. 1922
    Banach spaces (complete normed linear spaces)

    Banach defined complete normed spaces and proved the key theorems on operators, forming the Lwow school.

  6. 1927
    Representations of compact and continuous groups (Schur, Weyl, Peter-Weyl)

    Schur and Weyl used invariant integration to extend finite-group representation theory to compact Lie groups.

  7. 1928
    Hilbert-space quantum mechanics

    Matrix and wave mechanics were unified in the Hilbert-space framework by Weyl, Dirac and von Neumann.

  8. 1933
    Haar measure and abstract harmonic analysis (Weil)

    Haar measure and Pontryagin duality were systematised in a general setting.

  9. 1934
    Leray-Schauder degree and topological methods for nonlinear PDE

    Brouwer's degree is extended to compact perturbations of the identity in Banach spaces, giving existence results for nonlinear partial differential equations.

  10. 1938
    Sobolev spaces and generalised solutions

    Weak derivatives and function spaces were introduced to treat generalised solutions of wave and elliptic equations.

  11. 1941
    Gelfand theory of Banach algebras

    Maximal ideals of normed rings gave a topological representation.

  12. 1945
    Distributions

    Generalised functions were defined as continuous linear functionals on test functions, unifying Dirac delta, weak derivatives and Fourier transforms.

  13. 1965
    Pseudodifferential operators

    Symbol calculus extended Calderon-Zygmund theory to PDE, then to propagation of singularities.

  14. 1971
    Fourier integral operators and microlocal analysis

    Fourier transforms and symplectic geometry of the cotangent bundle are combined to study singularities of solutions of linear PDE.

  15. 1984
    Wavelets

    Multiresolution ideas from signal processing and quantum field theory are merged with harmonic analysis to give orthonormal bases of compactly supported functions.

  16. 2015
    Fractal uncertainty principle and quantum chaos

    A Fourier uncertainty principle for fractal-like sets was proved in one dimension and used to show waves cannot be trapped on a hyperbolic surface, then extended to higher dimensions.

  17. 2023
    Random hyperbolic surfaces have maximal spectral gap: random-graph technique transfer

    Friedman's method for handling problematic paths in random graphs was adapted to hyperbolic surfaces and Mirzakhani-style geodesic counts to prove the optimal spectral gap of 1/4 for random surfaces.

  18. 2025
    Chowla cosine problem via Cayley graph eigenvalues and MaxCut

    Showing that clique-free Cayley graphs must have very negative eigenvalues gave the first power-of-N lower bounds for the minimum of a sum of cosines, a problem posed in the 1950s-60s.
