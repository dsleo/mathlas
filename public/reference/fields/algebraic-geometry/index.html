Algebraic geometry

A curated selection of 37 recorded events in Algebraic geometry. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

≈ marks an approximate map placement where a dated first statement is not established.

Events in this field

  1. 1070
    Khayyam cubics via conics

    Cubics were classified and solved geometrically through intersections of conics, with a claim that ruler and compass are insufficient.

  2. 1827
    Homogeneous coordinates (Moebius, Pluecker)

    Algebraic coordinates made duality and line geometry computational.

  3. 1829
    Abel's theorem and abelian integrals

    A theorem on sums of integrals of algebraic functions introduced genus, lost until 1952.

  4. 1893
    Hilbert's Nullstellensatz and algebraic geometry as ideal theory

    Hilbert linked ideals of polynomials to geometric zero sets, starting the algebraisation of algebraic geometry.

  5. 1931
    Tarski's quantifier elimination for real algebra (decision method)

    Quantifier elimination showed the first-order theory of the reals is decidable.

  6. 1935
    Hodge theory of harmonic integrals

    Harmonic forms represent cohomology on Kaehler manifolds.

  7. 1940
    Rigorous foundations of algebraic geometry via commutative algebra

    Van der Waerden, Zariski and Weil used valuation and ideal theory to rebuild the Italian school's results rigorously.

  8. 1946
    Sheaf theory and sheaf cohomology

    Local-to-global problems from topology and several complex variables are packaged as sheaves with cohomology, then moved into algebraic geometry.

  9. 1955
    Coherent sheaves (FAC) in algebraic geometry

    Sheaf cohomology was imported into algebraic varieties over Zariski topology.

  10. 1955
    Serre GAGA

    Analytic and algebraic coherent sheaf cohomology on projective varieties were shown to agree.

  11. 1960
    Etale cohomology

    Grothendieck topologies gave cohomology theories for varieties over finite fields as required by Weil.

  12. 1960
    Scheme theory

    Algebraic geometry is rebuilt on arbitrary commutative rings so that geometry over the integers and over finite fields is treated uniformly with complex varieties.

  13. 1965
    Geometric invariant theory and moduli

    Nineteenth-century invariant theory is recast as a quotient construction for group actions on varieties, giving moduli spaces of curves and bundles.

  14. 1968
    Variation of Hodge structure and period domains (Griffiths)

    Periods of integrals on algebraic manifolds vary holomorphically with transversality, giving an analytic tool for moduli and cycle questions.

  15. 1971
    Mixed Hodge theory (Deligne)

    Cohomology of singular or open varieties carries a weight filtration, extending Hodge theory and mirroring weights in the Weil conjectures.

  16. 1974
    Deligne proof of Weil conjectures

    Etale cohomology plus Lefschetz pencils and Rankin methods proved the Riemann hypothesis for varieties over finite fields.

  17. 1982
    Teichmuller dynamics: homogeneous dynamics on moduli spaces

    Ratner-style measure rigidity is adapted to the SL(2,R) action on moduli of abelian differentials, proving the magic wand theorem.

  18. 1987
    Geometric Langlands and its physics

    The arithmetic correspondence is reformulated for curves over the complex numbers with sheaves in place of functions, and electric-magnetic duality in gauge theory is identified as its physical origin.

  19. 1990
    Mirror symmetry

    A duality between Calabi-Yau manifolds from string theory predicts numbers of rational curves, later proved mathematically and recast as an equivalence of categories.

  20. 1994
    Gromov-Witten / quantum cohomology

    Physical topological sigma models gave counts of curves; Ruan-Tian and Kontsevich-Manin made them rigorous.

  21. 1994
    Noncommutative projective geometry (Artin-Schelter-Zhang)

    Graded noncommutative rings are studied through the quasi-coherent sheaves on a 'projective scheme' built from their modules.

  22. 1996
    SYZ conjecture

    Mirror pairs were conjectured to be dual special Lagrangian torus fibrations, giving a geometric picture of mirror symmetry.

  23. 1998
    Donaldson-Thomas theory and wall-crossing

    Counting sheaves on Calabi-Yau threefolds gives invariants whose jumps across walls follow universal formulas.

  24. ≈ 1998
    Motivic integration

    Measures on arc spaces are valued in a Grothendieck ring of varieties, generalising p-adic integration with model-theoretic tools.

  25. 2002
    Derived algebraic geometry

    Toen-Vezzosi and Lurie combined algebraic geometry with homotopical algebra to build derived schemes.

  26. 2002
    Higher topos theory

    Quasicategory-based infinity-topoi and derived geometry reframed homotopy theory and moduli problems.

  27. 2005
    Gauge-theory heuristics for rational points (non-abelian Chabauty)

    Kim's programme uses spaces of path-collections on fundamental groups of curves, modelled on gauge-theory fields, in the hope of a least-action principle for finding rational solutions of Diophantine equations.

  28. ≈ 2006
    Mirzakhani moduli volumes

    Mirzakhani computed Weil-Petersson volumes, recovered Witten's conjecture, and established Teichmuller-dynamics rigidity (with Eskin).

  29. 2007
    Topological recursion and enumerative geometry

    Eynard-Orantin recursion recovers Gromov-Witten and Weil-Petersson volumes.

  30. 2007
    Tropical geometry transferred to Brill-Noether theory

    Degenerating curves to graphs and playing chip-firing games gave new proofs and extensions of classical statements about how many equations are needed to embed a curve.

  31. 2008
    Kontsevich-Soibelman wall crossing

    Wall-crossing formulas for DT invariants unify stability conditions and cluster transformations.

  32. ≈ 2012
    K-stability and Kahler-Einstein metrics on Fano manifolds

    An algebraic-geometric stability condition is shown to be equivalent to existence of Kahler-Einstein metrics.

  33. 2013
    Amplituhedron: scattering amplitudes as volumes in positive geometry

    Recursion relations for gluon amplitudes were shown to compute volumes of pieces of the positive Grassmannian, and amplitudes were then proposed to be the volume of a new geometric object.

  34. 2019
    Prismatic cohomology

    A single cohomology theory was constructed that specializes to etale, de Rham and crystalline p-adic theories.

  35. ≈ 2022
    Euler characteristic of Out(F_n) from Feynman-diagram counting

    Counting graphs weighted by symmetry was treated as a toy quantum field theory computation, showing the Euler characteristic of moduli spaces of graphs becomes hugely negative and hence that many cohomology classes exist.

  36. 2022
    Murmurations: machine learning and statistics reveal patterns in elliptic curves

    Classifiers trained on elliptic-curve data and averages of Frobenius traces exposed oscillating patterns that were then explained for Dirichlet characters and modular forms.

  37. 2025
    Non-rationality of cubic fourfolds attacked via mirror-symmetry-inspired Hodge atoms

    Curve counts on a fourfold, an idea inspired by mirror symmetry, split its Hodge structure into atoms and show that degree-3 five-variable equations cannot be parametrised.
