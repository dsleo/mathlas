Optimization & control

A curated selection of 8 recorded events in Optimization & control. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Events in this field

  1. 1696
    Calculus of variations from the brachistochrone

    The brachistochrone challenge and the Bernoullis' isoperimetric problems led Euler to a general variational method and Lagrange to an analytic version with the delta symbol.

  2. 1900
    Calculus of variations to Hilbert's direct methods

    Hilbert justified Dirichlet's principle with direct variational methods, rescuing Riemann's use of minimisation in complex analysis.

  3. 1939
    Kantorovich linear programming

    Production-planning problems were posed as linear optimisation with multipliers, with solution methods.

  4. 1939
    Linear programming and the simplex method

    An algorithm moving along vertices of a polyhedron solved linear programs efficiently.

  5. 1956
    Network flows and combinatorial optimisation

    Max-flow min-cut ties graph theory to LP duality (Ford-Fulkerson, Edmonds).

  6. 1956
    Pontryagin maximum principle

    Necessary conditions for optimal control extended variational calculus.

  7. 1960
    Control theory becomes a mathematical discipline (Kalman state space; ICM 1966 section)

    State-space methods, optimal control and filtering turn engineering control into a mathematical subject, and the 1966 Moscow congress lists a section on control theory.

  8. 1960
    Kalman filter (recursive state-space estimation)

    State-space recursive estimation unified filtering and control.
