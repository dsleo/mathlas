Logic & foundations

A curated selection of 29 recorded events in Logic & foundations. The dates describe these selected records, not the origin or size of the field.

Events in this field

  1. 870
    Islamic work on the parallel postulate

    Ibn al-Haytham, Khayyam and Tusi studied quadrilaterals as tests of the parallel postulate.

  2. 1666
    Leibniz combinatorial logic

    Leibniz proposed a calculus of reasoning by symbolic manipulation, largely unpublished.

  3. 1847
    Boolean algebra: logic as algebra, then topology (Boole to Stone)

    Propositional logic is treated as an algebra, and Stone's theorem gives a topological dictionary for it, anticipating duality methods in logic and model theory.

  4. 1869
    Arithmetization of analysis and the construction of the real numbers

    Weierstrass, Cantor and Dedekind defined real numbers from rationals via limits or cuts, removing reliance on geometric intuition.

  5. 1874
    Cantor's set theory

    Cantor's study of exceptional sets for trigonometric series led to countability, uncountability of the reals, and then transfinite ordinals and cardinals.

  6. 1884
    Logicism: reducing arithmetic to logic

    Frege tried to base arithmetic on logic alone; Russell's paradox shook this and Russell and Whitehead's Principia used a theory of types to avoid it.

  7. 1888
    Peano arithmetic axioms

    Dedekind and Peano gave axioms characterizing the natural numbers, Peano in formal logical notation.

  8. 1899
    Axiomatisation of geometry

    Hilbert gave a complete independent axiom system for Euclidean geometry that did not rely on diagrams, and used models to test independence.

  9. 1903
    Russell paradox, type theory and Principia Mathematica

    Russell's paradox led to the ramified theory of types and the formal derivation of mathematics.

  10. 1907
    Intuitionism

    Brouwer rejected the unrestricted law of excluded middle.

  11. 1908
    Axiomatic set theory (Zermelo)

    Zermelo proved well-ordering from choice and axiomatized set theory.

  12. 1915
    Model theory beginnings (Löwenheim-Skolem)

    Loewenheim and Skolem showed that first-order theories with infinite models have countable ones, opening semantic study of formal theories.

  13. 1917
    Descriptive set theory

    Baire, Lebesgue, Suslin and Lusin explored regularity of definable sets, leading to analytic and projective sets.

  14. 1920
    Many-valued logic

    Lukasiewicz and Post independently defined logics with more than two truth values.

  15. 1922
    Hilbert's programme

    Hilbert proposed to prove consistency of mathematics by finitary methods.

  16. 1930
    Ramsey theory

    Ramsey proved a combinatorial theorem on homogeneous subsets while studying a decision problem in logic, and van der Waerden proved a related Ramsey-type theorem on arithmetic progressions.

  17. 1931
    Gödel's incompleteness theorems

    Coding of syntax by numbers was used to show that consistent arithmetical systems have unprovable true statements.

  18. 1932
    Tarski's semantic truth definition and model theory

    A semantic definition of truth in formal languages gave the basis for the study of models of theories.

  19. 1934
    Curry-Howard correspondence (proofs as programs, propositions as types)

    Proofs were matched with programs.

  20. 1936
    Computability theory (Church-Turing)

    Turing defined an abstract machine, computable numbers and a universal machine, answering the Entscheidungsproblem negatively.

  21. 1945
    Recursion theory and realizability

    Recursive functions were used to interpret intuitionistic arithmetic.

  22. 1959
    Kripke semantics (possible-world semantics for modal logic)

    Relational models gave completeness theorems for modal logics.

  23. 1965
    Ax-Kochen

    Ultraproducts showed Artin's conjecture on p-adic forms holds for large primes.

  24. 1965
    Fuzzy sets

    Graded membership generalised set theory for imprecise concepts.

  25. 1967
    Automata and formal verification

    Floyd and Hoare apply logic to program correctness.

  26. 1969
    Classification theory in model theory (Morley, Shelah)

    Categoricity in uncountable cardinals leads to stability and a dividing-line classification of first-order theories.

  27. 1975
    Hilbert's tenth problem over rings of integers

    The remaining cases of undecidability for Diophantine equations over rings of integers were settled using a specially built elliptic curve with quadratic twists and prime selection from additive combinatorics.

  28. 1978
    Wu's method of characteristic sets for mechanised geometry theorem proving

    Ritt's characteristic sets turn elementary geometry statements into algebraic elimination so that machines can prove them.

  29. 2010
    Univalent foundations and homotopy type theory

    Voevodsky interpreted Martin-Lof type theory in simplicial sets, formulated the univalence axiom, and organised a special year in 2012-13 that launched homotopy type theory and the UniMath library.
