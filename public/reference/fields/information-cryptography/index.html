Events in this field

  1. 1939
    Wiener-Kolmogorov filtering

    Optimal prediction of stationary processes was solved by spectral methods.

  2. 1948
    Information theory

    Entropy and channel capacity were defined, with coding theorems for noisy communication.

  3. 1949
    Shannon: communication theory of secrecy

    Information-theoretic concepts were used to define and bound secrecy of ciphers.

  4. 1950
    Algebraic coding theory

    Error-correcting codes were built from algebra over finite fields.

  5. 1995
    Quantum error correction and fault tolerance

    Quantum codes and the threshold theorem showed reliable quantum computation is possible with noisy gates.

  6. ≈ 2000
    Elliptic curve and pairing-based cryptography

    Weil/Tate pairings on elliptic curves enabled new cryptographic protocols such as identity-based encryption.

  7. 2004
    Compressed sensing

    Sparse signals are recovered exactly from few random measurements by l1 minimisation, using harmonic analysis and random matrices.

  8. 2005
    Learning with errors and lattice cryptography

    Regev's LWE gave worst-case to average-case reductions and a quantum reduction from lattice problems.

  9. 2006
    Differential privacy

    Dwork-McSherry-Nissim-Smith defined privacy through noise calibration.
