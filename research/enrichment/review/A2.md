# Direct review, batch A2

Events P207, P203, P150, P195, P290, P356, P146, P133. Method: every milestone read against its cited source and an independent record (Crossref, zbMATH, Open Library, Internet Archive scans, MacTutor, Wikipedia); where a scan was available the passage was read. Years, roles, people, evidence claims, the first statement and missing milestones were checked. No dates or derived dates changed in this batch, so no relation distances changed.

**P207 Yang-Baxter and solvable lattice models.** Crossref and zbMATH confirm titles, years, pages and authors for McGuire 1964, Yang 1967, Baxter 1972, the quantum inverse problem paper and Baxter 1982. Fixed the 1981 naming milestone: Kulish, Reshetikhin and Sklyanin (Sklyanin was missing), with the Letters in Mathematical Physics record added; a zbMATH title search for "Yang-Baxter" over 1970-1981 returns only this paper. Added Onsager 1944 (precursor, low confidence, Wikipedia's account) and Jimbo 1985 (key development, Crossref). Role claims for McGuire, Yang and Onsager rest on Wikipedia's History section and the papers were not read; noted. Promoted to direct (grades B/B/C).

**P203 Distributions.** zbMATH and Crossref confirm Dirac 1930, Sobolev 1936, Schwartz 1945, 1948, 1950. Found a source conflict: MacTutor dates Schwartz's "Generalisation de la notion de fonction..." to 1948, zbMATH and the journal volume (Ann. Univ. Grenoble 21) give 1945; 1945 kept and the conflict recorded. Added Dirac 1927 as the earlier precursor (the 1930 book kept as the popularising exposition), adjusted the phase label. The 1945 paper's content was not read (conceptual grade C). Promoted to direct.

**P150 De Moivre's formula.** Crossref confirms the 1698, 1707, 1714 and 1722 papers. Read the 1722 "De sectione anguli" in the Internet Archive scan of Phil. Trans. vol. 32: it treats versed sines of arcs in the ratio n to 1 and refers back to the 1707 solutions; the OCR cannot confirm the explicit (cos x + i sin x)^n form MacTutor reports, so the first_statement confidence is now low and the note says priority among 1707, 1722 and 1730 is interpretive. Read Lemma I of the 1730 Miscellanea analytica (explicit cosine form, confidence high). Added a 1698 precursor (low, Wikipedia's account). Promoted to direct with conceptual grade C and a documented dispute.

**P195 Eudoxus.** Checked MacTutor (Eudoxus, Saint-Vincent), Wikipedia (Method of exhaustion), Heath's introduction to The Method of Archimedes (read in the Internet Archive text: Eudoxus first proved, Democritus first asserted the cone and pyramid results) and Crossref for Knorr 1975 and Stein 1990. All claims supported. Lowered the 1647 naming milestone to low confidence: the coinage claim rests on Wikipedia alone (MacTutor quotes the phrase but does not credit him with the term). Notes expanded; grades C/C/B because no Eudoxus text survives. Promoted to direct.

**P290 Sobolev spaces.** Confirmed Nikodym 1933 (Crossref, zbMATH), Sobolev 1936 (zbMATH), Sobolev 1938 (zbMATH 3035784 and the 1963 AMS translation, closing the earlier "zbMATH blocked" gap) and Adams 1975 (Open Library; replaced a search URL by the work record). Lowered the 1950 book to medium: only MacTutor and the 1963 translation date it, zbMATH has no 1950 record. Added a low-confidence 1936 Doklady note (zbMATH 2525918) and fixed stale notes. Not promoted: no paper was read, the 1936 note may state the spaces before 1938, and the naming and the Meyers-Serrin 1964 development are unadjudicated (listed in the event notes).

**P356 Riemann surfaces.** Confirmed Puiseux 1850 (Numdam), Riemann 1857 and Rado 1925 (Crossref, zbMATH, Wikipedia's Rado article for the statement), Weyl 1913, and read Neumann's 1865 preface, which cites Riemann's published 1851 dissertation as introducing multi-sheeted surfaces for multivalued functions; this confirms the 1851 first statement (confidence high). Corrected an evidence error: the Internet Archive item cited for Neumann (vorlriemannstheo00neumrich) holds Koenigsberger's 1878 book; replaced with item 10082480bsb. Added Klein 1882 (consolidation, records checked). Removed unverified "from memory" notes. Promoted to direct.

**P146 Fermat's adequality.** Resolved the open first print: the Internet Archive scan of Hérigone's Supplementum Cursus Mathematici is dated Paris 1642 and its Supplementum Algebrae, proposition XXVI "De maximis et minimis", gives the method and the tangent application; Fermat's 1679 Varia opera reprints that passage (quoted as Cursus Mathematici vol. 6, p. 68) naming Fermat as inventor and prints the Methodus itself ("Methodus ad disquirendam maximam et minimam", p. 63 ff.). First statement raised from low to medium, 1679 consolidation to high, with the caveat that a collected-works volume is a loose fit for "consolidation". The 1629-31 and 1636 precursors rest on MacTutor and Wikipedia (low and medium). Promoted to direct (B/B).

**P133 Newton's Principia.** Checked MacTutor (Newton, Huygens), Wikipedia (Principia, Keill, Gregory) and archive.org records for the 1687 and 1713 editions and Huygens 1673. The De motu title is now supported by Wikipedia. Added the 1679 Hooke-Newton correspondence (precursor) and Gregory's 1702 Astronomiae (read title page and preface in the scan); Keill 1702 kept with a note that its link to orbit theory is weak. The first edition's text was not read, so the first-application role (same work as the first statement) stays medium (methodological grade C). Promoted to direct.
