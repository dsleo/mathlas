# Direct review, batch A1

Eight source-first events reviewed milestone by milestone against Crossref, Open Library, MacTutor, Wikipedia and specialist history pages. Four are promoted to `direct`; four stay source-first with the outstanding work named. RAND report pages and zbMATH were blocked (HTTP 403 / Cloudflare), which is noted where it matters.

**P002 The calculus: promoted.** Checked Barrow 1670, Newton 1671, Leibniz 1684 and 1686, L'Hopital 1696 and Newton 1711 against Wikipedia, MacTutor, Open Library and Crossref. Added Leibniz's private 1675 notebooks (precursor), Newton's Principia 1687 and De quadratura 1704 (key developments), corrected the unsupported claim that Collins edited Barrow, and confirmed the 1696 first edition of the Analyse (Open Library, Wikipedia), raising it to high. The conceptual date stays Leibniz 1684, earlier than every printed Newton statement; grades B/B. Open: the 1693 Wallis print of Newton's notation.

**P199 Ergodic theory: promoted.** All 1931-1932 PNAS records, Hopf 1937, Furstenberg 1977 and the 1981 journal confirmed in Crossref or Open Library. Fixed an evidence entry that pointed Koopman at Birkhoff's DOI and one claim that Wikipedia's page does not support, moved Boltzmann's 'Ergode' from the 1898 lectures to the 1885 Crelle paper (Crossref; Stanford Encyclopedia dates the coinage 1884), and merged two same-paper duplicates (Koopman, von Neumann). First_application is now von Neumann 1932 (was 1931). Koopman's operator content rests on MacTutor and later surveys, not the paper text; priority between Birkhoff and von Neumann (Zund 2002) was not read. Grades C/C/B.

**P224 Dynamic programming: promoted.** Crossref confirms the 1952 PNAS paper, 1953 Shapley, 1954 survey; Open Library and a 1958 Science review confirm the 1957 book. Added Arrow-Harris-Marschak 1951 (low-confidence precursor) and Bellman's 1953 bottleneck paper as the earliest published application found (methodological date 1953, low). Lowered first_statement and naming to medium because 1952 RAND memoranda (RM-813) may predate the PNAS paper; the RAND pages could not be read, though the year is unaffected. Grades B/C/B.
