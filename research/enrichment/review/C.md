# Direct review, batch C

Ten source-first events, each read milestone by milestone against its cited source and an independent record (Crossref, zbMATH, Open Library, Euler Archive, MacTutor, Wikipedia). Where a source could not be read (AMS PDF blocked, zbMATH page blocked) the milestone says so.

**P265 Kantorovich linear programming.** Checked Crossref (Koopmans 1949, 1960 translation, Dantzig 1949), Open Library (1951 volume, 1953 textbook), MacTutor. MacTutor dates the Leningrad pamphlet to July 1939, which settles the first statement (confidence now high). Added Dantzig's Econometrica 17 (1949) paper, an earlier public statement of his model than the 1951 chapter; fixed stale notes. Promoted to direct (B/B): the remaining caveats (Koopmans's content, the "first textbook" claim, an undated name "linear programming") are stated in the notes.

**P446 Calculus of variations from the brachistochrone.** Checked MacTutor (brachistochrone, Lagrange), Euler Archive E9/E65/E296, Wikipedia. Promoted (B/B); Acta Eruditorum dates rest on MacTutor because no catalogue records exist.
