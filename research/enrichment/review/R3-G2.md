# Review R3-G2

Per-event notes on the outstanding items of P052, P190, P430, P198, P460, P122 and P187. Texts were read in Internet Archive scans (Royal Society papers, Miscellanea analytica, Traite de dynamique, the 1533 De triangulis, Woepcke's Extrait du Fakhri) and in the AMS scan of Bull. AMS 41(5). Nothing is promoted: every event stays source-first, because for each at least one milestone is still unread. `python3 scripts/validate_events.py` passes.

**P052 Computer science separates from mathematics (changed, not promoted).** Read: Church's abstract No. 205 in Bull. AMS 41(5), May 1935, pp. 332-333 (the AMS PDF now downloads). It adopts a Godel-Herbrand recursive function, maintains that effectively calculable functions should be identified with recursive ones, and states that the invariants-of-conversion problem is unsolvable. Changed: it is now the first statement (a printed abstract is publication; 1935), the 1936 Am. J. Math. paper and Turing become key developments, map date 1936 to 1935, shown methodological 1936, link distances to P284, P222, P063, P502, P086, P541, P426 recomputed on both sides. Still open: the 1956 proposal of the term and whether Fein 1959 is the first print use (Forsythe 1961 and Knuth's report of Fein in 1957 seen only in search excerpts); Stanford 1965 and ACM 1947 not examined; no first application; Fein, Newell-Perlis-Simon and Knuth texts unread.

**P190 Euler: complex logarithms and exponentials (changed, not promoted).** Read: Cotes, Logometria (Internet Archive scan); the Euler Archive records for E168 (written 1747, printed 1751) and E807 (written 1747, printed 1862); a university paper summarising Cajori and Langton's translation of E168; ProofWiki and Wikipedia on the history. Changed: Bernoulli's paper dated 1704 (printing of the Memoires for 1702, as in P460), with a low-confidence note; added precursors for the Leibniz-Bernoulli letters (16 March 1712 to 29 July 1713, printed 1745), Euler's letters to Bernoulli (about 1727-1729, low) and Euler's unpublished 1747 memoir (E807, printed 1862); notes on the E168 written-year conflict (1747 Archive, 1749 in the translation) and on the Introductio (Euler Archive). Still open: no source seen states ln(-1) = i*pi explicitly (Wikipedia, citing Wilson, says Euler never wrote the identity); E168, E807 and the Introductio passage unread (PDF 403, scan OCR too poor); the Bernoulli 1702/1704 paper not found in a scan; exact dates of Euler's letters to Bernoulli unverified.

**P430 Spectral ideas (changed, not promoted).** Read: d'Alembert, Traite de dynamique, 1743, arts. 98-104 (Internet Archive scan b30530593): the weightless string with two weights, equations ddx = (ax+by)dt^2 and ddy = (cx+ey)dt^2, the condition that both bodies reach the vertical together (equal-weights case YY = 2XX), and the extension to more weights; Brechenmacher quotes the same problem from the 1758 edition. Changed: new first statement 1743, Euler 1765 becomes a key development, map date 1765 to 1743. Because 1743 precedes P445 (1750), the old link "P430 follows P445" would reverse chronology; it was replaced by "P430 precedes P445" (d=7, kind context) on both sides. Person added: d'Alembert. Still open: Euler 1765, Lagrange 1766, Cauchy, Sturm, Hermite, Hilbert texts unread; whether d'Alembert's quadratic deserves "eigenvalue" is interpretive (he has no such concept); the 1758 expanded treatment and the 1747 Berlin memoir unread.

**P198 Regiomontanus (changed, not promoted).** Read: the 1533 Nuremberg edition (Internet Archive ARes352131): title page "anno Christi M.D.XXXIII", Petreius, ed. Schoener; Schoener's preface says the manuscript came through Pirckheimer. The 1464 dates printed in the volume belong to Regiomontanus's quadrature texts. Museo Galileo, EBSCO and Wikipedia confirm Epitome 1462 (printed Venice 1496) and De triangulis completed 1464, so the 1462 date no longer rests on MacTutor alone; the second evidence line (a 1543 print) was replaced. Changed (a judgement): under the publication-year rule the 1464 manuscript is a precursor and the 1533 print the first statement, so the map date moves 1464 to 1533 and the links to P158 (214 to 283) and P193 (129 to 60) are recomputed; shown methodological now empty (no key development remains). The earlier reading (first statement 1464) is recorded as a defensible alternative and can be restored by reverting this milestone. Still open: manuscript circulation before 1533, Zeller 1946, the text of the books, al-Jayyani's influence.

**P460 Trigonometry and the exponential (changed, not promoted).** Read: Cotes's Logometria (arc of a quadrant as measure of the ratio between EX + XC sqrt(-1) and CE, times sqrt(-1)); de Moivre 1707 (roots as n-th-root sums, sine-table rule), 1722 De sectione anguli (versed-sine multiple-angle theorem, no sqrt(-1)) and 1730 Miscellanea analytica Lemma I (cosines of an arc and its n-fold: x = 1/2 [(y + sqrt(yy-1))^(1/n) + (y - sqrt(yy-1))^(1/n)]). Changed: evidence and notes; 1730 raised to high, 1722 to medium; no dates moved. Still open: Bernoulli 1702/1704 (no scan of that Memoires volume found; stays low), Introductio passage (OCR unreadable), Euler E168 unread.

**P122 Al-Karaji polynomial algebra (evidence added, not promoted).** Read: Woepcke's 1853 Extrait du Fakhri (Internet Archive): powers of the unknown without limit, reciprocal powers, multiplication, division only by a single power; the work was dedicated to the vizir Fakhr al-Mulk, who died in September 1017 (Ibn Khallikan, quoted by Woepcke), and composed about the beginning of the 11th century. This supports the 1010-1015 range and gives 1017 as an upper bound; no date moved. Still open: al-Bahir (1149 derived from age 19), Abu Kamil and al-Khwarizmi dates inferred from lifespans; the Arabic manuscript, al-Bahir and Rashed unread.

**P187 Jaina combinatorics (notes only, not promoted).** Read: search excerpts for a second source (a Czech survey of ancient Indian mathematics) placing the Sthananga Sutra in the 3rd or 2nd century BC and listing combinatorics among its topics; added as a second evidence line with a low-confidence note. Still open: Kusuba, Plofker and Datta-Singh unread; no critical-edition dating of the Bhagavati, Sthananga or Anuyogadvara sutras; the Anuyogadvara factorial claim is single-source; the order against Pingala stays open.
