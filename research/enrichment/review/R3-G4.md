# Round 3, batch G4 (P162, P415, P216, P217, P448, P368, P065, P193)

Follow-up on the outstanding items left by rounds R2-R2b, R2-R3, R2-R3b and R2-R5. Two events are promoted (P162, P448); the rest are changed and stay as they were (P065 and P193 were already direct).

**P162 d'Alembert, Euler and the wave equation (promoted, grade B for the conceptual date).** Read: Taylor's De motu nervi tensi (Phil. Trans. 28, 1713, Internet Archive copy: Lemma 2 and Problem 1) and Methodus incrementorum (1715, prop. 22); Lagrange's Recherches sur la nature et la propagation du son (Miscellanea Taurinensia I, title page 1759, introduction and chapter II on strings, which summarises Taylor, d'Alembert and Euler); Euler's Eclaircissemens (Melanges de Turin III, title page 1766, arts. I-XXVII: arbitrary discontinuous initial curves, two arbitrary functions). Changed: Lagrange 1759 added as a key development (the string as n loaded masses, n to infinity); Taylor 1713 and Euler 1766 raised to high on the texts; Bernoulli's year in the people list corrected to 1755. Still open: d'Alembert 1749 and Euler 1750 read at the opening and solution passages only; Euler 1766 read to art. XXVII; Lagrange's own finite-mass solution not worked through.

**P415 Dirichlet's principle (changed, not promoted).** Read: Hilbert's JDMV 8 note (text dated Gottingen 12 October 1899, printed 1900), Hilbert's Math. Ann. 59 paper (its footnote says it is a reprint of the 1901 Gottingen Festschrift; it names Gauss 1839, Thomson 1847, Dirichlet 1856), Weber's Crelle 71 note (dated Heidelberg June 1869, printed 1870) and Kellogg 1929 (Dirichlet principle, Weierstrass 1870, Hilbert 1899). Changed: the former 1900-1904 Hilbert range split into the 1900 programme (lecture 1899) and the 1901 proof (reprinted 1904); the precursor now carries Hilbert's dates (1839-1856); Weber raised to high; Kellogg set to medium (broad potential-theory text); Courant 1950 added from Open Library and Crossref. Still open: Courant not read; Gauss 1839 and Thomson 1847 not read; the Monatsberichte printing of Weierstrass 1870 not seen; Schwarz, Neumann and Poincare routes not searched.

**P216 Hormander, Malgrange, Ehrenpreis (changed, not promoted).** Read in Numdam PDFs: Malgrange's 1956 memoir (introduction, chapter I theorem 1, bibliography) and Schwartz's Bourbaki exposé 87 (December 1953, after Malgrange). The 1953 C. R. note (237, 1620-1622) is confirmed by Malgrange's own bibliography and by the dated Bourbaki talk; Malgrange states that most of his chapter I was found independently by Ehrenpreis (Amer. J. Math. 76, 883-903). Malgrange 1956 raised to high; lifespans of Malgrange (1928-2024) and Ehrenpreis (1930-2010) added (Wikipedia descriptions). Still open: Hormander 1955 (Project Euclid, Springer and Intl. Press are all closed or bot-protected), Ehrenpreis 1954 (JSTOR) and the 1953 note itself unread; the 1963 book unread.

**P217 Pseudodifferential operators (changed, not promoted).** Crossref print dates and reference lists: Kohn-Nirenberg February 1965 (issue 1-2, pp. 269-305), Hormander August 1965 (issue 3, pp. 501-517), Kohn-Nirenberg's Non-coercive boundary value problems August 1965 (cites the algebra paper); CPAM 1965 author index (Internet Archive); zbMATH titles of the two Unterberger-Bokobza notes (1964: "Les operateurs de Calderon-Zygmund precises", no term; 1965, C. **P448 Beltrami and Klein models (promoted, grades B/B).** Read: Cayley's Sixth memoir (Collected Papers vol. 2, no. 158; read 6 January 1859, Phil. Trans. 149, pp. 61-90; the theory of distance, nos. 209-229); Beltrami's Teoria fondamentale and the original references in the Opere matematiche vol. 1 (Saggio: Giornale di Matematiche 6, 1868, pp. 284-312; Teoria: Annali (2) 2, 1868-69, pp. 232-255; in n dimensions the geodesics are linear equations, first-order surfaces play the role of planes); Klein's paper in Math. Ann. 4 (1871), which credits Cayley for the projective measure and says Beltrami had already found the linear geodesics and the quadric at infinity. Added: Beltrami 1865 (geodesics to straight lines, Annali (1) 7, 185-204) as a precursor, from Crossref, Beltrami's own opening of the Teoria and Klein's footnote; Houel's lifespan added. Not read: the 1865 paper, Klein's 1871 Gottingen note and Houel's translation of the Teoria.

**P368 Lyapunov stability (changed, not promoted).** Read in the Numdam PDF of the 1907 French translation: the translator's note says the memoir was printed in 1892 by the Kharkov Mathematical Society, the preface is dated Kharkov 5 April 1892, and the text sets out the first and second methods. Read in GDZ the first two pages of Perron 1930 (Math. Z. 32, 703-728): the conjecture that negative real parts of the linear part imply stability under small nonlinear terms fails for variable coefficients (an example in section 2); added as a key development. Still open: no library record of the Kharkov print; Chetaev's 1936 paper and 1946 monograph unread (Open Library has only the 1961 translation); first use of "Lyapunov function" not found; Perron read only at the introduction.
