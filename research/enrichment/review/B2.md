# Direct review, batch B2

Method: each milestone read against its cited record (Crossref, Euler Archive, MacTutor, Wikipedia reference lists, TCD Hamilton archive, library catalogues). zbMATH returned HTTP 403 and was not usable. `scripts/validate_events.py` does not exist in this checkout, so a local script checked ordering, derived dates, `shown`, `y` and the symmetric `rel` distances instead.

**P004 Graph theory from the Königsberg bridges (promoted).** Euler Archive E53 gives written 1735, published 1741 (Commentarii 8, pp. 128-140), so the separate 1736 first statement was a duplicate of the 1741 paper and was removed; conceptual moves 1736 to 1741, with `y`, `shown`, `derived` and the six `rel` distances updated on both sides. Crossref confirms Kirchhoff 1847, Cayley 1857, Hierholzer 1873 (posthumous, with Wiener), Sylvester 1878 (Nature, 7 Feb) and the König review. Added Sainte-Laguë's 1926 monograph as an earlier consolidation (low, history page) and softened König's "first". Sylvester's wording and Kirchhoff's content rest on secondary accounts. Promoted with grades B/C/C.

**P428 Quaternions (promoted).** Crossref confirms the three Phil. Mag. parts (July, Oct, Dec 1844). The Royal Irish Academy paper of 13 November 1843 is printed in Proc. RIA 2 (1844) 424-434 (TCD transcription), so no earlier print exists and 1844 is secure; both printings added to the notes and evidence. Tait 1867 first edition and Hamilton 1853 confirmed in catalogues. No first application found, so no methodological date. Promoted with B/B.

**P256 Intersection cohomology (changed, not promoted).** The 1979 Kazhdan-Lusztig Inventiones paper states the conjectures but is not shown to use intersection cohomology; the first application is now the 1980 "Schubert varieties and Poincaré duality" (Crossref), so methodological moves 1979 to 1980. The 1977 Comptes Rendus note (Sér. A 284, 1549-1551) still rests only on the Wikipedia reference list. Outstanding: independent record for 1977, 1981 proofs and BBD 1982 as milestones, contents unread.

**P366 Automorphic functions (changed, not promoted).** Poincaré's first note is C. R. 92, 14 Feb 1881, pp. 333-335 (Numdam bibliography; no scan); its title already names Fuchsian functions, so naming moved 1882 to 1881 (institutional and `took` updated) and the Acta "Théorie des groupes fuchsiens" became a key development. Koebe 1907 milestone no longer lists Poincaré as author. Added Klein's 1882 notes (low). Outstanding: scans of the 1881 notes, contents of Klein 1883 and the Acta memoirs, zbMATH for Koebe, coinage of "automorphic".

**P432 Complex numbers (changed, not promoted).** Added Wessel's full title and pages, corrected Gauss's citation to the 1831 Anzeige announcement, and swapped the Wessel/Argand person roles (Wessel originator). Outstanding: no bibliographic record for Wessel 1799 or Gauss 1831, Argand's 1806 printing status, Wallis, and unexamined forerunners (Kühn, Buée).

**P187 Jaina combinatorics (unchanged data, notes only; not promoted).** MacTutor supports the Bhagabati (c. 300 BC) and Sthananga (c. 2nd century BC) rules but gives no date or factorial claim for the Anuyogadvara Sutra and does not mention Pingala; caveats added. Dating remains single-source and low confidence; Kusuba and Plofker unread.

**P414 Noncommutative algebras and number theory (promoted).** Crossref confirms every cited paper. Brauer 1929 does not itself apply number-theoretic methods, so first application moved to Hasse 1931 (p-adic division algebras; added with a person entry; methodological 1929 to 1931) and Brauer became a key development. The 1932 Hauptsatz authors (Brauer, Hasse, Noether) are confirmed by Crossref. Added Molien 1892/93 and Dickson 1923 as precursors; Deuring is no longer called the first book on algebras. Wedderburn 1906 content was not read (confidence low). Promoted with B/C/C; the Hasse role rests on title and standard accounts.
