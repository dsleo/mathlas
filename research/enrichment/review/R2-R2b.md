**P007 Fourier series (changed, not promoted).** Read in the Internet Archive scan of the Nouveau Bulletin des sciences, Tome I: the extract of Fourier's 1807 memoir, signed P. (March 1808 issue, pp. 112-116), which prints the series for an arbitrary function of the plate, the coefficient integrals and the remark that the series equals the function only on the interval unless it is periodic. Riemann's 1868 history section (read in the Göttingen Abhandlungen volume) cites exactly this place ("Bulletin d. sc. Tom. I. p. 115"). The first statement therefore moves from the 1822 book to 1808; the 1822 book becomes the consolidation (institutional date 1822), and the map year changes from 1822 to 1808 with link distances updated. Riemann's habilitation moves from 1867 to 1868 (title page of the volume). Dirichlet 1829 is confirmed by Crossref, arXiv 0806.1294 and Riemann's account of it. Outstanding: attribution of "P." to Poisson rests on a dealer and Darboux (not read); Fourier's memoir, Dirichlet's text and Bessel 1819 were not read.

**P013 Functional analysis (changed, not promoted).** Read: Hadamard's 1903 note (Comptes rendus 136, Internet Archive scan), Fréchet 1906 (introduction, section 59, bibliography), Hadamard's 1910 Leçons (section 232) and Hilbert's 1912 book (preface and introduction). The 1903 note uses only "opérations fonctionnelles" and never the noun, so it is no longer a naming milestone but a key development (representation of linear functional operations); the noun is first printed by Fréchet 1906 (ascribed to Hadamard) and defined in Hadamard 1910; the institutional date becomes Lévy's 1922 title. Hilbert's 1904 first communication (pp. 49-91) is confirmed by his own preface. Volterra's 1889 Acta memoir is added (Crossref; Fréchet credits Volterra and Arzelà as first on functions of lines). Still open: Volterra 1887, 1889 and 1890, Riesz 1910 (lending-only scan), Fredholm, Banach and Lévy were not read.

**P188 Potential theory (changed, not promoted).** Read: Laplace's memoir (Mém. Acad. Sci. Paris, année 1782; 1785) in the Oeuvres complètes, Lagrange's 1773 attraction memoir (Oeuvres vol. 3), Poisson's 1813 note (Nouveau Bulletin des sciences, Tome III, "Remarques sur une équation...", which proves the interior equation with -4πρ) and Green's Essay introduction. The first statement moves from 1786 to 1785 (V and its partial differential equation in Laplace's memoir); Lagrange 1773 integrates the components directly and stays a precursor; the 1813 note is identified and reclassified from first application to key development; the 1823 memoir is a later unread item. Outstanding: whether another 1770s Lagrange memoir printed the potential function earlier, Poisson's 1811-12 electrostatic memoirs, Laplace's 1787 Cartesian form, Gauss 1840.
