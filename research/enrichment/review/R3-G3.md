# Review round 3, batch G3

Primary-text pass on eight source-first events (archive.org scans of Dirichlet's Werke, Gauss' Werke II, Lie-Engel, the Comptes rendus of February 1881, Poincare's Oeuvres VI, Math. Annalen vols 16, 31, 33, 36, Wallis 1685, the Nye Samling vol. 5; Crossref and zbMATH for the rest). None is promoted: every event still has milestones that rest on records or history pages only.

**P358 Lie theory (changed, not promoted).** Read: Lie's preface to Lie-Engel vol. 1 (work on PDEs 1872-73; discoveries of 1873 and 1874 found the theory; citations of the Goettingen note of 3 Dec 1874 and of the Archiv 1876), the opening of Lie 1880 (Math. Ann. 16, "Theorie der Transformationsgruppen", all groups of a one- and a two-dimensional manifold) and the opening of Killing part I (Math. Ann. 31, 1888: structure constants, Jacobi relations, cites his Programme 1884 and 1886). Changed: evidence and notes only; the 1873-74 precursor is now high. Still open: the 1874 note itself and Weyl 1925 unread; no first application. "Lie algebra" is attributed to Weyl in 1934 by the Encyclopedia of Mathematics (other sources: "the 1930s", one excerpt: 1935); no primary text, so a candidate in the notes, not a milestone.

**P432 Complex numbers (changed, not promoted).** Read: the Nye Samling vol. 5 contents and the opening of Wessel's memoir (pp. 469-518, volume record dated 1799), now high; Gauss' Anzeige of 23 April 1831 (Werke II pp. 169-178, passage on pp. 174-175: complex integers as a plane lattice, "wahre Metaphysik der imaginaeren Groessen"), the commentatio presented 15 April 1831 and printed in 1832, so the milestone is the Anzeige, high; Wallis 1685 preface (a discourse on negative squares and imaginary roots with geometrical constructions), precursor now medium. Open: Wessel and Wallis beyond the opening, Kuhn, Buee, Argand 1806, Hamilton items.

**P011 Algebraic topology (changed, not promoted).** Read Poincare's Analysis situs sections 5-6 (homologies with integer coefficients, Betti numbers) in Oeuvres VI: first_statement 1895 now high. Open: Noether's date, Vietoris 1927, Mayer 1929, Hopf 1928, Seifert-Threlfall, Alexandroff-Hopf unread.

**P256 Intersection cohomology (changed, not promoted).** zbMATH Open gives Beilinson-Bernstein, Localisation de g-modules, C. R. 292 (1981) 15-18 (folded into the Brylinski-Kashiwara 1981 milestone, same result) and Beilinson-Bernstein-Deligne, Faisceaux pervers, Asterisque 100 (1982), now a milestone (8 in total). Wikipedia supports the 1980 Kazhdan-Lusztig reinterpretation after a suggestion of Deligne. Open: no paper read (Goresky-MacPherson 1977, KL 1980, BB, BK, BBD).

**P012 Commutative algebra (changed, not promoted).** Read Hilbert 1890 (Math. Ann. 36), section I, Theorem I = the finite basis theorem for arbitrary systems of forms, with a footnote citing the Goettingen preliminary notes of 1888 and 1889; and Hilbert's Math. Ann. 33 note (new proof of Gordan's theorem for binary invariants, not the basis theorem). Crossref chapter records show the ideal theory (Allgemeine Idealtheorie der kommutativen Ringe, Theorie der Polynomideale) is in Moderne Algebra Teil II (1931), so the consolidation moves 1930 to 1931 and the derived institutional date to 1931 (map year unchanged, no rel updates). A title search finds no use of "commutative algebra" for this field before Zariski-Samuel 1958 (Vandiver 1934 uses it for another structure). Open: the 1888/89 Goettingen notes (could move the first statement), Dedekind, Lasker, Noether, Krull unread; Hilbert 1890 versus Dedekind 1871 remains a judgement.

**P005 Analytic number theory (changed, not promoted).** Read Dirichlet's Werke I: the Crelle 18 (1838) piece is a preliminary note (memoir "sous presse"; applies the Legendre-symbol series to quadratic forms), while the characters (indices modulo a primitive root, (p-1)th roots of unity, series L, Euler product) are in the Berlin memoir read 27 July 1837, printed in the Abhandlungen volume of 1837 (issued 1839 per Wikipedia). The first_application keeps 1838 with a corrected label and description (took label updated); the memoir is added as a key_development 1839. Legendre's attempt is confirmed from Dirichlet's own account. Open: the Monatsbericht 1837 notice (pp. 108-110, possibly the earliest print), the second half of the memoir, Euler E72 and Legendre unread; the 1839 printing year rests on Wikipedia.

**P366 Automorphic functions (changed, not promoted).** Read the printed notes of 14 February 1881 (C. R. 92 pp. 333-334: Fuchsian groups and functions defined and named after Fuchs) and 21 February 1881 (pp. 395-396: integration of linear equations with algebraic coefficients by Fuchsian functions); first_statement, naming and first_application are now high and primary-checked. "Automorphic" is placed about 1890 (Klein) by one secondary source (Adelmann-Gerbracht, arXiv 0712.4296, fn 8); the term is in the 1897 book title; no primary text, so not a milestone. Open: Acta memoirs, Klein 1882/83, Fricke-Klein, Koebe unread.

**P040 Modularity (changed, not promoted).** The 1955 mimeograph precursor was folded into the 1956 milestone's note and Wiles / Taylor-Wiles merged (same Annals issue) to make room under the cap of 8 for Conrad-Diamond-Taylor 1999 (J. AMS 12, 521-567) and Breuil-Conrad-Diamond-Taylor 2001 (J. AMS 14, 843-939), both confirmed in Crossref; Diamond 1996 (Ann. of Math. 144, 137-166, Crossref) is recorded in the 1999 note. Sugaku 7 (1956) 269 rests on Wikipedia and nLab; no independent record found. The names Shimura-Taniyama(-Weil), Taniyama-Shimura(-Weil), Taniyama-Weil and modularity conjecture are listed by Darmon; no dated coinage found (Lang 1995: Shimura stated the conjecture to Serre and Weil, most likely 1964, via arXiv 2003.08242), so no naming milestone and the derived institutional date stays null. Open: Sugaku print, Lang 1995, all primary papers.
