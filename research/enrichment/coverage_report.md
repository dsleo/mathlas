## Traditions

No field in `data/events.json` records the mathematical tradition (Greek, Chinese, Indian, Islamic, European, ...). `cat` is the harvest batch, `cl` the field and `from`/`to` the subjects, so a tradition breakdown cannot be computed without guessing from names. An optional `trad` tag is proposed in `research/enrichment/MILESTONE_SCHEMA.md`; events are not mass-tagged.
