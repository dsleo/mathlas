Field Emergence Map

{{N}} dated births and fusions of mathematical fields, arranged along time. Each event has a mathematical insignia: a subject symbol, a unique constellation derived from its catalogue ID, and a lead mathematician’s initials where the record names one. Open an entry for its milestones, people and works, each linked to its source.

Each dot is one historical event, placed by its idea date and a broader field grouping; its original field labels remain in the event details. Solid lines suggest a later use of a distinctive field or named idea; dotted lines mark explicit field context or a reviewed named continuation; dashed lines join related developments without a direction. A line does not by itself establish direct influence. Unlinked events remain visible. Hover to see neighbours, click to open.
