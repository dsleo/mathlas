## Q58: Umbral moonshine and pariah moonshine

- Type: dictionary-analogy
- From: finite group theory (Mathieu, O'Nan), mock modular forms, string theory on K3  To: moonshine beyond the Monster
- Dates: conceptual 2010-2012; methodological 2015-2017
- What: Mathieu moonshine and 23 further umbral moonshines link groups to mock modular forms, proved in 2015, and a pariah group (O'Nan) was tied to weight-3/2 modular forms in 2017.
- Source page: https://www.quantamagazine.org/mathematicians-chase-moonshine-string-theory-connections-20150312/
- Pass 1: accept (high). Genuine, distinct from E44 (monstrous moonshine). Pariah moonshine: Duncan-Mertens-Ono, Nat. Commun. 8 (2017) verified.
- Pass 2: accept (medium). A new bridge (mock modular forms, K3 sigma models) beyond E44. Add Gannon, Adv. Math. 301 (2016) and Duncan-Mertens-Ono, Nat. Commun. 8 (2017), both verified.

## Q3: Algorithmic discrepancy theory (Bansal) and the Komlos conjecture

- Type: technique-transfer
- From: combinatorial discrepancy theory, algorithm design  To: discrepancy bounds
- Dates: conceptual 2010; methodological 2010-2025
- What: Designing efficient vector-balancing algorithms, rather than existence arguments, turned out to reproduce and then improve the best known discrepancy bounds, including a new bound towards Komlos.
- Source page: https://www.quantamagazine.org/huge-breakthrough-in-the-math-of-imbalance-20260821/
- Pass 1: accept_with_corrections (high). Real TCS-to-combinatorics transfer; Quanta page agrees on 2010 start. Add Bansal-Dadush-Garg, FOCS 2016 (Komlos algorithm matching Banaszczyk; from memory) which fits the methodological 2016 date. Banaszczyk 1998 is the non-constructive baseline.
- Pass 2: accept (medium). Algorithm design improves existence bounds in combinatorial discrepancy (Komlos to O~(log^{1/4} n)). The Komlos conjecture is still open.

## P43: Kervaire invariant one problem solved by equivariant homotopy

- Type: technique-transfer
- From: equivariant stable homotopy theory, chromatic homotopy theory  To: differential topology (framed manifolds)
- Dates: conceptual 1960-1969; methodological 2009-2016
- What: Equivariant stable homotopy and a slice spectral sequence show that framed manifolds with Kervaire invariant one exist only in dimensions up to 126.
- Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Veblen_Prize_in_Geometry
- Pass 1: accept_with_corrections (medium). Real and major, but from/to as written (Algebra, Topology -> Topology) makes it look single-field. The bridge is equivariant stable homotopy plus the slice filtration (from Voevodsky motivic homotopy, per my recollection, not checked) applied to the surgery/differential-topology Kervaire problem (Kervaire 1960; Browder 1969 reduction). Methodological 2009 (arXiv) to 2016 is right.
- Pass 2: accept_with_corrections (medium). Borderline: both sides lie within topology, but this mirrors seeded B02. Browder's 1969 reformulation belongs in the conceptual range.
