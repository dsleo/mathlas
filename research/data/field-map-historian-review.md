# Historian–mathematician review, 2026-10-19

Review scope: the Field Emergence Map's event chronology, subject coverage, source claims, and graph language; plus the main proof explorer's source labels and navigation. The full 563-record map was surveyed, with targeted checks of doubtful milestones. This is a review of the application's historical claims, not an independent verification of every milestone.

## Corrections applied

- **P439, transcendence of π:** separated Hermite's 1873 proof for *e* from Lindemann's 1882 proof for π. The latter now supplies the event's first-statement date. [Lindemann's 1882 paper](https://www.digizeitschriften.de/download/pdf/235181684_0020/log31.pdf).
- **P444, fundamental theorem of algebra:** Girard's 1629 assertion and d'Alembert's 1746 attempted proof are precursors. The map now marks Gauss's 1799 dissertation as an influential proof attempt and Argand's 1806 argument as a later complete proof, with Gauss's gap stated in the record. [Gauss dissertation archive](https://edoc.hu-berlin.de/items/0d7bf8a1-c3a8-408f-bc0d-4e068559f3e2).
- **P307, local Langlands for GL(n):** retained the 1969/1970 origin date, which matches Langlands's lecture and printed account. Added Henniart's 2000 proof and the 2001 Harris–Taylor treatment as later milestones. [Langlands's account](https://publications.ias.edu/rpl/problems); [Henniart's paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s002220050012).
- **P398, early transcendence theory:** clarified the title so the 1844 Liouville milestone is no longer presented as a proof about *e* or π. Later proofs remain dated separately.
- Converted same-year relationships from “later related work” arrows to undirected connections and exposed the Field map in the main navigation.

## Outstanding source work

- [Seventeen prominent map milestones](field-map-evidence-review.json) still link only to search or query pages. Their dates should be checked against the actual publication or a reliable bibliographic record before the app claims direct source verification.
- In the proof explorer, 74 of 462 approaches still lack a route-level citation. The interface distinguishes independently reconstructed sketches from source-extracted ones; the next research pass should attach a source and locator to those 74 approaches.
- [Seven MSC2020 classes](field-map-msc2020-audit.md) are represented thinly. They are candidates for future sourced events, especially order theory, nonassociative algebra, integral transforms, and heat/thermodynamics.
