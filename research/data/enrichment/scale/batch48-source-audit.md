# Scale batch 48 source audit

Ten events, 37 source-linked milestones. The schema validator reports no warnings in this batch, and the work metadata checker found no year mismatches or absent bibliographic records among its cited works. The 2010 Voevodsky document and the IAS programme are marked as primary text, so they do not receive a bibliographic grade.

- **QC73:** The inherited name combines exotic spheres and smooth Poincaré with a summary about the Weinstein conjecture. The enriched name follows the summary. [Taubes's 2006 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611007) proves the 3D closed-Reeb-orbit result; the 2008 [Hutchings–Taubes work](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.0140) uses the ECH/Floer bridge for a stronger stable-Hamiltonian statement.
- **QC31:** The [Kapustin–Witten preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0604151) is public in 2006 and its [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.4310/CNTP.2007.v1.n1.a1) dates to 2007. The later proof of geometric Langlands is not treated as the same claim.
- **P27:** The checked [Bourgain–Gamburd expander paper](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2008.167.625) is 2008. The [affine-sieve publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-009-0225-3) distinguishes online publication in November 2009 from the March 2010 issue. The 2008 received date is not a public date.
- **Q52:** [Awodey–Warren 2007](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.0248) is a homotopy/type-theory precursor. Voevodsky's dated [October 2010 public project document](https://www.math.ias.edu/vladimir/sites/math.ias.edu.vladimir/files/univalent_foundations_project.pdf) states univalent foundations and describes Coq formalizations. The [IAS Special Year](https://www.ias.edu/math/sp/univalent) begins in 2012; the [collective book](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.0729) follows in 2013. Earlier private ideas and talks are not promoted to a publication date.
- **Q71:** [Baker 2007](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701075) establishes curve-to-graph specialization. [Jensen–Ranganathan 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.06579) and [Jensen–Payne 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01285) are later Brill–Noether and maximal-rank applications. The latter's revised abstract disclaims an earlier claimed moduli-space consequence; that claim is excluded.
- **QC52:** [Eynard–Orantin's February 2007 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0702045) defines the recursion and already applies it to the Kontsevich integral. The [May 2007 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.3600) treats Weil–Petersson volumes, and [2012 work](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.1103) proves the toric Calabi–Yau BKMP case.
- **WC100:** Razborov's [2007 journal paper](https://doi.org/10.2178/jsl/1203350785) defines, names and applies flag algebras. A [later survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.04741) supplies consolidation.
- **WC66/QC23:** The [Guth–Katz preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.4105) appeared in 2010, preceding the [2015 Annals version](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2015.181.1.2).
- **QC60:** [Duplantier–Sheffield 2008](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1560) supplies the probabilistic LQG precursor; the [mating-of-trees construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.7055) appears in 2014.
- **WC110:** The [2008 Kontsevich–Soibelman paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2435) introduces its motivic DT framework and wall-crossing identities. A [2013 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3253) develops wall-crossing structures further. The generic phrase “wall-crossing” is not assigned a false first naming date.
