# Scale batch 49 source audit

Ten event records, 44 milestones. The schema validator reports no errors or warnings in this batch. The metadata checker verifies the bibliographic links in this batch, except for the deliberately unrecorded 2011 Wise manuscript and the author-hosted 2023 relative-Langlands draft. Both are cited as primary text rather than as bibliographic records.

- **P47:** Wise lists the quasiconvex-hierarchy manuscript as 2011 on his [publication page](https://www.math.mcgill.ca/wise/papers.htm). Its indexed book edition is 2021, so the 2011 draft remains a precursor without a work-record date. [Agol's public proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.2810) is 2012, followed by the 2013 [journal version](https://doi.org/10.4171/dm/421).
- **Q28:** The [author-hosted first draft](https://www.math.ias.edu/~akshay/research/BZSVpaperV1.pdf) has PDF creation metadata dated 6 July 2023 and was publicly linked that month. Its [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04677) begins September 2024. The 2023 milestone therefore has primary-text evidence and a C grade; the 2024 bibliographic milestone remains distinct. The 2025 [Oberwolfach report](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2025/18) is the named institutional anchor.
- **Q65:** [Part I](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02678) began in 2023 and proves selected Friedman–Ramanujan cases. The [tangle-exclusion paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01601) in 2024 explicitly adapts Friedman’s graph method. [Part II](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12268), first posted in 2025, proves the asymptotically optimal spectral gap. These are three different stages.
- **B40:** [Ten Martini](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503363) is a 2005 precursor. Avila's [global theory preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.3902) is from 2009; the [Acta publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11511-015-0128-7) is 2015.
- **P70:** Hrushovski's [2009 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.2190) first gives the model-theoretic transfer. Breuillard–Green–Tao's [general structure theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5008) is a 2011 development.
- **Q7:** [Positive Grassmannian amplitudes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.5605) were public in 2012; the [named amplituhedron and volume interpretation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.2007) followed in 2013.
- **QC8:** Green–Tao's [2006 prime-pattern paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0606088) was conditional in higher complexity. [Möbius–nilsequence orthogonality](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.1736) supplied one input in 2008. The [inverse-theorem announcement](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.0205) appeared in June 2010 before the [full proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.3998) that September.
- **Q3:** [Bansal 2010](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.2259) made classical discrepancy bounds constructive. [Bansal–Dadush–Garg 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.02882) matches a Komlós bound, while the [2022 work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11427) proves a smoothed variant. Neither establishes the full Komlós conjecture.
- **Q42:** Fargues's [2016 conjectural overview](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00999) and Scholze's [2017 diamonds foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07343) precede the joint [2021 geometrization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13459). The latter constructs L-parameters but does not claim a complete local correspondence.
- **Q58:** The [2010 K3–Mathieu observation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.0956), [2012 umbral conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.2779), [2015 proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01472) and [2017 O'Nan branch](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.03516) are separate phases.

The arXiv export API rate-limited the metadata audit. `scripts/verify_field_works.py` now falls back to each arXiv abstract page's `citation_title` and original `citation_date`; this cleared transient unavailable results without treating a search candidate as a verified work.
