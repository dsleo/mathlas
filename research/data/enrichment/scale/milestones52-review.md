The [2025 Hodge-atoms preprint, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.05105v1) cites that blowup behavior and claims irrationality for **very general** cubic fourfolds; it does not prove every cubic fourfold irrational. The unsupported catalogue 2019 date was not copied.
- Q22's [original paper, section 1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.10821) explicitly uses the determinant method to cover lifted points by algebraic curves. This supports the methodological claim beyond the abstract.
- Q38's two [November 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.15323) [companion preprints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.15161) precede the [December Nature article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04086-x) within the same year. The schema stores year precision, so this finer ordering stays in the event note.
- Q62's checked [2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403) describes arithmetic holonomy bounds applied to Zagier's construction. The inherited phrase “analytic continuation of power series” was not supported by that abstract. Apéry's 1978 lecture is dated in the milestones by its [1979 publication](https://numdam.org/book-part/AST_1979__61__11_0/).
- Q68's [Nature article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05172-4) establishes a 2022 AlphaTensor application to exact small-matrix algorithms. It is not a claim about the general matrix-multiplication exponent.
- B33's [2024 result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12337) proves partial dimension cases; the [2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17655) states the full three-dimensional Kakeya result. A [2026 streamlined proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14411) is later development.
