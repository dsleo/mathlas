# Batch 41 source audit

Ten directly reviewed events; 25 cited milestones. The records are in [milestones41.json](milestones41.json), and [work verification](batch41-work-verification.json) separates metadata matches from manually read sources. A matched title/year is not evidence of priority.

- **P037 (two-dimensional gravity).** The [Witten article](https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/sdg/1990/0001/0001/SDG-1990-0001-0001-a005.pdf) says volume 1 (1991), though it records April 1990 lectures. The [MPIM archive](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/2283/) dates the Kontsevich preprint to 1991, before its 1992 journal version. [Mirzakhani's original article](https://www.ams.org/journals/jams/2007-20-01/S0894-0347-06-00526-1/S0894-0347-06-00526-1.pdf) says electronically published in 2006, before the 2007 issue. The metadata verifier does not support these archival URLs; their displayed primary documents were checked directly.
- **P039 (mirror symmetry).** [Greene–Plesser's 1990 paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(90)90622-K) records a Calabi–Yau duality before the [1991 Candelas et al. enumerative application](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(91)90292-6). The [1994 Kontsevich preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/alg-geom/9411018) is a categorical reformulation, and [Givental's 1996 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/alg-geom/9603021) proves the mirror conjecture for projective complete intersections. The latter's title is *Equivariant Gromov-Witten Invariants*, corrected after the automated title check. Earlier preprint public-release dates and institutional naming remain unresolved.
- **P238 / P276.** [Floer's 1987 paper](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-1987-15517-0) and [1988 Lagrangian paper](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214442477) support separate fixed-point and intersection stages; earlier Floer–Zehnder work still needs priority review. The [1989 BSS paper](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-1989-15750-9) defines real-number computation, while the [1998 book](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-0701-6) consolidates it.
- **P312 (topological data analysis).** The [FOCS 2000 paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.2000.892133) precedes the 2002 journal version; the [FOCS 2000 proceedings listing](https://dblp.org/db/conf/focs/focs2000) independently identifies the conference year because Crossref omits it. The [2004 computing paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/997817.997870) precedes its 2005 journal version. The [2009 survey](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-09-01249-X) is a consolidation. Earlier barcode precursors, first use on real data and naming of the entire field remain open.
- **P314 / P320.** The [1994 Kontsevich–Manin preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9402147) is verified as an axiomatic formulation, but physical and Ruan–Tian antecedents prevent an absolute firstness claim. [Vassiliev 1990](https://doi.org/10.1090/advsov/001/03), [Kontsevich 1993](https://www.ihes.fr/~maxim/TEXTS/publicationsfrancais.html) and [Bar-Natan 1995](https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-9383(95)93237-2) support distinct knot-theory developments. Kontsevich's 1993 work currently has an author publication-list record rather than a direct publisher URL.
- **P489 / P494.** [Atiyah's 1988 axioms](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1988__68__175_0/), [Witten's 1988 theory](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01223371) and [1989 knot application](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01217730) are separate phases; Segal's conformal axioms await a dated public source. Hitchin's [self-duality paper](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-55.1.59) and [integrable-systems paper](https://doi.org/10.1215/S0012-7094-87-05408-1) are both 1987; exact ordering is unresolved.
- **P536 (graph counting).** The [February 2022 public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08739) develops quantum-field counting for virtual Euler characteristics. The [January 2023 public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.01121) proves the negative asymptotic and derives rapid cohomology growth. Earlier graph-complex history is a precursor, not a 1987 start for this specific calculation.

This is one direct research pass. Its bibliography supports B-level dates at best; no A grades or inter-model agreement are claimed. Unestablished first applications and institutional dates remain blank.
