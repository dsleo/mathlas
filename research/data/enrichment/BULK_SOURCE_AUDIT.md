# Bulk source audit of the 410 pending events

The [machine-readable audit](bulk-source-audit.json) and its [reproducible script](../../../scripts/bulk_audit_pending_sources.py) checked **every one of the 410 pending events** in one bulk workflow. All 309 appearances of the 289 distinct cached bibliographic leads now match their source record's title and year. For the 210 events with no cached lead, the run attempted a fresh work search for each and returned 210 top results, with no remaining request errors. The [review queue](bulk-review-queue.json) places 200 entries with confirmed records first, 29 with a promising fresh title lead next, and 181 needing targeted primary or history searches last.

These counts are **source discovery, not milestone enrichment**. Search ranking is unreliable for historical firstness: only 29 of the 210 fresh top results have even 0.75 title similarity to the event name, and some high-scoring titles are modern histories rather than original works. The audit retains all fresh results as `candidateOnly`; it assigns no role, person, or derived date. The 200 events with confirmed cached records still need an abstract or primary-text reading and priority comparison before promotion. The 210 fresh leads need identity and relevance checking first.

This bulk pass therefore removes duplicate metadata lookup from the manual workflow without silently turning catalogue citations into dates. The event-by-event milestone review remains at 147 researched events, 410 open events, and 464 cited milestones. An independent agreeing pass and grade A remain outstanding.
