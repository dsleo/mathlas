# Thirty-event pilot: agreement and evidence coverage

The fixed pilot now has 30 milestone records containing 149 dated or explicitly undated stages. The original independent Opus and Sonnet flat-date passes agreed within three years on **24/30 conceptual**, **22/30 methodological**, and **12/27 institutional** dates where both supplied a date. The direct pass was not independent of the A/B input material during reconciliation, so its pair comparisons are descriptive only. The full raw counts remain in [`three-way-comparison.json`](three-way-comparison.json); they are agreement about old *single dates*, not grades for individual milestones.

After direct milestone reconciliation, a source-linked dated milestone exists for **28/30 first statements**, **27/30 first applications**, and **27/30 naming/consolidation/institution anchors**. These are coverage counts, not proof that each date is the undisputed earliest. The two missing first statements are ancient P186 and P119, for which a period or lifespan does not establish an original publication date. The missing first applications are those two cases and Euclid's *Elements* (P134). The missing institutional anchors are P048, P300 and P526; their event notes record the search boundary.

The higher raw A/B evidence coverage does not justify A grades. Bibliographic links must be checked against the exact work and interpretation, and the milestone stage may differ from the earlier flat date. For example, group theory's Lagrange, Galois and Cayley years describe successive phases, while P048's 2015 arXiv posting predates its 2018 journal issue. The direct audits for batches 1–3 explain these decisions. The 10% interpretation audit and independent milestone-level second pass still remain to be done before publication as a final historical dataset.
