# Control anchors for reconciliation

These are narrow checks on three known cases, **not** completed enrichment
records and **not** input to either independent research pass. Use them after
both passes are submitted to catch a change of date definition or event scope.

- **H43, non-Euclidean geometry.** MacTutor describes Gauss's 1817 conclusion
  as unpublished and Lobachevsky's 1829 work as published. A history of
  Lobachevsky at St Andrews dates the journal publication to **1829–1830**.
  The Hungarian Academy dates János Bolyai's independent *Appendix* to
  **1832**. Under this pilot's public-publication rule, 1817 belongs in a
  priority note, not the conceptual date. Sources:
  [MacTutor history](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Non-Euclidean_geometry/),
  [Lobachevsky biography](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/DSB/Lobachevsky.pdf),
  [Hungarian Academy](https://mta200.hu/kincseink/bolyai-janos-appendix-106388).
- **E34, Langlands programme.** The Institute for Advanced Study identifies
  the **1967** Weil letter as correspondence whose typed copy later circulated
  among specialists. Its page for *Problems in the Theory of Automorphic
  Forms* describes the **1970** Lecture Notes volume as the first published
  account of those conjectures. The 1967 letter therefore needs a note even
  when the conceptual date is anchored to the 1970 publication. Sources:
  [IAS letter record](https://publications.ias.edu/book/export/html/43),
  [IAS 1970 publication record](https://publications.ias.edu/rpl/problems).
- **E77, Boolean algebra and Stone duality.** Open Library lists Boole's
  *The Mathematical Analysis of Logic* in **1847**; the American Mathematical
  Society gives Stone's Boolean algebra representation paper in **1936**.
  This catalogue event joins the birth of algebraic logic to a later transfer
  into topology. If the two passes disagree over which idea the conceptual
  date describes, reconcile the event's **scope** before treating the date
  difference as a priority dispute. Sources:
  [Open Library edition list](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2630394W/The_mathematical_analysis_of_logic),
  [AMS Stone paper](https://www.ams.org/tran/1936-040-01/S0002-9947-1936-1501865-8/).
